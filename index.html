Readiness-Based Learning

Open academic pathways.

Courses are entered because the learner is ready for them—not because a birth date or grade label says they must be.

Mathematics policy: mathematics courses are open by readiness, prerequisite, mastery and learner goal. A Grade 9 student may study Algebra I; an older student may strengthen Pre-Algebra; an adult may enter either course without adopting a grade identity.

How Learning Works

Mastery before motion.

Students progress through understanding, practice, assessment and reflection. A course may recommend review when mastery has not yet been demonstrated.

01Learn

Understand

Read, observe, investigate and build the underlying concept before rushing to an answer.

02Practice

Apply

Use the skill repeatedly in new examples, projects, laboratories and problem-solving contexts.

03Assess

Demonstrate

Independent assessment logic measures what the learner can actually do without mentor interference.

04Master

Reflect & Advance

Review errors, reinforce weak prerequisite skills and continue when the course mastery standard is met.
