Adaptable Academic Pathways

Different levels without limiting potential.

The same campus can support students who need careful foundations, a complete core program, or deeper extended work.

01

Foundation

Clear instruction, smaller steps, repeated practice, visual support, and flexible pacing.

  • Prerequisite review
  • Guided examples
  • Accessible assessment
02

Core

A complete grade-level route balancing knowledge, communication, problem solving, and application.

  • Full course sequence
  • Projects and assessments
  • Portfolio-ready work
03

Extended

Research, advanced problems, interdisciplinary work, independent study, and deeper evidence use.

  • Enrichment and honors
  • Independent investigation
  • Advanced presentation
04

Capstone

Senior synthesis through projects, portfolios, research, service, presentations, and graduation records.

  • Thesis and portfolio
  • Public presentation
  • Graduation evidence
