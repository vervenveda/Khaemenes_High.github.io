Adaptive Academic Pathways

Depth can change without changing learner identity.

Challenge level is a learning decision. Formal grade placement is an Academy decision. Keeping those separate allows both support and acceleration without corrupting records.

01

Foundation

Prerequisite strengthening, guided instruction, flexible pacing, repeated practice, and early exit when readiness is verified.

  • Subject-specific support
  • Smaller instructional steps
  • 80% readiness exit evidence
02

Core

Complete grade-level study balancing knowledge, communication, application, and assessment.

  • Full course sequence
  • Projects and assessments
  • Portfolio evidence
03

Extended

Honors depth, interdisciplinary work, advanced problems, research, and independent investigation.

  • Advanced challenge
  • Cross-course inquiry
  • Public presentation
04

Capstone

Long-form research, portfolio synthesis, service, design, professional practice, and transition evidence.

  • Research or thesis
  • Portfolio defense
  • Graduation transition
