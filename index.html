The Four Major Classes

Four doors. One academic campus.

The front hall no longer asks students to hunt through the repository. These are the primary academic doors and each one leads directly to its department landing page.

MMajor I · Mathematics

Mathematics

Readiness-based mathematics with multiple course levels and a progression designed to meet students where they are and move them forward.

  • Pre-Algebra through Calculus pathway
  • Readiness and placement logic
  • Interactive tools and labs
  • Mentor and Beta pathways
Enter Mathematics
SMajor II · Science

Science

A four-year laboratory-science pathway built around investigation, modeling, evidence, engineering, and cumulative mastery.

  • Integrated Science 9
  • Biology & Biotechnology 10
  • Chemistry & Materials Science 11
  • Physics & Engineering 12
Enter Science
LAMajor III · Language Arts

Language Arts

Reading, literature, writing, rhetoric, research, speaking, media literacy, and publication across the four-year sequence.

  • English 9 is live
  • Four-year department pathway
  • Research and publication tools
  • PROSE and connected resources
Enter Language Arts
SSMajor IV · Social Studies

Social Studies

History, geography, civics, government, law, economics, global politics, media verification, and research.

  • Grades 9–12 sequence
  • Global Studies Honors in Grade 9
  • Mastery-based assessments
  • Senior research and defense
Enter Social Studies
