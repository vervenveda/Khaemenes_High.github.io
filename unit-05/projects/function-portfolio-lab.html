Unit 05 · Mission Board

Multiple-Representation Function Portfolio

Choose a real relationship and prove that you understand the function as more than an equation.

Unit Home

Possible investigations

  • Temperature over time
  • Cost versus quantity
  • Distance versus time
  • Allowance or savings plan
  • Tiered pricing
  • A repeating design or arithmetic sequence
  • Your own measurable relationship

Required portfolio

  • Define input/output variables and units.
  • State a meaningful domain and range.
  • Show at least three representations: rule, table, graph/sketch, mapping, sequence, or verbal model.
  • Explain why the relation is a function.
  • Evaluate at least three inputs.
  • Identify one restriction or limitation.
  • Create one transformation or alternate rule and explain its effect.
  • Check consistency across representations.

100-point rubric

Function definition 15 · Domain/range 15 · Multiple representations 20 · Mathematical accuracy 20 · Consistency checks 10 · Transformation/extension 10 · Context/limitations 5 · Communication 5
