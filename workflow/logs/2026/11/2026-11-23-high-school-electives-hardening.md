# High School Electives Hardening — 2026-11-23

## Scope
Psychology 101 remains the current active elective quality benchmark. Five planned elective shells were audited and found to have no hidden full-course implementations to recover.

- Computing & Digital Technology — 13 weeks / 65 planned daily learning experiences.
- Fine Arts & Visual Studio — 13 weeks / 65 planned daily learning experiences.
- Health, Wellness & Human Development — 13 weeks / 65 planned daily learning experiences.
- Music & Music Theory — 13 weeks / 65 planned daily learning experiences.

Total new architecture: 65 weekly arcs / 325 planned daily learning experiences.

## Quality contract
Added ELECTIVE_QUALITY_STANDARD_V1.md requiring:
- discipline-authentic evidence;
- weekly retrieval/model/practice/application/revision;
- cumulative transfer checkpoints at Weeks 4, 8 and 12;
- Week 13 culminating project/portfolio/defense;
- 80% mastery where objective checks apply;
- correction/revision below mastery;
- privacy, safety and accessible alternatives;
- no promotion to active-course authority until all 65 student lesson surfaces and assessments are implemented and runtime-tested.

## Authority boundary
The five new maps remain catalog plans with authority:none in the canonical ownership manifest. Curriculum architecture completion does not make a course executable or release-certified.

## CI
Added validate-high-school-elective-curriculum-v1.cjs and wired it into Discovery Hall validation. Ownership validation now verifies curriculum-ready catalog maps without allowing premature execution authority.
