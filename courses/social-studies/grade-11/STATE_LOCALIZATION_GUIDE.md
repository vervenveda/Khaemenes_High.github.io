# Grade 11 Government, Law & Economics Honors · State & Local Localization Guide

## Academy rule
Grade 11 is nationally portable and **state-localizable by requirement**. The U.S. constitutional-government, law, economics, public-policy, media-literacy, and civic-literacy core remains fixed. Jurisdiction-specific evidence is layered into that core.

## Government and law overlays
- Compare the U.S. Constitution with the learner's **state constitution** and selected amendments or rights provisions.
- Map state executive, legislative, judicial, county/municipal, special-district, and tribal-government relationships where relevant.
- Use official **state/local election administration, districting, ballot, campaign-finance, public-record, court, and legislative sources** for nonpartisan practice.
- Include state/local criminal/civil procedure or civil-liberties cases only when age-appropriate and legally accurate.
- Map state-specific civic-literacy or required-instruction expectations (for example Florida FCLE participation) into the existing national sequence.

## Economics overlays
- Use state/local **employment, wages, industries, housing, population, taxes, budgets, public spending, trade, agriculture, tourism, technology, energy, and business data** in economic laboratories.
- Compare state/local fiscal choices with federal monetary/fiscal institutions without confusing their powers.
- Include local labor-market, entrepreneurship, infrastructure, supply-chain, and public-policy cases.

## Non-negotiable core
The 36-week course, Government Final, Economics Final, assignments, weekly mastery, constitutional/economic reasoning, source verification, and Academy 80% standard remain fixed.

## Safeguards
Civic work remains nonpartisan. Do not require political affiliation, immigration/citizenship status, religion, family finances, health information, or other sensitive personal disclosures.
