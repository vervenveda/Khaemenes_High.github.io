1Week 25Which definition best matches “productivity”?AProductivity means output produced per unit of input.
2Week 26Which definition best matches “public good”?APublic good means a good that is difficult to exclude people from and can be used without substantially reducing availability to others.
3Week 24Which definition best matches “monopoly”?AMonopoly means a market in which one firm supplies a product without close substitutes and entry is blocked.
4Week 36Which definition best matches “revision memo”?DRevision memo means a record explaining how feedback, evidence, or error correction changed a final product.
5Week 22Which statement is most consistent with the evidence and reasoning developed in Week 22?DThe less responsive side of a market generally bears more of a tax’s economic burden regardless of who formally remits it.
6Week 32Which statement is most consistent with the evidence and reasoning developed in Week 32?DFiscal effects depend on economic slack, timing, composition, financing, expectations, monetary response, and implementation.
7Week 23Which statement is most consistent with the evidence and reasoning developed in Week 23?AFirms compare expected revenue with opportunity costs, including the owner’s time and capital.
8Week 35Which definition best matches “stakeholder”?DStakeholder means a person, group, institution, or community affected by or able to influence a decision.
9Week 30Which statement is most consistent with the evidence and reasoning developed in Week 30?DBanks transform maturities and allocate credit, creating benefits and vulnerability to liquidity crises.
10Week 34Which definition best matches “command economy”?ACommand economy means a system in which political authorities make major production and allocation decisions through directives and plans.
11Week 29Which definition best matches “unemployment rate”?DUnemployment rate means the unemployed actively seeking work as a percentage of the labor force.
12Week 20Which definition best matches “absolute advantage”?DAbsolute advantage means ability to produce more output with the same resources.
13Week 30Which definition best matches “financial intermediary”?BFinancial intermediary means an institution connecting savers, borrowers, investors, and payment users.
14Week 36Which definition best matches “oral defense”?DOral defense means a structured presentation in which a student answers questions and justifies methods and conclusions.
15Week 27Which definition best matches “budget deficit”?ABudget deficit means the amount by which government spending exceeds revenue during a period.
16Week 19Which statement is most consistent with the evidence and reasoning developed in Week 19?CScarcity requires choices even in wealthy societies because time, labor, land, capital, and attention are limited.
17Week 30Which definition best matches “systemic risk”?BSystemic risk means the danger that failure or distress spreads through interconnected financial institutions and markets.
18Week 29Which statement is most consistent with the evidence and reasoning developed in Week 29?DThe unemployment rate excludes people not in the labor force and should be interpreted with participation, hours, wages, and underemployment.
19Week 23Which statement is most consistent with the evidence and reasoning developed in Week 23?AEntrepreneurship includes innovation and risk but occurs within legal, financial, and social institutions.
20Week 25Which action best demonstrates mastery of the Week 25 inquiry?AA student distinguishes a median from an individual outcome and explains the labor-demand or labor-supply mechanism behind a wage change.
21Week 29Which definition best matches “business cycle”?CBusiness cycle means recurring expansions and contractions in aggregate economic activity.
22Week 24Which definition best matches “monopolistic competition”?CMonopolistic competition means a market with many firms selling differentiated products and relatively easy entry.
23Week 28Which definition best matches “productivity”?DProductivity means output per unit of labor or other input.
24Week 24Which definition best matches “antitrust”?AAntitrust means law and enforcement intended to protect competitive processes from unlawful restraints or monopolization.
25Week 23Which statement is most consistent with the evidence and reasoning developed in Week 23?BMarginal analysis guides output decisions, while average costs and market conditions affect entry and exit.
26Week 22Which definition best matches “elasticity”?DElasticity means a measure of how responsive one variable is to a change in another.
27Week 20Which statement is most consistent with the evidence and reasoning developed in Week 20?AComparative advantage, not absolute productivity alone, explains potential gains from specialization and exchange.
28Week 30Which definition best matches “liquidity”?ALiquidity means the ease with which an asset can be used for payment or converted to spendable funds without major loss.
29Week 29Which statement is most consistent with the evidence and reasoning developed in Week 29?CInflation is a rate of general price change, not simply a high price or one product becoming more expensive.
30Week 22Which action best demonstrates mastery of the Week 22 inquiry?DA student identifies which side is less elastic before predicting tax incidence and distinguishes statutory remittance from economic burden.
31Week 27Which definition best matches “proportional tax”?BProportional tax means a tax applying the same rate across the tax base.
32Week 21Which statement is most consistent with the evidence and reasoning developed in Week 21?CEquilibrium is a model of coordinated exchange, not a moral judgment that an outcome is fair or socially optimal.
33Week 26Which statement is most consistent with the evidence and reasoning developed in Week 26?BMarket prices may omit harms or benefits affecting people outside a transaction.
34Week 34Which statement is most consistent with the evidence and reasoning developed in Week 34?DEvery real economy combines market exchange, household production, organizations, law, and public authority.
35Week 27Which statement is most consistent with the evidence and reasoning developed in Week 27?CA sound public-spending analysis considers goals, evidence, alternatives, distribution, implementation, and opportunity cost.
36Week 23Which action best demonstrates mastery of the Week 23 inquiry?AA student includes explicit and implicit opportunity costs when evaluating profit and uses marginal rather than average reasoning for the next unit.
37Week 28Which definition best matches “real GDP”?AReal gdp means output measured with prices adjusted to remove the effect of inflation.
38Week 22Which definition best matches “tax incidence”?BTax incidence means the distribution of a tax’s economic burden between buyers and sellers.
39Week 21Which definition best matches “complement”?BComplement means a good commonly used with another good.
40Week 23Which definition best matches “profit”?CProfit means revenue minus explicit and implicit costs.
41Week 25Which definition best matches “compensating differential”?CCompensating differential means a wage difference associated with job risk, conditions, location, or nonwage characteristics.
42Week 36Which definition best matches “sensitivity analysis”?ASensitivity analysis means testing how a conclusion changes when assumptions or estimates change.
43Week 35Which definition best matches “implementation”?BImplementation means the administrative process of turning an adopted policy into operations and outcomes.
44Week 21Which action best demonstrates mastery of the Week 21 inquiry?BA student identifies whether the change is movement or shift and states the affected curve and determinant.
45Week 27Which statement is most consistent with the evidence and reasoning developed in Week 27?BBudgets express public priorities but are constrained by law, revenue, debt, capacity, and political bargaining.
46Week 27Which definition best matches “transfer payment”?BTransfer payment means a government payment that is not direct compensation for current production.
47Week 31Which statement is most consistent with the evidence and reasoning developed in Week 31?DPolicy affects borrowing costs, asset values, expectations, exchange rates, and credit conditions with uncertain lags.
48Week 31Which statement is most consistent with the evidence and reasoning developed in Week 31?DThe Federal Reserve has statutory goals involving maximum employment, stable prices, and moderate long-term interest rates.
49Week 23Which definition best matches “fixed cost”?DFixed cost means a cost that does not vary with current output in the relevant period.
50Week 36Which statement is most consistent with the evidence and reasoning developed in Week 36?AA conclusion should remain proportional to the evidence and should change when key assumptions fail.
