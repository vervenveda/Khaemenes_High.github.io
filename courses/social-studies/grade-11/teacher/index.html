Instructional Commitments

  • Grade accuracy, evidence, reasoning, calculation, citation, and revision—not political viewpoint.
  • Separate factual claims, legal holdings, economic models, ethical judgments, and policy preferences.
  • Use official records and multiple credible perspectives.
  • Provide accommodations without reducing the assessed standard.
  • Require correction before reassessment.
