Civic / Financial Action Planner

Course Home

Translate learning into a lawful, ethical, realistic action while protecting privacy and documenting results.

Goal and authority

Define the desired result, responsible institution or account, legal authority or contractual rule, and deadline.

Evidence and stakeholders

Identify affected people, strongest evidence, competing interests, and people whose perspective is missing.

Action steps

List specific communications, applications, budget changes, public comments, meetings, safeguards, or implementation tasks.

Risk and ethics

Consider privacy, consent, accuracy, security, unintended harm, conflicts of interest, and contingency plans.

Measure and reflect

Choose success indicators, record the outcome, compare it with the forecast, and identify the next revision.
