Evidence and Source Analysis

Course Home

Use this file for a statute, case, public record, dataset, contract, article, speech, advertisement, or financial offer.

Source identification

Record title, creator, institution, date, source type, URL or file location, and the question being investigated.

Authority and purpose

What can this source authoritatively establish? Why was it created, for whom, and under what rules or incentives?

Evidence and method

List the exact facts, language, data, sample, calculation, or legal reasoning used. Separate quotation from interpretation.

Limitations and missing information

What cannot be concluded from this source alone? Identify age, scope, jurisdiction, methodology, omissions, or conflicts.

Corroboration and conclusion

Compare at least one independent source. State whether the claim is verified, supported, complicated, contradicted, or unresolved and explain why.
