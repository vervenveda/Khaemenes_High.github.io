Personal Financial Literacy & Money Management Honors

Grade 11 · Separate ½ Credit · Income, Banking, Budgeting, Taxes, Credit, Consumer Law, Housing, Insurance, Saving, Investing, Education Finance, and Fraud Protection

An advanced 18-week personal-finance course in which students build and defend a documented independent-living plan while mastering income, taxes, banking, budgeting, credit, contracts, housing, insurance, saving, investing, education finance, and fraud protection.

Credit note: A separate one-half-credit Personal Financial Literacy and Money Management Honors course. It is not merged into the Government or Economics credit record.
