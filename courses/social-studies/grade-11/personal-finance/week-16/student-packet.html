Postsecondary Costs, FAFSA, Scholarships, and Student Loans

Essential question: How can a student compare education options by net price, completion probability, debt, and career value?

Key understandings

  • Sticker price is not net price, and a loan is financing rather than a discount.
  • Completion rate, transferability, accreditation, field outcomes, and total borrowing matter alongside admission.
  • FAFSA information and aid offers should be verified through official channels and compared line by line.

Vocabulary

cost of attendanceAn institution’s estimate of tuition, fees, housing, food, books, transportation, and other education costs.
net priceCost after grants and scholarships, before most loans and work earnings.
grantFinancial aid that usually does not require repayment when conditions are met.
scholarshipAid awarded under stated criteria, usually not repaid when conditions are met.
subsidized loanAn eligible federal student loan on which government pays specified interest during qualifying periods.
unsubsidized loanA loan on which interest generally accrues from disbursement.

Weekly Reading

Full Cost

Tuition is only one component. Fees, housing, food, books, transportation, technology, health coverage, and lost earnings can dominate the budget. Living at home can reduce cost but may change transportation and time.

Aid Types

Grants and scholarships reduce net price. Work-study is earned through work. Loans must be repaid with interest. Aid packages may contain different assumptions and should be standardized before comparison.

FAFSA and Verification

The FAFSA is completed through the official federal site. Students should protect credentials, respond to verification requests, and understand dependency and contributor rules. No one should charge to submit the federal form.

Debt and Completion

Borrowing for a completed, high-value credential differs from borrowing without completion. Students should examine program completion, licensing, transfer, earnings range, monthly payment, and alternatives such as apprenticeships or community college.
