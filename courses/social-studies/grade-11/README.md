- Grade 9 — Global Studies Honors

The Grade 11 upload packet includes only the updated department gateway and the new `grade-11` course folder. Existing Grade 9 and Grade 10 folders remain in the repository.


## State/localization rule
Grade 11 Government, Law & Economics Honors is **state-localizable by requirement**. State constitutions, state/local government and courts, election administration, public records, budgets/taxes, labor markets, economic data, and policy cases should be layered into the national federal/economic core. See `STATE_LOCALIZATION_GUIDE.md`. The Academy's 80% mastery gates remain fixed.


## Canonical mastery policy
- **80% is the fixed Academy mastery threshold.**
- Government, Law & Economics Honors: each week requires all five daily lessons, all three principal assignments with documented evidence, a completed constructed response, and at least 80% on the weekly objective quiz.
- Week 19 remains locked until the Government Final is mastered at 80% or higher.
- Full Government, Law & Economics course mastery requires all 36 weeks plus both the Government Final and Economics Final at 80% or higher.
- Personal Financial Literacy & Money Management Honors uses the same weekly contract across its 18 weeks; Week 10 additionally requires the Financial Literacy Midterm at 80% or higher, and full course mastery requires the cumulative final at 80% or higher.
- A completion checkbox or a blank submission is not academic evidence. Assignment submission requires a student response, evidence note, or a note identifying the submitted attachment.
- Teacher-scored written work remains teacher-reviewed; the browser must not silently convert submission status into an official academic grade.
