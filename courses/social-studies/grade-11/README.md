## State/localization rule
Grade 11 Government, Law & Economics Honors is **state-localizable by requirement**. State constitutions, state/local government and courts, election administration, public records, budgets/taxes, labor markets, economic data, and policy cases should be layered into the national federal/economic core. See `STATE_LOCALIZATION_GUIDE.md`. The Academy's 80% mastery gates remain fixed.
