# Week 04 — Constitutional Convention and Ratification

**Unit:** Unit 1 · Foundations of Constitutional Government  
**Frame:** 1787–1791 · convention · ratification · Bill of Rights  
**Essential question:** How did compromise make constitutional government possible, and what costs or unresolved conflicts did those compromises create?
