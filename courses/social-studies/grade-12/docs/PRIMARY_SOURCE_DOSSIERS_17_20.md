# Primary-source dossiers · Senior Social Studies · Weeks 17–20

Khaemenes Academy · Grade 12 · Original instructional annotations (2026-10-24)

**Use:** These are source-finding and analytical guides, not reproductions of the original documents. For offline study the factual extract below, source identification, and guiding questions remain readable without opening links. The full documents must be obtained separately and checked for rights before distribution. Student written responses should cite the named primary instrument and relevant article.

## Dossier A · UN Charter (1945) · Articles 1 and 2
**Issuer:** Founding United Nations member states. **Adopted:** 26 June 1945. **Effective:** 24 October 1945.
**Authenticated source:** https://www.un.org/en/about-us/un-charter/full-text
**Source-backed finding:** Article 1 establishes peace and security, friendly relations, cooperation and the UN as a harmonizing center. Article 2 establishes sovereign equality, peaceful dispute settlement, and restrictions on threats or use of force. This summary paraphrases, not quotes, the text.
**Questions:** How do sovereign equality and collective peace commitments interact? Which of the Articles describes an aspiration, an obligation, or an institutional function? What evidence beyond the Charter would be required to judge implementation?
**Weeks:** 17, 19, 20.

## Dossier B · UN Charter (1945) · Article 18
**Issuer/date:** As above. **Authenticated source:** https://www.un.org/en/about-us/un-charter/full-text
**Source-backed finding:** Article 18 distinguishes General Assembly important questions using a two-thirds voting threshold from other questions decided by a majority of members present and voting. Counting the correct denominator matters.
**Questions:** In a simulated ballot with 120 members present and voting, is 79 votes sufficient for a two-thirds threshold? Explain your calculation (80 required). What changes when absences or abstentions affect the denominator?
**Weeks:** 17–18.

## Dossier C · Universal Declaration of Human Rights (1948) · Articles 2, 7 and 10
**Issuer:** UN General Assembly, resolution 217 A. **Proclaimed:** 10 December 1948, Paris.
**Authenticated source:** https://www.un.org/en/about-us/universal-declaration-of-human-rights
**Source-backed finding:** Article 2 describes nondiscrimination in entitlement to Declaration rights, Article 7 addresses equality before the law and equal protection, and Article 10 addresses an independent and impartial tribunal in a fair and public hearing.
**Questions:** Distinguish principles stated in the Declaration from implementation evidence in a particular country. Identify which principle could frame a comparative investigation of court access. How would you avoid treating the Declaration as proof of domestic compliance?
**Weeks:** 17–18, 20.

## Dossier D · Original inquiry protocol: compare two authentic instruments
**Primary instruments:** UN Charter and Universal Declaration, links above.
**Original student task:** Construct a two-column evidence table with instrument, authoring institution, date, relevant article, claim supported by the article, potential misinterpretation, and evidence still required. Write a 200–300 word reasoned comparison; note that treaty obligations and a General Assembly declaration are not identical legal instruments. No real learner identity is needed for this template.
**Week:** 18 capstone proposal practice.

## Source custody and usage
- Cite the article number; do not turn instructional summaries into purported verbatim quotations.
- No claims about any nation's present compliance should be inferred from the founding documents alone.
- Offline users can study these summaries; availability of complete source texts offline is **not** yet certified.
- Fictional case questions remain identified as simulations, separate from the authenticated primary sources.
