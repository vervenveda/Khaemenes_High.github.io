# Primary-source dossier · Grade 12 Social Studies · Weeks 21–24

Khaemenes Academy · October 24, 2026 · locally readable instructional paraphrases

These are verified public **source references**, not invented historical records. Each synopsis is an original paraphrase for offline reading. Full-source offline reproduction has not been certified.

## Week 21 · Diplomacy and negotiation
**United Nations Charter, Article 33** (1945): Parties to disputes threatening international peace and security are directed first to pursue peaceful solutions, including negotiation, enquiry, mediation, conciliation, arbitration, judicial settlement or regional arrangements. Article 33 does not establish that any single technique guarantees settlement.
Official source: https://www.un.org/en/about-us/un-charter/full-text

**Dossier task:** Write a negotiating memo for a *fictional* boundary dispute: name two peaceful mechanisms, evidence both delegations need, an acceptable verification procedure, and a reservation-point risk. Label simulated facts clearly.

## Week 22 · Global governance
**UN Charter, Articles 18, 24 and 25** (1945): The General Assembly has voting rules for important and other questions; the Security Council has primary peace-and-security responsibility; member states undertake to carry out its decisions in accordance with the Charter. Do not treat Assembly recommendations and Council decisions as identical.
Official source: https://www.un.org/en/about-us/un-charter/full-text

**Dossier task:** Build a competence matrix for the Assembly and Council and calculate a simulated two-thirds Assembly vote with denominator stated.

## Week 23 · International law and courts
**Statute of the International Court of Justice, Article 36** (1945): The Court's contentious jurisdiction involves matters referred by states, treaty-based jurisdiction and specified forms of optional declarations, subject to their conditions. Merely naming a dispute does not establish Court jurisdiction.
Official source: https://www.icj-cij.org/statute

**Dossier task:** Compare the consequences of a specific treaty clause and a hypothetical declaration accepting compulsory jurisdiction; distinguish jurisdiction from merits.

## Week 24 · Human rights
**Universal Declaration of Human Rights, Articles 7, 8, 10, 19 and 25** (1948): The Declaration addresses equal protection, remedies, fair hearings, expression and social well-being, among other principles. It is a General Assembly declaration, not itself a record of implementation in any country.
Official source: https://www.un.org/en/about-us/universal-declaration-of-human-rights

**Dossier task:** Compare the rights stated with fictional measures of access to courts, information and basic services. Identify missing denominators and propose sources needed to assess implementation.

## Source and evidence rules
1. Document the issuer, original date, article and original URL; distinguish paraphrase from direct quotation.
2. Compare normative commitments with real implementation evidence; one does not prove the other.
3. Preserve student/teacher review separately from self-graded multiple-choice evidence.
4. No learner names or sensitive records belong in shared source files.
