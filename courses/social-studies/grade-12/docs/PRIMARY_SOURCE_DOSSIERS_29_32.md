# Khaemenes Academy · Grade 12 · Primary-source dossiers · Weeks 29–32

Original offline-readable educational summaries. The references below identify genuine primary instruments, not invented quotations or a claim that the full original documents are cached.

## Week 29 — Armed conflict and peacebuilding
**UN Charter (1945), Articles 2(4) and 33.** Article 2(4) addresses the threat or use of force in international relations; Article 33 lists peaceful methods of dispute settlement. Both must be interpreted within the Charter as a whole, including other applicable provisions.
Source: https://www.un.org/en/about-us/un-charter/full-text
**Study case:** Compare a fictional ceasefire declaration, independent monitoring record, and disputed incident log. Differentiate an announced obligation from verified adherence; propose corroboration and civilian-protection indicators.

## Week 30 — Security, nuclear risk, and emergency powers
**Treaty on the Non-Proliferation of Nuclear Weapons (1968), Articles I, II and VI.** These address obligations for nuclear-weapon states, non-nuclear-weapon states, and good-faith negotiations on nuclear disarmament respectively; treaty participation and reservations or interpretations must be checked against authentic records.
Source: https://disarmament.unoda.org/wmd/nuclear/npt/
**Study case:** Compare fictional compliance claims against the treaty's differentiated obligations; separate documentary evidence, verification and speculative motive.

## Week 31 — AI, surveillance and rights
**Universal Declaration of Human Rights (1948), Articles 12 and 19.** Article 12 concerns arbitrary interference with privacy and related interests; Article 19 concerns opinion and expression. Applying these broad principles to contemporary automated systems requires additional legal and technical evidence.
Source: https://www.un.org/en/about-us/universal-declaration-of-human-rights
**Study case:** Audit a *fictional* automated-benefits system for error rates across groups, appeal rights, data minimization, and evidence needed before claiming discriminatory implementation.

## Week 32 — Public health and humanitarian coordination
**Constitution of the World Health Organization (1946), Preamble and Articles 1–2.** States WHO's health objective and enumerates its functions; a founding mandate is not an empirical record of the effectiveness of any specific response.
Source: https://www.who.int/about/governance/constitution
**Study case:** Compare fictional outbreak records with different case definitions and sampling dates; identify why counts should not be added or treated as directly comparable without reconciliation.

## Academy evidence procedure
For each dossier, record date and issuing body, instrument and article, exact claim supported, its scope, corroborating evidence and limitations. Source-based assessments remain first-party and learner-specific. No student identity or grades are stored in these public research guides.
