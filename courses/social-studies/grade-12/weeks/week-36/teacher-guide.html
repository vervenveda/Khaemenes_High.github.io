Khaemenes High · Grade 12 Teacher Guide

Week 36 · Senior Thesis, Oral Defense, and Cumulative Final

Essential question: Can the student independently defend a rigorous conclusion, acknowledge limits, apply four years of social studies, and satisfy every graduation gate?

Teacher PortalStudent Packet

Instructional Purpose

Final thesis, executive summary, public product, oral presentation, questioning, cumulative examination, portfolio audit, evaluator verification, and final credit decision.

Evidence standard

Require claims to be traceable to Final course portfolio, thesis, examination, defense record, and graduation audit. Grade accuracy, authority, evidence, reasoning, comparison, limitation, citation, and revision—not viewpoint.

Daily Guidance

Monday · Concept and Inquiry Laboratory

Define the core concepts of Senior Thesis, Oral Defense, and Cumulative Final and frame a researchable response to the essential question.

Look for: explicit concepts, source evaluation, evidence-to-claim reasoning, counterargument, and revision.

Minimum time: 50 minutes.

Tuesday · Primary and Authoritative Source Laboratory

Evaluate evidence from Final course portfolio, thesis, examination, defense record, and graduation audit.

Look for: explicit concepts, source evaluation, evidence-to-claim reasoning, counterargument, and revision.

Minimum time: 50 minutes.

Wednesday · Comparative Data and Case Laboratory

Compare at least two cases or data series using common concepts and transparent methods.

Look for: explicit concepts, source evaluation, evidence-to-claim reasoning, counterargument, and revision.

Minimum time: 50 minutes.

Thursday · Argument, Seminar, and Performance Studio

Develop the Senior Thesis, Oral Defense, Cumulative Final, and Credit Review through evidence-based deliberation.

Look for: explicit concepts, source evaluation, evidence-to-claim reasoning, counterargument, and revision.

Minimum time: 50 minutes.

Friday · Mastery, Correction, and Capstone Checkpoint

Demonstrate weekly mastery and document correction or capstone progress.

Look for: explicit concepts, source evaluation, evidence-to-claim reasoning, counterargument, and revision.

Minimum time: 50 minutes.

Assignment Scoring Guidance

  • Accuracy and source authority: 8
  • Evidence and citation: 8
  • Reasoning and corroboration: 8
  • Limitations and clarity: 6

Do not accept uncited claims, fabricated evidence, or a response that substitutes political identity for analysis. Return incomplete work for correction.

Mastery Quiz Answer Key

  1. 1. B — Synthesis means integration of concepts and evidence into a coherent conclusion.
  2. 2. D — Oral defense means a presentation and questioning process testing independent mastery.
  3. 3. B — Graduation gate means a mandatory requirement that cannot be replaced by a weighted average.
  4. 4. B — Portfolio audit means a systematic review of required work, evidence, records, and corrections.
  5. 5. C — Transcript credit means a formally recorded unit of completed study verified by the responsible authority.
  6. 6. A — Honors distinction means recognition for mastery above the minimum course threshold.
  7. 7. A — Graduation credit requires verified mastery, not mere seat time.
  8. 8. D — The oral defense tests ownership, methods, evidence, limitations, and revision conditions.
  9. 9. C — Trace claims to Final course portfolio, thesis, examination, defense record, and graduation audit, evaluate authority and context, corroborate material facts, and state limitations.
  10. 10. D — Define the terms, compare evidence, explain reasoning, address a serious alternative, and name a revision condition.

Constructed Response

Can the student independently defend a rigorous conclusion, acknowledge limits, apply four years of social studies, and satisfy every graduation gate?

Scoring: 10 points: precise claim (2), accurate concepts (2), two specific pieces of evidence (2), reasoning (2), counterargument or limitation (1), revision condition (1).

Supports Without Lowering the Standard

  • Provide vocabulary previews and audio access.
  • Chunk complex sources while preserving essential evidence.
  • Permit speech-to-text, graphic organizers, and extended time.
  • Use additional practice and correction before reassessment.
  • Require the same final claim, evidence, reasoning, and integrity standard.
