Week 7 · Executives, Legislatures, and Bureaucracies

Essential question: How do presidential, parliamentary, and hybrid systems organize leadership, representation, and administration?

Teacher PortalStudent Packet

Instructional Purpose

Presidential and parliamentary executives, cabinets, legislatures, confidence votes, coalition government, bureaucratic capacity, and oversight.

Evidence standard

Require claims to be traceable to Cabinet formation, legislative oversight records, and comparative executive powers. Grade accuracy, authority, evidence, reasoning, comparison, limitation, citation, and revision—not viewpoint.

Daily Guidance

Define the core concepts of Executives, Legislatures, and Bureaucracies and frame a researchable response to the essential question.

Look for: explicit concepts, source evaluation, evidence-to-claim reasoning, counterargument, and revision.

Minimum time: 50 minutes.

Tuesday · Primary and Authoritative Source Laboratory

Evaluate evidence from Cabinet formation, legislative oversight records, and comparative executive powers.

Look for: explicit concepts, source evaluation, evidence-to-claim reasoning, counterargument, and revision.

Minimum time: 50 minutes.

Mastery Quiz Answer Key

  1. 1. C — Executive means the institutions responsible for leadership and implementation of law and policy.
  2. 2. B — Legislature means a representative body that deliberates, makes law, authorizes resources, and oversees government.
  3. 3. D — Parliamentary system means a system in which the executive depends on legislative confidence.
  4. 4. D — Presidential system means a system with a separately elected president serving a fixed term.
  5. 5. A — Bureaucracy means organizations and officials that administer laws and public programs.
  6. 6. C — State capacity means the ability of public institutions to implement decisions and provide core functions.
  7. 7. A — Separation or fusion of powers changes incentives for cooperation and deadlock.
  8. 8. C — Coalitions can broaden representation while complicating accountability.
  9. 9. B — Trace claims to Cabinet formation, legislative oversight records, and comparative executive powers, evaluate authority and context, corroborate material facts, and state limitations.
  10. 10. A — Define the terms, compare evidence, explain reasoning, address a serious alternative, and name a revision condition.

Constructed Response

How do presidential, parliamentary, and hybrid systems organize leadership, representation, and administration?

Scoring: 10 points: precise claim (2), accurate concepts (2), two specific pieces of evidence (2), reasoning (2), counterargument or limitation (1), revision condition (1).

Supports Without Lowering the Standard

  • Provide vocabulary previews and audio access.
  • Chunk complex sources while preserving essential evidence.
  • Permit speech-to-text, graphic organizers, and extended time.
  • Use additional practice and correction before reassessment.
  • Require the same final claim, evidence, reasoning, and integrity standard.
