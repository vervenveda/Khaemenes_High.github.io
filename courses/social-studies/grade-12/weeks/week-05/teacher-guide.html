Khaemenes High · Grade 12 Teacher Guide

Week 5 · Regimes, Democracy, and Authoritarianism

Essential question: Which institutional evidence distinguishes democracy, hybrid regimes, and authoritarian rule?

Teacher PortalStudent Packet

Instructional Purpose

Electoral democracy, liberal democracy, one-party systems, military rule, personalist rule, competitive authoritarianism, and democratic erosion.

Evidence standard

Require claims to be traceable to Freedom House methodology, V-Dem indicators, constitutional rules, and election-observer reports. Grade accuracy, authority, evidence, reasoning, comparison, limitation, citation, and revision—not viewpoint.

Daily Guidance

Monday · Concept and Inquiry Laboratory

Define the core concepts of Regimes, Democracy, and Authoritarianism and frame a researchable response to the essential question.

Look for: explicit concepts, source evaluation, evidence-to-claim reasoning, counterargument, and revision.

Minimum time: 50 minutes.

Tuesday · Primary and Authoritative Source Laboratory

Evaluate evidence from Freedom House methodology, V-Dem indicators, constitutional rules, and election-observer reports.

Look for: explicit concepts, source evaluation, evidence-to-claim reasoning, counterargument, and revision.

Minimum time: 50 minutes.

Wednesday · Comparative Data and Case Laboratory

Compare at least two cases or data series using common concepts and transparent methods.

Look for: explicit concepts, source evaluation, evidence-to-claim reasoning, counterargument, and revision.

Minimum time: 50 minutes.

Thursday · Argument, Seminar, and Performance Studio

Develop the Evidence-Based Regime Classification Brief through evidence-based deliberation.

Look for: explicit concepts, source evaluation, evidence-to-claim reasoning, counterargument, and revision.

Minimum time: 50 minutes.

Friday · Mastery, Correction, and Capstone Checkpoint

Demonstrate weekly mastery and document correction or capstone progress.

Look for: explicit concepts, source evaluation, evidence-to-claim reasoning, counterargument, and revision.

Minimum time: 50 minutes.

Mastery Quiz Answer Key

  1. 1. A — Regime means the durable rules and institutions governing access to and exercise of political power.
  2. 2. A — Democracy means a system in which citizens meaningfully choose leaders and can contest public power.
  3. 3. C — Authoritarianism means rule that sharply restricts political competition, accountability, or civil liberty.
  4. 4. C — Rule of law means the principle that public and private actors are governed by known, generally applicable law.
  5. 5. A — Accountability means the obligation to explain conduct and face consequences for misuse or failure.
  6. 6. A — Democratic erosion means incremental weakening of competitive elections, rights, checks, or lawful accountability.
  7. 7. B — Elections alone do not establish a constitutional democracy.
  8. 8. D — Regimes vary across multiple dimensions rather than a single democracy–dictatorship line.
  9. 9. C — Trace claims to Freedom House methodology, V-Dem indicators, constitutional rules, and election-observer reports, evaluate authority and context, corroborate material facts, and state limitations.
  10. 10. C — Define the terms, compare evidence, explain reasoning, address a serious alternative, and name a revision condition.

Constructed Response

Which institutional evidence distinguishes democracy, hybrid regimes, and authoritarian rule?

Scoring: 10 points: precise claim (2), accurate concepts (2), two specific pieces of evidence (2), reasoning (2), counterargument or limitation (1), revision condition (1).

Supports Without Lowering the Standard

  • Provide vocabulary previews and audio access.
  • Chunk complex sources while preserving essential evidence.
  • Permit speech-to-text, graphic organizers, and extended time.
  • Use additional practice and correction before reassessment.
  • Require the same final claim, evidence, reasoning, and integrity standard.
