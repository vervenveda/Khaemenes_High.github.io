Week 14 · Nigeria and India

Essential question: How do large, diverse federal democracies manage identity, development, representation, and uneven state capacity?

Teacher PortalStudent Packet

Instructional Purpose

Federalism, ethnicity, religion, language, party competition, judicial institutions, corruption, development, and regional inequality.

Evidence standard

Require claims to be traceable to Constitutions, election data, census evidence, development indicators, and court decisions. Grade accuracy, authority, evidence, reasoning, comparison, limitation, citation, and revision—not viewpoint.

Mastery Quiz Answer Key

  1. 1. A — Federal character means nigeria’s principle for broad regional and group representation in public institutions.
  2. 2. C — Resource federalism means distribution of natural-resource authority and revenue across levels of government.
  3. 3. D — Linguistic federalism means use of language and regional identity in territorial organization.
  4. 4. B — Coalition means an alliance of parties or groups cooperating to govern or pursue policy.
  5. 5. D — Informal institution means a durable unwritten rule or practice shaping behavior.
  6. 6. C — Developmental state means a state with capacity and policy commitment to direct long-term development.
  7. 7. A — Federalism can accommodate diversity while creating new conflicts over territory and resources.
  8. 8. C — Democratic scale produces both inclusion and administrative strain.
  9. 9. B — Trace claims to Constitutions, election data, census evidence, development indicators, and court decisions, evaluate authority and context, corroborate material facts, and state limitations.
  10. 10. B — Define the terms, compare evidence, explain reasoning, address a serious alternative, and name a revision condition.

Constructed Response

How do large, diverse federal democracies manage identity, development, representation, and uneven state capacity?

Scoring: 10 points: precise claim (2), accurate concepts (2), two specific pieces of evidence (2), reasoning (2), counterargument or limitation (1), revision condition (1).

Supports Without Lowering the Standard

  • Provide vocabulary previews and audio access.
  • Chunk complex sources while preserving essential evidence.
  • Permit speech-to-text, graphic organizers, and extended time.
  • Use additional practice and correction before reassessment.
  • Require the same final claim, evidence, reasoning, and integrity standard.
