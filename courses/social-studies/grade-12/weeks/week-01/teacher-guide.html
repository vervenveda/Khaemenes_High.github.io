Khaemenes High · Grade 12 Teacher Guide

Week 1 · Senior Inquiry and the Social Sciences

Essential question: How can a senior distinguish a defensible social-science conclusion from an opinion, slogan, or unsupported prediction?

Teacher PortalStudent Packet

Instructional Purpose

Comparative inquiry using political science, history, economics, geography, law, sociology, and ethical reasoning.

Evidence standard

Require claims to be traceable to United Nations Human Development data, constitutions, election records, and public-opinion evidence. Grade accuracy, authority, evidence, reasoning, comparison, limitation, citation, and revision—not viewpoint.

Daily Guidance

Monday · Concept and Inquiry Laboratory

Define the core concepts of Senior Inquiry and the Social Sciences and frame a researchable response to the essential question.

Look for: explicit concepts, source evaluation, evidence-to-claim reasoning, counterargument, and revision.

Minimum time: 50 minutes.

Tuesday · Primary and Authoritative Source Laboratory

Evaluate evidence from United Nations Human Development data, constitutions, election records, and public-opinion evidence.

Look for: explicit concepts, source evaluation, evidence-to-claim reasoning, counterargument, and revision.

Minimum time: 50 minutes.

Wednesday · Comparative Data and Case Laboratory

Compare at least two cases or data series using common concepts and transparent methods.

Look for: explicit concepts, source evaluation, evidence-to-claim reasoning, counterargument, and revision.

Minimum time: 50 minutes.

Thursday · Argument, Seminar, and Performance Studio

Develop the Senior Inquiry Diagnostic and Research Notebook Charter through evidence-based deliberation.

Look for: explicit concepts, source evaluation, evidence-to-claim reasoning, counterargument, and revision.

Minimum time: 50 minutes.

Friday · Mastery, Correction, and Capstone Checkpoint

Demonstrate weekly mastery and document correction or capstone progress.

Look for: explicit concepts, source evaluation, evidence-to-claim reasoning, counterargument, and revision.

Minimum time: 50 minutes.

Assignment Scoring Guidance

  • Accuracy and source authority: 8
  • Evidence and citation: 8
  • Reasoning and corroboration: 8
  • Limitations and clarity: 6

Mastery Quiz Answer Key

  1. 1. A — Social science means a disciplined study of human institutions, behavior, and relationships using explicit methods and evidence.
  2. 2. A — Research question means a focused, answerable question that guides evidence collection and analysis.
  3. 3. A — Claim means a conclusion or proposition that requires support.
  4. 4. B — Evidence means information used to test or support a claim.
  5. 5. D — Methodology means the organized procedures used to collect and analyze evidence.
  6. 6. B — Limitation means a condition that narrows what evidence or a study can establish.
  7. 7. D — Different disciplines ask different questions about the same public problem.
  8. 8. D — A strong claim is proportional to the quality and limits of its evidence.
  9. 9. B — Trace claims to United Nations Human Development data, constitutions, election records, and public-opinion evidence, evaluate authority and context, corroborate material facts, and state limitations.
  10. 10. A — Define the terms, compare evidence, explain reasoning, address a serious alternative, and name a revision condition.

Constructed Response

How can a senior distinguish a defensible social-science conclusion from an opinion, slogan, or unsupported prediction?

Scoring: 10 points: precise claim (2), accurate concepts (2), two specific pieces of evidence (2), reasoning (2), counterargument or limitation (1), revision condition (1).

Supports Without Lowering the Standard

  • Provide vocabulary previews and audio access.
  • Chunk complex sources while preserving essential evidence.
  • Permit speech-to-text, graphic organizers, and extended time.
  • Use additional practice and correction before reassessment.
  • Require the same final claim, evidence, reasoning, and integrity standard.
