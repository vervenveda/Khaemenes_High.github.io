Khaemenes High · Grade 12 Teacher Guide

Week 16 · Iran, Brazil, and South Africa

Essential question: How do constitutional design, identity, inequality, social movements, and political conflict shape different paths of change?

Teacher PortalStudent Packet

Instructional Purpose

Iran’s theocratic-republican institutions; Brazil’s presidential federalism and coalition politics; South Africa’s constitutional democracy and post-apartheid transformation.

Evidence standard

Require claims to be traceable to Constitutions, court decisions, election results, protest records, and inequality indicators. Grade accuracy, authority, evidence, reasoning, comparison, limitation, citation, and revision—not viewpoint.

Daily Guidance

Monday · Concept and Inquiry Laboratory

Define the core concepts of Iran, Brazil, and South Africa and frame a researchable response to the essential question.

Look for: explicit concepts, source evaluation, evidence-to-claim reasoning, counterargument, and revision.

Minimum time: 50 minutes.

Tuesday · Primary and Authoritative Source Laboratory

Evaluate evidence from Constitutions, court decisions, election results, protest records, and inequality indicators.

Look for: explicit concepts, source evaluation, evidence-to-claim reasoning, counterargument, and revision.

Minimum time: 50 minutes.

Wednesday · Comparative Data and Case Laboratory

Compare at least two cases or data series using common concepts and transparent methods.

Look for: explicit concepts, source evaluation, evidence-to-claim reasoning, counterargument, and revision.

Minimum time: 50 minutes.

Thursday · Argument, Seminar, and Performance Studio

Develop the Iran–Brazil–South Africa Political Change Seminar through evidence-based deliberation.

Look for: explicit concepts, source evaluation, evidence-to-claim reasoning, counterargument, and revision.

Minimum time: 50 minutes.

Friday · Mastery, Correction, and Capstone Checkpoint

Demonstrate weekly mastery and document correction or capstone progress.

Look for: explicit concepts, source evaluation, evidence-to-claim reasoning, counterargument, and revision.

Minimum time: 50 minutes.

Mastery Quiz Answer Key

  1. 1. D — Theocracy means a system in which religious authority or doctrine has a governing role.
  2. 2. A — Guardian institution means an unelected body empowered to supervise or constrain elected institutions.
  3. 3. A — Coalition presidentialism means presidential governance dependent on multiparty legislative coalitions.
  4. 4. D — Transformative constitution means a constitution designed to restructure institutions and remedy entrenched injustice.
  5. 5. D — Racialized inequality means unequal conditions structured by historical and institutional racial classification.
  6. 6. C — Constitutional court means a court with special responsibility for constitutional interpretation.
  7. 7. A — Hybrid institutional design can combine elected and unelected authority.
  8. 8. A — Constitutional transformation does not by itself eliminate social and economic inequality.
  9. 9. C — Trace claims to Constitutions, court decisions, election results, protest records, and inequality indicators, evaluate authority and context, corroborate material facts, and state limitations.
  10. 10. C — Define the terms, compare evidence, explain reasoning, address a serious alternative, and name a revision condition.

Constructed Response

How do constitutional design, identity, inequality, social movements, and political conflict shape different paths of change?

Scoring: 10 points: precise claim (2), accurate concepts (2), two specific pieces of evidence (2), reasoning (2), counterargument or limitation (1), revision condition (1).

Supports Without Lowering the Standard

  • Provide vocabulary previews and audio access.
  • Chunk complex sources while preserving essential evidence.
  • Permit speech-to-text, graphic organizers, and extended time.
  • Use additional practice and correction before reassessment.
  • Require the same final claim, evidence, reasoning, and integrity standard.
