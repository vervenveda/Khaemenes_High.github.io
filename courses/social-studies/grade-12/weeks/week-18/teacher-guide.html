Khaemenes High · Grade 12 Teacher Guide

Week 18 · Semester Examination and Capstone Proposal Defense

Essential question: Can the student integrate comparative concepts, institutional evidence, country knowledge, and a defensible research plan?

Teacher PortalStudent Packet

Instructional Purpose

Cumulative comparative politics examination, proposal, preliminary bibliography, method, ethics, feasibility, and oral defense.

Evidence standard

Require claims to be traceable to Semester evidence portfolio and capstone checkpoint record. Grade accuracy, authority, evidence, reasoning, comparison, limitation, citation, and revision—not viewpoint.

Daily Guidance

Monday · Concept and Inquiry Laboratory

Define the core concepts of Semester Examination and Capstone Proposal Defense and frame a researchable response to the essential question.

Look for: explicit concepts, source evaluation, evidence-to-claim reasoning, counterargument, and revision.

Minimum time: 50 minutes.

Tuesday · Primary and Authoritative Source Laboratory

Evaluate evidence from Semester evidence portfolio and capstone checkpoint record.

Look for: explicit concepts, source evaluation, evidence-to-claim reasoning, counterargument, and revision.

Minimum time: 50 minutes.

Wednesday · Comparative Data and Case Laboratory

Compare at least two cases or data series using common concepts and transparent methods.

Look for: explicit concepts, source evaluation, evidence-to-claim reasoning, counterargument, and revision.

Minimum time: 50 minutes.

Thursday · Argument, Seminar, and Performance Studio

Develop the Semester Examination and Capstone Proposal Defense through evidence-based deliberation.

Look for: explicit concepts, source evaluation, evidence-to-claim reasoning, counterargument, and revision.

Minimum time: 50 minutes.

Friday · Mastery, Correction, and Capstone Checkpoint

Demonstrate weekly mastery and document correction or capstone progress.

Look for: explicit concepts, source evaluation, evidence-to-claim reasoning, counterargument, and revision.

Minimum time: 50 minutes.

Required Capstone Checkpoint · Formal Proposal Defense

Assignment Scoring Guidance

  • Accuracy and source authority: 8
  • Evidence and citation: 8
  • Reasoning and corroboration: 8
  • Limitations and clarity: 6

Do not accept uncited claims, fabricated evidence, or a response that substitutes political identity for analysis. Return incomplete work for correction.

Mastery Quiz Answer Key

  1. 1. B — Proposal means a structured plan explaining a research problem, question, method, evidence, schedule, and significance.
  2. 2. D — Feasibility means whether a project can be completed ethically with available time, evidence, and skills.
  3. 3. A — Scope means the boundaries of a study, including cases, period, concepts, and evidence.
  4. 4. A — Research ethics means standards protecting integrity, people, communities, and the accuracy of knowledge.
  5. 5. B — Oral defense means a structured presentation and questioning process used to justify a project or conclusion.
  6. 6. B — Revision condition means evidence or circumstances that would require changing a claim or plan.
  7. 7. B — A proposal is evaluated before results are known.
  8. 8. D — Feasibility and ethics are part of research quality.
  9. 9. C — Trace claims to Semester evidence portfolio and capstone checkpoint record, evaluate authority and context, corroborate material facts, and state limitations.
  10. 10. A — Define the terms, compare evidence, explain reasoning, address a serious alternative, and name a revision condition.

Constructed Response

Can the student integrate comparative concepts, institutional evidence, country knowledge, and a defensible research plan?

Scoring: 10 points: precise claim (2), accurate concepts (2), two specific pieces of evidence (2), reasoning (2), counterargument or limitation (1), revision condition (1).

Supports Without Lowering the Standard

  • Provide vocabulary previews and audio access.
  • Chunk complex sources while preserving essential evidence.
  • Permit speech-to-text, graphic organizers, and extended time.
  • Use additional practice and correction before reassessment.
  • Require the same final claim, evidence, reasoning, and integrity standard.
