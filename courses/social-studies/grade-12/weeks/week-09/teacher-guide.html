Khaemenes High · Grade 12 Teacher Guide

Week 9 · Parties and Electoral Systems

Essential question: How do electoral rules shape parties, representation, coalition building, and accountability?

Teacher PortalStudent Packet

Instructional Purpose

Plurality, majority, proportional, mixed systems, district magnitude, thresholds, party systems, candidate selection, and malapportionment.

Evidence standard

Require claims to be traceable to Election laws and results from the UK, Mexico, Nigeria, India, Germany, and South Africa. Grade accuracy, authority, evidence, reasoning, comparison, limitation, citation, and revision—not viewpoint.

Mastery Quiz Answer Key

  1. 1. B — Electoral system means rules converting votes into offices or seats.
  2. 2. A — Plurality means a rule in which the candidate with the most votes wins, even without a majority.
  3. 3. A — Proportional representation means allocation of seats in approximate proportion to party vote shares.
  4. 4. D — District magnitude means the number of representatives elected from a district.
  5. 5. A — Party system means the pattern of parties that compete and influence government.
  6. 6. A — Threshold means a minimum share of votes required to qualify for representation.
  7. 7. B — Electoral rules create incentives but do not mechanically determine outcomes.
  8. 8. D — Representation, governability, accountability, and inclusion can conflict.
  9. 9. A — Trace claims to Election laws and results from the UK, Mexico, Nigeria, India, Germany, and South Africa, evaluate authority and context, corroborate material facts, and state limitations.
  10. 10. B — Define the terms, compare evidence, explain reasoning, address a serious alternative, and name a revision condition.

Constructed Response

How do electoral rules shape parties, representation, coalition building, and accountability?

Scoring: 10 points: precise claim (2), accurate concepts (2), two specific pieces of evidence (2), reasoning (2), counterargument or limitation (1), revision condition (1).

Supports Without Lowering the Standard

  • Provide vocabulary previews and audio access.
  • Chunk complex sources while preserving essential evidence.
  • Permit speech-to-text, graphic organizers, and extended time.
  • Use additional practice and correction before reassessment.
  • Require the same final claim, evidence, reasoning, and integrity standard.
