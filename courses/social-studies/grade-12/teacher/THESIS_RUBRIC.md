# Senior Thesis Rubric — 100 Points

- Research question, significance, and scope — 10
- Literature/evidence review — 15
- Ethical methodology and transparency — 15
- Evidence analysis and findings — 20
- Reasoning, synthesis, and competing explanations — 15
- Limitations and revision conditions — 10
- Organization, citation, writing, and disclosure — 15

Minimum graduation score: 80.
