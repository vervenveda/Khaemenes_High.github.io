Academic integrity: Submit only work you can explain and defend. Every material source and automated assistance must be disclosed. Fabricated evidence, falsified citations, plagiarism, or undisclosed generated work invalidates this assessment until corrected.

Purpose

Submit a 4,000–5,000-word evidence-based paper with ethical methodology, findings, limitations, citations, and implications.

Required components

  • 4,000–5,000 words
  • Approved question and ethical method
  • Literature review
  • Transparent evidence analysis
  • Findings and limitations
  • Counterargument
  • Standard citations
  • AI-assistance disclosure
  • Executive summary

Common rubric

  • Disciplinary accuracy and concepts — 20%
  • Source authority and evidence — 20%
  • Analysis, method, and reasoning — 25%
  • Comparison, counterargument, and limitations — 15%
  • Communication, citation, and integrity — 20%
