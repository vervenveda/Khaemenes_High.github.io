# Grade 10 Social Studies · Unit 1 Deepening Standard
**Date:** 2026-11-16  
**Course:** United States History Honors  
**Canonical source:** `course-data.js`

## Completed
Weeks 1–4 were rebuilt in place without changing chronology, mastery, assignments, or assessment counts.

### Unified daily contract
1. **Inquiry & Context** — establish chronology, geography, and the compelling question.
2. **Primary Evidence** — source, contextualize, corroborate, and qualify claims.
3. **Map & Data** — interpret spatial and quantitative evidence and state limitations.
4. **Argument & Deliberation** — construct and revise an evidence-based historical argument.
5. **Mastery & Correction** — transfer, assess, explain errors, and revise the essential-question response.

The workflow label stays predictable; the visible lesson title is content-specific.

## Content-depth target for Weeks 5–36
Each week should contain:
- approximately 400–650 words of original core reading across four purposeful sections;
- explicit primary/authoritative evidence work;
- one geographic or quantitative reasoning task;
- one historical argument with a counterclaim or competing interpretation;
- one transfer-oriented Friday mastery cycle with correction reasoning;
- support and honors extension retained;
- three principal assignments retained;
- 80% mastery retained;
- content-specific daily titles while preserving the five-day workflow.

## Non-negotiable guardrails
- Do not rewrite the 36-week chronology unless separately approved.
- Do not replace historical evidence with simulations when primary/authoritative evidence is available.
- Do not treat maps or data as proof of causation by themselves.
- Do not collapse aspiration, legal text, policy, and lived outcome into the same category.
- Keep correction-based retakes and the Academy 80% mastery standard.
