Week 26 Teacher Guide

Vietnam: Containment, Escalation, and Protest

Teacher Portal
Essential Question: Why did the United States escalate in Vietnam, and why did public support collapse?

Standards: SS.912.A.7; History of Communism standards · D2.His.1.9-12 · D3.1.9-12 · D4.1.9-12 · CCSS.ELA-LITERACY.RH.9-10.1 · CCSS.ELA-LITERACY.RH.9-10.7 · CCSS.ELA-LITERACY.WHST.9-10.1

Instructional Emphasis

  • Vietnam emerged from colonialism, nationalism, civil conflict, Cold War ideology, and great-power intervention.
  • Presidents escalated incrementally through assumptions about credibility and containment.
  • The draft, casualties, media, official secrecy, protest, and battlefield developments transformed public debate.

Differentiation: Use vocabulary preview, chunked texts, oral processing, accessible maps, extended time, and alternative response modes. Honors students should source independently, write a stronger counterclaim, and compare a second interpretation.

Assignment Guidance

Assignment 1 · Source Lab — Escalation and the Credibility Gap

Teacher note: Award evidence credit for specific details and source reasoning, not summary alone.

Exemplar characteristics: A full-credit response identifies the origin and purpose of the sources, uses at least two details, and explains how the evidence bears on: Why did the United States escalate in Vietnam, and why did public support collapse? It also states one limitation.

Evidence: Uses accurate, relevant evidence and identifies its source.

Reasoning: Explains how evidence supports the claim and addresses complexity or limitation.

Accuracy: Uses historical, geographic, economic, and civic concepts correctly.

Communication: Organizes ideas clearly with appropriate academic language.

Assignment 2 · Atlas and Data Investigation

Teacher note: Check labels, legend, scale or units, and whether the student avoids claiming that correlation alone proves causation.

Exemplar characteristics: A full-credit product uses accurate labels and units, identifies a meaningful pattern, proposes an evidence-based explanation, and names what the visualization cannot prove.

Evidence: Uses accurate, relevant evidence and identifies its source.

Assignment 3 · Evidence-Based Seminar or Argument

Teacher note: Score evidence and reasoning rather than the political, moral, or policy position selected.

Exemplar characteristics: Reasonable conclusions may differ. Full credit directly answers “Was U.S. failure caused primarily by strategy, political assumptions, domestic limits, local legitimacy, or an unattainable objective?,” uses accurate evidence, explains reasoning, and addresses a substantial counterargument.

Constructed Response

Using at least two specific pieces of evidence, answer: Why did the United States escalate in Vietnam, and why did public support collapse?

A full-credit response states a defensible claim, uses two accurate details, explains how each supports the claim, and acknowledges one complexity or limitation.
