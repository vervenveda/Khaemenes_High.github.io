Week 32 Teacher Guide

The Financial Crisis, Recovery, and Political Realignment

Teacher Portal
Essential Question: Why did financial risk become a national crisis, and why did recovery produce continuing political anger?

Standards: SS.912.A.7; Florida history · D2.His.1.9-12 · D3.1.9-12 · D4.1.9-12 · CCSS.ELA-LITERACY.RH.9-10.1 · CCSS.ELA-LITERACY.RH.9-10.7 · CCSS.ELA-LITERACY.WHST.9-10.1

Instructional Emphasis

  • Housing, leverage, complex securities, weak risk controls, and interconnected finance contributed to the crisis.
  • Emergency interventions stabilized major institutions but created disputes about fairness, accountability, and moral hazard.
  • Recovery reduced unemployment over time while wealth loss and regional inequality shaped political reaction.

Differentiation: Use vocabulary preview, chunked texts, oral processing, accessible maps, extended time, and alternative response modes. Honors students should source independently, write a stronger counterclaim, and compare a second interpretation.

Assignment Guidance

Assignment 1 · Source Lab — From Mortgage to Systemic Crisis

Teacher note: Award evidence credit for specific details and source reasoning, not summary alone.

Exemplar characteristics: A full-credit response identifies the origin and purpose of the sources, uses at least two details, and explains how the evidence bears on: Why did financial risk become a national crisis, and why did recovery produce continuing political anger? It also states one limitation.

Evidence: Uses accurate, relevant evidence and identifies its source.

Reasoning: Explains how evidence supports the claim and addresses complexity or limitation.

Accuracy: Uses historical, geographic, economic, and civic concepts correctly.

Constructed Response

Using at least two specific pieces of evidence, answer: Why did financial risk become a national crisis, and why did recovery produce continuing political anger?

A full-credit response states a defensible claim, uses two accurate details, explains how each supports the claim, and acknowledges one complexity or limitation.
