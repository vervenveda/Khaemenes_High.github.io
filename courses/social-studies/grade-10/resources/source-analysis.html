Primary and Secondary Source Analysis

Use precise evidence rather than assumptions.

Learning Tools

Student: __________________________ Date: __________

Source identification: creator, date, place, source type, and title

Historical context: what conditions or events shaped this source?

Audience and purpose: whom was it meant to reach and what was it meant to do?

Claims and evidence: identify three specific details

Value: what can this source help establish?

Limitation: what can it not establish alone?

Corroboration: what independent evidence would confirm, complicate, or contradict it?
