  1. Prompt 1: Using evidence from at least three eras, explain how the meaning of American citizenship changed from the founding through Reconstruction.
    A full-credit response presents a defensible thesis, uses specific accurate evidence, explains reasoning, addresses complexity or counterargument, and is historically precise.
  2. Prompt 2: Evaluate the extent to which federal power expanded in response to industrialization and the Great Depression. Include a counterargument.
