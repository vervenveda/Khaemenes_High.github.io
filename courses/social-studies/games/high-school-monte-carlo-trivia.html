How the Monte Carlo game works

The game loads read-only educational question banks from the Academy's weekly Social Studies curricula. It randomly samples original course questions, shuffles answer positions, balances unlocked grade levels, and shows explanations after each response.

Private practice only. This is a supplemental, ungraded Arcade experience. It never writes to Academy enrollment, lesson progression, quizzes, grades, or academic portfolios. Scores remain in memory unless you deliberately export the result. No advertising, tracking, third-party scripts, or external network services.

Offline: For the entire 144-week bank to function offline, the accompanying course quiz files must be saved with this page; the game reports missing files rather than inventing questions.
