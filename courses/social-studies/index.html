About

Students progress from foundational global inquiry to United States history, constitutional government and economics, then senior comparative politics and independent research. Across all four years, claims must be supported by identifiable evidence, chronology, geography, corroboration, and appropriately qualified reasoning.

Course Features

Grade 9 · Global Studies

Foundations in world history, geography, economics, historical inquiry, source verification, systems thinking, and civic transfer.

Grade 10 · United States History

United States history through primary sources, chronology, constitutional development, civic literacy, geography, and historical argument.

Grade 11 · Government, Law & Economics

Constitutional government, law, economics, public policy, media verification, civic reasoning, and personal financial literacy.

Grade 12 · Global Politics & Research

Comparative politics, international relations, international law, global challenges, ethical research, senior thesis, and oral defense.
