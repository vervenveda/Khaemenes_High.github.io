Reasoning safeguard: The model can test a mechanism under stated assumptions. It cannot decide whether capitalism, socialism, regulation, unions, public ownership, or another institution is historically or morally superior. Those conclusions require definitions, distributional evidence, institutional context, and argument.

Five-Run Investigation

  1. Define the institution or policy claim you are examining.
  2. Predict the direction of price and quantity before moving a slider.
  3. Change one variable and record the output.
  4. Ask who could gain, lose, or remain excluded in the historical setting.
  5. Identify one factor the model omits.
  6. Corroborate or challenge the mechanism using Week 27 historical evidence.

Historical Corroboration

Choose one Week 27 claim about markets, labor, reform, ownership, regulation, or social welfare. Explain whether the model supports a narrow mechanism, complicates the claim, or cannot test it. Distinguish model evidence from historical evidence.
