Nonpartisan Civic Action Capstone

Select a public problem that can be investigated with evidence and addressed through lawful, respectful civic participation.

  1. Compelling question: Define the problem precisely.
  2. Historical context: Explain how the issue developed.
  3. Geography: Map who and what is affected.
  4. Authority: Identify the government agency, institution, or decision-maker with jurisdiction.
  5. Stakeholders: Include directly affected groups and competing interests.
  6. Evidence: Use public data, primary records, credible research, and transparent interviews.
  7. Options: Compare at least three approaches, including costs, benefits, risks, and unintended consequences.
  8. Recommendation: Choose a feasible option and define measures of success.
  9. Action: Present, write, attend, comment, volunteer, petition, or communicate through a lawful channel.
  10. Reflection: Explain what changed in your understanding.
