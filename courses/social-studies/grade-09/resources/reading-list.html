Curriculum Reading Library

A curated companion library synchronized to the evidence-first course. Readings are assigned as core, source-lab, extension, or teacher/background resources rather than as unstructured links.

Return to Course

Reading Protocol

For every assigned reading record: author/institution, date or historical period, genre, intended audience, central claim, two pieces of evidence, one limitation, one corroboration question, and one connection to the week's essential question. A linked source is evidence to evaluate—not an authority to copy.

Access: The core spine prioritizes free/open resources. Teachers should assign excerpts rather than entire difficult primary texts when appropriate for Grade 9.
