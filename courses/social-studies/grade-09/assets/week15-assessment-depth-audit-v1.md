# Week 15 Assessment Depth Audit v1

## Status
REPAIRED — legacy student assessment replaced with canonical curriculum-aligned assessment.

## Finding
The prior `student-packet.html` contained several inherited, out-of-scope questions from other weeks (including Indigenous Americas, Mongol expansion, Black Death, and earlier course themes) instead of measuring the Week 15 Networks of Exchange curriculum. This was a genuine assessment-depth and synchronization defect.

## Repair
The student packet now uses the canonical `W15Q1–W15Q10` questions from `quiz.json`, preserving the 80% mastery gate and teacher-scored constructed response.

## Depth alignment
The repaired assessment directly measures:
- network rather than single-route reasoning;
- archaeological/material evidence and limits of imported artifacts;
- commodity biography and evidence/inference separation;
- merchant diasporas, credit, caravanserais, ports, navigation, and risk;
- state facilitation, regulation, protection, and extraction;
- diffusion versus local adoption/adaptation/rejection;
- uneven network effects;
- map limitations and corroboration;
- traveler-source perspective and provenance;
- cross-system synthesis beyond merchants alone.

## Mastery invariant
80% objective mastery. Below 8/10 requires concept review, reasoning corrections, and retake. Constructed response remains teacher-scored separately.

## Teacher guide
The existing Week 15 teacher guide already contains the matching canonical key, depth criteria, research studio guidance, and correction protocol. No teacher-key rewrite was necessary after the student packet repair.

## Result
Week 15 assessment now measures the actual depth of the Week 15 curriculum rather than inherited generic content. Close Week 15 after post-write verification.
