## Historical-integrity findings
- The Achaemenid Empire is not reduced to “tolerant” or “tyrannical”; local accommodation is presented alongside imperial hierarchy, taxation/tribute, loyalty demands, and coercive power.
- Athens is not treated as representative of all Greek poleis.
- Athenian democracy is explicitly bounded by a restricted male citizen body; women, enslaved people, and resident foreigners are not silently included as citizens.
- Sparta and the exploited helot population prevent a simplistic Greek political model.
- Greek narratives and Persian royal inscriptions are treated as situated evidence requiring corroboration rather than as either unquestionable truth or useless propaganda.
- The “Greek freedom versus Persian tyranny” binary is explicitly qualified.
- Political ideals are distinguished from social and institutional practice.
