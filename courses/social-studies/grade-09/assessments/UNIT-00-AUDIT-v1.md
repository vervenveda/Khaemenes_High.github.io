# Unit 00 Forensic Audit Contract

## Canonical thresholds

- **Mastery:** 80%.
- **Readiness overall:** 80%.
- **Readiness essential-strand minimum:** 80% in every strand.
- **Honors reporting target:** 85%.
- **Official core:** 36 weeks.
- **Supported pathway:** six Unit 0 prep lessons + 36 official weeks = 42 instructional weeks.

## Unit 0 separation rules

Unit 0 is a readiness/refresh layer. Its assessments:

- do not count as official weekly quizzes;
- do not count as official course weeks;
- do not enter the official course grade;
- do not erase or replace Course Mastery;
- remain available as NAIB Readiness and Refresh evidence.

## Advancement

A learner advances from the readiness gateway only when the learner has **80% overall and 80% in every essential strand**. Otherwise the learner receives targeted Unit 0 support and reasoning corrections.

Each Unit 0 lesson requires 80% mastery before the next lesson unlocks. A below-mastery attempt requires a reasoning correction before another attempt.

## Historical-integrity invariants

1. Evidence is distinguished from inference.
2. Correlation is not treated as causation.
3. Model/simulation output is never presented as historical evidence.
4. Historical uncertainty is preserved when evidence is incomplete or disputed.
5. Sources are evaluated by provenance, purpose, audience, context, access, and limitations.
6. Comparison does not rank cultures or societies as inherently superior/inferior.
7. Historical actors retain agency, including ordinary people, marginalized communities, collaborators, reformers, and resistance movements where evidence supports their inclusion.
8. Colonialism, slavery, exploitation, conquest, resistance, and other difficult subjects are not sanitized when supported by the record.
9. Competing interpretations are distinguished from unsupported claims.
10. Later curriculum edits must not silently weaken an evidence-supported historical claim.
