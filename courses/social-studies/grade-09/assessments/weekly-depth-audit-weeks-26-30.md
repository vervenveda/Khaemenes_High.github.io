## Audit principle

Do not confuse:

- 10 questions with rigor;
- vocabulary recognition with mastery;
- 80% with assessment validity;
- a primary source with automatic truth;
- a map association with causation;
- model output with historical proof; or
- a polished curriculum with an adequately aligned assessment.

The course should preserve these distinctions throughout all remaining weeks.
