# Grade 9 Global Studies Honors — Forensic Curriculum Audit

**Date:** 2026-08-16  
**Branch:** `hardening/archaemenes-highschool`  
**Status:** ACTIVE REPAIR — not yet A+++ certified

## Executive finding

The Grade 9 Global Studies Honors package is structurally large and ambitious, but its original generated student portal did not meet the Academy's forensic A+++ standard.

The structural counts are real — 36 weeks, 180 Monday–Friday lesson slots, 108 principal assignments, weekly quizzes, a midterm, a final, and a civic capstone — but forensic inspection found that many of those slots were generated from a small set of repeated substantive templates.

A structural count therefore must not be treated as proof of curricular uniqueness.

## Confirmed original defects

1. The generated portal used a 70% passing target instead of the Academy's 80% mastery threshold.
2. The same five substantive Monday–Friday lesson shells recurred with topic substitution.
3. The same three assignment structures recurred across most weeks.
4. Small shared objective banks were reused across many weeks.
5. Midterm/final items reused questions from weekly banks.
6. Correct-answer index 0 was heavily overrepresented.
7. The generated portal created its own local students instead of using Academy learner authority.

## Repairs now staged on the hardening branch

- Academy learner continuity and learner-scoped records.
- Grade 08 preview isolation.
- 80% formal progression.
- Monday → Friday prerequisite sequence.
- Three weekly evidence assignments required before formal mastery check.
- Next week unlocked only after prior week reaches 80%.
- Midterm after first-semester mastery; Semester II requires an 80% midterm.
- Final after full-year mastery and required prior exam gate.
- Friendly lock explanations and reassessment.
- Thirty-six topic-specific inquiry upgrades covering historical methods, source analysis, map/data work, argumentation, and synthesis.
- Week-specific Verve N Veda resources with explicit learning purposes.
- **A new week-specific objective bank now supplies five dedicated objective items for each of the 36 weeks (180 weekly items total).**
- **The midterm is now an independent 18-item cumulative transfer assessment rather than copied weekly-bank questions.**
- **The final is now an independent 24-item cumulative transfer assessment rather than copied weekly-bank questions.**
- **A deterministic answer-position balancing layer rotates four-choice items across answer positions, removing the original index-0 pattern.**

- Renaissance/printing: image-text comparison and diffusion evidence.

- Holocaust/genocide study: careful chronology, institutional evidence, survivor-source protocols, dignity, agency, and responsibility without collective-guilt shortcuts.

- Globalization: trade/development/migration data with limitations.
- Comparative government/media literacy: lateral reading, source triangulation, institutional comparison.
- Civic capstone: nonpartisan evidence file, stakeholder map, options matrix, public product, reflection.
