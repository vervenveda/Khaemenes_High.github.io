Historical Integrity

This course does not treat historical comfort as a criterion for historical truth. Students examine colonialism, slavery, imperialism, displacement, resistance, racism, nationalism, war, state violence, contested political histories, and human rights through identifiable evidence and context.

Students are expected to distinguish evidence from interpretation, ideals from implementation, correlation from causation, and similarity from equivalence. A source's perspective is analyzed—not automatically accepted or discarded.
