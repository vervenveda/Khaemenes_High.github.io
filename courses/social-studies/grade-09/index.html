About Global Studies Honors

Global Studies Honors examines world history from early human societies through the modern world while integrating geography, economics, comparative government, human rights, media literacy, and historical systems. Students learn to distinguish evidence from interpretation, investigate claims through context and corroboration, and build responsible arguments from identifiable sources.

Course Features

World History & Geography Students investigate societies, regions, migration, exchange, conflict, continuity, and change while using geography and chronology to understand historical development.
Government, Economics & Human Rights Comparative government, economic systems, institutions, rights, power, public policy, and civic questions connect historical study to the modern world.
Evidence & Media Analysis Students examine provenance, perspective, corroboration, source quality, uncertainty, visual evidence, media claims, and the difference between interpretation and proof.
Mastery & Civic Capstone The 36-week core includes readiness support, 80% mastery expectations, written revision, systems thinking, historical inquiry, portfolio evidence, and a culminating civic capstone.
