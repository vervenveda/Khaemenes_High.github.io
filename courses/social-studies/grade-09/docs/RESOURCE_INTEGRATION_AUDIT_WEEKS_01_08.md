# Grade 9 Global Studies Honors — Resource Integration Audit, Weeks 01–08

Status: live resource bridge deployed for Weeks 1–8

## Institutional boundary

Khaemenes Social Studies remains the instructional, mastery, and grading authority. External ecosystem resources are approved research/evidence/production environments, not automatic credit generators.

- ARSHIF: historical research, archives, source context, oral traditions, philosophy, travelogues, and humanities.
- ProReSources: evidence organization, citation, atlas/data analysis, writing/editing, research navigation, and production.
- One Nation for All: approved educational civic-transfer tools only. Campaign/candidate pages are excluded from required curriculum.
- Student work must represent the student’s own reasoning. Opening or completing a tool does not establish mastery.

## ProTool forensic findings

### Evidence & Citation Studio

High-value capabilities for Social Studies include research-question/project briefs; claim tracking; source metadata; primary/secondary/tertiary classification; supporting/opposing/contextual/insufficient evidence relationships; short quotation records with exact locations; reliability notes; SHA-256 file provenance; research timelines; draft APA 7, MLA 9, Chicago, and plain source records; PROSE-ready Markdown; ARSHIF provenance manifests; evidence-matrix CSV exports; HTML reports; local backups; and optional encrypted local storage.

Generated citations remain drafting aids and require manual review for unusual source types, laws, archives, datasets, translated works, audiovisual materials, and incomplete publication histories.

### Atlas Evidence Analysis

Best used for map/data claims, evidence-status tracking, source intake, claims/timelines, provenance, competing interpretations, and report generation. It organizes evidence and uncertainty; it does not decide truth, guilt, deception, plagiarism, or causation for the student.

### PROSE Editorial Suite

Canonical course route used by the bridge: `Protools/PROSE/editorial-gateway.html`.

PROSE is used as the production studio for drafting, structuring, formatting, revision, outline development, evidence-rich essays/reports, preservation, and export. It supports student writing but does not supply the historical thesis or become the evaluator.

Note: the current ProReSources mentor manifest still references an older `Protools/PROSE_index.html` path. Grade 9 uses current filesystem truth rather than the stale manifest route.

## Week-by-week embellishment

### Week 1 — How We Know the Past
- ARSHIF: contrasting evidence forms and archive/source limits.
- Evidence & Citation Studio: provenance/evidence ledger.
- PROSE: 350–500 word Historical Methods Brief on reliable knowledge from incomplete evidence.

### Week 2 — Geography and Human Systems
- ARSHIF Travelogues & Exploration: traveler perspective and geographic corroboration.
- Atlas Evidence Analysis: projection/spatial evidence case with pattern, distortion, explanation, and limitation.
- PROSE: 250–400 word Geographic Interpretation Brief.

### Week 3 — Early Humanity and Agricultural Change
- ARSHIF Indigenous Oral Traditions & Storywork: public resource review with community-sovereignty, consent, and archival-ethics boundaries.
- Evidence & Citation Studio: archaeology/environment/demography/oral-traditional evidence matrix.
- PROSE: 450–650 word Agricultural Transition Essay with counterargument and evidence limitation.

### Week 4 — Rivers, Cities, Law, and Government
- ARSHIF Bible & History Parallels: historical/archaeological/textual genre comparison, not devotional instruction.
- Evidence & Citation Studio: legal ideal vs administrative practice vs inference vs unresolved claim ledger.
- PROSE: 500–700 word Early Law Analytical Report.

### Week 5 — Early Africa, South Asia, East Asia, and the Americas
- ARSHIF Palimpsest Vault: archive survival, lost works, fragments, preservation, and silence.
- Evidence & Citation Studio: four-region evidence catalog with confidence and missing-evidence fields.
- PROSE: 550–750 word Comparative Historiography Report.

### Week 6 — Belief, Ethics, and Philosophy
- ARSHIF Philosophy Hall: contextualized ethical/philosophical materials.
- Evidence & Citation Studio: three-tradition comparison ledger.
- PROSE: 600–800 word Comparative Ethics Essay; academic rather than devotional judgment.

### Week 7 — Persia and the Greek World
- ARSHIF World Classics & Humanities: situated classical narrative/political evidence.
- Evidence & Citation Studio: Greek/Persian corroboration ledger with agreement, tension, silence, and unresolved claims.
- PROSE: 650–900 word Persia–Greece comparative essay on the usefulness and limits of the freedom-versus-tyranny framework.
- One Nation High School Voter Portal: optional civic transfer only; compare ancient political membership to modern civic processes while explicitly rejecting false equivalence.

### Week 8 — Rome and Han China
- Atlas Evidence Analysis: parallel Rome/Han infrastructure/frontier/administrative-reach case.
- Evidence & Citation Studio: Roman/Han central-policy and local-response source ledger.
- PROSE: 700–1000 word comparative research report with thesis, administration, infrastructure, status/membership, interacting causes of fragmentation, continuity, and conclusion.

## Required resource-card anatomy

Every approved course resource card must state:

1. Resource name and role.
2. Specific student task.
3. Required evidence/product.
4. Intellectual or ethical limitation.
5. Whether the task is required or optional.
6. A direct approved route rather than a broad campaign or ecosystem landing page when a safer educational route exists.

## Validation requirements

- Resource bridge appears only in weekly lesson view, not the dashboard.
- One bridge instance per selected week.
- Weeks 1–8 have deliberate mappings; no random links.
- Campaign/candidate pages are absent from required resource plans.
