## Non-negotiable course standards

- 36 instructional weeks / 180 daily lessons.
- Every daily lesson must contain genuinely topic-specific instructional substance, not only a renamed procedural template.
- Recurring Monday–Friday learning routines may remain, but each day must have unique historical evidence, readings, maps/data, questions, and intellectual work.
- Weekly quizzes require a minimum 80% mastery score before the week is considered mastered.
- Retakes and corrections are permitted; best objective score may be preserved.
- Assignment completion and assignment academic score must remain separate concepts.
- Published grading weights and runtime grade calculations must match exactly.
- Written historical/civic work must be scored for evidence, accuracy, reasoning, and communication, not political agreement.
- Sensitive historical topics must remain precise, contextualized, and non-spectacularized.
- Religions and philosophies are studied academically and comparatively without devotional requirements or ranking.
- Mentor/tool integrations may guide learning but may not silently replace the curriculum, assessment engine, or evaluator.
- Student-facing files must contain no secrets, credentials, private topology, or sensitive student data.
- Relative links, printable packets, teacher guides, assessment files, and offline/PWA assets must resolve in production.
