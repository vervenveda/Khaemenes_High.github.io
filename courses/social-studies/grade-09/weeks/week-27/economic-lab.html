Protocol

  1. Define the institution or market mechanism.
  2. Predict before changing a variable.
  3. Change one variable and hold the other steady.
  4. Record the modeled price and quantity.
  5. Ask who might gain, lose, or bear a cost.
  6. Name a limitation and corroborate with historical evidence.

Local supply-and-demand model
