Economic Reasoning Lab

Use a simplified market model to investigate claims about institutions, incentives, distribution, and tradeoffs. The model is evidence for model behavior—not proof of a historical outcome.

Protocol

  1. Define: identify the institution or market mechanism being studied.
  2. Predict: state what you expect and why.
  3. Change one variable: keep other settings stable.
  4. Record: capture the observed equilibrium.
  5. Distribution: ask who might gain, lose, or bear a cost.
  6. Limit: identify what the simplified model leaves out.
  7. Corroborate: compare the model with historical evidence.
