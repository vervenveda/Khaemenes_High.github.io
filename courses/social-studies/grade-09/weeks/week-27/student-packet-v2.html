Weekly Reading

Economic Systems Are Not Single Boxes

Adam Smith described how specialization and exchange could increase productivity while also discussing monopoly and repetitive labor. Later advocates of laissez-faire emphasized private property and limited regulation. Industrial crises and inequality encouraged socialist, cooperative, and labor alternatives.

People, Power, and Reform

Karl Marx and Friedrich Engels argued that capitalist ownership produced class conflict and exploitation. Other socialists favored democratic reform, cooperatives, or municipal ownership. Labor unions pursued shorter hours, safer workplaces, wages, and political rights.

Reading Economic Claims

Separate description from prediction and moral judgment. A source can explain a mechanism, predict an outcome, propose an ideal, or defend the interests of a group.

Vocabulary

CapitalismEconomic organization based largely on private ownership, markets, wage labor, and investment.
Laissez-faireA view favoring minimal government interference in economic exchange.
SocialismA family of ideas favoring social/public control of major productive resources and greater economic equality.
MarxismA theory emphasizing class conflict, historical change, and collective ownership of production.
Collective bargainingNegotiation between workers acting together and employers.
Social insurancePrograms that spread risks such as illness, unemployment, disability, or old age.

Five-Day Investigation

Monday · Frame the Inquiry

Build a c. 1776–1914 chronology. Mark important developments in economic thought, industrial labor, union organization, and reform. Write an initial answer to the Essential Question and two questions you need evidence to answer.

Tuesday · Source Laboratory

Compare Adam Smith, Marx and Engels, a cooperative reformer, and a labor petition. For each, record origin, audience, purpose, problem, proposed mechanism, useful evidence, and limitation.

Wednesday · Economic Reasoning Lab

Open the Economic Lab. Run five controlled Supply & Demand scenarios. For each run record your prediction, the one variable changed, result, distributional effect, and model limitation.

Thursday · Corroborate and Argue

Compare your simulation results with historical evidence. Ask: What does the model illuminate? What does it leave out? Build a claim using at least three pieces of historical evidence and one serious counterclaim or qualification.

Friday · Demonstrate and Correct

Complete Assessment v2.0. The mastery threshold is 80%. If you do not reach mastery, complete the reasoning correction process and retry.
