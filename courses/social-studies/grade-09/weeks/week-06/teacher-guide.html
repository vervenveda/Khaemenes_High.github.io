Assignment Guidance

1. Source Lab

Accept multiple interpretations when claims are supported. Full evidence credit requires analysis rather than summary and identifies at least one limitation and corroboration need.

2. Atlas and Data Investigation

Check labels, scale/units, observed pattern, explanation, and limitation. Do not treat a map or visualization as proof of causation.

3. Evidence-Based Argument

Score reasoning rather than the student's religious, political, or moral position. Require at least three pieces of evidence and a counterclaim or qualification.

Constructed Response

Using contextualized evidence from at least three traditions studied in Week 6, compare how people addressed one shared question—duty, suffering, justice, identity, or good government. Explain at least one important difference and one limitation of the surviving evidence.

Full-credit characteristics: one clear comparative question; accurate contextualized evidence from at least three traditions; a meaningful difference; and an evidentiary limitation or interpretive caution.
