## Content safeguard

Week 2 explicitly rejects geographic determinism. Physical geography creates opportunities and constraints, but students must analyze how technology, institutions, culture, networks, law, wealth, and power shape human responses.

The Historical Systems Laboratory follows the same boundary: a map or model can reveal patterns and generate hypotheses, but it does not establish historical causation by itself.
