Canonical Mastery Quiz

  1. Q1. A site contains standardized streets, drainage, coordinated buildings, and repeated rebuilding. What is best supported?
    A. Deliberate planning and organized construction.   B. Universal consent.   C. Absolute monarchy.   D. Direct copying.
  2. Q2. Why is archaeology especially important for the Indus civilization?
    A. Material remains are the only evidence.   B. Every inscription is translated.   C. The script remains undeciphered, making material/spatial evidence especially important.   D. Royal biographies are complete.
  3. Q3. Which evidence most directly identifies a Shang political/religious practice?
    A. Inscribed oracle bone associated with divination.   B. Modern map.   C. Later European account.   D. Olmec sculpture.
  4. Q4. What did the Mandate of Heaven connect to legitimate rule?
    A. Permanent legitimacy.   B. Military conquest only.   C. Popular election.   D. Moral performance, with legitimacy capable of being forfeited.
  5. Q5. Which African evidence best supports large-scale organized political or ceremonial activity?
    A. Monumental/material evidence showing coordinated construction and resource use.   B. Modern boundary.   C. Later stereotype.   D. Lack of deciphered writing.
  6. Q6. Which American evidence best supports Olmec ceremonial organization?
    A. Contextualized monumental sculpture/site feature showing coordinated production or placement.   B. Roman trade record.   C. Mesopotamian map.   D. Claim that all American societies were identical.
  7. Q7. Two distant societies build large ceremonial structures. What should historians conclude first?
    A. Copying occurred.   B. Religions were identical.   C. Similarity is an observation requiring evidence before claiming diffusion/shared origin.   D. Both had kings.
  8. Q8. Which evidence most strongly supports long-distance exchange?
    A. Securely provenanced nonlocal materials.   B. Large local building.   C. Unprovenanced later story.   D. Agriculture.
  9. Q9. Which comparison best tests whether complex societies required one universal form of centralization?
    A. Rank them against Mesopotamia.   B. Require writing.   C. Compare Indus, Kush/Nubian, Shang/Zhou, and Olmec evidence across organization, authority, labor, exchange, and uncertainty.   D. Treat monuments as proof of kingship.
  10. Q10. Which synthesis best represents Week 5?
    A. One universal sequence.   B. Multiple pathways shaped by environment, institutions, exchange, labor, belief, and local history, with uneven evidence.   C. Archaeology identifies every ruler.   D. Centralization is the only measure of complexity.

Constructed Response · Teacher Scored

Using evidence from at least three of the four regional strands—Africa, South Asia, East Asia, and the Americas—explain how early societies developed complex urban or political systems through different historical pathways. Include one specific regional comparison, one limitation or uncertainty in the evidence, and one reason similarity alone cannot establish direct cultural diffusion.
