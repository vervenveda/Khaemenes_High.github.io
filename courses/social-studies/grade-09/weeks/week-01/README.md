## Methods-lab boundary

The Week 1 town-meeting source set is explicitly **simulated**. It exists to teach historical method and must not be represented as evidence about a real historical event.

The Historical Systems Laboratory follows the same boundary: model output can generate observations or hypotheses, but it does not establish historical causation. Historical claims must be corroborated with appropriate evidence.

## Gold-standard purpose

Week 1 is the reference implementation for later Grade 9 Social Studies repairs. Recurring instructional routines may remain consistent across the course, but historical content, evidence, questions, vocabulary, maps/data, assignments, assessment items, and systems applications must be genuinely week-specific.
