# Week 1: How We Know the Past

- Unit: Unit 1 · The Historian’s World
- Period: Foundations
- Regions: Global
- Essential question: How can we build a trustworthy account of the past when evidence is incomplete or disputed?
- Five distinct daily lessons focused on evidence, inference, sourcing, contextualization, corroboration, spatial evidence, counterclaims, and qualified historical conclusions
- Three principal assignments: source/OPVL lab, evidence map & reliability matrix, evidence-based seminar/argument
- Ten unique objective mastery questions plus one teacher-scored constructed response
- Objective mastery threshold: **80%**
- Sub-80 workflow: review → error analysis/corrections → retake
- Correct-answer distribution: A=2 · B=3 · C=3 · D=2
- Student printable: `student-packet.html`
- Teacher guide and key: `teacher-guide.html`
- Runtime synchronization layer: `../../assets/week01-gold-standard-v1.js`

## Methods-lab boundary

The Week 1 town-meeting source set is explicitly **simulated**. It exists to teach historical method and must not be represented as evidence about a real historical event.

## Gold-standard purpose

Week 1 is the reference implementation for later Grade 9 Social Studies repairs. Recurring instructional routines may remain consistent across the course, but historical content, evidence, questions, vocabulary, maps/data, assignments, and assessment items must be genuinely week-specific.
