Week 7: Persia and the Greek World

Essential Question: How did empires and city-states organize citizenship, diversity, military power, and political participation?

Mastery: At least 80% (8/10 objective questions). Constructed response and assignments require evaluator judgment.

Historical Safeguards

  • Do not reduce the comparison to “Greek freedom versus Persian tyranny.” Require students to identify participation, exclusion, hierarchy, coercion, and negotiation in each system.
  • Do not describe Persian accommodation of local institutions as modern equal citizenship or modern religious freedom.
  • Do not present Athens as representative of every Greek polis. Sparta is a major counterexample, and other poleis varied as well.
  • Always specify the restricted citizen body when discussing Athenian democracy; women, enslaved people, and resident foreigners were excluded from citizenship.
  • Do not equate source perspective with uselessness. Greek narratives and Persian royal inscriptions are situated evidence requiring corroboration.
  • Distinguish political ideals from social and institutional practice.

Assignment Guidance

1 · Source Lab: Compare a Greek narrative with Persian royal or administrative evidence. Require origin, audience, purpose, context, useful evidence, limitation, and a corroboration plan. Students should not reject a source merely because it has a viewpoint.

2 · Atlas and Political-Status Investigation: Map Persian administrative geography, communication routes, selected poleis, and conflict zones. Pair the map with an Athens–Sparta–Persia matrix addressing political membership, obligations, administration, coercion, and local variation. Students must state what the visualization cannot prove.

3 · Comparative Argument: “How useful is the freedom-versus-tyranny framework for understanding Persia and the Greek world?” Require three accurate pieces of evidence, at least two source types, and a serious qualification or counterargument. Score reasoning rather than ideological conclusion.

Constructed Response Rubric

Prompt: Compare how the Achaemenid Persian Empire and Greek poleis organized political membership, diversity, military power, and participation, and explain one limitation of a simple freedom-versus-tyranny interpretation.

5 points: 1 defensible comparative claim; 2 accurate evidence across Persia and at least one Greek polis; 1 use of at least two source types/reasoning; 1 explicit limitation or qualification.

Correction Protocol

Below 8/10: identify the missed concept, revisit the relevant evidence, explain why the original answer failed, and retake. Completion alone is not mastery.
