Industrialization Model Lab

Use a small local classroom model to test how savings, depreciation, population growth, and technology can change modeled output. It is a reasoning aid, not a historical measurement or financial tool.

Lab protocol

  1. Predict before changing a variable.
  2. Change one variable at a time.
  3. Record the modeled output.
  4. Explain the mechanism in plain language.
  5. Name one missing variable or limitation.
  6. Corroborate the historical claim with the Week 26 readings.

Local growth model

The model is intentionally simple and transparent. Capital index = 35 + 80×savings − 55×depreciation − 20×population growth + 25×technology. Output and potential are bounded classroom indices from 0–100.
