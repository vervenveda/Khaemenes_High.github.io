Grade 9 Global Studies Honors · Week 26

Industrialization Simulation Lab

Use the existing Khaemenes Finance simulators as controlled models. The goal is not to “win” the simulator; it is to make, test, and qualify historical claims.

Lab Protocol

  1. Predict: state what you expect before changing a variable.
  2. Change one variable: avoid changing several factors at once.
  3. Observe: record the output or equilibrium.
  4. Explain: connect the result to an economic mechanism.
  5. Limit: identify what the simplified model leaves out.
  6. Corroborate: compare the model with historical evidence from Week 26 readings.

Lab A · Long-Run Growth

Explore savings/investment, depreciation, population growth, and technology. The simulator reports steady-state capital, output per worker, and growth potential.

Open Growth Simulator

Student Record

Record three runs. For each: settings → prediction → observed result → explanation → limitation.

Lab B · Supply & Demand

Adjust demand and supply strength and observe the equilibrium price and quantity. Treat the output as a simplified model, not a complete historical economy.

Open Supply & Demand Simulator

Student Record

Record five runs and explain one change using supply/demand language.

Historical reasoning safeguard: A simulator demonstrates the behavior of its assumptions. It does not by itself prove that historical industrialization followed exactly the same pattern. Use primary/secondary evidence to test the connection.
