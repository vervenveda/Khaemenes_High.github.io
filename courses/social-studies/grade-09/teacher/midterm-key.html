Part II · Constructed Responses · 20 points

21 · Source & Implementation · 10 points

2 clear claim explaining that normative/official evidence does not establish uniform implementation; 4 at least three appropriate evidence categories such as local administrative/fiscal records, court/petition evidence, inscriptions, archaeology/material culture, spatial evidence, narrative/oral evidence; 2 corroboration/reasoning; 2 meaningful limitation or uncertainty.

22 · Networks & Uneven Consequences · 10 points

2 defensible claim; 4 accurate evidence from at least three regions/units; 2 two traced mechanisms; 2 qualification showing uneven outcomes or limits of analogy.

Part III · Cumulative DBQ Outline · 20 points

Thesis 4: defensible, comparative, previews reasoning.

Grouping 4: at least two analytical groups that help answer the prompt; not merely continent/source-type sorting.

Evidence 6: at least four documents and four regions plus relevant course knowledge beyond the set.

Reasoning / Counterevidence 4: explains relationships and uses an outlier, contradiction, or counterclaim to refine the argument.

Limitation / Qualification 2: identifies a meaningful source, category, chronology, map, or evidentiary limitation.

Mastery Rule

80–100: mastery demonstrated. Below 80: error-forensics correction, targeted relearning, new practice, and reassessment. Do not award mastery for copied answer letters or repeated recognition alone.
