  1. Explain one major continuity and one major change in political legitimacy from ancient empires to modern states.
    Rubric: Accurate claim (2), relevant evidence (4), reasoning (3), clarity (1).
  2. Compare the role of technology in two transformations from different course units.
    Rubric: Accurate claim (2), relevant evidence (4), reasoning (3), clarity (1).
  3. Explain how one economic system or network distributed benefits and burdens unequally.
    Rubric: Accurate claim (2), relevant evidence (4), reasoning (3), clarity (1).

Essay

Choose one enduring issue—legitimacy, inequality, migration, human rights, environmental change, information control, or global interdependence. Develop an argument explaining how the issue changed and persisted across at least four historical periods and three world regions.

Rubric: Thesis 4; contextualization 4; evidence 10; reasoning 8; organization and citation 4.
