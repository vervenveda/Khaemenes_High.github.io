Grade 9 Global Studies Honors · Week 26 · Local-first

Industrialization Simulation Lab

Use two simplified local models to test mechanisms, then corroborate the results with historical evidence. These models demonstrate their assumptions; they do not prove what happened in a historical society.

Return to Week 26

Protocol

  1. Predict before changing a variable.
  2. Change one variable at a time when testing a mechanism.
  3. Record the model output.
  4. Explain the mechanism in words and units.
  5. Name at least one omitted historical factor.
  6. Corroborate with Week 26 sources before making a historical claim.
