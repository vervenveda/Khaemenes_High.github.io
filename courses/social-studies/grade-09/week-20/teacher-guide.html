Assignment Guidance

1. Source Lab — Pamphlet War Source Set

Teacher note: Accept multiple interpretations when claims are supported. Do not award full evidence credit for summary without analysis.

Sample-answer guidance: A strong response will identify the source’s origin and audience, use at least two specific details, and explain how the source contributes to the question “Why did religious reform become a political, social, and media revolution?” while naming one limitation.

2. Atlas and Data Investigation

Teacher note: Check labels, legend, scale or units, and whether the student distinguishes observation from explanation.

Sample-answer guidance: A strong product uses accurate labels and units, identifies a meaningful pattern, proposes an evidence-based explanation, and notes what the visualization cannot prove.

3. Evidence-Based Seminar or Argument

Teacher note: Score the quality of reasoning rather than the political or moral position selected.

Sample-answer guidance: Answers may reasonably take different positions. Full-credit work directly answers “Does toleration require agreement, neutrality, or simply limits on coercion?,” uses accurate evidence from at least two source types, and addresses a serious counterargument.

Constructed Response

Using at least two specific pieces of evidence, answer: Why did religious reform become a political, social, and media revolution?

Exemplar: A full-credit response states a defensible answer to the essential question, uses two accurate details from the week, explains how each detail supports the claim, and acknowledges one complexity or limitation.
