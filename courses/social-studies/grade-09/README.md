# Khaemenes High — Grade 9 Global Studies Honors

A 36-week integrated social studies course combining world history, geography, civics, economics, media literacy, research, and a nonpartisan civic capstone.

## Current forensic status

The course is under active forensic hardening on `hardening/archaemenes-highschool` and is **not yet final A+++ certified**.

The original generated portal has been preserved for the hardening transition, and Academy continuity/mastery/differentiation assets have been prepared for the hardened entry. Final deployment remains blocked on objective-bank reconstruction, semantic uniqueness checks, and browser validation.

See `FORENSIC_AUDIT_2026-08-16.md` and the root `GRADE09_FORENSIC_CURRICULUM_AUDIT.md` for the active findings.

## Confirmed forensic issues

- repeated Monday–Friday lesson bodies with topic substitution;
- repeated weekly assignment structures;
- small shared quiz banks reused across many weeks;
- quiz-bank items reused in the midterm and final;
- strong correct-answer-position bias;
- old `70%` passing threshold instead of the Academy's `80%` mastery gate;
- independent browser-created student identity rather than the Academy Family Registry.

## Hardened target

The staged design establishes:

- Academy Family Registry learner authority;
- learner-scoped formal records;
- Grade 08 preview separated from Grade 09 mastery;
- 80% progression;
- Monday → Friday sequential lessons;
- three weekly evidence assignments before the mastery check;
- an 80% midterm gate before Semester II;
- a final gate;
- friendly lock reminders;
- 36 topic-specific inquiry upgrades;
- purposeful Verve N Veda resource assignments.

The objective banks and cumulative exams remain the next major forensic repair.

## Preserved academic scope

The course continues to target 36 chronological weeks and 180 learning blocks spanning historical methods, ancient and classical worlds, regional/interregional systems, early modern transformations, revolutions/industry/imperialism, global conflict, decolonization, globalization, comparative government, media literacy, and a nonpartisan civic capstone.

The final standard requires those 180 blocks to be **substantively distinct**, not merely structurally counted.

Formal Academy advancement uses **80% mastery**. Honors distinction may remain above 80%, but cannot replace or lower the advancement threshold.
