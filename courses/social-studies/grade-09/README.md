# Khaemenes High — Grade 9 Global Studies Honors

A complete **36-week core** integrated social studies course with a **conditional six-week readiness bridge**, creating a **42-week supported pathway** for learners who benefit from prerequisite skill-building before Official Week 1.

Public path:

`courses/social-studies/grade-09/`

## Entry architecture
- `assessments/readiness.html` — low-stakes prerequisite-skills diagnostic.
- Direct-entry evidence: 80% overall with no readiness domain below 60%.
- `prep/index.html` — six-week Prep Unit P1–P6 when bridge support is recommended.
- Prep Gateway: 80% mastery required before transition to Official Week 1.
- NAIB remains the routing authority; the browser assessment publishes bounded readiness evidence and does not assign mentors or make permanent placement decisions.
- Prep work is readiness evidence and does not lower the official course grade.

## Pathways
- **36-week core:** readiness evidence supports direct entry → Official Week 1.
- **42-week supported:** Prep P1–P6 → 80% Gateway mastery → Official Weeks 1–36.

## Prep Unit
1. P1 · Reading History Like an Investigator
2. P2 · Sources, Provenance & Corroboration
3. P3 · Time, Chronology & Causation
4. P4 · Maps, Geography & Human Systems
5. P5 · Data, Graphs & Quantitative Claims
6. P6 · Argument, Ethics & Course Readiness

## Core course
- 36 distinct official weeks
- 180 Monday–Friday lessons
- 108 principal assignments
- 36 weekly mastery quizzes
- Midterm and cumulative final
- Original offline weekly readings plus synchronized free reading library
- Primary-source, map, data, economics, civics, and media-literacy work
- ARSHIF, Evidence & Citation Studio, Atlas Evidence Analysis, and PROSE workflows where appropriate
- Student portal, teacher portal, reports, export, printing, and local retakes
- Printable student packets and teacher guides

## Launch
Open `index.html`. New learners should begin with the Readiness Assessment.

## Student data
Records use browser `localStorage`. Export backups regularly. Data is not synchronized across devices.

## Teacher portal
Open `teacher/index.html`. Initial local passcode: `KHAE09`. Change it after first use.

Because GitHub Pages is a static host, this passcode is only a convenience layer. It is not secure server authentication, and the site must not contain sensitive student information.
