# Khaemenes High — Grade 9 Global Studies Honors

A complete **36-week core** integrated social studies course with a **conditional six-week readiness bridge**, creating a **42-week supported pathway** for learners who benefit from prerequisite skill-building before Official Week 1.

Public path:

`courses/social-studies/grade-09/`

## Canonical mastery policy
- **80% is the canonical mastery threshold across the course.**
- Any older 70% mastery/passing value in legacy metadata is a relic and must not be treated as a mastery gate.
- Weekly progression requires all five daily lessons, all three principal assignments submitted, and at least **80% on the weekly objective quiz**.
- The midterm and final objective mastery gates are **80%**.
- Sub-80% objective attempts follow the review → error analysis/correction → retake workflow.
- HonorsTarget is a reporting distinction, not a progression gate.
- Teacher-reviewed constructed responses and essays remain human-evaluated and are not silently converted into objective mastery scores.

## Historical Systems Laboratory
The ProResource Empire Systems Lab is integrated longitudinally rather than introduced as a one-off simulator.

- `systems-lab-roadmap.html` — course-wide Historical Systems Laboratory progression.
- `assets/historical-systems-lab-bridge-v1.js` — skill ladder from Observer through Forensic Historian.
- `assets/grade09-socialstudies-systems-integration-v1.js` — weekly systems-thinking checkpoints.
- Weeks 1–2: Observer.
- Weeks 3–5: Mapper.
- Weeks 6–8: Causation Investigator.
- Weeks 9–12: Network Analyst.
- Weeks 13–17: Exchange Analyst.
- Weeks 18–23: Historical Investigator.
- Weeks 24–25: Systems Analyst.
- Weeks 26–27: Applied Analyst.
- Weeks 28–36: Forensic Historian.

**Guardrail:** simulation output generates observations and hypotheses; it is not historical evidence by itself. Historical claims must be corroborated with appropriate primary and secondary evidence.

## Entry architecture
- `assessments/readiness.html` — low-stakes prerequisite-skills diagnostic.
- Direct-entry evidence: 80% overall with no readiness domain below 60%.
- `prep/index.html` — six-week Prep Unit P1–P6 when bridge support is recommended.
- Prep Gateway: 80% mastery required before transition to Official Week 1.
- NAIB remains the routing authority; the browser assessment publishes bounded readiness evidence and does not assign mentors or make permanent placement decisions.
- Prep work is readiness evidence and does not lower the official course grade.

## Pathways
- **36-week core:** readiness evidence supports direct entry → Official Week 1.
- **42-week supported:** Prep P1–P6 → 80% Gateway mastery → Official Weeks 1–36.

## Core course
- 36 distinct official weeks
- 180 Monday–Friday lessons
- 108 principal assignments
- 36 weekly mastery quizzes
- Midterm and cumulative final
- Original offline weekly readings plus synchronized free reading library
- Primary-source, map, data, economics, civics, and media-literacy work
- ARSHIF, Evidence & Citation Studio, Atlas Evidence Analysis, and PROSE workflows where appropriate
- Student portal, teacher portal, reports, export, printing, and local retakes
- Printable student packets and teacher guides
