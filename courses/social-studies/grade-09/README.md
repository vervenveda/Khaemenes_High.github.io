# Khaemenes High — Grade 9 Global Studies Honors

A 36-week integrated social studies course combining world history, geography, civics, economics, media literacy, research, and a nonpartisan civic capstone.

## Current forensic status

The course is under active forensic hardening on `hardening/archaemenes-highschool` and is **not yet final A+++ certified or deployment-ready**.

The pre-forensic portal has been retained as a repository blob for attachment as `legacy.html`. The Academy-facing `index.html` is the continuity/progression entry while the curriculum is repaired. Do not publish the wrapper until the preserved legacy portal and its hardening assets are confirmed attached on the active branch.

See `FORENSIC_AUDIT_2026-08-16.md` for the detailed findings and repair status.

## Confirmed forensic issues

- repeated Monday–Friday lesson bodies with topic substitution;
- repeated weekly assignment structures;
- small shared quiz banks reused across many weeks;
- quiz-bank items reused in the midterm and final;
- strong correct-answer-position bias;
- old `70%` passing threshold instead of the Academy's `80%` mastery gate;
- independent browser-created student identity rather than the Academy Family Registry.

## Hardened target

The staged hardening design establishes Academy learner authority, learner-scoped records, Grade 08 preview isolation, 80% progression, sequential lessons, three evidence assignments before each weekly mastery check, an 80% midterm gate before Semester II, a final gate, friendly lock reminders, week-specific inquiry differentiation, and purposeful Verve N Veda resource assignments.

The objective banks and cumulative exams remain a separate forensic repair before final approval.

## Preserved academic scope

The course continues to target 36 chronological weeks and 180 learning blocks spanning historical methods, ancient and classical worlds, regional/interregional systems, early modern transformations, revolutions/industry/imperialism, global conflict, decolonization, globalization, comparative government, media literacy, and a nonpartisan civic capstone.

The final standard requires those 180 blocks to be **substantively distinct**, not merely structurally counted.

## Academy continuity

`Academy Family Registry → NAIB delegation → Khaemenes Academy High School → Archaemenes · Academy Scholar → Global Studies Honors 9`

Formal records are learner-scoped. Grade 08 advanced preview cannot create formal Grade 09 mastery or promotion.

## Mastery progression

Formal Academy advancement uses **80% mastery**. Honors distinction may remain above 80%, but cannot replace or lower the advancement threshold.

## Static-hosting boundary

Browser storage and any local teacher convenience gate are not secure server authentication. Do not store sensitive student information in the public static site.
