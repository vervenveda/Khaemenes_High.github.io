# Khaemenes High — Grade 9 Global Studies Honors

A 36-week integrated social studies course combining world history, geography, civics, economics, media literacy, research, and a nonpartisan civic capstone.

## Current forensic status

The course is being hardened on `hardening/archaemenes-highschool` before Grade 09 receives final A+++ approval.

The pre-forensic student portal is preserved as a repository blob during the transition, and the Academy-facing `index.html` is now the continuity/progression surface. The legacy portal must remain attached as `legacy.html` before the wrapper is considered deployment-ready.

The forensic audit has confirmed several issues that ordinary structural validation did not detect:

- repeated Monday–Friday lesson bodies with topic substitution;
- repeated weekly assignment structures;
- small shared quiz banks reused across many weeks;
- exact quiz-bank reuse in the midterm and final;
- strong correct-answer-position bias;
- old `70%` passing threshold instead of the Academy's `80%` mastery gate;
- independent browser-created student identity rather than the Academy Family Registry.

See `FORENSIC_AUDIT_2026-08-16.md` for the detailed findings and repair standard.

## Preserved academic scope

The course continues to target:

- 36 distinct chronological weeks;
- 180 Monday–Friday learning blocks;
- world history from historical-method foundations through the contemporary world;
- geographic and quantitative reasoning;
- civics and comparative government;
- economics and development;
- source analysis and corroboration;
- media literacy;
- evidence-based historical writing;
- nonpartisan civic capstone;
- printable packets, reports, and portfolio evidence.

The goal of the forensic pass is to make those 180 lesson blocks **substantively distinct**, not merely structurally present.

## Academy continuity

Formal learner authority follows:

`Academy Family Registry → NAIB delegation → Khaemenes Academy High School → Archaemenes · Academy Scholar → Global Studies Honors 9`

The course must not create a second authoritative learner identity.

Formal Grade 09 records are learner-scoped. Grade 08 advanced-preview access remains exploratory and cannot create formal Grade 09 mastery or promotion.

## Mastery progression

The Academy standard is **80% mastery**.

The hardened course is designed to enforce:

1. Week 1 available to the eligible Grade 09 learner.
2. Lessons completed in instructional order.
3. Three substantial weekly evidence assignments completed before the formal weekly mastery check.
4. Weekly mastery check opens only after the required weekly learning evidence is reached.
5. Next week opens only after the prior mastery check reaches 80% or higher.
6. Midterm opens after the first-semester sequence is mastered.
7. Semester II opens only after the midterm reaches 80% or higher.
8. Final opens after the full-year sequence and required midterm gate.
9. Reassessment remains available after review.
10. Friendly reminders explain locked activities without punitive language.

An honors distinction may remain above 80%, but it does not replace or lower the Academy mastery threshold.

## Assessment integrity requirement

Before final release:

- weekly objective questions must be semantically distinct;
- correct answers must be verified;
- answer positions must not reveal a pattern;
- cumulative assessments must not simply repeat weekly-bank items;
- historical claims, dates, chronology, terminology, and geographic references must be checked;
- constructed-response work must use explicit evidence/rubric criteria;
- sensitive historical topics must use careful source and context protocols.

## Verve N Veda resource layer

## Static-hosting boundary

Browser storage and any local teacher convenience gate are not secure server authentication. Do not store sensitive student information in the public static site.
