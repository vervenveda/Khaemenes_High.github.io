# Social Studies Weekly Learner Rubric Contract

This is the canonical learner-facing weekly contract for Grades 9–12 Social Studies.

Every live weekly engine must present these items near the top of the week:

1. **Essential Question**
2. **Learning Targets** drawn from the existing daily lesson objectives
3. **Required Evidence**: five daily lessons, the week's three principal assignments, constructed response, and objective mastery check
4. **Success Criteria / Rubric** drawn from the existing assignment/evidence criteria
5. **Unlock Condition** that matches the actual progression code and the Academy 80% mastery floor
6. **Academic Vocabulary** remains visible in the weekly experience

Course-specific gates remain authoritative:
- Grade 9: assignments require evaluator scores and the constructed response requires evaluator scoring; Week 18 also requires the Midterm at 80%+.
- Grade 10: Week 18 also requires the Midterm at 80%+ before Week 19.
- Grade 11 Government/Law/Economics: Week 18 also requires the Government Final at 80%+ before Week 19.
- Grade 11 Personal Finance: Week 9 also requires the Financial Literacy Midterm at 80%+ before Week 10.
- Grade 12: Week 18 also requires the Semester Exam at the course threshold before Week 19.

Printable student packets may use **Objective** for the daily print-form equivalent of a Learning Target, but must still expose the Essential Question, vocabulary, required assignments/evidence, rubric or success criteria, constructed response, and 80% mastery/reassessment language.

This contract does not replace course-specific curriculum, rubrics, evaluator authority, or graduation-credit gates.
