2 · Core Lesson

Entrepreneurship & value creation

Entrepreneurship begins with a problem worth solving, not with a logo. A value proposition explains who benefits, what problem is addressed, and why the solution is useful. A simple model considers users, costs, price or funding, delivery, and risk. Break-even reasoning asks how much must be sold or funded before specified costs are covered. Ethical entrepreneurship avoids deception, hidden harms, exploitative labor, and manipulative design.

4 · Worked Model

Inspect the reasoning

Before buying tools for a bike tune-up service, estimate costs, compare local prices, interview users, and test whether a basic service solves a real need.

Misconception

A good idea succeeds if the creator works hard enough.

Correction

Effort matters, but demand, costs, access, execution, timing, and external conditions also matter.
