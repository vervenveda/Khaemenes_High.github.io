2 · Core Lesson

Professional portfolio & defense

A professional portfolio is not a storage dump. Curation means selecting evidence that supports specific claims about growth, skill, judgment, and readiness. Strong portfolios include artifacts, context, reflection, revision, and limitations. A final defense explains why each artifact was selected, what changed during revision, what evidence is strongest, and what still needs development. The goal is not to appear finished; it is to demonstrate responsible self-assessment and a credible next step.

4 · Worked Model

Inspect the reasoning

A learner selects a source-comparison dossier, project plan, compensation analysis, and pathway experiment. For each, they explain the original problem, evidence used, revision made, and skill demonstrated.

Misconception

A portfolio should contain only perfect work.

Correction

A strong portfolio can include revision evidence and limitations because growth and judgment are part of readiness.

5 · Guided Practice
