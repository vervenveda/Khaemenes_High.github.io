Week 12 · Day 04

Career pathway experiment

What can I learn by testing a small real-world task before making a long-term decision?

1 · Retrieval & Goal

Start from evidence

Retrieve a prior artifact or idea that can support today's task. Write a concrete goal and identify one constraint.

2 · Core Lesson

Career pathway experiment

Career exploration is stronger when students test small pieces of real work rather than making decisions from titles alone. A pathway experiment might include an informational interview, simulated task, short volunteer role, supervised job shadow, or project modeled on a field. The goal is not to prove permanent fit; it is to gather evidence about tasks, environments, tools, values, constraints, and learning needs.

Today's focus: Apply Career pathway experiment in a new situation. Build, perform, design, plan, or solve something authentic to the discipline, then test it against explicit criteria.

4 · Worked Model

Inspect the reasoning

A learner curious about graphic design creates an event information sheet, records revision cycles, and interviews a designer about client feedback. They learn they enjoy layout but want more practice with deadlines.

Misconception

One enjoyable or difficult experience tells you whether a career is right for you.

Correction

Pathway experiments provide evidence, not destiny; multiple contexts improve judgment.

5 · Guided Practice

Compare before deciding

Develop two plausible approaches. Compare evidence, constraints, audience, ethics, time, cost, or access; then justify a choice.
