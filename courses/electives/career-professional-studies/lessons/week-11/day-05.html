2 · Core Lesson

Workplace rights, responsibilities & safety

Workplaces rely on policies, laws, contracts, and professional norms. Responsible reasoning includes locating current authoritative information, documenting observable facts, preserving relevant records, following safety procedures, and using appropriate escalation channels. Serious safety or harassment concerns may require formal reporting or emergency help rather than informal problem-solving. Accommodation and accessibility support fair participation.

4 · Worked Model

Inspect the reasoning

In a mock workplace, a learner notices a blocked emergency exit. The correct response is to report the hazard through the designated safety channel, not enter an unsafe area to prove initiative.

Misconception

Professionalism means handling every workplace problem privately.

Correction

Some issues require formal reporting, documentation, authorized support, or emergency procedures.
