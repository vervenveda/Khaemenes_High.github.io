Week 04 · Day 05

Resumes, portfolios & evidence of work

How can I present evidence of learning and experience truthfully and effectively?

1 · Retrieval & Goal

Revisit the evidence

Retrieve your strongest Week 4 artifact and one piece of feedback. Identify what claim the artifact supports and what still needs clarification.

2 · Core Lesson

Truthful presentation is stronger than exaggeration

A resume is a concise evidence summary and a portfolio is a curated set of artifacts that can show the work behind the summary. Strong accomplishment statements name an action, context, and supportable outcome. Student evidence can come from academic projects, clubs, community work, independent learning, or other non-sensitive experiences. Provenance matters: what the artifact is, when it was created, what role you had, and what changed during revision.

Today's focus: revise the Week 4 resume and evidence portfolio, defend one wording decision, identify one limitation, and prepare the artifact for cumulative review.
3 · Vocabulary

Use the language accurately

  • resume — concise evidence summary.
  • portfolio — curated evidence set.
  • artifact — a concrete work sample.
  • accomplishment statement — action/context/outcome statement.
  • provenance — traceable origin and role information.
  • verification — checking that a claim is supportable.
4 · Worked Model

Evidence-backed wording

Weak: “Excellent at research.” Stronger: “Compared five sources for a science investigation, documented credibility criteria, and revised the conclusion after finding conflicting evidence.”

Misconception

A resume should make every experience sound impressive, even if the wording stretches the truth.

Correction

Strong resumes translate real evidence into concise language without inventing responsibility or results.

5 · Guided Practice

Audit two claims

Select two resume or portfolio claims. For each, identify the artifact that supports it, any wording that overstates the evidence, and a more accurate revision.

6 · Independent Application

Resume and evidence portfolio

Revise the Week 4 portfolio so every major claim points to traceable evidence. Add provenance notes and one revision explanation.

7 · Test & Revise

Reviewer test

Ask: Could another person verify this claim from the artifact? Does the wording accurately describe my role? Is any important limitation hidden? Revise accordingly.

8 · Reflection & Transfer

Defend the final version

Local drafts are learning evidence only—not transcript credit or teacher verification.
