2 · Core Lesson

Workplace collaboration & conflict

Teams work better when responsibilities and decision rules are explicit. Conflict can reveal hidden constraints or competing goals. A position is what someone says they want; an interest is the underlying need. Productive conflict protocols separate people from the problem, describe observable facts, ask questions, identify shared goals, generate options, and document decisions. Psychological safety means people can raise concerns and admit uncertainty without humiliation while still being accountable.
