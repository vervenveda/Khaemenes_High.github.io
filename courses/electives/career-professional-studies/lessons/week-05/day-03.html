2 · Core Lesson

Interviewing & professional presence

An interview is a two-way evidence conversation. Preparation means understanding the role, selecting accurate examples, practicing concise explanations, and preparing questions. Situation–Task–Action–Result can organize an answer, but the result may include learning rather than perfect success. Active listening matters because a memorized answer that ignores the actual question is weak evidence. Professional presence is not a personality contest; accent, disability, eye contact, clothing access, or extroversion should not be mistaken for competence.

4 · Worked Model

Inspect the reasoning

A learner answers a project-failure question by naming the missed dependency, the action used to rebuild the schedule, the partial recovery, and the checklist adopted afterward.

Misconception

The best interview answer always shows perfect success.

Correction

Credible answers may show mistakes, learning, revision, and responsible judgment.
