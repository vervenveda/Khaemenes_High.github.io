2 · Core Lesson

Project planning & time management

Projects fail when goals are vague, dependencies are hidden, or schedules assume perfect conditions. Milestones mark meaningful progress. Dependencies show what must happen before another task can proceed. Scope defines what is included and excluded. Buffers make uncertainty visible. A risk register names possible problems, likelihood, impact, warning signs, and mitigation. Planning is not predicting the future exactly; it is creating a system that can adapt.

Today's focus: Apply Project planning & time management in a new situation. Build, perform, design, plan, or solve something authentic to the discipline, then test it against explicit criteria.

4 · Worked Model

Inspect the reasoning

A three-week event plan treats venue confirmation as a dependency for printing, adds a two-day buffer, and creates a digital fallback.

Misconception

A detailed schedule proves a project is under control.

Correction

A useful schedule includes dependencies, uncertainty, ownership, and revision triggers.
