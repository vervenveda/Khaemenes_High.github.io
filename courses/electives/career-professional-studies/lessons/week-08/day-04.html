2 · Core Lesson

Financial literacy for work

Employment decisions involve more than hourly wage or salary. Gross pay is earnings before deductions; net pay is what remains after applicable deductions. Benefits, schedules, commuting costs, equipment, unpaid time, stability, and training can matter. A fair comparison uses the same time frame and states assumptions. Students should use fictional or public scenarios rather than disclosing family finances.

Today's focus: Apply Financial literacy for work in a new situation. Build, perform, design, plan, or solve something authentic to the discipline, then test it against explicit criteria.

4 · Worked Model

Inspect the reasoning

Offer A pays more per hour but has fewer guaranteed hours and a longer commute. Offer B pays slightly less but offers predictable hours and paid training. The learner compares monthly estimates and names assumptions.

Misconception

The job with the highest advertised pay is always the best financial choice.

Correction

Compensation decisions require context, costs, benefits, stability, and personal constraints.
