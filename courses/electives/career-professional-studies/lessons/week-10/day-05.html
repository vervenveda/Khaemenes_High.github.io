2 · Core Lesson

Marketing, persuasion & ethics

Marketing can inform, persuade, or manipulate. Ethical marketing uses supportable claims, identifies an intended audience without exploiting vulnerability, and does not hide important conditions. Persuasion is not inherently unethical; deception is. Students should distinguish emotional appeal from factual support and identify dark patterns that pressure users into actions they may not freely choose.

4 · Worked Model

Inspect the reasoning

A subscription ad says “only $5” but hides weekly billing and auto-renewal. An ethical redesign places billing period and cancellation terms next to the price.

Misconception

If a statement is technically true, it is automatically ethical marketing.

Correction

Context, omissions, visual emphasis, audience vulnerability, and likely interpretation also matter.
