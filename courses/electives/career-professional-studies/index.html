How the course works

Daily learning

Each lesson includes retrieval, explicit teaching, vocabulary in context, a worked model, guided comparison, authentic application, revision, and transfer.

Assessment

Weekly checks are open-book and require 80% mastery. Weeks 4, 8, and 12 include cumulative transfer checkpoints. Below mastery requires targeted correction before reassessment.

Evidence

Students build resumes, source dossiers, communication samples, project plans, compensation analyses, ethical marketing critiques, pathway experiments, and portfolio artifacts.

Record boundary

Browser-local drafts and scores are recoverable learning evidence, not teacher verification, transcript credit, or employment certification.
