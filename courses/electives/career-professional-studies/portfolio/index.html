Career & Professional Studies
Week 13 · Culminating Evidence
Professional Portfolio

Curate, Defend, and Plan the Next Step

Select evidence that demonstrates growth, judgment, communication, quantitative reasoning, ethics, and responsible career exploration.

Required evidence set

  1. Skills inventory and evidence map.
  2. Career-source comparison dossier.
  3. Professional communication or resume/portfolio artifact.
  4. Project plan or compensation analysis.
  5. Entrepreneurship/marketing or workplace-ethics artifact.
  6. Career pathway experiment.
  7. One artifact showing meaningful revision after feedback.

Artifact annotations

For each selected artifact, state the problem, your role, evidence used, decision made, revision completed, limitation, and competency demonstrated.

Portfolio defense

Prepare a 6–10 minute oral, written, or recorded defense answering: What is your strongest evidence of growth? Which decision was hardest? Where did evidence change your mind? What limitation remains? What is your next realistic learning or career-exploration step?
