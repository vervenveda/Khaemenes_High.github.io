# Khaemenes Academy — High School Elective Program Model v1

The elective program is intentionally lighter in **calendar burden**, not lighter in academic quality.

## Academic-year pacing

- Core instructional design: **36 weeks**.
- Flexible regular-year completion window: **up to 42 weeks**.
- Standard required class load: **6 classes**.
- Maximum active class load: **8 classes**.
- Students may use the extra completion window to recover time, slow down in demanding core courses, or finish work at an appropriate pace without reducing mastery expectations.

## Compact elective model

The standard High School elective is **13 weeks / 65 daily lessons**. This compact structure gives students room to explore creative, practical, technical, and intellectual subjects while preserving time for demanding core classes.

Three sequential 13-week elective blocks equal 39 weeks and can fit inside the Academy's 42-week flexible completion window when a learner chooses that pace. Students are not required to fill every elective block.

During the regular academic year, electives count toward the **maximum of eight active classes**.

## Summer

Summer elective study is open-ended by Academy policy. There is no Academy-wide numerical cap on how many electives a learner may complete over summer, provided prerequisites, safety rules, and reasonable learner-selected workload are respected.

Summer electives may be used for curiosity, enrichment, portfolio development, practical skills, or additional exploration. They do not need to displace core-course catch-up.

## Course-design principle

Every elective should use evidence authentic to its discipline.

- Writing: drafts, revision records, finished pieces, publication/presentation.
- Art: studies, process pages, critique, original works, artist statements, portfolio.
- Trades: safety checks, measurements, plans, builds/repairs, competency evidence.
- Life Skills: practical scenarios, plans, demonstrations, decisions, reflection.
- Critical Thinking: claims, source comparisons, arguments, counterarguments, statistical reasoning, defense.
- Computing: code, prototypes, debugging evidence, documentation.
- Music: listening, notation, performance/composition, analysis, revision.
- Career: professional artifacts, research, communication, budgets, portfolio and defense.
- Psychology: research methods, data literacy, evidence, ethics and bounded inference.

Compact duration never permits fabricated completion, weaker privacy, or lower mastery standards.

## Authority

A catalog card, route, curriculum map, or attractive landing page is not course authority. A new elective becomes learner-selectable only after the production and promotion requirements in `ELECTIVE_QUALITY_STANDARD_V1.md` pass, including runtime acceptance.
