Student Path

Electives follow the same academic grammar.

A new elective should feel like it belongs in Khaemenes Academy, even when its subject is completely different from the core.

01 · HallChoose an elective category.
02 · CourseEnter the course landing page.
03 · LearnFollow lessons, labs, projects, and resources.
04 · MasterComplete assessments and preserve the record.
Design rule: the High School Front Hall points to this Electives Hall; this Hall points to individual elective courses. That keeps the main campus clean while allowing the elective catalog to grow indefinitely.
