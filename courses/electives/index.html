Design rule: the High School Front Hall points to this Electives Hall; this Hall points to individual elective courses. That keeps the main campus clean while allowing the elective catalog to grow indefinitely.

Growing the Electives Hall

When you add an elective course, we can convert its category card into a direct doorway and keep the same visual and navigational language across the campus.

Future-ready architecture

One Hall. Many disciplines.

New electives do not require a redesign of the High School home. We simply add the course to the appropriate category, verify its path, and connect the student doorway.
