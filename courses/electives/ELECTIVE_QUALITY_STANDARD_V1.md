# Khaemenes Academy — High School Elective Quality Standard v1

Program pacing and workload policy: `courses/electives/ELECTIVE_PROGRAM_MODEL_V1.md` and `courses/electives/elective-program-contract-v1.json`.

Psychology 101 is the current reference implementation for elective academic depth. New electives do not need identical pedagogy, but they must meet equivalent evidence, mastery, safety, privacy, and production standards before being promoted from catalog plan to active course authority.

## Minimum academic architecture
Each 13-week elective must provide:
- 13 coherent weekly arcs and 65 planned daily learning experiences.
- A permanent reasoning or creation routine appropriate to the discipline.
- Retrieval, explicit instruction/modeling, guided practice, independent application, and reflection/revision.
- Vocabulary taught in context rather than isolated matching alone.
- Authentic student evidence every week.
- At least three cumulative transfer checkpoints.
- A culminating project, portfolio, performance, or defense.
- An 80% mastery floor where objective mastery checks are appropriate.
- Correction/revision pathways below mastery; no fabricated completion.

## Evidence
Evidence must be discipline-authentic:
- Career: professional artifacts, plans, budgets, communication, portfolio, interview/pitch evidence.
- Computing: code/prototypes, debugging logs, data structures, accessibility/security reasoning, documentation.
- Fine Arts: studies, process pages, critiques, original works, artist statements, curated portfolio.
- Health/Human Development: source evaluation, decision plans, communication artifacts, case analysis, public-health reasoning; never forced private disclosures.
- Music/Theory: listening analysis, notation, rhythm/harmony work, composition/performance artifacts, revision and reflection.

## Assessment
Weekly checks may be open-book. Cumulative assessments should require transfer, not pasted weekly questions. Major projects require transparent rubrics. Protected or teacher-reviewed evidence remains separate from browser-local self-report.

## Safety, privacy, and ethics
- No elective may require disclosure of diagnosis, trauma, medication, sexuality, finances, family conflict, or other sensitive personal information.
- Health content is educational, not diagnosis or treatment advice.
- Career/finance tasks may use fictional budgets and scenarios.
- Computing may teach cybersecurity defensively but must not require unsafe exploitation.
- Arts/music critique must evaluate the work and process, not identity or inherent talent.
- Accessible alternatives must preserve the learning target.

## Promotion gate
A planned elective may be promoted to active-course authority only when:
1. its curriculum map passes the elective curriculum validator;
2. all 65 student lesson surfaces exist;
3. assessments and cumulative checkpoints are implemented;
4. learner-scoped persistence/export is tested where used;
5. browser, tablet, keyboard, print, and offline/recovery acceptance is recorded;
6. the ownership manifest and release matrix are updated together.

Route presence or a completed curriculum map alone is never release certification.
