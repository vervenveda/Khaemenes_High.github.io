About

Art 101 is the Academy's foundational high-school studio course. Its established source curriculum was developed in Bazaar Art and later became the instructional foundation used to expand the Higher Learning ARTS-111 pathway. High School elective authority now begins here while the source lesson library remains intact during migration.
