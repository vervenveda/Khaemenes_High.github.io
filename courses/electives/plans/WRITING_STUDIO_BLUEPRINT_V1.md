# Writing Studio — 13-Week Elective Blueprint

**State:** curriculum architecture complete · student production pending  
**Authority:** none until the elective promotion gate passes  
**Target:** Grades 9–12 · 13 weeks · 65 lessons

Writing Studio is a compact but substantial elective covering creative writing, journalism, rhetoric, research synthesis, technical writing, publishing, and portfolio defense.

## Permanent writing routine

**Notice → Draft → Test with Audience → Revise → Edit → Publish → Reflect**

## Weeks

1. Voice, Audience & Purpose
2. Observation, Detail & Description
3. Narrative Structure & Scene
4. Character, Dialogue & Point of View
5. Poetry, Image & Sound
6. Rhetoric & Persuasion
7. Journalism, Interviewing & Verification
8. Research Writing & Synthesis
9. Technical & Instructional Writing
10. Revision, Editing & Style
11. Publishing, Layout & Accessibility
12. Portfolio Curation & Editorial Judgment
13. Writing Portfolio & Author Defense

## Evidence model

Writing cannot be reduced to quiz scores. Weekly evidence should include drafts, annotations, revision records, source notes, finished pieces, publishing choices, and reflections. Objective craft checks may use the Academy 80% mastery floor, while major writing evidence must be revised against transparent rubrics.

## Privacy

Students are never required to disclose trauma, family conflict, diagnosis, sexuality, finances, or other sensitive personal material to demonstrate writing ability. Fictional, public, historical, observed, researched, or supplied scenarios are always acceptable alternatives.

## Critique

Feedback evaluates the work:
- clarity;
- craft;
- evidence;
- organization;
- audience fit;
- style;
- correctness;
- accessibility;
- revision.

It does not grade inherent talent or personal identity.

## Production rule

There is intentionally no live Writing Studio course route yet. The next phase is production of 65 student-facing lessons, weekly assessment/revision surfaces, cumulative checkpoints, portfolio storage/export, and runtime certification.
