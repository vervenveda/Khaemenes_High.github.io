# Khaemenes Academy — High School Whole-Student Progression v1

This progression coordinates the four-year program without replacing any subject engine. English, Mathematics, Science, and Social Studies retain independent curricular and mastery authority.

| Competency | Grade 9 | Grade 10 | Grade 11 | Grade 12 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Research | distinguish source from claim; basic credibility | compare sources and synthesize evidence | reconcile competing evidence and provenance | conduct and defend independent research |
| Writing & communication | structured evidence-based explanation | analytical argument for an audience | disciplinary synthesis and counterargument | sustained scholarly/technical defense |
| Quantitative reasoning | units, ratios, graphs, foundational models | applied interpretation and proportional reasoning | uncertainty, multivariable decisions, model limits | independent modeling, optimization, sensitivity |
| Scientific inquiry | controlled evidence and uncertainty | biological mechanism, sampling and controls | calibration and quantitative experimentation | model discrimination, engineering validation |
| Civic/historical reasoning | global systems and source context | historical causation and evidence | government/economics and institutional analysis | comparative/global policy and independent civic argument |
| Independence | guided workflow and reliable routines | supported planning and revision | self-management across long tasks | independent project management and defense |
| Portfolio | collect authentic evidence | curate growth and revisions | connect evidence to pathway goals | select graduation evidence and defend readiness |

## Cross-year expectations
- Grade 10 reuses Grade 9 research, writing, quantitative and evidence practices in new disciplinary contexts.
- Grade 11 requires longer-horizon planning, explicit uncertainty/limitations, counterevidence and source provenance.
- Grade 12 requires independent decisions, defensible models/arguments, revision after critique and public-facing or evaluator-facing defense.

## Senior Capstone role
The Senior Capstone is the culminating whole-program evidence experience. It may draw artifacts from any subject, but it does not overwrite subject grades or mastery. Evaluator review confirms synthesis, provenance, revision, ethics, communication and independent execution.

## Higher Learning transition
A graduate-ready learner should be able to plan a multi-week inquiry, locate and evaluate evidence, use quantitative or qualitative models appropriately, communicate a defensible conclusion, document limitations, revise after feedback, preserve provenance, and manage work independently.
