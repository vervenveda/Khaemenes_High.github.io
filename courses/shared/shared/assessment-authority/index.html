High School Assessment Authority Boundary

Learning evidence

Browser-delivered practice, diagnostics, weekly checks, unit checks, and public cumulative forms are self-check/learning evidence. They are editable on the learner device and are not confidential or independently authoritative by themselves.

Protected assessment

Confidential supervised questions, answer keys, private grading, administrative review, and credential signing remain behind the Noema + NAIB/Administration backend boundary. Archaemenes supports learning and preparation but does not reveal locked assessments, grade protected evidence, or sign records.
