# Chemistry 11 — Curriculum Map

## Course
International Chemistry & Materials Science 11 · KH-SCI-CHEM11 · 36 weeks · 180 daily lesson blocks.

## Sequence
- Week 01: Chemical Safety, Measurement & Evidence
- Weeks 02–05: Atomic Models, Isotopes, Electrons/Spectra, Periodic Patterns
- Weeks 06–09: Ionic/Covalent Bonding, Molecular Geometry/Polarity, Metals/Solids
- Weeks 10–13: Intermolecular Forces, Gases, Solutions, Mixtures/Separation/Spectroscopy
- Weeks 14–17: Reaction Evidence, Mole/Stoichiometry, Limiting Reactants/Yield, Redox/Solution Stoichiometry
- Week 18: MID — Midterm Examination & Corrections
- Weeks 19–22: Kinetics
- Weeks 23–25: Thermochemistry, Hess's Law, Entropy/Direction
- Weeks 26–28: Equilibrium and Solubility
- Weeks 29–31: Acids, Bases, pH, Titration, Buffers
- Weeks 32–34: Free Energy, Electrochemistry, Nuclear/Materials/Environmental Chemistry
- Week 35: Materials Science & Sustainable Chemistry Capstone
- Week 36: FINAL — Comprehensive Examination & Portfolio Defense

## Instructional rhythm
Model → concept → data/math → investigation/design → CER/mastery.

## Depth standard
Ordinary teaching lessons should expose substantial mechanism, quantitative reasoning, evidence/source quality, uncertainty/model limits, retrieval, and transfer. Assessment weeks may be shorter when they preserve a supervised assessment boundary.

## Laboratory / inquiry
Target ≥25% qualifying inquiry/laboratory instructional time. Safe simulation/data alternatives are required when physical chemistry procedures need equipment, PPE, ventilation, disposal controls, or qualified supervision.

## Release boundary
Source-level curriculum quality is separate from browser/device, accessibility, protected assessment, transcript authority, accreditation, AP authorization, and jurisdictional credit approval.
