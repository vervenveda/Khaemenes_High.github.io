Solutions Synthesis and Transfer

Integrate particle models, molarity, dilution, and solubility into a defensible explanation.

Weekly EvidenceChemistry CourseScience Hall

Explicit science instruction

Solutions connect particle-level interactions with quantitative concentration. A complete explanation should distinguish whether the question concerns how much dissolves, how much is present, or how concentration changes.

Choose equations only after defining the process. M=n/V describes concentration; M₁V₁=M₂V₂ models dilution with conserved solute; neither by itself predicts solubility.

Use calculations together with particle reasoning so the numerical result and physical model agree.

Vocabulary: solution model; molarity; dilution; solubility; transfer.

Worked example with reasoning

A student dilutes a stock solution and calculates a lower molarity. The particle model agrees: solute count stays constant while solvent addition increases total volume.

Guided question

Given three scenarios, decide whether each is primarily a solubility, molarity, or dilution problem and justify the model choice.
