VSEPR Model-Building Investigation

Use safe physical or paper models to test shape and polarity predictions against structural evidence.

Weekly EvidenceChemistry CourseScience HallArchaemenesPreviousNext

Explicit science instruction

This investigation compares Lewis structures, electron-domain counts, three-dimensional models, and predicted net dipoles. The goal is to preserve the reasoning chain from representation to prediction.

For each molecule, record domain count before building the model. Then note whether the three-dimensional arrangement allows bond dipoles to cancel.

Model kits and paper geometries are representations; they do not show actual electron density or molecular vibration.

Vocabulary: model validation; symmetry; dipole cancellation; evidence table; representation.

Worked example with reasoning

For BF₃, three bonding domains produce a trigonal-planar arrangement. Equal B–F bond dipoles are arranged symmetrically, so their vector sum is approximately zero in the idealized model.

Guided question

Build or sketch CH₄ and NH₃. Which change—the number of bonds or the presence of a lone pair—most directly changes their molecular shapes?

Independent work

  1. Create a table for at least six molecules: Lewis structure, domain count, geometry, bond polarity, molecular polarity.
  2. Build or fold safe paper models for three entries.
  3. Record one disagreement between an initial sketch and the 3-D model and revise your explanation.

Investigation / Data Lab

Preserve the Lewis structures, domain counts, model sketches, polarity predictions, uncertainty, and one revision.

Misconception check

A physical model is not evidence by itself unless its construction rules are connected to an evidence-based molecular model.

Formative understanding check

What observation in your model most directly supports dipole cancellation?

Reveal response

A symmetric arrangement of equivalent bond-dipole directions supports cancellation in the idealized vector model.

Public self-check only.

Mentor evidence conference

Ask Archaemenes to audit the representation chain and identify any unsupported leap.

Teacher guidance

Foundation: Use preprinted geometry templates.

Core: Complete six model/evidence entries and revise one prediction.

Extended: Represent dipole directions as vectors and discuss where ideal symmetry may fail in real molecules.
