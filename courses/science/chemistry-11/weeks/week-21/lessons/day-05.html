Mechanisms & Catalysts Synthesis

Integrate elementary steps, rate control, catalysts, and evidence into a defensible pathway model.

Weekly EvidenceChemistry CourseScience Hall

Safety boundary

Use energy-profile diagrams, supplied datasets, and approved simulations only. No catalyst/reaction demonstrations are required.

Explicit chemistry instruction

Mechanistic chemistry explains how a reaction may occur, not just the before/after stoichiometry.

A good mechanism is consistent with the overall equation, intermediates, rate behavior, and energy evidence.

Catalysts alter the route through that mechanism while preserving overall reactant/product energetics.

Vocabulary: pathway model; evidence; catalyst; rate control; model limit.

Worked example

A catalyzed mechanism may add extra elementary steps yet react faster because the highest activation barrier is lower.

Guided question

Write a CER comparing a catalyzed and uncatalyzed pathway using an energy profile and rate evidence.

Independent work

  1. Analyze one mechanism.
  2. Interpret one energy profile.
  3. Write one catalyst CER.
  4. Record one corrected misconception.

Spiral retrieval bridge

  1. From Week 20: distinguish collision frequency from successful-collision fraction.
  2. From Week 19: interpret rate data from graph slope.
  3. From Week 14: read a balanced equation without assuming it reveals the reaction mechanism.

Misconception check

More mechanism steps do not necessarily mean a slower reaction.

Formative check

What must remain unchanged when a catalyst changes the pathway?

Reveal response

The overall reactants/products and overall thermodynamic energy difference.
