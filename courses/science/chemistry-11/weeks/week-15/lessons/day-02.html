Molar Mass & Mass–Mole Conversion

Calculate molar mass from formula and convert between grams and moles.

Weekly EvidenceChemistry CourseScience Hall

Safety boundary

Use paper models, supplied data, and approved simulations only. No unsupervised reactive mixtures are required.

Explicit chemistry instruction

Molar mass is the mass of one mole of a substance, usually expressed in g/mol.

For a compound, add atomic masses according to subscripts. Parentheses and coefficients must be interpreted carefully.

Mass-to-mole conversion uses n=m/M. The molar mass belongs to the chemical formula, not to the sample size.

Vocabulary: molar mass; formula mass; grams; moles; conversion factor.

Worked example

H₂O has molar mass about 18.02 g/mol. A 36.04 g sample therefore contains 2.00 mol.

Guided question

Calculate the molar mass of CO₂ and determine moles in 22.0 g.

Independent work

  1. Calculate molar masses for four formulas.
  2. Convert three masses to moles.
  3. Convert two mole amounts to grams.

Misconception check

Do not multiply molar mass by a coefficient unless the question is about that many moles of substance.

Formative check

What determines a compound's molar mass?

Reveal response

Its chemical formula and constituent atomic masses.
