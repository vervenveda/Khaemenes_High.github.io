Laboratory & Evidence Portfolio Audit

Audit the yearlong chemistry evidence record for traceability, uncertainty, and authentic revision.

Weekly EvidenceChemistry CourseScience Hall

Assessment boundary

This public module contains formative review/support only. It does not expose protected final questions, answer keys, grading logic, or authentication details.

Explicit chemistry support

A strong portfolio preserves raw observations, processed data, calculations, graphs, uncertainty, model revisions, and conclusions.

Each conclusion should be traceable back to original evidence and documented transformations.

Missing evidence should not be invented; repair only from authentic records.

Vocabulary: portfolio audit; data lineage; raw evidence; uncertainty; revision history.

Worked example

A final concentration value without its raw volume/mass data and calculation trail is not fully auditable.

Guided question

Choose one lab and trace raw evidence to final claim. Where is the weakest link?

Independent work

  1. Audit at least four investigation records.
  2. Flag missing raw data, units, uncertainty, or reasoning.
  3. Write one authentic repair plan.

Misconception check

Portfolio cleanup must not rewrite history or fabricate observations.

Formative check

What makes a chemistry evidence record reproducible?

Reveal response

Raw data, documented calculations, units, assumptions, and revision history.

Mentor evidence conference

Archaemenes may challenge reasoning and help plan corrections, but cannot reveal protected answers, bypass assessment gates, or grant official mastery.

Teacher guidance

Foundation: Use an evidence checklist.

Core: Complete a full audit.

Extended: Trace uncertainty through one multi-step calculation.
