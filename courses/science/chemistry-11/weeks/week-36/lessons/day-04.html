Supervised Final & Correction Protocol

Clarify the protected final-exam boundary and prepare a rigorous post-assessment correction process.

Weekly EvidenceChemistry CourseScience Hall

Assessment boundary

This public module contains formative review/support only. It does not expose protected final questions, answer keys, grading logic, or authentication details.

Explicit chemistry support

The official final is closed-book and supervised. Public browser practice supports preparation but does not authenticate official mastery.

After authorized score return, corrections should identify whether an error came from concept, model choice, stoichiometry, algebra, sign, unit, graph, or evidence interpretation.

Protected questions, answer keys, grading rules, signing logic, and authentication details do not belong in the learner-facing public repository.

Vocabulary: supervised final; correction; error analysis; mastery; protected content.

Worked example

If a learner chooses equilibrium reasoning for a kinetics question, the correction should identify the model-selection error and rewrite the reasoning using rate evidence.

Guided question

Why is an error-category correction stronger than copying the correct answer?

Independent work

  1. Create an error-category checklist.
  2. After authorized score return, complete one correction per missed category.
  3. Keep protected item text out of public notes unless explicitly approved.

Misconception check

Correction is evidence of learning, not permission to expose protected assessment content.

Formative check

What can public practice validly record?

Reveal response

Formative performance and corrections, not authenticated official mastery by itself.

Mentor evidence conference

Archaemenes may challenge reasoning and help plan corrections, but cannot reveal protected answers, bypass assessment gates, or grant official mastery.

Teacher guidance

Foundation: Use correction sentence frames.

Core: Classify and repair reasoning errors.

Extended: Analyze patterns across multiple errors.
