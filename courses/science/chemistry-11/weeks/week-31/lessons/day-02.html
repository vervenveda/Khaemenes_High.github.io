Buffer Capacity & Composition

Interpret how amount and ratio of conjugate partners influence buffer behavior.

Weekly EvidenceChemistry CourseScience Hall

Safety boundary

Use buffer simulations and supplied datasets only. Do not use biological or medical dosing contexts or unsupervised chemical mixtures.

Explicit chemistry instruction

Buffer capacity increases with the total amount of buffering species available.

The acid/base ratio influences the pH region where the buffer is most effective.

Two buffers can have similar initial pH but very different capacities if their total concentrations differ.

Vocabulary: capacity; composition; ratio; concentration; buffering range.

Worked example

A dilute and a concentrated buffer can begin at the same pH, but the concentrated one can absorb more added acid/base before pH shifts strongly.

Guided question

Why doesn't initial pH alone determine buffer capacity?

Independent work

  1. Compare three buffer compositions.
  2. Rank expected capacity.
  3. Explain one ratio-versus-total-concentration distinction.

Misconception check

Buffer capacity and buffer pH are related but not the same property.

Formative check

What mainly increases capacity at fixed ratio?

Reveal response

Increasing the total amount/concentration of buffering species.
