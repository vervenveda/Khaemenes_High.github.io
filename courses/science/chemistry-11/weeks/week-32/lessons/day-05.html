Free-Energy Synthesis

Integrate enthalpy, entropy, temperature, equilibrium, and kinetics without conflating them.

Weekly EvidenceChemistry CourseScience Hall

Safety boundary

Use supplied graphs and calculations only. No new reactive chemistry is required.

Explicit chemistry instruction

Thermodynamic favorability depends on ΔG; equilibrium occurs where net driving force vanishes; kinetics controls rate.

Temperature can shift favorability because it scales the entropy term.

A complete explanation states conditions and keeps direction separate from speed.

Vocabulary: thermodynamics; equilibrium; kinetics; temperature; driving force.

Worked example

A reaction can have ΔG<0 yet proceed slowly if the activation barrier is large.

Guided question
