Energy vs Entropy Tradeoffs

Explain why enthalpy alone cannot predict every spontaneous direction.

Weekly EvidenceChemistry CourseScience Hall

Safety boundary

Use particle-distribution simulations and supplied data only. No new chemical mixing is required.

Explicit chemistry instruction

Some endothermic processes can proceed because entropy gain is large enough.

Some exothermic processes may still be limited by other thermodynamic factors.

Spontaneity is a thermodynamic direction statement, not a speed statement.

Vocabulary: spontaneity; enthalpy; entropy; direction; kinetics.

Worked example

Ice can melt above its melting point even though melting absorbs heat because the combined thermodynamic balance favors the liquid state.

Guided question

Why can a spontaneous process be slow?

Independent work

  1. Classify examples by enthalpy/entropy tendencies.
  2. Separate spontaneity from rate.
  3. Write one bounded direction claim.

Misconception check

Spontaneous does not mean instantaneous.

Formative check

What topic controls speed rather than thermodynamic direction?

Reveal response

Kinetics/activation barriers.
