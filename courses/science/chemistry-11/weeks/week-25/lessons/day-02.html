Phase Change & Entropy

Relate phase and temperature to qualitative entropy trends.

Weekly EvidenceChemistry CourseScience Hall

Safety boundary

Use particle-distribution simulations and supplied data only. No new chemical mixing is required.

Explicit chemistry instruction

Solids constrain particle positions more than liquids; gases generally allow many more positional arrangements.

Melting and vaporization therefore usually increase entropy of the substance.

Cooling or condensation can reduce entropy locally while total surroundings/system effects still matter.

Vocabulary: phase; entropy change; melting; vaporization; surroundings.

Worked example

Liquid water becoming vapor greatly increases positional freedom and typically increases entropy of the water.

Guided question

Does freezing always mean the entropy of the entire universe decreases?

Independent work

  1. Rank solid/liquid/gas entropy.
  2. Analyze three phase changes.
  3. Distinguish system from surroundings.

Misconception check

A local entropy decrease can occur while total entropy still increases overall.

Formative check

Which phase usually has greatest molecular positional freedom?

Reveal response

Gas.
