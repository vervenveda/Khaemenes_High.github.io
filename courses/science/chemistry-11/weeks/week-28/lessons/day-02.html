Ksp Concept & Ion Products

Interpret a simplified solubility-product expression and compare ion-product reasoning qualitatively.

Weekly EvidenceChemistry CourseScience Hall

Safety boundary

Use solubility curves, supplied datasets, and simulations only. Do not perform unsupervised precipitation chemistry or mix household chemicals.

Explicit chemistry instruction

For a sparingly soluble ionic solid, Ksp describes the equilibrium relationship among dissolved ion concentrations.

A current ion product Qsp can be compared with Ksp: QspKsp suggests precipitation is favored in the simplified model.

Stoichiometric exponents come from the dissolution equation.

Vocabulary: Ksp; ion product; dissolution equilibrium; precipitation; stoichiometry.

Worked example

For MX(s)⇌M⁺+X⁻, Ksp=[M⁺][X⁻]. For MX₂, the X concentration term is squared.

Guided question

Write a Ksp expression for M(OH)₂(s)⇌M²⁺+2OH⁻.

Independent work

  1. Write four Ksp expressions.
  2. Compare Qsp and Ksp qualitatively.
  3. Predict dissolve/precipitate direction.

Misconception check

A small Ksp does not mean zero solubility.

Formative check

What does Qsp>Ksp suggest?

Reveal response

The dissolved-ion product exceeds equilibrium and precipitation is favored.
