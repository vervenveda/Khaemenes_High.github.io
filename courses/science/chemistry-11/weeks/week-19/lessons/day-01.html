Reaction Rate as Change Over Time

Define reaction rate from concentration or amount change over time.

Weekly EvidenceChemistry CourseScience Hall

Safety boundary

Use supplied kinetics datasets and approved simulations. No unsupervised reactive mixtures are required.

Explicit chemistry instruction

Reaction rate measures how quickly a measurable quantity changes. In solution kinetics, concentration change over time is a common observable.

Average rate over an interval is Δquantity/Δt. Sign conventions depend on whether a reactant is disappearing or product is forming.

Always identify the measured species and units; a rate number without context is incomplete.

Vocabulary: reaction rate; concentration; interval; average rate; sign convention.

Worked example

If reactant concentration falls from 0.80 M to 0.50 M in 10 s, average disappearance rate magnitude is 0.030 M/s.

Guided question

A product concentration rises from 0.10 M to 0.34 M in 8 s. Find average formation rate.

Independent work

  1. Calculate four average rates.
  2. Label species and units.
  3. Explain why reactant and product signs can differ.

Misconception check

A faster reaction does not mean a larger final amount; rate and extent are different.

Formative check

What two quantities define an average reaction rate?

Reveal response

Change in a measured quantity and the elapsed time.
