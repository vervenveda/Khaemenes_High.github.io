pOH, Kw & Acid/Base Balance

Connect hydronium and hydroxide concentrations in water.

Weekly EvidenceChemistry CourseScience Hall

Safety boundary

Use virtual titration or teacher-supervised neutralization in a properly equipped lab only. Never titrate household cleaners or unknown solutions.

Explicit chemistry instruction

At 25°C in the simplified model, Kw=[H3O+][OH-]=1.0×10^-14.

pH+pOH≈14 under those conditions.

Temperature affects Kw, so the familiar 14 relation has a stated condition.

Vocabulary: pOH; Kw; hydronium; hydroxide; neutral.

Worked example

If pOH=4.0 at 25°C, pH≈10.0.

Guided question

A solution has [OH-]=1×10^-5 M. Find pOH and approximate pH at 25°C.

Independent work

  1. Solve three pH/pOH pairs.
  2. Convert one [OH-] value.
  3. State the temperature assumption.

Misconception check

Neutral does not universally mean pH 7 at every temperature.

Formative check

What temperature condition usually accompanies pH+pOH=14?

Reveal response

About 25°C.
