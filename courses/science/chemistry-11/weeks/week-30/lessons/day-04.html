Virtual Titration Investigation

Use a titration curve to locate equivalence and evaluate indicator choice.

Weekly EvidenceChemistry CourseScience Hall

Safety boundary

Use virtual titration or teacher-supervised neutralization in a properly equipped lab only. Never titrate household cleaners or unknown solutions.

Explicit chemistry instruction

A titration curve plots pH against titrant volume.

The steep region helps locate equivalence for many strong acid/base cases.

Indicator selection should match its transition range to the steep pH-change region.

Vocabulary: titration curve; equivalence; indicator range; endpoint; buret.

Worked example

If the steep rise centers near 25.0 mL, an indicator changing within that pH jump can provide a useful endpoint.

Guided question

What curve feature is more useful than simply reading the final pH?

Independent work

  1. Preserve raw volume/pH data.
  2. Graph or inspect the titration curve.
  3. Estimate equivalence volume.
  4. Compare two indicator ranges and state uncertainty.

Investigation / Data Lab

Preserve raw titrant-volume/pH data, curve interpretation, equivalence estimate, uncertainty, and one revision.

Misconception check

The highest pH point is not automatically the equivalence point.

Formative check

Where is equivalence inferred on a typical strong acid/base titration curve?

Reveal response

Near the center/inflection of the steep pH-change region.
