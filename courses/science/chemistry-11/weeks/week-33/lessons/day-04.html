Electrochemistry Investigation

Use a virtual galvanic cell to connect half-reactions, voltage, and electron flow.

Weekly EvidenceChemistry CourseScience Hall

Safety boundary

Use a galvanic-cell simulation or teacher-supervised low-risk cells only. No mains electricity, concentrated electrolytes, or improvised hazardous cells.

Explicit chemistry instruction

Preserve selected electrodes/electrolytes and measured simulated voltage before interpretation.

Write the oxidation and reduction half-reactions that correspond to the observed electron direction.

Compare two cells while changing only one component where possible.

Vocabulary: virtual cell; half-reaction; voltage; controlled comparison; uncertainty.

Worked example

If Cell A gives 1.10 V and Cell B 0.50 V under comparable conditions, A provides larger electrical energy per coulomb.

Guided question

Which change should be controlled when comparing two electrode combinations?

Independent work

  1. Preserve cell setup and voltage.
  2. Write half-reactions.
  3. Trace electron/ion paths.
  4. Compare two cells and state one uncertainty.

Investigation / Data Lab

Use simulation or supervised low-risk cells only. Preserve setup, voltage, half-reactions, comparison, uncertainty, and one revision.

Misconception check

A larger voltage does not prove every other battery-performance metric is better.

Formative check

What raw evidence should be saved before ranking cells?

Reveal response

The cell composition/setup and measured voltage under stated conditions.

Mentor evidence conference

Ask Archaemenes to audit electron flow, electrode roles, voltage interpretation, and energy accounting.

Teacher guidance

Foundation: Use supplied simulation data.

Core: Link cell setup to redox roles and voltage.

Extended: Compare model predictions with nonstandard conditions.
