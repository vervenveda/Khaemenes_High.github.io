Kinetics Inquiry Defense

Defend the final rate model and explain what evidence would change your conclusion.

Weekly EvidenceChemistry CourseScience Hall

Safety boundary

Use supplied datasets and approved simulations. Any physical demonstration must be teacher-supervised and explicitly classroom-safe; no improvised reactive mixtures.

Explicit chemistry instruction

A complete inquiry defense includes the question, competing models, design, raw evidence, analysis, residuals, uncertainty, and revision.

The conclusion should be proportional to the data range and controls actually tested.

Name one additional experiment or variable that could distinguish remaining ambiguity.

Vocabulary: defense; model revision; uncertainty; scope; next test.

Worked example

A proportional concentration-rate model may be supported from 0.10–0.40 M without claiming it remains valid at every possible concentration.

Guided question

Write a CER defending one rate model and identifying one condition that could overturn it.

Independent work

  1. Submit the inquiry evidence chain.
  2. Write a final CER.
  3. Identify one limitation.
  4. Propose one next discriminating test.

Spiral retrieval bridge

  1. From Week 21: compare a proposed mechanism with observed rate behavior.
  2. From Week 20: isolate one condition in a controlled comparison.
  3. From Week 19: use slope/residual evidence rather than visual impression alone.

Misconception check

Good science can end with a qualified model rather than absolute certainty.

Formative check

What makes an inquiry conclusion appropriately bounded?

Reveal response

It states the evidence, tested range, uncertainty, and what remains untested.

Mentor evidence conference

Ask Archaemenes to audit controls, competing predictions, residuals, uncertainty, and whether the conclusion exceeds the evidence.

Teacher guidance

Foundation: Use a defense template.

Core: Integrate design, residuals, and revision.

Extended: Evaluate model scope and propose next-step evidence.
