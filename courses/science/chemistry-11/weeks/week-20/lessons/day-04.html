Conditions & Rate Simulation

Use supplied or simulated data to isolate effects of temperature, concentration, and surface area.

Weekly EvidenceChemistry CourseScience Hall

Safety boundary

Use simulations and supplied datasets only unless a teacher provides an explicitly approved classroom-safe demonstration. Do not mix household chemicals or improvise reactive systems.

Explicit chemistry instruction

A controlled comparison changes one condition while holding others fixed.

Preserve raw trial conditions and measured rates before calculating ratios or drawing conclusions.

If several variables change together, causal attribution becomes weaker.

Vocabulary: controlled variable; rate ratio; trial; causal inference; uncertainty.

Worked example

Two trials differ only in concentration and show rates 0.015 and 0.030 M/s, supporting a twofold rate change under that tested condition.

Guided question

Which trial design best isolates a temperature effect?

Independent work

  1. Preserve raw condition/rate data.
  2. Calculate rate ratios.
  3. Identify the controlled variable.
  4. Write one causal claim with uncertainty.

Investigation / Data Lab

Use simulation/supplied data. Preserve raw conditions, measured rates, ratios, uncertainty, and one revision.

Misconception check

A correlation from uncontrolled trials is weaker evidence for cause.

Formative check

What makes a rate comparison causally stronger?

Reveal response

Changing one target variable while controlling relevant others.
