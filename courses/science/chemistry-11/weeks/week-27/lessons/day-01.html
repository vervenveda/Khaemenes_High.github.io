Concentration Stress

Predict how equilibrium composition responds to changing reactant or product concentration.

Weekly EvidenceChemistry CourseScience Hall

Safety boundary

Use virtual equilibrium perturbations and supplied datasets only; no unsupervised color-change chemistry.

Explicit chemistry instruction

A concentration disturbance changes Q immediately while K remains fixed at constant temperature.

The system then evolves in the direction that brings Q back toward K.

Le Châtelier language is a shortcut; Q/K reasoning gives the stronger mechanistic basis.

Vocabulary: stress; concentration; shift; Q; equilibrium position.

Worked example

Adding reactant changes Q immediately and can drive a compensating composition change while K stays fixed.

Guided question

Why does K stay unchanged after a concentration perturbation at constant temperature?

Independent work

  1. Predict four concentration shifts.
  2. Explain one with Q/K.
  3. Separate immediate change from subsequent response.

Misconception check

The system does not 'know' how to oppose stress; rates respond to changed concentrations.

Formative check

What changes immediately after adding reactant: K or Q?

Reveal response

Q changes; K does not at fixed temperature.

Mentor evidence conference

Ask Archaemenes to audit whether Q, K, and rate effects are being kept distinct.

Teacher guidance

Foundation: Use before/after tables.

Core: Predict concentration responses.

Extended: Explain shifts with Q/K.
