Equilibrium Synthesis

Integrate rates, K, Q, and composition into one equilibrium model.

Weekly EvidenceChemistry CourseScience Hall

Safety boundary

Use simulations and supplied concentration data only; no unsupervised reactive mixtures.

Explicit chemistry instruction

Equilibrium reasoning combines kinetics at the point of balance with thermodynamic composition information.

K describes the equilibrium ratio tendency; Q describes the current composition relative to that target.

A complete explanation keeps rate, composition, and direction distinct.

Vocabulary: K; Q; rate; composition; direction.

A system with Q

Misconception check

Equilibrium concepts become misleading when rate and composition are treated as the same thing.

Formative check

What three ideas must be kept distinct?

Reveal response

Rate, equilibrium composition (K), and current-composition direction (Q).
