Equilibrium Simulation Investigation

Use simulation data to test equal-rate and constant-concentration features of equilibrium.

Weekly EvidenceChemistry CourseScience Hall

Safety boundary

Use simulations and supplied concentration data only; no unsupervised reactive mixtures.

Explicit chemistry instruction

Record forward and reverse rates and concentrations over time.

Identify when rate equality emerges and whether concentration curves plateau.

Perturb initial amounts and compare the new approach without assuming the same transient path.

Vocabulary: simulation; rate equality; plateau; transient; uncertainty.

Worked example

A simulation shows rates converging to 0.15 while concentrations level at different values; this is dynamic equilibrium evidence.

Guided question

Which graph directly tests the equal-rate criterion?

Independent work

  1. Preserve raw time/rate/concentration data.
  2. Identify equilibrium interval.
  3. Compare two starting conditions.
  4. Record one limitation.

Investigation / Data Lab

Use an equilibrium simulation to track forward/reverse rates and concentration plateaus.

Preserve raw concentrations/rates, graphs, uncertainty, and one revision.

Misconception check

A flat concentration graph alone does not reveal whether a reversible microscopic process continues; rate evidence strengthens the claim.

Formative check

What two graph features support dynamic equilibrium?

Reveal response

Constant concentrations and equal forward/reverse rates.

Mentor evidence conference

Ask Archaemenes to audit equilibrium assumptions, graph interpretation, and whether the evidence supports the conclusion.

Teacher guidance

Foundation: Use supplied graphs.

Core: Analyze rate and concentration together.

Extended: Compare approach-to-equilibrium transients.
