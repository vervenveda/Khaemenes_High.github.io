Equilibrium Expressions & K

Write and interpret simple equilibrium-constant expressions.

Weekly EvidenceChemistry CourseScience Hall

Safety boundary

Use simulations and supplied concentration data only; no unsupervised reactive mixtures.

Explicit chemistry instruction

For aA+bB⇌cC+dD, an equilibrium expression compares product and reactant concentrations raised to stoichiometric powers in the simplified model.

Pure solids and liquids are commonly omitted from concentration-based K expressions.

K magnitude indicates product- or reactant-favored composition, not reaction speed.

Vocabulary: equilibrium constant; K; expression; stoichiometric exponent; composition.

Worked example

For A⇌2B, Kc=[B]²/[A].

Guided question

Write Kc for 2A+B⇌3C and explain each exponent.

Independent work

  1. Write four K expressions.
  2. Interpret large/small K qualitatively.
  3. Identify species omitted in simple heterogeneous cases.

Misconception check

Large K does not mean the reaction is fast.

Formative check

What does K mainly describe?

Reveal response

Equilibrium composition tendency at a given temperature.

Mentor evidence conference

Ask Archaemenes to audit equilibrium assumptions, graph interpretation, and whether the evidence supports the conclusion.
