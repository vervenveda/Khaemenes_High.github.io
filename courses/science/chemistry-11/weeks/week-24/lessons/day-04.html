Hess-Cycle Investigation

Use card-model pathways and supplied data to test path independence.

Weekly EvidenceChemistry CourseScience Hall

Safety boundary

Use equation cards, simulations, and supplied energy data only. No combustion or reactive demonstrations are required.

Explicit chemistry instruction

Construct two routes between the same initial and final states.

Add step enthalpies for each route and compare totals.

Any mismatch should be preserved and traced to arithmetic, sign, or data uncertainty.

Vocabulary: Hess cycle; path independence; residual; thermochemical data; audit.

Worked example

Route 1 totals −85 kJ and Route 2 totals −83 kJ using rounded data; the 2 kJ difference should be discussed rather than hidden.

Guided question

What evidence supports path independence?

Independent work

  1. Build two pathway totals.
  2. Calculate a residual.
  3. Trace one discrepancy.
  4. Revise one sign or scaling error if found.

Investigation / Data Lab

Use Hess-cycle cards and bond-energy data to compare two pathways to the same net reaction.

Preserve raw data/cards, calculations, pathway comparison, uncertainty, and one revision.

Misconception check

Exact equality is not required when rounded experimental data are used.

Formative check

What should be compared between two Hess routes?

Reveal response

Their net ΔH for the same initial/final states.

Mentor evidence conference

Ask Archaemenes to audit signs, pathway logic, units, and model limits.

Teacher guidance

Foundation: Use supplied cards.

Core: Complete two paths.

Extended: Analyze residuals and rounding uncertainty.
