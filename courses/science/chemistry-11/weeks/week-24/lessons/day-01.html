Bond Energy as a Model

Estimate reaction-energy change from bonds broken and formed.

Weekly EvidenceChemistry CourseScience Hall

Safety boundary

Use equation cards, simulations, and supplied energy data only. No combustion or reactive demonstrations are required.

Explicit chemistry instruction

Breaking bonds requires energy input; forming bonds releases energy.

Average bond energies can estimate reaction enthalpy by ΣE_broken−ΣE_formed.

Because tabulated bond energies are averages, the estimate is approximate.

Vocabulary: bond energy; bond breaking; bond formation; estimate.

Worked example

Breaking 2 H–H bonds requires more energy than breaking one identical H–H bond; coefficients matter in the accounting.

Guided question

Why can a bond-energy estimate differ from measured ΔH?

Independent work

  1. Count broken/formed bonds for three reactions.
  2. Estimate one ΔH.
  3. State one approximation.

Misconception check

Bond breaking does not release energy; it requires input.

Formative check

What sign contribution does bond formation make in the bookkeeping?

Reveal response

Negative/releasing contribution in the chosen convention.

Mentor evidence conference

Ask Archaemenes to audit signs, pathway logic, units, and model limits.

Teacher guidance

Foundation: Use bond-count tables.

Core: Estimate reaction ΔH.

Extended: Compare estimates with measured values and discuss model error.
