Where Did the Gas Go?

Learn to account for matter inside and outside the defined weighing boundary. No chemicals are required.

Safety and model limitations

This is a synthetic mathematical simulation. Do not cap or seal gas-producing chemical reactions or mix hazardous chemicals. Trapped gas can build pressure. Nothing in this tool measures or produces a real gas.

The fictional initial apparatus and contents have a mass of 120.0 g. The gas-amount slider chooses a hypothetical portion of matter redistributed as gas. In closed accounting, all gas stays within the modeled boundary; in open accounting, that mass leaves the region measured on the balance. All matter is still counted when outside gas is included. The model ignores uncertainty, spillage, gas remaining dissolved and air-flow effects.
