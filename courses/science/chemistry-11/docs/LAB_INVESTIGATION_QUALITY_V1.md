# Chemistry 11 · investigative laboratory standard v1
Khaemenes Academy · academic evidence standard · intended for 36-week audit

A lab title, vocabulary list, or one-trial calculation is **not** by itself evidence of investigative depth. This standard must be used before certifying the course map's >=25% lab inquiry target.

## Required eight anchor investigations (proposed placements; verify weekly engine before release)
1. **Week 01, measurement reliability:** compare repeated safe water-volume readings; characterize systematic versus random measurement differences; use simulated data when equipment is unavailable. No chemical mixing.
2. **Week 03, isotope inference:** use an offline simulated mass-spectrum table with at least three isotopes; calculate weighted mean and explain uncertainty.
3. **Week 05, periodic patterns:** compare element-property data and test whether position alone predicts a chosen trend; identify exceptions.
4. **Week 10, reaction evidence:** use provided synthetic mass data to test mass conservation with uncertainty, no at-home reagents.
5. **Week 15, stoichiometric yield:** compare three or more simulated runs, compute limiting reagent and percent yield.
6. **Week 20, thermal change:** analyze offline time-temperature datasets, distinguish observation from an energy-model claim.
7. **Week 25, equilibrium:** change model parameters in a safe simulation, track repeated predictions and alternate explanations.
8. **Week 32, material design:** evaluate simulated material properties using a defined criterion, constraints and documented tradeoffs.

**Every anchor dossier requires:** clear question/hypothesis, safe protocol or simulation attribution, independent/dependent/controlled variables, at least three trials or a justified alternative, labeled data and units, graph/table, uncertainty or limitation, claim-evidence-reasoning, safe disposal/non-lab alternative, teacher feedback and Academy academic evidence metadata.

**Safety boundary:** Never instruct unsupervised students to heat sealed containers, mix cleaning chemicals, use concentrated acids or bases, produce gas under pressure, or handle radioactive substances. Provide a data-only/offline route with equal conceptual rigor.

**Release:** Proposals only. These investigations require surgical implementation in the relevant actual HTML/JS weekly engine and student tests; this document makes no claim that eight labs have already been installed.
