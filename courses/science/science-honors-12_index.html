What changed

History is no longer the endpoint; it becomes the context that makes advanced science more intelligent.

Scientific Practice

Students ask questions, model systems, plan investigations, analyze data, use mathematics/computation, explain, argue from evidence, and communicate.

Laboratory & Field Boundaries

Home learning is limited to low-risk activities. Hazardous chemical, biological, electrical, pressure, radiation, flame, human/animal subject, or specialized equipment work requires trained supervision and institutional procedures.

Research Integrity

Data stewardship, authorship, conflicts, peer review, collaboration, consent, misconduct, community authority, and knowledge sovereignty are treated as science competencies, not footnotes.

Research Experience

The final year shifts from consuming content to producing a documented research, design, field, data, archive, or public-science project with revision and defense.

Formal 401–603 Program

Senior honors first. Two associate-level years after graduation. One research portfolio across the entire pathway.

Course completion records are local to this browser. Check a course only after its required units, evidence, and assessment have actually been completed.

Eleven permanent competencies

Progress is tracked across distinct abilities rather than collapsed into one misleading “science score.”

Use Not Observed, Developing, and Secure as evidence-based judgments. “Secure” should mean the portfolio contains repeated independent evidence.

Course Assessments

Standards, Sources & Safety

Current external frameworks are used as benchmarks—not as accreditation or endorsement claims.

The original 20-source History of Science Vault is preserved below the current program benchmarks.

Laboratory boundary: a browser curriculum cannot create an OSHA-compliant laboratory, chemical hygiene plan, biosafety program, radiation program, machine shop, IRB, IACUC, or other institutional authorization. Concentrated/reactive chemicals, flame, pressure/vacuum, high voltage/current, radiation sources, cultures/pathogens, hazardous specimens, controlled drugs, human-subject research, animal research, specialized shop equipment, or comparable hazards require appropriate institutions, trained supervision, risk controls, and applicable rules.
