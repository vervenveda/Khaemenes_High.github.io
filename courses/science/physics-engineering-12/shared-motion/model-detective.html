Motion Model Detective

When does a straight-line model fit a set of position measurements, and when is a constant-acceleration curve more defensible?

Start with the right question

Teaching dataset, not experimental observation. All included positions are invented and purpose-built to practice fitting. Never report these values as measured motion-sensor results. Real experiments require documented apparatus, consent for any video data, safety procedures and uncertainty analysis.

Linear model: x=b+mt. Quadratic model: x=b+mt+ct². Both are fit by least squares to the first five points t=0,1,2,3,4 s. The sixth at t=5 s is held out from fitting. Residual = observed position − predicted position, with unit meters. Training RMSE = √(mean squared training residual), also in meters.

Models differ in complexity: quadratic has three parameters and may fit training data better because it is more flexible. Also examine the holdout, residual pattern and plausible motion mechanism.
