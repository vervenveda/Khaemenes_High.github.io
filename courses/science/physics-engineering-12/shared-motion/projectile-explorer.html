Projectile Explorer

Make predictions about horizontal and vertical components in a safe, fully offline two-dimensional model.

1. Experimental safety and assumptions

Digital simulation only. Do not launch or drop hard objects, operate launchers, run hazardous experiments or place people near moving objects. This model assumes a point object, level ground y=0, x horizontal, y upward, constant downward gravity, no air drag, no spin and no obstacles. It does not predict safe real-world trajectories.

vx0 = speed × cos(angle), vy0 = speed × sin(angle); x(t)=vx0t; y(t)=height+vy0t−gt²/2. The computed impact time is the nonnegative solution of y=0, and range is vx0 times that time. All plotted values are generated by these equations, not physical measurements.
