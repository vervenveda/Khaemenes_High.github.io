Motion Graph Studio

Explore the meaning of position, velocity, acceleration, signed displacement and distance traveled in two constant-velocity reference frames.

Model: x(t)=x₀+v₀t+½at², v(t)=v₀+at, x′(t)=x(t)−ut, v′(t)=v(t)−u. Motion is one-dimensional with constant acceleration.

1. Predict before running

Pick one variable to change. What will happen to the position slope, velocity slope, and signed area in the velocity–time graph?

Teal = observer frame; amber = ground frame

This is a synthetic, idealized algebraic simulator, not a physical motion sensor. No launchers, motor vehicles, outdoor stunts or hazardous moving equipment are required. No personal data leave your browser.
