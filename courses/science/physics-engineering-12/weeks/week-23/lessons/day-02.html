SHM Graphs & Phase

What causes repeated motion around equilibrium?

Weekly EvidencePhysics CourseScience Hall

Explicit physics instruction

Position, velocity, and acceleration vary in phase-shifted ways during ideal oscillation.

At maximum displacement, speed is momentarily zero; at equilibrium, speed magnitude is often greatest.

Graph reading should distinguish amplitude, period, phase, and slope rather than treating the curve as a path through space.

Core relation: f=1/T; for ideal SHM, restoring force ∝ −displacement.

Worked example

A position-time graph repeats every 2.0 s, so T=2.0 s and f=0.50 Hz.

Guided question

Where on a position-time graph is speed likely greatest in ideal SHM?

Independent work

  1. Solve three period/frequency or graph questions.
  2. Annotate one oscillation graph.
  3. State one ideal-model limitation.

Misconception check

Greater amplitude does not automatically mean greater frequency in the ideal small-amplitude SHM model.

Formative check

If T doubles, what happens to f?

Reveal response

Frequency halves because f=1/T.
