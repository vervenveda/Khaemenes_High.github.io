Explicit physics instruction

Oscillation synthesis links restoring behavior, graphs, period/frequency, and energy transfer.

Choose whether the question is asking about timing, phase, force, or energy before selecting a model.

Greater amplitude does not automatically mean greater frequency in the ideal small-amplitude SHM model.

Core relation: f=1/T; for ideal SHM, restoring force ∝ −displacement.

Worked example

An oscillator completes one cycle every 0.50 s, so f=1/0.50=2.0 Hz. Ten cycles would take about 5.0 s if the period remains constant.
