Oscillator Investigation

What causes repeated motion around equilibrium?

Weekly EvidencePhysics CourseScience Hall

Explicit physics instruction

This investigation measures period and repeatability.

Use a small safe pendulum or approved oscillator simulation. Measure period over multiple cycles and compare repeated trials.

Time several cycles at once to reduce reaction-time error per cycle; preserve raw timing data.

Core relation: f=1/T; for ideal SHM, restoring force ∝ −displacement.

Worked example

If 10 cycles take 12.4 s, T=1.24 s. Repeat trials can be averaged and spread reported.

Misconception check

Greater amplitude does not automatically mean greater frequency in the ideal small-amplitude SHM model.

Formative check

If T doubles, what happens to f?

Reveal response

Frequency halves because f=1/T.
