Energy in Oscillation

What causes repeated motion around equilibrium?

Weekly EvidencePhysics CourseScience Hall

Explicit physics instruction

Energy shifts between potential-like storage and kinetic energy while total mechanical energy remains constant in the ideal undamped model.

At turning points, kinetic energy is minimum and stored potential energy maximum; at equilibrium, the reverse occurs.

Damping transfers mechanical energy into other forms, reducing amplitude over time.

Core relation: f=1/T; for ideal SHM, restoring force ∝ −displacement.

Worked example

At an ideal oscillator’s maximum displacement, v=0 at that instant, so kinetic energy is minimum while stored energy is maximum.

Guided question

Misconception check

Greater amplitude does not automatically mean greater frequency in the ideal small-amplitude SHM model.

Formative check

If T doubles, what happens to f?

Reveal response

Frequency halves because f=1/T.
