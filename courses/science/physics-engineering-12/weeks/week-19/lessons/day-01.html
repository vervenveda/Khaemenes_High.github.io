Angular Position, Velocity & Acceleration

How can angular quantities describe rotational motion?

Weekly EvidencePhysics CourseScience Hall

Explicit physics instruction

Angular position describes orientation; angular velocity ω=Δθ/Δt and angular acceleration α=Δω/Δt describe rotational change.

Points on a rigid body share angular displacement, angular velocity, and angular acceleration, but their linear distance, speed, and tangential acceleration depend on radius.

The bridge between angular and linear motion is s=rθ and v=rω when θ is measured in radians.

Core relations: s=rθ; v=rω; a_t=rα.

Worked example

A wheel turns through 3.0 rad in 0.50 s, so average angular velocity is 6.0 rad/s.

Guided question

A disk rotates 4.0 rad in 2.0 s. Find average angular velocity.

Independent work

  1. Solve three angular-motion calculations.
  2. Draw or interpret one rotational graph.
  3. State one model assumption or limitation.

Misconception check

Same angular speed does not mean same linear speed for points at different radii.

Formative check

What quantity must be known, in addition to ω, to determine a point’s linear speed?

Reveal response

Its distance r from the rotation axis.
