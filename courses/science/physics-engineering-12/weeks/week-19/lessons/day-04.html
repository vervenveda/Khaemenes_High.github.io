How can angular quantities describe rotational motion?

Use a turntable/wheel simulation or supplied rotation dataset. Track angular position and two radii; compare predicted and measured linear speeds.

Core relations: s=rθ; v=rω; a_t=rα.

Investigation / Data Lab

Use a turntable/wheel simulation or supplied rotation dataset. Track angular position and two radii; compare predicted and measured linear speeds.

Evidence record: raw data, calculations, graph, uncertainty, and one revision.

Misconception check

Same angular speed does not mean same linear speed for points at different radii.

Formative check

What quantity must be known, in addition to ω, to determine a point’s linear speed?

Reveal response

Its distance r from the rotation axis.

Teacher guidance

Vocabulary and data-lineage extension

Vocabulary: angular displacement; radius; residual; uncertainty; calibration.

Preserve raw time, angular position, and radius values before calculating ω or tangential speed. If angle comes from image tracking, record the angular calibration and frame timing. If radius is measured, its uncertainty contributes directly to uncertainty in v=rω.

Compare predicted v=rω with simulated or measured point speed at two radii. Define residual r_v=v_observed−v_predicted and look for systematic dependence on radius. A growing residual with radius may indicate calibration or rigid-body-model limitations rather than random noise.

Evidence revision / CER

Write a CER deciding whether the data support the angular–linear model across the tested radii. Cite residuals, uncertainty, and one method revision.

Spiral retrieval

Connect Week 05 residual reasoning with today's rotation-data comparison.

Residual and uncertainty practice

Suppose ω=5.0±0.1 rad/s and r=0.20±0.01 m. The predicted tangential speed is 1.00 m/s. A simulated value of 1.02 m/s is likely consistent with input uncertainty, while 1.30 m/s suggests either a calibration issue or model mismatch.

Repeat the comparison at r=0.40 m. If the residual roughly doubles with radius, suspect an angular-speed calibration error because v=rω magnifies a shared ω error with r. If the residual stays nearly constant in m/s, investigate a different source. Pattern analysis is stronger than merely labeling each trial pass/fail.

Preserve the raw θ(t) data so another reviewer can recompute interval ω values. A processed speed list without its angular source prevents independent validation of the relation being tested.

Calibration transfer check

Recalculate one trial after a small radius-calibration change and state whether the observed residual shifts as predicted. This distinguishes calibration sensitivity from random variation.

Transfer note

Explain which extra measurement would best separate radius error from angular-speed calibration error.
