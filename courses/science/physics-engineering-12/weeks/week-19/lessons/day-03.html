Rotational Graphs & Model Limits

How can angular quantities describe rotational motion?

Weekly EvidencePhysics CourseScience Hall

Explicit physics instruction

Angular-position versus time slope is angular velocity; angular-velocity versus time slope is angular acceleration.

A constant angular velocity produces a linear θ(t) graph, while constant angular acceleration produces a changing ω(t).

Same angular speed does not mean same linear speed for points at different radii.

Core relations: s=rθ; v=rω; a_t=rα.

Worked example

If ω rises from 2 to 8 rad/s in 3 s, α=(8−2)/3=2 rad/s².

Guided question

What does the slope of an ω–t graph represent, and what units must it have?

Independent work

  1. Solve three angular-motion calculations.
  2. Draw or interpret one rotational graph.
  3. State one model assumption or limitation.

Misconception check

Same angular speed does not mean same linear speed for points at different radii.

Formative check

What quantity must be known, in addition to ω, to determine a point’s linear speed?

Reveal response

Its distance r from the rotation axis.
