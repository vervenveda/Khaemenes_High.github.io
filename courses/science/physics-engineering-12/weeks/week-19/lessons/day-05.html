Rotational Kinematics Synthesis

How can angular quantities describe rotational motion?

Weekly EvidencePhysics CourseScience Hall

Explicit physics instruction

A complete rotational-kinematics explanation connects angular variables, radius, graph evidence, and model assumptions.

Use the Friday synthesis to select the correct relationship rather than treating angular and linear variables as interchangeable.

Same angular speed does not mean same linear speed for points at different radii.

Core relations: s=rθ; v=rω; a_t=rα.

Worked example

A point at twice the radius shares the same ω but has twice v; this is the key transfer from angular to linear description.

Guided question

Build a representation chain from θ(t) to ω to v for one rotating point.

Formative check

What quantity must be known, in addition to ω, to determine a point’s linear speed?

Reveal response

Its distance r from the rotation axis.
