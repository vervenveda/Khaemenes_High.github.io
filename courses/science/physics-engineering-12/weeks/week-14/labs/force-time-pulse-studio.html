Scientific scope and safety

Digital experiment only. Do not perform collisions, test protection on living beings, drop masses or move loaded carts. All values are synthetic; the tool cannot assess real injury, equipment ratings or actual maximum contact force.

A prescribed net impulse is the signed force–time area. A rectangle has constant force J/Δt; a symmetric linear triangle has peak 2J/Δt and mean J/Δt. Those peaks belong ONLY to the selected assumed waveforms, never to measured real-world impacts.
