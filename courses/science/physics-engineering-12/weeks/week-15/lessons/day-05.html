Momentum Conservation Synthesis

Defend a momentum model across collision and recoil contexts.

Weekly EvidencePhysics CourseScience Hall

Explicit physics instruction

A mature momentum solution begins with system choice and external-impulse reasoning, then uses signed vector accounting.

Momentum and kinetic energy answer different questions. Momentum may be conserved in a collision while kinetic energy changes form.

Use corrections as evidence: preserve one sign or system-boundary error and annotate the revised model.

Vocabulary: system; impulse; vector conservation; kinetic energy; revision.

Worked example with reasoning

A sticking collision conserves system momentum but usually reduces kinetic energy because some mechanical energy becomes deformation, thermal energy, or sound.

Guided question

Write a CER defending whether momentum conservation is an appropriate model for a supplied collision dataset.

  4. Record one corrected system-boundary or sign error.

Spiral retrieval bridge

  4. From Week 07: connect net force with momentum change over time.

Misconception check

Conservation of momentum is not a claim that no forces act; internal forces can be large.

Formative understanding check

Reveal response

Define the system and justify why net external impulse is negligible.
