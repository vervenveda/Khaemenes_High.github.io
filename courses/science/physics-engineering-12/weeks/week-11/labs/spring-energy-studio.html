Sovereign Virtual Energy Lab · Week 11

Spring Energy Studio

Track stored elastic potential energy, kinetic energy and prescribed transfer to other forms during a partial spring release.

Assumptions and safety

Digital-only investigation. No dropping objects, bending powerful springs, launching projectiles, using heavy ramps or moving machinery. All energy values are synthetic and must not be used for device safety or physical engineering.

In the Hooke-linear spring approximation, U_s=½kx², with k in N/m and x in METERS. The simulation converts displayed centimeters into meters and assumes a body released from rest while spring compression decreases without reversing. Released potential energy becomes kinetic energy and a user-prescribed fraction transferred to other forms. The fraction is an instructional assumption, not a measured drag, friction, or damping coefficient.
