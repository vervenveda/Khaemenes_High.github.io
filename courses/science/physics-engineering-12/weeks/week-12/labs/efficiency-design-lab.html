Energy Efficiency Design Lab

Compare useful output, other transfers and simultaneous design criteria using exact energy budgets.

Model rules and safety

Safe, synthetic investigation. No strenuous stair test, heavy load lifting, live electrical equipment or improvised machinery is required. Outputs are algebraic predictions, not performance ratings or engineering safety approvals.

Efficiency = useful output energy / energy input (0–100%) in an ideal no-stored-energy-change accounting model. E_input=E_useful+E_other and each average power uses the same elapsed time. An actual device could have changing stored energy and other unmodeled flows.
