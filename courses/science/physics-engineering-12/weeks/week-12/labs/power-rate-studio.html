Work Rate & Power Studio

Explore how elapsed time affects average power while total energy stays fixed.

Model rules and safety

Safe, synthetic investigation. No strenuous stair test, heavy load lifting, live electrical equipment or improvised machinery is required. Outputs are algebraic predictions, not performance ratings or engineering safety approvals.

Average power = energy transferred / positive elapsed time, P_avg=E/t. This model does not identify instantaneous power or include measured machinery behavior. It is an invented energy transfer during one specified interval.
