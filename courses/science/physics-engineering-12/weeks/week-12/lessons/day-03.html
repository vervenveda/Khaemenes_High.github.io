Efficiency Is a Ratio, Not a Speed

Objective: Calculate transfer efficiency and distinguish it from power and total energy.

45–60 minutes · Offline learning · Local evidence

MondayTuesdayWednesdayThursdayFriday

Scientific reading

Write the ratio

For an appropriately defined interval and output, efficiency η=E_useful/E_input, or η%=100×E_useful/E_input. Provided energies refer to the same comparable input/output account, equivalent mean power ratio η=P_useful/P_input follows because the time cancels. In this simplified model, 0≤η≤1. If a calculation exceeds 100% on this basis, reconsider the inputs, stored energy, definition of useful output or instrument measurements.

A useful distinction

Two devices may share the same useful energy and time while differing in efficiency because one demands more input. Conversely, two devices may have identical efficiency but different input energies and powers. Efficiency answers 'what fraction was useful?', whereas power answers 'how fast did this particular transfer occur?' No single number answers both questions.

Solve the balance with decimals

If E_input=400 J and η=75%, E_useful=0.75×400=300 J and E_other=100 J. With t=5 s, P_in=80 W and P_useful=60 W. A second device at η=60% with the same input and interval has useful energy 240 J and useful power 48 W. These outputs are synthetic and do not characterize real products.

Respect the steady-run assumption

This equation accounts for an input energy budget separated into outputs with no net change of stored energy in the device. If a battery, spring or capacitor is charging/discharging internally, changes to stored energy require an additional ledger term. Our model deliberately excludes those complexities but asks students to name them.

All numeric experiments are simulated. Do not lift unsafe loads, operate electrical devices, perform strenuous stair tests, or use these outputs to certify real equipment.

Key vocabulary

efficiency
Ratio of intended useful output energy to input energy.
percentage
A ratio scaled by 100.
energy conversion
Transfer into different forms while total energy remains accounted for.
stored-energy change
Energy accumulated or released within a chosen device boundary.
physical feasibility
Whether a claimed ledger is compatible with conservation and stated boundaries.

Worked physics example

400 J in at 75% useful efficiency yields 300 J useful and 100 J other. At 5 seconds, P_in=80 W and P_useful=60 W. Output cannot exceed input in this no-storage-change model.

Show the system, time interval, conversion, numerical substitutions and units.
