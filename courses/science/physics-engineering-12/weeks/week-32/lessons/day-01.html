Electromagnetic Spectrum & Wave Model

Connect wavelength, frequency, and energy across the electromagnetic spectrum.

Weekly EvidencePhysics CourseScience Hall

Safety boundary

Use simulations, printed optics, and eye-safe light only. Do not direct lasers or intense light at people.

Explicit physics instruction

Electromagnetic waves can travel through vacuum and obey v=fλ, with v≈3.00×10⁸ m/s in vacuum.

Frequency and wavelength are inversely related at fixed wave speed. Different regions of the spectrum are classified primarily by frequency/wavelength, not by different physical laws.

Photon energy increases with frequency; wavelength alone does not determine signal quality or hazard without considering intensity/exposure.

Vocabulary: EM spectrum; wavelength; frequency; photon; vacuum.

Worked example

A 100 MHz radio wave has λ=(3.00×10⁸)/(1.00×10⁸)=3.0 m.

Guided question

A wave has frequency 6.0×10¹⁴ Hz. Estimate wavelength in vacuum.

Independent work

  1. Solve three v=fλ problems.
  2. Order spectrum regions by frequency.
  3. State one limitation of using wavelength alone to judge risk.

Misconception check

All electromagnetic waves travel at the same speed only in vacuum, not necessarily in materials.

Formative check

What happens to wavelength as frequency increases in vacuum?

Reveal response

It decreases.
