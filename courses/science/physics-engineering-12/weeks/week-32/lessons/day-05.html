EM Waves & Information Synthesis

Integrate wave physics, optics, and information-transfer models.

Weekly EvidencePhysics CourseScience Hall

Safety boundary

Use simulations, printed optics, and eye-safe light only. Do not direct lasers or intense light at people.

Explicit physics instruction

Electromagnetic communication combines a physical carrier with encoding, transmission, interaction with matter, and decoding.

A strong model identifies which quantity carries information, which physics controls propagation, and which metric evaluates fidelity.

Engineering choices trade among power, bandwidth, range, resolution, robustness, and safety.

Vocabulary: carrier; encoding; propagation; fidelity; tradeoff.

Worked example

A radio system can use frequency/wavelength physics to choose a carrier while a digital code determines how information is represented on that carrier.

Guided question

Design a simple communication model from source to receiver and label where noise or loss can enter.

Independent work

  1. Solve one EM-wave calculation.
  2. Interpret one optics diagram.
  3. Analyze one digital-error dataset.
  4. Write one design tradeoff.
