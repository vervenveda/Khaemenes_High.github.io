Magnetic Fields & Forces

Model magnetic fields around currents and magnets and predict qualitative force direction.

Weekly EvidencePhysics CourseScience Hall

Safety boundary

Use simulations or supervised low-voltage coil/magnet activities only. Do not use mains electricity, high-current coils, or improvised high-voltage equipment.

Explicit physics instruction

Magnetic fields describe how magnets and moving charges interact. Field lines are a representation of direction and relative strength, not physical strings.

A current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field can experience force whose direction depends on current and field orientation.

Magnetic force on a moving charge is perpendicular to both velocity and field in the ideal model, so it can change direction of motion without doing mechanical work on speed.

Vocabulary: magnetic field; current; magnetic force; moving charge.

Worked example

A positive charge moving perpendicular to a magnetic field experiences a sideways force; reversing charge sign reverses force direction.

Guided question

Why can a magnetic force bend a particle path without changing its speed in the ideal model?

Independent work

  1. Sketch field patterns for a bar magnet and straight current.
  2. Predict force direction in three simple diagrams.
  3. State one limit of field-line drawings.

Misconception check

Magnetic field lines are representations, not material tracks.

Formative check

What motion condition gives zero magnetic force on a moving charge in the simple model?

Reveal response

When velocity is parallel or antiparallel to the field.
