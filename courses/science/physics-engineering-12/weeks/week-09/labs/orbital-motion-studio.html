Calculate a circular orbit's speed, gravitational field and period, and inspect velocity versus inward acceleration.

Physics model and safety

Mathematical simulation only. No rockets, live satellites, physical drop tests, vehicles or unsafe launches. This ideal spherical-central-body model is not spacecraft or safety guidance. It omits atmosphere, nonspherical gravity, third bodies and trajectory maneuvers.

For central gravitational parameter μ=GM and center-to-center distance r=R+h, gravitational acceleration is μ/r² and force on a small test body is mμ/r². For a circular two-body orbit, v=√(μ/r), T=2π√(r³/μ); velocity points tangent while acceleration points inward. Constants: Earth μ=3.986004418×10¹⁴ m³/s², radius 6371 km; Moon μ=4.9048695×10¹² m³/s², radius 1737.4 km.
