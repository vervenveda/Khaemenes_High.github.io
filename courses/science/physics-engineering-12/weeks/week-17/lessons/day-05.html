Semester Mechanics Synthesis

Defend a coherent mechanics model across motion, force, energy, momentum, and center of mass.

Weekly EvidencePhysics CourseScience Hall

Explicit physics instruction

Semester synthesis means choosing the right representation and explaining how models connect, not memorizing an equation list.

A complete defense uses diagrams, equations, units, assumptions, and evidence. Where models disagree with data, preserve and analyze the residual.

Center-of-mass and system-boundary thinking provide a bridge between single-object mechanics and multi-object interactions.

Vocabulary: mechanics synthesis; representation; system boundary; residual; defense.

Worked example with reasoning

A collision case can include a motion graph before impact, a free-body diagram during approach, an energy model for speed, a momentum model for impact, and a center-of-mass description of the two-cart system.

Guided question

Design a representation map for one unfamiliar mechanics scenario using at least three model types.

Misconception check

A strong synthesis is organized around questions and evidence, not around using every formula.
