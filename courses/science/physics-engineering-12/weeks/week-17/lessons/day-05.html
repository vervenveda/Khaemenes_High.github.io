Explicit physics instruction

Semester synthesis means choosing the right representation and explaining how models connect, not memorizing an equation list.

A complete defense uses diagrams, equations, units, assumptions, and evidence. Where models disagree with data, preserve and analyze the residual.

Center-of-mass and system-boundary thinking provide a bridge between single-object mechanics and multi-object interactions.

Vocabulary: mechanics synthesis; representation; system boundary; residual; defense.

Semester synthesis depth extension

A high-quality mechanics synthesis should move among representations without losing the physical meaning of the system. For one complex scenario, combine a motion graph, free-body diagram, energy bar chart, momentum table, and center-of-mass description. Each representation should answer a different question rather than repeat the same claim.

Include at least one cross-check. A speed found from energy can be compared with a kinematics prediction when both models are valid. A post-collision result can be checked against system momentum. A center-of-mass trend can be used to test whether external impulse is truly negligible.

Mastery also requires limits. State where drag, friction, deformation, variable force, or a changing system boundary would invalidate a simple model. Preserve two corrected errors and explain how the correction changed the model choice or quantitative reasoning.

Evidence revision / CER

Write a final mechanics CER that uses at least three nonredundant representations and one model limitation. Revise any synthesis that becomes an equation inventory without evidence or system reasoning.

Spiral retrieval

Semester transfer defense

Choose one unfamiliar scenario and build a five-representation defense: motion graph, FBD, energy account, momentum table, and center-of-mass/system statement. For each representation, write the specific question it answers and one limit it cannot resolve. This prevents decorative diagrams from masquerading as evidence.

Then identify one contradiction test. If an energy calculation predicts a speed that conflicts with a measured graph slope, or a momentum result implies unexplained center-of-mass acceleration, trace the disagreement to assumptions, uncertainty, or system definition. A mature mechanics solution treats contradictions as signals for model revision rather than hiding them.
