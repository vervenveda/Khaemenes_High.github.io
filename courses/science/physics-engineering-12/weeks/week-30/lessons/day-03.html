Electrical Power & Energy

Calculate electrical power and connect it with energy transfer.

Weekly EvidencePhysics CourseScience Hall

Safety boundary

Use only low-voltage classroom batteries/components under supervision or an approved circuit simulation. Never use mains voltage.

Explicit physics instruction

Electrical power is the rate of energy transfer. For circuit elements, P=VI and can combine with Ohm's law into P=I²R or P=V²/R when the ohmic model applies.

Energy transferred over time is E=Pt.

Power ratings describe transfer rate, not total energy by themselves.

Core relation/model: P=VI; E=Pt

Worked example

A 6 V device drawing 2 A uses 12 W. In 30 s it transfers 360 J.

Guided question

A 9 V component draws 0.5 A for 20 s. Find power and energy.

Independent work

  1. Solve three power problems.
  2. Convert one power-time result to energy.
  3. Compare two devices at equal voltage.

Misconception check

A watt is a rate unit, not an energy unit.

Formative check

What extra quantity is needed to convert power into energy?
