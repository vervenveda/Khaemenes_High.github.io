Center of Gravity & Stability

Why does where a force is applied matter?

Weekly EvidencePhysics CourseScience Hall

Explicit physics instruction

Center of gravity determines where the body’s weight can be modeled as acting for torque analysis.

Stability increases when the line of action of weight remains within the support base.

A low center of gravity and wide support base generally improve resistance to tipping.

Core relation: τ=rF⊥ and Στ=0 in rotational equilibrium.

Worked example

If a 10 N weight acts 0.20 m from a pivot, an opposing 5 N force must act 0.40 m away for equal 2 N·m torques.

Guided question

Why can widening a support base make tipping less likely?

Vocabulary and stability extension

Vocabulary: center of gravity; support base; line of action; tipping threshold; stability.

A body is statically stable against tipping while the vertical line of action of its weight falls within the support polygon. At the tipping threshold, the effective pivot shifts to an edge of the base and gravitational torque can drive rotation beyond it.

Lowering the center of gravity reduces the tipping tendency for a given lateral displacement because a larger tilt is needed before the weight line exits the base. Widening the support base has a similar stabilizing effect. These are model principles, not safety ratings for real furniture or structures.

Evidence revision / CER

Compare two geometries and defend which is more stable using line-of-action and torque reasoning. Revise any claim that treats low center of gravity alone as sufficient regardless of base width.

Spiral retrieval

Recall Week 17 center-of-mass ideas and distinguish center of mass from the gravitational line of action used in a uniform field.

Stability-threshold practice

Model a rectangular object with half-width 0.30 m. If its center of gravity projects vertically within ±0.30 m of the centerline, the weight line stays inside the base. Once the projection reaches an edge, the object is at the tipping threshold. Beyond the edge, gravity creates a torque that tends to increase the tilt.

Compare two designs with the same mass: one has center-of-gravity height 0.40 m and the other 0.80 m. For the same lateral displacement or tilt, the higher center of gravity reaches the support edge sooner. Increasing base width delays that threshold.

Real stability also depends on dynamic motion, surface compliance, contact changes, and external forces, so this static geometry model must not be used as a safety certification for furniture or structures.

Tipping-boundary check

Compare two support widths at the same center-of-gravity height and identify which requires the larger tilt before the weight line reaches the base edge.

Phenomenon / Prediction

A door opens more easily when pushed farther from the hinge.

Investigation / model check

Safe lever/door-handle force reasoning; avoid loading furniture. Preserve observations or supplied data, compare them with the model, and record one limitation or source of uncertainty.
