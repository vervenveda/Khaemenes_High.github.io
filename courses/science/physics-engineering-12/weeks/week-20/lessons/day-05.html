Torque Synthesis & Design

Why does where a force is applied matter?

Weekly EvidencePhysics CourseScience Hall

Explicit physics instruction

Torque reasoning connects force, geometry, balance, and engineering design.

Use the synthesis to explain not just whether a lever balances, but why the force placement creates the observed rotational effect.

A larger force does not always produce larger torque; lever arm and force direction matter.

Core relation: τ=rF⊥ and Στ=0 in rotational equilibrium.

Worked example

A 20 N perpendicular force applied 0.50 m from a hinge produces 10 N·m of torque. The same force at 0.25 m produces only 5 N·m.

Guided question

Design a handle position that reduces required force for the same target torque.
