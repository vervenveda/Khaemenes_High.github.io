Evidence record: raw force/distance data, signed torques, residual, uncertainty, and revision.

Misconception check

A larger force does not always produce larger torque; lever arm and force direction matter.

Formative check

What two geometric features determine torque besides force magnitude?

Reveal response

Distance from the pivot and the force’s perpendicular component/direction.

Vocabulary and investigation-depth extension

Vocabulary: torque residual; lever arm; measurement uncertainty; repeated trial; balance point.

Preserve raw force and distance values, then calculate signed torques independently. Define torque residual r_τ=Στ. A small nonzero residual may be consistent with force-resolution or distance-measurement uncertainty.

Estimate which measurement dominates uncertainty. Because τ=rF, relative uncertainty in either r or F contributes to torque uncertainty. Repeat the balance with different force-distance combinations that predict the same torque and examine whether residuals remain randomly distributed or show a systematic bias.

Evidence revision / CER

Write a CER deciding whether the lever data support rotational equilibrium within uncertainty. Revise the setup if residuals show a consistent sign.
