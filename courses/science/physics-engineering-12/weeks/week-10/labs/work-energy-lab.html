Work–Energy Theorem Lab

Test the work–energy theorem with prescribed NET work and realistic nonnegative kinetic-energy constraints.

Scientific model and safety

Computed teaching model only. No heavy-load pulls, unsafe ramps, moving machinery or hazardous physical tests. Results are simulated, not sensor measurements or engineering certifications.

For an ideal constant-mass object, kinetic energy K=½mv². The work–energy theorem is W_net=ΔK. Enter NET work from all contributing external forces, not work by one arbitrarily chosen force. The result is a final speed magnitude, not direction. A computed negative final kinetic energy is physically incompatible with the assumed continued-motion scenario.
