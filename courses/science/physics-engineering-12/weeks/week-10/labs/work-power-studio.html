Work & Power Studio

Investigate signed mechanical work by one constant force and average power for a time interval.

Scientific model and safety

Computed teaching model only. No heavy-load pulls, unsafe ramps, moving machinery or hazardous physical tests. Results are simulated, not sensor measurements or engineering certifications.

A constant force F acts through straight displacement d at angle θ. Work by that force is W=Fd cosθ (J); average signed mechanical power by that force is W/Δt (W). This is not automatically net work when other forces also act.
