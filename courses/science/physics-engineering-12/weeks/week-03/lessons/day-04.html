Test Constant Acceleration

Which parameters change a model's position and velocity graphs?

Objective: Design a controlled simulation of initial velocity and constant acceleration, calculate signed area, and compare output to an analytic solution.

45–60 minutes · Offline-capable · Public practice is not a supervised examination

MondayTuesdayWednesdayThursdayFriday

Read and reason

A mathematical experiment

This week's lab models x(t)=x₀+v₀t+½at² and v(t)=v₀+at in one dimension. Select duration T and experiment with positive and negative acceleration. Record initial position, initial velocity, observer-frame speed and acceleration. Predict the final signed displacement and whether a direction reversal occurs before pressing Record Trial. The simulation uses exact ideal formulas, not sensor measurements.

Change one quantity only

To study acceleration's effect, hold x₀, v₀, T and observer velocity fixed while running a matched second trial with different a. The velocity–time plot will have a new slope. A position–time plot will change curvature. With a=0 the first graph is linear and v(t) horizontal. With a positive or negative acceleration, the curve in x(t) can bend and v(t) tilts.

Check geometry against algebra

For a linearly changing velocity, compute signed area T(vstart+vend)/2; it should match Δx from the position formula. If a reversal takes place, total path length exceeds |Δx|. For instance v₀=4 m/s, a=−2 m/s², T=4 s ends at −4 m/s, has displacement 0 m and distance 8 m. The simulator should calculate these numbers while the graph shows a velocity line crossing zero.

Limits and safe alternatives

The laboratory does not model impacts, vehicle control, human motion, air drag, curved two-dimensional paths or changing acceleration. Its graph is generated rather than measured. Students can use the bundled artificial CSV records if device inputs are inaccessible. No fast-moving projectiles, ramps or hazardous equipment should be used for these investigations.

Safety and scope: Investigate with supplied synthetic datasets and browser models. Do not launch hard objects, operate motor vehicles, set up hazardous ramps, or place anyone near moving machinery. The simulator calculates ideal kinematics; it does not measure a real device.

Vocabulary

initial velocity
Velocity v0 at time t=0, measured in m/s and signed according to the chosen axis. Explain with an original example.
constant acceleration
Model assumption that acceleration has the same value at all times in a specified interval. Explain with an original example.
control
A setting held unchanged when comparing trials to isolate another setting's effect. Explain with an original example.
trial
One documented repetition of a modeled or observed investigation. Explain with an original example.
residual
Difference between an observed data value and a model's predicted value at the same independent-variable value. Explain with an original example.

Worked example

With v₀=4 m/s, a=−2 m/s² and T=4 s, vfinal=−4 m/s. Displacement=4×4+0.5×(−2)×16=0 m. Signed velocity–time area agrees and distance traveled is 8 m.

Check signs and units. Show each calculation rather than memorizing a number.
