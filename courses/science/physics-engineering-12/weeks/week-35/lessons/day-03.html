Prototype / Simulation Plan

Design a safe test that can discriminate between the model and alternatives.

Weekly EvidencePhysics CourseScience Hall

Explicit capstone instruction

A useful experiment or simulation changes one relevant feature at a time when possible and records raw evidence.

Define independent, dependent, and controlled variables before testing.

Plan repeated trials, calibration, and a revision criterion so the capstone does not become a one-shot demonstration.

Vocabulary: test plan; independent variable; control; trial; calibration.

Worked example

If model A predicts 10 units and model B predicts 16 units, a test with ±5-unit uncertainty cannot discriminate well; improving measurement precision may be more valuable than adding complexity.

Guided question

How will your test decide whether the model is good enough for the stated criterion?

Independent work

  1. Define variables and controls.
  2. Choose raw measurements.
  3. Specify trial count.
  4. Write a revision trigger.

Misconception check

A test that cannot distinguish competing outcomes is weak even if it produces data.

Formative check

What should a revision trigger be tied to?

Reveal response

A measurable criterion, model residual, or repeatable test failure.

Mentor evidence conference

Ask Archaemenes to challenge system boundaries, predictions, uncertainty, and whether revisions follow the evidence.

Teacher guidance

Foundation: Use a test-plan checklist.

Core: Design a reproducible comparison.

Extended: Optimize information gained per trial.
