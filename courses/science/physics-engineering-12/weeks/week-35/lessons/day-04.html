Explicit capstone instruction

Use teacher-approved safe materials or a simulation. Preserve raw trial data before averaging or scoring.

Calculate the model residual or percent error, then compare performance with criteria.

If results disagree, distinguish between model failure, measurement uncertainty, build variation, and an incorrect system assumption.

Vocabulary: trial data; residual; percent error; validation; revision.

Worked example

A model predicts 50 units; trials are 46, 47, 45. Mean is 46, so residual is −4 and percent error relative to prediction is 8%.

Capstone data-analysis depth

Preserve every raw trial before calculating averages, residuals, percent error, or scores. Report instrument resolution, simulation settings, and any exclusion rule. Repeated trials make it possible to distinguish random variation from a persistent model mismatch.

For trials of 46, 47, and 45 against a prediction of 50, the mean is 46 and the residual is −4. The relative error is 8%. Because the spread among trials is small compared with the residual, the pattern suggests a systematic mismatch more strongly than random trial noise.

Residual-pattern reasoning

Do not look only at the average. Plot residuals against the independent variable when possible. Random scatter around zero supports the model more strongly than a curved or one-sided pattern, which can reveal bias, an omitted mechanism, or a changing parameter.

Evidence revision / CER

Write a claim about whether the design and model are adequate for the stated criterion. Cite raw and processed evidence, compare the discrepancy with uncertainty, and explain why the chosen revision addresses the measured shortfall rather than merely changing the design.

Iteration check

After revising one feature, predict the direction and approximate size of the expected change before retesting. Use the same metric and test conditions when comparing versions unless the change in conditions is itself part of the investigation.

Safety and authenticity

Use teacher-approved materials or simulation only. Do not discard inconvenient trials without a documented methodological reason, and do not reconstruct missing measurements as if they were observed.

Spaced retrieval

Without notes, retrieve one prerequisite model or representation from an earlier lesson, state its units and assumptions, and explain how it supports today’s reasoning.
