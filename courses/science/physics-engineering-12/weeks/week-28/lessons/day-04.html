Explicit physics instruction

This investigation uses temperature-time data to test a cooling model.

Use a cooling-curve dataset or safe water-temperature simulation. Track temperature versus time and compare energy-transfer models.

Preserve raw time/temperature values before calculating rates or fitting trends.

Core relation: q=mcΔT; energy conservation across a defined thermal system.

Worked example

If a sample cools from 80°C to 60°C in 10 min, average rate is −2°C/min over that interval; later intervals may cool more slowly.
