Model scope and safety

Digital simulation only. No real collisions, pushing people, fast carts, strong springs, heavy objects or motor-driven apparatus are required. The one-dimensional model assumes fixed mass and constant prescribed net external force. It omits automatic friction, drag, impacts and contact-force histories. The two-body acceleration calculation additionally assumes each modeled interaction force is the only net horizontal force on that body.

Choose +x to the right. For one cart, a=Fnet/m and over duration T, v(T)=v₀+aT and Δx=v₀T+½aT². For the interaction pair, A pushes B right with +F, while B pushes A left with −F. They act on different bodies and do not cancel in a one-body diagram.
