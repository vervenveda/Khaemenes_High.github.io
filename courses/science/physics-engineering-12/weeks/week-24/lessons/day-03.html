Superposition, Reflection & Standing Waves

How do disturbances carry energy without transporting matter over long distances?

Weekly EvidencePhysics CourseScience Hall

Explicit physics instruction

When waves overlap, their displacements add. Constructive interference increases resultant amplitude; destructive interference reduces it.

Reflection can invert a pulse depending on the boundary condition.

Standing waves arise from interference between oppositely traveling waves and contain nodes and antinodes.

Core relation: v=fλ.

Worked example

Two identical upward pulses overlap completely, producing twice the displacement at that instant; an equal upward/downward pair can cancel momentarily.

Guided question

What distinguishes a node from an antinode in a standing wave?

Independent work

  1. Solve three wave-speed or wavelength questions.
  2. Sketch one interference case.
  3. State one medium/source assumption.

Misconception check

Matter in the medium does not generally travel with the wave over long distances; the disturbance and energy propagate.

Formative check

If speed is constant and frequency doubles, what happens to wavelength?

Reveal response

It halves.
