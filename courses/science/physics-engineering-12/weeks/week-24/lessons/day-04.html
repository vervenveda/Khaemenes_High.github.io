Explicit physics instruction

This investigation tests the v=fλ relation with supplied or simulated wave data.

Use a rope/slinky simulation or virtual wave tool with safe spacing. Measure wavelength and frequency, then compare v=fλ predictions.

Preserve raw frequency, wavelength, and timing evidence before calculating speed.

Core relation: v=fλ.

Worked example

If f=4.0 Hz and measured λ=0.75 m, predicted speed is 3.0 m/s; compare repeated trials and residuals.

Misconception check

Matter in the medium does not generally travel with the wave over long distances; the disturbance and energy propagate.

Formative check

If speed is constant and frequency doubles, what happens to wavelength?

Reveal response

It halves.
