Wave Speed Calculations

How do disturbances carry energy without transporting matter over long distances?

Weekly EvidencePhysics CourseScience Hall

Explicit physics instruction

Use v=fλ. Units must be consistent: Hz is s⁻¹, wavelength in metres gives speed in m/s.

A wave with f=5 Hz and λ=2 m travels at v=10 m/s. If the same medium keeps v fixed and f doubles, λ must halve.

Changing source frequency does not automatically change wave speed in the same medium.

Core relation: v=fλ.

Worked example

A wave with f=5 Hz and λ=2 m travels at v=10 m/s. If the same medium keeps v fixed and f doubles, λ must halve.

Guided question

A wave travels 12 m/s at 3 Hz. Find wavelength.

Independent work

  1. Solve three wave-speed or wavelength questions.
  2. Sketch one interference case.
  3. State one medium/source assumption.

Misconception check

Matter in the medium does not generally travel with the wave over long distances; the disturbance and energy propagate.

Formative check

If speed is constant and frequency doubles, what happens to wavelength?

Reveal response

It halves.
