Supervised Final & Correction Protocol

Clarify the closed-book final boundary and plan high-quality corrections after score return.

Weekly EvidencePhysics CourseScience Hall

Assessment boundary

This page contains public formative support only. It does not expose protected final questions, answer keys, grading logic, or authentication details.

Explicit course synthesis

The official comprehensive final is closed-book and supervised. Public browser practice is formative and non-authoritative.

After the authorized score is returned, corrections should identify the error type and rebuild the reasoning chain.

Protected final questions, keys, grading logic, and authentication material must remain outside the public learner repository.

Vocabulary: supervised final; correction; error analysis; mastery; protected content.

Worked example

A missed circuit item caused by confusing current and voltage should be corrected by naming the conceptual error, redrawing the circuit model, and recomputing with correct variables.

Guided question

Why is copying a correct answer weaker than documenting the error category and revised reasoning?

Independent work

  1. Prepare an error-category checklist.
  2. After authorized score return, complete corrections by concept category.
  3. Keep protected question text out of public notes unless explicitly approved.

Misconception check

Corrections demonstrate learning; they do not authorize exposure of protected exam content.

Formative check

What can a public practice system validly record?

Reveal response

Formative practice performance and corrections, not authenticated official mastery by itself.

Mentor evidence conference

Archaemenes may challenge reasoning and help plan corrections but cannot reveal protected answers or grant official final mastery.

Teacher guidance

Foundation: Use correction sentence frames.

Core: Classify and repair errors.

Extended: Analyze patterns across multiple error categories.
