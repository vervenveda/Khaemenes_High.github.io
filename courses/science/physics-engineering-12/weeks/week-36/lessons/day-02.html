Assessment boundary

This page contains public formative support only. It does not expose protected final questions, answer keys, grading logic, or authentication details.

Explicit course synthesis

Mixed practice should begin with system definition and model choice, not arithmetic.

Use public practice scenarios different from protected final items so preparation tests transfer rather than memorization.

Each solution should include units, assumptions, direction/sign where relevant, and one reasonableness check.

Vocabulary: transfer; model selection; unit audit; assumption; reasonableness.

Worked example

A problem that combines orbital motion with remote sensing might require gravitational reasoning first and EM-wave interpretation second.

Guided question

Choose the best first model for a supplied mechanics, circuits, and waves scenario and justify each choice.

Independent work

  1. Solve one mechanics problem.
  2. Solve one waves/fluids problem.
  3. Solve one circuits/fields problem.
  4. Perform a unit and assumption audit for each.

Misconception check

Hard problems do not require every formula; they require the appropriate representation.

Formative check

What should happen before substituting numbers?

Reveal response

Define the system, target quantity, model, assumptions, and units.

Mentor evidence conference

Archaemenes may challenge reasoning and help plan corrections but cannot reveal protected answers or grant official final mastery.

Teacher guidance

Foundation: Use a model-choice scaffold.

Core: Solve cross-domain transfer problems.

Extended: Compare multiple valid solution paths.
