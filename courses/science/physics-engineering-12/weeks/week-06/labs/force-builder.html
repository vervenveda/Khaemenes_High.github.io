Defined experiment and safety

This is a digital mathematical model—not a real spring scale. No heavy-object pushing, hazardous force demonstrations or moving equipment are needed. The block remains in contact with a level table. The model assumes constant prescribed horizontal forces, no automatic friction law, no lift-off, and no impacts. Predictions for later times apply only while the same forces remain valid.

Choose right and up as positive. Applied horizontal forces are modeled separately from any automatic kinetic/static friction. The surface maintains vertical acceleration zero; its normal is N = mg + Fdown − Fup, which is positive for every permitted input. Therefore ΣFy = 0. Horizontal acceleration is ax = (Fright − Fleft)/m. The two-second velocity and displacement estimates assume constant net force and contact throughout the entire interval.
