Rotation Unit Synthesis

How is energy partitioned in rolling systems?

Weekly EvidencePhysics CourseScience Hall

Explicit physics instruction

Rotation synthesis combines angular kinematics, torque, angular momentum, and rotational energy.

Choose the representation that matches the question: motion, balance, conservation, or energy partition.

Rolling does not mean all kinetic energy is translational; rotation stores part of the total.

Core relations: K_total=½Mv²+½Iω² and v=rω.

Worked example

For a rolling body with M=2 kg, v=3 m/s, I=0.50 kg·m², and ω=6 rad/s, K_trans=9 J and K_rot=9 J, so total KE=18 J.
