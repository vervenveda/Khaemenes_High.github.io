Rolling-Energy Investigation

How is energy partitioned in rolling systems?

Weekly EvidencePhysics CourseScience Hall

Explicit physics instruction

This investigation compares modeled or measured rolling behavior across shapes.

Use a ramp/rolling simulation or safe lightweight objects. Compare arrival times or modeled energy partitions for shapes with different moments of inertia.

Preserve raw time/height data and calculated energy terms before interpreting differences.

Core relations: K_total=½Mv²+½Iω² and v=rω.

Worked example

If two shapes descend the same height, compare mgh with ½Mv²+½Iω² and calculate a residual for each.

Guided question

Which measurements are necessary to test rolling-energy conservation?

Independent work

  1. Solve three rotational-energy or rolling calculations.
  2. Draw an energy bar chart.
  3. State one condition where v=rω fails.

Investigation / Data Lab

Use a ramp/rolling simulation or safe lightweight objects. Compare arrival times or modeled energy partitions for shapes with different moments of inertia.

Evidence record: raw data, energy calculations, residuals, uncertainty, and revision.

Misconception check

Rolling does not mean all kinetic energy is translational; rotation stores part of the total.

Formative check

What extra energy term must be included for a rolling object?

Reveal response

Rotational kinetic energy ½Iω².
