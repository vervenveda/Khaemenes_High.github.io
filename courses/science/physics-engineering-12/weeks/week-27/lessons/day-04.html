Misconception check

Faster flow does not universally mean lower pressure in every real situation; the simple Bernoulli relation has assumptions.

Formative check

If cross-sectional area halves for steady incompressible flow, what happens to average speed?

Reveal response

It doubles.

Vocabulary and investigation-depth extension

Vocabulary: continuity residual; pressure residual; percent difference; uncertainty; calibration.

Preserve raw area, speed, pressure, and elevation values before calculating model outputs. For continuity, compare Q₁=A₁v₁ with Q₂=A₂v₂. Define a residual or percent difference and compare it with measurement uncertainty.

Then test pressure trends only after checking whether continuity is supported. If the area-speed data already violate the incompressible steady-flow assumption, a Bernoulli comparison built on them is not trustworthy.

Evidence revision / CER

Write a CER deciding whether the dataset supports continuity and whether Bernoulli reasoning is appropriate as a second-stage model. Include one method revision.

Residual-pattern investigation extension

Suppose three trials yield Q₂/Q₁ ratios of 0.99, 1.02, and 1.00. These support continuity within likely measurement scatter. If the ratios are 0.86, 0.84, and 0.85, the consistent deficit suggests a systematic issue such as leakage or area calibration.

For pressure, compare measured and ideal Bernoulli changes separately. Preserve both continuity residuals and pressure residuals so one failed model does not obscure the other.
