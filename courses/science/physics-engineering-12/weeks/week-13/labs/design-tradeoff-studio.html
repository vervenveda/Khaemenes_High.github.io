Engineering Design Trade-Off Studio

Test energy-system designs against simultaneous input, useful output, thermal-transfer and delivery-time constraints.

Scientific scope and safety

Entirely virtual model. Do not open mains-powered devices, run improvised motors or carry out dangerous physical energy audits. No measured performance, actual temperature or electrical safety is inferred.

Assume a fictional steady process with no net change in device-stored energy: E_input=E_useful+E_thermal+E_other. 'Useful' is defined by the device goal. Percentages cannot sum beyond 100%. Power is each transfer's joules divided by the SAME positive interval.

4. Four engineering constraints

Our classroom design brief requires input no more than 600 J, useful output at least 300 J, thermal transfer no more than 140 J and a completion interval no more than 8 seconds. These are illustrative mathematical constraints, NOT engineering safety ratings.
