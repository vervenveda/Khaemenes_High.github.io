Physical assumptions and safety

**Digital-only laboratory.** No ramps, heavy lifting, vehicles, forceful pulling or risky experiments. The model is not traction or safety guidance. All outputs are synthetic predictions, not measurements. It models an initially stationary block on a level support with a constant horizontal applied force and no other horizontal interactions.

Gravity magnitude g=9.8 m/s², normal N=mg. The static maximum is f_s,max=μ_s N, but actual static friction only matches the requested horizontal force when its magnitude is within that limit. After the applied magnitude exceeds the maximum, the model uses kinetic friction magnitude μ_kN directed against the initial sliding direction. Values describe initial slip rather than later stick–slip or reversal behavior.
