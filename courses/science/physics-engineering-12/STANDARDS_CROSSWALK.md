# Physics & Engineering 12 — Standards Crosswalk

**Course:** International Physics & Engineering 12  
**Code:** KH-SCI-PHY12  
**Duration:** 36 weeks / 180 daily lesson blocks  
**Status:** Canonical course-level standards crosswalk  
**Updated:** 2026-11-23

## Benchmark boundary

This crosswalk supports curriculum design and internal audit. The course is benchmarked to NGSS high-school physical science and engineering practices with selected Earth/space applications. It is not an AP-authorized course and does not by itself establish jurisdiction-specific credit or accreditation.

## Course-level standards spine

- **HS-PS2** — Motion, forces, interactions, gravitation, momentum, circular motion, rotational applications.
- **HS-PS3** — Energy, work, power, conservation, thermal systems, electrical energy, engineering energy systems.
- **HS-PS4** — Waves, sound, electromagnetic waves, light, information transfer, remote sensing.
- **HS-ETS1** — Criteria/constraints, modeling, optimization, prototyping, testing, evidence-based design iteration.
- **Selected HS-ESS1 / HS-ESS2** — orbital systems, remote sensing, climate-energy applications.
- **Science & Engineering Practices (SEP 1–8)** — question definition, model development, investigation design, data analysis, quantitative reasoning, explanation/argument, information evaluation, communication.

## Daily evidence expectation

A standards connection is considered instructionally meaningful when the daily lesson visibly links the student's work to the relevant physics model, evidence practice, or engineering decision—not merely by placing a standards code on the page.

The course-wide certification program therefore distinguishes:
1. standards mapped in the canonical course map,
2. standards meaningfully represented in daily instruction,
3. standards codes visibly exposed in daily source.

Those three conditions should not be treated as interchangeable.
