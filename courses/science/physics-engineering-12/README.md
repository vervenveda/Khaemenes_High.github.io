# International Physics & Engineering 12

**Course code:** KH-SCI-PHY12  
**Grade:** 12  
**Duration:** 36 weeks / 180 daily lesson blocks  
**Credit recommendation:** 1 high-school laboratory science credit, subject to local law/school policy  
**Path:** `courses/science/physics-engineering-12/`

## Purpose

A 36-week algebra-based physics and engineering course covering mechanics, rotation, oscillations, waves, fluids, thermal physics, electricity, magnetism, electromagnetic information technologies, space-system applications, and engineering design.

## Instructional contract

- Foundation, Core, and Extended pathways
- phenomenon-first weekly instruction
- five-day rhythm: model → concept → data/math → investigation/design → CER/mastery
- local-first progress storage and JSON export
- ≥80% mastery target with correction/reassessment
- evidence-based rubrics for constructed responses
- safe supervised investigations or equivalent simulations/data work
- target laboratory/inquiry time: at least 25%
- print-friendly records and portfolio expectations
- explicit separation of observation, model, inference, uncertainty, and broader worldview/philosophical claims where relevant

## Standards / benchmark basis

- NGSS HS-PS2-1–6
- NGSS HS-PS3-1–5
- NGSS HS-PS4-1–5
- NGSS HS-ETS1-1–4
- Selected HS-ESS1/ESS2 applications

**College-readiness benchmark:** AP Physics 1 depth-informed benchmark plus broader electricity, magnetism, waves, remote sensing, and engineering; not an AP-authorized course.

This course is not represented as College Board AP-authorized. The AP frameworks are used only as public college-readiness depth benchmarks.

## Laboratory rule

Target ≥25% hands-on/inquiry laboratory time using safe low-energy systems or supervised/virtual alternatives; no mains-voltage work.

No student should perform a hazardous procedure without qualified educator supervision, appropriate facilities, PPE, disposal planning, and a current risk review. Never improvise chemistry by mixing household cleaners or unknown chemicals.

## Assessment model

- weekly CER / mastery portfolio
- unit performance tasks
- cumulative midterm
- comprehensive final
- capstone / portfolio defense
- 80% target on essential outcomes
