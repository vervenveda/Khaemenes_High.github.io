About International Physics & Engineering 12

International Physics & Engineering 12 is the senior laboratory-science capstone of the High School pathway. Students use algebraic models, experiments, simulations, data, and engineering design to explain motion, forces, energy, momentum, rotation, waves, fluids, thermal systems, electricity, magnetism, electromagnetic information, and space applications. The course emphasizes quantitative reasoning, model limits, safe investigation, design constraints, and evidence-based decision making.

Course Features

Mechanics, Energy & Rotation Motion, vectors, Newton’s laws, gravitation, work, energy, momentum, collisions, rotational kinematics, torque, angular momentum, and system modeling build the quantitative foundation of the course.
Waves, Fluids & Thermal Physics Oscillations, mechanical waves, sound, resonance, interference, fluids, pressure, buoyancy, flow, temperature, heat transfer, and energy balance connect motion with continuous systems.
Electricity, Magnetism & Information Charge, fields, potential, circuits, magnetic interactions, induction, electromagnetic waves, signal transmission, sensing, and information technologies extend physical models into modern systems.
Space Systems & Engineering Design Orbital and space-system applications, remote sensing, engineering criteria and constraints, prototype testing, quantitative evaluation, revision, cumulative assessment, and a senior engineering capstone document readiness.
