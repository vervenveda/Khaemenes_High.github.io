# Khaemenes Academy — High School Science 9–12 Coherence Map

**Authority:** department curriculum coherence contract  
**Established:** 2026-11-23  
**Scope:** Integrated Science 9 → Biology & Biotechnology 10 → Chemistry & Materials Science 11 → Physics & Engineering 12

## Purpose

The four science courses are one progression, not four isolated credits. A concept is considered coherent when a later course explicitly retrieves, deepens, or transfers a prior scientific model rather than merely repeating vocabulary.

## Four-year progression

| Strand | Grade 9 foundation | Grade 10 deepening | Grade 11 deepening | Grade 12 transfer/mastery |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Investigation | variables, controls, measurement, evidence quality | biological controls, replication, sampling, source quality | quantitative design, calibration, uncertainty, chemical evidence | model selection, residuals, engineering test/iteration |
| Matter | particles, atoms, periodic patterns, bonding | chemistry of life, macromolecules, membranes | atomic/molecular models, stoichiometry, equilibrium, materials | matter as physical system; fluids, thermal and electrical applications |
| Energy | energy transfer/conservation | cellular respiration, photosynthesis, ecosystem energy | thermochemistry, entropy, free energy, electrochemistry | work-energy, thermal systems, circuits and engineering efficiency |
| Systems | cells, organisms, ecosystems, Earth systems | homeostasis, signaling, populations, ecosystems | equilibrium, coupled chemical systems, materials/environment | mechanical, electrical, thermal, wave and engineered systems |
| Information | heredity, DNA, waves/information | gene expression, biotechnology, evolution evidence | spectroscopy, analytical evidence, chemical information | waves, EM information, remote sensing and signal systems |
| Quantitative reasoning | units, graphs, rates, proportional reasoning | probability, population models, rates and normalized biological data | dimensional analysis, mole relationships, logs, equilibrium and energetics | algebraic models, vectors, slopes/areas, inverse-square, optimization |
| Modeling | model vs observation, limitations | molecular/cellular/ecological mechanism models | particle/structure/energy/equilibrium models | mathematical/graphical models and engineering decision models |
| Engineering | criteria, constraints, sustainable capstone | biotechnology and conservation decision design | materials and sustainable chemistry design | prototype, optimization, testing, trade-offs and senior defense |

## Required handoffs

1. **Science 9 → Biology 10**
   - cells and organization → structure/function and cell energetics
   - heredity → molecular genetics and biotechnology
   - ecosystems/evolution → population genetics, selection, ecology
   - inquiry/evidence → controlled biological investigations

2. **Science 9 → Chemistry 11**
   - atomic structure and periodicity → electron structure and periodic prediction
   - bonding/reactions → molecular structure, stoichiometry and reaction evidence
   - energy → thermochemistry and free-energy reasoning
   - units/graphs → dimensional analysis and calibration

3. **Science 9 → Physics 12**
   - motion/forces/energy → algebraic mechanics and conservation
   - electricity/waves → fields, circuits, waves and EM information
   - Earth/space → gravitation, orbital systems and remote sensing
   - engineering capstone → senior design constraints, testing and optimization

4. **Biology 10 → Chemistry 11**
   - enzyme/rate evidence → kinetics
   - molecular structure and polarity → intermolecular forces and solutions
   - biological energy → thermochemistry/free energy
   - environmental systems → materials/environmental chemistry

5. **Chemistry 11 → Physics 12**
   - energy accounting → work, thermal and electrical energy systems
   - quantitative uncertainty → mathematical-model validation
   - electrochemistry → electrical energy and circuit reasoning
   - materials decisions → engineering constraints and optimization

## Retrieval rule

Every later-course use of a prior strand should include at least one of: explicit retrieval, changed-context application, misconception repair, model comparison, or quantitative transfer. Repetition without transfer does not satisfy this contract.

## Graduation outcome

A student completing Grades 9–12 should be able to move from observation to model, from model to quantitative prediction, from prediction to investigation, from evidence to revision, and from scientific evidence to an engineering or explanatory defense with stated uncertainty and limitations.
