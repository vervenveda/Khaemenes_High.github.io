# Khaemenes High School — Science Department

## Four-year core pathway

1. Grade 9 — International Integrated Science 9
2. Grade 10 — International Biology & Biotechnology 10
3. Grade 11 — International Chemistry & Materials Science 11
4. Grade 12 — International Physics & Engineering 12

The pathway is cumulative: Grade 9 establishes integrated foundations; Grade 10 deepens life science; Grade 11 deepens chemistry/materials; Grade 12 deepens physics/engineering and reconnects to Earth/space applications.

## Federated extensions

- Solanar Observatory — weather, atmosphere, climate, Earth systems
- River-to-Road — environmental fieldwork and stewardship
- Medicament Hub — health and medical-learning extensions

## National benchmark model

The program is organized around the National Academies Framework and NGSS three-dimensional model. Public AP Biology, AP Chemistry, and AP Physics 1 frameworks are used as depth/pacing benchmarks only; Khaemenes courses must not be described as AP courses unless separately authorized through College Board Course Audit.

## Laboratory expectation

Grades 10–12 target at least 25% inquiry/laboratory/field/simulation-with-data time. Hazardous work requires qualified educator supervision, appropriate facilities, PPE, disposal procedures, and risk review. Home learners use safe investigations, simulations, supplied data, or educator-supervised alternatives.

## Mastery

Essential outcomes use an 80% target with corrections and reassessment.
