# High School Science — Assessment Retention Contract

## Purpose
Assessment should verify durable learning, not only the most recently taught unit. Grades 10–12 must intentionally retrieve scientifically relevant prior-year knowledge and practices while keeping protected summative content private.

## Public formative and cumulative practice
For Biology 10, Chemistry 11, and Physics & Engineering 12:
- target **5–10%** of cumulative public practice for inherited prior-year retrieval;
- use changed contexts rather than duplicate old questions;
- require explanation, model selection, calculation, data interpretation, evidence evaluation, or transfer;
- avoid definition-only recall as the sole evidence of retention.

## Protected midterm/final boundary
Official closed-book or supervised assessments may satisfy inherited retrieval through confidential items or supervised performance evidence. Public repositories must not reveal protected questions, keys, authentication logic, or grading secrets merely to demonstrate compliance.

## Required correction evidence
When an inherited concept contributes to an error, the learner correction should document:
1. the inherited concept or practice;
2. how it applies in the current course;
3. the error type;
4. corrected reasoning/model/calculation;
5. one changed-context transfer check.

## Course inheritance
- **Biology 10:** Science 9 cells, systems, energy/matter, probability, ecology/evolution evidence, graphing, uncertainty, source quality and CER.
- **Chemistry 11:** Science 9 atomic/matter/energy foundations plus Biology quantitative evidence, rates, controls, model limits and CER.
- **Physics 12:** Science 9 mechanics/waves/systems, Biology evidence/model reasoning, and Chemistry energy/rates/equilibrium/materials/calibration.

## Audit rule
The upper-grade course maps must carry a machine-readable `prior_year_retrieval` contract. Department CI must reject removal of the contract, the 5–10% target, inherited anchors, checkpoint weeks, correction requirements, or protected-assessment boundary.
