About International Integrated Science 9

International Integrated Science 9 builds a broad high-school science foundation by connecting life science, physical science, Earth and space systems, scientific inquiry, engineering design, and information literacy. Students investigate phenomena, work with measurements and data, build and critique models, conduct safe investigations, communicate uncertainty, and explain conclusions using evidence and reasoning.

Course Features

Life, Matter & Energy Students connect biological systems, matter, energy transfer, interactions, patterns, and quantitative relationships across an integrated scientific foundation.
Earth & Space Systems Earth systems, environmental processes, space systems, cycles, scale, change over time, and system interactions develop cross-disciplinary scientific thinking.
Inquiry, Data & CER Safe investigation, SI measurement, uncertainty, graphing, data interpretation, source quality, models, inference, and Claim-Evidence-Reasoning strengthen scientific argument.
Engineering & Mastery Foundation, Core, and Extended pathways support varied readiness while engineering design, revision, generated mastery checks, cumulative assessments, and portfolio evidence document growth.
