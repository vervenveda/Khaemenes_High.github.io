# Unit 03 Teacher Scoring Guide

## Mastery standard
Unit 03 uses the course-wide **≥80% mastery standard**. Submission alone does not establish mastery.

- Integrated Systems Case Study: **32/40 required**
- Constructed Response 1: **16/20 required**
- Constructed Response 2: **16/20 required**
- Objective quiz: **16/20 required**
- Objective assessment: **24/30 required**

A learner who is below threshold should complete corrections and a new transfer response or revised performance artifact before mastery is verified.

## Integrated Systems Case Study — 40 points
Score each dimension from 0–5.

| Dimension | 5-point mastery description |
|---|---|
| Systems integration | Accurately links at least five body systems and explains why their interactions matter to the scenario. |
| Matter & energy pathways | Correctly traces oxygen, carbon dioxide, glucose, water, heat, and a metabolic waste through relevant systems. |
| Feedback regulation | Builds a complete, mechanistically correct negative-feedback loop including stimulus, receptor, coordinator, effector, response, and return toward range. |
| Quantitative reasoning | Correctly calculates and interprets heart-rate and breathing-rate changes with units/percent reasoning where appropriate. |
| Evidence vs inference | Clearly separates supplied observations/data from physiological interpretation and does not overclaim. |
| Limitations & uncertainty | States at least four meaningful limitations and explains how they constrain conclusions. |
| Health/privacy boundaries | Avoids diagnosis, health ranking, unsafe recommendations, and unnecessary personal health data; states appropriate privacy safeguards. |
| Scientific communication & revision | Explanation is coherent, causal, model-aware, and includes at least one defensible revision after critique. |

### Performance bands
- **36–40:** Advanced mastery
- **32–35:** Mastery
- **24–31:** Developing — revise before mastery
- **0–23:** Substantial reteaching/revision required

## Constructed Response 1 — Feedback & Systems — 20 points
Score each dimension from 0–4.

1. **Feedback sequence:** stimulus, receptor, coordinator, effectors, response are all correctly linked.
2. **Systems integration:** at least four relevant systems are connected mechanistically rather than merely listed.
3. **Causal reasoning:** explains how the response reduces the original deviation and returns conditions toward a functional range.
4. **Evidence/model boundaries:** distinguishes the fictional scenario from diagnosis and states at least two relevant limitations.
5. **Scientific communication:** organized, precise, and no broader than the evidence/model supports.

**Mastery: 16/20.**

## Constructed Response 2 — Response Data — 20 points
Score each dimension from 0–4.

1. **Calculation:** correctly calculates percent increase from 68 to 102 and shows defensible work.
2. **Pattern interpretation:** accurately describes the recovery sequence from 102 to 86 to 72 without inventing measurements.
3. **Physiological explanation:** links circulatory/respiratory regulation, cellular demand, gas transport, heat/waste handling, and recovery at an appropriate Grade 9 level.
4. **Limitations:** gives four meaningful reasons the short fictional dataset cannot diagnose health or fitness.
5. **Evidence language:** distinguishes observation from inference and uses calibrated, non-diagnostic language.

**Mastery: 16/20.**

## Verification rule
Record human verification only after reviewing the actual student artifacts. Mentor, Atlas, Evidence & Citation Studio, calculator, simulations, or PROSE may support learning and revision, but the first objective assessment attempt and final human-scored explanations must remain attributable to the student.
