Coordinated Response & Recovery

Analyze heart-rate and breathing-rate changes during a fictional low-intensity activity.

Safety

The fictional dataset is the default. Optional movement requires adult approval. Stop for pain, dizziness, faintness, unusual shortness of breath, or concern.

Privacy

Do not publish names, diagnoses, medicines, or identifiable health records.
