Synthesis map

Connect homeostasis, receptor, coordinator, effector, feedback, circulation, alveolus, villus, nephron, ADH, insulin, glucagon, neurone, hormone, antigen, antibody, evidence, and limitation.

Assessment sequence · 80% mastery gate

  1. Pass the 20-question mastery quiz at 16/20 or higher.
  2. Pass the 30-question cumulative assessment at 24/30 or higher.
  3. Complete corrections and earn 16/20 or higher on each constructed response through human scoring.
  4. Earn 32/40 or higher on the Integrated Systems Case Study through human scoring.
  5. Complete the Unit 03 reflection and educator/reviewer verification. Submission alone does not establish mastery.

Mastery principle: Unit completion records demonstrated learning, not merely page visits or assignment submission. Best demonstrated objective mastery is preserved across retakes.
