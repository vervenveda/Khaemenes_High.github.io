Synthesis & Mastery

Integrate genetic information, division, reproduction, meiosis, probability, variation, environment, evidence, and ethics.

Materials

Safe genetics learning

Use fictional organisms, supplied datasets, paper models, or digital tools. No biological samples or medical tests are required.

Genetic privacy and respect

Do not collect family medical history, ancestry, paternity evidence, reproductive information, or identifiable genetic data. Human worth is never ranked by genes.

Synthesis map

Connect DNA, gene, allele, chromosome, replication, mitosis, differentiation, meiosis, crossing over, assortment, fertilization, genotype, phenotype, probability, mutation, environment, consent, and privacy.

Assessment sequence

  1. Pass the quiz at 16/20.
  2. Correct errors.
  3. Pass the assessment at 24/30.
  4. Complete two constructed responses.
