Unit 04 · Day 13

Beyond Simple Dominance

Compare incomplete dominance, codominance, multiple alleles, polygenic traits, pleiotropy, and environment.

45–60 minutesModel and data basedPortfolio Evidence

Learning objectives

  • Distinguish incomplete dominance and codominance
  • Recognize polygenic traits
  • Explain genotype–environment interaction

Materials

  • Variation dataset
  • Notebook
Safe genetics learning

Use fictional organisms, supplied datasets, paper models, or digital tools. No biological samples or medical tests are required.

Genetic privacy and respect

Do not collect family medical history, ancestry, paternity evidence, reproductive information, or identifiable genetic data. Human worth is never ranked by genes.

Allele relationships

Incomplete dominance gives an intermediate heterozygote; codominance gives detectable expression of both products.

Many genes and effects

Polygenic traits involve multiple genes. Pleiotropy occurs when one gene influences multiple traits.

Environment and development

Conditions can affect phenotype without changing genotype.
