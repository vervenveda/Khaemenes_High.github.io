Unit 04 · Day 2

The Cell Cycle & DNA Replication

Model how cells grow, copy DNA, check conditions, divide the nucleus, and separate the cytoplasm.

45–60 minutesModel and data basedPortfolio Evidence

Learning objectives

  • Sequence G1, S, G2, mitosis, and cytokinesis
  • Explain why replication precedes mitosis
  • Describe checkpoints

Materials

  • Cell-cycle laboratory
  • Notebook
Safe genetics learning

Use fictional organisms, supplied datasets, paper models, or digital tools. No biological samples or medical tests are required.

Genetic privacy and respect

Do not collect family medical history, ancestry, paternity evidence, reproductive information, or identifiable genetic data. Human worth is never ranked by genes.

Interphase

G1 supports growth and function, S phase copies DNA, and G2 supports preparation. Interphase is active and usually much longer than mitosis.

Replicated chromosomes

After S phase, one chromosome has two sister chromatids. DNA amount doubles before chromosome number changes.

Misconception: An X-shaped replicated chromosome is not automatically a homologous pair.

Regulation

Checkpoints monitor DNA integrity, replication, and chromosome attachment. They reduce risk but are not perfect.
