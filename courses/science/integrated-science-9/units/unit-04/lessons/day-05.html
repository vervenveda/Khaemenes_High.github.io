Unit 04 · Day 5

Cell-Cycle Control & Evidence

Evaluate regulatory changes, abnormal growth, and why cancer cannot be reduced to one mutation or behavior.

45–60 minutesModel and data basedPortfolio Evidence

Learning objectives

  • Explain cell-cycle regulation
  • Describe cancer as a broad group of diseases
  • Evaluate claims without blame

Materials

  • Notebook
  • Fictional cell-count dataset
Safe genetics learning

Use fictional organisms, supplied datasets, paper models, or digital tools. No biological samples or medical tests are required.

Genetic privacy and respect

Do not collect family medical history, ancestry, paternity evidence, reproductive information, or identifiable genetic data. Human worth is never ranked by genes.

Regulated growth

Internal and external signals affect division, repair, specialization, and cell death. Multiple systems can be altered in abnormal growth.

Cancer is not one disease

Cancers differ by tissue, mutations, development, environment, immunity, and treatment response.

Ethics: Risk factors change probability; they do not prove why one individual became ill.
