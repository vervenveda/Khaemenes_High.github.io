Unit 04 · Day 10

Chromosome Inheritance Laboratory

Use a two-pair model to generate gametes and evaluate assumptions.

45–60 minutesModel and data basedPortfolio Evidence

Learning objectives

  • Generate gametes from AaBb
  • Explain equal expected probability
  • Identify why real frequencies may differ

Materials

  • Meiosis laboratory
  • Allele cards
Safe genetics learning

Use fictional organisms, supplied datasets, paper models, or digital tools. No biological samples or medical tests are required.

Genetic privacy and respect

Do not collect family medical history, ancestry, paternity evidence, reproductive information, or identifiable genetic data. Human worth is never ranked by genes.

Two-pair model

For unlinked AaBb, independent assortment produces AB, Ab, aB, and ab gametes with equal expected probability.

aB
ab

Assumptions

Genes are unlinked, gametes equally viable, mutation ignored, and sampling random.

Evidence

Generate 20 model gametes and compare with an expected five of each.
