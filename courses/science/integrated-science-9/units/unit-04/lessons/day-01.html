Unit 04 · Day 1

DNA, Genes, Alleles & Chromosomes

Relate genetic information across scales and distinguish DNA sequence, gene, allele, chromosome, genome, genotype, and phenotype.

45–60 minutesModel and data basedPortfolio Evidence

Learning objectives

  • Order genome, chromosome, gene, and nucleotide by scale
  • Define allele accurately
  • Distinguish genotype and phenotype

Materials

  • Notebook
  • Genetic scale template
Safe genetics learning

Use fictional organisms, supplied datasets, paper models, or digital tools. No biological samples or medical tests are required.

Genetic privacy and respect

Do not collect family medical history, ancestry, paternity evidence, reproductive information, or identifiable genetic data. Human worth is never ranked by genes.

Genetic information across scales

A genome is the complete genetic material. Eukaryotic genomes are organized into chromosomes—long DNA molecules packaged with proteins. Genes are DNA regions involved in functional products or regulation.

Boundary: Many traits involve multiple genes, regulation, development, environment, and chance.

Alleles and homologues

Alleles are variants of a gene. Homologous chromosomes carry corresponding loci but may carry different alleles.

Portfolio evidence

Create a nested diagram: organism → cell → nucleus → chromosome → DNA → gene → nucleotide. Add one limitation.
