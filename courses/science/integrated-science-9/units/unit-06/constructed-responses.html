Unit 06 · Rubric-scored reasoning

Constructed Responses

Response 1 · Atomic identity and isotope evidence

A particle is written as ³⁷₁₇Cl⁻. Determine protons, neutrons, electrons, mass number, atomic number, and net charge. Explain which change produces another chlorine isotope and which creates a different element. Use the supplied chlorine masses and abundances to calculate weighted atomic mass. Distinguish isotope mass, mass number, and standard atomic weight, and identify two limitations.

Response 2 · Periodic prediction from evidence

Compare sodium through argon across Period 3 using atomic number, shell configuration, outer electrons, broad metallic character, and common simple ions. Explain three patterns, one boundary or exception, and predict the group, period, and ion tendency of atomic number 19. State why the shell model is useful but not literal.
