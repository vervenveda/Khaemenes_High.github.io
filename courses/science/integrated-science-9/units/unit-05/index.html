Evidence-first three-week sequence

Ecological systems, population change, origins evidence, worldviews, and stewardship

Real observations and public datasets are prioritized. Models, historical inferences, and worldview claims are labeled rather than presented as direct observations.

Week 3 · Evolutionary evidence, origins interpretations, and stewardship

11

Variation, Natural Selection & Observed Change
