Unit 05 · Source literacy

Evidence & Worldview Source Dossier

Use empirical records for measurements and observations, official standards for course alignment, and advocacy sources to represent each worldview in its own words.

Critical source rule

A fossil database, population table, or DNA record is not the same type of source as an editorial or organizational worldview statement. Advocacy sources are valuable for accurately reporting what a position argues, but their empirical claims should be traced to underlying methods and evidence.

Real ecosystem population record

U.S. National Park Service · Isle Royale wolf and moose populations

Annual population estimates and explanatory context. Use as a measured time series, not a controlled one-factor experiment.
