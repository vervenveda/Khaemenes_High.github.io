Unit 05 · Written scientific reasoning

Constructed Responses

Response 1 · Population and ecosystem evidence

Using the Isle Royale wolf–moose record, identify two directly observed population patterns, calculate one absolute or percentage change, and construct a causal explanation that includes predator–prey interaction plus at least three alternative factors. State why the time series alone cannot isolate one cause and propose two additional measurements that would strengthen the explanation.

Response 2 · Origins evidence and interpretation

Using the equid and human-origins source dossier, distinguish at least five direct observations or measurements from classifications, historical ancestry inferences, and worldview conclusions. Accurately compare an evolutionary common-descent interpretation with at least two creation/design interpretations. State what the evidence strongly establishes, what remains uncertain, and what evidence could revise your position. Your personal belief is not scored.
