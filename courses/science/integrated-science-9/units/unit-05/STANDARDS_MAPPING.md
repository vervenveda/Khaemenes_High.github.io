## International benchmark roles

- NGSS HS-LS2-1–8: population data, matter and energy, interactions, resilience, group behavior, biodiversity, and design
- NGSS HS-LS4-1–6: evidence, variation, natural selection, adaptation, environmental change, and biodiversity solutions
- Cambridge IGCSE Biology 0610/0970 (2026–2028): variation and selection; organisms and environment; human influences and conservation
- Australian Curriculum V9 AC9S9U01 and AC9S10U02: ecosystem interdependence and evidence for evolution by natural selection
- Academy enrichment: observation–inference–worldview distinctions and respectful comparative origins literacy

These references are curriculum-planning anchors, not claims of formal authorization or automatic legal equivalence.
