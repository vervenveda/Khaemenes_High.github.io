Transit-model boundaries

The depth relation assumes a simple central transit and uniform stellar brightness. Limb darkening, grazing geometry, starspots, blended stars, eclipsing binaries, cadence, and noise can alter the signal. A habitable-zone orbit does not prove habitability or life.
