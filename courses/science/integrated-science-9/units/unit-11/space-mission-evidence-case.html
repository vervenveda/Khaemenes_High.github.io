Unit 11 · Evidence-based space systems

Space Mission Evidence Case

Evaluate a real or proposed telescope, spacecraft, planetary mission, solar observatory, exoplanet survey, Earth-orbit system, debris-removal project, or astronomy data program.

Required evidence

Case workspace

Recommended structure: question and users; target and scale; instrument and wavelength; orbit or platform; direct data; calculations; processing; evidence classification; uncertainty and alternatives; mission benefits; costs and risks; debris and planetary protection; cultural and access considerations; decision and monitoring plan.

Observation, privacy, and operational boundary

Do not use lasers, launch devices, radio transmitters, high-voltage equipment, restricted tracking systems, private-location surveillance, or unfiltered solar viewing. Do not publish personal schedules, private observation locations, protected cultural knowledge, or sensitive infrastructure details.
