# Unit 11 Pacing Guide — Space Systems, Astronomy & Cosmic Evidence

**Course:** KH-SCI-IIS9 · International Integrated Science 9  
**Course weeks:** 31–33  
**Mastery target:** 80% or higher  
**Delivery:** 15 detailed daily lessons across three instructional weeks, with weekly portal mastery checks and a separate unit record.

## Three-week teaching rhythm

- Week 31 — apparent sky motion, seasons, lunar phases, eclipses, scale, gravity, orbital motion, and Solar-System formation
- Week 32 — solar structure, spectra, telescope bands, stellar properties, life cycles, and element formation
- Week 33 — exoplanets, galaxies, redshift, Hubble relationships, CMB evidence, missions, uncertainty, and synthesis

Each week should retain the Science 9 rhythm:

1. **Monday — Phenomenon and vocabulary:** observe first, separate observation from interpretation, and build an initial model.
2. **Tuesday — Concept and model:** read, annotate, revise the model, and identify assumptions or limitations.
3. **Wednesday — Evidence and data:** calculate, graph, classify, compare, or evaluate source evidence.
4. **Thursday — Investigation / fieldwork / engineering:** complete the named investigation with safety and stop conditions visible before procedures.
5. **Friday — Synthesis:** complete Claim–Evidence–Reasoning, corrections, reflection, portfolio evidence, and the randomized open-book weekly mastery check.

## Assessment sequence

- Weekly portal mastery checks are **open-book formative**, randomized from reviewed banks, and require **80%** to clear the next weekly mastery gate.
- The generated form remains stable during an active attempt and through refresh.
- A later retry may generate a **reshuffled form** from the larger bank.
- The unit mastery quiz and unit assessment remain separate from the weekly portal check.
- Corrections should identify the misconception or reasoning error, not merely copy the answer.
- Public practice and formative records do not substitute for separately supervised official midterm/final mastery.

## Evidence expectations

By the end of Unit 11, the learner should be able to show:

- completed detailed daily lessons recorded in the learner-scoped unit record;
- dated investigation or model evidence;
- calculations with units and stated assumptions where relevant;
- at least one graph, table, model, or structured source comparison;
- Claim–Evidence–Reasoning or equivalent scientific explanation;
- corrections after missed mastery items;
- unit quiz and assessment evidence at the Academy mastery threshold;
- a final unit reflection identifying what changed in the learner’s model or reasoning.

## Pathway differentiation

**Foundation:** vocabulary support, guided models, worked examples, structured tables, smaller calculation steps, and teacher-selected data.  
**Core:** full Grade 9 sequence, required investigations, calculations, source evaluation, and independent reasoning.  
**Extended:** unfamiliar applications, sensitivity/uncertainty analysis, deeper modeling, research comparison, and more independent design choices.

## Safety and evidence boundary

Preserve the corrected canonical Weeks 31–33 astronomy sequence. Clearly distinguish direct observation, processed data, inference, and cosmological model.

No simulation, AI tool, mentor, or external source should be presented as a substitute for the learner’s own evidence, calculation, model defense, or protected assessment work.

## Teacher closeout

Before advancing from Unit 11, verify:

- learner identity matches the active Academy Student Profile;
- detailed lesson completion belongs to that learner;
- required investigation evidence is present;
- mastery records are internally plausible and at or above 80%;
- any human-scored work has a named reviewer where required;
- exported backup/portfolio evidence is available when moving devices;
- the learner can explain at least one important model limitation from the unit.
