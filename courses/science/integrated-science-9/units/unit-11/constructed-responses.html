Unit 11 · Rubric-scored cosmic reasoning

Constructed Responses

Response 1 · Scale, orbit, spectra, and model limits

A spacecraft signal travels 1.80 × 10¹¹ m to Earth. Using c = 3.00 × 10⁸ m/s, calculate its one-way light time in seconds and minutes. A planet follows a circular approximation with average radius 2.00 × 10¹¹ m and period 5.00 × 10⁷ s. Calculate average orbital speed. A spectral line with rest wavelength 656.3 nm is observed at 657.0 nm. Calculate redshift and the small-shift speed approximation using c = 300,000 km/s. Explain the directions of velocity and gravitational acceleration in orbit, identify four model limitations, and state three observation-safety boundaries.

Response 2 · Transit, galaxies, expansion, and evidence

A star dims by 0.81% during transits recorded at days 1.2, 6.2, and 11.2. Calculate fractional depth, planet-to-star radius ratio, and period. A galaxy is modeled at 80 Mpc using H₀ = 70 km/s/Mpc. Calculate its Hubble-law speed and the corresponding Hubble time in billions of years. Explain why neither a transit nor habitable-zone location proves life. Then construct an evidence-based Big Bang explanation using galaxy redshift, cosmic microwave background radiation, and primordial composition while distinguishing observation, calculation, model, extrapolation, and open question.
