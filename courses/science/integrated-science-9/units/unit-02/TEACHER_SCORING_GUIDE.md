# Unit 02 Teacher / Parent-Educator Scoring Guide

## Mastery principle
Unit 02 follows the course-wide **≥80% mastery standard**. Objective tests, performance tasks, and constructed responses are separate forms of evidence. Submission or page completion is not equivalent to mastery.

## Cell Model Performance Task — 40 points
Score each dimension from 0–5. A score of **32/40 or higher** is required.

| Dimension | 5 — Mastery evidence | 3 — Developing | 1 — Limited / inaccurate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cell identification & classification | Cell type is precisely named and classification is supported with appropriate evidence | Cell type is broadly correct but evidence is incomplete | Cell type/classification is unclear or inaccurate |
| Structures selected | Appropriate major structures are represented; universal/common/optional features are distinguished | Mostly appropriate structures with minor omissions/confusions | Major structures missing or biologically inappropriate |
| Structure–function reasoning | Labels explain mechanisms connecting structure to function | Functions are mostly correct but mechanisms are thin | Labels are names only or contain major misconceptions |
| Scale & spatial honesty | Scale is quantified where feasible or explicitly marked not-to-scale; distortions are explained | Scale status is stated but weakly defended | Scale is misleading or unstated |
| Model limitations | At least three specific limitations explain what the model cannot establish | Limitations are present but generic | Limitations absent or treated as defects that invalidate all use |
| Scientific process connection | Model accurately connects to transport, energy, specialization, or another Unit 02 mechanism | Connection is present but mostly descriptive | Connection is absent or inaccurate |
| Evidence / provenance | External images, measurements, or claims are traceable; conventions are distinguished from evidence | Some provenance is present | Sources/evidence cannot be traced or conventions are presented as observations |
| Revision & defense | Student tests the model, identifies a misleading feature or weakness, and makes/defends a revision | Revision is superficial | No meaningful testing, defense, or revision |

**Scoring note:** Intermediate scores 2 and 4 may be used when evidence falls between descriptors.

## Constructed Response 1 — Microscopy & Scale — 20 points
A score of **16/20 or higher** is required.

- Calculation and units: 0–4
- Conversion / scale reasoning: 0–4
- Two defensible observations supporting classification: 0–4
- Observation vs inference / evidence boundary: 0–4
- Limitation of a single image and scientific clarity: 0–4

Mastery response should calculate **36 mm ÷ 600 = 0.060 mm = 60 µm**, while explaining that classification evidence from one image remains bounded by what the image and method can actually show.

## Constructed Response 2 — Membrane Transport CER — 20 points
A score of **16/20 or higher** is required.

- Claim aligned to the supplied data: 0–4
- Quantitative evidence and correct use of percent mass change: 0–4
- Osmosis / membrane-transport reasoning: 0–4
- Zero-change estimate and interpolation assumption: 0–4
- Two limitations plus one meaningful improvement: 0–4

A defensible local linear interpolation places the zero-change estimate between 0.3 M and 0.5 M, approximately **0.414 M**. Students must identify it as an estimate based on the supplied means and interpolation assumption, not as the exact internal concentration of every cell.

## Verification rule
Human-scored mastery is complete only when all three conditions are met:

- Cell model: **≥32/40**
- Constructed Response 1: **≥16/20**
- Constructed Response 2: **≥16/20**

If any component is below mastery, the learner should revise the specific weakness and submit new evidence. Do not average a below-mastery component away with a stronger score elsewhere.

## Tool and Mentor boundary
Mentor, Evidence & Citation Studio, Atlas Evidence Analysis, simulations, calculators, and PROSE may support instruction, source organization, critique, correction, and revision. The first mastery attempt and final scored evidence must remain attributable to the student. Record meaningful tool-assisted revisions in the portfolio provenance record.
