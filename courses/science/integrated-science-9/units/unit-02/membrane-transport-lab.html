Required Dataset Laboratory

Potato Tissue, Solute Concentration & Osmosis

Calculate percent mass change and estimate the concentration associated with no net mass change.

Dataset status: Fictional but biologically plausible values for controlled instructional analysis.

Method summary

Equal-size potato cylinders began at 5.00 g, were placed in different sucrose concentrations for the same time, blotted consistently, and reweighed.

Tasks

  1. Verify each percent change.
  2. Graph concentration against percent change.
  3. Identify approximate zero change.
  4. Explain net water movement at 0.0 and 0.8 mol/L.
  5. State whether zero mass change means water molecules stopped.
  6. Identify four controls.
  7. Identify two limitations and improvements.
  8. Write a CER conclusion limited to the tested tissue and concentrations.
