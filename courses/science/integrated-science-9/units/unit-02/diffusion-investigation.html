Required Investigation · Two Equivalent Pathways

Diffusion in Cool and Warm Water

Investigate or analyze how temperature affects the spread of food coloring in still water.

Safety

Use cool and warm tap water only; keep warm water at or below 40°C. Do not use boiling water. Protect clothing and surfaces. Keep cups away from electronics and walking areas.

Question

How does water temperature affect the diameter of a food-coloring cloud over 120 seconds when cup shape, water volume, drop size, and observation method are controlled?

Pathway A · Direct observation

  1. Add equal water volumes to two cups.
  2. Use cool water in one and warm tap water in the other.
  3. Allow currents to settle.
  4. Add one equal drop without stirring.
  5. Measure cloud diameter at 0, 30, 60, 90, and 120 seconds.
  6. Repeat when possible.

Analysis

  1. Graph time against diameter.
  2. Calculate average diameter increase per second from 0–120 s.
  3. State the observed relationship.
  4. Identify three controls.
  5. Explain why convection could confound interpretation.
  6. State one limitation of cloud diameter.
