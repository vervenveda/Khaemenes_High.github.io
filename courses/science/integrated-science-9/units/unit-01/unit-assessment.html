Objective Result

Constructed Response 1 · Investigation Design

Scenario: Design a safe investigation of how drop height affects the mean bounce height of the same ball on the same surface.

Your response must include a testable question; preregistered directional prediction; independent/dependent variables; operational definitions for drop height and bounce height; at least five meaningful controls; at least five trials per height; instrument/resolution considerations; safety and stop conditions; raw-data table headings; graph choice with axes/units; anomaly/deviation handling; and two limitations with matched improvements.

Constructed Response 2 · Dataset Reasoning

A pendulum study reports mean periods of 0.90 s at 20 cm, 1.10 s at 30 cm, 1.28 s at 40 cm, 1.43 s at 50 cm, and 1.55 s at 60 cm.

Write a CER explanation that: (1) describes the numerical pattern and tested range, (2) makes a claim no broader than these data, (3) cites relevant values as evidence, (4) gives cautious scientific reasoning, (5) states one measurement/design limitation, (6) proposes one specific improvement, and (7) identifies one extrapolation the dataset does not justify.
