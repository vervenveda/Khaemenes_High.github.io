Unit 09 · Binary encoding and controlled noise

Information Signal Laboratory

Encode short text as 8-bit character data with an even-parity check, then introduce controlled bit flips and evaluate what the error check detects.

Information-model boundary

This is a character-code and parity demonstration. Real systems use specified encodings, framing, synchronization, modulation, compression, encryption, stronger error correction, and physical channels.

Technology responsibility

Digital reliability does not eliminate privacy, security, access, environmental, energy, or accessibility concerns.
