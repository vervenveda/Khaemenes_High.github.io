Unit 09 · Evidence-based engineering

Communication Engineering Case

Evaluate a real wired, optical, acoustic, radio, sensing, or assistive communication system without constructing unsafe electrical or transmitting equipment.

Required evidence

Case workspace

Recommended structure: context and users; source and evidence; energy source; circuit/signal path; carrier and wave behavior; encoding; receiver; noise and error control; calculations; model limits; privacy/security/access; safety and environmental implications.

Engineering safety boundary

Do not access mains wiring, open powered devices, build transmitters, aim lasers, create high-volume sound, dismantle batteries, or use strong magnets near medical devices. Analyze authoritative records and approved models instead.
