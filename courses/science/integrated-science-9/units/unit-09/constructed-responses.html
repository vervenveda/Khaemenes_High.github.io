Unit 09 · Rubric-scored reasoning

Constructed Responses

Response 1 · Circuit evidence and electromagnetic conversion

A 6.0 V source is connected to 10 Ω and 20 Ω resistors. Calculate equivalent resistance, total current, component voltage/current, and total power when the resistors are in series and when they are in parallel. Explain why adding a parallel branch lowers equivalent resistance. Then describe how an electromagnet, motor, generator, and transformer each use current or changing magnetic fields. State at least three ideal-model limitations and two safety boundaries.

Response 2 · Waves and digital information

A wave travels at 300 m/s with frequency 150 Hz. Calculate wavelength and period. Explain what changes and what remains constant during refraction into a slower medium. Compare reflection, diffraction, interference, and photon evidence. Then encode the capital letter A as 8-bit ASCII, explain even parity, describe one error parity can detect and one it can miss, and evaluate two advantages and three tradeoffs of digital information transfer.
