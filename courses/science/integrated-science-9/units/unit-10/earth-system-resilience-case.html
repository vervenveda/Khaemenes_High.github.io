Unit 10 · Evidence-based stewardship and design

Earth-System Resilience Case

Evaluate a real watershed, erosion, earthquake, volcano, coastal, groundwater, nutrient, resource, heat, drought, flood, wildfire-smoke, or climate-risk challenge without entering a hazardous location.

Required evidence

Case workspace

Recommended structure: location and communities; system boundary and scale; reservoirs and fluxes; direct evidence; calculations; causal model; feedbacks; uncertainty; options; costs and benefits; access and justice; safety; monitoring plan; conclusion.

Field and community safety boundary

Do not enter disaster zones, floodwater, caves, mines, wildfire areas, unstable slopes, sinkholes, contaminated sites, restricted lands, sensitive habitats, archaeological locations, or private property. Protect exact locations of vulnerable cultural and ecological resources.
