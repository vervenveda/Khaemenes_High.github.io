Unit 10 · Rubric-scored systems reasoning

Constructed Responses

Response 1 · Interior, plates, water, and surface change

A station records a P-wave arrival at 14.0 s and an S-wave arrival at 24.0 s. Using the labeled classroom factor 8.4 km per second of S–P lag, calculate the practice distance estimate and explain why one station cannot locate an epicenter. A 3 km² watershed receives 120 mm precipitation, loses 50 mm to evapotranspiration and 45 mm to runoff. Calculate storage change and runoff volume. Connect plate processes, uplift, weathering, erosion, sediment transport, and watershed form in a causal model. State four model or measurement limitations and three safety boundaries.

Response 2 · Carbon, nutrients, climate, and resilience

A carbon reservoir contains 2400 units, receives 90 units/year and loses 70 units/year. Calculate net annual change, modeled amount after one year, and simple residence time using total outflow. An energy-balance model uses incoming solar energy 340 W/m², albedo 0.29 and outgoing energy 239 W/m². Calculate reflected energy, absorbed energy, and net imbalance. Explain one fast and one slow carbon pathway, compare nitrogen and phosphorus cycling, describe eutrophication, distinguish forcing from feedback, and evaluate one mitigation and one adaptation strategy with uncertainty and justice.
