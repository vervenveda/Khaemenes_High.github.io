Unit 08 · Evidence-based engineering

Safe-Motion Engineering Case

Evaluate a real transport, sports, mobility, lifting, braking, or protective design using motion, force, momentum, energy, power, evidence, and safety.

Required evidence

Case workspace

Recommended structure: source and context; system boundary; direct evidence; graph or diagram; calculations; model assumptions; design tradeoffs; uncertainty; safety and accessibility; conclusion.

Engineering safety boundary

Do not perform crash, restraint, helmet, impact, road, vehicle, height, heavy-load, or human-subject testing. Use authoritative records, approved videos, manufacturer data with independent verification, mathematical models, or light low-speed tabletop systems.
