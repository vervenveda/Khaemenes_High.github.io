Unit 08 · Rubric-scored reasoning

Constructed Responses

Response 1 · Motion, graphs, and force

A 5 kg cart moves from position 0 m at 0 s to 3 m at 2 s, 8 m at 4 s, and 15 m at 6 s. Calculate interval velocities and overall average velocity. Explain how the graph slope changes. A 4 N resistive force acts left while a pulling force of 14 N acts right. Calculate net force and acceleration. Draw a free-body diagram, state the reference direction, and identify two measurement or model limitations.

Response 2 · Momentum, energy, and engineering safety

A 2 kg cart moving at 4 m/s collides with a 2 kg cart initially at rest and they move together. Calculate total momentum before, shared speed after, and initial and final kinetic energy. Explain why momentum can be conserved while kinetic energy decreases. If the moving system stops in 0.20 s, calculate the average force magnitude; then calculate it for 0.50 s. Explain the safety principle without claiming that stopping time alone determines injury. Identify one safe method of studying the system.
