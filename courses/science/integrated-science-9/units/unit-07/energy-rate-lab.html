ACS-style effervescent-tablet protocol

With adult approval and goggles, use open cups, equal water volumes, and one tablet product. Compare one factor at a time: whole/broken/powdered tablet or cold/room-temperature/warm tap water. Do not ingest materials, use boiling water, seal the reaction, or combine with other chemicals.
