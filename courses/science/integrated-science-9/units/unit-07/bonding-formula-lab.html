Unit 07 · Charge-balance model

Bonding & Ionic Formula Laboratory

Select actual common ions and derive the smallest neutral whole-number formula. The calculator is a model of charge balance, not evidence that ionic solids consist of isolated molecules.

Formula meaning

The subscripts represent the smallest ion ratio in an ionic solid. They do not indicate a single free-standing molecule. Polyatomic-ion parentheses group a repeated ion.

Electron and bonding evidence

Use Unit 06 electron configurations to explain cation and anion formation. Then distinguish electron transfer as a bookkeeping model from the electrostatic attraction that constitutes the ionic lattice.
