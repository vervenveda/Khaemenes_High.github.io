Equation & Conservation Laboratory

Adjust coefficients for documented chemical reactions and verify that every atom type is conserved.

Conservation boundary

Coefficients may be changed because they represent amounts. Subscripts may not be changed because they define substance composition. The tool audits atoms, not charge or energy.

No direct reaction is required

Combustion, strong-acid, high-temperature, and gas-producing examples are equation records only unless performed under a qualified laboratory protocol.
