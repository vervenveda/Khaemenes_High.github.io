Chemical safety boundary

Wear properly fitting splash goggles for any approved hands-on activity. Never mix household cleaners, heat sealed containers, taste substances, burn materials, handle strong acids or bases, or use unknown chemicals. The effervescent-tablet option requires adult approval, water only, open containers, and careful handling of warm—not boiling—water.

Evidence standard

Separate measured properties, observed changes, calculated atom counts, published thermochemical values, and explanatory models. A simulation result is not independent experimental evidence.

Synthesis

A complete explanation links electron arrangements to bonding, bonding to structure, structure to properties, and atom rearrangement to reactions.

Assessment sequence

Pass four laboratory checks at 7/8, submit the case study, pass the quiz at 16/20, pass the assessment at 24/30, and complete reflection.

Integrity

Every model must identify what is directly measured, calculated, represented, or inferred.

Portfolio evidence

Submit the real-reaction case, corrections, constructed responses, and final reflection.

Model boundary

Dot-and-cross, ball-and-stick, lattice, particle-collision, and energy-pathway diagrams are useful representations. They distort scale, motion, electron density, and complexity.

Essential question and conceptual model

Essential question: How can bonding, structure, equations, energetics, rate, and equilibrium be connected into one coherent chemical explanation?

Chemical behavior links particle structure to observable evidence. Bonding and intermolecular interactions influence properties; formulas and equations represent composition and atom conservation; energetics describes transfer and pathway; kinetics describes rate; equilibrium describes competing forward and reverse processes. Strong explanations choose the right model and state its limits.

Worked reasoning example

For a reversible exothermic gas reaction, a complete explanation could include correct formulas and balanced equation, an energy profile, factors affecting rate, and a qualitative equilibrium prediction. A catalyst would increase rate but not change ΔH or equilibrium position; pressure effects depend on gas-particle counts; temperature can influence both rate and equilibrium.

Vocabulary and misconception checks

Vocabulary: structure–property relationship · conservation · energetics · kinetics · equilibrium · model limit

Misconception check: One rule—such as the octet rule, collision theory, or Le Chatelier principle—cannot explain every feature of a chemical system.

Retrieval and explanation practice

Answer from memory first. Then check formulas, charge balance, atom counts, units, energy direction, evidence quality, and the limits of each model.

  1. Build a concept map linking bonding to properties and reactions to conservation.
  2. Balance one equation and identify its energy/rate evidence.
  3. Explain rate vs equilibrium using the same reaction.
  4. Repair one misconception from the unit.

Mentor evidence conference

Bring your structure model, formula, balanced equation, rate calculation, or equilibrium explanation to the Science Mentor. The mentor may challenge your reasoning but does not provide protected assessment answers.
