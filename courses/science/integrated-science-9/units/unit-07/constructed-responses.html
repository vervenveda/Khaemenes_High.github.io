Unit 07 · Rubric-scored reasoning

Constructed Responses

Response 1 · Structure, formula, and properties

Compare sodium chloride, carbon dioxide, graphite, and copper. For each, state the formula or element symbol, dominant bonding/structure model, room-condition state, electrical behavior, and one mechanical or phase-change property. Explain how the structure model accounts for the evidence, identify one limitation of each model, and clarify why an ionic formula is a ratio rather than a molecule.

Response 2 · Reaction, conservation, energy, and rate

Use the combustion of methane and the decomposition of calcium carbonate. Balance both equations and audit C, H, O, and Ca atoms. Explain conservation in a closed system, classify each reaction as exothermic or endothermic using the supplied ΔH records, sketch and label a pathway diagram, and explain how temperature or a catalyst affects rate without changing atom conservation. Include one measurement limitation and one safety boundary.
