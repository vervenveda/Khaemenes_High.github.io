Unit 12 · Final integrated science portfolio

Sustainable Engineering Capstone

Design or evaluate a responsible solution to a meaningful challenge without constructing or deploying a hazardous or regulated system.

Required portfolio evidence

Portfolio workspace

Recommended order: executive summary; need and stakeholders; system map; baseline; specifications; research; concept set; decision matrix; selected design; science basis; model/prototype; test plan and results; uncertainty; life cycle; risks and controls; accessibility and justice; revision history; implementation boundary; monitoring; communication; references.

Each submission saves a dated revision of this portfolio locally for the selected learner.

Non-deployment boundary

This Grade 9 capstone does not authorize construction or deployment of medical, electrical, structural, transportation, water-treatment, food-production, public-safety, surveillance, biological, chemical, weapons, or regulated infrastructure. Use safe models, supplied data, simulations, and educator-approved prototypes.
