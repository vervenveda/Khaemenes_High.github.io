Unit 12 · Rubric-scored engineering reasoning

Constructed Responses

Response 1 · Concept selection and test evidence

Three concepts are scored on performance, cost, environmental impact, and accessibility with weights 40%, 25%, 20%, and 15%. Concept A scores 8, 5, 8, 7; B scores 7, 9, 6, 8; C scores 9, 6, 7, 5. Concept C fails a hard safety constraint. Calculate all weighted scores, identify the feasible recommendation, and conduct a qualitative sensitivity analysis. A baseline has trials 20, 21, 19, 20, 20 and the selected prototype has 15, 16, 14, 15, 15; lower is better and the threshold is 16. Calculate means, ranges, percent improvement, and observed success rate. Explain systematic bias, random variation, test limitations, and the next redesign decision.

Response 2 · Life cycle, risk, reliability, and responsibility

A product uses 10 kg of material at 5 impact units/kg, 20 manufacturing units, transport of 0.01 tonnes for 500 km at 0.05 units/(t·km), 100 kWh of lifetime energy at 0.4 units/kWh, a 5-year life, and a separately justified 10-unit recovery credit. Eight kilograms are recovered. Calculate gross impact, net impact, annualized impact, and recovery rate. Initial risk categories are probability 4 and severity 5; after controls they are 2 and 4. Three required independent components have reliabilities 0.98, 0.95, and 0.99. Calculate initial and residual screening risk, percentage score reduction, and series reliability. Evaluate one design revision using safety, accessibility, privacy, environmental justice, maintenance, resilience, and residual-risk authorization.
