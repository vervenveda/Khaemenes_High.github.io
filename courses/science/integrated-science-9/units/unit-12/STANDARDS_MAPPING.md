## International benchmark roles

- NGSS HS-ETS1-1–4: criteria, constraints, decomposition, tradeoffs, evaluation, modeling, simulation and optimization
- Cambridge IGCSE Design & Technology 0445: creative problem solving, planning, technical knowledge, materials, making and communication
- Australian Curriculum Design and Technologies Years 9–10: preferred futures, criteria, sustainability, ethics, life-cycle thinking and project management
- UNESCO Engineering for Sustainable Development and ESD for 2030: inclusive action for human needs, environmental integrity, economic viability and just societies
- IB MYP Sciences and PISA scientific literacy: inquiry, systems, evidence, evaluation, impacts and communication

These references are curriculum-planning anchors, not claims of formal authorization or automatic legal equivalence.

## Evidence and release rules

1. Lesson completion evidence is not interchangeable with mastery evidence.
2. Automatically scored items must have one definitive, defensible answer and an explanation.
3. Constructed responses require an explicit evidence-based rubric or educator review.
4. Investigations must follow the course laboratory-safety tier, supervision, disposal, accessibility and stop-condition rules.
5. Student evidence remains learner-scoped and local-first unless intentionally transferred through an approved Academy record contract.
6. Public practice assessments do not replace the protected, supervised official midterm/final authority.
7. Any exact standards code used for formal reporting must be rechecked against the current official framework and jurisdiction.
