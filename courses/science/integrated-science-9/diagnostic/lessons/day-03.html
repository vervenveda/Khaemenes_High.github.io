Unit 00 · Day 3

Measurement & SI Units

Use suitable units and tools, record repeated measurements honestly, and establish a complete science-notebook standard.

45–75 minutes Tier 1 · Home-safe Portfolio Evidence

Learning objectives

  • Select appropriate units and measuring tools
  • Record repeated measurements with units
  • Describe variation without changing raw data

Materials

  • Metric ruler or measuring tape
  • Three safe household objects
  • Science notebook
  • Calculator optional
Tier 1 · Home-safe

Follow the written procedure. Stop whenever materials, supervision, health needs, or environmental conditions differ from the lesson.

1. Quantity, unit, and tool

A measurement needs a number and a unit. Select a tool whose range and smallest marked interval are appropriate for the object or event.

QuantityCommon SI unitPossible tool
Lengthmeter (m)ruler or measuring tape

2. Repeated-measurement activity

  1. Select three safe household objects.
  2. Measure one dimension of each object three times in millimeters.
  3. Record every result without changing an inconvenient value.
  4. Calculate the range: largest measurement minus smallest measurement.
  5. Explain one likely source of variation.
