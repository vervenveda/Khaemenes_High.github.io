Unit 00 · Day 5

Readiness & Learning Goals

Use diagnostic evidence to select supports, establish a portfolio, and set a personal scientific inquiry goal.

45–75 minutes Tier 1 · Home-safe Portfolio Evidence

Learning objectives

  • Complete the readiness diagnostic
  • Interpret results without attaching a permanent label
  • Set up the course portfolio and define a learning goal

Materials

  • Device or printed diagnostic
  • Science notebook
  • Portfolio folder or digital directory
Tier 1 · Home-safe

Follow the written procedure. Stop whenever materials, supervision, health needs, or environmental conditions differ from the lesson.

1. Complete the readiness diagnostic

The diagnostic identifies strengths and review needs. It is untimed, may be retaken, and is not a permanent grade.

Open Readiness Diagnostic

2. Interpret results responsibly

  • Use domain results to select supports, not to label ability.
  • Begin with the pathway that provides an appropriate level of support.
  • Change pathways by skill when needed.
  • Correct misconceptions and retain evidence of growth.
Reflection

Identify one current strength, one skill to review, and one strategy that will help you learn science this year.

3. Establish the portfolio and goal

The portfolio will preserve investigations, graphs, source evaluations, revisions, projects, assessments, and reflections.
