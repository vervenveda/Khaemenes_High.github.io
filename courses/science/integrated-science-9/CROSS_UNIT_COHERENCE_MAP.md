# Science 9 — Cross-Unit Coherence Map

## Purpose
Integrated Science 9 is one course, not twelve disconnected mini-courses. The bridges below are required conceptual transfers.

| From | To | Required bridge |
|---|---|---|
| U01 measurement/evidence | all units | units, uncertainty, source quality, observation vs inference |
| U02 cell membranes & transport | U03 homeostasis | gradients and regulated exchange support whole-organism stability |
| U03 feedback | U05 ecosystems / U10 climate / U12 engineering | stability depends on interacting feedbacks at different scales |
| U04 inheritance & probability | U05 evolution | inherited variation + differential reproductive success changes populations |
| U05 energy/matter in ecosystems | U06–07 chemistry | biological matter is made of atoms; reactions rearrange conserved atoms |
| U06 electron structure | U07 bonding/reactions | valence/electrostatic models explain bonding and chemical behavior |
| U07 reaction energy/rates | U08 energy | chemical and mechanical systems share conservation and rate reasoning |
| U08 force/energy | U09 electricity/waves | fields, energy transfer, power, and quantitative models extend into circuits/signals |
| U09 waves/information | U11 astronomy | spectra and electromagnetic radiation carry evidence across space |
| U10 Earth cycles/climate | U11 planetary/solar systems | Earth is one planetary system receiving solar energy and interacting with space environment |
| U10–11 evidence | U12 engineering | design decisions must use system models, uncertainty, trade-offs, and source quality |

## Boundary synthesis prompts
At the end of every unit, students should be able to answer one bridge question:
- **U03 → U04:** How can stable body systems depend on accurate cell division and gene expression?
- **U04 → U05:** How does inherited variation become population-level change?
- **U05 → U06:** What does an ecosystem matter-flow diagram leave out about the atoms involved?
- **U06 → U07:** How does electron arrangement help predict bonding?
- **U07 → U08:** How is conservation used differently in chemical and mechanical systems?
- **U08 → U09:** How do energy, force, and rate ideas appear in electrical systems?
- **U09 → U10:** How can sensors, waves, and data support Earth-system evidence?
- **U10 → U11:** How does incoming solar energy connect Earth climate to astronomy?
- **U11 → U12:** How should scientific uncertainty affect an engineering decision?

## Certification expectation
Quarterly and final assessments should sample these bridges, not only same-unit recall.
