# Science 9 — Spiral Retrieval & Vocabulary Map

## Purpose
This map prevents vocabulary from becoming unit-isolated memorization. Core terms are introduced, used in context, retrieved after delay, and transferred into later disciplines.

## Retrieval cadence
- **Same lesson:** define, use, explain.
- **2–5 lessons later:** retrieve without notes.
- **Next unit:** use the term in a new scientific context.
- **Quarterly investigation:** apply across disciplines.
- **Midterm/final practice:** cumulative retrieval with explanation, not definition-only recall.

## Cross-course vocabulary families

| Family | Introduced / strengthened | Required later transfer |
|---|---|---|
| evidence · observation · inference · model · uncertainty | U00–U01 | every unit; quarterly investigations |
| system · boundary · input · output · feedback | U01–U03 | ecosystems, circuits, Earth systems, engineering |
| structure · function · scale | U02–U04 | atoms, materials, waves, planetary systems |
| energy · transfer · conservation · efficiency | U02/U05 | chemistry, mechanics, circuits, climate, design |
| matter · particle · atom · molecule · reservoir · flux | U05–U07 | Earth cycles and engineering life-cycle analysis |
| variation · probability · population · sampling | U04–U05 | isotope abundance, experimental uncertainty, reliability |
| force · interaction · field · gradient | U06–U09 | gravity, electromagnetism, transport, Earth processes |
| rate · change · equilibrium · stability | U03/U07 | motion, circuits, climate, resilient design |
| signal · information · noise · source quality | U01/U09 | astronomy, climate evidence, engineering communication |
| criteria · constraint · trade-off · optimization | recurring engineering thread | U12 capstone |

## Quarterly retrieval targets
### Quarter 1 — U00–U03
Students must accurately use: evidence, inference, uncertainty, system, feedback, structure, function, transport, scale.

### Quarter 2 — U04–U06
Retrieve Quarter 1 terms plus: variation, probability, population, selection, atom, isotope, abundance, model limitation.

### Quarter 3 — U07–U09
Retrieve earlier terms plus: conservation, rate, equilibrium, force, momentum, energy, current, resistance, frequency, information.

### Quarter 4 — U10–U12
Retrieve earlier terms plus: reservoir, flux, feedback, forcing, resilience, criteria, constraint, risk, reliability, life cycle, optimization.

## Student expectation
A student has not mastered a term merely by matching it to a definition. Mastery requires:
1. accurate meaning,
2. correct use in a scientific explanation,
3. recognition in data/model context,
4. transfer to a different unit,
5. correction of a related misconception.

## Audit rule
Each quarterly investigation and cumulative assessment should include at least one task requiring vocabulary transfer from a previous quarter.
