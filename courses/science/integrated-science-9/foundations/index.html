Science Foundations Bridge

A targeted six-week refresher for learners who need stronger prerequisite foundations before entering Official Unit 1. The bridge adds support without changing or erasing previous course-completion records.

80% mastery requiredReasoning corrections42-week supported pathway

How this pathway works

1. NAIB reads the readiness strand profile, not only the total score.

2. Strong strands can use confirmation challenges; weaker strands receive fuller reteaching and practice.

3. Each week follows diagnostic connection → teach/reteach → guided practice → independent practice → application → mastery evidence.

4. Bridge exit requires ≥80% mastery plus required reasoning corrections.

5. Successful completion routes directly to Official Unit 1.

Week 1

Scientific Thinking & Evidence

Observation and inference, testable questions, variables, claims, evidence, reasoning, uncertainty, source quality, and model limits.

Full lesson package: next build step.

Week 2

Measurement & Quantitative Science

SI units, conversions, estimation, measurement precision, density, rates, percentages, units, and calculator fluency.

Week 3

Tables, Graphs & Data

Axes, variables, graph choice, trends, slope/change, anomalies, sampling, uncertainty, and evidence-supported conclusions.

Full lesson package: planned.

Week 4

Matter & Energy Foundations

Particles, atoms and molecules, physical versus chemical change, conservation of matter, thermal energy, and energy transfer.

Full lesson package: planned.

Week 5

Cells, Scale & Biological Organization

Cell structures, membranes, diffusion, cellular respiration, organization across scales, and structure-function reasoning.

Full lesson package: planned.

Week 6

Systems, Models & Scientific Reading

Inputs/outputs, causal arrows, feedback, interacting systems, figure reading, source-supported explanation, and citation practice.

Full lesson package: planned.

Bridge exit

The final bridge mastery assessment will report overall performance and strand evidence to NAIB. Scores below 80% require targeted reasoning corrections and another demonstration opportunity. Once the bridge is mastered, students do not repeat the readiness gateway—they proceed directly into the official curriculum.
