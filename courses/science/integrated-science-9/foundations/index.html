Science Foundations Refresher Course

A complete six-week preparatory course for learners whose NAIB readiness profile shows that additional foundations will improve success in Integrated Science 9. It is free, supportive, mastery-based, and routes directly into Official Unit 1.

6 weeks80% masteryReasoning corrections42-week supported pathway

How the refresher works

1. NAIB reads the readiness strand profile rather than relying only on one total score.

2. Learners complete six connected weeks covering scientific reasoning, quantitative work, data literacy, matter and energy, life science, systems, models, and scientific reading.

3. Strong strands may use confirmation challenges while developing strands receive full guided practice.

4. Every week uses application and mastery evidence. Missed reasoning is corrected rather than hidden by repeated guessing.

5. The cumulative Science Foundations Mastery Assessment requires ≥80% overall plus required reasoning corrections and no unresolved essential gap.

6. Successful completion routes directly to Official Unit 1. Prior course-completion records are never erased by placement or refresh results.

Final bridge gateway

Science Foundations Mastery Assessment

After the six refresher weeks, students complete a 30-prompt cumulative assessment spanning all six strands plus an integrated scientific performance task. 24/30 = 80%. NAIB reports both the overall result and strand profile.

Students below mastery receive the smallest effective targeted refresh and a parallel reassessment rather than automatically repeating the entire course.
