## International benchmark roles

The course-wide standards crosswalk identifies the following benchmark roles for Unit 01:

- **NGSS-style three-dimensional learning:** science and engineering practices, crosscutting reasoning, evidence-based performance
- **IB MYP Sciences:** inquiry, criteria-based evidence, reflection, communication, ethics
- **Cambridge / England science:** working scientifically, experimental skills, measurement, data interpretation
- **Australian Curriculum / British Columbia / Singapore / New Zealand:** inquiry skills, evidence evaluation, scientific communication, context and ethics
- **OECD PISA Science:** scientific literacy, evidence interpretation, source evaluation, and real-world decision making

These are benchmark roles, not claims of formal authorization or exact legal equivalence.

## Tool and interaction evidence

Unit 01 integrates tools only where they support the scientific practice being assessed:

- **Khaemenes Scientific Calculator:** measurement, averages, percent change, scientific notation, quantitative verification
- **Evidence Citation Studio:** source records and transparent evidence attribution
- **Atlas Evidence Analysis:** claim/evidence/assumption/limitation classification
- **PROSE Editorial Suite:** revision of CER, methods, conclusions, and scientific communication
- **Unit 01 Dataset Laboratory:** graphing, patterns, anomalies, uncertainty, and causal limits
- **Investigation Design Task:** variables, controls, safety, sampling, measurement, and procedure design
- **Mentor:** academic reflection, questioning, explanation, revision, and reasoning support
- **Beta Program:** page-specific usability/quality feedback; not an assessment pathway

Tool use does not replace measurements, reasoning, source evaluation, written evidence, safety review, or mastery assessment.
