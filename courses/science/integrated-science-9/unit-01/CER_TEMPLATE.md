# Claim–Evidence–Reasoning Template

## Question

## Claim

Answer the question directly and use wording no broader than the evidence permits.

## Evidence

Include relevant observations, measurements, calculated values, comparisons, and patterns.

## Reasoning

Explain why the evidence supports the claim using scientific ideas.

## Uncertainty and limitations

Identify variation, measurement limits, possible confounders, and conditions under which the claim may not apply.

## Next investigation

State one question or improvement that would strengthen or extend the evidence.
