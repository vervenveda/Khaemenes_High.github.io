# Unit 01 Student Checklist

## Instruction and evidence
- [ ] Days 1–10 completed
- [ ] Paper helicopter investigation completed
- [ ] Raw observations retained separately from calculated summaries
- [ ] Protocol deviations and anomaly decisions documented
- [ ] Mean, minimum, maximum, and range calculated where required
- [ ] Graph completed with descriptive title, labels, units, and defensible scale
- [ ] CER conclusion completed with limitation / uncertainty
- [ ] Dataset Laboratory completed
- [ ] Independent Investigation Design Task submitted before feedback-assisted revision

## Mastery
- [ ] Mastery Quiz passed at **12/15 or higher (≥80%)**
- [ ] Objective Unit Assessment passed at **20/24 or higher** (20/24 is the first whole-number score at or above 80%)
- [ ] Investigation Design Task human-scored at **32/40 or higher (≥80%)**
- [ ] Constructed Response 1 human-scored at **16/20 or higher (≥80%)**
- [ ] Constructed Response 2 human-scored at **16/20 or higher (≥80%)**
- [ ] Corrections / misconception repair completed for below-mastery attempts
- [ ] Instructor or parent-educator verification recorded

## Reflection and provenance
- [ ] Final reflection recorded
- [ ] Student Record printed or exported
- [ ] Raw observations, calculated products, external sources, and Mentor/ProTool-assisted revisions are distinguishable in the portfolio
- [ ] Mentor support was used for coaching/revision rather than to generate independent assessment answers
- [ ] Beta Program control was available for feedback/testing
- [ ] Recommended Science Studio / ProTools were used only where they supported the scientific task

## Progression rule
Unit 02 should remain locked until all Unit 01 requirements above are complete and Unit 01 mastery is recorded in the course-level progress state.
