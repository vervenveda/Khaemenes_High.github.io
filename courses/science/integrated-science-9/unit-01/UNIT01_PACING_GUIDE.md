# Unit 01 Pacing Guide

## Duration

Two weeks — ten instructional days.

| Day | Focus | Required evidence |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | From phenomena to questions | Experimental and observational questions |
| 2 | Variables and operational definitions | Variable map and definitions |
| 3 | Fair tests, controls, and sampling | Revised design and sample plan |
| 4 | Measurement quality | Measurement audit |
| 5 | Plan the paper helicopter | Approved investigation plan |
| 6 | Conduct and analyze | Raw data, calculations, graph, evaluation |
| 7 | Tables, graphs, and scale | Complete graph and pattern statement |
| 8 | Patterns, uncertainty, and causation | Dataset laboratory |
| 9 | CER and source evaluation | CER and source audit |
| 10 | Design challenge and mastery | Design task, quiz, assessment, reflection |

## Mastery rules

- Mastery quiz: at least 12 of 15
- Objective unit assessment: at least 20 of 24
- Corrections and retakes are allowed
- Constructed responses are evaluated with the Unit 01 rubric
