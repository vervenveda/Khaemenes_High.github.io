Unit 10

Earth Systems, Carbon Budgets, and Climate Evidence

A reservoir contains 850 units of carbon. During one year, 120 units enter and 145 units leave. Calculate net change and final reservoir size. Explain reservoir, flux, feedback, and forcing. Use a simple global energy-budget model to distinguish reflected, absorbed, and outgoing energy, and explain why an observed energy imbalance is evidence about system change rather than proof of one single cause. Propose one hazard-resilience or stewardship action, one measurable outcome, and one uncertainty or justice consideration.

Unit 11

Astronomical Scale, Spectra, Orbits, and Cosmic Evidence

A star is 12 light-years away. Explain what that distance means for lookback time. A transit blocks 1% of a star’s light; calculate the planet-to-star radius ratio using transit depth ≈ (Rp/Rs)². Explain how spectra provide evidence about composition, temperature, or line-of-sight motion. Distinguish direct measurement from inference, and describe how redshift, the cosmic microwave background, and elemental abundance provide different evidence for modern cosmology. Include one instrument or selection-bias limitation.

Unit 12

Sustainable Engineering: Criteria, Testing, Risk, and Iteration

A proposed design has criterion scores 8, 6, and 9 with weights 0.40, 0.30, and 0.30. Calculate its weighted score. Identify one hard constraint that could still disqualify the design. Describe a predictive model, a safe prototype or simulation test, the raw evidence that should be preserved, one uncertainty source, and an evidence-based revision. Include a life-cycle or end-of-life consideration and one accessibility, equity, privacy, labor, cultural, or environmental-justice consideration.

Integration

Earth–Space–Engineering Evidence Defense

Design a monitoring system for an Earth-system risk using remote sensing or another defensible measurement approach. State the scientific question, measured variables and units, relevant Earth-system process, how electromagnetic or remote-sensing evidence becomes an inference, and engineering criteria and constraints. Include calibration, uncertainty, data stewardship, safety, maintenance, and a rule for deciding when new evidence should trigger a design or policy revision.
