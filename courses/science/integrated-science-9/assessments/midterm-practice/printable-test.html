Practical / Data Analysis

Inquiry, Measurement, and Uncertainty

A student measures cooling water at 0, 2, 4, 6, and 8 minutes and records 80.0, 71.8, 65.1, 59.5, and 55.0 °C. Identify the independent and dependent variables, calculate the average temperature change per minute from 0 to 8 minutes, choose an appropriate graph, distinguish a direct observation from a causal inference, identify one uncertainty source, and propose one method revision. State what evidence would be required before claiming one factor caused the cooling pattern.

Life Science Integration

Cells, Homeostasis, Inheritance, and Ecosystems

Explain how cell structure supports one transport or energy function, then connect that function to organism-level homeostasis. For a simple inherited trait scenario, distinguish genotype from phenotype and explain why a Punnett square predicts probabilities rather than guarantees. Finally, connect inherited variation to one population or ecosystem question and identify which evidence would be observational versus experimental.

Physical Science / Model Defense

Atomic Evidence and Model Revision

For ²⁴Mg²⁺, determine protons, neutrons, and electrons. Explain how scattering evidence changed the atomic model, how isotope evidence relates to weighted atomic mass, and how a line spectrum supports discrete energy differences. Include one limitation of a simplified shell model and one example of how scientific models improve when new evidence conflicts with an older explanation.
