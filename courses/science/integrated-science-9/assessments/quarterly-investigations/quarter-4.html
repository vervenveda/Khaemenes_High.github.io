Khaemenes Academy

Quarter 4 · Earth Systems & Sustainable Design

Course span: Units 10–12 · Week 36

Driving question

How can Earth-system evidence guide a sustainable engineering decision without hiding uncertainty or shifting harm?

Investigation

  1. Define a local or simulated Earth-system problem using a baseline and stakeholder need.
  2. Trace matter and energy through at least three connected Earth-system components.
  3. Establish measurable criteria, constraints, and one equity/accessibility requirement.
  4. Compare at least three design concepts using a weighted decision matrix.
  5. Test or analyze one prototype/model with repeated evidence and uncertainty.
  6. Complete a life-cycle/risk review and revise the recommendation.

Required evidence

System map · baseline · criteria/constraints · decision matrix · prototype/test data · uncertainty · life-cycle/risk analysis · final CER/design defense.

Capstone relationship

This investigation is the Quarter 4 cumulative evidence package and may incorporate Unit 12 capstone artifacts without duplicating work.
