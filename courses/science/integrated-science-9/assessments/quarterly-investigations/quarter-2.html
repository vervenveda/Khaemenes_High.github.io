Quarter 2 · Inheritance, Ecosystems & Matter

Course span: Units 04–06 · Week 18 · public midterm-practical practice layer

Driving question

How can microscopic variation in matter and inheritance produce measurable patterns in organisms and populations?

Investigation

  1. Use a supplied inheritance/population dataset and an atomic/isotope evidence set.
  2. Calculate at least one probability or frequency and one atomic/isotope quantity.
  3. Build a model connecting variation, evidence, and scale.
  4. Distinguish direct observation from inference in both biological and atomic evidence.
  5. Evaluate uncertainty and one plausible alternative explanation.
  6. Produce a practical-style evidence record and a written synthesis.

Required evidence

Calculations · graph/table · biological model · atomic model · observation/inference audit · source evaluation · synthesis explanation.

Assessment boundary

This is public formative practical practice. It does not replace the separately supervised, authenticated official midterm.
