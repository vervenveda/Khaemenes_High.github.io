Khaemenes Academy

Quarter 1 · Cells, Systems & Evidence

Course span: Readiness + Units 01–03 · Week 9

Driving question

How can evidence at different biological scales explain how a living system maintains stable conditions?

Investigation

  1. Choose a safe supplied dataset or model involving cells, transport, or a body-system response.
  2. Define the measured variable, units, baseline, and uncertainty.
  3. Construct one cell-to-system model showing at least three causal links.
  4. Analyze the data with a table and appropriate graph; identify one pattern and one limitation.
  5. Evaluate one source used to support the mechanism.
  6. Write a CER explaining how structure, transport, feedback, or homeostasis accounts for the evidence.

Required evidence

Question · variables · data table · graph · model · source note · uncertainty statement · CER · revision after feedback.

Mastery rubric

25% scientific accuracy · 25% investigation/data quality · 25% evidence/reasoning · 25% communication, ethics, and limitations. Revise below 80% mastery.
