Quarter 3 · Reactions, Motion & Information

Course span: Units 07–09 · Week 27

Driving question

How do matter, forces, energy, electricity, and waves work together in a measurable technological system?

Investigation

  1. Choose a low-voltage or supplied-data system such as a sensor, signal path, motor model, or energy-transfer device.
  2. Identify system boundary, input, output, energy transfers, and relevant matter changes.
  3. Use at least two equations from chemistry/physics/electricity/waves with units.
  4. Graph one relationship and explain slope, trend, or proportionality.
  5. Identify one misconception that the data rule out.
  6. Evaluate efficiency, uncertainty, safety, and information fidelity.

Required evidence

System diagram · calculations · graph · energy account · uncertainty · safety note · CER/design recommendation.

Mastery

At least 80% across scientific accuracy, quantitative reasoning, evidence quality, and communication before completion.
