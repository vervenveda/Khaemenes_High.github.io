# Science 9 — Inquiry, Laboratory & Design Spine

## Purpose
This spine makes practical science cumulative. Students repeatedly plan, measure, analyze, evaluate, revise, and communicate rather than completing isolated activities.

| Unit | Core inquiry emphasis | Evidence artifact |
|---|---|---|
| U00 | safety, measurement habits, evidence boundaries | readiness/safety record |
| U01 | variables, controls, repeatability, graphs | investigation design + data analysis |
| U02 | microscopy/transport/model evidence | cell investigation record |
| U03 | physiological-response datasets and systems models | homeostasis evidence case |
| U04 | chromosome/probability models | inheritance model + evidence explanation |
| U05 | ecological sampling/population data | biodiversity/population investigation |
| U06 | atomic/isotope/spectral evidence | atomic evidence laboratory record |
| U07 | reaction evidence, rate/energy models | chemistry investigation + CER |
| U08 | motion/force/energy measurements | motion-data laboratory |
| U09 | low-voltage circuit/wave data | circuit/wave investigation |
| U10 | Earth-system, watershed, cycle data | Earth-system model/lab |
| U11 | scale, spectra, transit/orbit data | space evidence investigation |
| U12 | prototype testing, risk, optimization | engineering capstone portfolio |

## Skill progression
1. **U00–U01:** follow safe methods and distinguish observation from inference.
2. **U02–U03:** collect/interpret biological evidence and connect scales.
3. **U04–U06:** use probability, sampling, models, and invisible-structure evidence.
4. **U07–U09:** combine equations, controlled comparisons, graphs, and energy/system reasoning.
5. **U10–U12:** handle multi-variable systems, uncertainty, stakeholders, risk, and design trade-offs.

## Required practical evidence quality
Every substantial investigation should record:
- driving question,
- variables or comparison structure,
- units and measurement method,
- safety/risk boundary,
- raw or supplied data,
- processed data/graph/model,
- uncertainty or limitation,
- evidence-based conclusion,
- revision or next test.

## Quarterly checkpoints
- Week 9: biology/systems inquiry.
- Week 18: inheritance/ecology/atomic evidence practical.
- Week 27: chemistry/physics/electricity integrated investigation.
- Week 36: Earth-system sustainable engineering investigation.

Public practical work is formative portfolio evidence unless separately authenticated and supervised for official mastery.
