# Khaemenes Academy — High School Science Progression Map

## Purpose
This map makes Grades 9–12 one cumulative scientific education. Upper courses may deepen or specialize prior learning, but they must not silently restart the scientific practices established earlier.

## Four-year progression

| Strand | Grade 9 · Integrated Science | Grade 10 · Biology & Biotechnology | Grade 11 · Chemistry & Materials Science | Grade 12 · Physics & Engineering |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence | observation vs inference; claim/evidence/reasoning; source quality | biological evidence chains; controls; sampling; causal limits | measurement lineage; calibration; competing chemical models | model discrimination; residuals; parameter sensitivity; engineering defense |
| Quantitative reasoning | SI units; ratios; graphs; uncertainty; probability | rates, percentages, population/genetic probability, biological datasets | dimensional analysis, mole relationships, concentration, equilibrium, energy | vectors, functions, conservation, uncertainty, optimization |
| Systems/modeling | cross-disciplinary systems and boundaries | feedback, structure–function, information flow, ecosystems | particle/molecular models, equilibrium, energy pathways | mathematical models, fields, energy systems, engineering systems |
| Inquiry | fair tests, variables, repeatability | sampling/model choice and biological controls | calibration, quantitative experimental design, competing explanations | discriminating tests, residual analysis, prototype iteration |
| Communication | CER, source attribution, limitations | mechanism-based explanation and ethical evidence use | quantitative argument with assumptions/units/model limits | technical defense, trade-offs, sensitivity, design recommendation |
| Engineering | criteria/constraints and simple iteration | biotechnology/ecology design and stakeholder reasoning | materials/sustainable chemistry decisions | constrained optimization and prototype validation |

## Required inherited competencies

### Grade 10 inherits from Grade 9
- measurement, graphing, uncertainty, evidence/source evaluation;
- cell structure/function, energy and matter, homeostasis foundations;
- probability, sampling, systems, feedback, CER;
- safety and ethical evidence handling.

### Grade 11 inherits from Grades 9–10
- atomic/matter foundations from Integrated Science;
- ratios, graphing, uncertainty, dimensional reasoning;
- biological energy/matter transfer and rate reasoning;
- model limits, source quality, controlled investigation, CER.

### Grade 12 inherits from Grades 9–11
- measurement, graphing, uncertainty, systems and energy;
- particle/matter and chemical energy foundations;
- quantitative model selection and evidence revision;
- laboratory controls, calibration, residual/error reasoning;
- criteria, constraints, ethics, source provenance and technical communication.

## Department rule
Every upper-grade unit must intentionally retrieve at least one prior-year competency when it is scientifically relevant. Retrieval must be used in reasoning, data interpretation, modeling, investigation, or explanation—not definition-only recall.

## Exit profile
A Grade 12 completer should be able to frame a scientific question, select and defend a model, plan or critique an investigation, analyze quantitative evidence with uncertainty, distinguish observation from inference, evaluate sources, revise a claim after contrary evidence, and defend an engineering/scientific decision under stated constraints.
