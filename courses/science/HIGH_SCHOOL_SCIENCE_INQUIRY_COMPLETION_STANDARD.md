# High School Science — Inquiry Completion Evidence Standard

**Established:** 2026-11-23  
**Scope:** Grades 9–12

## Purpose

Planned inquiry time and completed learner inquiry are different claims. This standard defines the evidence states required before student-facing progress, parent reporting, portfolio summaries, or future authoritative systems may describe an investigation as completed.

## Canonical evidence states

1. **planned** — curriculum allocates a qualifying inquiry task.
2. **started** — learner has opened or begun the investigation and created learner-scoped work.
3. **submitted** — required observations/data/model and explanation are present in a learner-scoped evidence record.
4. **reviewed** — educator/authorized review status exists; browser-local self-marking cannot create this state.
5. **mastered** — evidence meets the course rubric or reassessment contract. This state may not be inferred solely from page visits or localStorage.
6. **archived** — a portable portfolio/export record preserves the evidence identity, course, activity, dates and review/mastery provenance.

## Minimum submitted investigation record

- schema/version
- learner scope identifier
- course code
- investigation/activity identifier
- driving question
- prediction or initial model where applicable
- observations/data with units where applicable
- analysis/model/graph evidence
- uncertainty, limitations, or evidence-quality note
- CER/design defense or equivalent constructed response
- safety/alternative pathway acknowledgement when applicable
- created/updated timestamps
- content digest or equivalent integrity field when authoritative custody becomes available

## Counting rules

- Planned inquiry minutes may be reported from curriculum allocation.
- Completed inquiry counts require at least **submitted**.
- Reviewed inquiry counts require an authorized **reviewed** state.
- Mastery counts require **mastered** and may not be manufactured by instructional engines.
- A page visit, opened accordion, animation, or practice score alone is not completed inquiry.
- Supplied-data and simulation alternatives qualify when they preserve the same question → evidence → analysis → explanation reasoning.

## Authority boundary

Current browser-local records are formative evidence. They may be exported and preserved, but they do not independently establish transcript credit, protected assessment results, educator approval, or credential authority.
