# Assessment Framework — Biology & Biotechnology 10

## Mastery target
Essential outcomes require **80% mastery** or documented correction/reassessment.

## Four evidence domains

| Domain | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Biological knowledge and mechanism | 25% |
| Inquiry, investigation and design | 25% |
| Quantitative/data analysis and evaluation | 25% |
| Science, society, biotechnology ethics and communication | 25% |

## Required assessment evidence

- readiness/safety evidence,
- weekly mastery/CER,
- laboratory, field, simulation, or dataset investigations,
- quantitative biology tasks,
- model construction and critique,
- source-quality evaluation,
- unit performance tasks,
- cumulative midterm,
- correction/reassessment records,
- comprehensive final,
- Biology & Biotechnology capstone,
- portfolio defense.

## Objective-item standard

Automatically scored items must:
1. have one defensible intended answer,
2. state conventions, units and assumptions,
3. avoid duplicate/equivalent options,
4. use distractors tied to identifiable misconceptions,
5. include an explanation,
6. test taught biology rather than trivia.

## Constructed-response standard

Every scored open response should specify:
- biological idea/mechanism,
- required evidence,
- reasoning expectation,
- vocabulary/representation expectation,
- data/quantitative requirement where appropriate,
- uncertainty/limitation,
- communication quality,
- acceptable alternate reasoning,
- common misconception,
- point allocation/mastery descriptor.

## Laboratory / inquiry standard

Substantial investigations should contain:
- driving question,
- safety and authorization boundary,
- variables/comparison structure,
- measurement plan and units,
- raw or supplied data,
- graph/model,
- uncertainty or limitation,
- evidence-based conclusion,
- revision or next step.

Target **≥25% of instructional time** in inquiry, laboratory, simulation-with-data, fieldwork, or supervised hands-on investigation.

## Reassessment

Retain:
- first attempt,
- feedback,
- correction,
- revised evidence,
- final mastery.

Do not overwrite the learning history with only the final score.

## Summative security boundary

Public midterm/final resources are practice/formative unless separately supervised and authenticated. Public browser storage and openly served repositories are not transcript authority.
