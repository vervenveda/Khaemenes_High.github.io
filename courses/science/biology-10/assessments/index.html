Biology 10 Assessment

Weekly CER, portfolio evidence, corrections, unit performance tasks, midterm and final evidence all use an 80% mastery floor.

Public learner evidence

Practice, weekly self-checks, portfolio work and browser-delivered review are editable learner evidence and are not confidential or independently authoritative by themselves.

Supervised secure evidence

When a confidential supervised assessment is required, the learner repository sends responses using the Academy public-safe response contract. Questions/keys remain behind the Noema + Administration assessment boundary. Reviewed records may be issued through the Academy credential authority; private signing material never belongs here.
