## Evidence design

Every ordinary instructional week uses the five-day sequence: phenomenon/model → concept revision → data/quantitative reasoning → investigation/design → CER/mastery. Weekly mastery is open-book formative evidence and requires at least 80% to unlock the next week. Below-80 attempts retain the same randomized form and option order for correction rather than silently reshuffling the learner's work.

Constructed responses, investigations, models, and capstone work require human review. Browser-local evidence is not automatically an official grade.

## Benchmark boundary

The course uses AP Biology depth only as a public college-readiness benchmark. It is not represented as an AP-authorized course. Jurisdiction-specific standards, credit, diploma, scholarship, athletics, or accreditation determinations require separate review.
