About International Biology & Biotechnology 10

International Biology & Biotechnology 10 is an advanced life-science course that moves from the chemistry of life and cellular organization through energetics, signaling, heredity, molecular genetics, biotechnology, evolution, ecology, and biodiversity. Students develop scientific reasoning through laboratory inquiry, quantitative analysis, source evaluation, model building, and evidence-based explanation while considering the ethical responsibilities that accompany biological research and biotechnology.

Course Features

Cells, Energetics & Regulation Cell structure, membranes, transport, organelles, photosynthesis, respiration, metabolism, signaling, feedback, homeostasis, mitosis, and cell-cycle regulation build a systems-level understanding of life.
Heredity, Genetics & Biotechnology Meiosis, inheritance, probability, pedigrees, DNA, replication, gene expression, regulation, genomics, biotechnology, and bioethics connect molecular mechanisms with variation and application.
Evolution, Ecology & Biodiversity Evolutionary evidence, population genetics, natural selection, speciation, phylogeny, ecosystem energy flow, biogeochemical cycles, population dynamics, biodiversity, resilience, and human impacts develop large-scale biological reasoning.
Inquiry, Data & Scientific Responsibility Laboratory investigation, quantitative reasoning, model limits, source quality, data interpretation, revision, cumulative assessment, portfolio evidence, and a responsible biology/biotechnology capstone support advanced scientific practice.
