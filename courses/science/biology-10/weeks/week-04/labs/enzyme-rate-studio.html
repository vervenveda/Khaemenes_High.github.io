Enzyme-Rate Studio

Investigate a simplified mathematical model of reaction rate, collect synthetic trials, and reason from evidence.

Read before starting

This is a fictional teaching simulation. Values are relative product units/minute, not observed enzyme results, not a clinical prediction, and not validated for any real enzyme. The selected temperature peak and pH center are illustrative only. Do not ingest chemicals, culture unknown organisms, or use hazardous laboratory materials.

Model: rate = 80 × temperature response × pH response × substrate/(substrate+2). The response curves are artificial bell-shaped functions centered at 37°C and pH 7 for learning, not general enzyme laws. Synthetic repeat variation is deterministic and small.
