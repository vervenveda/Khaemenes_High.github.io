Purpose and instruction

Biology 10 quantitative reasoning includes percent change, rates, ratios, surface-area-to-volume, energy budgets, feedback deviation, mitotic index, inheritance probability, chi-square concept, allele frequencies, Hardy–Weinberg expectations, fold change, sequence distance, ecological efficiency, residence time, population growth and biodiversity measures.

The public Practice Final in the course portal can be used as open-book rehearsal, but it is not the official final.

Student task

  1. Solve at least eight mixed quantitative problems from different units.
  2. For each, write why that tool fits the question.
  3. Interpret two unfamiliar graphs and one table.
  4. Complete the Practice Final if useful.
  5. Classify misses by concept, calculation, graph, vocabulary or inference.

Mastery check

A formula chosen without understanding the biological quantity can produce a correct-looking but meaningless answer. Units, assumptions and interpretation remain required.
