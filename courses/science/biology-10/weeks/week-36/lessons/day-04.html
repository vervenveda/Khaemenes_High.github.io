Final-assessment integrity boundary

This public page contains review, reflection, correction and portfolio-defense support only. It does not contain confidential final-exam questions, protected answer keys, private grading criteria, authentication material or signing keys. Public Practice Final results remain formative/browser-local; official final evidence requires the approved supervised assessment process.

Purpose and instruction

A final correction is a learning artifact: identify the error class, explain why the original reasoning failed, state the corrected principle, and transfer the principle to a fresh public-safe example. Correction should preserve the original record rather than overwrite history.

Protected final questions stay inside the supervised correction process. Public notes should summarize the biological concept without reproducing confidential item wording.

Student task

  1. Classify each assigned correction.
  2. Write the corrected principle.
  3. Redo the reasoning as permitted by the supervisor.
  4. Create a parallel example with different numbers/context.
  5. Explain how the correction changes your course concept map.

Mastery check

A copied answer is not correction evidence. Mastery is demonstrated when the principle transfers to a new context.

Mentor conference

Archaemenes may challenge concepts, graphs, models, correction reasoning and portfolio explanations. The mentor cannot reveal protected final content or certify an official grade.
