Purpose and instruction

The final asks whether you can transfer biological reasoning into unfamiliar contexts. Build a course map linking chemistry of life, cells, membranes, energy, signaling, homeostasis, division, genetics, molecular information, evolution and ecology. Use arrows labeled with causal or functional relationships.

Mark each connection by evidence type: model, quantitative data, investigation, or CER. This reveals whether knowledge is integrated or merely memorized as isolated units.

Student task

  1. Create a one-page map with at least 25 concepts.
  2. Include at least eight cross-unit links.
  3. Mark five quantitative tools and five model limitations.
  4. Color-code secure/uncertain/review-needed concepts.
  5. Choose three weakest links for focused review.

Mastery check

Readiness means you can explain relationships and transfer them, not merely recognize familiar vocabulary.
