Correlation, Prediction & Causal Limits

Interpret trait correlations without treating prediction as proof of mechanism.

Weekly Classroom & EvidenceBiology CourseScience HallArchaemenesPrevious DayNext Day

Safety, privacy and evidence boundary

Use fictional organisms and de-identified/public datasets. Do not infer intelligence, personality, worth, disease, ancestry, race, or family traits from genetics. Complex-trait models are population-level scientific tools, not labels for individuals.

Explicit science instruction

Complex-trait studies often report correlations among genotype scores, environments, and phenotypes. Correlation measures association; it does not by itself establish direction, mechanism, or absence of confounding.

Prediction can be useful even when causal understanding is incomplete, but predictive accuracy must be evaluated in the population and conditions where the model will be used.

Worked example with reasoning

A fictional polygenic score correlates r = 0.45 with plant height in one greenhouse. That indicates moderate association in that sample. It does not mean genes explain 45% of an individual's height, nor does it prove the same prediction in another environment.

Guided question

Why can a model predict reasonably well while still being a poor causal explanation?

Independent work

  1. Interpret three fictional scatterplots.
  2. Separate association, prediction, and causation statements.
  3. Name two confounders.
  4. Explain why validation in a new sample matters.

Investigation / Data Lab

Reaction-norm dataset investigation

Question: Does environmental condition change genotype effects in the supplied fictional organism dataset?

  1. Graph phenotype for at least two genotypes across three environmental conditions.
  2. Calculate the phenotype difference between genotypes within each environment.
  3. Describe whether the response lines are roughly parallel, diverging, converging, or crossing.
  4. State what pattern is consistent with gene–environment interaction and what the graph cannot prove about mechanism.
  5. Propose one controlled follow-up measurement and one confounder to avoid.

Evidence standard: keep the raw table, graph, difference calculations, and a bounded causal claim.

Misconception check

A correlation coefficient is not a percentage of causation and should not be converted into personal destiny.

Formative understanding check

What does r = 0 mean?

Worked response

No linear association in the sample; nonlinear relationships may still exist.

Open-book formative practice only; not an official mastery attempt or grade.
