Replication Fidelity, Proofreading & Repair

Explain why high fidelity comes from layered error prevention and correction rather than perfect copying.

Weekly Classroom & EvidenceBiology CourseScience HallArchaemenesPrevious DayNext Day

Safety, privacy and evidence boundary

Use paper/digital molecular models and fictional DNA sequences. No biological samples, genetic testing, ancestry inference, or personal genomic data are required. Molecular models are representations and should not be treated as photographs of atoms.

Explicit science instruction

Base pairing reduces errors, polymerase active sites discriminate among nucleotides, many polymerases proofread, and post-replication repair systems correct mismatches. These layers make replication highly accurate but not error-free.

Unrepaired sequence changes that persist can become mutations. Mutation provides biological variation, but most molecular copying events do not create sequence change.

Worked example with reasoning

Imagine a raw polymerase error rate of 1 in 10⁵ bases in a fictional model. Proofreading reduces it 100-fold and repair another 100-fold, yielding 1 in 10⁹ in the simplified multiplicative model. Real rates and pathways vary; the arithmetic illustrates layered fidelity.

Guided question

Why is “DNA copies itself perfectly” scientifically weaker than a layered-fidelity model?

Independent work

  1. Calculate the simplified layered-error example.
  2. Distinguish mismatch, damage, and mutation.
  3. Explain why rare errors can still matter across large genomes and many divisions.
  4. State two model limits.

Investigation / Data Lab

Paper DNA-replication model audit

Question: Can a physical strand model reproduce semiconservative replication while preserving directionality?

  1. Build one antiparallel parental duplex with labeled 5′ and 3′ ends using paper strips or a digital equivalent.
  2. Separate the parental strands and construct complementary new strands without changing the originals.
  3. Label each daughter duplex as one parental + one new strand.
  4. Introduce one fictional mismatch card, decide where proofreading/repair would act, and preserve both the pre-correction and corrected model.
  5. List two molecular features the paper model does not represent.

Evidence standard: photograph/draw the before-and-after model or preserve a labeled diagram plus an error-correction note.

Misconception check

Mutation is not synonymous with damage, and replication errors are not automatically harmful or inherited.

Formative understanding check

Why can high-fidelity replication still generate variation?

Worked response

Error rates are low, not zero; some changes escape repair and persist.
