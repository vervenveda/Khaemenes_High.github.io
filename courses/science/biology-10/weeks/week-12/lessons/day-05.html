Safety and evidence boundary

This week emphasizes supplied datasets, simulations, and design reasoning. Do not improvise sealed-organism chambers, temperature stress, chemical gas absorbers, or invasive measurements. Use only teacher-approved procedures under appropriate supervision.

Explicit science instruction

Experimental energetics is fundamentally an inference problem. Instruments provide measurements; scientists interpret those measurements using models. Strong conclusions make the chain visible: measurement → processed data → pattern → biological inference → limitation.

A high-quality metabolic claim should identify the proxy, comparison condition, units, normalization, replicate behavior, and important confounders. It should also distinguish statistical or numerical difference from biological mechanism.

Weeks 09–12 now form one evidence sequence: photosynthesis captures and stores energy while incorporating carbon; respiration transfers energy from organic molecules; matter moves through metabolic networks; experimental proxies provide indirect evidence about those processes.

Worked example with reasoning

Claim: Sample A shows higher mass-specific aerobic metabolic activity than Sample B under the tested conditions. Evidence: its normalized oxygen-consumption rate is 0.050 compared with 0.025 mL·min⁻¹·g⁻¹ across repeated measurements. Reasoning: oxygen consumption is a validated proxy for aerobic respiration under the model assumptions. Limitation: oxygen consumption alone does not reveal ATP yield, substrate identity, or every pathway contributing to the difference.

Guided question

Which words in the worked claim appropriately limit it to the tested conditions, and why does that language matter?

Independent work

  1. Write a final CER answering the Week 12 essential question.
  2. Include one rate or normalization calculation.
  3. Identify one direct measurement and one inferred process.
  4. Name one confounder and one uncertainty source.
  5. Propose one follow-up design revision.

Misconception check

More data points do not automatically remove bias or confounding. Large datasets can still support weak conclusions if the measurement, comparison, or causal model is flawed.

Formative understanding check

What is the strongest general rule for interpreting metabolic proxies?

Show the worked response

State what was measured directly, explain the validated connection to the biological process, account for controls and confounders, quantify uncertainty where possible, and keep the claim within those limits.
