Purpose and instruction

A cumulative exam should test connected understanding rather than isolated vocabulary. Build one concept map linking chemistry of life, cell structure, membranes, energetics, signaling, homeostasis, cell cycle, and scientific practice. Use arrows labeled with relationships—not decorative lines.

Then audit readiness in four evidence classes: concept (can I explain it?), model (can I represent it?), quantitative (can I calculate or graph it?), and evidence (can I defend a claim with limits?).

Student task

  1. Create a one-page map with at least 12 concepts from Weeks 1–17.
  2. Include at least four cross-unit links, such as membrane transport ↔ ATP or signaling ↔ homeostasis.
  3. Mark each node green/yellow/red for confidence.
  4. For every red node, cite the exact prior week or artifact you will review.

Mastery and correction check

You are ready when you can explain relationships without relying on the original wording of notes. A red/yellow map is useful evidence—not failure—because it identifies where correction time should go.
