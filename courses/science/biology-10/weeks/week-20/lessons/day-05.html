When Mendelian Models Work—and Fail

Synthesize simple inheritance as a bounded model and diagnose when added biology is required.

Safety, privacy and evidence boundary

Use fictional organisms, coins/cards, and published-style datasets. Do not require personal family histories, ancestry testing, fertility information, medical pedigrees, or personal genetic data. Trait inheritance never ranks human worth.

Explicit science instruction

A strong genetics student asks whether a simple model's assumptions fit the system. Mendelian monohybrid models work best when one locus has a clear genotype–phenotype mapping, alleles segregate normally, transmission and survival are not strongly biased, and environmental effects do not obscure the categories.

More complex inheritance can involve linkage, multiple loci, gene interaction, variable penetrance, environmental influence, mitochondrial inheritance, or developmental effects.

Worked example with reasoning

A fictional trait fits 3:1 in large controlled crosses but not in one small class set. Sampling is a plausible explanation. A second trait repeatedly shows a 1:2:1 phenotype ratio because heterozygotes are distinct; segregation still works but complete-dominance mapping does not.

Guided question

What evidence would tell you to expand a Mendelian model rather than merely blame random sampling?

Spiral retrieval bridge

Try these from memory before reopening earlier lessons. Then check your reasoning and record one correction if needed.

  1. From Week 19: explain why meiosis, not mitosis, is the chromosome behavior behind Mendel's segregation model.
  2. From Week 15: compare chromosome-number maintenance in mitosis with reduction in meiosis.
  3. Calculate the expected aa proportion in Aa × Aa, then explain why four offspring need not contain exactly one aa.
  4. Correct this statement: “A dominant allele is more common and stronger.”

Retrieval is formative. The goal is durable connection across units, not a separate grade.

Misconception check

Simple models are not “wrong” because they have limits; they are useful when assumptions are explicit and tested.

Formative understanding check

What is the scientific value of stating model assumptions?

Worked response

Assumptions define when predictions are expected to hold and make failures informative rather than mysterious.
