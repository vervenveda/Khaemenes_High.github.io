Energy-Flow Synthesis

Connect cellular energetics to ecosystem energy flow across scales.

Weekly Classroom & EvidenceBiology CourseScience HallArchaemenesPrevious Day

Safety and evidence boundary

Use supplied ecological datasets, safe non-contact observation, and paper/digital food-web models. Do not disturb wildlife or reveal sensitive species locations. Energy-flow models are ecosystem approximations and should include units, area/time scales, and uncertainty.

Explicit science instruction

Ecosystem energy flow links photosynthesis, respiration, biomass production and feeding. Energy captured by producers becomes chemical energy, much is used in metabolism, and a fraction enters consumer biomass. Decomposers process dead organic matter but do not recycle energy back into sunlight.

Worked example with reasoning

GPP/NPP calculations establish producer energy supply; transfer efficiencies track consumer production; a food web identifies pathways. Together they explain why upper trophic levels have less production available.

Guided question

How does cellular respiration help explain the shape of an ecosystem energy pyramid?

Misconception check

Decomposers recycle matter, not usable energy back to producers. Energy ultimately dissipates while elements can re-enter cycles.

Formative understanding check

What is the central difference between energy and matter in ecosystems?

Worked response

Energy flows and dissipates; matter is transferred and recycled among reservoirs.
