# Curriculum Map — International Biology & Biotechnology 10

## Thirty-six-week sequence

| Block | Weeks | Focus |
|---|---:|---|
| U0 | 1 | Readiness, Safety & Quantitative Biology |
| U1 | 2–8 | Chemistry of Life, Cells & Structure–Function |
| U2 | 9–12 | Photosynthesis, Respiration & Metabolism |
| U3 | 13–17 | Signaling, Homeostasis, Cell Cycle & Semester Synthesis |
| MID | 18 | Midterm Examination, Corrections & Portfolio Audit |
| U4 | 19–22 | Meiosis, Inheritance, Probability & Complex Traits |
| U5 | 23–26 | DNA, Gene Expression, Regulation & Biotechnology |
| U6 | 27–30 | Evolution, Population Genetics, Selection & Speciation |
| U7 | 31–34 | Ecology, Cycles, Population/Community Dynamics & Biodiversity |
| U8 | 35 | Biology & Biotechnology Capstone |
| FINAL | 36 | Comprehensive Final & Portfolio Defense |

**Total:** 36 weeks / 180 daily lesson blocks.

## Weekly instructional rhythm

A normal week should preserve:
1. phenomenon / question,
2. concept and model,
3. quantitative/data reasoning,
4. investigation, simulation, fieldwork, or design,
5. CER/mastery, correction, and portfolio evidence.

Assessment weeks may adapt the rhythm but must retain cumulative evidence, correction, and reflection.

## Course threads

These should recur across the year:
- structure and function,
- energy and matter,
- information flow,
- systems and feedback,
- inheritance and variation,
- evolution,
- ecological interactions,
- quantitative biology,
- data quality and uncertainty,
- source evaluation,
- biotechnology ethics,
- engineering/design reasoning,
- scientific communication.

## Major checkpoints

- Week 1 — readiness and safety
- Week 17 — semester synthesis investigation
- Week 18 — cumulative midterm + corrections + portfolio audit
- Week 26 — molecular genetics/biotechnology synthesis
- Week 30 — evolution synthesis
- Week 34 — ecology/biodiversity synthesis
- Week 35 — Biology & Biotechnology capstone
- Week 36 — comprehensive final + portfolio defense

## Release boundary

Public assessments and portfolio evidence are formative unless separately supervised, authenticated, and accepted by the Academy's authoritative record system.
