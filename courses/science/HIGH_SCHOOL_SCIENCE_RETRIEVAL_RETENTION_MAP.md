# High School Science — Spiral Retrieval & Retention Map

## Common cadence
Every upper-year course uses four retrieval distances:
1. **Near retrieval:** 2–5 lessons after introduction.
2. **Unit retrieval:** in a later unit or synthesis task.
3. **Semester retrieval:** midterm/cumulative correction work.
4. **Prior-year retrieval:** inherited knowledge or practice from an earlier science course.

Retrieval must require explanation, calculation, model use, evidence evaluation, or transfer. Definition-only matching does not satisfy the retention contract.

## Biology 10
Prior-year anchors from Science 9:
- cells, membranes, structure/function;
- energy and matter;
- homeostasis and feedback;
- inheritance probability and sampling;
- ecosystems/evolution evidence;
- graphing, uncertainty, source quality and CER.

Required checkpoints: Weeks 4–5, 9–12, 14–17, 21–22, 27–30, 31–35, MID and FINAL.

## Chemistry 11
Prior-year anchors:
- Grade 9 atomic structure, periodic patterns, bonding, reactions, energy;
- Grade 10 biological rates, energy/matter transfer, quantitative evidence and controls;
- SI units, ratios, graphs, uncertainty, source quality and CER.

Required checkpoints: Weeks 2–5, 10–17, 19–25, 26–31, 32–35, MID and FINAL.

## Physics & Engineering 12
Prior-year anchors:
- Grade 9 motion/force/energy, waves/electricity, systems, Earth/space;
- Grade 10 energy/matter, feedback, biological data/model limits;
- Grade 11 energy, rates, equilibrium, materials, measurement/calibration;
- shared quantitative practices: units, graphing, proportional reasoning, uncertainty, evidence revision.

Required checkpoints: Weeks 2–5, 10–17, 19–28, 29–35, MID and FINAL.

## Assessment rule
Upper-grade cumulative assessments must reserve **5–10% of public formative/cumulative practice** for scientifically relevant inherited retrieval. Protected official assessments may satisfy the same requirement through confidential items or supervised performance evidence; public repositories must never expose protected keys/items merely to prove compliance.

## Correction rule
When inherited knowledge causes an error, the correction record must identify:
- the prior concept or practice;
- the current application;
- the error type;
- a corrected explanation/model/calculation;
- one changed-context transfer check.
