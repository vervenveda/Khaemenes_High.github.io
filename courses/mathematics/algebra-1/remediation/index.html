Algebra I Mastery Recovery

Use evidence to identify the specific skill family needing support, repair the misconception, practice deliberately, and reassess without erasing prior scored evidence.

1 · Diagnose

Use lesson, practice, unit, midterm, or final evidence to identify the exact misconception or prerequisite gap.

2 · Repair

Return to the relevant Foundation pathway, worked example, vocabulary, or prerequisite skill rather than repeating unrelated work.

3 · Repractice

Complete fresh problems and explain the corrected reasoning.

4 · Reassess

Attempt new mastery evidence. Score history and best evidence remain preserved for transparency.

A support flag does not automatically block progression. The next course diagnostic may confirm or adjust Foundation/Core/Extended routing.
