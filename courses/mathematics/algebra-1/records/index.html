Algebra I Records & Evidence

Keep course-grade evidence and next-course readiness evidence distinct, transparent, and preserved with the learner portfolio.

Course-grade model

Coursework 40% · Midterm 20% · Final 30% · Capstone 10%. Automatically scored work and human-reviewed evidence should be clearly distinguished.

Readiness evidence

Readiness records describe secure skill families and support flags for Geometry or Algebra II. They do not replace or redefine the official course grade.

Mastery target

The course target is 80%. Corrections, reassessment, and additional instruction are part of the evidence cycle.

Preserve with the learner record

Keep representative coursework, corrections, assessment exports, project and capstone evidence, the completion certificate, and the readiness evidence record according to the learner's educational requirements.
