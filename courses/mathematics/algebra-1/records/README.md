# Algebra I Records & Trust Policy

The course completion record is a parent- or program-issued educational record supported by browser-local evidence generated by the canonical Algebra I course engines.

## Certificate readiness gate
Before the page reports that the evidence threshold is met, local evidence must show:

- all 13 unit mastery records at or above 80%;
- midterm best score at or above 80%;
- final examination best score at or above 80%.

The capstone remains a separately reviewed portfolio component and is included in the course-grade calculation when entered by the parent/program administrator.

## Grade calculation
The current completion record calculates:

- Coursework: 40%
- Midterm: 20%
- Final examination: 30%
- Capstone: 10%

These weights are explicit and are not changed silently by the evidence scanner.

## Evidence bundle
The export contains the entered course record plus a summary of locally detected unit and exam evidence. When browser cryptography is available, the export receives a SHA-256 integrity fingerprint; otherwise it receives a local fallback fingerprint.

The fingerprint can help detect later modification of the exported file. It is **not** an identity signature, accreditation mark, government validation, or independent authentication.

## Trust boundary
Browser localStorage evidence is intentionally classified as locally generated course evidence. It can show what the course engine recorded on that browser, but it cannot independently prove which learner completed the work. Learner identity, instructional hours, portfolio review, credit award, signatures, and jurisdiction-specific compliance remain human-validated fields.

The certificate must not claim state/district issuance, accreditation, or universal acceptance unless a separately authorized institution actually provides that status.
