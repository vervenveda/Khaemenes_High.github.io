How to support the course

Mastery target

The course target is 80%. Below-mastery work should lead to instruction, correction, and another opportunity to demonstrate understanding.

Readiness is not a grade

Course grades and next-course readiness are related evidence streams but are recorded separately.

Preserve evidence

Keep corrected work, assessment exports, projects, explanations, capstone evidence, and the readiness record with the learner portfolio.

Student & family resource · Optional independent math practice

Evidence & Interval Studio

What this does: practice explaining an algebraic claim with evidence and classifying real numbers or intervals. Students can choose a topic, attempt a question, ask for a hint, examine a worked explanation, and export local practice results.

What this does not do: it is not an official gradebook, an administrative record, or a requirement for lesson completion. Opening it does not change mastery scores. Students should use it when they want extra practice; parents and teachers can help review the exported practice file.
