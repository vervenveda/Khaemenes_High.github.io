Algebra I Family Guide

Use unit family guides, mastery evidence, corrections, and learner reflection to support progress without replacing the learner's own mathematical reasoning.

How to support the course

Mastery target

The course target is 80%. Below-mastery work should lead to instruction, correction, and another opportunity to demonstrate understanding.

Readiness is not a grade

Course grades and next-course readiness are related evidence streams but are recorded separately.

Preserve evidence

Keep corrected work, assessment exports, projects, explanations, capstone evidence, and the readiness record with the learner portfolio.
