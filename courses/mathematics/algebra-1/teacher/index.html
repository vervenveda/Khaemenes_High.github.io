A+++ instructional rule

Do not treat a sub-80% result as a terminal failure. Inspect the item and skill evidence, assign targeted recovery, allow correction and reassessment, preserve attempt history, and unlock progression only when required mastery reaches at least 80%.

Labs and projects strengthen evidence and transfer. They do not silently override formal mastery requirements. Learner identity and final credit award remain human-validated records.
