Algebra I Assessment Center

Readiness evidence, 36 week-specific mastery checks, 13 substantial unit mastery checks, a mixed-evidence midterm and final, administration guidance, and completion documentation.

Readiness Diagnostic

36 prerequisite questions. Readiness evidence remains separate from the Algebra I course grade.

Weekly Mastery Center

36 aligned checks · 10 questions per week · 8/10 mastery target · 360 week-specific prompts.

Midterm · Mixed Evidence

60 selected-response questions plus 6 required constructed responses covering Units 01–06 through Week 18.

Final · Mixed Evidence

100 selected-response questions plus 10 required constructed responses across the complete 36-week course.

Administration & Scoring Guide

80% mastery rules, 70/30 cumulative weighting, evaluator rubric, accommodations, corrections, retesting, security, and record trust.

Completion Record

Parent/program completion documentation and verification page.

Public assessment boundary: browser-delivered forms are public learning evidence, not confidential examinations. Full midterm/final mastery requires ≥80% selected response, ≥80% human-reviewed constructed response, and ≥80% overall.
