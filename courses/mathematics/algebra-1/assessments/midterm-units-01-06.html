Algebra I Midterm Assessment

Mixed-evidence mastery across the first half of Algebra I

60 selected-response questions plus 6 required constructed responses. Selected response contributes 70%; structured evaluator-reviewed reasoning contributes 30%.

Administration Guide
Learning ObjectiveDemonstrate cumulative Algebra I mastery across mathematical reasoning, expressions, equations, inequalities, functions, linear models, data interpretation, units, and verification.
DirectionsComplete all 60 selected-response questions and all 6 written reasoning tasks. Full mastery requires structured evaluator review of the written evidence.

Selected-Response Assessment

6 sections · 10 questions per unit · 60 total

0/60 answered · 0/6 written complete

Constructed-Response Evidence

Complete all six written tasks. Each response needs at least 50 meaningful characters and will be reviewed with the 0–4 rubric.

Evaluator Review · Written Reasoning

Full mastery requires ≥80% selected response, ≥80% constructed response, and ≥80% overall. Reviewer identity, role, note, and attestation are required before this review can count toward progression.

  • 4 · Complete and correct reasoning; representations, units, verification, interpretation, and relevant limitations are explicit.
  • 3 · Substantially correct reasoning with a minor omission or small non-conceptual error.
  • 2 · Partial reasoning shows important understanding but contains a conceptual gap, incomplete verification, or weak interpretation.
  • 1 · Limited relevant work; major misconceptions or unsupported conclusions remain.
  • 0 · Blank, unrelated, or no assessable mathematical reasoning.
