Mastery architecture

Learn → Practice → Assess → Correct → Reassess

Students may return to lessons, review exact missed skills, complete targeted practice or an applied lab, and retake assessments until the required mastery is demonstrated. Earlier mastery evidence is preserved.

Progression standard: 80% mastery is required before sequential advancement. Labs and projects strengthen evidence but never silently replace the assessment threshold.
