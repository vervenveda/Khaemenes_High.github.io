Khaemenes Global Algebra I & Integrated Mathematics I

A 36-week course in algebraic reasoning, equations, inequalities, functions, modelling, systems, exponents, polynomials, quadratics, radicals, statistics, financial mathematics, and integrated problem solving.

Mathematics Readiness AssessmentOpen Course Units

Course structure

36 Weeks

35 instructional weeks plus 1 integrated modelling capstone week. The shared mathematics readiness assessment is completed separately and never occupies an instructional week.

Foundation · Core · Extended

Support and challenge adjust to readiness while preserving the same essential course identity.

Assessment

60-question midterm after Week 18, 100-question final in Week 36, plus unit mastery, projects, corrections, and portfolio evidence.

Course Grade

Coursework 40% · Midterm 20% · Final 30% · Capstone 10%. Readiness evidence remains separate from the official grade.

13 instructional units

01 · Real Numbers & Precision

Reasoning, notation, properties, units, estimation, and verification.

02 · Expressions & Polynomial Foundations

Equivalent expressions, structure, distribution, and polynomial foundations.

03 · Linear Equations & Proportional Models

Equations, formulas, proportions, rates, and contextual models.

04 · Inequalities & Absolute Value

Solution sets, compound constraints, tolerances, and interpretation.

05 · Functions

Domain, range, notation, multiple representations, and sequences.

06 · Linear Functions & Modelling

Slope, intercepts, regression, prediction, and model limitations.

07 · Systems

Equations, inequalities, intersections, feasible regions, and decisions.

08 · Exponents & Exponential Functions

Exponent laws, sequences, growth, decay, and percent change.

09 · Polynomials & Factoring

Operations, structure, common factors, factoring, and zeros.

10 · Quadratic Functions

Parabolas, forms, zeros, vertices, equations, and modelling.

11 · Radicals & Coordinate Geometry

Radicals, rational exponents, distance, and algebra-geometry connections.

12 · Statistics, Finance & Evidence

Data, rates, financial mathematics, assumptions, and evidence-based decisions.

13 · Integrated Modelling Capstone

Build, test, defend, and communicate a mathematical model.
