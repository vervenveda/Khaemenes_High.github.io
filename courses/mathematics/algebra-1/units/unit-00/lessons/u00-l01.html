Rational Numbers & Algebraic Expressions

Strengthen the arithmetic and symbolic fluency Algebra 1 assumes: signed rational numbers, fractions and decimals, order of operations, substitution, like terms, distribution, and equivalent expressions.

Math word · Structure

Structure is the way a mathematical expression is organized. Recognizing structure helps you decide what may be combined, distributed, factored, or evaluated without changing meaning.

1 · Core refresh

Signed rational numbers: keep operation meaning separate from number sign. Fractions: use common denominators for addition/subtraction and reciprocals for division. Expressions: terms may be combined only when their variable parts match. Distribution: a(b+c)=ab+ac. Substitution: replace variables first, then follow order of operations.

2 · Worked examples

A. −7+3(5−2)=−7+9=2.
B. 3/4−1/6=9/12−2/12=7/12.
C. 5x+3−2x+8=3x+11.
D. 4(2x−3)+x=8x−12+x=9x−12.

3 · Guided practice

5 · Application Lab · Subscription Cost Audit

A learning service charges a $24 setup fee plus $7.50 per month. A family receives a one-time $9 credit.

  1. Define a variable for months.
  2. Write and simplify an expression for total cost.
  3. Find the cost after 6 months.
  4. Estimate first and use the estimate to audit the exact answer.
  5. Explain why 24(7.5m−9) does not represent the situation.

6 · Critical-thinking challenge

A student says 2(x+6)=2x+6. Another says the expressions are “close enough” because they agree for some possible value. Determine whether either claim is acceptable in Algebra 1. Use structure and numerical evidence.
