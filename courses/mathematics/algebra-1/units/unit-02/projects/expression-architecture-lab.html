Unit 02 · Mission Board

Expression Architecture Lab

Build several algebraic forms that describe the same designed situation, then prove which forms are equivalent and explain what each form reveals.

Unit Home

Choose a design

  • Garden or room border
  • Art-print pricing plan
  • Event seating arrangement
  • Game scoring system
  • Your own measurable design

Required evidence

  • Define every variable and unit.
  • Write an expression from the situation.
  • Create at least two equivalent forms.
  • Justify each rewrite with properties.
  • Evaluate at two inputs.
  • Classify polynomial structure where applicable.
  • Factor a GCF when one exists.
  • Explain which form is most useful and why.

100-point rubric

Model & variable definitions 15 · Structural accuracy 20 · Equivalent forms 20 · Property-based justification 15 · Evaluation/verification 10 · Polynomial classification/factoring 10 · Interpretation 5 · Organization 5
