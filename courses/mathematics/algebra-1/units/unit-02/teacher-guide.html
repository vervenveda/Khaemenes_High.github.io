Useful prompts

  • What are the terms? What are the factors?
  • Which parts are alike?
  • What property licenses that rewrite?
  • Can you verify the two forms at an input?
  • Can you reverse the transformation?

Misconceptions to watch

  • Adding exponents while adding terms.
  • Losing parentheses during negative substitution.
  • Partial distribution.
  • Combining unlike terms.
  • Using coefficient size to determine degree.
  • Changing only one sign when subtracting a polynomial.
  • Stopping before the greatest common factor is removed.

Evidence to preserve

Written structural defense, corrected misconception, pathway practice, Expression Architecture Lab, Boss Battle, and formal mastery evidence.

Unit 02 exceptional-depth standard

Exceptional-depth evidence standard

Use all 7 lessons, Weeks 4–6 weekly mastery/retention evidence, differentiated Foundation/Core/Extended practice, the 20-question unit mastery check, corrections, and the 100-point applied investigation as complementary evidence.

  • Weekly mastery: 10 questions balanced across five reasoning dimensions; graded Weeks 2–36 use eight current-focus + two delayed-retrieval items.
  • Unit mastery: 20 lesson-balanced, difficulty-mixed questions; every lesson is represented and immediate retakes prioritize unseen items.
  • Differentiation: pathways increase independence, transfer, proof, model critique, and generalization without fixed ability labels.
  • Investigation: use the 100-point analytic rubric for context/ethics, accuracy, representations, verification/sensitivity, limitations, and reflection.
  • Mastery: 80% remains the progression threshold; below-mastery work requires correction and a fresh attempt.
