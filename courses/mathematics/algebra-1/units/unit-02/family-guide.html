Useful prompts

  • What are the terms? What are the factors?
  • Which parts are alike?
  • What property licenses that rewrite?
  • Can you verify the two forms at an input?
  • Can you reverse the transformation?

Misconceptions to watch

  • Adding exponents while adding terms.
  • Losing parentheses during negative substitution.
  • Partial distribution.
  • Combining unlike terms.
  • Using coefficient size to determine degree.
  • Changing only one sign when subtracting a polynomial.
  • Stopping before the greatest common factor is removed.

Evidence to preserve

Written structural defense, corrected misconception, pathway practice, Expression Architecture Lab, Boss Battle, and formal mastery evidence.
