Unit 03 · Mission Board

Equation Modeling Lab

Choose a real constraint, build a linear equation, solve it, verify it, and decide whether the mathematical solution makes sense in context.

Unit Home

Mission choices

  • Compare two phone/service plans.
  • Model a trip using distance, rate, and time.
  • Build a budget or wage equation.
  • Create a percent/discount model.
  • Design a mixture or recipe constraint.
  • Propose your own linear constraint.

Required evidence

  • Define variables and units.
  • State assumptions/domain restrictions.
  • Write the equation from the situation.
  • Solve with justified equivalent operations.
  • Verify in the original equation.
  • Interpret the result in context.
  • Reject or revise any impossible solution.
  • Change one parameter and explain the effect.

100-point rubric

Model construction 20 · Algebraic accuracy 20 · Equality reasoning 15 · Verification 15 · Units/domain 10 · Context interpretation 10 · Sensitivity/change analysis 5 · Communication 5
