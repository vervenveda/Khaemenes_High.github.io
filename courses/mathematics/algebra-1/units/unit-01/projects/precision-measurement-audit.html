Unit 01 · Mission Board

Precision & Measurement Audit

Choose a real object, room, recipe, artwork, sports space, or household task. Build evidence showing how units and precision affect a trustworthy conclusion.

Unit Home

Mission choices

  1. Measure one object with two tools or methods.
  2. Audit measurements in a recipe or design.
  3. Compare a map/scale measurement with a real measurement.
  4. Design your own precision investigation.

Required evidence

  • Question and prediction
  • Raw measurements with units
  • At least one conversion
  • Justified rounding/significant figures
  • Percent error or discrepancy when appropriate
  • Independent check
  • Limitation and reflection
