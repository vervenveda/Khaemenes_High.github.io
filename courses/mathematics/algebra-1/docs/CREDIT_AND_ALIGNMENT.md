# Credit and Alignment

This course is designed as a complete placement-based Grades 9–10 Algebra I / Integrated Mathematics I pathway.

A parent, school, evaluator, or jurisdiction determines the credit actually awarded. The included completion record supports documentation but does not itself create accreditation, district approval, state issuance, or automatic transfer equivalency.

Before formal reporting, verify:
- local course title and code
- instructional-hour or mastery requirements
- graduation sequence
- end-of-course examination rules
- approved calculator and reference-sheet policy
- transcript and annual-evaluation requirements
- accommodations and retesting procedures
