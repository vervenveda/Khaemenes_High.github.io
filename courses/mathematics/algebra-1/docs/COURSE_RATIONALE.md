# Course Rationale

Khaemenes Global Algebra I & Integrated Mathematics I is designed as a placement-based Grades 9–10 Algebra I / Integrated Mathematics I pathway following a substantial Pre-Algebra foundation.

It exceeds a narrow procedural course by integrating proof and error analysis, dimensional analysis and precision, multiple representations, regression and residuals, systems and optimization, financial mathematics, coordinate geometry, study design and data ethics, sensitivity and fairness, and a portfolio with oral modelling defence.
