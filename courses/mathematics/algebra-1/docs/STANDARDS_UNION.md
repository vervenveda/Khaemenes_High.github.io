# placement-based Grades 9–10 Algebra I / Integrated Mathematics I Standards Union

This course is intentionally broader than a single-state minimum. It combines recurring expectations in these official frameworks while requiring jurisdiction-specific review before formal reporting.

- [Florida B.E.S.T. Mathematics](https://www.fldoe.org/academics/standards/subject-areas/math-science/mathematics/)
- [Florida B.E.S.T. Algebra 1 Resources](https://www.fldoe.org/academics/standards/subject-areas/math-science/mathematics/bestmath.stml)
- [Florida Algebra 1 EOC Resources](https://www.fldoe.org/accountability/assessments/k-12-student-assessment/end-of-course-eoc-assessments/)
- [Texas Mathematics TEKS](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/texas-administrative-code/19-tac-chapter-111)
- [New York Next Generation Algebra I](https://www.nysed.gov/state-assessment/algebra-i)
- [Virginia 2023 Mathematics Standards of Learning](https://www.doe.virginia.gov/teaching-learning-assessment/instruction/mathematics/standards-of-learning-for-mathematics)
- [California Mathematics Framework](https://www.cde.ca.gov/ci/ma/cf/)
- [Common Core High School Mathematics](https://corestandards.org/mathematics-standards/)

## Union domains
- mathematical reasoning, argument, precision, units, and modelling
- real numbers, expressions, equations, inequalities, and formulas
- functions, domain, range, transformations, and sequences
- linear functions, systems, regression, residuals, and constraints
- exponents, scientific notation, and exponential change
- polynomial arithmetic, factoring, zeros, and quadratics
- radicals, rational exponents, coordinate geometry, and circles
- statistics, probability, study design, finance, and evidence
- technology judgment, data ethics, sensitivity, uncertainty, fairness, and communication

## Reporting note
Course breadth does not guarantee automatic equivalency in every jurisdiction. Review current local course codes, graduation rules, end-of-course testing, calculator policy, credit-hour expectations, and transcript requirements.
