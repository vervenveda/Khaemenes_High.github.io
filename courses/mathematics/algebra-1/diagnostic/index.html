Algebra I Readiness Diagnostic

A 36-question diagnostic covering prerequisite number sense, expressions, equations, functions, slope, units, statistics, and evidence interpretation. Results guide support by skill rather than labeling a learner.

Administration GuideReadiness Bridge

Readiness evidence, not a grade: this Week 1 diagnostic is separate from the official Algebra I course grade. Use the category breakdown together with prior work and learner needs to select Foundation, Core, or Extended support; no single unfinished prerequisite automatically bars the learner from Algebra I.

Definitive-answer standard: every scored item has exactly one mathematically or definitionally correct option. Equivalent duplicate choices, overlapping forms, and undefined subjective criteria are excluded.
