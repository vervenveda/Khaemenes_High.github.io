Algebra 1 Readiness Gateway

This assessment checks prerequisite skills before official Algebra 1. It is a placement and support tool, not a punishment. Strong readiness routes directly to Unit 1; prerequisite gaps route to the six-week Unit 0 refresher.

Mastery contract

Direct entry requires ≥80% overall and ≥75% in every essential strand. Otherwise NAIB recommends Unit 0. The six strands are Rational & Algebraic Fluency, Equations & Proportion, Coordinates & Linear Reasoning, Functions & Representations, Exponents & Roots, and Integrated Modeling.

Why strand floors matter

An overall score can hide a serious prerequisite gap. Algebra 1 depends on several skills working together, so NAIB checks both the total score and the weakest essential strand.
