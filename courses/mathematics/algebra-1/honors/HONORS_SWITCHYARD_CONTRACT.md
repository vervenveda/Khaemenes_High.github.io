# Algebra I Honors — switchyard contract (staged 2026-11-10)

## Evidence and trigger
- **Only** Unit 01 and Unit 02 formal **unit mastery assessments** count toward the Honors candidacy trigger. Exclude lesson review scores, weekly mastery, completion speed, and any inferred legacy completion-only flags.
- Both units must score **>=90% on the first graded unit mastery attempt**, with **exactly one stored mastery attempt per unit** and recorded `attemptNumber: 1`. Missing or invalid attempt provenance is **unverified**, never a fabricated pass.
- Trigger is a **candidacy recommendation only**, not Honors placement, an official grade, or an authenticated learner decision. Public localStorage may be edited and may lack cross-device history.

## Authorizations required
1. NAIB, Academy administrator: recommends Honors based on authenticated unit evidence, with recorded identity, evidence refs, and decision timestamp.
2. Archaemenes, principal: approves or declines Honors placement in an independently auditable record.
3. Verified parent/legal guardian: explicit consent for the named learner and Honors route, retained as an auditable authority-issued receipt. Consent cannot be inferred from visiting a page or checking a browser-only checkbox.
- No student switches lanes until **all three** approvals are verified through the Academy Account Cloud / authoritative identity and permission system. Public frontend cannot sign/forge decisions.
- Parent may decline. Standard Algebra I remains available. Revocation / lane-return workflow must preserve all past attempts without destructive migration.

## Honors curriculum specification
- Units 01–02 are common foundation; Honors begins with advanced Unit 03.
- Lesson reviews: 20 questions each, with meaningful depth, not repeated fillers.
- Weekly quizzes: 20 aligned questions each, twice the standard 10-question forms.
- Difficulty: increased transfer, representations, proof and justification, modelling, multi-step nonroutine cases.
- Honors midterm: 100 questions; Honors final: 100 questions. Written reasoning and authenticated evaluator requirements must be preserved or strengthened.
- Threshold remains 80%; score calculations and historical attempt history remain separate from Standard Algebra I.
- Require new, substantial, independently validated question banks, answer keys, curricular coverage and test harnesses before graded Honors activation.
- Never re-label Standard work as Honors evidence. No STOS terminal commissioning claimed.

## Current implementation scope
- Public **read-only** inspection and explanatory Honors turnout. No enrollment write, no parent-approval imitation, no scoring change, no student record mutation, no activation or changes to production.
- Next: secure authorization contracts and receipts, then Honors lesson bank + assessment engine with tests; only activate after verified authority.
