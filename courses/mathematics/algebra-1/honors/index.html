Algebra I Honors Switchyard

After Unit 02, outstanding first-attempt unit mastery may qualify a student for an Honors placement review. NAIB, Archaemenes, and the verified parent or guardian must authorize the switch before enrollment.

The Mastery Turnout

Both formal unit mastery checks must reach at least 90% on the first graded attempt with no unit mastery retakes. Lesson reviews, weekly quizzes, and speed are not used as eligibility triggers.

Checking this browser's unit mastery evidence.

Three approvals required

NAIB recommends placement based on authenticated academic evidence. Archaemenes approves the academic transfer. A verified parent or legal guardian gives explicit consent. Until all three decisions are authorized and auditable, the Honors lane stays closed.

Browser-local evidence is advisory. This public page cannot approve, sign, or enroll a learner. Existing standard Algebra I records remain untouched.

Honors Learning Requirements

20-question lesson reviews, 20-question weekly quizzes, advanced mathematical modelling and rigorous explanations, an 80% mastery threshold, and separate 100-question midterm and final examinations. The Honors assessment banks still require construction and certification.
