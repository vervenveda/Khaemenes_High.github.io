Mathematics Readiness Bridge

Prior-course evidence helps select support, but it does not replace the Algebra I diagnostic. Readiness evidence remains separate from the official course grade.

Incoming evidence

Pre-Algebra

Number fluency, proportional reasoning, expressions, equations, inequalities, coordinates, geometry, and data evidence can inform initial support.

Diagnostic confirmation

The Algebra I readiness diagnostic confirms or adjusts Foundation, Core, or Extended support. A single unfinished skill does not automatically block progression.

Outgoing evidence

Geometry readiness

Coordinate fluency, equations, slope, systems, radicals, distance, precision, modelling, and reasoning are carried forward as skill evidence.

Algebra II readiness

Functions, systems, exponents, polynomial structure, quadratics, radicals, statistics, and modelling are carried forward as skill evidence.
