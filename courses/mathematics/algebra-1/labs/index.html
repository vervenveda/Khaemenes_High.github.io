Applied Mathematical Evidence

Algebra I Labs

Applied investigations connect equations, functions, data, modelling, finance, and coordinate reasoning to observable or decision-based contexts.

Linear Model Lab

Collect or use supplied paired data, build a linear model, interpret slope and intercept, and state limitations.

Systems & Constraints Lab

Compare simultaneous conditions, identify feasible solutions, and justify a decision.

Exponential Change Lab

Model repeated percent change and compare exponential and linear predictions.

Quadratic Design Lab

Use a quadratic model to analyze a maximum, minimum, area, or trajectory-style design problem.

Financial Evidence Lab

Compare rates, costs, growth, or payment scenarios while documenting assumptions.

Integrated Modelling Lab

Prepare evidence for the Unit 13 capstone by defining variables, constraints, representations, and validation checks.

Labs may require human review when reasoning, modelling choices, communication, or contextual judgment are part of the evidence.
