Applied Mathematical Evidence

Algebra I Labs

Six complete investigations connect equations, functions, data, modelling, finance, optimization, and decision-making to observable evidence.

Lab evidence standard: each lab preserves learner work locally and uses a 0–4 evaluator rubric. A complete review of at least 80% records lab mastery. Lab mastery is supplemental evidence and does not silently bypass or replace lesson, weekly, unit, Midterm, Final, or capstone gates.

Lab 01 · Unit 06

Linear Model Lab

Use paired data, build and test a linear model, interpret slope/intercept, inspect residuals, and defend limitations.

Systems & Constraints Lab

Exponential Change Lab

Quadratic Design Lab
