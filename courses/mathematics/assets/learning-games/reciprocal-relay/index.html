A complex fraction is still a division expression.

Identify the main fraction bar first. Treat the entire numerator as the dividend and the entire denominator as the divisor.

Main bar = division.
0 ÷ 5/7 = 0zero split into nonzero groups stays zero
5/7 ÷ 0undefined
2/3 ÷ (−4/5) = −5/6different signs → negative quotient
(−3/7) ÷ (−9/14) = 2/3same signs → positive quotient

Zero and signs require separate checks.

Zero divided by a nonzero value is zero. Division by zero is undefined. For nonzero signed fractions, quotient signs follow integer division rules.

Check zero first, then sign, then magnitude.
