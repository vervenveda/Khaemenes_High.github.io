This lesson is a strong Unit 2 capstone because it combines ordered pairs, signed horizontal/vertical movement, all four integer operations, multi-step expressions, contextual modeling, and reasonableness checks.

The Animated Instruction Studio includes:

Move on the Plane — animates (4, −3) moving 7 left and 5 up to (−3, 2).
Choose the Operation — connects addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division to their contextual roles.
Multi-Step Chain — models a diver starting at −6 m, descending 4 m/min for 3 minutes, then combining 3(−4)=−12 with the starting position to reach −18 m.
Check Reasonableness — demonstrates why the sign and magnitude must both agree with the story.

The mission bank covers coordinate identification, quadrants, horizontal/vertical signed change, expressions such as:

5(−4) + 7

and

−36 ÷ 6 + 10

plus temperature, finance, elevation, repeated movement, error analysis, operation selection, vector-style coordinate changes, and full multi-stage problems.

I also preserved the lesson’s important distinction between calculating an answer and verifying that the answer makes contextual sense.
