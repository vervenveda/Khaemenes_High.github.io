Addition is movement from a starting point.

For −3 + 5, start at −3 and move five units to the right because the added value is positive.

Adding positive → move right. Adding negative → move left.
+1 and −1 form a zero pair.
Cancel matching pairs; whatever remains determines the sum.

Zero pairs explain unlike signs.

For +5 + (−3), three positive counters cancel with three negative counters. Two positive counters remain.

The uncancelled counters show both the magnitude and sign of the result.

12 − 7 = 5, and the larger magnitude belonged to −12 → result −5

Different signs create a magnitude contest.

When signs differ, subtract magnitudes. The sign of the larger magnitude controls the result.

−12 + 7 = −5 because 12 > 7.
+8 and −8 are opposites.
Their magnitudes match exactly.
8 + (−8) = 0
General rule: a + (−a) = 0

Opposites are additive inverses.

A number and its opposite cancel completely because their signed changes are equal in size and opposite in direction.

Equal magnitudes + opposite signs = zero.
