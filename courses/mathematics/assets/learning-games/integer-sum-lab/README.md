The lesson specifically teaches addition through same-sign structure, different-sign magnitude comparison, number-line movement, additive inverses, zero pairs, and real-world signed change.

The Animated Instruction Studio includes four models:

Number-Line Movement — animates −3 + 5 from −3 five units right to +2.
Zero-Pair Cancellation — builds +5 + (−3) with counters, cancels three positive/negative pairs, and leaves +2.
Magnitude Decides the Sign — compares −12 + 7, showing 12 > 7, then 12 − 7 = 5, so the result is −5.
Additive Inverses — shows why 8 + (−8) = 0 and generalizes to a + (−a) = 0.

The actual game then mixes:

same-sign sums, different-sign sums, number-line movement, zero sums, sign prediction, temperature, sports, money, elevation/movement, error analysis, additive inverses, and strategic regrouping.

For Extended students, I also included sums such as:

−8 + 5 + 8 + (−2)

where the efficient move is to pair −8 and +8 first.

And:

14 + (−6) + (−14) + 9

where additive inverses simplify the expression before the remaining sum is evaluated.
