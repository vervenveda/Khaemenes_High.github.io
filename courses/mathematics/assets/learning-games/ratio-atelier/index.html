Lesson 01 field guide

Ratio reasoning

Order matters3:5 compares the first named quantity with the second. Reversing the terms changes the meaning.
Part-to-partCompares one category with another category.
Part-to-wholeCompares one category with the total of all categories.
Equivalent ratiosMultiply or divide both terms by the same nonzero factor.
MultiplicativeRatios preserve “times as much,” not a constant difference.
