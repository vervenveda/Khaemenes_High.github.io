A fraction is a quotient and an exact number.

The denominator names the unit-sized parts; the numerator counts them. The whole expression identifies one value.

a/b = a ÷ b, with b ≠ 0.

Fractions locate exact positions.

−3/4 lies between −1 and 0. 5/4 = 1 1/4 lies between 1 and 2. Sign gives side of zero; magnitude gives distance.

Negative rationals lie left of zero; positive rationals lie right.

Rational means “can be written as a ratio.”

If a value can be expressed as a/b using integers a and b with b ≠ 0, it is rational.

Integers, terminating decimals, and repeating decimals are rational.
