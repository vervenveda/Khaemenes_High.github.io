For inverse trig, use degrees: θ = sin⁻¹(opposite/hypotenuse), cos⁻¹(adjacent/hypotenuse), or tan⁻¹(opposite/adjacent). The right angle is at B, and θ is at A. Diagrams are schematic, not drawn to scale.
