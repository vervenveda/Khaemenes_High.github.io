PEMDAS Precision Guide

1 · ParenthesesWork inside grouping symbols before outside operations.
2 · ExponentsEvaluate powers after grouped work is simplified.
3 · Multiply / DivideEqual priority. Perform whichever occurs first from the left.
4 · Add / SubtractEqual priority. Perform whichever occurs first from the left.
Common error

PEMDAS does not mean multiplication always comes before division or addition always before subtraction.
