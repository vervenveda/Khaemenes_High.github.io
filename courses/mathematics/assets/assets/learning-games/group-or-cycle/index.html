Decision Guide

Look for GCF“Largest equal groups,” “same size,” “no leftovers,” “greatest possible length,” or dividing supplies evenly.
Look for LCM“When will they meet again?”, “first time together,” repeating schedules, flashing lights, rotations, or cycles.
Trap to avoidWords like “groups” do not automatically mean GCF. Ask what quantity the problem is trying to maximize or synchronize.
Strategy

If you are dividing into equal chunks, think GCF. If two repeating patterns must meet again, think LCM.
