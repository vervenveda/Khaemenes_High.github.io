This companion follows the lesson’s core structure closely: students create common denominators as compatible units, operate with positive and negative rational values, connect subtraction to adding the opposite, choose an efficient strategy for mixed numbers, and estimate sign and magnitude before exact computation.

The Animated Instruction Studio contains four synchronized demonstrations:

Build Common Units — transforms 7/15 + 2/9 into 21/45 + 10/45 = 31/45.
Add the Opposite — shows why 3/4 − (−1/8) becomes 3/4 + 1/8 = 7/8.
Mixed Number Route — converts 2 1/4 − 1 2/3 into 9/4 − 5/3 = 7/12.
Estimate Before Exact — predicts 5/8 + 7/10 near 1 1/4, then confirms the exact result 53/40 = 1 13/40 = 1.325.

The mission bank includes all the lesson’s major skills and contexts—LCDs, like/unlike denominators, signed fraction addition, subtracting negatives, mixed numbers, estimation, error analysis, tanks, balances, temperatures, and structural reasoning about why denominators represent units rather than decorative numbers.

It also adds Extended missions such as:

5/12 + 7/18 = 29/36

−5/6 − (−1/4) = −7/12

and

3 1/3 − 1 5/6 = 1 1/2

The established architecture remains intact: Foundation / Core / Extended, 12/16/20 mission rounds, score and streak tracking, immediate explanations, hints, missed-item replay, local best score, print support, keyboard controls, responsive layout, and the 80% mastery target.
