Mathematics Readiness & Placement

A shared readiness pathway from arithmetic foundations through Calculus I. Strong performance unlocks the next level; the assessment stops when the evidence is sufficient.

This is not Week 1. Placement evidence is stored separately from grades, mastery, instructional weeks, and course completion. NAIB may use the result as one source of placement evidence.

How placement works

  1. Begin with foundational arithmetic and number reasoning.
  2. Score each domain separately.
  3. Unlock the next readiness tier when the current evidence is strong enough.
  4. Stop when prerequisites become inconsistent or all tiers are complete.
  5. Save a recommendation and review profile for NAIB, the learner, family, or teacher.
