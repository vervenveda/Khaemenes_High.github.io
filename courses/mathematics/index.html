Flexible progression

Different pathways without limiting potential.

Students can move among foundation, core, advanced, data, college-readiness, and applied mathematics while preserving a coherent secondary record.

Foundation Pathway

Number sense, arithmetic fluency, algebra readiness, mathematical language, modelling, and confidence before advancement.

Core Pathway

Algebra, geometry, functions, statistics, reasoning, investigation, and applied problem solving across the traditional sequence.

Advanced Pathway

Algebra II, trigonometry, precalculus, calculus, deeper modelling, proof, and college-readiness mathematics.

Applied Mathematics

Finance, engineering, technology, architecture, business, science, data, design, and quantitative decision making.

Intervention & Recovery

Targeted review, correction cycles, alternative explanations, reassessment, and mastery-based movement rather than permanent failure labels.

Honors & Enrichment

Deeper reasoning, proof, modelling, challenge problems, projects, independent investigation, and preparation for Higher Learning.
