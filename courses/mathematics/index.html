Flexible progression

Different pathways without limiting potential.

Support, acceleration, application, and enrichment can coexist inside one coherent secondary mathematics record.

Foundation

Number sense, arithmetic fluency, algebra readiness, mathematical language, modelling, and confidence before advancement.

Core

Algebra, geometry, functions, statistics, reasoning, investigation, and applied problem solving.

Advanced

Algebra II, trigonometry, precalculus, calculus, deeper modelling, proof, and college readiness.

Applied Mathematics

Finance, engineering, technology, architecture, business, science, data, design, and quantitative decision making.

Intervention & Recovery

Targeted review, correction cycles, alternative explanations, reassessment, and mastery-based movement.

Honors & Enrichment

Deeper reasoning, proof, challenge problems, projects, independent investigation, and Higher Learning preparation.
