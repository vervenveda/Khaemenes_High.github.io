- Games are formative practice only.
- Tools do not award mastery or progression.
- Calculator access remains controlled by assessment policy.
- Mathematics Core templates remain metadata-ready but public-viewer-pending.
- Geometry does not require a game on every lesson when CAD or structured investigation is pedagogically stronger.
- Do not create competing per-course toolbar systems.
