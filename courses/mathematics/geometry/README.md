# Khaemenes Open-Age Geometry & Spatial Design

A complete 36-week, placement-based high-school Geometry course designed for national and international use. The course is local-first, printable, evidence-based, and uses a strict 80% mastery threshold for graded progression.

## Course totals

- 36 weeks
- 180 study sessions
- 13 units
- 90 detailed visual printable lessons
- 90 separate teacher answer keys
- 90 evaluator-scored lesson mastery gates at ≥80%
- 36 week-specific mastery gates
- 10 questions per week / 360 unique weekly mastery prompts
- 5 weekly depth dimensions: procedural fluency, multi-step application, representation/interpretation, error analysis, and transfer reasoning
- 13 formal unit mastery checks
- 20 questions per unit check, balanced across every instructional week in the unit and all five depth dimensions
- 13 applied investigations
- 40-question non-gating readiness diagnostic
- 156-item cumulative selected-response source bank
- Midterm: 60 selected-response questions + 6 required constructed-response tasks
- Final: 100 selected-response questions + 10 required constructed-response tasks
- Cumulative exam weighting: 70% selected response / 30% human-reviewed constructed response
- Full Midterm/Final mastery requires ≥80% selected response, ≥80% constructed response, and ≥80% overall
- Geometry modelling capstone and defense at ≥80%
- Printable evidence-gated completion record
- Foundation, Core, and Extended pathways
- Browser-local learner and evaluator records

## Strict progression

A below-80 attempt is retained as learning evidence but does not unlock the next graded stage. Corrections support reteaching and retesting; they do not substitute for reaching the mastery threshold.

The progression chain is:

`lesson evidence ≥80% → weekly mastery ≥80% → unit mastery ≥80% → reviewed cumulative exams ≥80% → investigations + capstone/defense → completion record`

Unit 07 additionally requires reviewed Midterm mastery before entry. The Final requires all 13 unit mastery checks, all 36 weekly gates, and reviewed Midterm mastery.

## Assessment and record trust

Assessment and completion records are stored in the browser. They are editable, are not digitally signed, and are not independently verified by a server. The software treats them as local evidence rather than a self-authenticating transcript or credential. Official credit remains the responsibility of the parent, teacher, school, or authorized evaluator reviewing the retained evidence.
