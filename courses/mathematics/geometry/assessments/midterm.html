Units 01–06 · Mid-Course Examination

Geometry Midterm Examination

Mixed-evidence mastery across the first six Geometry units

60 selected-response questions plus 6 required constructed-response geometry tasks. Selected response contributes 70%; evaluator-reviewed geometric reasoning contributes 30%.

Administration Guide
Learning ObjectiveDemonstrate cumulative Geometry mastery across foundations, transformations, congruence, coordinate proof, similarity, right triangles, trigonometry, measurement, and mathematical justification.
DirectionsComplete all 60 selected-response questions and all 6 written geometry tasks. Use diagrams, calculations, theorem references, units, and verification where appropriate.

Selected-Response Assessment

6 sections · 10 questions per unit · 60 total

0/60 answered · 0/6 written complete

Constructed-Response Geometry Evidence

Complete all six written tasks. Each response needs at least 60 meaningful characters and should include diagrams or representations when useful.

Evaluator Review · Geometry Reasoning

Full mastery requires ≥80% selected response, ≥80% constructed response, and ≥80% overall. Reviewer identity, role, note, and attestation are retained with the local review record.

  • 4 · Complete and correct geometric reasoning; diagram/representation, theorem or formula, units, verification, interpretation, and relevant limitations are explicit.
  • 3 · Substantially correct reasoning with a minor omission or small non-conceptual error.
  • 2 · Partial reasoning shows important understanding but contains a conceptual gap, incomplete verification, or weak interpretation.
  • 1 · Limited relevant work; major misconceptions or unsupported conclusions remain.
  • 0 · Blank, unrelated, or no assessable geometric reasoning.
