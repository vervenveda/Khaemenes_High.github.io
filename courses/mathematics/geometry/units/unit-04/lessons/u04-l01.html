Parallel Lines Cut by a Transversal

Use corresponding, alternate interior, alternate exterior, and same-side interior angle relationships.

Open-AgePrintableFoundation · Core · ExtendedMastery target 80%

Learning intention

Use accurate diagrams, definitions, measurements, and reasoning to use corresponding, alternate interior, alternate exterior, and same-side interior angle relationships.

Success evidence

  • Label the figure and units before calculating.
  • Select and justify the governing definition, theorem, relationship, or construction.
  • Show enough work that another learner can audit the conclusion.
  • Check the result against the diagram, scale, and stated constraints.

Visual model

Combine angle relationships, slope, distance, midpoint, and equations of lines to prove geometric properties analytically.

m=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}d=\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2}m_1m_2=-1

Worked reasoning

Process: Compute only the measurements needed for the claim, compare them with a definition, and write a conclusion tied to the coordinates.

  1. Read the figure and rewrite all given information with correct notation.
  2. Identify the exact relationship that connects the known and unknown quantities.
  3. Substitute, construct, or prove one justified step at a time.
  4. State the result with units and a sentence explaining what it means.

Misconception check: Perpendicular slopes are negative reciprocals only when both slopes are defined; vertical and horizontal lines must be treated separately.
