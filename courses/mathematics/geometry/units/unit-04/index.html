Lesson sequence

6 visual, printable lessons

Lesson 01

Parallel Lines Cut by a Transversal

Use corresponding, alternate interior, alternate exterior, and same-side interior angle relationships created by a transversal.

 Lesson 02

Proving Lines Parallel from Angle Relationships

Use converses of transversal-angle theorems to determine and justify when two lines must be parallel.

 Lesson 03

Slope as Direction and Rate

Interpret slope as vertical change over horizontal change and connect equal slopes with parallel nonvertical lines.

 Lesson 04

Parallel and Perpendicular Slopes

Use equal slopes, negative reciprocals, and horizontal/vertical special cases to establish line relationships.

 Lesson 05

Coordinate Proof with Distance, Midpoint & Slope

Choose slope, distance, and midpoint evidence strategically to prove geometric properties on the coordinate plane.

 Lesson 06

Equations of Lines in Geometric Context

Write and interpret equations of lines from points, slopes, and required parallel or perpendicular relationships.
