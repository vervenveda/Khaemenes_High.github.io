Triangle Congruence & Proof

Weeks 6–8 · Seven lessons · Essential question: How can limited measurements prove that triangles and their corresponding parts must agree?

What learners will study

  1. Triangle classification and angle relationships
  2. SSS and SAS congruence
  3. ASA, AAS, and HL congruence
  4. CPCTC and multi-step proof
  5. Isosceles and equilateral triangle theorems
  6. Triangle inequality and feasible construction
  7. Perpendicular bisectors, angle bisectors, and triangle centers

How to support without replacing learner reasoning

  • Ask the learner to identify the exact marked or given evidence before naming a theorem.
  • Ask, “Which vertex corresponds to which?” before accepting a congruence statement.
  • Have the learner explain why CPCTC can only be used after the triangles are proven congruent.
  • For triangle inequality, ask whether the boundary value makes a real triangle or a straight segment.
  • For triangle centers, ask whether the point is equidistant from vertices, sides, or neither.
  • Encourage written corrections when a proof step is missing or a theorem is misapplied.

Mastery evidence

The unit mastery target remains 80%. Objective selected-response evidence can be scored locally in the browser. Proofs, constructions, explanations, written corrections, and project work may require review by a teacher, mentor, parent, or other qualified educational reviewer.

A lower objective score should trigger targeted review of the specific lesson/skill evidence rather than repeating the entire unit without diagnosis.

Applied investigation

The unit project is Triangle-Center Construction & Congruence Defense. A strong submission preserves construction marks, identifies correspondence accurately, uses a valid congruence argument, justifies a center or bisector property, and includes verification and revision evidence.

Questions families can ask

  • What information is given, and what are you trying to prove?
  • Which congruence theorem fits the evidence, and why do the others not fit?
  • What becomes available by CPCTC only after congruence is established?
  • Is this triangle feasible, degenerate, or impossible?
  • Which construction lines create this triangle center, and what equal-distance property follows?
