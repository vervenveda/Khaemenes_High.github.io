Unit 03 · Lesson 05 · Triangle Congruence & Proof

Isosceles and Equilateral Triangle Theorems

Connect equal sides with equal opposite angles, use the converses, and reason about the special structure of equilateral triangles.

Open-AgePrintableFoundation · Core · ExtendedMastery target 80%

Learning intention

Use the isosceles triangle theorem and its converse to move logically between side congruence and angle congruence.

Success evidence

  • If two sides of a triangle are congruent, identify the congruent opposite angles.
  • If two angles are congruent, identify the congruent opposite sides.
  • Use the 180° angle sum with isosceles structure.
  • Recognize that an equilateral triangle is also equiangular, with three 60° angles.

Side-angle pairing

AB≅AC ⇒ ∠B≅∠C∠B≅∠C ⇒ AB≅ACequilateral ⇔ equiangularequilateral angles = 60° each

The equal angles in an isosceles triangle are opposite the equal sides. Keeping the opposite side-angle pairing straight is the central reasoning skill.

Worked reasoning

Given: AB≅AC and m∠A=44°. Find m∠B and m∠C.

  1. AB≅AC, so the opposite base angles ∠C and ∠B are congruent.
  2. Let m∠B=m∠C=x.
  3. 44+x+x=180.
  4. 2x=136, so x=68.

Converse: If instead ∠B≅∠C were given, then AB≅AC would follow.
