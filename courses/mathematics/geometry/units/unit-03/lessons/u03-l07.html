Perpendicular Bisectors, Angle Bisectors & Centers

Connect each triangle center to the lines that create it, its equal-distance property, and its geometric location.

Open-AgePrintableFoundation · Core · ExtendedMastery target 80%

Learning intention

Distinguish circumcenter, incenter, centroid, and orthocenter by construction and purpose.

Success evidence

  • Match perpendicular bisectors to the circumcenter and angle bisectors to the incenter.
  • Match medians to the centroid and altitudes to the orthocenter.
  • Use equal-distance properties of circumcenter and incenter.
  • Predict whether a center lies inside, on, or outside a triangle when appropriate.

Four concurrency centers

perpendicular bisectors → circumcenterangle bisectors → incentermedians → centroidaltitudes → orthocenter

Circumcenter: equidistant from the vertices. Incenter: equidistant from the sides. Centroid: balances the triangle and divides each median 2:1 from vertex to midpoint. Orthocenter: intersection of the altitudes.

Worked reasoning

Question: A point P lies on the perpendicular bisectors of AB and BC. What distances are equal?

  1. On the perpendicular bisector of AB, PA=PB.
  2. On the perpendicular bisector of BC, PB=PC.
  3. Therefore PA=PB=PC.
  4. P is the circumcenter of ΔABC.

Do not swap the distance properties: circumcenter → vertices; incenter → sides.
