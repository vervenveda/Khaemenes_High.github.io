Unit 03 · Lesson 03 · Triangle Congruence & Proof

ASA, AAS and HL Congruence

Use angle-side evidence and the special right-triangle HL theorem to decide when triangle congruence is guaranteed.

Open-AgePrintableFoundation · Core · ExtendedMastery target 80%

Learning intention

Distinguish ASA from AAS by the location of the known side, and apply HL only when both triangles are right triangles with congruent hypotenuses and one pair of congruent legs.

Success evidence

  • Identify the included side in ASA.
  • Recognize a non-included side in AAS.
  • Verify the right-triangle condition before using HL.
  • Explain why AAA establishes shape but not size.

The evidence patterns

ASA: angle–included side–angleAAS: angle–angle–non-included sideHL: right triangles + hypotenuse + legAAA → similarity, not congruence

Congruence evidence must determine both shape and size. Two angles determine the third angle automatically, but at least one appropriate length is still needed to fix scale.

Worked reasoning

Given: ∠A≅∠D, ∠B≅∠E, and AB≅DE.

  1. Match A↔D and B↔E from the angle evidence.
  2. AB and DE lie between the two known angles.
  3. The evidence therefore has the pattern angle–side–angle.
  4. Conclude ΔABC≅ΔDEF by ASA.

HL caution: Hypotenuse-leg is not a general SSA shortcut. It works because the right angles already supply an additional fixed condition.
