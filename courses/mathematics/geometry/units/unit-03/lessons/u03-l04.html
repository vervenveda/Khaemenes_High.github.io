Unit 03 · Lesson 04 · Triangle Congruence & Proof

CPCTC and Multi-Step Triangle Proofs

Establish triangle congruence first, then use correspondence to justify new side and angle relationships.

Open-AgePrintableFoundation · Core · ExtendedMastery target 80%

Learning intention

Build proof chains in the correct logical order: givens and definitions → triangle congruence → corresponding parts.

Success evidence

  • Read correspondence correctly from a congruence statement.
  • Use CPCTC only after triangle congruence has been established.
  • Distinguish the theorem that proves congruence from CPCTC, which uses congruence.
  • Write short auditable multi-step arguments with a reason for every claim.

The proof chain

1. Gather evidence2. Prove Δ ≅ Δ3. Match vertices4. Apply CPCTC

CPCTC means Corresponding Parts of Congruent Triangles are Congruent. It is not a triangle-congruence theorem. It becomes available only after a valid congruence theorem such as SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, or HL has established the triangles are congruent.

Worked proof

Given: AB≅DE, BC≅EF, AC≅DF. Prove: ∠B≅∠E.

  1. AB≅DE, BC≅EF, AC≅DF — given.
  2. ΔABC≅ΔDEF — SSS.
  3. B corresponds to E from the congruence statement.
  4. ∠B≅∠E — CPCTC.

Order matters: Writing “∠B≅∠E by CPCTC” before proving the triangles congruent is circular and incomplete.
