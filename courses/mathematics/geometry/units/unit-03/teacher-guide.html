Teacher guide

Triangle Congruence & Proof

Weeks 6–8 · Seven lessons · Mastery target 80%

Suggested sequence

  1. Triangle Classification & Angle Relationships
  2. SSS and SAS Congruence
  3. ASA, AAS and HL Congruence
  4. CPCTC and Multi-Step Triangle Proofs
  5. Isosceles and Equilateral Triangle Theorems
  6. Triangle Inequality & Feasible Construction
  7. Perpendicular Bisectors, Angle Bisectors & Centers

Instructional priorities

  • Require correspondence to be established before theorem names or CPCTC conclusions are written.
  • Keep congruence criteria distinct: SSS/SAS in Lesson 02, ASA/AAS/HL in Lesson 03.
  • Emphasize that CPCTC is downstream of a valid triangle-congruence proof.
  • Connect isosceles/equilateral side-angle relationships to their converses.
  • Use strict inequalities and endpoint reasoning for triangle feasibility.
  • Distinguish circumcenter, incenter, centroid, and orthocenter by both construction and defining property.
  • Treat diagrams as evidence only when markings, givens, constructions, or proven relationships support the claim.

Differentiated practice

Foundation: supported correspondence, theorem selection, angle/side relationships, and center identification.

Core: independent congruence reasoning, proof chains, algebraic side/angle problems, inequality ranges, and center applications.

Extended: proof diagnosis, construction justification, underdetermination checks, design constraints, and multi-step generalization.

Pathways are evidence routes rather than permanent learner labels.

Assessment and mastery evidence

  • The Unit 03 mastery layer should use a lesson-aligned 35-item bank: five objective items for each of the seven lessons.
  • The browser mastery check remains 12 questions with an 80% target.
  • Missed question IDs should route review to the associated lesson and skill rather than to a generic unit label.
  • Automatically scored selected responses do not replace human review of proofs, constructions, corrections, and project evidence.
  • Below mastery, assign targeted correction or Foundation/Core practice before another attempt.

Applied investigation

Triangle-Center Construction & Congruence Defense: require a reproducible construction, correct use of at least one triangle-congruence argument, explicit correspondence, a justified center or bisector relationship, verification, and revision evidence.

Common misconceptions to watch

  • Using SSA as a general triangle-congruence theorem.
  • Calling AAA a congruence theorem instead of a similarity condition.
  • Using CPCTC before proving triangle congruence.
  • Mismatching vertex order in a congruence statement.
  • Pairing equal sides with adjacent rather than opposite equal angles in an isosceles triangle.
  • Using ≥ instead of > in triangle inequality.
  • Confusing circumcenter distance-to-vertices with incenter distance-to-sides.
  • Assuming centroid, circumcenter, incenter, and orthocenter always occupy the same point.

Record language

Keep objective mastery scores, written correction evidence, proof/construction review, and project rubric evidence distinguishable. Report only evidence the learner actually produced and that was actually reviewed.
