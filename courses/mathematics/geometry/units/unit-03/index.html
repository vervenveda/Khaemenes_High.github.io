Unit 03 · Weeks 6, 7, 8

Triangle Congruence & Proof

How can limited measurements prove that triangles and their corresponding parts must agree?

Begin UnitCore PracticeMastery CheckApplied Investigation

Lesson sequence

7 visual, printable lessons

Lesson 01

Triangle Classification & Angle Relationships

Classify by sides and angles, use angle-sum and exterior-angle relationships, and distinguish consistent from impossible triangle information.

Lesson 02

SSS and SAS Congruence

Match corresponding vertices and use three side pairs or two sides with the included angle to establish congruence while rejecting insufficient SSA evidence.

Lesson 03

ASA, AAS and HL Congruence

Distinguish included and non-included side evidence, verify right-triangle conditions for HL, and explain why AAA proves similarity rather than congruence.

Lesson 04

CPCTC and Multi-Step Triangle Proofs

Build proof chains in the correct order: establish triangle congruence, preserve correspondence, then justify new side and angle relationships by CPCTC.

Lesson 05

Isosceles and Equilateral Triangle Theorems

Connect equal sides with equal opposite angles, use the converse, and reason with equilateral/equiangular structure.

Lesson 06

Triangle Inequality & Feasible Construction

Distinguish feasible, degenerate, and impossible side sets and solve strict missing-side intervals for construction and design.

Lesson 07

Perpendicular Bisectors, Angle Bisectors & Centers

Match concurrency constructions with circumcenter, incenter, centroid, and orthocenter and use each center's defining geometric property.
