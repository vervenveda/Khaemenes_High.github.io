Polygons & Quadrilaterals

Move from polygon angle structure through quadrilateral proof and coordinate evidence into regular-polygon measurement and constraint-driven design.

Seven-stage learning path

  1. Interior & Exterior Angles — derive sums, regular angles, and inverse side-count problems.
  2. Parallelogram Properties & Tests — distinguish properties from sufficient converse tests.
  3. Rectangles, Rhombi & Squares — use hierarchy and diagonal evidence.
  4. Trapezoids, Kites & Midsegments — compare bases, legs, diagonals, symmetry, and midsegments.
  5. Quadrilateral Coordinate Proofs — classify with slope, distance, and midpoint.
  6. Regular Polygons, Apothem & Central Angles — connect triangle decomposition, perimeter, and area.
  7. Polygon Decomposition & Design Constraints — optimize and defend a polygon pavilion design.

Unit evidence

Printable lesson work, 35-item hardened assessment bank, 12-question mastery sampling, applied pavilion project, reflection/corrections, and human-reviewed proof/design evidence.
