Regular Polygons, Apothem & Central Angles

Decompose regular polygons into congruent triangles to connect central angle, apothem, perimeter, and area.

Regular-polygon model

Connecting the center to all vertices creates n congruent isosceles triangles. Each central angle is 360°/n. The apothem is perpendicular to a side and acts as the triangle height.

central angle=360°/nP=nsA=½aP

Worked example

A regular hexagon with side 8 and apothem 4√3 has P=48, so A=½(4√3)(48)=96√3 square units.

Misconception: The apothem goes to the midpoint of a side, not to a vertex.
