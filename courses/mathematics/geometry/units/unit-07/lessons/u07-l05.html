Unit 07 · Lesson 05

Quadrilateral Coordinate Proofs

Use slope, distance, and midpoint evidence to classify quadrilaterals on the coordinate plane.

Evidence toolkit

m=(y₂−y₁)/(x₂−x₁)d=√((x₂−x₁)²+(y₂−y₁)²)M=((x₁+x₂)/2,(y₁+y₂)/2)

Use slope for parallel/perpendicular evidence, distance for congruent sides or diagonals, and midpoint for diagonal bisection. Choose the smallest sufficient evidence set.

Worked example

For A(0,0), B(4,0), C(4,3), D(0,3), adjacent slopes are 0 and undefined, giving right angles; opposite sides have equal lengths. ABCD is a rectangle.

Precision: Do not classify from appearance; calculate evidence.
