Unit 07 · Lesson 01

Interior and Exterior Angles of Polygons

Derive and apply polygon angle sums, then reverse the formulas to determine an unknown number of sides.

Mastery target 80%Foundation · Core · Extended

Learning intention

Connect polygon triangulation to S=(n−2)180°, use the constant exterior-angle sum 360°, and distinguish sums from individual angles in regular polygons.

Worked example

A regular 12-gon has interior sum (12−2)180°=1800°. Each exterior angle is 360°÷12=30°, so each interior angle is 150°.

Misconception check: 360° is the sum of one exterior angle at every vertex, not each exterior angle.
