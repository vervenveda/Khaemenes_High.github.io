Parallelogram Properties & Tests

Use properties and converse tests to prove when a quadrilateral is a parallelogram.

Mastery target 80%

Core reasoning

In a parallelogram, opposite sides are parallel and congruent, opposite angles are congruent, consecutive angles are supplementary, and diagonals bisect each other.

Sufficient tests: both opposite-side pairs parallel; both opposite-side pairs congruent; one opposite-side pair both parallel and congruent; diagonals bisect each other; or both opposite-angle pairs congruent.

Worked example

If diagonals AC and BD meet at M with AM=MC and BM=MD, the diagonals bisect each other; therefore ABCD is a parallelogram.

Misconception: Congruent diagonals alone do not prove parallelogram.
