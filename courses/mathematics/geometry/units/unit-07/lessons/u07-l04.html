Worked reasoning

Process: List the properties required by the classification, calculate or prove each one, then state the most specific valid name.

  1. Read the figure and rewrite all given information with correct notation.
  2. Identify the exact relationship that connects the known and unknown quantities.
  3. Substitute, construct, or prove one justified step at a time.
  4. State the result with units and a sentence explaining what it means.

Misconception check: A square belongs to both the rectangle and rhombus families; classifications are hierarchical rather than mutually exclusive.
