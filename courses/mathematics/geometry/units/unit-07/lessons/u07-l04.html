Unit 07 · Lesson 04

Trapezoids, Kites & Midsegments

Compare non-parallelogram quadrilaterals using bases, legs, diagonals, symmetry, and midsegments.

Core relationships

A trapezoid has one pair of parallel bases. Its midsegment is parallel to the bases and has length m=(b₁+b₂)/2. In an isosceles trapezoid the legs are congruent, each pair of base angles is congruent, and diagonals are congruent.

A kite has two distinct pairs of adjacent congruent sides. Its diagonals are perpendicular; one diagonal bisects the other and bisects a pair of opposite angles.

Misconception: Kite congruent sides are adjacent, not opposite.

Trapezoid & Kite Reasoning

2

3

4

5

6
