Unit 07 · Lesson 03

Rectangles, Rhombi & Squares

Use the quadrilateral hierarchy and diagonal properties to classify special parallelograms.

Property map

Rectangle: parallelogram with four right angles; diagonals congruent. Rhombus: parallelogram with four congruent sides; diagonals perpendicular and bisect opposite angles. Square: both rectangle and rhombus, so it inherits all of these properties.

Worked example

A parallelogram whose diagonals are both congruent and perpendicular must be a square: congruent diagonals give the rectangle condition; perpendicular diagonals give the rhombus condition.

Hierarchy check: Every square is a rectangle and rhombus; not every rectangle or rhombus is a square.
