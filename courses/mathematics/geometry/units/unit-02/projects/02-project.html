Design brief

Create a geometric investigation or design that demonstrates the unit relationships in a form another learner can inspect, reproduce, and challenge.

  1. State the question, audience, constraints, units, and success criteria.
  2. Create a labeled planning sketch or construction.
  3. Show at least three mathematical relationships from the unit.
  4. Verify one result using a second method, digital tool, construction, or measurement.
  5. Explain assumptions, precision, limitations, and one revision.
  6. Submit a printable one- to three-page portfolio with source files or exports when applicable.
