Unit 02 · Weeks 3, 4, 5

Transformations, Symmetry & Congruence

How do transformations preserve, alter, and organize figures in the plane?

Begin UnitCore PracticeMastery CheckApplied Investigation

Lesson sequence

7 visual, printable lessons

Lesson 01

Translations on the Coordinate Plane

Apply and recover translation vectors, track preimage-to-image coordinates, and justify the distance and angle measures preserved by translations.

 Lesson 02

Reflections Across Axes and Lines

Reflect points and figures across coordinate axes and other lines while connecting coordinate rules to perpendicular-bisector geometry and orientation.

 Lesson 03

Rotations About the Origin and Other Centers

Rotate figures about specified centers, distinguish clockwise from counterclockwise motion, and use preserved distance from the center to verify images.

 Lesson 04

Compositions of Transformations

Apply rigid motions in the stated order, compare reversed compositions, and describe the complete mapping from preimage to final image.

 Lesson 05

Rigid Motion Definition of Congruence

Establish congruence through sequences of rigid motions and identify the corresponding lengths, angles, and points preserved by the mapping.

 Lesson 06

Line, Rotational & Point Symmetry

Use exact transformations to justify line, rotational, and point symmetry, including rotational order and smallest positive rotation angle.

 Lesson 07

Tessellations, Frieze Patterns & Design Rules

Analyze repeating transformation rules, vertex-angle constraints, gaps and overlaps, and the symmetries that generate tessellations and frieze patterns.
