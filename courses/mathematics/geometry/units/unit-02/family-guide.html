Family guide

Transformations, Symmetry & Congruence

Weeks 3–5 · Seven lessons · Essential question: How do transformations preserve, alter, and organize figures in the plane?

What learners will study

  1. Translations and vectors
  2. Reflections across axes and lines
  3. Rotations about a center
  4. Compositions and order of transformations
  5. Rigid motions and congruence
  6. Line, rotational, and point symmetry
  7. Tessellations, frieze patterns, and design rules

How to support without giving away the mathematics

  • Ask, “What changed, and what stayed invariant?” after each transformation.
  • Have the learner point to the preimage, image, center/line/vector, and corresponding points.
  • Ask the learner to perform a composition one step at a time and verify the final coordinates.
  • Use graph paper, mirrors, tracing paper, floor tiles, maps, packaging, or repeating household patterns when helpful.
  • Encourage exact labels, units, and written reasons rather than accepting “it looks right.”
  • Treat corrections as evidence of learning, not as punishment for an earlier attempt.

Mastery evidence

The local mastery check draws 12 questions from a 35-item bank aligned five questions to each lesson. The target remains 80%. A lower score should trigger review of the missed lesson skills, written corrections or targeted practice, and a new attempt when ready.

Selected-response questions are scored automatically in the browser. Explanations, design work, constructions, and project/portfolio evidence may need review by a teacher, mentor, parent, or other qualified educational reviewer.

Applied investigation

The unit project is Arcade Symmetry Studio & Transformation Design. A strong submission shows a repeatable transformation rule, at least three accurate unit relationships, a verification method, an explanation of constraints, and evidence of revision.

Questions families can ask

  • How do you know this move is rigid?
  • What transformation would undo the one you just performed?
  • Does changing the order change the final image?
  • What exact transformation proves this symmetry?
  • Why does this pattern tessellate without gaps or overlaps?
