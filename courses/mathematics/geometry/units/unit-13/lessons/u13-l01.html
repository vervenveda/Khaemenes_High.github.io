Learning intention

Use accurate diagrams, definitions, measurements, and reasoning to frame, build, test, document, and defend a geometric investigation or design using evidence, proof, and responsible limitations.

Success evidence

  • Label the figure and units before calculating.
  • Select and justify the governing definition, theorem, relationship, or construction.
  • Show enough work that another learner can audit the conclusion.
  • Check the result against the diagram, scale, and stated constraints.

Worked reasoning

Process: Define the question and constraints, select appropriate geometry, build and test the model, document limitations, and revise after critique.

  1. Read the figure and rewrite all given information with correct notation.
  2. Identify the exact relationship that connects the known and unknown quantities.
  3. Substitute, construct, or prove one justified step at a time.
  4. State the result with units and a sentence explaining what it means.

Misconception check: A polished final product does not replace mathematical evidence; the model must show how measurements, constructions, and conclusions were validated.
