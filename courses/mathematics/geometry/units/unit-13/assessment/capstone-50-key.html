Unit 13 · Teacher Guide

50-Question Cumulative Geometry Capstone

This assessment is additional to the comprehensive final examination. Suggested weighting: 1 point per item = 50 total points. Questions 41–50 are mathematical judgment/explanation prompts, not tests of personal morality.

Part A answer sequence · 1–25

C, B, B, B, C, A, B, A, B, C, D, C, B, D, B, A, B, A, B, B, B, B, C, C, D

Part B expected responses · 26–40

  1. If two angles are congruent, then they are vertical angles. Note: the converse is a valid grammatical converse but is not generally true; this is intentional critical thinking.
  2. (-4,-3)
  3. SAS
  4. 4/3
  5. 12 cm
  6. 3/5 or 0.6
  7. 720°
  8. 7 cm
  9. (2,7)
  10. 54 cm²
  11. 180 cm³
  12. 3.5 m
  13. Accept included angle, corresponding altitude, or third side with a valid area method.
  14. measurements/givens (or equivalent evidence/data wording)
  15. Robust over the tested range.

Part C scoring · 41–50

Use 1 point each. Award the point when the response identifies the central mathematical responsibility and gives a relevant reason. Do not score writing style, confidence, ideology, or agreement with a teacher's personal values.

  1. Appearance alone does not establish parallelism; additional evidence is required.
  2. Shared bad input can make independent-looking outputs agree; verify source data.
  3. Correct the calculation/model, revise affected conclusions, and document the change.
  4. Output can change while the decision remains robust if all tested values stay on the same safe side of the threshold.
  5. Respond with verified evidence; remain open to a new substantive measurement/theorem challenge.
  6. When evidence is insufficient, inventing a number overclaims what is known.
  7. A true conclusion does not repair an invalid inference; proof validity depends on every required step.
  8. Tested-range evidence supports only the tested range/conditions unless further evidence justifies generalization.
  9. Recheck endpoints, units, slope calculations, CAD constraints/settings, and data entry; resolve discrepancy rather than choosing authority.
  10. Revise the conclusion or narrow it when verified evidence overturns a premise; record why.

Mastery

40/50 = 80%. Require corrections for missed items. For reasoning items, corrections must explain the original error. This capstone does not replace or reduce the separate final-exam requirement.
