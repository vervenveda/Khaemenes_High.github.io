What happened to the Unit 13 lessons?

Concepts preserved without adding workload

The capstone questions reuse the strongest reasoning habits developed across the course: constraint, validation, sensitivity, traceability, rebuttal, and sufficiency. The former lesson files remain available to teachers as optional remediation/reference resources, but they are no longer part of the required student path.

Students who need targeted support may revisit Foundation, Core, or Extended practice before reassessment. Course mastery remains 80% plus corrections.

Mathematical judgment

Responsible reasoning counts

Some capstone responses ask students to explain what responsible mathematical practice requires: verify conflicting evidence, acknowledge uncertainty, correct known errors, distinguish appearance from proof, document revisions, and avoid claiming more than the evidence establishes. These prompts assess mathematical judgment—not a student's personal beliefs.
