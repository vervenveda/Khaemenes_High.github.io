Instructional sequence

Seven distinct CAD + geometry lessons

L01 · Word: Snap

CAD Workspace, Units, Grid & Snap

Control units, workspace boundaries, grids, snap increments, coordinate placement, and recovery before drafting.

L02 · Word: Tolerance

Precision Lines, Angles & Dimensions

Connect exact construction with dimensions, tolerance, absolute/relative error, and appropriate significant figures.

L03 · Word: Layer

Layers, Objects & Geometric Organization

Organize geometry for visibility, locking, grouping, revision, and technical communication.

L04 · Word: Scale

Scale Plans, Rooms & Openings

Draft spaces proportionally and test openings, door swing, clear width, circulation, and design constraints.

L05 · Word: Quantity

Area, Perimeter & Material Schedules

Turn measured plans into auditable material estimates, waste allowances, and purchase quantities.

L06 · Word: Transformation

Transformations, Repetition & Modular Design

Use translations, rotations, reflections, dilation, invariants, and symmetry to build verified modular systems.

L07 · Word: Vector

Export, Print/PDF, SVG, DXF & Design Defense

Choose output by purpose, preserve editable source geometry, verify print scale, and defend design choices with mathematical evidence.
