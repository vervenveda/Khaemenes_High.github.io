Unit 12 · Weeks 32, 33, 34

CAD, Scale Drawing & Spatial Design

How can precise digital drafting turn geometric constraints into measurable, printable, and defensible designs?

Begin UnitCore PracticeMastery CheckApplied Investigation

Lesson sequence

7 visual, printable lessons

Lesson 01

CAD Workspace, Units, Grid and Snap

Configure OHMIC units, sheet size, grid, snap, scale, and project recovery before drawing.

Lesson 02

Precision Lines, Angles and Dimensions

Report measurement, absolute error, relative error, and appropriate significant figures.

Lesson 03

Layers, Objects and Geometric Organization

Separate walls, dimensions, annotations, and symbols into purposeful layers and object groups.

Lesson 04

Scale Plans, Rooms and Openings

Draft rooms and openings at scale and justify dimensions, accessibility, and circulation constraints.

Lesson 05

Area, Perimeter and Material Schedules

Use live length and area analysis to estimate quantities and document assumptions.

Lesson 06

Transformations, Repetition and Modular Design

Create modular or radial designs using repeated, rotated, reflected, or translated components.

Lesson 07

Export, Print/PDF, SVG, DXF and Design Defense

Prepare technical outputs as Print/PDF, SVG, PNG, simplified DXF, JSON, and CSV while explaining the role of each format.
