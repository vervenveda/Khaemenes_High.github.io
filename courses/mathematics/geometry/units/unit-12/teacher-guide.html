Unit 12 · Teacher Guide

CAD, Scale Drawing & Spatial Design

Teach CAD as mathematical modeling and technical communication. The software is a laboratory for geometry, not a substitute for setup, calculation, verification, or explanation.

Seven-lesson progression

  1. L01 Workspace, Units, Grid & Snap: establish a controlled drafting environment and recovery strategy.
  2. L02 Precision Lines, Angles & Dimensions: connect exact construction to tolerance, error, and appropriate precision.
  3. L03 Layers, Objects & Organization: structure geometry for selective visibility, locking, editing, and communication.
  4. L04 Scale Plans, Rooms & Openings: use proportional scale and check clear width, swing, circulation, and constraints.
  5. L05 Area, Perimeter & Material Schedules: convert measured plans into auditable quantity estimates and purchase reasoning.
  6. L06 Transformations, Repetition & Modular Design: create repeated systems from explicit transformation rules and invariants.
  7. L07 Export & Design Defense: choose outputs by purpose, preserve source geometry, verify print scale, and defend the design mathematically.

Math Word of the Day

Use one vocabulary anchor per daily lesson: snap · tolerance · layer · scale · quantity · transformation · vector. Read the word in context, require the retrieval check, then revisit it during the worksheet/lab. The cumulative vocabulary file contains extended definitions and related technical terms.

Instructional contract

  • Predict or calculate first; verify with CAD second.
  • Require units, scale, source measurements, and assumptions on technical work.
  • Distinguish exact construction, measured observation, and acceptable tolerance.
  • Use Foundation/Core/Extended as temporary support/challenge pathways, never permanent labels.
  • Keep teacher keys separate during independent work.
  • Corrections are evidence of learning and occur before reassessment.

Assessment & intervention

The mastery check now uses a dedicated 14-question Unit-12 bank with exactly two questions from each lesson. Target is 80%.

  • 80–100%: correct missed items, complete/defend the portfolio, then advance.
  • 60–79%: identify the missed lesson tags, assign targeted Foundation/Core work and a short CAD recheck, then reassess.
  • Below 60%: return to setup, unit/scale reasoning, visible models, and teacher-guided construction before reassessment.

Do not regenerate or repeat assessment attempts without instruction and corrections between attempts.

Positive reinforcement

Challenge Unlocked appears only after successful lesson checkpoints. Geometry Sanctuary, Mandala Rings, or a brief logic challenge may reinforce the concept. Arcade work is optional, ungraded, and never substitutes for mastery evidence or corrections.

Applied portfolio

The OHMIC CAD Spatial Design Portfolio now requires setup/precision records, layer organization, scale and circulation evidence, material quantities, transformation/module evidence, editable plus presentation outputs, print-scale verification, and a 60–90 second design defense. Project mastery is 20/24 (at least 80%) with corrections.
