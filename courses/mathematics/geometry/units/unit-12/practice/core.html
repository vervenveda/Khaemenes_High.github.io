Unit 12 · Core Pathway

CAD, Scale Drawing & Spatial Design

Grade-level mixed practice across all seven lesson identities. Show complete calculations, units, and design reasoning.

1 · L01

A project changes from millimeters to centimeters after several dimensions are entered. Describe two errors this can create and a recovery procedure.

2 · L01

Choose a sensible snap increment for drafting a room whose dimensions are normally recorded to the nearest centimeter. Defend your choice.

3 · L02

A dimension is 80.0 mm ±0.4 mm. A measured part is 80.3 mm. Determine pass/fail, absolute error from target, and relative percent error.

4 · L02

An intended 35° line is measured at 34.6°. Find angular error and explain how annotation differs from constructing the exact angle.

5 · L03

Design a layer/group strategy for walls, doors, dimensions, furniture, electrical symbols, and notes. Explain one hide/show decision and one lock decision.

6 · L03

A student draws every object on one layer. Give three concrete problems this creates during revision or review.

7 · L04

A 7.2 m × 4.8 m room is drawn at 1:60. Find both plan dimensions in centimeters.

8 · L04

A passage measures 2.1 cm on a 1:50 plan. A design constraint requires 1.00 m clear width. Determine whether it passes and by what margin.

9 · L05

A 8 m × 5.5 m room needs flooring with 7% waste. Flooring comes in 2.2 m² boxes. Find the minimum number of boxes.

10 · L05

A 9 m × 3 m wall has a 2.0 m × 1.5 m window and 0.9 m × 2.1 m door. Find net paintable area.

11 · L06

Rotate A(3,−1), B(5,−1), C(3,2) 90° counterclockwise about the origin, then state two invariants.

12 · L06

A module of area 18 cm² is dilated by k=1.5. Find the image area. Then find the smallest rotation for a 10-module radial repetition.

13 · L07

A 4.8 m wall intended for 1:40 prints at 11.5 cm. Find the intended print length, estimate the actual scale denominator, and diagnose the output.

14 · L07

For a final design submission, specify which artifacts you would preserve for (a) classroom presentation and (b) continued CAD editing/exchange. Defend each choice.

Mastery expectation

Core work should demonstrate independent setup, quantitative reasoning, spatial checking, and defensible communication. Target remains 80% plus corrections.
