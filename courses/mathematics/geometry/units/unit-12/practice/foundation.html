Unit 12 · Foundation Pathway

CAD, Scale Drawing & Spatial Design

Supported practice across all seven lessons. Use the hints, show units, and explain one mathematical decision in each problem.

Pathway purpose

This is a temporary support pathway, not a lower course. Problems emphasize recognition, direct calculation, labeled steps, and concrete CAD decisions before moving into less-scaffolded Core work.

1 · L01

A drawing uses centimeters and a 0.5 cm snap. Which point is valid: (3.0,4.5) or (3.2,4.5)? Explain using the snap increment.

2 · L01

Before drafting a 12 m × 8 m site, name four setup checks involving units, workspace/boundary, grid, and snap.

3 · L02

A target length is 50.0 mm with tolerance ±0.5 mm. Write the acceptable interval.

4 · L02

A 100 mm segment is measured as 99 mm. Find the absolute error. Is reporting 99.000000 mm justified by the measurement given?

5 · L03

A plan has walls, dimensions, furniture, and notes. Propose four layer names and state which layer you would lock while moving furniture.

6 · L03

Explain one difference between a layer and a group.

7 · L04

At 1:50, a 4.0 m wall becomes how many centimeters on the plan? Hint: convert 4.0 m to 400 cm first.

8 · L04

A 1.8 cm doorway on a 1:50 plan represents what actual width? Why should door swing also be checked?

9 · L05

A 6 m × 4 m floor needs tile. Find its area. Then find the order quantity with 10% waste.

10 · L05

The same room needs trim except across one 0.9 m doorway. Find the trim length.

11 · L06

Translate (2,3) by vector ⟨4,−1⟩. Give the image point.

12 · L06

A radial pattern has 8 equal modules. Find the smallest positive rotation that maps it onto itself.

13 · L07

Choose PDF or editable CAD source for a classroom handout. Explain your choice in one sentence.

14 · L07

A 5 m wall should print at 1:50. How long should it measure on paper in centimeters?
