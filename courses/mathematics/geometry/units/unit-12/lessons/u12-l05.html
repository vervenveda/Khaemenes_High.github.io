Worked reasoning

Process: Set units and scale, construct with grid and snap, dimension critical objects, verify area and length, then export a print-ready artifact with a design note.

  1. Read the figure and rewrite all given information with correct notation.
  2. Identify the exact relationship that connects the known and unknown quantities.
  3. Substitute, construct, or prove one justified step at a time.
  4. State the result with units and a sentence explaining what it means.

Misconception check: A visually attractive drawing is not automatically precise; dimensions, scale, units, and constraints must be explicitly verified.
