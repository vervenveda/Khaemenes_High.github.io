Applied investigation · Unit 09

Nearest-Site Access & Analytic Loci Design

Design a service, safety, ecology, or access map whose boundaries and special locations can be justified by distance loci and coordinate geometry.

Scientific CalculatorEvidence & Citation Studio

Design brief

  1. Choose a real or invented region and place at least three fixed sites with coordinates.
  2. State the design question, audience, units, assumptions, and success criteria.
  3. Construct at least two perpendicular-bisector or other locus boundaries and explain the equidistance condition behind each.
  4. Use at least one point-to-line distance, intersection/system, circle-line, or parabola relationship from L03–L06.
  5. Build or analyze nearest-site regions and identify at least one boundary or vertex whose meaning is explained.
  6. Verify one calculation by a second method. Exact work comes before calculator approximation.
  7. Identify at least one limitation of the model—road network, terrain, traffic, barriers, jurisdiction, staffing, measurement error, or another relevant factor.
  8. Revise one design decision after reviewing the evidence.

Evidence requirements

  • A labeled coordinate diagram or construction.
  • At least four mathematical relationships drawn from at least four different Unit-09 lessons.
  • Visible equations, substitutions, or construction reasoning.
  • A short written explanation distinguishing a locus boundary from a region.
  • A verification table or annotated calculation.
  • If outside factual claims are used, cite them with the Evidence & Citation Studio or equivalent citation record.
