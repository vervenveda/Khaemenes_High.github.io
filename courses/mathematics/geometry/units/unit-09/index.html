Unit 09 · Weeks 25, 26, 27

Analytic Geometry, Loci & Conic Connections

How can distance conditions and coordinate systems describe loci, intersections, boundaries, conics, and nearest-site regions?

Begin UnitCore PracticeMastery CheckApplied Investigation

Lesson sequence

7 visual, printable lessons

Lesson 01

Locus as a Set of Points Meeting a Condition

Describe complete point sets defined by fixed distance, equidistance, boundaries, and regions.

Lesson 02

Perpendicular and Angle Bisector Loci

Use equidistance theorems and converses to construct and justify perpendicular- and angle-bisector loci.

Lesson 03

Distance from a Point to a Line

Measure shortest distance along a perpendicular and use the coordinate point-to-line distance formula.

Lesson 04

Coordinate Intersections and Geometric Systems

Interpret common solutions as points satisfying multiple geometric conditions simultaneously.

Lesson 05

Circle-Line Intersections and Tangency

Predict and solve secant, tangent, and external circle-line relationships using distance and systems.

Lesson 06

Parabolas as Distance Loci

Construct and analyze a parabola as points equidistant from a focus and directrix.

Lesson 07

Voronoi Thinking, Nearest-Point Regions & Design

Partition a region by nearest-site distance and explain active boundaries as perpendicular-bisector loci.
