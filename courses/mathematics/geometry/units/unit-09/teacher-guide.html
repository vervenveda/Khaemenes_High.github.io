Teacher guide · Unit 09

Analytic Geometry, Loci & Conic Connections

Seven-lesson progression from defining a locus through nearest-site spatial design.

Suggested sequence

  1. L01: locus, complete solution sets, boundaries, and regions.
  2. L02: perpendicular- and angle-bisector loci through equidistance and converse reasoning.
  3. L03: perpendicular distance from a point to a line, including coordinate formula use.
  4. L04: geometric systems and coordinate intersections as simultaneous conditions.
  5. L05: circle-line systems, secants, tangency, and center-to-line distance.
  6. L06: parabola as focus-directrix equidistance locus.
  7. L07: Voronoi/nearest-site regions as a synthesis of loci, bisectors, and distance.

Instructional priorities

  • Ask learners to state the condition before naming the locus.
  • Keep geometric meaning visible beneath coordinate formulas and algebraic systems.
  • Require verification: candidate points must satisfy every governing condition.
  • Use exact values first; calculators verify arithmetic or useful decimal interpretations afterward.
  • Use Foundation/Core/Extended as temporary pathways, not permanent learner labels.
  • Keep teacher keys separate from student-facing print materials.

Assessment and mastery

The Unit 09 mastery check draws 12 questions from a dedicated 28-question bank. Every attempt includes at least one question from each of L01–L07, then additional mixed questions. Mastery target: 80%. Require written corrections for missed concepts before advancing when appropriate.

Use lesson artifacts, differentiated practice, oral explanation, mastery history, and the applied investigation together; do not treat one score as the entire evidence record.

Applied investigation

The nearest-site/access design should demonstrate at least three Unit 09 relationships, include a reproducible coordinate model, verify one result by a second method, and state assumptions and real-world limitations. The Scientific Calculator and Evidence & Citation Studio are verification/documentation tools, not substitutes for the student's mathematics.
