Inscribed Angles & Intercepted Arcs

Use intercepted arcs to determine inscribed angles and angles formed by chords.

Open-AgePrintableFoundation · Core · ExtendedMastery target 80%

Learning intention

Use accurate diagrams, definitions, measurements, and reasoning to use intercepted arcs to determine inscribed angles and angles formed by chords.

Success evidence

  • Label the figure and units before calculating.
  • Select and justify the governing definition, theorem, relationship, or construction.
  • Show enough work that another learner can audit the conclusion.
  • Check the result against the diagram, scale, and stated constraints.

Visual model

Connect radii, chords, arcs, angles, tangents, secants, sectors, and coordinate equations in a unified circle model.

C=2\pi rA=\pi r^2s=\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}2\pi r(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2

Worked reasoning

Process: Identify the angle or segment type, locate its intercepted arc or related products, apply the correct theorem, and confirm the result fits the diagram.

  1. Read the figure and rewrite all given information with correct notation.
  2. Identify the exact relationship that connects the known and unknown quantities.
  3. Substitute, construct, or prove one justified step at a time.
  4. State the result with units and a sentence explaining what it means.

Misconception check: An inscribed angle is half its intercepted arc, while a central angle has the same measure as its intercepted arc.
