Unit 08 · Lesson 07 · Circles, Arcs & Tangency

Sector Area, Segment Area & Annuli

Decompose circular regions into sectors, triangles, and concentric-circle differences to calculate exact areas.

Open-AgePrintableFoundation · Core · ExtendedMastery target 80%

Learning intention

Use area decomposition to distinguish and calculate sectors, circular segments, and annuli.

Success evidence

  • Find sector area as a fraction of the area of a full circle.
  • Recognize a circular segment as a sector minus its central triangle.
  • Find annulus area by subtracting the inner-circle area from the outer-circle area.
  • Keep exact π and radical forms until a decimal is genuinely useful.

Three circular regions

sector = (θ/360°)πr²segment = sector − triangleannulus = π(R²−r²)

Worked reasoning

Example: A circle has radius 12 cm and central angle 60°. Find the minor segment area.

  1. Sector area = (60/360)π(12²)=24π cm².
  2. The two radii and included 60° angle form an equilateral triangle with side 12.
  3. Triangle area = (√3/4)(12²)=36√3 cm².
  4. Segment area = 24π−36√3 cm².

Misconception check: A circular segment is not an arc length. It is a two-dimensional region bounded by a chord and its arc.

Vocabulary

sector — region bounded by two radii and an arc.
circular segment — region bounded by a chord and its arc.
annulus — region between concentric circles.
concentric — sharing the same center.

Learning pathways

Foundation: Shade the target region before choosing an operation.

Core: Calculate sectors, segments, and annuli in exact form.

Extended: Compare composite circular regions and defend design choices.
