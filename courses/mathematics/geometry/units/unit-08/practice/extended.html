Unit 08

Extended pathway

Circles, Arcs & Tangency

Reverse reasoning, proof, error analysis, and modeling across the full unit.

1

Explain why every diameter is a chord but not every chord is a diameter. Give a counterexample description.

2

An arc in a circle of radius 15 has length 5π. Determine the central angle and justify the proportional equation.

3

Two inscribed angles intercept the same arc. Prove or explain why they are congruent. Then describe the special case when that arc is a semicircle.

4

A circle has radius 17. One chord is 16 units long. Another chord is 30 units long. Which chord is closer to the center? Compute both center distances.

5

From external point P, tangent PT=20 and OP=29. Find the radius and explain why the tangent theorem creates a right triangle.

6

A student writes external×internal for a secant-secant problem. Explain the error and state the correct product structure. Create a numerical example that verifies your explanation.

7

A 120° sector has radius 9. A concentric circular hole of radius 3 is removed from that sector. Find the exact remaining area and explain the decomposition.

8

Diameter endpoints are A(−4,8) and B(10,−2). Find the circle equation. Then classify point (3,8) as inside, on, or outside using squared distance rather than a decimal radius.
