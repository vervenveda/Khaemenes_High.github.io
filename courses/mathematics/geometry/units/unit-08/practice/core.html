Unit 08

Core pathway

Circles, Arcs & Tangency

Balanced practice across all eight lesson identities. Show theorem/formula, exact work, and units.

1

A diameter is 18 cm. Find the radius. Is every diameter a chord? Explain.

2

A circle has radius 12 cm and central angle 150°. Find the exact intercepted arc length.

3

An inscribed angle intercepts a 132° arc. Find the angle. Then state what happens if an inscribed angle subtends a diameter.

4

A circle has radius 13 and a chord length 10. Find the perpendicular distance from the center to the chord.

5

From external point P, tangents PA and PB are drawn. If PA=4x+1 and PB=6x−9, find x and the tangent length.

6

Two chords intersect inside a circle. Their segment lengths are 3 and 8 on one chord, and 4 and x on the other. Find x.

7

Find the exact area of an annulus with outer radius 11 and inner radius 7.

8

Write the equation of a circle centered at (−2,5) with radius 7. Determine whether (5,5) lies on the circle.
