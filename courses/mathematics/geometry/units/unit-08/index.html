Unit 08 · Weeks 22, 23, 24

Circles, Arcs & Tangency

How do radius, chord, arc, angle, tangent, secant, area, and coordinate relationships describe and constrain circles?

Begin UnitCore PracticeMastery CheckApplied Investigation

Lesson sequence

8 visual, printable lessons

Lesson 01

Circle Vocabulary, Radius, Diameter & Chords

Build the circle language system: center, radius, diameter, chord, arc, and the relationships among them.

Lesson 02

Central Angles, Arcs & Arc Length

Connect central-angle measure, intercepted-arc measure, circumference, and exact arc length.

Lesson 03

Inscribed Angles & Intercepted Arcs

Use the one-half intercepted-arc theorem, diameter subtension, and cyclic-quadrilateral consequences.

Lesson 04

Chords, Perpendicular Bisectors & Distances

Use perpendicular center-to-chord segments, chord bisection, and right triangles to solve length and distance problems.

Lesson 05

Tangents and Radius Relationships

Use radius–tangent perpendicularity and equal tangent segments from an external point.

Lesson 06

Secant, Chord & Tangent Power Theorems

Apply chord-chord, secant-secant, and tangent-secant product relationships with correct inside/outside structure.

Lesson 07

Sector Area, Segment Area & Annuli

Decompose circular regions into sectors, central triangles, and concentric-circle differences to calculate exact area.

Lesson 08

Equation of a Circle & Coordinate Applications

Read and build standard-form circle equations from centers, radii, points, diameter endpoints, and distance evidence.
