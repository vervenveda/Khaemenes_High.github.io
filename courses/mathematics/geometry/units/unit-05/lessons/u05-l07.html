Unit 05 · Lesson 07

Indirect Measurement, Shadows & Mirrors

Use similar triangles to measure heights and distances that are difficult or unsafe to measure directly.

Mastery target 80%Printable

Learning intention

Build and justify similar-triangle models for shadow, mirror, and line-of-sight situations, then solve for inaccessible lengths.

Success evidence

  • Identify the two similar triangles and the AA evidence.
  • Match vertical and horizontal quantities correctly.
  • Use consistent units.
  • Interpret the calculated length in context and discuss measurement error.

Worked reasoning

A 1.5 m meter stick casts a 2 m shadow while a tree casts a 12 m shadow. Under the same sunlight, the triangles are similar, so 1.5/2=h/12 and h=9 m.

Field-study rule: Shadow measurements must be taken at the same time so the sun angle is effectively the same.
