Unit 05 · Lesson 02 · Similarity, Dilations & Proportional Reasoning

Similarity Through Transformations

Explain similarity as a sequence of rigid motions and one dilation, and use that sequence to identify corresponding parts.

Open-AgePrintableFoundation · Core · ExtendedMastery target 80%

Learning intention

Determine whether two figures are similar by describing a valid transformation sequence consisting of translations, rotations, reflections, and a dilation.

Success evidence

  • Distinguish rigid motions from dilations.
  • Identify a scale factor from corresponding lengths.
  • Track corresponding vertices in the correct order.
  • Explain why angle measures are preserved while lengths scale.

Worked reasoning

Triangle ABC has A(0,0), B(2,0), C(0,3). Triangle DEF has D(4,1), E(8,1), F(4,7). Translate ABC by ⟨4,1⟩ and dilate from D by scale factor 2. The translated image has vertices D,(6,1),(4,4); the dilation sends these to D,E,F. Therefore ΔABC∼ΔDEF.

Key idea: Rigid motions preserve size and shape; dilation preserves shape and multiplies all lengths by one common factor.
