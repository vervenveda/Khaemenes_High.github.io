Dilations from a Center

Construct and interpret dilations from a center using positive, fractional, and negative scale factors.

Mastery target 80%Printable

Learning intention

Use a center of dilation and scale factor to locate image points, distinguish enlargement from reduction, and interpret negative scale factors.

Success evidence

  • Apply P'=C+k(P-C) on the coordinate plane.
  • Recognize k>1 as enlargement and 0<k<1 as reduction.
  • Explain why k=1 preserves the figure.
  • Interpret k<0 as placing the image on the opposite ray from the center.

Worked reasoning

Center C=(1,1), point P=(5,3), scale factor k=1/2. Then P'=C+1/2(P-C)=(1,1)+1/2(4,2)=(3,2).

Check: CP'=1/2·CP and C, P, P' are collinear.
