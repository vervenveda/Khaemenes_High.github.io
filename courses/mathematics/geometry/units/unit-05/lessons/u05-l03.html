AA Similarity and Triangle Proportions

Prove triangles similar using two angle pairs, then use the similarity statement to match and solve corresponding sides.

Mastery target 80%Printable

Learning intention

Use AA to establish triangle similarity and write proportions in an order consistent with the similarity statement.

Success evidence

  • Identify two corresponding congruent angle pairs.
  • Write vertices in matching order.
  • Match corresponding sides correctly.
  • Solve and check a proportion.

Worked reasoning

If angle A corresponds to angle D and angle B corresponds to angle E, then triangle ABC is similar to triangle DEF by AA. Therefore AB corresponds to DE, BC to EF, and AC to DF. If AB=6, DE=9, and BC=8, then 6/9=8/EF, so EF=12.

Remember: AA proves similarity, not congruence.
