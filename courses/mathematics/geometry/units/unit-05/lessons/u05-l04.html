Unit 05 · Lesson 04

SSS and SAS Similarity

Decide when proportional sides establish triangle similarity, and distinguish SSS similarity from SAS similarity.

Mastery target 80%Printable

Learning intention

Use three proportional side pairs for SSS similarity or two proportional side pairs with a congruent included angle for SAS similarity.

Success evidence

  • Compare corresponding side ratios in a consistent order.
  • Identify the included angle in SAS similarity.
  • Reject evidence that is insufficient.
  • Write a correct similarity statement.

Worked reasoning

Triangle ABC has sides 6, 8, 10 and triangle DEF has sides 9, 12, 15. Because 6/9=8/12=10/15=2/3, the triangles are similar by SSS. For SAS, two corresponding side ratios must match and the angle between those side pairs must be congruent.

Misconception check: Two proportional sides alone do not prove similarity without the included-angle condition.
