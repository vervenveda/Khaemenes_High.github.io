Unit 05 · Lesson 06

Scale Drawings, Maps & Models

Convert accurately between drawing dimensions and real dimensions while preserving units and scale.

Mastery target 80%Printable

Learning intention

Interpret numerical and verbal scales, convert units before comparing lengths, and build or audit a scale model.

Success evidence

  • Write the scale with compatible units.
  • Use one consistent drawing-to-actual ratio.
  • Convert units before multiplying or dividing.
  • Check whether the result is reasonable for an enlargement or reduction.

Worked reasoning

A plan uses 1 cm : 2.5 m. A wall measures 7.2 cm on the plan. Actual length =7.2×2.5=18 m.

Unit check: A scale such as 1:50 is unitless only when both measurements use the same unit.
