Side-Splitter and Proportional Segments

Use a segment parallel to one side of a triangle to establish proportional side divisions, and use the converse to test parallelism.

Mastery target 80%Printable

Learning intention

Apply the Triangle Proportionality (Side-Splitter) Theorem and its converse to solve segment relationships and justify parallel lines.

Success evidence

  • Identify the parallel segment that creates proportional side divisions.
  • Write matching ratios from the same two sides.
  • Solve for an unknown segment.
  • Use proportional division as evidence for the converse.

Worked reasoning

In triangle ABC, D lies on AB and E lies on AC with DE parallel to BC. If AD=4, DB=6, and AE=10, then AD/DB=AE/EC gives 4/6=10/EC, so EC=15.

Misconception check: Keep whole-side ratios and split-segment ratios consistent; do not mix unmatched parts.
