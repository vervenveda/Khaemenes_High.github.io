Lesson sequence

6 visual, printable lessons

Lesson 01

Points, Lines, Planes & Defined Terms

Use definitions, measurements, constructions, and logical statements to establish a precise geometric argument.

Lesson 02

Segments, Rays, Angles & Notation

Use definitions, measurements, constructions, and logical statements to establish a precise geometric argument.

Lesson 03

Distance, Midpoint & Segment Partition

Partition a segment numerically and on the coordinate plane; distinguish distance from directed change.

Lesson 04

Compass-and-Straightedge Constructions

Construct congruent segments and angles, perpendicular bisectors, angle bisectors, and perpendicular or parallel lines using classical tools.

Lesson 05

Conditional Statements, Converses & Counterexamples

Write conditional, converse, inverse, and contrapositive statements; evaluate truth and produce counterexamples.

Lesson 06

Two-Column, Flow & Paragraph Proofs

Build a proof from givens through definitions, postulates, and theorems while comparing proof formats.
