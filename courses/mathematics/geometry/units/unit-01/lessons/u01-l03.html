Distance, Midpoint & Segment Partition

Measure distance, locate midpoints, and divide segments in specified ratios on number lines and the coordinate plane.

Foundation · Core · ExtendedMastery target 80%20 problemsPrintable

Learning intention

I can calculate distance and midpoint and locate points that partition a segment in a given ratio.

Success evidence

  • Use absolute difference for one-dimensional distance.
  • Use the distance formula for coordinate-plane segments.
  • Average corresponding coordinates to find a midpoint.
  • Interpret ratios as fractional positions along a segment.

Visual model

If AP:PB = 2:3, the whole segment contains 5 equal ratio-parts. P is therefore 2/5 of the way from A to B.

number line: |b−a|M=((x₁+x₂)/2,(y₁+y₂)/2)d=√((x₂−x₁)²+(y₂−y₁)²)

Worked reasoning

Question: Find the point P that divides A(2,1) to B(12,6) in the ratio AP:PB = 2:3.

  1. The ratio has 2 + 3 = 5 total parts.
  2. P is 2/5 of the way from A toward B.
  3. The change A→B is (10,5). Two-fifths of that change is (4,2).
  4. Add to A: P = (2+4,1+2) = (6,3).

Misconception check: Distance is never negative. A directed change such as x₂−x₁ may be negative, but geometric distance represents magnitude.

Extended: Derive a general coordinate rule for locating a point that is a fraction t of the way from A to B.
