Unit 01 · Lesson 06 · Foundations, Constructions & Geometric Reasoning

Two-Column, Flow & Paragraph Proofs

Build valid geometric arguments from givens to conclusions and express the same reasoning in multiple proof formats.

Foundation · Core · ExtendedMastery target 80%20 proof tasksPrintable

Learning intention

I can organize a proof so every statement follows from a given, definition, postulate, theorem, or previously justified result.

Success evidence

  • Separate statements from reasons in a two-column proof.
  • Sequence dependent claims correctly in a flow proof.
  • Write a paragraph proof that preserves every justification.
  • Detect circular reasoning, unsupported diagram assumptions, and missing reasons.

Proof architecture

Given → justified step → justified step → conclusionstatement | reasondiagram ≠ proof

A proof is an audit trail. Every new statement must have a reason that was already established or accepted.

Worked two-column proof

Given: M is the midpoint of segment AB. Prove: AM = MB.

StatementReason
M is the midpoint of AB.Given
AM ≅ MB.Definition of midpoint
AM = MB.Definition of congruent segments

Misconception check: “It looks equal in the diagram” is not a valid reason. Use only givens, markings, definitions, postulates, theorems, or earlier proved statements.
