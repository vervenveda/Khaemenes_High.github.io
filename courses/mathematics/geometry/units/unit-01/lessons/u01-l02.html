Unit 01 · Lesson 02 · Foundations, Constructions & Geometric Reasoning

Segments, Rays, Angles & Notation

Read and write geometric notation, measure segments and angles, and distinguish an object from its numerical measure.

Foundation · Core · ExtendedMastery target 80%20 problemsPrintable

Learning intention

I can use correct notation for segments, rays, lines, and angles and calculate segment lengths and angle measures from given information.

Success evidence

  • Distinguish segment AB from its length AB.
  • Name rays in endpoint-first order and angles with the vertex in the middle.
  • Apply segment addition and angle addition.
  • Classify angles as acute, right, obtuse, or straight from their measures.

Visual model

Because B lies between A and C, AB + BC = AC. The angle formed by rays BA and BD may be named ∠ABD or ∠DBA because B is the vertex and must appear in the middle.

Worked reasoning

Given: M is between P and Q. PM = 3x + 2, MQ = 11, and PQ = 31. Find x.

  1. Use segment addition: PM + MQ = PQ.
  2. Substitute: (3x + 2) + 11 = 31.
  3. Solve: 3x + 13 = 31, so 3x = 18 and x = 6.
  4. Check: PM = 20; 20 + 11 = 31.

Misconception check: The notation for a geometric object and the notation for its measure are not interchangeable. A segment is an object; its length is a number.
