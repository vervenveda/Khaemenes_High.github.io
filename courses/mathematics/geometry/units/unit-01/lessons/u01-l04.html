Compass-and-Straightedge Constructions

Construct congruent segments and angles, perpendicular bisectors, angle bisectors, and perpendicular or parallel lines using classical tools.

Open-AgePrintableFoundation · Core · ExtendedMastery target 80%

Learning intention

Use accurate diagrams, definitions, measurements, and reasoning to construct congruent segments and angles, perpendicular bisectors, angle bisectors, and perpendicular or parallel lines using classical tools.

Success evidence

  • Label the figure and units before calculating.
  • Select and justify the governing definition, theorem, relationship, or construction.
  • Show enough work that another learner can audit the conclusion.
  • Check the result against the diagram, scale, and stated constraints.

Visual model

Use definitions, measurements, constructions, and logical statements to establish a precise geometric argument.

Worked reasoning

Process: State what is given, translate each fact into a definition or theorem, and justify every conclusion in order.

  1. Read the figure and rewrite all given information with correct notation.
  2. Identify the exact relationship that connects the known and unknown quantities.
  3. Substitute, construct, or prove one justified step at a time.
  4. State the result with units and a sentence explaining what it means.

Misconception check: A diagram suggests relationships, but only stated markings, definitions, and proven results may be used as evidence.
