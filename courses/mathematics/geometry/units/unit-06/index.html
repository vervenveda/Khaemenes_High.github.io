Unit 06 · Weeks 14–17

Right Triangles, Trigonometry & Bearings

How do right-triangle relationships turn inaccessible lengths, heights, angles, and directions into measurable quantities?

Begin UnitCore PracticeMastery CheckApplied Investigation

Lesson sequence

8 distinct competencies

Lesson 01

Pythagorean Theorem & Its Converse

Solve right-triangle sides and use the converse to classify triangles from side lengths.

Lesson 02

Special Right Triangles: 45-45-90

Use 1:1:√2 in both directions while preserving exact radical form.

Lesson 03

Special Right Triangles: 30-60-90

Use 1:√3:2 and match short leg, long leg, and hypotenuse to their opposite angles.

Lesson 04

Geometric Mean & Right-Triangle Altitudes

Use altitude-to-hypotenuse similarity to justify h²=pq and leg-projection relationships.

Lesson 05

Sine, Cosine & Tangent Ratios

Identify side roles relative to a reference angle and select the ratio containing the known and unknown sides.

Lesson 06

Inverse Trigonometry & Angle Measure

Recover acute angles from side ratios with correct inverse-function, degree-mode, and rounding choices.

Lesson 07

Angles of Elevation and Depression

Build line-of-sight models, transfer horizontal angles, and account for observer height and measurement error.

Lesson 08

Bearings, Vectors & Multi-Step Navigation

Interpret quadrant bearings, resolve vector components, combine travel legs, and recover resultant displacement.
