Special Right Triangles: 30-60-90

Use the exact 1:√3:2 side ratio and identify each side by the angle opposite it.

Exact valuesPrintableMastery target 80%

Learning intention

Connect angle size to opposite side length: the short leg x lies opposite 30°, the long leg x√3 lies opposite 60°, and the hypotenuse 2x lies opposite 90°.

Success evidence

  • Identify short leg, long leg, and hypotenuse from angle labels.
  • Use x:x√3:2x in either direction.
  • Preserve exact radicals when possible.
  • Explain why the hypotenuse is twice the short leg.

Visual model

x:x√3:2xlong=x√3hypotenuse=2x

Worked reasoning

Example: A 30-60-90 triangle has hypotenuse 18. Find both legs.

  1. Since 2x=18, x=9.
  2. The short leg is 9.
  3. The long leg is 9√3.
  4. Match each value to the angle opposite it.

Misconception check: The √3 factor belongs to the long leg, not automatically to the vertical or horizontal side.
