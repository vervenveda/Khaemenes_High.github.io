Area, Measurement & Geometric Probability

How do decomposition, scale, precision, and probability support responsible measurement and optimization?

Lesson sequence

7 visual, printable lessons

Lesson 01

Area of Triangles, Parallelograms & Trapezoids

Decompose figures, manage units and precision, and use area ratios to model probability and optimize design constraints.

Lesson 02

Area of Regular Polygons and Composite Figures

Use apothem, perimeter, central angle, and decomposition into congruent triangles.

Lesson 03

Heron’s Formula and Coordinate Area

Find triangle area from three side lengths and compare with coordinate or base-height methods.

Lesson 04

Effects of Scale on Perimeter and Area

Distinguish linear, area, and volume scale factors.

Lesson 05

Units, Precision, Error & Significant Figures

Report measurement, absolute error, relative error, and appropriate significant figures.

Lesson 06

Geometric Probability by Length and Area

Use length or area ratios under a uniform-selection assumption.

Lesson 07

Optimization with Perimeter and Area Constraints

Compare feasible designs under fixed perimeter or area constraints.
