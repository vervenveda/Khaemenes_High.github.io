Area, Measurement & Geometric Probability

Build area methods, reason responsibly about scale and measurement, model probability geometrically, and optimize only among feasible designs.

Lesson sequence

Seven distinct mathematical investigations

L01

Triangles, Parallelograms & Trapezoids

Derive and apply foundational area formulas using perpendicular height and decomposition.

L02

Regular Polygons & Composite Figures

Use apothem and perimeter and decompose composite regions strategically.

L03

Heron’s Formula & Coordinate Area

Choose and compare methods when height is not directly supplied.

L04

Effects of Scale

Connect linear scale k to perimeter factor k and area factor k².

L05

Precision, Error & Significant Figures

Interpret measurement resolution and report only defensible precision.

L06

Geometric Probability

Use compatible length or area ratios under a uniform-selection model.

L07

Optimization Under Constraints

Separate objective from constraints and compare only feasible designs.
