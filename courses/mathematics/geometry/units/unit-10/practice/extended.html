Unit 10 · Extended Pathway

Area, Measurement & Geometric Probability

Fourteen proof, modeling, audit, and generalization problems. Each lesson identity appears twice.

1

L01 · Derive A=½(b₁+b₂)h for a trapezoid by rearranging two congruent copies into a parallelogram.

2

L01 · A triangle has fixed base b. Prove algebraically and explain geometrically why doubling its perpendicular height doubles area.

3

L02 · Derive A=½aP for a regular n-gon by decomposing it into congruent triangles, explicitly using P=ns.

4

L02 · Create two different valid decompositions of the same composite figure and explain why both area ledgers must agree without double-counting.

5

L03 · For A(0,0), B(6,0), C(2,4), verify area 12 using both base-height and the coordinate-area formula. Explain why agreement is meaningful.

6

L03 · A student chooses Heron's formula for a coordinate triangle with an obvious horizontal base. Audit the method choice: when is Heron valid but inefficient?

7

L04 · Generalize why a k-fold dilation multiplies every polygon area by k² using decomposition into triangles or rectangles.

8

L04 · A student claims a 30% increase in every linear dimension produces a 30% area increase. Quantify the error and explain the multiplicative model.

9

L05 · A rectangle is reported as 6.4 cm by 3.2 cm to the nearest tenth. Determine measurement intervals and discuss bounds on the true area instead of trusting 20.48 cm² as exact.

10

L05 · Compare “18.42 m” and “18 m.” Construct a scenario where the second is more accurate despite lower apparent precision.

11

L06 · Derive the probability of landing in an annular region between radii r and R under uniform area selection. State all assumptions.

12

L06 · Design a counterexample showing why favorable area/total area fails when landing density is nonuniform.

13

L07 · For rectangles with fixed perimeter P, use W=P/2−L to explain why the product LW is maximized at balanced dimensions. A table, symmetry argument, or algebraic completion is acceptable.

14

L07 · A CAD design maximizes usable area but violates a minimum-access width. Write a short design audit that separates objective, constraints, feasibility, and revision.
