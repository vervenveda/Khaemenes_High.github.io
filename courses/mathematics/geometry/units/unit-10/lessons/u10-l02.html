Area of Regular Polygons & Composite Figures

Use apothems and decomposition to turn complex regions into familiar area problems.

Regular polygonsComposite areaMastery target 80%

Learning intention

Find areas of regular polygons and composite figures by decomposing them into simpler regions.

Success evidence

  • Identify the apothem as the perpendicular center-to-side distance of a regular polygon.
  • Explain A=½aP by decomposing a regular polygon into congruent triangles.
  • Add non-overlapping component areas to find a composite total.
  • Subtract holes or cutouts from a larger region.
  • Keep a clear area ledger so no region is omitted or counted twice.

Regular polygon area

A=½aPP=nscentral angle=360°/n

Connecting the center to every vertex divides a regular n-gon into n congruent triangles. Each has height a and base s, so the total area is n(½as)=½a(ns)=½aP.

Worked reasoning

Example: A regular hexagon has side length 8 cm and apothem 4√3 cm.

  1. Perimeter P=6(8)=48 cm.
  2. A=½aP=½(4√3)(48).
  3. A=96√3 cm².

Composite strategy: Sketch separators first. Label every component with + for material included and − for a cutout or hole.

Composite figures: build an area ledger

A composite figure has no single magic formula. Decompose it into rectangles, triangles, trapezoids, regular polygons, semicircles, or other known regions. Then write an explicit ledger such as Total = rectangle + triangle − cutout.

Regular Polygons & Composite Figures

U10 · L02
1

A regular pentagon has apothem 6 cm and perimeter 40 cm. Find its area.

2

A regular octagon has side length 5 m and apothem 6.04 m. Find its area to the nearest tenth.

3

Explain why the area formula A=½aP follows from decomposing a regular polygon into congruent triangles.

4

An L-shaped floor can be viewed as a 12 m by 10 m rectangle with a 4 m by 3 m corner removed. Find the floor area and write the area ledger.

5

A sign consists of a 10 ft by 6 ft rectangle topped by a triangle with base 10 ft and height 4 ft. Find the total area.

6

A regular hexagonal patio has apothem 5√3 ft and side length 10 ft. A rectangular planter measuring 8 ft by 5 ft occupies part of the patio. Find the remaining usable area in exact form.

Vocabulary

regular polygon — equilateral and equiangular polygon.
apothem — perpendicular distance from center to a side.
composite figure — region made from multiple simpler regions.
cutout — excluded region subtracted from a total.

Audit habit

Before calculating, point to every part of the figure and identify exactly once where its area appears in your ledger.
