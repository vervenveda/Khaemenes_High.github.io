Optimization with Perimeter and Area Constraints

Compare feasible designs under fixed perimeter or area constraints.

Open-AgePrintableFoundation · Core · ExtendedMastery target 80%

Learning intention

Use accurate diagrams, definitions, measurements, and reasoning to compare feasible designs under fixed perimeter or area constraints.

Success evidence

  • Label the figure and units before calculating.
  • Select and justify the governing definition, theorem, relationship, or construction.
  • Show enough work that another learner can audit the conclusion.
  • Check the result against the diagram, scale, and stated constraints.

Visual model

Decompose figures, manage units and precision, and use area ratios to model probability and optimize design constraints.

Worked reasoning

Process: Decompose or select a suitable formula, keep exact values until the final step, attach square units, and assess whether rounding is reasonable.

  1. Read the figure and rewrite all given information with correct notation.
  2. Identify the exact relationship that connects the known and unknown quantities.
  3. Substitute, construct, or prove one justified step at a time.
  4. State the result with units and a sentence explaining what it means.

Misconception check: Area uses square units and scales by the square of a linear scale factor; it does not scale linearly.
