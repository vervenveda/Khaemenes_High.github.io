Instructional sequence

Seven distinct lesson identities

L01

Prisms, Cylinders & Cavalieri

Use base area, perpendicular height, and cross-sectional reasoning to understand volume.

L02

Pyramids & Cones

Develop the one-third volume relationship and distinguish perpendicular from slant height.

L03

Spheres

Connect radius and diameter to exact surface-area and volume relationships.

L04

Nets & Surface Area

Move between three-dimensional solids and two-dimensional face structures.

L05

Cylinders & Cones Surface Area

Derive and apply curved lateral-area relationships using circumference and slant height.

L06

Cross-Sections & Revolution

Slice 3D forms into 2D sections and rotate 2D regions to generate solids.

L07

Similarity, Scale & Volume

Reason forward and backward with length k, area k², and volume k³.
