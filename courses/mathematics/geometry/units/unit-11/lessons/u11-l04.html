Nets & Surface Area of Prisms/Pyramids

Unfold solids into faces, account for every exposed region exactly once, and connect nets to lateral and total surface area.

NetsLateral areaTotal surface areaMastery target 80%

Learning intention

Use two-dimensional nets to reason about the exposed faces of prisms and pyramids and calculate total surface area without omitting or double-counting faces.

Success evidence

  • Identify which polygons make up a solid's net.
  • Distinguish base area from lateral area.
  • Calculate total surface area by summing each exposed face exactly once.
  • Recognize that different valid nets can fold into the same solid.
  • Use slant height—not perpendicular volume height—for the triangular lateral faces of a regular pyramid.

Visual model · rectangular-prism net

SA = sum of exposed facesPrism SA = 2B + PhRegular pyramid SA = B + ½Pℓ

For a right prism, the lateral rectangles together have area Ph, where P is the base perimeter and h is prism height. For a regular pyramid, the triangular lateral faces combine to ½Pℓ, where ℓ is slant height.

Worked net reasoning

Example: A rectangular prism measures 8 cm by 5 cm by 3 cm.

  1. Two 8×5 faces contribute 80 cm².
  2. Two 8×3 faces contribute 48 cm².
  3. Two 5×3 faces contribute 30 cm².
  4. Total surface area = 80+48+30 = 158 cm².

Net audit: six faces must be represented. Shared edges in the folded solid do not remove face area; they simply show where faces meet.

Pyramid connection

A square pyramid with base side s and slant height ℓ has one square base plus four congruent triangular faces. Its surface area is s² + 4(½sℓ), equivalent to B + ½Pℓ.

A rectangular prism measures 10 cm by 6 cm by 4 cm. List the six faces by dimensions and find total surface area.

2

A right triangular prism has a 3-4-5 triangular base and prism length 10 cm. Find total surface area.

3

A regular square pyramid has base side 8 m and slant height 5 m. Find lateral area and total surface area.

4

A net contains two congruent hexagons and six congruent rectangles. Identify the solid and explain which faces are bases and which are lateral faces.

5

A student finds the surface area of a rectangular prism by adding only lw+lh+wh. Diagnose the error and write the corrected expression.

6

Two different-looking nets each contain six congruent squares arranged so they fold without overlap into a cube. Must their total surface areas be equal? Explain.

Vocabulary

net — a 2D arrangement of faces that folds into a solid.
lateral face — a non-base face.
lateral area — combined area of lateral faces.
total surface area — area of all exposed faces.

Spatial audit

Before calculating, count faces. After calculating, check that every face was included once—and only once.

CAD connection
