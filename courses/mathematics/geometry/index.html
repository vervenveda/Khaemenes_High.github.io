About Geometry

Geometry develops visual reasoning, formal proof, spatial thinking, and quantitative modelling. Students move between diagrams, constructions, coordinates, algebraic relationships, and written justification.

Course Features

Proof & ReasoningStudents develop claims, justify relationships, analyze counterexamples, and communicate geometric reasoning clearly.
