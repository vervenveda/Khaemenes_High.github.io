Spheres: Surface Area & Volume

Instructional anchor

Keep the two measures distinct: SA=4πr² measures boundary area and V=⁴⁄₃πr³ measures capacity. Require radius identification before substitution and dimensional units in every answer.

Misconception watch

Check diameter/radius conversion, r² versus r³, square versus cubic units, and whether students preserve exact π before approximating.

Mastery decision

Target: 80% plus corrections. A learner should be able to explain why doubling radius produces different scale factors for surface area and volume.
