10

Reasoning: Rigid rotations/reflections of a drawing do not change corresponding side lengths.
11

Reasoning: WZ≅ZW by the reflexive property.
12

Reasoning: Reflection preserves lengths and angle measures; two sides and their included angle still satisfy SAS.

Reasoning: x+2=7 and 2x=10 both give 5; 11=11 supplies the third pair, so SSS is consistent.
15

16
Answer: The shared bar is congruent to itself by the reflexive property. Together with the two corresponding outer-bar pairs, it gives SSS.
17
Answer: ΔBAC≅ΔDAC by SAS.
Reasoning: AB≅AD (given), ∠BAC≅∠CAD (definition of angle bisector), AC≅AC (reflexive).
18

Reasoning: One corresponding pair differs by 0.1 m. The teams may report approximate agreement within an appropriate measurement tolerance, not exact congruence from these numbers alone.
19
