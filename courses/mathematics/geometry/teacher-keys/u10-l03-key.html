Heron's Formula & Coordinate Area · Teacher Key

Instructional focus

Method selection is part of the lesson. Heron's formula is powerful when three sides are known; coordinate structure can make base-height faster. Independent methods can verify one another.

Misconception watch

The semiperimeter is not the perimeter. Also, the absolute value in the coordinate-area formula matters because vertex order can reverse the sign of the determinant without changing geometric area.
