Surface Area of Cylinders & Cones

Instructional anchor

Derive before memorizing: cylinder lateral area is the area of an unwrapped rectangle with dimensions 2πr by h. Cone lateral area is πrℓ, where ℓ is slant height. Add circular base areas only when total surface area requires them.

2π(4)(9)=72π; two bases=2π(16)=32π.

Diameter 10 gives r=5. SA=2π(25)+2π(5)(7)=50π+70π.

Misconception watch

  • Using diameter as radius.
  • Using perpendicular cone height where slant height is required.
  • Adding bases to a lateral-area-only problem.
  • Forgetting one or both circular bases in total cylinder surface area.
  • Reporting cubic units for surface area.

Mastery: 80% plus corrections. Ask learners to explain the unwrapped geometry if formulas are being applied mechanically.
