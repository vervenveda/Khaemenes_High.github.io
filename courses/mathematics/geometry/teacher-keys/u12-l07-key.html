Export, Print/PDF, SVG, DXF & Design Defense

Evaluate output decisions by purpose, verify print scale mathematically, and require evidence-based design claims.

Final CAD evidence

Require an editable/source artifact, a presentation/print output, declared units and scale where applicable, essential dimensions, organized layers, a known verification dimension, and a 60–90 second defense containing at least three mathematical claims.

Unit Complete Challenge guidance

Sanctuary or Mandala Rings may be offered only after required Unit-12 evidence is complete. The celebration is optional and ungraded. Ask for one transformation, symmetry, or measurable geometric relationship in the resulting design; do not turn the celebration into another high-stakes assessment.

Mastery

80% plus corrections. A successful learner can communicate the design, verify output scale, preserve appropriate source/output formats, and defend decisions with mathematical evidence.
