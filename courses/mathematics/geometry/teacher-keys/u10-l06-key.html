Geometric Probability by Length and Area · Teacher Key

Instructional focus

Require students to state or recognize uniform selection. The favorable and total quantities must use the same kind of geometric measure.

Misconception watch

Geometric probability is not “favorable number divided by total number” when outcomes form a continuum. Use the appropriate geometric measure and make the selection assumption explicit.
