Regular Polygons & Composite Figures · Teacher Key

Apothem/perimeter reasoning and explicit add-subtract area ledgers.

Instructional focus

Students should be able to justify A=½aP rather than use it as an unexplained rule. For composite figures, require a decomposition sketch or written area ledger before arithmetic.

Misconception watch

The apothem is not the side length and not the radius to a vertex. It is perpendicular to a side. In composite work, overlapping decompositions can double-count area unless the ledger is explicit.
