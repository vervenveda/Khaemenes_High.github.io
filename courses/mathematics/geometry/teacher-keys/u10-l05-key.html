Units, Precision, Error & Significant Figures · Teacher Key

Instructional focus

Separate calculator output from justified reporting. Precision describes resolution or consistency; accuracy describes closeness to an accepted value. Require units throughout.

1
Answer: 7.55 cm ≤ actual length < 7.65 cm. Values in this interval round to 7.6 cm to the nearest tenth.
2
Answer: Absolute error=0.3 m. Percent error=(0.3/25.0)×100%=1.2%.
3
Answer: 0.00450 has 3 significant figures; 120.05 has 5; 8.00 has 3. Leading zeros in 0.00450 are placeholders; the final zero is significant. The zeros in 120.05 lie between nonzero digits and are significant. Trailing decimal zeros in 8.00 communicate measured precision.
4
Answer: Computed area=20.48 cm². Writing 20.480000 cm² invents unsupported precision because the input dimensions were only reported to the nearest tenth. Accept a sensible rounded report accompanied by a justification tied to measurement resolution.

Misconception watch

“More decimal places” is not synonymous with “more accurate.” Also watch area-unit conversions: the linear conversion factor must be squared.
