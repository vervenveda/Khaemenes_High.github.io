Voronoi Thinking, Nearest-Point Regions & Design · Teacher Key

Core reasoning

Voronoi regions encode nearest-site inequalities. Edges are the relevant portions of pairwise perpendicular bisectors where the two tied sites are not beaten by another site.

Extension

Ask students to move one site and predict which edges/regions change before recomputing. This develops dynamic spatial reasoning rather than treating a Voronoi diagram as a static picture.
