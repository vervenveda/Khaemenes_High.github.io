14
Answer: Vertex-to-centroid must be twice centroid-to-midpoint: 2x+4=2(x+2), which is an identity. Therefore x is not uniquely determined from these expressions; the problem data encode the 2:1 ratio for every admissible x. Full median=3(x+2). This is an intentional underdetermination check.
15
Answer: By the perpendicular-bisector theorem, every point on a segment's perpendicular bisector is equidistant from its endpoints.
16
Answer: By the angle-bisector distance theorem, a point on an angle bisector is equidistant from the two sides when distance is measured perpendicularly.

19
Answer: Circumcenter: perpendicular bisectors, equal distances to vertices, may be inside/on/outside depending on triangle type. Incenter: angle bisectors, equal perpendicular distances to sides, always inside.
20
Answer: In an equilateral triangle, each median is simultaneously an altitude, perpendicular bisector, and angle bisector by symmetry, so all four concurrency constructions meet at the same point.
