Power Theorems
1

Intersecting chords: 4, 9, 6, x.

Answer: x=6.
Reasoning: 4·9=6x, so 36=6x.
2

Secants: 5 external, 20 whole; 8 external, x whole.

Answer: x=12.5.
Reasoning: 5·20=8x, so 100=8x.
3

Tangent 15; secant external 9 and whole x.

Answer: x=25.
Reasoning: 15²=9x, so 225=9x.
4

Intersecting chords: x and 12; 8 and 18.

Answer: x=12.
Reasoning: 12x=8·18=144.
5

Secant external 7, internal 9; find tangent.

Answer: 4√7.
Reasoning: Whole secant=7+9=16. Then t²=7·16=112, so t=√112=4√7.
6

Diagnose 6·10=4·x when externals are 6 and 4 and internals are 10 and 11.

Answer: The setup incorrectly uses external·internal. Whole lengths are 16 and 15, so the correct equation is 6·16=4·15. Because 96≠60, the stated measurements cannot belong to two secants from the same external point; the diagram/data are inconsistent. This is a deliberate error-analysis item.

Teacher prompts

Before students calculate, ask: Is the power point inside or outside? If outside, which number is the external part and which is the whole length? For a tangent, why is the tangent factor repeated?
