Effects of Scale on Perimeter & Area · Teacher Key

Instructional focus

Students must distinguish the linear factor k from the area factor k² and reverse that relationship by taking a positive square root when an area ratio is given.

Misconception watch

Do not add percentages across dimensions. A 20% increase in each linear dimension multiplies area by 1.2×1.2, not by 1.2.
