Unit 10 · Lesson 01

Area of Triangles, Parallelograms & Trapezoids · Teacher Key

Base-height reasoning, decomposition, inverse area problems, and square-unit discipline.

Instructional focus

Require students to identify the corresponding perpendicular height before substituting. Formula recall without recognizing the correct height is not sufficient evidence of mastery.

6
Expected reasoning: A=½bh. With b fixed, replacing h by 2h gives A'=½b(2h)=bh=2(½bh)=2A. Geometrically, doubling the perpendicular height doubles the amount of triangular region for the same base.

Misconception watch

A slanted side is not automatically a height. The height must be perpendicular to the chosen base or to the line containing that base.
