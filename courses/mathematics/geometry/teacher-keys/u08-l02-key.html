Central Angles, Arcs & Arc Length

Teacher reference for central-angle, intercepted-arc, and exact arc-length reasoning.

Exact π formsReasoning requiredMastery target 80%

Instructional focus

Students should connect a central angle to the same-degree intercepted arc, interpret θ/360° as a fraction of the circle, and use that fraction of circumference to determine or reverse-solve arc length.

m∠AOB = m⌢ABs = (θ/360°)(2πr)

Scoring note: Accept equivalent exact forms. Decimal approximations do not replace a requested exact π-form answer.

L02 Answer & Reasoning Key

U08 · L02
1

Central angle ∠AOB measures 72°. What is the degree measure of its minor intercepted arc AB? What fraction of the circle is that arc?

Answer: 72°; 72/360 = 1/5 of the circle.
Reasoning: A central angle and its intercepted arc have equal degree measure.
2

Minor arc CD measures 135°. Find central angle ∠COD and the degree measure of the corresponding major arc CD.

Answer: ∠COD = 135°; major arc = 225°.
Reasoning: 360° − 135° = 225°.
3

A circle has radius 9 cm. Find the exact length of the arc intercepted by a 120° central angle.

Answer: 6π cm.
Reasoning: s=(120/360)(2π·9)=(1/3)(18π)=6π.
4

A circle has radius 15 m and an arc length of 5π m. Find the measure of the central angle that intercepts the arc.

Answer: 60°.
Reasoning: 5π=(θ/360)(30π), so 5=θ/12 and θ=60.
5

Circle P has radius 6 and a 150° central angle. Circle Q has radius 10 and a 90° central angle. Which intercepted arc is longer?

Answer: They are equal; each arc is 5π units long.
Reasoning: P: (150/360)(12π)=5π. Q: (90/360)(20π)=5π.
6

A circular walking path has radius 24 m. A marked arc along the path is 16π m long. What central angle subtends the marked arc?

Answer: 120°.
Reasoning: 16π=(θ/360)(48π). Thus 16=2θ/15, giving θ=120°. The arc is one third of the 48π circumference, so one third of 360° is reasonable.

Calculator policy

Students may use the Khaemenes Scientific Calculator to verify decimal approximations after producing the exact geometric relationship. The calculator should not substitute for identifying θ/360°, circumference, or the governing arc-length relationship.
