Unit 13 · Lesson 04 · Teacher Key

Prepare a Printable Technical Portfolio

Evaluate traceability and mathematical completeness before presentation quality.

Math Word: Traceability

Expected retrieval: A conclusion is traceable when a reader can follow it backward through visible reasoning/evidence to measurements, givens, assumptions, or sources.

1
Expected: A concise statement preserving the approved L01 question/purpose and measurable constraints. Do not allow portfolio editing to silently change the project question.
2
Expected: A complete chain such as conclusion → theorem/calculation → dimensions/data → measurement/source/assumption. Missing links should be repaired before L05.
3
Expected: Every included visual has a mathematical purpose. Decorative visuals are acceptable but do not count as evidence. Figures should be labeled/captioned and referenced from the reasoning.
4
Expected: Low/base/high or equivalent sensitivity evidence, robustness/fragility conclusion, limitation, and revision or justified no-revision decision from L03.
5
Expected: At least four checks such as consistent units, symbols defined, dimensions labeled, precision appropriate, variables used consistently, equations readable, figure references correct. Require actual corrections when failures are found.
6
Expected: At least four print/PDF checks: readable text/math at normal viewing size, no clipping, figures/captions together and visible, adequate contrast, sensible page breaks, all referenced evidence present. Require correction of failures before presentation.

Portfolio minimum evidence

Before L05, verify the portfolio contains the L01 brief/constraints, L02 model and validation, L03 sensitivity/limitations/revision, a conclusion answering the original question, labeled mathematical visuals, consistent units/notation, and a successful print/PDF audit.

Instructional checkpoint

Do not reward graphic polish for concealing a broken evidence chain. Conversely, do not require unnecessary decoration from a mathematically complete portfolio. The standard is professional clarity and auditability.

Mastery

80% plus corrections. Advancement to L05 requires a technically readable portfolio draft whose major conclusions are traceable and whose print/PDF output has been checked.
