Worked reasoning

Process: Establish similarity first, write a proportion with matching sides, solve, and check that the magnitude agrees with the scale factor.

  1. Read the figure and rewrite all given information with correct notation.
  2. Identify the exact relationship that connects the known and unknown quantities.
  3. Substitute, construct, or prove one justified step at a time.
  4. State the result with units and a sentence explaining what it means.

Misconception check: Corresponding side ratios must be written in a consistent order; mixing image-to-preimage and preimage-to-image reverses the scale factor.

1

A dilation has scale factor 2. A preimage side is 6. Find the image side.

Answer: 12
Reasoning: Multiply by the scale factor: 6·2.
2

Triangles are similar. A side 5 corresponds to 8; a side 6 corresponds to x. Find x.

Answer: 9.6
Reasoning: Use 5/8=6/x.
3

Which angle evidence alone is sufficient for triangle similarity?

Answer: Two pairs of congruent angles (AA)
Reasoning: The third angle is then congruent automatically.
4

How does a dilation with scale factor 1/2 affect angle measure?

Answer: It preserves angle measure
Reasoning: Dilations change lengths proportionally but preserve angles.
5

A 1:50 drawing shows a wall 8 cm long. What is the actual length?

Answer: 400 cm or 4 m
Reasoning: Multiply the drawing length by 50.
6

Why should scale units be written explicitly?

Answer: To prevent comparing unlike units and to make conversions auditable
Reasoning: A scale ratio is meaningful only when units are controlled.
