Optimization with Perimeter and Area Constraints · Teacher Key

Instructional focus

Students must distinguish objective from constraint and verify feasibility before comparing outcomes. A mathematically attractive design that violates a stated requirement is not a solution.

Misconception watch

Optimization is not simply “choose the largest number.” First define the objective, enforce every constraint, and then compare feasible candidates.
