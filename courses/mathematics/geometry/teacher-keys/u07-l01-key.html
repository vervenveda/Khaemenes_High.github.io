Find the sum of the interior angles of a 10-gon.

Answer: 1440
Reasoning: (n−2)180°=(10−2)180°.
2

Find each exterior angle of a regular 10-gon.

Answer: 36
Reasoning: Regular exterior angles equal 360°/10.
3

Which quadrilateral has four congruent sides and four right angles?

Answer: Square
Reasoning: A square is both a rhombus and a rectangle.
4

What do the diagonals of a parallelogram do?

Answer: Bisect each other
Reasoning: This property also provides a converse test.
5

What additional property turns a parallelogram into a rectangle?

Answer: Congruent diagonals or one right angle
Reasoning: Either condition is sufficient.
6

A regular polygon has perimeter 48 and apothem 7. Find its area.

Answer: 168
Reasoning: A=1/2·aP=1/2·7·48=168.
