Scoring the critical-thinking items

Do not penalize a learner for refusing to calculate when information is genuinely insufficient. Award reasoning credit for identifying irrelevant data, missing theorem conditions, overclaims, equivalent exact/approximate forms, or a need to resolve conflicting evidence. A numerical answer reached through invalid reasoning should not receive full reasoning credit.

Completion record

Require the L01–L05 portfolio plus the L06 synthesis. The student should identify one strengthened claim, one narrowed/limited claim, and one revision caused by evidence or critique.

Mastery

80% plus corrections. Corrections must explain the reasoning error—not merely replace the answer. Completion recognizes demonstrated mastery after correction, not first-attempt perfection.
