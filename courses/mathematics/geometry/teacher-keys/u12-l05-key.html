Unit 12 · Lesson 05 · Teacher Key

Area, Perimeter & Material Schedules

Evaluate geometry, units, estimating assumptions, and the distinction between mathematical requirement and purchase quantity.

Math Word: Quantity

Expected retrieval: A defensible quantity identifies what is measured and includes an appropriate unit; “18” alone is incomplete.

Instructional anchor

Ask what the material physically does before selecting a measure: surface coverage usually requires area, boundary material usually requires length, and packaged materials require a final discrete purchase conversion. Waste factors are contextual assumptions, not universal constants.
