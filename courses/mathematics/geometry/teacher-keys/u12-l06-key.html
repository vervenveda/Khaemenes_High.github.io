Transformations, Repetition & Modular Design

Require explicit transformation rules and verification of invariants; visual similarity alone is insufficient evidence.

Math Word: Transformation

Expected retrieval: Dilation changes size while preserving shape; translation, rotation, and reflection are rigid motions.

Challenge Unlocked guidance

Mandala Rings is the preferred reinforcement because students can connect radial repetition to rotational symmetry. Ask the learner to identify the symmetry order and compute the smallest positive rotational angle. Sanctuary may be used for a creative modular build. Arcade participation remains optional, ungraded, and post-completion.

Mastery

80% plus corrections and a CAD modular design containing at least eight module instances, two transformation types, declared transformation rules, and a statement of an invariant property.
