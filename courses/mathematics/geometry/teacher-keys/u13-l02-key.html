Unit 13 · Lesson 02 · Teacher Key

Develop Constructions, Models & Proof

Evaluate whether the learner has converted the L01 brief into a traceable mathematical model rather than merely producing a polished diagram.

Math Word: Validation

Expected retrieval: Strong validation uses an independent check capable of catching an error in the original method; simply repeating the same calculation is weaker evidence.

1
Answer: Project-specific. Require the same central question from L01, two genuinely measured/verified inputs, two measurable constraints, and one clearly labeled modeling assumption. Reject preferences presented as constraints.
2
Answer: Project-specific. Require at least three relevant mathematical tools and a purpose for each. Examples may include similarity for scale, distance formula for spacing, Pythagorean theorem for diagonals, area/perimeter for material quantities, transformations for modular patterns, or circle/solid formulas where appropriate.
3
Answer: The model must identify variables, units, fixed geometry, and assumptions. Strong evidence allows another person to reconstruct or audit the model without guessing what symbols mean.
4
Answer: Responses vary. Require a specific mathematical claim, visible quantitative or proof evidence, and reasoning that explains why that evidence supports the claim. Preference statements alone do not satisfy this item.
5
Answer: Valid methods vary. Examples: coordinate calculation checked by CAD measurement; area decomposition checked by a second formula; algebraic result checked by physical measurement; theorem result checked by an independent construction. The second method should not simply repeat the same assumptions and arithmetic.
6
Answer: Project-specific. Require one correctly identified example in each category: proven, measured, assumed, approximate. The labels matter because they communicate different levels and sources of confidence in the model.

Instructional checkpoint

Do not let CAD polish mask missing mathematics. Before L03, the learner should have a model that can be audited, at least one defensible claim-evidence-reasoning chain, and a planned independent validation.

Mastery

80% plus corrections. Capstone evidence should be saved for the technical portfolio rather than treated as disposable lesson work.
