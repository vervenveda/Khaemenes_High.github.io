Nets & Surface Area of Prisms/Pyramids

Instructional anchor

Surface area is a face-accounting problem. Require learners to identify the net or face families before using a compressed formula. For regular pyramids, lateral area uses slant height ℓ, not perpendicular volume height.

Misconception watch

  • Missing the second base of a prism.
  • Counting a face twice because it appears in two mental orientations.
  • Using perpendicular pyramid height in a triangular-face area calculation.
  • Assuming only one planar arrangement can be a valid net.

Mastery: 80% plus corrections and a defensible face-count explanation.
