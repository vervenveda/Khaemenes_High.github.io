CPCTC and Multi-Step Triangle Proofs · Teacher Key

Answers correspond to the hardened 20-problem lesson.

Answer: ∠E and DF.
2
Answer: XY, YZ, ∠Z.
3
Answer: ΔABC≅ΔDEF by SSS; therefore ∠A≅∠D by CPCTC.
4
Answer: CPCTC requires congruent triangles already to be known. It cannot be used as the premise that proves those triangles congruent.
5
Answer: ΔABC≅ΔADC by SSS using AB≅AD, BC≅DC, AC≅AC; then ∠BAC≅∠DAC by CPCTC.
6
Answer: ΔJKL≅ΔMNP by ASA. Then, for example, JL≅MP by CPCTC.
7
Answer: UV=14, VW=9, ∠W=42° by CPCTC.
8
Answer: Incorrect. BC corresponds to EF; AC corresponds to DF.
9
Answer: AC≅AC by reflexive property. With AB≅AD and ∠BAC≅∠DAC, ΔBAC≅ΔDAC by SAS; therefore BC≅DC by CPCTC.
10
Answer: ΔABC≅ΔDEF by HL; therefore BC≅EF by CPCTC.
11
Answer: 3x+2=20, x=6. The equality follows because AB≅DE by CPCTC.
12
Answer: 5x−7=3x+17, so x=12 and each angle is 53°. ∠Q≅∠Y by CPCTC.
13
Answer: AC≅CA reflexively. AB≅CD and ∠BAC≅∠DCA with included side AC/CA give ΔBAC≅ΔDCA by SAS; then BC≅AD by CPCTC.
14
Answer: SAS proves the triangles congruent; CPCTC then proves the corresponding third sides congruent.
15
Answer: A statement establishing the two triangles congruent is missing. CPCTC cannot be invoked from two angle pairs alone.
16
Answer: 2y+5=5y−16, so y=7 and BC=19.
17
Answer: CPCTC guarantees every corresponding angle and side pair is congruent after SSS establishes the triangular panels themselves are congruent. SSS is the proof of triangle congruence; CPCTC is the consequence.
18
Answer: Answers vary. Valid structure: two side givens + included-angle given → SAS → congruent triangles → desired corresponding angle pair by CPCTC.
19
Answer: Answers vary. Check that the three vertex pairs implied by the congruence statement are used consistently for all three sides and all three angles.
20
Answer: Answers vary. The repaired proof must establish triangle congruence with SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, or HL before invoking CPCTC.
