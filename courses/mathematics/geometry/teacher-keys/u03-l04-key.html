CPCTC and Multi-Step Triangle Proofs · Teacher Key

Use proven triangle congruence to justify new side and angle relationships in multi-step arguments.

Learning intention

Use accurate diagrams, definitions, measurements, and reasoning to use proven triangle congruence to justify new side and angle relationships in multi-step arguments.

Success evidence

  • Label the figure and units before calculating.
  • Select and justify the governing definition, theorem, relationship, or construction.
  • Show enough work that another learner can audit the conclusion.
  • Check the result against the diagram, scale, and stated constraints.

Visual model

Identify sufficient evidence for triangle congruence and organize theorem-based proofs about corresponding parts and centers.

Worked reasoning

Process: Match vertices in a consistent correspondence, mark the known evidence, select the valid congruence theorem, then use CPCTC only after congruence is established.

  1. Read the figure and rewrite all given information with correct notation.
  2. Identify the exact relationship that connects the known and unknown quantities.
  3. Substitute, construct, or prove one justified step at a time.
  4. State the result with units and a sentence explaining what it means.

Misconception check: AAA proves similarity, not congruence; SSA is not a general congruence theorem.

Name

1

A triangle has angles 43° and 48°. Find the third angle.

Answer: 89
Reasoning: Triangle angles sum to 180°.
2

Two sides are 10 and 11. State the integer range for the third side x.

Answer: 1<x<21
Reasoning: Use the triangle inequality and the difference inequality.
3

Which congruence theorem uses two sides and the included angle?

Answer: SAS
Reasoning: The angle must be between the two known sides.
4

After proving ΔABC≅ΔDEF, which angle corresponds to ∠B?

Answer: ∠E
Reasoning: The order of vertices determines correspondence.
5

Where do the perpendicular bisectors of a triangle meet?

Answer: Circumcenter
Reasoning: It is equidistant from all three vertices.
6

Why does AAA not prove triangle congruence?

Answer: It fixes shape but not size
Reasoning: AAA establishes similarity.
