Algebra II Midterm Examination

Balanced mixed-evidence mastery across the first six Algebra II units

60 selected-response questions plus 6 required constructed-response tasks. Selected response contributes 70%; evaluator-reviewed reasoning contributes 30%.

Administration Guide
Learning ObjectiveDemonstrate cumulative mastery of advanced functions, polynomial and complex-number structure, polynomial equations, rational functions, radicals, and exponential models.
DirectionsComplete all 60 selected-response questions and all 6 written reasoning tasks. Preserve exact forms when appropriate and verify solutions against domain or model constraints.

Selected-Response Assessment

6 sections · 10 questions per unit · balanced across five reasoning dimensions

0/60 answered · 0/6 written complete

Constructed-Response Algebra II Evidence

Complete all six written tasks. Each response needs at least 60 meaningful characters and will be reviewed using the 0–4 rubric.

Evaluator Review · Algebra II Reasoning

Full mastery requires ≥80% selected response, ≥80% constructed response, and ≥80% overall. Reviewer identity, role, note, and attestation are retained with the local review record.

  • 4 · Complete and correct reasoning; algebraic structure, representation, verification, units or interpretation, and relevant limitations are explicit.
  • 3 · Substantially correct reasoning with a minor omission or small non-conceptual error.
  • 2 · Partial reasoning shows important understanding but contains a conceptual gap, incomplete verification, or weak interpretation.
  • 1 · Limited relevant work; major misconceptions or unsupported conclusions remain.
  • 0 · Blank, unrelated, or no assessable mathematical reasoning.
