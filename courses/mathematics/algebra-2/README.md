## Strict lesson progression

Lesson activity completion and mastery are separate states. A learner may read, practice, write notes, and finish the page without unlocking the next lesson. Normal progression now requires an evaluator-reviewed lesson score of at least 80%.

The learner-facing sequence is:

`Learn → Practice → Submit Assignment → Score Recorded → Mastery Checked → Local Review Available → Continue`

A below-80 attempt remains in local history for correction and retake. Local workflow or review markers never substitute for the canonical lesson score or formal unit assessments.
