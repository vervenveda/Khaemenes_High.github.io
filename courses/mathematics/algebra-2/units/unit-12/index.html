Unit 12 · 2 Weeks · 7 Lessons

Probability, Distributions & Statistical Inference

How do counting, probability models, distributions, and sampling support responsible conclusions under uncertainty?

Detailed Lessons

Lessons include objectives, formulas, concept development, worked examples, guided and independent practice, differentiated pathways, application, misconception analysis, mastery evidence, reflection, and local completion.

Lesson 01

Fundamental Counting Principle, Permutations & Combinations

Use permutations when order matters and combinations when order does not. The Fundamental Counting Principle multiplies independent stage choices.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 02

Probability Rules, Complements & Unions

Complements, unions, intersections, and mutually exclusive events organize probability calculations. Probabilities must remain between 0 and 1.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 03

Conditional Probability, Independence & Bayes Reasoning

Conditional probability updates the sample space after information is known. Independence means P(A|B)=P(A), not merely that events are different.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 04

Binomial Experiments & Expected Value

A binomial model requires fixed n, two outcomes, constant p, and independent trials. Mean is np and standard deviation is √[np(1−p)].

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 05

Normal Distributions, z-Scores & Percentiles

The normal model describes symmetric bell-shaped distributions. z-scores standardize distance from the mean, enabling percentile comparisons.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 06

Sampling, Bias, Margin of Error & Study Design

Sampling design, randomization, sample size, and response quality affect inference. Larger random samples reduce sampling variability but do not automatically remove systematic bias.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 07

Evaluating Statistical Claims & Communicating Uncertainty

Responsible statistical communication distinguishes observational studies from randomized experiments, reports uncertainty, and avoids misleading scales or selective summaries.

Open detailed lesson
