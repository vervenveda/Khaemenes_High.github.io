Binomial Experiments & Expected Value

A binomial model requires fixed n, two outcomes, constant p, and independent trials. Mean is np and standard deviation is √[np(1−p)].

02 · See

See the governing relationship

P(X=k)=C(n,k)p^k(1−p)^(n−k).

A binomial model requires fixed n, two outcomes, constant p, and independent trials. Mean is np and standard deviation is √[np(1−p)].

03 · Worked

Follow complete examples

n=5,p=0.2, find P(X=2).C(5,2)(0.2)²(0.8)³=0.2048.
Mean for n=80,p=0.35.28.

Reasoning habit: state assumptions before calculating and interpret results in context.
