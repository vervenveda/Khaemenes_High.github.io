Lesson 01

Arithmetic Sequences: Explicit & Recursive Forms

Arithmetic sequences have constant first differences. Explicit form gives direct access; recursive form emphasizes step-by-step generation.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 02

Geometric Sequences: Growth, Decay & Recursion

Geometric sequences have a constant ratio. The sign and magnitude of r determine growth, decay, and alternation.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 03

General Sequences, Patterns & Difference Tables

Sequence rules may be explicit, recursive, piecewise, or generated by patterns. Constant kth differences suggest a degree-k polynomial model.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 04

Finite Arithmetic & Geometric Series

A series is a sum of sequence terms. Formula choice depends on additive or multiplicative structure.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 05

Infinite Geometric Series & Convergence

An infinite geometric series converges only when repeated terms shrink toward zero. Otherwise partial sums do not approach a finite limit.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 06

Sigma Notation & Summation Properties

Sigma notation compresses sums. Properties allow constants to factor out and sums to split, but bounds and index meaning must remain clear.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 07

Pascal’s Triangle & the Binomial Theorem

Pascal’s Triangle and combinations provide coefficients for binomial expansion. Term structure can be predicted without expanding every term.
