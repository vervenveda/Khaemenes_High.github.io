Nonlinear Systems & Matrices

Lesson 01

Graphical Solutions of Linear–Nonlinear Systems

A system may have zero, one, or several solutions. Graphing gives estimates and structural insight; exact algebra confirms when possible.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 02

Solving Nonlinear Systems by Substitution

Substitution reduces a nonlinear system to one equation. Each resulting input must be paired with its output and checked.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 03

Polynomial, Rational & Radical Systems

Polynomial, rational, and radical systems may create extraneous candidates or high-degree equations. Technology can support but not replace verification.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 04

Matrix Vocabulary, Dimensions & Operations

Matrix dimensions control which operations are defined. Multiplication is not generally commutative and represents composition of transformations or linked systems.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 05

Matrix Equations, Row Reduction & Systems

Augmented matrices organize elimination. A row of zeros may signal dependence; an impossible row signals inconsistency.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 06

Determinants, Inverses & Transformation Models

The determinant indicates invertibility and area-scale behavior for 2D transformations. Inverse matrices reverse nonsingular linear transformations.
