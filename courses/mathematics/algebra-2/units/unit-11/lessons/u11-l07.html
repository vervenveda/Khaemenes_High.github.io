06 · Independent

Complete the practice independently

  1. Using a=7, b=9, C=60°, solve for c and the included-angle area. Carry exact radicals before rounding.
  2. For sides 5, 5, 6, use Heron's formula to determine the area and then check with an altitude.
  3. Explain why a=2, b=3, c=6 cannot be used in Heron's formula for a real triangle.
  4. Verify your model in Triangle Law Expedition; record which quantities were SAS or SSS.
  1. When a=3, b=4 and C=90°, find c and the area, then verify using Heron.
  2. For side lengths 5, 5, 6, find s and K exactly.
  3. For a=7, b=9, C=60°, express c in radical form.
Reveal worked verification and answers
  1. c=√(9+16)=5, K=½×3×4=6; s=6 and Heron K=√[6×3×2×1]=6.
  2. s=8 and K=√[8×3×3×2]=√144=12 square units.
  3. c²=49+81−2×7×9×½=67, so c=√67≈8.19.
