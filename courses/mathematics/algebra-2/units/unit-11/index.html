Detailed Lessons

Lessons include objectives, formulas, concept development, worked examples, guided and independent practice, differentiated pathways, application, misconception analysis, mastery evidence, reflection, and local completion.

Lesson 01

Fundamental, Reciprocal & Pythagorean Identities

Fundamental identities follow from the unit circle and reciprocal or quotient definitions. They permit equivalent rewrites and simplification.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 02

Verifying Identities with Equivalent Transformations

Identity verification is proof, not equation solving. Work on one side, preserve domain restrictions, and name the transformation.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 03

Sum and Difference Identities

Sum and difference identities produce exact values and connect angle combinations to known angles.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 04

Double-Angle and Half-Angle Identities

Double- and half-angle identities transform frequency and powers. Choose the form matching available information and domain.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 05

Solving Trigonometric Equations on Intervals

Trigonometric equations may have infinitely many solutions. Exact interval solutions and general solutions require quadrant and period reasoning.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 06

Law of Sines, Ambiguous Case & Applications

The Law of Sines solves AAS, ASA, and sometimes SSA triangles. SSA may yield zero, one, or two triangles, so the ambiguous case must be checked.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 07

Law of Cosines & Heron-Type Area Reasoning

The Law of Cosines generalizes the Pythagorean theorem and solves SAS or SSS triangles. It also supports classification and distance models.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 08

Navigation, Vectors & Multi-Step Triangle Models

Multi-step trigonometric models require a diagram, angle convention, units, and reasonableness checks. Rounding should be delayed.
