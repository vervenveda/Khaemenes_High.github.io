Advanced Functions, Transformations & Inverses

How do notation, transformations, composition, and inverse thinking reveal the structure and behavior of functions?

Suggested pacing

Use approximately 10 sessions: concept development, guided practice, application, error analysis, mastery, corrections, and project evidence. Adjust by demonstrated mastery rather than age.

Three pathways

Foundation: prerequisite retrieval, scaffolded steps, tables and graphs. Core: complete unit targets. Extended: proof, parameter analysis, modelling, and pre-calculus connections.

Assessment evidence

Use lesson checks, practice explanations, the randomized mastery check, corrections, project rubric, oral explanation, and representative work together. Do not infer mastery from one score alone.

Key misconception

Replacing f(x) with multiplication f·x. The notation names an output; it is not a product.

Require the learner to identify the first invalid step and provide a rule, verification, or counterexample.

Standards categories

Functions, Building Functions, Interpreting Functions, Mathematical Modelling. The course-wide standards union and local-reporting disclaimer are in STANDARDS_UNION.md.

Unit 01 exceptional-depth standard

Exceptional-depth evidence standard

Use all 6 lessons, differentiated pathway practice, Weeks 2–3 spaced retrieval, the 20-question five-dimension mastery form, corrections, and the applied investigation as complementary evidence. Do not substitute one score for the full evidence pattern.

  • Foundation: scaffolded access, prerequisite fluency, representations, and correction.
  • Core: complete unit targets across fluency, application, representation, error analysis, and transfer.
  • Extended: proof-quality justification, parameter reasoning, modelling, and generalization.
  • Retention: non-gating retrieval deliberately returns to prior learning before it fades.
  • Mastery: 80% remains the progression threshold; retakes prioritize unseen items and missed-work correction.

Unit 01 exceptional-depth pilot

Evidence standard

Use the six lessons, pathway practice, two non-gating retention checks, the 20-question balanced mastery form, and the applied investigation as complementary evidence.

  • Foundation: scaffolded access and prerequisite fluency.
  • Core: complete unit targets with mixed reasoning.
  • Extended: parameter reasoning, transfer, critique, and generalization.
  • Lesson evidence: score with the common five-part analytic rubric rather than an arbitrary percentage.
  • Retakes: require correction evidence; the selector prioritizes items not used on the immediately prior form.
