Trigonometric Functions & Periodic Models

How do angle measure, the unit circle, graphs, and periodic functions model rotation, waves, cycles, and repeated behavior?

What the learner will study

  • Angle Measure, Radians & Coterminal Angles
  • The Unit Circle & Coordinate Definitions
  • Exact Trigonometric Values & Reference Angles
  • Six Trigonometric Functions & Reciprocal Relationships
  • Graphs of Sine and Cosine
  • Amplitude, Period, Phase & Vertical Shifts
  • Graphs of Tangent, Cotangent, Secant & Cosecant
  • Inverse Trigonometric Functions & Restricted Domains
  • Sinusoidal Regression & Periodic Data
  • Vectors, Bearings & Component Models

How to support without taking over

  • Ask the learner to define inputs, outputs, units, and domain.
  • Ask which property or theorem justifies each transformation.
  • Request an independent check by substitution, graph, table, or alternate method.
  • Encourage correction notes rather than erasing evidence of learning.
  • Use the calculator after the mathematical setup is visible.

Evidence to preserve

Keep one worked example, one correction, one application or project artifact, the mastery result, and a reflection explaining growth and next steps.

Applied investigation

Periodic Phenomenon Modelling Studio asks the learner to define a real question, construct mathematics, validate results, state uncertainty, and communicate a defensible conclusion.
