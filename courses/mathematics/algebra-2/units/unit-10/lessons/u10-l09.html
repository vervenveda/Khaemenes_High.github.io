Sinusoidal Regression & Periodic Data

Periodic regression estimates amplitude, cycle length, phase, and midline. Data span should cover enough cycles to support the model.

← Previous LessonUnit 10 HomeNext Lesson →

Learning objectives

  • Explain the central structure of sinusoidal regression & periodic data using precise mathematical language.
  • Apply the governing relationship and verify results with an alternate representation or substitution.
  • State domain, unit, precision, and context restrictions whenever they affect the result.
  • Analyze a misconception and communicate a correction with evidence.

Essential question: How do angle measure, the unit circle, graphs, and periodic functions model rotation, waves, cycles, and repeated behavior?

Governing relationship

Fit y=A sin(Bx+C)+D and inspect residuals.

Periodic regression estimates amplitude, cycle length, phase, and midline. Data span should cover enough cycles to support the model.

Worked examples

Read each example, close it, and reconstruct the reasoning independently. Then verify by substitution, graph, table, or alternate method.

Reasoning workshop · Periodic data, model parameters and residuals

Study the governing rule, follow every step, and verify the conclusion before attempting independent work.

Investigation A · Build a sinusoidal model from measured points.

  1. Suppose (t,y) is (0,8),(3,12),(6,8),(9,4),(12,8).
  2. Midline is (12+4)/2=8 and amplitude is (12−4)/2=4.
  3. Peak-to-peak period is 12, so B=2π/12=π/6.
  4. Since the curve starts at the midline and rises, y=8+4sin(πt/6) fits all five points.

Verification: At t=9, the model predicts 8+4sin(3π/2)=4, matching the observed minimum.

Investigation B · Interpret observed-minus-predicted residual.

  1. At t=6 this model predicts y=8.
  2. If a new observation is y=8.3, residual=observed−predicted=8.3−8=+0.3.
  3. Plot residuals against time over several cycles; a pattern indicates the model misses important structure.

Verification: A single close fit is not proof that every future cycle will agree.

Concept diagram · Periodic data, model parameters and residuals

Independent transfer · check after solving

Complete these without the game or answer key first. Give exact values where possible and show a short verification.

  1. Model the observations (0,3),(2,7),(4,3),(6,−1),(8,3).
  2. Predict your model's value at t=10.
  3. If observed y(2)=6.8 and predicted y(2)=7, calculate the residual.
Reveal independent answer reasoning
  1. One model: y=3+4sin(πt/4), amplitude 4, midline 3, period 8.
  2. y(10)=3+4sin(5π/2)=7.
  3. Residual=6.8−7=−0.2.

Misconception to correct: Data may look periodic without being perfectly sinusoidal. Examine units, uncertainty, residual patterns and extrapolation limits.
