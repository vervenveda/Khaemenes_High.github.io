Exponential Functions & Growth Models

Lessons include objectives, formulas, concept development, worked examples, guided and independent practice, differentiated pathways, application, misconception analysis, mastery evidence, reflection, and local completion.

Lesson 01

Exponential Function Structure & Parameters

The initial value a and growth factor b determine an exponential function. Equal input increases multiply outputs by a constant factor rather than adding a constant difference.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 02

Growth, Decay & Percent Change

Discrete exponential change uses a per-period multiplier. Growth uses 1+r; decay uses 1−r. Time units must match the stated rate period.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 03

Compound Interest & Effective Rates

Compound interest depends on principal, nominal annual rate, compounding frequency, and time. Effective annual rate allows fair comparisons.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 04

Continuous Growth, Decay & the Constant e

Continuous growth and decay use the natural base e. The sign and units of r determine direction and time scale.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 05

Solving Exponential Equations by Common Bases

If exponential expressions can be expressed with the same base, equate exponents. Otherwise logarithms are required later.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 06

Exponential Regression & Data Modelling

Exponential regression estimates multiplicative change from data. A strong fit requires more than a coefficient: inspect residuals, domain, mechanism, and uncertainty.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 07

Comparing Linear, Quadratic & Exponential Models

Linear models have constant differences, exponential models have constant ratios, and quadratic models have constant second differences. Model choice should be evidence-based.
