Polynomial Operations & Equivalent Forms

Polynomial addition and subtraction combine coefficients of like powers. Multiplication uses distribution and exponent rules. Equivalent forms should be checked by substitution or reverse operations.

02 · See

See the governing relationship

Combine like powers; distribute every factor.

Polynomial addition and subtraction combine coefficients of like powers. Multiplication uses distribution and exponent rules. Equivalent forms should be checked by substitution or reverse operations.

07 · Error

Find and repair the misconception

Common misconception: Failing to distribute a subtraction sign to every term in the second polynomial.

Locate the first invalid step, state the rule that corrects it, and verify the repaired reasoning.
