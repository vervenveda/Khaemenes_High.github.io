Unit 13 applied investigation

Algebra II Modelling Exploration & Oral Defense

How can a transparent, tested, and ethically communicated mathematical model support a real decision?

1. Question and context

Frame a specific, ethical, measurable question that genuinely uses this unit’s mathematics. Identify stakeholders, desired outcome, and what would count as useful evidence.

2. Variables and assumptions

Define all quantities, units, domain, data source, constraints, and assumptions. Explain why the selected mathematical structure fits.

3. Build and compute

Create at least two representations, show exact and approximate work, use technology strategically, and preserve enough detail for reproduction.

4. Validate and revise

Check with substitution, residuals, alternate methods, or cases. Analyze sensitivity, uncertainty, fairness, and model limitations.

5. Communicate

Present claim, evidence, reasoning, visualizations, limitations, and a conclusion that distinguishes mathematical result from recommendation.

6. Defense and reflection

Answer critique, revise transparently, and explain what the model can and cannot support.
