Detailed Lessons

Lessons include objectives, formulas, concept development, worked examples, guided and independent practice, differentiated pathways, application, misconception analysis, mastery evidence, reflection, and local completion.

Lesson 01

Frame a Meaningful, Ethical & Answerable Question

A capstone question should identify a decision, population or system, variables, time frame, and ethical constraints. It must be answerable with available or responsibly collected evidence.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 02

Select Variables, Functions, Data & Assumptions

Model design translates a real situation into mathematics. Assumptions should be explicit and testable, and the chosen function family should match observed structure.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 03

Build, Compute, Visualize & Validate the Model

Construction includes equations, graphs, tables, technology outputs, exact reasoning, and checks. A reproducible record should let another learner follow the work.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 04

Test Sensitivity, Uncertainty, Fairness & Limitations

A model is only as useful as its assumptions and limits. Vary important parameters, compare alternatives, identify who may be affected, and state unsupported regions.

Open detailed lesson

Lesson 05

Present, Defend, Revise & Reflect

A strong defense communicates mathematics coherently, answers questions, distinguishes result from recommendation, and revises when critique reveals a weakness.
