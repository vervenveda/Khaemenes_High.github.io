Unit 13 · Lesson 01 · Open-Age

Frame a Meaningful, Ethical & Answerable Question

A capstone question should identify a decision, population or system, variables, time frame, and ethical constraints. It must be answerable with available or responsibly collected evidence.

← Previous LessonUnit 13 HomeNext Lesson →

01 · Recall

Recall the investigation structure

Before starting Frame a Meaningful, Ethical & Answerable Question, identify the question, variables, assumptions, data source, model type, validation plan, and ethical constraints that govern the investigation.

Check

Name what evidence would support the claim and what evidence would force a revision.

02 · See

See the governing relationship

A strong model begins with a specific, ethical, measurable question.

A capstone question should identify a decision, population or system, variables, time frame, and ethical constraints. It must be answerable with available or responsibly collected evidence.

03 · Worked

Follow complete examples

Weak: 'What is the best city?'Stronger: 'Which of three transit routes minimizes average travel time and cost for a defined commuter profile over one month?'
A question requires private health data.Revise to use public or anonymized data and minimize harm.

Reasoning habit: separate what the mathematics shows from what the model only suggests.

04 · Explain

Explain why the method works

Connect the model to its variables, assumptions, data, validation method, uncertainty, fairness, stakeholder impact, and stated limitations.

05 · Guided

Try one with support

Narrow a broad question.

  1. Identify the question, evidence, assumptions, and constraints.
  2. Carry out one justified modeling decision at a time.
  3. Verify with alternate representations, residuals, sensitivity tests, holdout checks, domain review, or ethical analysis.

06 · Independent

Complete the investigation work independently

  1. Identify stakeholders.
  2. Write measurable outcomes.
  3. Create one original example involving frame a meaningful, ethical & answerable question and solve it completely.
  4. Use the Khaemenes Scientific Calculator to verify one result, then document the setup and reasonableness check.

07 · Error

Find and repair the modeling error

Common misconception: Choosing a question because it sounds impressive even though data, scope, or ethics make it unanswerable.

Locate the first unsupported assumption, calculation, interpretation, or fairness issue; state the correction; and show how the conclusion changes.

08 · Transfer

Apply the idea to the capstone

Frame a real decision question suitable for Algebra II functions and data.

09 · Feedback

Check the strength of the argument

Ready

The question is answerable, assumptions are explicit, evidence supports the claim, uncertainty is communicated, and limitations are visible.

Revise

If any piece is missing, return to Worked, Guided, or Error before presenting mastery evidence.

10 · Reflect

One-minute reflection

What assumption, source of uncertainty, fairness concern, or limitation mattered most in Frame a Meaningful, Ethical & Answerable Question, and how did it change your conclusion?
