About Algebra II

Algebra II extends Algebra I into advanced function families and multi-representation reasoning. Students analyze structure, transform functions, solve sophisticated equations, model data, and prepare for precalculus and higher mathematics.

Course Features

Advanced FunctionsPolynomial, rational, exponential, logarithmic, radical, piecewise, and related function families are studied deeply.
Multiple RepresentationsStudents connect symbolic, graphical, numerical, tabular, and contextual representations throughout the course.
80% MasteryProgress remains evidence-based, with mastery thresholds, correction cycles, and cumulative review.
Higher-Math ReadinessThe course builds the algebraic fluency, modelling skill, and function reasoning needed for precalculus, trigonometry, and college mathematics.
