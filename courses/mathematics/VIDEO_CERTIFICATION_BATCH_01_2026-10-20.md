## Gates still required

1. Watch/review each complete video for mathematical accuracy, pacing, and lesson fit.
2. Verify the privacy-enhanced `youtube-nocookie.com` embed in the published Academy lesson.
3. Verify captions in the actual playback environment.
4. Verify responsive rendering on phone, tablet, and desktop.
5. Verify the transcript/provider fallback remains reachable if video playback fails.
6. Confirm video remains optional and cannot write to mastery, evidence, or progression authority.
7. Only after all gates pass may both the course placement and canonical record be explicitly approved for release.

## Release rule

A Batch 01 candidate is **not** student-ready merely because an official transcript exists. Official transcript verification removes one accessibility uncertainty; it does not certify runtime playback, captions, complete mathematical content, or Academy rendering.
