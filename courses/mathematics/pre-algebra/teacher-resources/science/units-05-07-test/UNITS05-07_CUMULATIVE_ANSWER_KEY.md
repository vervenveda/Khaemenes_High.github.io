# Units 05–07 Cumulative Benchmark Definitive Answer Key

Course: International Integrated Science 9 (`KH-SCI-IIS9`)

Objective mastery threshold: **48/60**.
Recommended diagnostic threshold: **16/20 per unit**.

## Unit 05

### 1. Which sequence increases in ecological scale?
**Correct answer:** A. Organism → population → community → ecosystem

Individuals form populations; interacting populations form communities; communities plus abiotic environment form ecosystems.

### 2. Which statement is a direct observation?
**Correct answer:** B. Twelve individuals were counted in a 10 m transect

A documented count is an observation; causal and historical statements are inferences.

### 3. Which statement correctly distinguishes habitat and niche?
**Correct answer:** C. Habitat is location; niche includes role and resource use

Habitat describes where an organism lives; niche includes its ecological role and requirements.

### 4. Why can removal of one species affect several others?
**Correct answer:** D. Food webs contain direct and indirect interactions

Networked interactions can transmit effects through several pathways.

### 5. A trophic level receives 320 kJ from a preceding level with 4,000 kJ. Efficiency is:
**Correct answer:** A. 8%

320 ÷ 4,000 × 100 = 8%.

### 6. Which process converts atmospheric nitrogen into biologically available forms?
**Correct answer:** B. Nitrogen fixation

Nitrogen fixation converts N₂ into compounds that enter biological cycles.

### 7. A sample contains counts 4, 4, 4, and 4 for four species. Compared with counts 13, 1, 1, and 1, the first sample has:
**Correct answer:** C. The same richness and greater evenness

Both contain four species and 16 organisms, but the first is more even.

### 8. A population changes from 800 to 680. What is the percentage change?
**Correct answer:** D. −15%

(680−800) ÷ 800 × 100 = −15%.

### 9. Which condition violates a simple mark–recapture assumption?
**Correct answer:** A. Marked animals become much easier to capture than unmarked animals

Different capture probability biases the ratio.

### 10. A predator and prey time series shows opposite trends. What additional evidence strengthens a causal explanation?
**Correct answer:** B. Feeding records, prey survival, vegetation, disease, weather, and alternative-factor analysis

Mechanistic and comparative evidence helps test causation.

### 11. Which statement avoids teleological language?
**Correct answer:** C. Antibiotic exposure favored resistant variants that left more descendants

Selection changes variant frequencies; it does not require purposeful mutation.

### 12. Which process creates new alleles?
**Correct answer:** D. Mutation

Mutation changes DNA sequence and can create a new allele.

### 13. A measured allele-frequency shift across generations is commonly classified as:
**Correct answer:** A. Microevolution

Microevolution includes measurable inherited-frequency changes in populations.

### 14. Which provides direct evidence relevant to speciation in living populations?
**Correct answer:** B. Reduced interbreeding or reproductive compatibility between diverging populations

Reproductive isolation is central to many species concepts.

### 15. Which statement is consistent with the standard biological use of macroevolution?
**Correct answer:** C. It includes speciation and diversification across lineages

Macroevolution refers to patterns at or above the species level and is not the origin of life.

### 16. Which statement about fossil evidence is accurate?
**Correct answer:** D. Fossils can preserve anatomy and context but rarely identify a direct parent–descendant pair

Anatomy and context are observed; ancestry is reconstructed and classifications may be revised.

### 17. Why is a ladder model of horse evolution misleading?
**Correct answer:** A. The record is interpreted as branching, with coexisting forms and extinct side branches

Equid diversity is not a single uninterrupted march.

### 18. Which sequence moves from most direct to most interpretive?
**Correct answer:** B. Specimen → measurement → classification → ancestry inference → worldview conclusion

Evidence should be separated from progressively broader interpretation.

### 19. Which statement is correct?
**Correct answer:** C. It is a method that seeks testable natural mechanisms and does not itself settle theological truth

Methodological naturalism defines empirical method, not a compulsory metaphysical belief.

### 20. Which student response should receive the stronger score?
**Correct answer:** D. One that accurately classifies evidence, fairly represents alternatives, and supports a reasoned conclusion

Assessment is based on literacy, evidence, reasoning, and respectful representation.

## Unit 06

### 21. Which sample is a compound?
**Correct answer:** A. Pure sodium chloride

Sodium chloride contains elements chemically combined in a fixed ratio.

### 22. Which observation led Thomson to infer negatively charged particles within atoms?
**Correct answer:** B. Cathode rays were deflected by electric and magnetic fields

Cathode-ray deflection supported negatively charged subatomic particles.

### 23. In Rutherford scattering, most alpha particles passed through foil. This supports the inference that atoms are:
**Correct answer:** C. Mostly empty space at that scale

Most particles encountered no concentrated mass or charge.

### 24. Why did atomic models change over time?
**Correct answer:** D. New evidence exposed limitations and supported revised models

Scientific models are revised when new evidence requires better explanations.

### 25. Which set of relative charges is correct for proton, neutron, electron?
**Correct answer:** A. +1, 0, −1

Proton +1, neutron 0, electron −1.

### 26. A neutral atom contains 8 protons and 10 neutrons. Which statement is correct?
**Correct answer:** B. It is oxygen-18 with 8 electrons

Atomic number 8 is oxygen; mass number 18; neutrality gives 8 electrons.

### 27. A particle has 13 protons and 10 electrons. It is:
**Correct answer:** C. Al³⁺

Atomic number 13 is aluminium; three fewer electrons gives 3+.

### 28. In the notation ³⁹₁₉K, the lower number represents:
**Correct answer:** D. Atomic number

The subscript is atomic number.

### 29. Which property differs between neutral chlorine-35 and chlorine-37 atoms?
**Correct answer:** A. Neutron number

They differ in neutron number.

### 30. An element has isotope masses 10 and 11 with abundances 20% and 80%. Its weighted mass is:
**Correct answer:** B. 10.8

10(0.20)+11(0.80)=10.8.

### 31. Which statement about standard atomic weight is most accurate?
**Correct answer:** C. It may be a weighted value or interval reflecting natural isotope composition

Standard atomic weights summarize representative isotope masses and abundances.

### 32. Which configuration belongs to aluminium?
**Correct answer:** D. 2,8,3

Aluminium has atomic number 13.

### 33. Which species has configuration 2,8?
**Correct answer:** A. Na⁺

Na⁺ has 10 electrons; Cl⁻ and K⁺ have 18.

### 34. Which element is in Period 4 among the first 20?
**Correct answer:** B. Potassium

Potassium has four occupied shells.

### 35. Which pair has related chemical properties because both have one outer electron?
**Correct answer:** C. Li and K

Lithium and potassium are Group 1.

### 36. Across Period 3, which general change occurs?
**Correct answer:** D. Metallic character decreases toward nonmetals

Metallic character generally declines across the period.

### 37. Which property generally increases from lithium to potassium?
**Correct answer:** A. Reactivity with water

Group 1 reactivity generally increases down the group.

### 38. Which property generally decreases from fluorine to iodine?
**Correct answer:** B. Reactivity

Halogen reactivity generally decreases down the group.

### 39. A line spectrum contains discrete wavelengths because:
**Correct answer:** C. Electron energies occur in allowed states and transitions have specific energy differences

Specific transitions produce photons with specific energies.

### 40. Which statement avoids overstating a model?
**Correct answer:** D. The shell model predicts useful outer-electron patterns but does not depict literal fixed paths

The shell model is useful but not literal.

## Unit 07

### 41. Which force is central to ionic bonding?
**Correct answer:** A. Electrostatic attraction between opposite charges

Ionic bonding is electrostatic attraction.

### 42. Which statement best describes a covalent bond?
**Correct answer:** B. A shared electron pair between atoms

The introductory covalent model uses shared electron pairs.

### 43. What formula results from K⁺ and O²⁻?
**Correct answer:** C. K₂O

Two K⁺ ions balance one O²⁻ ion.

### 44. What formula results from Al³⁺ and SO₄²⁻?
**Correct answer:** D. Al₂(SO₄)₃

Two Al³⁺ give +6 and three sulfate ions give −6.

### 45. Which substance is expected to conduct when molten but not when solid?
**Correct answer:** A. Sodium chloride

Molten ionic compounds have mobile ions.

### 46. Which property is characteristic of many simple molecular substances?
**Correct answer:** B. Low melting and boiling points

Weak intermolecular attractions often give low phase-change temperatures.

### 47. Why is diamond hard?
**Correct answer:** C. Its atoms form a three-dimensional covalent network

Many strong bonds extend through the network.

### 48. Which statement distinguishes bond polarity from molecular polarity?
**Correct answer:** D. A molecule’s shape can cause bond dipoles to cancel or reinforce

Whole-molecule polarity depends on both bond dipoles and arrangement.

### 49. Using C=12 and O=16, Mr of CO₂ is:
**Correct answer:** A. 44

12 + 2(16) = 44.

### 50. In 2H₂O, the coefficient 2 indicates:
**Correct answer:** B. Two water molecules or formula units in the represented ratio

The coefficient multiplies the entire formula.

### 51. Which equation balances aluminium oxide formation?
**Correct answer:** C. 4Al + 3O₂ → 2Al₂O₃

Four Al and six O atoms appear on both sides.

### 52. Why is mass conserved in a closed ordinary chemical reaction?
**Correct answer:** D. The same atoms are rearranged into products

Chemical reactions rearrange existing atoms.

### 53. A gas-producing reaction loses measured mass in an open beaker. The best explanation is:
**Correct answer:** A. Gas left the balance system

The escaped product is no longer included in the measured system.

### 54. Which observation alone is least sufficient to prove a chemical reaction?
**Correct answer:** B. A temperature change without a control

Physical processes can also change temperature.

### 55. CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O is classified as:
**Correct answer:** C. Combustion

A hydrocarbon reacting with oxygen to form CO₂ and water is combustion.

### 56. Which sign belongs to an exothermic ΔH?
**Correct answer:** D. Negative

Products are lower in enthalpy than reactants under this convention.

### 57. Which statement about a catalyst is accurate?
**Correct answer:** A. It lowers activation energy but does not change ΔH

Catalysts alter the pathway, not overall energy difference.

### 58. Why can higher temperature increase rate?
**Correct answer:** B. More particles exceed activation energy

The energy distribution shifts so more collisions can succeed.

### 59. At dynamic equilibrium:
**Correct answer:** C. Forward and reverse rates are equal

Equal rates maintain constant concentrations.

### 60. Which action is prohibited?
**Correct answer:** D. Mixing household cleaners to look for a reaction

Household cleaners can generate toxic gases or corrosive mixtures.

## Constructed Response 1 — Unit 05

- Population percentage change: `(680 − 800) ÷ 800 × 100 = −15%`, a 15% decrease.
- Both sites have richness 4 and total abundance 16.
- Site A is maximally even for the supplied counts and has higher diversity; Site B is dominated by one species.
- A direct observation may be the recorded counts or measured decrease. A causal statement such as habitat loss caused the decline requires additional comparative evidence and controls.
- A conservation action must name a measurable outcome, such as habitat restoration followed by annual abundance, richness, evenness, recruitment, or survival measurements.
- Microevolution: measured allele-frequency change within populations across generations.
- Speciation evidence: reproductive isolation, reduced gene flow, assortative mating, or stable lineage separation.
- Macroevolution: patterns at or above species level, including speciation, extinction, diversification, and deep-history reconstruction.
- A fossil dimension is a direct measurement; placement on a phylogenetic tree is a historical inference tested against multiple evidence lines.
- Students are scored on accuracy, evidence classification, reasoning and fair representation—not personal affirmation of creation, evolution, intelligent design, evolutionary creation, naturalism, or an undecided position.

## Constructed Response 4 — Integration Bridge

- A valid carbon path may be: atmospheric CO₂ → plant glucose/biomass through photosynthesis → consumer tissue or respiration products → decomposer/fuel molecules → CO₂ through respiration, decomposition or combustion.
- Photosynthesis may be represented as `6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂` with light energy input.
- Methane combustion: `CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O`; atoms are conserved.
- Matter cycles because atoms are reused and rearranged. Energy flows because chemical energy is transferred and transformed, with substantial transfer to thermal energy and waste at each trophic step.
- Less usable energy is available at higher trophic levels because organisms respire, move, maintain homeostasis, release heat and produce unconsumed or undigested material.
- Chemical potential energy is associated with molecular arrangements; thermal energy is transferred to surroundings during exothermic processes.
- Conservation of energy does not mean all energy remains available for biological work; energy quality and distribution change.
- Direct measurement examples: temperature, CO₂ concentration, biomass, isotope abundance or population count. Model examples: food web, particle diagram or balanced equation. Inference examples: a reconstructed carbon pathway or causal ecological explanation.
