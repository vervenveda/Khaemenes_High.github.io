# Unit 12 Definitive Answer Key

Course: International Integrated Science 9 (`KH-SCI-IIS9`)

## Decision-Matrix Check

### 1. Which is a design criterion?
**Correct answer:** A. Filter removes at least 90% of particles

A criterion is a measurable performance target.

### 2. Which is a hard constraint?
**Correct answer:** B. Must not expose users to live mains voltage

A non-negotiable safety boundary is a hard constraint.

### 3. Criterion weights are 40, 35, and 25. Their total is:
**Correct answer:** C. 100

40+35+25=100.

### 4. A concept scores 8/10 on a criterion weighted 30%. Contribution is:
**Correct answer:** D. 2.4 points

8×0.30=2.4 points on a 10-point total scale.

### 5. A concept has the highest weighted score but fails a legal constraint. It should:
**Correct answer:** A. Be rejected or redesigned before selection

Hard constraints cannot be overridden by preference scores.

### 6. Sensitivity analysis changes:
**Correct answer:** B. Reasonable weights or assumptions to test ranking stability

Sensitivity analysis tests whether conclusions are robust to reasonable assumptions.

### 7. Why document who selected criterion weights?
**Correct answer:** C. Because weights encode priorities and value judgments

Weights reflect priorities and should be transparent.

### 8. A decision matrix cannot by itself prove:
**Correct answer:** D. That the top-ranked design will work safely in reality

Testing and evidence are still required.

## Prototype Testing Check

### 1. Trials are 8, 10, and 12 units. Mean is:
**Correct answer:** B. 10

(8+10+12)/3=10.

### 2. Trials are 8, 10, and 12 units. Range is:
**Correct answer:** C. 4

Range=12−8=4.

### 3. Baseline time is 20 s and prototype time is 15 s; lower is better. Percent improvement is:
**Correct answer:** D. 25%

(20−15)/20×100=25%.

### 4. A prototype succeeds in 9 of 10 trials. Observed success rate is:
**Correct answer:** A. 90%

9/10×100=90%.

### 5. Which improves validity?
**Correct answer:** B. Using the same procedure and controlled conditions for each concept

Controlled procedures support fair comparison.

### 6. A scale reads 2 g high on every measurement. This is mainly:
**Correct answer:** C. Systematic bias

A consistent offset is systematic error.

### 7. A requirement is at least 50 N. Test means are A=48 N, B=52 N, C=55 N. Which meet the mean threshold?
**Correct answer:** D. B and C

B and C meet or exceed 50 N.

### 8. The best result among three tested settings is:
**Correct answer:** A. The best observed result within the tested settings

Untested settings may perform better.

### 5. A hazard is:

Hazard describes potential harm.

### 6. Residual risk is:
**Correct answer:** A. Risk remaining after controls

Controls reduce but may not eliminate risk.

### 7. A resilient system can:
**Correct answer:** B. Anticipate, withstand, adapt, and recover

Resilience includes preparation, response, adaptation, and recovery.

### 8. An ordinal 1–5 risk matrix provides:
**Correct answer:** C. A transparent screening priority, not exact probability

Ordinal scores support prioritization but are not precise probabilities.
