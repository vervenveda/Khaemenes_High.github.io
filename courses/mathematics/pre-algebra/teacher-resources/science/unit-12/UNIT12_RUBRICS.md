# Unit 12 Rubrics

## Sustainable Engineering Capstone — 48 Points

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Need, users, stakeholders and boundary | Missing | Confused | Partial | Clear | Clear and independently verified |
| Criteria, constraints and requirements | Missing | Vague | Some measurable | Complete | Prioritized, verifiable and sensitivity-tested |
| Science basis and sources | Missing | Unsupported | Limited | Accurate | Multiple independent sources and uncertainty |
| Concepts and decision process | Missing | One concept | Several concepts | Matrix and selection | Constraints, sensitivity and alternatives evaluated |
| Model, prototype and test plan | Missing | Unsafe or invalid | Partial | Fair and safe | Calibrated, repeated and limitation-aware |
| Data, calculations and uncertainty | Missing | Major errors | Partial | Correct | Correct with reasonableness and bias analysis |
| Life cycle and circularity | Missing | Use phase only | Several stages | Consistent boundary | Functional unit, credits and uncertainty evaluated |
| Risk, reliability and resilience | Missing | Generic | Some risks | Quantified screening and controls | Residual risk, redundancy and recovery evaluated |
| Iteration and optimization | Missing | No revision | Cosmetic change | Evidence-based revision | Versioned sensitivity and stopping rule |
| Accessibility, equity and ethics | Missing | Exclusion unaddressed | Generic | Specific | Co-designed, privacy- and justice-aware |
| Communication and documentation | Incomplete | Confusing | Understandable | Clear | Accessible, cited, transparent and reproducible |
| Monitoring, maintenance and governance | Missing | Vague | Partial | Measurable | Roles, triggers, authorization and end-of-life included |

Suggested mastery: **38/48**.

## Constructed Responses — 10 Points Each

- Scientific and engineering accuracy: 2
- Calculations, units and data: 2
- Evidence, models and uncertainty: 2
- Criteria, tradeoffs, iteration and risk: 2
- Sustainability, accessibility, ethics and communication: 2

- Prototype Testing Check: 7/8
- Life-Cycle Check: 7/8
- Risk & Reliability Check: 7/8
- Mastery Quiz: 16/20
- Objective Unit Assessment: 24/30
