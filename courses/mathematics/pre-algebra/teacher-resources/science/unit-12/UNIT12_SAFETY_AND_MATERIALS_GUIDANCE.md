# Unit 12 Safety and Materials Guidance

## Prohibited without qualified institutional authorization

- Mains electricity, high voltage, exposed batteries, high-current shorts, or grid connection
- Combustion, engines, pyrotechnics, pressure vessels, compressed gases, or projectile launchers
- Human structural loading, vehicle or road testing, drones, lasers, radio transmitters, or weapons
- Medical diagnosis, treatment, body-fluid testing, ingestion, skin exposure, or human-subject experimentation
- Culturing unknown microorganisms, environmental pathogens, genetic modification, or animal testing
- Hazardous chemicals, unknown materials, acids, solvents, fumes, dust, or contaminated water/soil
- Deployment into drinking water, food systems, public infrastructure, emergency systems, or surveillance
- Personal-data collection without consent, minimization, security, and deletion plans

## Approved approaches

- Cardboard, paper, craft wood, low-force mechanisms, sealed low-voltage educational components, simulations, CAD, spreadsheets, public datasets, mock-ups, diagrams, and nonfunctional scale models
- Tests with small masses, low forces, eye protection when appropriate, stable work areas, adult approval, and clear stop conditions
- Remote stakeholder research, anonymized surveys when approved, and supplied case data
