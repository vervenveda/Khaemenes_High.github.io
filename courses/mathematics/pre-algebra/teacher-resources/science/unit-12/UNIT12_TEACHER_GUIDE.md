# Unit 12 Teacher and Parent-Educator Guide

## Unit Identity

**Course:** International Integrated Science 9 (`KH-SCI-IIS9`)  
**Unit:** 12 — Sustainable Engineering Capstone  
**Duration:** Three weeks, fifteen lessons, Weeks 34–36

## Required Evidence

- Fifteen lessons
- Four investigation checks at 7/8
- Sustainable Engineering Capstone
- Mastery Quiz, 16/20
- Objective Assessment, 24/30
- Two constructed responses
- Corrections and 150-word final reflection

## Safeguards

- Criteria are desired measurable outcomes; constraints are limits or non-negotiable requirements.
- Weighted scores cannot override safety, law, ethics, accessibility, or feasibility constraints.
- A prototype answers a question and is not automatically a deployable product.
- The best tested setting is not proof of a global optimum.
- Mean performance without spread, sample size, and conditions is incomplete.
- A life-cycle comparison needs a common functional unit and consistent boundary.
- Recycled content, recyclability, recovery, and circularity are different claims.
- Ordinal risk scores are screening tools, not precise probabilities.
- Series reliability multiplication assumes independent components and that every component is required.
- Residual risk must be documented and accepted by an authorized decision-maker.
- Sustainability includes environmental, social, cultural, accessibility, health, labor, and economic considerations.
- A student capstone does not certify a regulated or safety-critical system.
