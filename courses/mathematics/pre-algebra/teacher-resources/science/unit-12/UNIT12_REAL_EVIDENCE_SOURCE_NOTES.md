# Unit 12 Real-Evidence Source Notes

Use official standards and public-agency sources for design expectations, materials life cycles, reliability and resilience. Record source date, jurisdiction, method, functional unit, boundary, units, assumptions, uncertainty, stakeholders, and conflicts.

Evidence classes:
- direct measurement
- calibrated test result
- calculated metric
- mathematical model or simulation
- prototype behavior
- life-cycle inventory estimate
- ordinal risk screening
- stakeholder preference or requirement
- legal or standards constraint
- projection, scenario or policy judgment

Do not present a classroom score as certification, legal compliance, exact probability, professional engineering approval, or guaranteed field performance.
