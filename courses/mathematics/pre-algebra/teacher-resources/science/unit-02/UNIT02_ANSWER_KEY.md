# Unit 02 Definitive Answer Key

## Mastery Quiz

### 1. Which statement is part of modern cell theory?
**Correct answer:** A. All living organisms are made of one or more cells.

Living organisms are composed of one or more cells.

### 2. Which observation most directly supports the conclusion that a specimen is cellular?
**Correct answer:** B. Repeated microscope views show membrane-bounded units with internal contents.

Repeated observation of membrane-bounded units is direct evidence of cellular organization.

### 3. Which structure is present in both prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells?
**Correct answer:** A. Ribosome

All cells use ribosomes to build proteins.

### 4. Which feature distinguishes a eukaryotic cell from a prokaryotic cell?
**Correct answer:** C. DNA enclosed within a membrane-bound nucleus

Eukaryotic DNA is enclosed within a membrane-bound nucleus.

### 5. Which organelle is the main site of aerobic cellular respiration in eukaryotic cells?
**Correct answer:** A. Mitochondrion

Most stages of aerobic cellular respiration occur in mitochondria.

### 6. What is the primary role of ribosomes?
**Correct answer:** A. Protein synthesis

Ribosomes assemble amino acids into proteins.

### 7. Which statement about plant cells is correct?
**Correct answer:** C. Photosynthetic plant cells commonly contain chloroplasts and mitochondria.

Photosynthetic plant cells commonly contain both chloroplasts and mitochondria.

### 8. An image is 30 mm long and the specimen is 0.06 mm long. What is the magnification?
**Correct answer:** C. ×500

30 ÷ 0.06 = 500.

### 9. Which conversion is correct?
**Correct answer:** C. 1 mm = 1000 µm

One millimeter equals one thousand micrometers.

### 10. What does selectively permeable mean?
**Correct answer:** B. The membrane allows some substances to cross more readily than others.

Passage depends on properties such as size, charge, concentration, and transport proteins.

### 11. Diffusion is best described as:
**Correct answer:** A. Net movement of particles from higher concentration to lower concentration due to random motion

Diffusion is net movement down a concentration gradient arising from random particle motion.

### 12. Osmosis specifically refers to the net movement of:
**Correct answer:** B. Water through a selectively permeable membrane

Osmosis is net water movement through a selectively permeable membrane.

### 13. Which process can move a substance against its concentration gradient?
**Correct answer:** C. Active transport

Active transport uses cellular energy and transport proteins.

### 14. As a cube-shaped cell becomes larger, what generally happens to its surface-area-to-volume ratio?
**Correct answer:** B. It decreases

Volume increases faster than surface area.

### 15. Why is a higher surface-area-to-volume ratio often advantageous for a cell?
**Correct answer:** A. It increases exchange surface relative to the material that must be supplied.

More membrane surface relative to volume can support faster exchange per unit volume.

### 16. Which adaptation most directly helps a red blood cell carry oxygen?
**Correct answer:** B. A biconcave shape and abundant hemoglobin

The biconcave shape increases exchange area, and hemoglobin binds oxygen.

### 17. Which sequence shows increasing biological organization in a multicellular organism?
**Correct answer:** B. Cell → tissue → organ → organ system

Specialized cells form tissues; tissues form organs; organs cooperate in systems.

### 18. Which statement correctly compares photosynthesis and cellular respiration?
**Correct answer:** B. Photosynthesis stores energy in organic molecules; cellular respiration releases usable chemical energy from organic molecules.

Photosynthesis stores light energy; respiration transfers energy from organic molecules to usable cellular forms.

### 19. Which is a limitation of a typical textbook cell diagram?
**Correct answer:** A. It may show structures at unrealistic relative sizes and fixed positions.

Diagrams simplify and reorganize structures for clarity.

### 20. A microscope image shows a cell wall and chloroplasts. Which conclusion is best supported?
**Correct answer:** A. The cell is a photosynthetic eukaryotic cell.

A cell wall plus chloroplasts supports classification as a photosynthetic eukaryotic cell.

## Objective Unit Assessment

### 1. Which statement is NOT part of modern cell theory?
**Correct answer:** D. Every cell contains a membrane-bound nucleus.

Prokaryotic cells do not contain a membrane-bound nucleus.

### 2. Why did improved microscopes strengthen cell theory?
**Correct answer:** A. They allowed repeated observation of cells and cell division in many organisms.

Improved microscopes provided repeatable observations of cellular structure and reproduction.

### 3. Which entity is acellular rather than a complete cell?
**Correct answer:** C. Virus particle

Viruses are acellular particles that depend on host cells for replication.

### 4. Which feature is found in a typical bacterial cell?
**Correct answer:** A. Circular DNA in a nucleoid region

Typical bacteria have DNA in a nucleoid region.

### 5. Which pair is present in all known cells?
**Correct answer:** A. Plasma membrane and ribosomes

All cells have a plasma membrane and ribosomes.

### 6. Which statement avoids an inaccurate hierarchy between prokaryotes and eukaryotes?
**Correct answer:** B. Prokaryotes and eukaryotes have different cellular organizations adapted to their ways of life.

Both groups are highly evolved and differ in organization.

### 7. Which organelle modifies, sorts, and packages many proteins in eukaryotic cells?
**Correct answer:** A. Golgi apparatus

The Golgi apparatus modifies, sorts, and packages many proteins and lipids.

### 8. Which structure contains most of a eukaryotic cell's DNA?
**Correct answer:** A. Nucleus

Most eukaryotic DNA is enclosed in the nucleus.

### 9. Which structure provides rigid support outside the plasma membrane of plant cells?
**Correct answer:** A. Cellulose cell wall

The cellulose cell wall supports and protects plant cells.

### 10. What is a central role of a large plant-cell vacuole?
**Correct answer:** A. Maintaining internal water storage and turgor pressure

The central vacuole stores water and solutes and helps maintain turgor.

### 11. A 24 mm image was produced at ×400 magnification. What is the actual specimen length?
**Correct answer:** A. 0.06 mm

24 ÷ 400 = 0.06 mm.

### 12. What is 0.06 mm expressed in micrometers?
**Correct answer:** C. 60 µm

0.06 mm × 1000 = 60 µm.

### 13. A ×10 eyepiece and ×40 objective produce what total magnification?
**Correct answer:** C. ×400

10 × 40 = 400.

### 14. Which observation method gives the strongest evidence about a cell's three-dimensional internal structure?
**Correct answer:** B. Multiple images or sections from different depths and orientations

Multiple sections or orientations provide stronger three-dimensional evidence.

### 15. Which factor can directly influence whether an ion crosses a cell membrane?
**Correct answer:** A. Availability and state of a suitable membrane transport protein

Charged ions generally require specific channels or carriers.

### 16. At diffusion equilibrium, particles:
**Correct answer:** B. Continue random motion, but there is no net concentration change.

Particles continue random motion while net movement is balanced.

### 17. Which change would generally increase the diffusion rate of dye in water?
**Correct answer:** A. Increasing temperature within the safe tested range

Higher temperature generally increases particle motion.

### 18. A plant cell is placed in a solution more dilute than its cell contents. What is the expected net water movement?
**Correct answer:** A. Into the cell

Water tends to move into the more concentrated cell contents.

### 19. A potato sample changes from 5.0 g to 4.5 g. What is the percent mass change?
**Correct answer:** A. −10%

(4.5 − 5.0) ÷ 5.0 × 100 = −10%.

### 20. Which condition is required for active transport?
**Correct answer:** A. A cellular energy supply and suitable transport proteins

Active transport depends on energy and specific membrane proteins.

### 21. Which example is best explained by active transport?
**Correct answer:** A. Mineral ions accumulated by root cells against a concentration gradient

Root cells can accumulate mineral ions against their gradient.

### 22. What is the surface-area-to-volume ratio of a cube with side length 2 cm?
**Correct answer:** A. 3:1

Surface area 24 and volume 8 give 3:1.

### 23. Which adaptation increases exchange surface without greatly increasing cell volume?
**Correct answer:** A. Folds or projections in the membrane

Folds and projections increase membrane surface area.

### 24. Which structure-function relationship is correct?
**Correct answer:** A. Root hair cell — long projection increases absorption surface

A root hair cell's projection increases absorption surface.

### 25. Why can specialized cells in a multicellular organism depend on one another?
**Correct answer:** A. They divide labor and exchange materials through tissues and systems.

Specialization divides functions and creates interdependence.

### 26. Which example is an organ?
**Correct answer:** A. Heart

The heart is an organ composed of multiple tissues.

### 27. Which substances are reactants in photosynthesis?
**Correct answer:** A. Carbon dioxide and water

Photosynthesis uses carbon dioxide and water with light energy.

### 28. Which statement about plant cellular respiration is correct?
**Correct answer:** A. Plant cells perform cellular respiration as well as photosynthesis.

Plant cells respire continuously; photosynthetic cells also photosynthesize in suitable light.

### 29. Why should a model include a stated limitation?
**Correct answer:** A. Every model emphasizes some features and omits or simplifies others.

Models are selective representations.

### 30. A student claims every plant cell contains chloroplasts after viewing one leaf cell. What is the best evaluation?
**Correct answer:** A. The claim is too broad because one leaf cell does not represent all plant cell types.

One leaf cell cannot justify a universal claim about every plant cell type.
