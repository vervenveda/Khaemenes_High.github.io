# Unit 02 Teacher and Parent-Educator Guide

## Purpose
Unit 02 develops cell biology as evidence, scale, structure–function relationships, transport, mathematical modeling, and communication. It avoids teaching organelles as an isolated vocabulary list.

## Three-week sequence
- Week 1: cell theory, microscopy, scale, prokaryotic and eukaryotic organization
- Week 2: membranes, diffusion, osmosis, active transport, and cell-size constraints
- Week 3: specialization, biological hierarchy, cellular energy, model evaluation, and assessment

## Required evidence
- Fifteen lesson records
- Virtual microscopy laboratory
- Diffusion investigation or equivalent dataset pathway
- Membrane transport dataset analysis
- Surface-area-to-volume model
- Cell model performance task
- Mastery quiz and corrections
- Objective assessment and corrections
- Two constructed responses
- Final reflection

## Pathways
### Foundation
Use guided diagrams, conversion frames, partially completed tables, and sentence starters. Oral explanations may accompany a written evidence summary.

### Core
Complete all calculations, graphs, analyses, model explanations, and stated limitations.

### Extended
Quantify uncertainty, compare models, examine nonstandard cell examples, relate membrane transport to electrochemical gradients, and evaluate relationships.

## Safety and access
- Virtual and provided-data pathways are complete alternatives.
- Use warm tap water only in the diffusion activity.
- Any cutting of fresh tissue requires adult supervision and may be omitted.
- Do not culture unknown microorganisms.
- Do not collect human tissue or bodily fluids.

## Assessment thresholds
- Virtual microscopy: 5/6
- Mastery quiz: 16/20
- Objective assessment: 24/30
- Cell model and constructed responses: rubric-scored
