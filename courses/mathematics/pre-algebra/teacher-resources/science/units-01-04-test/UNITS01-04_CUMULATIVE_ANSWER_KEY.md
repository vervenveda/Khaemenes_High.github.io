## Unit 01

### 1. Which question is directly testable with measurements?
**Correct answer:** B. How does paper-helicopter rotor length affect mean fall time when paper type and drop height are controlled?

Rotor length can be changed and mean fall time measured while conditions are controlled.

### 2. A student changes water temperature and measures how far dye spreads in 60 seconds. What is the independent variable?
**Correct answer:** C. Water temperature

The independent variable is the deliberately changed water temperature.

### 3. In an investigation of ramp height and toy-car travel distance, which factor should be controlled?
**Correct answer:** A. The toy car used in every trial

Using the same car helps isolate the effect of ramp height.

### 4. Why are repeated trials useful?
**Correct answer:** D. They reveal variation and improve confidence in an estimate such as the mean.

Repeated trials show variation and strengthen estimates of typical results.

### 5. Measurements of 9.8 cm, 9.8 cm, 9.9 cm, and 9.8 cm are best described as:
**Correct answer:** B. Highly precise

The values are close to one another, showing precision; accuracy needs a trusted value.

### 6. Which statement is an operational definition of seedling growth?
**Correct answer:** C. Growth is the change in stem height, in centimeters, from Day 1 to Day 14.

An operational definition states exactly how a variable is measured.

### 7. What is the mean of 1.4 s, 1.5 s, 1.5 s, 1.6 s, and 1.5 s?
**Correct answer:** A. 1.5 s

The sum is 7.5 s; 7.5 divided by 5 is 1.5 s.

### 8. What is the range of 2.1 cm, 2.3 cm, 2.2 cm, 2.5 cm, and 2.4 cm?
**Correct answer:** D. 0.4 cm

Range equals 2.5 minus 2.1, or 0.4 cm.

### 9. Which graph is generally most suitable for showing the relationship between two continuous numerical variables?
**Correct answer:** B. Scatter plot

A scatter plot displays paired values for two continuous numerical variables.

### 10. One result is far from all repeated measurements. What is the best first response?
**Correct answer:** C. Check the method and equipment, repeat if possible, and document the result.

Anomalies should be investigated, repeated when appropriate, and reported transparently.

### 11. An observational study finds that longer sleep is associated with higher test scores. Which conclusion is justified?
**Correct answer:** A. Sleep duration and test score were associated in the observed sample.

An observational association does not by itself establish causation.

### 12. Which statement is reasoning in a claim-evidence-reasoning explanation?
**Correct answer:** D. Longer rotors can increase air resistance, so the longer measured fall times support the claim.

Reasoning connects evidence to a claim using a scientific principle.

### 13. A graph axis begins at 95 and displays values from 96 to 100. What should a reader do?
**Correct answer:** B. Read the scale carefully because visual differences may appear exaggerated.

A truncated axis can visually exaggerate small numerical differences.

### 14. Which feature most strengthens a causal experiment?
**Correct answer:** C. Deliberately changing one factor while controlling alternative explanations

Controlled manipulation helps isolate the effect of the independent variable.

### 15. A planned method creates a risk that cannot be reasonably controlled with available equipment. What should the investigator do?
**Correct answer:** A. Revise or replace the method before beginning.

An unsafe method must be revised or replaced before work begins.

## Unit 02

### 16. Which statement is part of modern cell theory?
**Correct answer:** C. All living organisms are made of one or more cells.

All living organisms are composed of one or more cells.

### 17. Which pair is present in all known cells?
**Correct answer:** B. Plasma membrane and ribosomes

All cells have a plasma membrane and ribosomes.

### 18. Which feature distinguishes a typical eukaryotic cell from a typical prokaryotic cell?
**Correct answer:** D. DNA enclosed within a membrane-bound nucleus

Typical eukaryotic cells enclose most DNA in a membrane-bound nucleus.

### 19. Which organelle modifies, sorts, and packages many proteins in a eukaryotic cell?
**Correct answer:** A. Golgi apparatus

The Golgi apparatus modifies, sorts, and packages many proteins and lipids.

### 20. Which statement about photosynthetic plant cells is correct?
**Correct answer:** C. They commonly contain both chloroplasts and mitochondria.

Photosynthetic plant cells commonly contain chloroplasts and mitochondria.

### 21. An image is 25 mm long and the specimen is 0.05 mm long. What is the magnification?
**Correct answer:** B. x500

Magnification equals image size divided by actual size: 25 divided by 0.05 equals 500.

### 22. What is 0.075 mm expressed in micrometers?
**Correct answer:** A. 75 micrometers

0.075 mm multiplied by 1000 equals 75 micrometers.

### 23. What does selectively permeable mean?
**Correct answer:** D. Some substances cross more readily than others.

Passage depends on properties such as size, charge, gradients, and transport proteins.

### 24. Diffusion is best described as:
**Correct answer:** C. Net movement of particles from higher to lower concentration due to random motion

Diffusion is net movement down a concentration gradient due to random motion.

### 25. A plant cell is placed in a solution more dilute than its cell contents. What is the expected net water movement?
**Correct answer:** B. Into the cell

Water tends to move into the more concentrated cell contents.

### 26. Which process can move mineral ions into a root cell against their concentration gradient?
**Correct answer:** A. Active transport

Active transport uses cellular energy and suitable membrane proteins.

### 27. What is the surface-area-to-volume ratio of a cube with side length 3 cm?
**Correct answer:** D. 2:1

Surface area is 54 and volume is 27, so the ratio is 2:1.

### 28. Why is a high surface-area-to-volume ratio often advantageous for a cell?
**Correct answer:** C. It provides more exchange surface relative to the material that must be supplied.

More membrane surface relative to volume supports exchange.

### 29. Which sequence shows increasing biological organization?
**Correct answer:** B. Cell to tissue to organ to organ system

Cells form tissues, tissues form organs, and organs cooperate in organ systems.

### 30. Which statement correctly compares photosynthesis and cellular respiration?
**Correct answer:** A. Photosynthesis stores energy in organic molecules; respiration releases usable chemical energy from organic molecules.

Photosynthesis stores light energy; respiration transfers energy from organic molecules.

## Unit 03

### 31. Homeostasis is best described as:
**Correct answer:** D. Maintaining internal conditions within functional ranges despite change

Homeostasis is dynamic regulation within functional ranges.

### 32. Which sequence correctly represents a basic feedback pathway?
**Correct answer:** B. Stimulus to receptor to coordinator to effector to response

A change is detected, coordinated, and acted on by an effector.

### 33. In negative feedback, the response generally:
**Correct answer:** A. Opposes the initial change

Negative feedback counteracts deviation from a functional range.

### 34. Which process is commonly modeled as positive feedback?
**Correct answer:** C. Blood clotting at a damaged vessel

Clotting amplifies locally until the vessel break is sealed.

### 35. Which route carries blood from the right ventricle to the left atrium?
**Correct answer:** B. Pulmonary artery to lung capillaries to pulmonary veins

The pulmonary artery carries blood to lungs and pulmonary veins return it to the left atrium.

### 36. Which statement correctly defines arteries and veins?
**Correct answer:** D. Arteries carry blood away from the heart; veins carry blood toward the heart.

Direction relative to the heart defines arteries and veins.

### 37. Which alveolar feature most directly supports rapid gas exchange?
**Correct answer:** A. Large surface area and thin exchange surfaces

Large area and short diffusion distance support rapid exchange.

### 38. How do breathing and cellular respiration differ?
**Correct answer:** C. Breathing moves air; cellular respiration transfers chemical energy in cells.

Ventilation moves air; cellular respiration transfers energy in cells.

### 39. Heart rate rises from 64 beats per minute to 96 beats per minute. What is the percent increase?
**Correct answer:** B. 50%

The increase is 32; 32 divided by 64 times 100 equals 50%.

### 40. Chemical digestion mainly:
**Correct answer:** D. Breaks large insoluble molecules into smaller soluble molecules

Smaller soluble molecules can be absorbed.

### 41. Which organ produces bile?
**Correct answer:** A. Liver

The liver produces bile.

### 42. During dehydration, increased ADH generally causes:
**Correct answer:** C. More water reabsorption and lower urine volume

ADH helps conserve water by increasing reabsorption.

### 43. Which response helps increase heat loss when body temperature rises?
**Correct answer:** B. Sweating and increased skin blood flow

Evaporation and skin blood flow help transfer heat away.

### 44. Which statement correctly compares insulin and glucagon?
**Correct answer:** D. They have opposing regulatory effects on blood glucose.

Insulin generally lowers elevated glucose; glucagon helps raise low glucose.

### 45. Which comparison is accurate?
**Correct answer:** A. Nervous signals are often rapid and targeted; endocrine signals often begin more slowly and last longer.

The systems differ in route, speed, duration, and targets.

## Unit 04

### 46. Which statement correctly relates a gene to DNA?
**Correct answer:** C. A gene is a DNA region involved in a functional product or regulation.

Genes are DNA regions involved in functional products or regulation.

### 47. An allele is:
**Correct answer:** A. A variant form of a gene

Alleles are variant forms of the same gene.

### 48. During which phase is DNA copied before mitosis?
**Correct answer:** D. S phase

DNA replication occurs during S phase.

### 49. Mitosis followed by cytokinesis in a typical diploid body cell usually produces:
**Correct answer:** B. Two daughter cells with the same chromosome number as the parent cell

Mitosis maintains chromosome number in the two daughter cells.

### 50. Cells with similar DNA can develop different functions mainly because:
**Correct answer:** C. Different sets of genes are expressed in different cells.

Regulated gene expression supports cell specialization.

### 51. Asexual offspring are generally:
**Correct answer:** A. Genetically very similar to one parent except for mutation and other changes

Asexual reproduction usually copies one parent but variation can still arise.

### 52. Why is meiosis called a reduction division?
**Correct answer:** D. It halves the chromosome number in the resulting cells.

Meiosis produces haploid cells from a diploid starting cell.

### 53. Homologous chromosomes are:
**Correct answer:** B. A pair carrying corresponding genes, one inherited through each parent

Homologues carry corresponding loci but may carry different alleles.

### 54. Which process creates new combinations of existing alleles during meiosis?
**Correct answer:** C. Crossing over

Crossing over creates recombinant chromatids.

### 55. Independent assortment refers to:
**Correct answer:** A. Random orientation and separation of homologous chromosome pairs

Homologous pairs can orient independently, generating chromosome combinations.

### 56. What does fertilization restore in a typical diploid life cycle?
**Correct answer:** D. The diploid chromosome number

Fusion of two haploid gametes restores diploidy.

### 57. A dominant allele is one that:
**Correct answer:** B. Is expressed in the heterozygous phenotype under the stated model

Dominance describes heterozygous expression, not value or frequency.

### 58. For the cross Aa x Aa, what is the expected probability of genotype aa?
**Correct answer:** C. 25%

The four combinations are AA, Aa, Aa, and aa.

### 59. Red RR flowers crossed with white WW flowers produce pink RW offspring. This pattern is:
**Correct answer:** A. Incomplete dominance

The heterozygote has an intermediate phenotype.

### 60. Which statement is scientifically accurate?
**Correct answer:** D. Mutation effects and phenotype depend on biological and environmental context.

Mutation effects vary and phenotypes often reflect genetic and environmental interactions.
