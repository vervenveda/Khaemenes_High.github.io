# Units 01–04 Cumulative Benchmark Administration Guide

## Purpose

This benchmark measures cumulative mastery after Unit 04, covering the first twelve instructional weeks after readiness.

## Suggested administration

- Objective section: 60–90 minutes
- Constructed responses: 60–90 minutes
- One extended session or two sessions
- Calculator and blank paper permitted
- Accessibility supports may include read-aloud, additional time, enlarged print, keyboard input, breaks, and language scaffolds without changing the scientific reasoning assessed

## Security

Keep the answer key outside student-facing directories. Student pages contain no answer-key links.

## Scoring

Objective: 60 points. Constructed: four responses at 10 points each. Recommended thresholds: 48/60 objective and 80/100 overall. Record each unit's 15-point diagnostic score. Permit corrections and retakes.

## Privacy and safety

Do not require personal health, family medical, ancestry, paternity, reproductive, or identifiable genetic information. Use the supplied fictional datasets. Homeostasis data are not diagnostic.
