# Units 01–04 Cumulative Benchmark Rubric

## Objective section — 60 points

One point per correct answer. Objective mastery: 48/60. Each unit contributes 15 diagnostic points. Corrections and retakes are permitted.

## Constructed responses — 10 points each

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Scientific accuracy | Missing or fundamentally incorrect | Partly accurate | Accurate and complete |
| Calculation or model | Missing | Partly correct | Correct, shown, and labeled |
| Evidence | Unsupported | Some relevant evidence | Specific and sufficient evidence |
| Reasoning | Missing or circular | Partial mechanism | Clear mechanism links evidence and claim |
| Limitations, safety, privacy, or ethics | Missing | Generic | Specific and appropriate |

## Final score

Objective 60 + constructed 40 = 100 points. Recommended mastery requires at least 48/60 objective, all four responses scored, and at least 80/100 overall.
