# Unit 04 Definitive Answer Key

## Mastery Quiz

### 1. Which statement correctly relates a gene to DNA?
**Correct answer:** B. A gene is a segment of DNA involved in a functional product or regulation.

A gene is a functional or regulatory DNA region.

### 2. An allele is:
**Correct answer:** C. A variant form of a gene

Alleles are variant forms of a gene.

### 3. During which part of the cell cycle is DNA copied?
**Correct answer:** A. S phase of interphase

DNA replication occurs during S phase.

### 4. Mitosis followed by cytokinesis in a typical diploid body cell usually produces:
**Correct answer:** B. Two cells with the same chromosome number as the parent

Chromosome number is maintained.

### 5. Cells with similar DNA can have different functions mainly because:
**Correct answer:** B. Different genes are expressed

Differential gene expression supports specialization.

### 6. A checkpoint can:
**Correct answer:** B. Delay division when problems are detected

Checkpoints monitor conditions but are imperfect.

### 7. Asexual offspring are generally:
**Correct answer:** B. Very similar to one parent except for mutation and other changes

Asexual reproduction copies one parent's genetic information.

### 8. Meiosis is a reduction division because:
**Correct answer:** A. It halves chromosome number

Meiosis produces haploid cells.

### 9. Homologous chromosomes are:
**Correct answer:** B. A pair carrying corresponding genes, one from each parent

Homologues carry corresponding loci and may have different alleles.

### 10. Which process creates new allele combinations during meiosis?
**Correct answer:** A. Crossing over

Crossing over recombines chromosome segments.

### 11. Independent assortment refers to:
**Correct answer:** A. Random orientation and separation of homologous pairs

Pair orientation creates chromosome combinations.

### 12. Fertilization restores:
**Correct answer:** B. The diploid number

Gamete fusion restores diploidy.

### 13. A dominant allele is one that:
**Correct answer:** B. Is expressed in a heterozygote under the model

Dominance describes heterozygous expression, not value.

### 14. Genotype refers to:
**Correct answer:** C. The allele combination considered

Genotype is the allele combination.

### 15. For Aa × Aa, the probability of aa is:
**Correct answer:** B. 25%

One of four combinations is aa.

### 16. After three dominant-phenotype offspring in Aa × Aa, the next offspring's probability of aa is:
**Correct answer:** B. 25%

Each event remains independent.

### 17. Incomplete dominance commonly gives a heterozygote:
**Correct answer:** A. An intermediate phenotype

The heterozygote is intermediate.

### 18. Codominance means a heterozygote:
**Correct answer:** A. Expresses both allele products

Both products are detectably expressed.

### 19. Which statement about mutation is correct?
**Correct answer:** B. Effects can be harmful, beneficial, or neutral

Effects depend on context.

### 20. Phenotype can reflect:
**Correct answer:** B. Genotype, environment, development, and chance

Phenotype often reflects multiple influences.
