# Unit 04 Teacher and Parent-Educator Guide

## Purpose

Unit 04 connects cell division, reproduction, chromosome behavior, probability, variation, environment, technology, privacy, and ethics.

## Sequence

- Week 1: DNA organization, cell cycle, mitosis, differentiation, and control
- Week 2: asexual and sexual reproduction, meiosis, chromosome behavior, and variation
- Week 3: inheritance language, Punnett probability, complex traits, mutation, technology, and ethics

## Required evidence

Fifteen lessons, cell-cycle laboratory, meiosis laboratory, probability simulation, variation dataset, moonflower case study, quiz, assessment, two constructed responses, corrections, and reflection.

## Privacy and ethical boundaries

- Do not require personal or family medical histories.
- Do not conduct ancestry, paternity, carrier, disease-risk, or reproductive testing.
- Do not collect biological samples.
- Use fictional organisms and supplied datasets.
- Chromosomal and reproductive models are simplified and do not define complete identity.
- Human value, intelligence, morality, disability, ethnicity, ancestry, sex, gender, or social worth are not ranked by genes.
- Address eugenics as scientifically and ethically discredited.

## Thresholds

Cell-cycle laboratory 7/8; meiosis laboratory 7/8; quiz 16/20; assessment 24/30.
