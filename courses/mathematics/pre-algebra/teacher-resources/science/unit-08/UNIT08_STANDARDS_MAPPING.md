## Alignment Boundaries

Unit 08 uses one-dimensional vectors and collisions for required assessment. Two-dimensional projectile equations, circular-force equations, calculus, rotational dynamics, detailed material stress, and relativistic mechanics are outside the required Grade 9 boundary.

Khaemenes Academy uses these frameworks as planning references and does not claim endorsement, examination registration, or automatic credit equivalence.
