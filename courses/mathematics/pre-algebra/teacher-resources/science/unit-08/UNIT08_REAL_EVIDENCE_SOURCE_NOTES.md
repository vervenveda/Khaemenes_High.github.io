# Unit 08 Real-Evidence Source Notes

## NIST

Use NIST for SI units, symbols and measurement language.

## NASA Glenn Research Center

NASA educational pages support motion equations and Newtonian model explanations. Treat diagrams and idealized examples as models, not independent experimental evidence.

## NHTSA

NHTSA may support transport-safety source analysis. Students must not reproduce vehicle, restraint or impact tests.

## Standards Sources

Use current NGSS, Cambridge IGCSE Physics 0625 (2026–2028) and Australian Curriculum V9 documents for comparative curriculum planning.

## Evidence Classification

Always identify:
- direct measurement or recorded observation
- calculated quantity
- graph interpretation
- idealized model
- engineering inference
- source conditions and uncertainty
- system boundary and reference frame
