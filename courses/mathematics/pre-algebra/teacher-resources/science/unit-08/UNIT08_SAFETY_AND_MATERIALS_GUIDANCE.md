# Unit 08 Safety and Materials Guidance

## Prohibited Activities

- Road or parking-lot testing
- Motor-vehicle experiments
- Human or animal collision testing
- Testing helmets, restraints or protective gear on a person
- Heavy falling masses
- Roof, balcony, stairwell or high-drop experiments
- Hard or weapon-like projectiles
- Suspended loads above people
- Improvised lifting devices carrying people
- High-tension springs or elastic launchers
- Unsupervised powered machinery

## Approved Low-Risk Approaches

- Walking-speed measurements in clear enclosed areas
- Light low-speed carts on level tables or floors
- Paper, foam or soft objects used without launching toward people
- Instructor-approved video analysis
- Mathematical models and published data
- Stable commercial simple-machine kits used according to instructions

Stop when surfaces are unstable, paths are obstructed, objects are too heavy, equipment is damaged, spectators enter the path, or adult supervision is unavailable.
