# Unit 08 Teacher and Parent-Educator Guide

## Unit Identity

**Course:** International Integrated Science 9 (`KH-SCI-IIS9`)  
**Unit:** 08 — Motion, Forces & Energy  
**Duration:** Three weeks, fifteen lessons, Weeks 22–24

## Purpose

Students measure and graph motion; distinguish scalar and vector quantities; apply Newton’s laws; calculate mass, weight, net force and acceleration; analyze momentum, impulse and one-dimensional collisions; calculate work, kinetic and gravitational energy, power and efficiency; and evaluate safe engineering designs.

## Required Evidence

- Fifteen daily lessons
- Motion Graph Check, 7/8
- Force and Newton Check, 7/8
- Momentum and Impulse Check, 7/8
- Energy, Work and Power Check, 7/8
- Safe-Motion Engineering Case
- Mastery Quiz, 16/20
- Objective Assessment, 24/30
- Two constructed responses
- Corrections and final reflection

## Concept Safeguards

- Distance is not displacement; speed is not velocity.
- A graph line is not the physical path of an object.
- Zero net force permits constant velocity and does not imply no forces.
- Newton’s third-law forces act on different objects.
- Use net external force in `F = ma`.
- Mass and weight are different quantities.
- Momentum conservation requires a defined system and negligible external impulse.
- Momentum can be conserved when kinetic energy is not.
- Dissipated energy is transferred, not destroyed.
- Gravitational potential energy belongs to an interacting system.
- Efficiency depends on a consistent energy boundary.
- A model calculation does not certify real-world protective equipment or human safety.

## Accessibility

Permit calculators, enlarged graphs, tactile arrows, graphing software, read-aloud support, oral explanation, keyboard input, alternate color-independent labels, extended time, unit reference cards, and scaffolded equation sheets.
