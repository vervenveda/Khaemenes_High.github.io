## Alignment Boundaries

NGSS HS-PS1-1 focuses on main-group patterns and excludes quantitative ionization-energy assessment. Unit 06 likewise keeps advanced quantum mechanics and quantitative ionization-energy analysis optional.

Cambridge content proceeds into bonding and structures. Unit 06 introduces ion formation but reserves full bonding, formulae, structures, and reactions for Unit 07.

Khaemenes Academy uses these frameworks for comparative planning and does not claim endorsement or automatic credit equivalence.
