# Unit 06 Laboratory Safety and Materials Guidance

## Prohibited Unsupervised Activities

- Alkali metals in water
- Concentrated or elemental halogens
- Unknown salts or powders
- Open-flame tests
- High-voltage discharge tubes
- Radioactive minerals or sources
- Dismantling smoke detectors
- Unverified lasers or ultraviolet sources

## Approved Approaches

- Published NIST, CIAAW, and IUPAC data
- Low-risk computer simulations
- Printed or digital spectra
- Commercial periodic tables
- Qualified recorded demonstrations
- Instructor-approved low-risk spectroscopy equipment with eye-safety controls

Stop any activity when equipment is damaged, substances are unidentified, ventilation is inadequate, or qualified supervision is unavailable.
