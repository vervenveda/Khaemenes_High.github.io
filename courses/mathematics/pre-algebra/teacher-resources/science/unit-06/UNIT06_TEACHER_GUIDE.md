# Unit 06 Teacher and Parent-Educator Guide

## Unit Identity

**Course:** International Integrated Science 9 (`KH-SCI-IIS9`)  
**Unit:** 06 — Atomic Structure & Periodic Patterns  
**Duration:** Three weeks, fifteen lessons, Weeks 16–18

## Purpose

Students connect element identity to proton number; isotope identity to neutron number; ion charge to electron count; atomic weight to isotope abundance; electron patterns to period and group; and periodic position to bounded property predictions.

## Required Evidence

- Fifteen daily lessons
- Atomic Composition Laboratory, 7/8
- Isotope and Atomic-Mass Check, 7/8
- Electron Configuration Check, 7/8
- Periodic Patterns Check, 7/8
- Element Evidence Case
- Mastery Quiz, 16/20
- Objective Assessment, 24/30
- Two constructed responses
- Corrections and final reflection

## Model Safeguards

- Do not call shell diagrams photographs or literal electron paths.
- Atomic diagrams are not to scale.
- Atomic number identifies an element; standard atomic weight is not a universal mass number.
- The octet and simple-ion model is useful but not exceptionless.
- Periodic trends are patterns with a defined scope, not absolute laws.
- Keep measured records separate from explanatory models.

## Safety

Do not permit unsupervised handling of alkali metals, concentrated or elemental halogens, radioactive sources, unknown salts, open-flame tests, dismantled smoke detectors, high-voltage tubes, or unverified lasers. Use verified data, simulations, approved demonstrations, or qualified laboratory protocols.

## Accessibility

Permit calculators, enlarged periodic tables, screen-reader-compatible tables, keyboard entry, oral explanation, extended time, alternate color-independent labels, and scaffolded notation.
