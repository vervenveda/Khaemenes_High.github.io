## Official-framework notes used for this release

- NGSS high-school practice expects students to decide data types, amounts, and accuracy, and to consider precision limitations such as trials, risk, time, and cost.
- Cambridge combined science emphasizes data handling, practical problem-solving, scientific method, accuracy, precision, objectivity, integrity, and practical skills.
- Australian Year 9 inquiry includes selecting investigation types, collecting reliable data, assessing risk, and addressing ethical issues.
