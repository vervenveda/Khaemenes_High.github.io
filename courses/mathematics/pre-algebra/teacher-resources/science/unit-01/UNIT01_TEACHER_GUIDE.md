# Unit 01 Teacher and Parent-Educator Guide

## Unit purpose

Unit 01 establishes the inquiry practices used throughout Science 9. Students should leave the unit able to design a defensible investigation, collect and display data transparently, and limit conclusions to what the evidence supports.

## Two-week sequence

Week 1 develops questions, variables, controls, sampling, measurement, and an approved investigation plan. Week 2 conducts the paper-helicopter investigation, develops graphing and evaluation skills, analyzes an observational dataset, constructs CER explanations, and completes the design and assessment sequence.

## Required evidence

- Experimental and observational questions
- Variable map and operational definitions
- Sampling and comparison plan
- Measurement audit
- Paper-helicopter plan, raw data, calculations, graph, CER, and evaluation
- Urban-shade dataset laboratory
- Scientific-source audit
- Paper-absorbency design task
- Mastery quiz and corrections
- Objective assessment and corrections
- Two constructed responses
- Final reflection

## Pathway adjustments

### Foundation

- Compare two or three variable levels.
- Provide sentence frames and partially completed tables.
- Use guided calculations and graph scales.
- Permit oral explanation with a written evidence record.

### Core

- Use at least three variable levels and five trials per level.
- Independently calculate mean and range.
- Produce a complete graph and CER evaluation.

### Extended

- Use five or more variable levels.
- Compare alternative graph or model fits.
- Quantify uncertainty where appropriate.
- Evaluate whether the relationship is linear.
- Propose a replication and an observational extension.

## Assessment

- Quiz mastery: 12/15
- Objective assessment mastery: 20/24
- Constructed responses: rubric-scored
- Corrections and retakes retain the original attempt

## Safety

The paper-helicopter activity is Tier 1 when scissors are used normally and the drop area is clear and level. Adapt or replace any activity when safe access cannot be assured.
