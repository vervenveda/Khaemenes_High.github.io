# Unit 00 Definitive Answer Key

## Readiness Diagnostic

### 1. Which statement is a direct observation?
**Correct answer:** B. The liquid is blue.

“The liquid is blue” reports a directly observable property. The other choices predict or infer causes or internal states.

### 2. Which question is testable with a controlled investigation?
**Correct answer:** B. Does water temperature affect how quickly a fixed mass of sugar dissolves?

Water temperature and dissolving time can be measured while other variables are controlled.

### 3. A student changes the amount of light and measures plant growth. What is the independent variable?
**Correct answer:** B. Amount of light

The independent variable is the factor deliberately changed: the amount of light.

### 4. Why do scientists use models?
**Correct answer:** C. Models help represent, explain, or predict systems that may be complex, large, small, slow, or inaccessible.

Models are useful representations, but they have assumptions and limitations and are not exact copies of reality.

### 5. Which SI unit is most appropriate for the length of a classroom desk?
**Correct answer:** B. Meter

Length is measured in meters or a related SI length unit.

### 6. Convert 2.5 liters to milliliters.
**Correct answer:** C. 2,500 mL

One liter equals 1,000 milliliters, so 2.5 × 1,000 = 2,500 mL.

### 7. A ruler has marks every 1 millimeter. Which recorded length is appropriate for an object ending exactly at the 42-millimeter mark?
**Correct answer:** A. 42 mm

The ruler measures length in millimeters, and the object ends at 42 mm.

### 8. A sample has a mass of 60 grams and a volume of 20 milliliters. What is its density?
**Correct answer:** B. 3 g/mL

Density = mass ÷ volume = 60 g ÷ 20 mL = 3 g/mL.

### 9. Which graph is generally most appropriate for showing how temperature changes every hour during one day?
**Correct answer:** A. Line graph

A line graph clearly displays change across ordered time intervals.

### 10. A seedling study records average heights of 2.0, 2.6, 3.4, 4.1, and 4.3 cm as daily light increases from 0 to 8 hours. Which statement is supported by these data?
**Correct answer:** A. In this study, average seedling height increased as daily light increased.

The averages rise as light increases. The data do not prove universality, exclude other factors, or identify the tallest individual.

### 11. Two variables are correlated. What can be concluded from correlation alone?
**Correct answer:** B. The variables change together in a pattern, but causation is not established.

Correlation identifies an association; it does not by itself demonstrate causation.

### 12. Four repeated measurements are 10.1 cm, 10.2 cm, 10.1 cm, and 14.8 cm. What should the investigator do first about 14.8 cm?
**Correct answer:** C. Check the procedure and equipment, repeat the measurement if possible, and document the anomaly.

An unusual result should be investigated and documented, not silently changed or discarded.

### 13. Which cell structure is the main site of cellular respiration in eukaryotic cells?
**Correct answer:** A. Mitochondrion

Mitochondria are the principal site of cellular respiration in eukaryotic cells.

### 14. During simple diffusion, particles have a net movement from:
**Correct answer:** B. Higher concentration to lower concentration

Simple diffusion produces net movement down a concentration gradient, from higher to lower concentration.

### 15. Which organism is a producer in a typical food web?
**Correct answer:** A. Green grass

Green grass produces organic molecules through photosynthesis.

### 16. The atomic number of an element equals the number of:
**Correct answer:** A. Protons in each atom of that element

An element is defined by its number of protons, which is its atomic number.

### 17. Which change is chemical?
**Correct answer:** C. Iron rusting

Rusting forms new substances through chemical reactions. The other examples are physical changes.

### 18. In a closed system, 12 grams of substances react completely. What total mass should the products have?
**Correct answer:** C. 12 g

Mass is conserved in a closed system, so the products total 12 g.

### 19. A cyclist travels 120 meters in 20 seconds. What is the cyclist’s average speed?
**Correct answer:** A. 6 m/s

Average speed = distance ÷ time = 120 m ÷ 20 s = 6 m/s.

### 20. An object has equal forces acting in opposite directions. What is the net force?
**Correct answer:** A. Zero

Equal opposite forces cancel, producing a net force of zero.

### 21. A switch opens a simple series circuit containing one battery and two lamps. What happens?
**Correct answer:** A. Both lamps go out because the circuit path is broken.

Opening a series circuit breaks its only conducting path, so current stops through both lamps.

### 22. Ocean water evaporates into the air. Which Earth-system transfer is occurring?
**Correct answer:** B. Hydrosphere to atmosphere

Ocean water belongs to the hydrosphere; water vapor enters the atmosphere.

### 23. Which statement describes climate rather than weather?
**Correct answer:** C. A region’s summers have usually been hot and dry over several decades.

Climate describes long-term patterns, while weather describes short-term atmospheric conditions.

### 24. Why are earthquakes common near many tectonic plate boundaries?
**Correct answer:** A. Stress can build as plates move and then be released suddenly.

Plate motion can build stress along faults; sudden release of that stress produces earthquakes.

## Laboratory Safety Verification

### 1. When an activity requires eye protection, when should safety glasses be worn?
**Correct answer:** B. From before materials are handled until cleanup is complete

Required eye protection must be worn before materials are handled and remain on through cleanup.

### 2. What is the correct rule about tasting investigation materials?
**Correct answer:** C. Never taste materials unless the activity is an explicitly identified food activity supervised for consumption.

Laboratory and field materials must not be tasted. Food activities must be explicitly designed and supervised as food activities.

### 3. An unknown liquid spills during an investigation. What should a student do first?
**Correct answer:** C. Move away, warn others, and notify the responsible adult or instructor.

Unknown spills require distance, warning others, and immediate adult or instructor notification.

### 4. What should be done with cracked or chipped glassware?
**Correct answer:** C. Do not use it; notify the responsible adult or instructor.

Damaged glassware can fail unexpectedly and must not be used.

### 5. Why should long hair and loose clothing be secured?
**Correct answer:** B. To reduce contact with flames, chemicals, moving equipment, and samples

Securing hair and loose clothing reduces entanglement and contact hazards.

### 6. A container has just been heated. How should it be treated?
**Correct answer:** C. Treat it as hot, use appropriate tools, and allow safe cooling time.

Recently heated objects may remain dangerously hot even when they do not look hot.

### 7. When may substances be mixed?
**Correct answer:** B. Only when the written procedure explicitly directs the mixture and required supervision is present

Substances may be mixed only under an explicit, reviewed procedure with the required supervision.

### 8. Which electrical source is appropriate for an independent student circuit activity?
**Correct answer:** C. The low-voltage battery specified in the written activity

Independent student circuits must use the specified low-voltage battery, never household mains electricity.

### 9. Before collecting a sample in a park or natural area, a student should:
**Correct answer:** B. Confirm permission, site rules, safety, and whether collection is necessary.

Fieldwork requires permission, compliance with site rules, safety review, and minimal environmental disturbance.

### 10. Which practice is prohibited in an ordinary home investigation?
**Correct answer:** C. Culturing unknown microorganisms from household or environmental surfaces

Culturing unknown microorganisms can amplify harmful organisms and is not home-safe.

### 11. How should investigation waste be discarded?
**Correct answer:** B. Follow the activity’s specific disposal instructions and local rules.

Disposal depends on the material and must follow the written procedure and local requirements.

### 12. A student has an allergy or accessibility need related to an activity. What is the correct response?
**Correct answer:** C. Pause and arrange a safe, scientifically meaningful alternative before beginning.

Safety and access needs require an appropriate alternative before the activity begins.

### 13. A person is injured during an activity. What should happen first?
**Correct answer:** C. Stop the activity, get appropriate adult or emergency help, and follow the stated emergency procedure.

Human safety takes priority over the experiment or data.

### 14. What is the correct food and drink rule during science investigations?
**Correct answer:** A. Food and drink are kept away unless the lesson is an explicitly designed food activity.

Food and drink must be separated from investigation materials unless the lesson is explicitly a supervised food activity.

### 15. A local environmental project records observations near a student’s home. Which public record is safest?
**Correct answer:** C. A broader city or region with personal identifiers removed

Public science records should use only the location precision needed and remove personal identifiers.
