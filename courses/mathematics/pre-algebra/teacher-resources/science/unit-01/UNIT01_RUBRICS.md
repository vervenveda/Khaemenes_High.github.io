# Unit 01 Rubrics

## Investigation Design Task — 24 points

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Question and hypothesis | Missing | Vague or partly measurable | Testable question and prediction | Precise question, directional hypothesis, scientific rationale |
| Variables and definitions | Missing | Some variables | Correct variables and basic definitions | Complete variables, operational definition, confounders |
| Controls and comparison | Missing | Few controls | Major controls identified | Controls justified and comparison design is strong |
| Method and replication | Missing | Incomplete | Repeatable sequence with trials | Detailed, efficient, reproducible, and auditable |
| Measurement and data plan | Missing | Units or table incomplete | Suitable tools, units, and table | Resolution, calculations, graph, and raw-data integrity addressed |
| Safety and ethics | Missing | Generic safety | Relevant controls | Hazards, controls, stop conditions, access, and disposal addressed |
| Evaluation plan | Missing | Generic limitations | Two relevant limitations | Limitations connected to effects and specific improvements |
| Communication | Missing | Difficult to follow | Clear organization | Precise, concise, and scientifically transparent |

Suggested mastery: 19/24, with no zero in safety.

## Constructed Response Rubric — 8 points each

- Scientific accuracy: 0–2
- Relevant evidence or design detail: 0–2
- Reasoning linking evidence to claim: 0–2
- Limitations and communication: 0–2

## Paper Helicopter Investigation — 20 points

- Approved question, hypothesis, and variables: 4
- Safety and repeatable method: 4
- Complete raw data and correct calculations: 4
- Appropriate graph: 3
- CER conclusion: 3
- Limitations and improvement: 2
