# Unit 01 Definitive Answer Key

## Mastery Quiz

### 1. Which question is directly testable with measurements?
**Correct answer:** B. How does wing length affect the flight distance of a paper airplane made from the same paper?

Wing length and flight distance can be measured while other features are controlled.

### 2. A student changes ramp height and measures the distance a toy car travels. What is the dependent variable?
**Correct answer:** B. Distance traveled

The dependent variable is the measured outcome: distance traveled.

### 3. In the ramp investigation, which factor should be controlled?
**Correct answer:** C. The toy car used for every trial

Using the same toy car helps isolate the effect of ramp height.

### 4. Why are repeated trials useful?
**Correct answer:** B. They help reveal variation and improve confidence in the typical result.

Repeated trials show variation and support a more reliable estimate such as a mean.

### 5. Which measurement includes both a quantity and an appropriate unit?
**Correct answer:** C. 12 centimeters

A complete measurement includes a number and a unit.

### 6. Four measurements are 9.8 cm, 9.8 cm, 9.9 cm, and 9.8 cm. These results are best described as:
**Correct answer:** A. Highly precise

The measurements are close to one another, indicating high precision. Accuracy requires comparison with a trusted value.

### 7. Which variable is usually placed on the horizontal x-axis?
**Correct answer:** A. Independent variable

The independent variable is usually plotted on the x-axis.

### 8. Which graph is generally most suitable for comparing the mean absorbency of four paper types?
**Correct answer:** A. Bar graph

A bar graph compares numerical values across discrete categories.

### 9. A result is far from all repeated measurements. What is the best first response?
**Correct answer:** C. Check the method and equipment, repeat if possible, and document it.

Anomalies should be investigated and documented, not silently changed or discarded.

### 10. What does correlation alone establish?
**Correct answer:** B. The variables show an association, but causation is not established.

Correlation identifies an association; additional evidence is required for a causal claim.

### 11. In claim-evidence-reasoning, what is evidence?
**Correct answer:** A. A measurable result or observation relevant to the claim

Evidence consists of relevant observations or data used to support a claim.

### 12. Researchers record sleep duration and test scores without assigning sleep schedules. This is:
**Correct answer:** B. An observational study

The researchers observe existing conditions rather than assigning the explanatory variable.

### 13. Which is an operational definition of plant growth for an investigation?
**Correct answer:** B. Growth is the change in stem height in centimeters from Day 1 to Day 14.

An operational definition specifies exactly how a variable will be measured.

### 14. Which change most directly improves the reliability of a mean fall-time measurement?
**Correct answer:** A. Use more repeated trials under the same conditions.

Additional repeated trials provide more information about variation and the typical result.

### 15. A planned method creates a risk that cannot be reasonably controlled with available equipment. What should the investigator do?
**Correct answer:** C. Revise or replace the method before beginning.

An investigation must be redesigned or replaced when risk cannot be reasonably controlled.

## Objective Unit Assessment

### 1. Which question identifies both a variable to change and a variable to measure?
**Correct answer:** B. How does salt concentration affect the time required for an ice cube of fixed mass to melt?

Salt concentration is changed and melting time is measured.

### 2. Which hypothesis is testable and directional?
**Correct answer:** B. If daily light duration increases, then mean seedling height after 14 days will increase under the tested conditions.

The hypothesis predicts a measurable direction between defined variables.

### 3. A hypothesis is not supported by the results. What is the scientifically appropriate conclusion?
**Correct answer:** C. Report that the results did not support the hypothesis and evaluate the evidence and method.

A hypothesis may be unsupported; the evidence and method should be reported and evaluated honestly.

### 4. A student investigates how pendulum length affects period. Which is the independent variable?
**Correct answer:** A. Pendulum length

Pendulum length is deliberately changed.

### 5. In that pendulum investigation, which should be controlled?
**Correct answer:** C. Release angle

The release angle should remain the same so length is the main planned difference.

### 6. What is the purpose of a comparison or control condition?
**Correct answer:** A. To provide a baseline for interpreting the effect of the tested condition

A control or comparison condition provides a baseline.

### 7. Which tool is most appropriate for measuring a paper helicopter fall time of about 1.5 seconds?
**Correct answer:** A. Stopwatch displaying hundredths of a second

A stopwatch with suitable time resolution is appropriate.

### 8. Which statement correctly distinguishes accuracy from precision?
**Correct answer:** A. Accuracy is closeness to a trusted value; precision is closeness among repeated measurements.

Accuracy concerns closeness to a reference value; precision concerns repeatability.

### 9. A digital balance reads to the nearest 0.1 g. Which recorded value matches that display resolution?
**Correct answer:** A. 18.4 g

A balance displaying tenths of a gram supports recording 18.4 g.

### 10. A length is recorded as 23.6 cm. Which part is the unit?
**Correct answer:** C. cm

The unit is centimeters, abbreviated cm.

### 11. Which design gives the strongest estimate of a typical fall time?
**Correct answer:** B. Five trials under the same condition with all results recorded

Repeated trials under the same condition reveal variation and support a typical value.

### 12. Why should a method describe how a variable is measured?
**Correct answer:** A. So the variable has an operational definition and the procedure can be repeated

An operational definition improves clarity and repeatability.

### 13. Which table heading is complete?
**Correct answer:** B. Fall time (s)

The heading identifies the variable and its unit.

### 14. A line graph shows time on the x-axis and temperature on the y-axis. What does the slope represent?
**Correct answer:** A. The rate of temperature change with time

Slope represents change in temperature divided by change in time.

### 15. Which graph is most suitable for showing the relationship between two continuous numerical variables?
**Correct answer:** A. Scatter plot

A scatter plot displays paired values for two numerical variables.

### 16. A graph axis begins at 95 instead of 0 and displays values from 96 to 100. What should a reader do?
**Correct answer:** B. Read the scale carefully because the visual differences may appear exaggerated.

A truncated axis may be valid but can visually exaggerate small differences.

### 17. The values 1.4 s, 1.5 s, 1.5 s, 1.6 s, and 1.5 s have a mean of:
**Correct answer:** B. 1.5 s

The sum is 7.5 s; 7.5 ÷ 5 = 1.5 s.

### 18. For the values 1.4 s, 1.5 s, 1.5 s, 1.6 s, and 1.5 s, the range is:
**Correct answer:** B. 0.2 s

Range = largest − smallest = 1.6 − 1.4 = 0.2 s.

### 19. Which statement is most defensible when a trend appears in one small study?
**Correct answer:** A. The pattern was observed in this study and should be tested with more evidence.

Scientific conclusions should match the scope and limits of the evidence.

### 20. Which action is improper data handling?
**Correct answer:** C. Removing an inconvenient result without explanation

Silently removing an inconvenient result undermines transparency and integrity.

### 21. An observational study finds that students who sleep longer tend to have higher test scores. Which conclusion is justified?
**Correct answer:** B. Sleep duration and test score were associated in the observed sample.

The study supports an association, not a definitive causal claim.

### 22. Which feature most strengthens a causal experiment?
**Correct answer:** B. Controlling alternative explanations while deliberately changing one factor

A controlled manipulation helps isolate the effect of the independent variable.

### 23. Which statement is reasoning in a claim-evidence-reasoning explanation?
**Correct answer:** C. Longer rotors increase air resistance, which can reduce downward speed; therefore the measured longer fall times support the claim.

Reasoning links the evidence to the claim using scientific principles.

### 24. Which source feature most directly supports scientific credibility?
**Correct answer:** B. The method and evidence are available for evaluation.

Transparent methods and evidence allow independent evaluation.

## Dataset Laboratory

- Overall association: as estimated shade increases, measured surface temperature generally decreases.
- Less-consistent point: Site H is slightly warmer than Site G despite greater estimated shade.
- Site A minus Site G: 49.2°C − 35.6°C = **13.6°C**.
- Supported claim: in these fictional observations, sites with more estimated shade generally had lower surface temperatures.
- Unsupported causal claim: shade alone caused the complete temperature difference.
- Possible confounders include surface material, moisture, wind, time since direct sunlight, thermometer angle, surrounding structures, and measurement timing.

## Constructed Response 1 — Design essentials

The design must identify drop height as the independent variable and mean bounce height as the dependent variable; define how bounce height is measured; use the same ball, surface, release method, location, measuring method, and environmental conditions; include at least five trials per height; state safety and stop conditions; retain raw data; and identify a graph and limitations.

## Constructed Response 2 — CER essentials

- Claim: mean period increased as pendulum length increased in the reported study.
- Evidence: period increased from 0.90 s at 20 cm to 1.55 s at 60 cm, with intermediate values increasing.
- Reasoning: a longer pendulum has a longer oscillation timescale under otherwise comparable small-angle conditions.
- Acceptable limitations: release angle, timing reaction, length measurement, small set of lengths, apparatus differences, or lack of individual trial values.
- Acceptable improvement: automated timing, more trials, more lengths, controlled small release angle, or reporting uncertainty.
