## Alignment Boundaries

Required assessment uses direct-current circuit relationships, simple series/parallel networks, qualitative magnetic effects, simple ideal transformer ratios, algebraic wave relationships, qualitative superposition and introductory digital coding.

Kirchhoff loop systems beyond simple circuits, capacitance, complex impedance, semiconductor device physics, Maxwell’s equations, antenna design, transmitter construction, detailed quantum mechanics and advanced signal processing are outside the required Grade 9 boundary.

Khaemenes Academy uses these frameworks as planning references and does not claim endorsement, examination registration or automatic credit equivalence.
