# Unit 09 Safety and Materials Guidance

## Prohibited Activities

- Wall outlets, mains wiring, power strips or household circuits
- Opening powered devices, chargers or appliances
- Battery short circuits
- Damaged, swollen, punctured or disassembled batteries
- Heating wires or components
- Capacitor discharge experiments
- Homemade transformers, spark coils or high-voltage devices
- Improvised radio transmitters or antennas connected to power
- Laser pointers or concentrated light directed at eyes or reflective surfaces
- High-volume headphones or speakers
- Strong magnets near medical implants, magnetic storage or sensitive electronics
- Microwave, ultraviolet, X-ray or radioactive-source experiments

## Approved Low-Risk Approaches

- Approved battery-powered kits at 6 V or less with current limiting
- Commercial meters used according to instructions
- Printed/data-based circuit analysis
- Low-strength classroom magnets with adult supervision
- Simulations and mathematical models
- Recorded sound and light data without exposure
- Binary encoding and signal-noise models

Stop immediately if a battery warms, wires heat, insulation is damaged, a smell appears, a device is unidentified, sound is uncomfortable, light is dazzling, or qualified supervision is unavailable.
