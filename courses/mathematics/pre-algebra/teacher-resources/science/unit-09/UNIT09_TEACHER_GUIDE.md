# Unit 09 Teacher and Parent-Educator Guide

## Unit Identity

**Course:** International Integrated Science 9 (`KH-SCI-IIS9`)  
**Unit:** 09 — Electricity, Waves & Information  
**Duration:** Three weeks, fifteen lessons, Weeks 25–27

## Purpose

Students connect charge, current, potential difference, resistance, power, circuits, magnetism, induction, waves, sound, light, electromagnetic radiation, and digital information through measurement, algebraic models, evidence, engineering, and safety.

## Required Evidence

- Fifteen daily lessons
- Circuit Data Check, 7/8
- Magnetism and Induction Check, 7/8
- Wave Equation and Behavior Check, 7/8
- Information Signal Check, 7/8
- Communication Engineering Case
- Mastery Quiz, 16/20
- Objective Assessment, 24/30
- Two constructed responses
- Corrections and final reflection

## Concept Safeguards

- Current is charge flow rate; potential difference is energy per charge.
- Conventional current direction is opposite electron drift in metals.
- Ohm’s law applies to ohmic behavior under controlled conditions.
- Current is conserved at junctions; energy transfer is represented by potential difference.
- Field lines are models, not physical objects.
- Induction requires changing magnetic flux.
- Transformers do not create energy and require changing current/flux.
- Wave speed depends on wave type and medium.
- Frequency remains set by the source during refraction.
- Sound requires matter; electromagnetic waves do not require a material medium.
- Wave and photon models are complementary within different evidence domains.
- Digital systems improve regeneration and checking but do not eliminate error, security, privacy, energy or access concerns.

## Accessibility

Permit calculators, enlarged circuit diagrams, tactile wires without energized circuits, screen-reader-friendly tables, captions and transcripts, no required audio exposure, keyboard input, extended time, color-independent signal labels, and scaffolded equation sheets.
