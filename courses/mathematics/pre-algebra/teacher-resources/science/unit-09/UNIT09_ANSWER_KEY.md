## Circuit Data Laboratory Check

### 1. A current of 2 A flows for 5 s. Charge transferred is:
**Correct answer:** A. 10 C

Q=It=2×5=10 C.

### 2. A device transfers 12 J to 3 C of charge. Potential difference is:
**Correct answer:** B. 4 V

V=E/Q=12/3=4 V.

### 3. A component has 6 V across it and 0.5 A through it. Resistance is:
**Correct answer:** C. 12 Ω

R=V/I=6/0.5=12 Ω.

### 4. Two resistors, 4 Ω and 6 Ω, are in series. Equivalent resistance is:
**Correct answer:** D. 10 Ω

Series resistances add.

### 5. Two identical 8 Ω resistors are in parallel. Equivalent resistance is:
**Correct answer:** A. 4 Ω

Two equal resistors in parallel have half the resistance.

### 6. At a junction, 3 A enters and 1.2 A leaves one branch. Current in the other branch is:
**Correct answer:** B. 1.8 A

Current conservation gives 3−1.2=1.8 A.

### 7. A device uses 12 V and 0.5 A. Power is:
**Correct answer:** C. 6 W

P=VI=12×0.5=6 W.

### 8. Which activity is appropriate?
**Correct answer:** D. Measuring a 3 V battery-resistor circuit with adult supervision

Only the low-voltage supervised circuit is appropriate.

## Constructed Response 1 — Essential Calculations

- Series: `Req = 10 + 20 = 30 Ω`; `I = 6/30 = 0.20 A`; `V10 = 2.0 V`; `V20 = 4.0 V`; `Ptotal = 6(0.20) = 1.2 W`.
- Parallel: `Req = 1/(1/10 + 1/20) = 6.667 Ω`; `Itotal = 6/6.667 = 0.90 A`; branch currents `0.60 A` and `0.30 A`; each branch has `6.0 V`; total power `5.4 W`.
- Adding a branch lowers equivalent resistance by providing another current pathway.
- Electromagnet: current produces a magnetic field. Motor: current in a field experiences force. Generator: changing magnetic flux induces potential difference. Transformer: changing flux links coils and changes voltage/current ratios.
- Limitations include ideal wires, no source resistance, constant component temperature, no magnetic saturation, no coil heating, no leakage flux, and no mechanical losses.
- Safety includes ≤6 V approved circuits, current limiting, no battery shorting, no mains access, no powered-device disassembly, and magnet precautions.

## Constructed Response 2 — Essential Calculations

- `λ = v/f = 300/150 = 2.0 m`; `T = 1/f = 1/150 ≈ 0.00667 s`.
- Entering a slower medium: speed and wavelength decrease; frequency stays set by the source.
- Reflection redirects at equal angles from the normal; diffraction spreads around openings; interference results from superposition; photoelectric evidence supports quantized photon energy.
- ASCII capital A: decimal 65, binary `01000001`.
- Even parity appends a bit so the total number of 1s is even. It detects any single-bit error but can miss an even number of flipped bits within the same block.
- Digital advantages include regeneration, reliable copying, error detection, storage, and rapid transmission.
- Tradeoffs include sampling limits, compression loss, bandwidth, energy use, e-waste, privacy, security, infrastructure access, and accessibility.
