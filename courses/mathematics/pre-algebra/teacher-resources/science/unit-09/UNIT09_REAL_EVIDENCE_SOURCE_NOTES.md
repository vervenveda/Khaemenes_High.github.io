# Unit 09 Real-Evidence Source Notes

## NIST

Use NIST for SI units, cybersecurity context and traceable measurement language.

## NASA

Use NASA for electromagnetic-spectrum and wave applications. Treat diagrams as models and verify quantitative values against the cited record.

## CDC/NIOSH

Use current public-health sources for sound exposure and hearing safety. No student sound-exposure testing is required.

## NGSS, Cambridge and Australian Curriculum

Use the current official standards for comparative planning and depth boundaries.

## Evidence Classification

Identify:
- direct measurement or published record
- calculated electrical or wave quantity
- circuit, field, ray, wave, photon or signal model
- engineering inference
- medium and system boundary
- uncertainty and conditions
- privacy, security, access, environmental and safety implications
