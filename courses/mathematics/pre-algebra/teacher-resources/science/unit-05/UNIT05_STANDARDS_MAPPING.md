## Important alignment boundary

NGSS HS-LS4-1 explicitly expects communication that common ancestry and biological evolution are supported by multiple lines of empirical evidence. Unit 05 teaches that official scientific expectation accurately, while requiring students to distinguish the underlying observations from the ancestry inference and to evaluate source methods and limitations.

The creation, intelligent-design, evolutionary-creation, and other worldview comparison is Khaemenes Academy enrichment. It is not claimed as an NGSS, Cambridge, or Australian Curriculum science requirement. Student worldview agreement is not assessed.

Khaemenes Academy uses these frameworks as comparative references and does not claim authorization, endorsement, or automatic credit equivalence.
