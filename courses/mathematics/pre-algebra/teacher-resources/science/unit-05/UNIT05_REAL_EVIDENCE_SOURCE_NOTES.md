# Unit 05 Real-Evidence Source Notes

## Source hierarchy

### Direct evidence and public records

- U.S. National Park Service Isle Royale wolf–moose population record
- CDC National Antimicrobial Resistance Monitoring System data
- Florida Museum of Natural History Fossil Horses collection and Amazing Feets evidence
- Smithsonian Human Origins fossil, genetics, dating, and behavior evidence collections

These sources provide specimens, images, records, measurements, or documented scientific syntheses. Students must still identify method, provenance, uncertainty, and whether a statement is measured or inferred.

### Official curriculum frameworks

- Next Generation Science Standards HS-LS2 and HS-LS4
- Cambridge IGCSE Biology 0610/0970, 2026–2028
- Australian Curriculum V9 AC9S9U03 and AC9S10U02

These identify educational expectations; they are not themselves fossil or population evidence.

### Worldview and advocacy sources

- Answers in Genesis for a young-earth creation position
- Reasons to Believe for an old-earth/progressive-creation position
- Discovery Institute for intelligent-design arguments
- BioLogos for evolutionary creation

These are primary sources for what each organization argues. They must not be treated as though advocacy language were itself a specimen, measurement, or DNA sequence. Their empirical claims should be traced to underlying evidence.

## Isle Royale record

The supplied 1980–2019 table is a historical instructional extract of NPS annual estimates. It is observational rather than a controlled experiment. Counts and methods may be revised or contextualized by the agency. Students should consult the live agency page before using it for current population status.

## Antimicrobial resistance

NARMS records demonstrate measured changes and differences in resistance among sampled microbial populations. They do not by themselves establish every claim about deep-time common ancestry. Students must identify organism, antimicrobial, sampling source, time, denominator, and surveillance limitations.

## Equid fossils

Physical equid specimens document real anatomical diversity, including feet, teeth, skulls, limbs, and body-size patterns. Phylogenetic relationships are scientific reconstructions. The modern account is branching, not a simple ladder, and does not propose an instantaneous horse-to-unrelated-organism jump.

## Human origins

Fossils, artifacts, geology, dating results, ancient DNA, and living-genome comparisons are different evidence types. Specimen existence and measurement are direct; species assignment, divergence date, and ancestry placement involve methods and inference. A single fossil rarely establishes direct ancestry.
