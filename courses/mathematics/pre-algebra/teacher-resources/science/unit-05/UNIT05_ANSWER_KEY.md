# Unit 05 Definitive Answer Key

Course: International Integrated Science 9 (`KH-SCI-IIS9`)

## Food-Web and Energy Laboratory

### 1. In a standard food web, an arrow from grass to rabbit represents:
**Correct answer:** C. Matter and chemical energy transfer from grass to rabbit

Food-web arrows normally point from a resource or prey toward the consumer.

### 2. Which organism is a producer?
**Correct answer:** A. A green plant that builds sugars through photosynthesis

Producers capture energy and build organic matter.

### 3. Which statement best distinguishes a decomposer from a detritivore?
**Correct answer:** D. Decomposers chemically break material down; detritivores ingest particles of detritus

Fungi and bacteria often chemically decompose material, while detritivores ingest fragments.

### 4. Producers contain 18,000 kJ and primary consumers contain 2,160 kJ. What is the transfer efficiency?
**Correct answer:** B. 12%

2,160 ÷ 18,000 × 100 = 12%.

### 5. Which statement is correct?
**Correct answer:** C. Matter cycles through reservoirs while usable energy flows and is dissipated as heat

Matter is recycled; energy flows through the system and much is dissipated as heat.

### 6. Why can an omnivore occupy more than one trophic level?
**Correct answer:** A. It may consume producers and consumers

An omnivore can feed at different positions in a food web.

### 7. A predator declines and one prey population increases. Which conclusion is most defensible from that observation alone?
**Correct answer:** D. The pattern is consistent with predator influence, but other factors must be evaluated

Counts can support a hypothesis, but weather, food, disease, migration, and sampling may also matter.

### 8. Which statement identifies a food-web model limitation?
**Correct answer:** B. Diet, abundance, and interactions can change by season and life stage

A diagram simplifies changing diets, strengths of interaction, space, and time.

## Population Evidence Laboratory

### 1. According to the supplied Isle Royale table, what were the recorded wolf and moose counts in 2014?
**Correct answer:** C. 9 wolves and 1,050 moose

The 2014 row records 9 wolves and 1,050 moose.

### 2. Moose counts increased from 1,500 in 2018 to 2,060 in 2019. What was the approximate percent increase?
**Correct answer:** B. 37.3%

(2,060 − 1,500) ÷ 1,500 × 100 ≈ 37.3%.

### 3. What can the wolf–moose time series establish by itself?
**Correct answer:** A. Recorded abundance patterns and associations across sampled years

A time series documents estimated counts and patterns, but causal attribution needs additional evidence.

### 4. A population of 420 organisms occupies 35 hectares. What is its density?
**Correct answer:** D. 12 per hectare

420 ÷ 35 = 12 organisms per hectare.

### 5. In a mark–recapture estimate, 40 animals are marked first. Later 50 are captured and 10 are marked. What is the simple estimate N ≈ M×C/R?
**Correct answer:** C. 200

40 × 50 ÷ 10 = 200.

### 6. Which assumption is required by the simple mark–recapture estimator?
**Correct answer:** A. Marked and unmarked individuals have reasonably similar capture probability

Unequal capture probability can strongly bias the estimate.

### 7. Which statement about carrying capacity is most accurate?
**Correct answer:** B. It can change with food, habitat, climate, disease, competitors, predators, and human activity

Carrying capacity is a model-dependent and changing property of environmental conditions.

### 8. Wolf counts were low while moose counts were high in several later years. What is the best scientific response?
**Correct answer:** D. Evaluate predator influence together with vegetation, weather, disease, migration, genetics, and methods

Predator–prey interaction is plausible, but a responsible explanation evaluates multiple mechanisms and source limitations.

## Evidence Classification Laboratory

### 1. A curator measures the width of an equid fossil tooth with calipers. This is primarily:
**Correct answer:** A. A direct measurement

The specimen and measurement are directly observed.

### 2. Researchers place two fossil species on neighboring branches of a phylogenetic tree. The branch relationship is primarily:
**Correct answer:** C. A historical inference tested against available data

Relationships among extinct populations are reconstructed from evidence and models.

### 3. “These equids diversified within a created horse kind” is best classified as:
**Correct answer:** B. A worldview or creation-model interpretation

The statement interprets evidence within a creation framework.

### 4. In science, a theory is:
**Correct answer:** D. A broad explanatory framework supported and tested by evidence

Scientific theories organize evidence, mechanisms, and predictions and remain open to testing and revision.

### 5. Which statement about the horse-family record is most accurate?
**Correct answer:** A. It documents equid variation and branching interpretations, not an instantaneous horse-to-nonhorse jump

The record includes real fossils and substantial change, but no instantaneous jump or complete generation-by-generation sequence.

### 6. Which statement distinguishes observed speciation evidence from deep-time reconstruction?
**Correct answer:** B. Reproductive isolation can be measured in living populations, while ancient lineages are reconstructed from surviving evidence

Living reproductive outcomes can be observed; extinct relationships require historical inference.

### 7. Methodological naturalism means that empirical science normally:
**Correct answer:** C. Tests natural mechanisms through observable consequences

It is a working method for testable natural explanations, not a proof for or against God.

### 8. Which item is direct biological evidence rather than an advocacy interpretation?
**Correct answer:** D. A published fossil measurement or DNA sequence with method and provenance

Measurements and sequences with transparent methods are direct data; advocacy sources express interpretations.

## Mastery Quiz

### 1. Which level includes interacting populations and abiotic conditions?
**Correct answer:** C. Ecosystem

An ecosystem includes a community and its abiotic environment.

### 2. A niche is best described as:
**Correct answer:** B. The organism’s role, resource use, tolerances, timing, and interactions

A niche is broader than habitat and includes ecological function and requirements.

### 3. What does an arrow from seed to mouse indicate?
**Correct answer:** A. Transfer of matter and energy from seed to mouse

The consumer receives matter and chemical energy from the resource.

### 4. If 5,000 kJ is available to one trophic level and 600 kJ to the next, efficiency is:
**Correct answer:** B. 12%

600 ÷ 5,000 × 100 = 12%.

### 5. Which process moves carbon from organisms to atmospheric carbon dioxide?
**Correct answer:** C. Cellular respiration

Cellular respiration releases carbon dioxide.

### 6. Two sites have the same species richness. Which additional information is needed to compare evenness?
**Correct answer:** A. Relative abundance of each species

Evenness depends on how balanced species abundances are.

### 7. Why must sampling effort be recorded in a biodiversity survey?
**Correct answer:** A. More effort can detect more organisms and affect comparisons

Different effort can create apparent differences unrelated to actual diversity.

### 8. A count of 900 organisms across 60 hectares equals:
**Correct answer:** B. 15 per hectare

900 ÷ 60 = 15 per hectare.

### 9. Why is carrying capacity not necessarily constant?
**Correct answer:** A. It changes with environmental resources and pressures

Resources, habitat, climate, disease, interactions, and human actions change capacity.

### 10. Which interaction is commonly represented +/+?
**Correct answer:** D. Mutualism

Mutualism describes an average benefit to both partners under stated conditions.

### 11. Secondary succession differs from primary succession because secondary succession usually begins with:
**Correct answer:** B. Existing soil or biological legacies

Soil, seeds, roots, microbes, or survivors often persist after secondary disturbance.

### 12. Which conservation plan is strongest?
**Correct answer:** C. One with a baseline, causal pressure, indicator, community rights, and revision rule

Transparent goals, indicators, causes, rights, and adaptive revision support evaluation.

### 13. Natural selection requires:
**Correct answer:** A. Heritable variation and differences in reproductive success

Selection changes populations when heritable variants differ in reproductive contribution.

### 14. Which statement about antimicrobial resistance is correct?
**Correct answer:** B. Microbial populations can change in the frequency of resistant variants

Selection can increase resistant microbial variants; one example does not settle every origins claim.

### 15. In standard biology, macroevolution includes:
**Correct answer:** B. Speciation, extinction, and diversification at or above species level

Macroevolution is defined as patterns at or above the species level.

### 16. Why can fossil species boundaries be uncertain?
**Correct answer:** B. Reproductive compatibility cannot usually be tested and variation may overlap

Fossil classification relies on preserved anatomy and context rather than direct breeding tests.

### 17. Modern scientific diagrams of equid history are usually branching because:
**Correct answer:** A. Multiple equid forms coexisted and many branches became extinct

The evidence is interpreted as diversification with contemporaneous and extinct branches.

### 18. A fossil’s geological location is an observation; calling it a direct ancestor is:
**Correct answer:** B. A historical inference

Ancestry is inferred from multiple lines of evidence.

### 19. How should student worldview positions be graded in this unit?
**Correct answer:** C. By accuracy, evidence classification, reasoning, source fairness, and respect

The curriculum evaluates literacy and reasoning, not compelled belief.

### 20. Which statement identifies possible common ground across origins views?
**Correct answer:** B. Protection of biodiversity and responsible stewardship can be supported from different worldviews

People with different origins views can support evidence-based stewardship.

## Objective Unit Assessment

### 1. Which sequence increases in ecological scale?
**Correct answer:** B. Organism → population → community → ecosystem

Individuals form populations; interacting populations form communities; communities plus abiotic environment form ecosystems.

### 2. Which statement is a direct observation?
**Correct answer:** C. Twelve individuals were counted in a 10 m transect

A documented count is an observation; causal and historical statements are inferences.

### 3. Which statement correctly distinguishes habitat and niche?
**Correct answer:** A. Habitat is location; niche includes role and resource use

Habitat describes where an organism lives; niche includes its ecological role and requirements.

### 4. Which is a detritivore?
**Correct answer:** C. Earthworm ingesting leaf fragments

A detritivore ingests detrital particles.

### 5. Why can removal of one species affect several others?
**Correct answer:** A. Food webs contain direct and indirect interactions

Networked interactions can transmit effects through several pathways.

### 6. A trophic level receives 320 kJ from a preceding level with 4,000 kJ. Efficiency is:
**Correct answer:** B. 8%

320 ÷ 4,000 × 100 = 8%.

### 7. Why is less energy available at higher trophic levels?
**Correct answer:** B. Organisms use energy and release heat, and not all biomass is consumed or assimilated

Metabolism, heat loss, uneaten biomass, and incomplete digestion reduce transfer.

### 8. Which process converts atmospheric nitrogen into biologically available forms?
**Correct answer:** A. Nitrogen fixation

Nitrogen fixation converts N₂ into compounds that enter biological cycles.

### 9. Which statement is accurate?
**Correct answer:** C. Matter cycles while energy flows through and dissipates

Matter is conserved and recycled, while usable energy is dissipated.

### 10. A sample contains counts 4, 4, 4, and 4 for four species. Compared with counts 13, 1, 1, and 1, the first sample has:
**Correct answer:** B. The same richness and greater evenness

Both contain four species and 16 organisms, but the first is more even.

### 11. Which practice best protects threatened species?
**Correct answer:** C. Avoid revealing precise sensitive locations and use non-contact records

Location privacy and non-disturbance reduce risk.

### 12. A population changes from 800 to 680. What is the percentage change?
**Correct answer:** A. −15%

(680−800) ÷ 800 × 100 = −15%.

### 13. Which condition violates a simple mark–recapture assumption?
**Correct answer:** C. Marked animals become much easier to capture than unmarked animals

Different capture probability biases the ratio.

### 14. Exponential growth assumes approximately:
**Correct answer:** A. Constant proportional growth with no immediate resource limit

Exponential models approximate unrestricted proportional growth.

### 15. A predator and prey time series shows opposite trends. What additional evidence strengthens a causal explanation?
**Correct answer:** A. Feeding records, prey survival, vegetation, disease, weather, and alternative-factor analysis

Mechanistic and comparative evidence helps test causation.

### 16. Which interaction is +/−?
**Correct answer:** B. Predation

A predator benefits while prey is harmed.

### 17. Ecosystem resilience refers most closely to:
**Correct answer:** B. Capacity to absorb disturbance while retaining or reorganizing key functions

Resilience concerns persistence or recovery of functions despite change.

### 18. Which result is an outcome rather than a projection?
**Correct answer:** C. A monitored population increased from 200 to 260 after intervention, with stated method

A measured post-intervention record is an observed outcome, though causal evaluation still needs design and comparison.

### 19. Which statement avoids teleological language?
**Correct answer:** B. Antibiotic exposure favored resistant variants that left more descendants

Selection changes variant frequencies; it does not require purposeful mutation.

### 20. Which process creates new alleles?
**Correct answer:** A. Mutation

Mutation changes DNA sequence and can create a new allele.

### 21. A measured allele-frequency shift across generations is commonly classified as:
**Correct answer:** A. Microevolution

Microevolution includes measurable inherited-frequency changes in populations.

### 22. Which provides direct evidence relevant to speciation in living populations?
**Correct answer:** A. Reduced interbreeding or reproductive compatibility between diverging populations

Reproductive isolation is central to many species concepts.

### 23. Which statement is consistent with the standard biological use of macroevolution?
**Correct answer:** B. It includes speciation and diversification across lineages

Macroevolution refers to patterns at or above the species level and is not the origin of life.

### 24. Which statement about fossil evidence is accurate?
**Correct answer:** A. Fossils can preserve anatomy and context but rarely identify a direct parent–descendant pair

Anatomy and context are observed; ancestry is reconstructed and classifications may be revised.

### 25. Why is a ladder model of horse evolution misleading?
**Correct answer:** B. The record is interpreted as branching, with coexisting forms and extinct side branches

Equid diversity is not a single uninterrupted march.

### 26. Which sequence moves from most direct to most interpretive?
**Correct answer:** B. Specimen → measurement → classification → ancestry inference → worldview conclusion

Evidence should be separated from progressively broader interpretation.

### 27. Which statement is correct?
**Correct answer:** B. It is a method that seeks testable natural mechanisms and does not itself settle theological truth

Methodological naturalism defines empirical method, not a compulsory metaphysical belief.

### 28. How should an advocacy source be used?
**Correct answer:** B. As a primary source for what that organization argues, checked against underlying data

Advocacy sources accurately document viewpoints, but scientific claims must be traced to data and methods.

### 29. Which student response should receive the stronger score?
**Correct answer:** B. One that accurately classifies evidence, fairly represents alternatives, and supports a reasoned conclusion

Assessment is based on literacy, evidence, reasoning, and respectful representation.

### 30. A strong stewardship proposal includes:
**Correct answer:** A. Target, baseline, action, indicator, community consideration, uncertainty, and revision rule

A testable and accountable plan defines evidence, action, rights, and adaptation.

## Biodiversity Survey

No single numeric answer applies because students use real observations or public data. Verify: source and effort are documented; at least five taxa have positive counts; richness equals the number of represented taxa; total abundance is the sum of counts; and Simpson diversity uses `1 − Σn(n−1) / N(N−1)` when `N > 1`. Evaluate detectability, season, effort, taxonomic resolution, spatial scale, rare-species undercounting, and location privacy.

## Constructed Response 1 — Population and ecosystem evidence

Expected: two accurate patterns from the NPS record; one correct absolute or percentage calculation; predator–prey interaction described as a candidate mechanism rather than the only cause; at least three alternatives such as vegetation, winter severity, disease, genetics, migration, age structure, or estimation method; explanation of why the time series is observational; and two useful additional measurements.

## Constructed Response 2 — Origins evidence and interpretation

Expected: at least five accurately cited specimens, observations, or measurements; separate classifications and ancestry inferences; accurate descriptions of evolutionary common descent and at least two creation/design interpretations; acknowledgment that equid history is branching and does not require an instantaneous horse-to-nonhorse jump; explicit uncertainties; fair source representation; and evidence that could revise the conclusion. Personal belief is not scored.
