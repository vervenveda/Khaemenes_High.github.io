# Unit 05 Rubrics

## Origins Evidence Hearing — 32 points

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Definitions and scope | Missing | Major errors | Partly clear | Accurate | Accurate with usage differences |
| Direct evidence | Missing | Unsupported | Some evidence | Five accurate items | Multiple independent lines with provenance |
| Evidence classification | Collapsed categories | Frequent errors | Mixed | Observation/inference separated | Full six-level classification |
| Scientific interpretation | Misrepresented | Very incomplete | Basic | Accurate | Accurate with assumptions and revisions |
| Creation/design interpretations | Mocked or absent | Major misrepresentation | One view | Three views represented | Multiple views from primary sources |
| Counterargument and fairness | Hostile/absent | Straw person | Limited | Fair response | Strongest-form response and reply |
| Uncertainty and revision | Claims certainty | Generic | One limit | Specific limitations | Evidence that could revise conclusion |
| Stewardship and communication | Missing | Unclear | General | Clear and respectful | Actionable, sourced, and accessible |

Suggested mastery: 26/32.

Personal belief is not a scoring criterion.

## Constructed Responses — 10 points each

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Scientific accuracy | Missing/incorrect | Partly accurate | Accurate and complete |
| Calculation or evidence record | Missing | Partial | Correct and labeled |
| Reasoning | Missing/circular | Partial link | Clear mechanism and logic |
| Limitations and alternatives | Missing | Generic | Specific and consequential |
| Safety, privacy, fairness, or communication | Missing | Partial | Appropriate and explicit |

## Biodiversity Survey — 20 points

Source and effort 4; valid count table 4; calculations 4; interpretation 4; bias, safety, and location privacy 4.

## Conservation Decision — 24 points

Target and baseline 3; causal pressure 3; evidence strength 3; strategy comparison 3; community rights and equity 3; measurable indicators 3; uncertainty 3; revision rule 3.

## Objective thresholds

- Food-Web and Energy Laboratory: 7/8
- Population Evidence Laboratory: 7/8
- Evidence Classification Laboratory: 7/8
- Mastery Quiz: 16/20
- Objective Unit Assessment: 24/30
