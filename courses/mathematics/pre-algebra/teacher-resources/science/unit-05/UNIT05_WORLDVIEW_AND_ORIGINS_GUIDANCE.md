# Unit 05 Worldview and Origins Guidance

## Governing principle

Teach the evidence openly, identify inferential steps, represent worldviews accurately, and never compel personal belief.

## Required distinctions

- A scientific theory is a tested explanatory framework, not merely a guess.
- Evolutionary theory includes observed processes and historical reconstructions; those should not be described as identical forms of observation.
- Standard biological macroevolution includes speciation and diversification at or above species level. Some creation models use “macroevolution” to mean crossing a proposed created-kind boundary; students must identify which definition is in use.
- Speciation does not mean an individual suddenly becomes unrelated to its parents.
- In evolutionary classification, descendants remain nested within ancestral groups.
- A created “kind” is a theological or creation-model category without one universally accepted scientific operational definition.
- Methodological naturalism defines how empirical science seeks testable mechanisms; it does not prove or disprove God.

## Position summaries

### Evolutionary common descent

Interprets fossil, anatomical, developmental, biogeographic, and molecular patterns as evidence of branching common ancestry, with population mechanisms accumulating across generations.

### Young-earth creation

Typically interprets major groups as separately created within a recent chronology, accepts substantial diversification within created kinds, and often disputes conventional geological time and universal common ancestry.

### Old-earth or progressive creation

Accepts an ancient Earth and much conventional geology while proposing episodic creative action or designed origins for major groups and disputing some common-descent claims.

### Intelligent design

Argues that some biological features or information are better explained by intelligent causation than by unguided mechanisms. It is not identical to one age-of-Earth position.

### Evolutionary creation

Accepts mainstream evolutionary science while interpreting creation, purpose, and divine action theologically.

### Secular naturalism

Interprets biological history without invoking divine action and may extend methodological naturalism into a broader metaphysical worldview. That metaphysical extension should not be confused with the scientific method itself.

## Assessment

Grade definitions, source accuracy, evidence classification, calculations, reasoning, limitations, counterargument, and respect. Do not grade a student’s private faith conclusion.
