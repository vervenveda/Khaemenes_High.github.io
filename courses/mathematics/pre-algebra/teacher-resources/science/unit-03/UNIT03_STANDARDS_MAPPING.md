Cambridge 0610 for 2026–2028 includes human nutrition, transport, disease and immunity, gas exchange, respiration, excretion, coordination, response, and experimental skills. Australian Year 9 emphasizes coordinated and interdependent body systems responding to environmental change. These are comparative planning references, not claims of authorization or equivalence.
