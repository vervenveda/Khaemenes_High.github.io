# Unit 03 Teacher and Parent-Educator Guide

## Purpose

Unit 03 teaches physiology as interacting systems, emphasizing feedback, transport, evidence, privacy, and scientific limits rather than diagnosis.

## Sequence

- Week 1: homeostasis, circulation, gas exchange, response data
- Week 2: digestion, liver, excretion, water, temperature, glucose
- Week 3: nervous, endocrine, immunity, integrated case, mastery

## Required evidence

Fifteen lessons, simulator, circulation pathway, response lab, feedback lab, case study, quiz, assessment, two constructed responses, corrections, and reflection.

## Safety and privacy

- Fictional datasets are the default.
- Optional movement requires adult approval and suitable ability.
- Stop for pain, dizziness, faintness, unusual shortness of breath, or concern.
- Do not collect diagnoses, medicines, weight, disability, menstrual status, or identifiable health records.
- Do not conduct blood, urine, saliva, fasting, dehydration, extreme-temperature, breath-holding, or maximal exercise tests.
- This unit does not diagnose, screen, rank, or treat medical conditions.

## Thresholds

Circulation 7/8; feedback 7/8; quiz 16/20; assessment 24/30. Case and constructed responses are rubric-scored.
