# Unit 03 Definitive Answer Key

## Mastery Quiz

### 1. Homeostasis is best described as:
**Correct answer:** A. Maintaining internal conditions within functional ranges despite change

Homeostasis is dynamic regulation within ranges.

### 2. In negative feedback, the response generally:
**Correct answer:** A. Opposes the initial change

Negative feedback counteracts deviation.

### 3. Which sequence is correct?
**Correct answer:** A. Stimulus → receptor → coordinator → effector → response

This is the basic feedback sequence.

### 4. Which is commonly modeled as positive feedback?
**Correct answer:** A. Blood clotting at a damaged vessel

Clotting amplifies locally until the break is sealed.

### 5. Right ventricle to left atrium route:
**Correct answer:** A. Pulmonary artery → lung capillaries → pulmonary veins

This is pulmonary circulation.

### 6. Which statement is correct?
**Correct answer:** A. Arteries carry blood away; veins carry blood toward the heart

Direction defines the vessel type.

### 7. Which alveolar feature supports rapid exchange?
**Correct answer:** A. Large surface area and thin surfaces

Large area and short distance support diffusion.

### 8. Breathing and cellular respiration differ because:
**Correct answer:** A. Breathing moves air; respiration transfers chemical energy in cells

They are distinct processes.

### 9. Why does heart rate often rise during activity?
**Correct answer:** A. To increase transport and heat/waste removal

Transport demand increases.

### 10. Chemical digestion mainly:
**Correct answer:** A. Breaks large insoluble molecules into small soluble molecules

Small soluble molecules can be absorbed.

### 11. Which increases small-intestine absorption area?
**Correct answer:** A. Villi and microvilli

Villi and microvilli increase surface area.

### 12. Kidneys mainly excrete:
**Correct answer:** A. Urea, excess water, and excess ions

These are major urine components.

### 13. During dehydration, increased ADH causes:
**Correct answer:** A. More water reabsorption and lower urine volume

ADH helps conserve water.

### 14. Which helps increase heat loss?
**Correct answer:** A. Sweating and increased skin blood flow

These support cooling.

### 15. After blood glucose rises, insulin promotes:
**Correct answer:** A. Uptake and storage

Insulin lowers elevated glucose.

### 16. A simple reflex arc is:
**Correct answer:** A. Receptor → sensory → relay → motor → effector

This is the correct pathway.

### 17. At a chemical synapse, neurotransmitters:
**Correct answer:** A. Cross the gap and bind receptors

They bind receptors on the next cell.

### 18. Endocrine signals are often:
**Correct answer:** A. Slower to begin and longer lasting

Hormonal signals often have slower onset and longer duration.

### 19. A first-line defense includes:
**Correct answer:** A. Skin and mucus

Skin and mucus reduce entry.

### 20. Body systems are best described as:
**Correct answer:** A. Interdependent networks exchanging matter, energy, and information

Systems interact continuously.
