# Comprehensive Final Exam Rubric

## Objective Section — 104 Points

- One point per correct answer
- Eight points per unit, Unit 00 through Unit 12
- Objective mastery threshold: **84/104**
- Recommended unit diagnostic: **6/8**
- A unit diagnostic below 6/8 identifies a focused review need; it does not by itself determine final course failure
- Corrections and retakes are permitted

## Constructed Responses — 16 Points Each

Score each response using four criteria, 0–4 points per criterion.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Scientific accuracy and completeness | Missing | Major misconceptions | Partly accurate | Accurate with minor omissions | Accurate, complete, and well integrated |
| Quantitative work, diagrams, and units | Missing | Mostly incorrect | Partial or weakly labeled | Correct and clearly shown | Correct, checked, and interpreted |
| Evidence, models, uncertainty, and reasoning | Missing | Claims unsupported | Some evidence or reasoning | Clear evidence-to-claim reasoning | Distinguishes evidence types, alternatives, assumptions, and limitations |
| Safety, ethics, impacts, and communication | Missing | Unsafe or unethical | Generic treatment | Specific and appropriate | Specific, balanced, accessible, and responsive to affected communities |

Maximum per response: **16 points**.  
Six responses: **96 points**.

## Final Score

- Objective: 104 points
- Constructed: 96 points
- Total: 200 points

Recommended final-exam mastery requires:

- At least **84/104** objective
- All six constructed responses scored
- At least **160/200** overall

## Performance Interpretation

- 180–200: Mastered
- 160–179: Proficient / recommended final mastery reached
- 140–159: Developing; corrections and targeted reassessment recommended
- Below 140: Beginning; substantial review and reassessment recommended

Local schools and homeschool programs should apply their own lawful grading and credit policies.
