# Comprehensive Final Exam Administration Guide

## Purpose

This exam measures cumulative mastery of International Integrated Science 9 after Unit 12. It samples every released unit, including readiness and safety, and requires students to transfer learning across disciplines.

## Recommended Schedule

The examination may be administered across four to six sessions:

1. Objective Units 00–04: 40 questions, 50–70 minutes
2. Objective Units 05–08: 32 questions, 40–60 minutes
3. Objective Units 09–12: 32 questions, 40–60 minutes
4. Constructed Responses 1–2: 60–90 minutes
5. Constructed Responses 3–4: 60–90 minutes
6. Constructed Responses 5–6: 60–90 minutes

Shorter sessions, breaks, or multi-day administration are appropriate.

## Permitted Materials

Unless local policy differs:

- Calculator
- Blank paper
- Official formula and constants reference
- Ruler and drawing tools
- Printed exam or interactive edition
- Accessibility supports that do not supply the scientific reasoning being assessed

## Accessibility

Permitted supports may include:

- Extended time and additional breaks
- Read-aloud or text-to-speech
- Speech-to-text or keyboard response
- Enlarged print and high-contrast display
- Screen-reader navigation
- Tactile or verbally described diagrams
- Colour-independent labels
- Quiet location
- Vocabulary or bilingual glossary without answer content
- Chunked administration
- Alternate oral presentation for constructed responses with a preserved scoring record

## Security and Integrity

- Keep the answer key and rubric outside student-facing directories.
- Preserve the student’s original response and correction history.
- Do not require private medical, genetic, family, location, biometric, or device data.
- Do not use hazardous demonstrations as exam tasks.
- Verify exported JSON records before using them as official evidence.
- Local browser storage is device-specific and is not a secure institutional database.

## Scoring

1. Score the objective section automatically or with the definitive key.
2. Record the 13 unit diagnostics.
3. Score each constructed response from 0–16.
4. Enter all six scores in the student record.
5. Confirm objective threshold, all-response completion, and overall score.
6. Document corrections, reassessment, and educator judgment.

## Corrections and Reassessment

Students may:
- Explain corrected reasoning
- Rework calculations
- Submit an improved diagram or model
- Retake objective items or the complete objective section
- Revise constructed responses using rubric feedback

Preserve both the initial and corrected record when required by local policy.

## Homeschool and Credit Record

The final record may support a homeschool portfolio when combined with:
- Course description and 36-week map
- Unit records and cumulative benchmarks
- Laboratory and project evidence
- Final exam record
- Evaluator signature
- Attendance or instructional-hour records
- Local legal documentation

The exam does not itself guarantee jurisdictional credit, accreditation, registry acceptance, or equivalence.
