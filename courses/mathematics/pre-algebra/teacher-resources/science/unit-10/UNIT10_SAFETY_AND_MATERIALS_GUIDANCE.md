# Unit 10 Safety and Materials Guidance

## Prohibited Activities

- Floodwater, storm drains, culverts or fast-stream entry
- Cave, mine, quarry or construction-site entry
- Climbing road cuts, cliffs, unstable slopes or eroding banks
- Approaching sinkholes, landslides, active faults, lava, geothermal vents or disaster sites
- Wildfire or smoke-plume observation from unsafe locations
- Acid testing of unknown rocks or soils
- Tasting minerals or inhaling dust
- Breaking rocks without approved eye protection and supervision
- Collecting protected fossils, minerals, cultural materials or sensitive-species records
- Entering contaminated or restricted sites
- Publishing exact locations of vulnerable archaeological, cultural or ecological resources

## Approved Low-Risk Approaches

- Public maps, photographs, remote sensing and authoritative datasets
- Observation from stable public paths and overlooks
- Rain gauges and weather observations in safe locations
- Commercial rock specimens with known identity
- Soil-texture observations without chemical reagents
- Mathematical models and supplied records
- Community and Indigenous knowledge used with consent, attribution and appropriate location privacy

Stop when weather changes, ground is unstable, water is rising, smoke or dust is present, access is restricted, wildlife is disturbed, or adult supervision is unavailable.
