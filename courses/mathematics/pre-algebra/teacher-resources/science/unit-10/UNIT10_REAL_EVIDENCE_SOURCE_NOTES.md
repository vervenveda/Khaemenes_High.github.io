# Unit 10 Real-Evidence Source Notes

## NGSS

Use current HS-ESS2 and HS-ESS3 pages for performance expectations and assessment boundaries involving Earth’s interior, surface feedbacks, carbon cycling, climate, resources, hazards and human activity.

## USGS

Use USGS for plate tectonics, earthquakes, water-cycle diagrams, groundwater, watersheds, hazards and geologic data. Distinguish explanatory educational pages from live observations and hazard products.

## NOAA and NASA

Use NOAA for carbon-cycle and ocean–atmosphere records. Use NASA for Earth observation, climate indicators, energy flows and remote-sensing context. Record date, variable, method and uncertainty.

## Cambridge and Australian Curriculum

Use official curriculum pages as comparative planning references, not as claims of course endorsement or examination equivalence.

## Evidence Classification

Identify:
- direct measurement or specimen
- mapped or remotely sensed observation
- calculated quantity
- laboratory evidence
- system or process model
- historical inference
- forecast, projection or scenario
- engineering or policy decision
- uncertainty, scale and alternative explanations
