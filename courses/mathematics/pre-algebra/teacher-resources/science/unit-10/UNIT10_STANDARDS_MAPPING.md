## Alignment Boundaries

Required assessment uses qualitative interior models, introductory seismic timing, plate-boundary patterns, rock and water cycles, simple water budgets, reservoir/flux calculations, residence time, introductory nutrient cycles, global-mean energy bookkeeping and evidence-based climate reasoning.

Advanced seismology, radiometric dating calculations, mineral crystallography, structural geology, groundwater differential equations, atmospheric dynamics, ocean general circulation, isotope geochemistry and full climate-model computation are outside the required Grade 9 boundary.

Khaemenes Academy uses these frameworks as planning references and does not claim endorsement, examination registration or automatic credit equivalence.
