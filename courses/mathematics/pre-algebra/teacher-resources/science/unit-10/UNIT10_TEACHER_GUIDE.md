# Unit 10 Teacher and Parent-Educator Guide

## Unit Identity

**Course:** International Integrated Science 9 (`KH-SCI-IIS9`)  
**Unit:** 10 — Earth Systems & Biogeochemical Cycles  
**Duration:** Three weeks, fifteen lessons, Weeks 28–30

## Purpose

Students model Earth’s interacting spheres; use evidence for interior structure and plate tectonics; connect internal and solar energy to change; analyze rocks, weathering, erosion, watersheds and groundwater; quantify carbon and nutrient reservoirs and fluxes; apply simple residence-time and energy-balance models; interpret climate evidence and feedbacks; and evaluate hazards, resources, resilience, justice and stewardship.

## Required Evidence

- Fifteen daily lessons
- Interior & Plate Check, 7/8
- Watershed Check, 7/8
- Biogeochemical Cycle Check, 7/8
- Climate Energy-Balance Check, 7/8
- Earth-System Resilience Case
- Mastery Quiz, 16/20
- Objective Assessment, 24/30
- Two constructed responses
- Corrections and final reflection

## Concept Safeguards

- A sphere or reservoir boundary is selected for a question; it is not a literal wall.
- Positive feedback amplifies change and is not automatically beneficial. Negative feedback opposes change and is not automatically harmful.
- Seismic evidence constrains interior models; humans have not directly sampled most of Earth’s interior.
- The mantle is mostly solid and deforms slowly; convection is not rapid boiling.
- Plate maps support hazard probability, not exact event prediction.
- The rock cycle has many pathways and timescales.
- Weathering breaks down material in place; erosion transports it.
- Groundwater occupies pores and fractures and is not usually a single underground river.
- Reservoir change equals inputs minus outputs for the defined period and boundary.
- Residence time is an average model, not an exact lifetime for each atom.
- Matter is conserved while compounds, reservoirs and fluxes change.
- Weather is not climate.
- Greenhouse gases absorb and emit infrared radiation; the atmosphere is not a solid blanket.
- A simple global energy balance is not a complete climate model.
- Projection, scenario and forecast are not interchangeable with observation.
- Risk depends on hazard, exposure and vulnerability.
- Stewardship decisions must consider affected communities, access, cultural knowledge, uncertainty and unequal burdens.

## Accessibility

Permit calculators, enlarged maps, tactile or raised-relief diagrams, text descriptions, screen-reader tables, keyboard input, captions, color-independent legends, local examples, extended time, vocabulary scaffolds, and alternate oral or visual explanations.
