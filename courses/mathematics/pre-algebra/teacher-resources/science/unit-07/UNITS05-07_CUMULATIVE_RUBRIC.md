# Units 05–07 Cumulative Benchmark Rubric

## Objective Section — 60 Points

One point per correct answer.

- Unit 05: 20 points
- Unit 06: 20 points
- Unit 07: 20 points
- Objective mastery: 48/60
- Recommended unit diagnostic: 16/20
- Corrections and retakes permitted

## Constructed Responses — 10 Points Each

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Scientific accuracy | Missing or fundamentally incorrect | Partly accurate | Accurate and complete |
| Calculation, formula, equation or model | Missing | Partly correct | Correct, shown and labeled |
| Evidence classification and source use | Unsupported or categories confused | Some relevant evidence | Direct evidence, model and inference clearly distinguished |
| Reasoning | Missing or circular | Partial mechanism | Clear mechanism connects evidence and conclusion |
| Limitations, safety, ethics or worldview fairness | Missing | Generic | Specific, respectful and appropriate |

## Belief-Neutral Scoring

No student is rewarded or penalized for affirming creationism, intelligent design, evolutionary creation, secular naturalism, evolutionary theory, or an undecided position. Score scientific definitions, accurate representation of perspectives, evidence–inference distinctions, reasoning, source quality, uncertainty and respectful communication.

## Final Score

Objective 60 + constructed 40 = 100 points.

Recommended mastery requires:

- At least 48/60 objective
- All four constructed responses scored
- At least 80/100 overall
