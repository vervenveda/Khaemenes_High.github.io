## Alignment Boundaries

NGSS HS-PS1-4 emphasizes energy-system models and does not require total bond-energy calculations from bond tables in the performance assessment. Unit 07 treats numerical bond-energy calculations as optional enrichment.

Cambridge includes extended mole, electrochemistry, acids/bases, and industrial-process detail. Unit 07 introduces only the concepts needed for this integrated Grade 9 sequence; advanced quantitative work is reserved for later courses.

Khaemenes Academy uses these standards as planning references and does not claim endorsement, examination registration, or automatic credit equivalence.
