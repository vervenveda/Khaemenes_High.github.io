# Unit 07 Definitive Answer Key

Course: International Integrated Science 9 (`KH-SCI-IIS9`)

## Bonding and Formula Check

### 1. What is the formula formed by Mg²⁺ and Cl⁻?
**Correct answer:** A. MgCl₂

Two chloride ions are needed to balance one magnesium ion.

### 2. Why is Al₂O₃ electrically neutral?
**Correct answer:** B. 2(+3)+3(−2)=0

Two Al³⁺ ions give +6 and three O²⁻ ions give −6.

### 3. An ionic bond in a solid is best described as:
**Correct answer:** C. Attraction between opposite ions throughout a lattice

Ionic solids contain extended electrostatic attractions among cations and anions.

### 4. A covalent bond is represented at this level as:
**Correct answer:** D. A shared pair of electrons

A shared electron pair is the introductory covalent-bond model.

### 5. Which formula correctly combines Ca²⁺ and NO₃⁻?
**Correct answer:** A. Ca(NO₃)₂

Two nitrate ions are required, and parentheses group the repeated polyatomic ion.

### 6. Which statement is most accurate?
**Correct answer:** B. Bonding labels describe dominant character; real bonds can have mixed character

Ionic and covalent character can exist on a continuum.

### 7. What does the subscript 2 in H₂O indicate?
**Correct answer:** C. Two hydrogen atoms per water molecule

The molecular formula contains two H atoms and one O atom per molecule.

### 8. When sodium forms Na⁺ in a simple ionic model, it:
**Correct answer:** D. Loses one electron

Sodium loses one electron; its nucleus is unchanged.

## Structure–Property Check

### 1. Why does solid sodium chloride conduct poorly?
**Correct answer:** B. Its ions are fixed in the lattice

The ions cannot move through the solid lattice.

### 2. Why can molten sodium chloride conduct?
**Correct answer:** C. Its ions can move

Mobile ions carry charge in the molten state.

### 3. Why do many simple molecular substances have low melting and boiling points?
**Correct answer:** D. Weak attractions between molecules require less energy to overcome

Intermolecular attractions are overcome during melting or boiling, not the covalent bonds within molecules.

### 4. Which property is best explained by diamond’s network structure?
**Correct answer:** A. Very high hardness

A three-dimensional network of strong covalent bonds supports hardness.

### 5. Why can graphite conduct electricity?
**Correct answer:** B. It has delocalised electrons within its layers

Delocalised electrons can move through the layered structure.

### 6. Why are many metals malleable?
**Correct answer:** C. Metal-ion layers can shift while attraction to delocalised electrons persists

Metallic attraction is non-directional enough to persist as layers shift.

### 7. Which observation most directly distinguishes copper from diamond?
**Correct answer:** D. Copper is an excellent electrical conductor at room temperature

Room-temperature electrical conduction differs strongly.

### 8. Which statement about CO₂ is correct in its standard linear model?
**Correct answer:** A. Its C=O bonds are polar, but their dipoles cancel

The two equal bond dipoles point in opposite directions in the linear molecule.

## Equation and Conservation Check

### 1. Which equation is balanced?
**Correct answer:** C. 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O

The balanced equation has four H atoms and two O atoms on each side.

### 2. What may be changed when balancing an equation?
**Correct answer:** D. Coefficients only

Coefficients change amounts; subscripts would change substance identity.

### 3. In CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O, how many oxygen atoms appear on each side?
**Correct answer:** A. 4

Two O₂ molecules contain four O atoms; products also contain four.

### 4. What does (aq) mean?
**Correct answer:** B. Dissolved in water

Aqueous means dissolved in water.

### 5. Why can measured mass appear to decrease in an open gas-producing reaction?
**Correct answer:** C. Gas escapes the measured system

Escaping gas is no longer on the balance.

### 6. What coefficient belongs before O₂ in C₃H₈ + __O₂ → 3CO₂ + 4H₂O?
**Correct answer:** D. 5

Products contain ten O atoms, requiring five O₂ molecules.

### 7. Formation of a solid from two clear solutions is called:
**Correct answer:** A. Precipitation

An insoluble solid product is a precipitate.

### 8. Which is a physical change?
**Correct answer:** B. Ice melting

Melting changes state without changing molecular identity.

## Energy, Rate and Equilibrium Check

### 1. Which statement defines an exothermic reaction?
**Correct answer:** D. It transfers thermal energy to the surroundings

Exothermic reactions transfer energy to surroundings and have negative ΔH under the stated convention.

### 2. Which sign of ΔH identifies an endothermic reaction?
**Correct answer:** A. Positive

Endothermic reactions have positive enthalpy change.

### 3. Activation energy is:
**Correct answer:** B. The minimum energy needed for a successful reaction pathway

Reacting collisions need sufficient energy to cross the barrier.

### 4. A catalyst generally:
**Correct answer:** C. Lowers activation energy and is regenerated

A catalyst provides an alternative pathway without changing overall ΔH or equilibrium position.

### 5. Which statement is correct?
**Correct answer:** D. Bond breaking requires energy; bond making releases energy

Overall energy change depends on the balance between those processes.

### 6. Increasing temperature usually speeds a reaction because:
**Correct answer:** A. More particles have sufficient kinetic energy for successful collisions

A larger fraction of collisions can overcome the activation barrier.

### 7. Why does a powdered effervescent tablet usually react faster than a whole one?
**Correct answer:** B. More surface is exposed to the liquid

Greater exposed surface allows more frequent interaction.

### 8. At dynamic equilibrium in a closed system:
**Correct answer:** C. Forward and reverse rates are equal

Both directions continue at equal rates while concentrations remain constant.

### 2. What is the correct formula for calcium oxide?
**Correct answer:** B. CaO

Ca²⁺ and O²⁻ combine in a 1:1 ratio.

### 3. What is the correct formula for aluminium chloride?

Three Cl⁻ ions balance Al³⁺.

### 4. Which substance consists of simple covalent molecules?
**Correct answer:** D. Carbon dioxide

CO₂ contains discrete molecules.

### 5. Which substance contains a giant metallic lattice?
**Correct answer:** A. Copper

Copper is metallic.

**Correct answer:** B. Delocalised electrons

Electrons can move within the layers.

### 7. Which bond is expected to be most polar among these?
**Correct answer:** C. H–F

H–F has the largest electronegativity difference of the options.

### 8. Using Ar values Na=23 and Cl=35.5, Mr of NaCl is:
**Correct answer:** D. 58.5

23 + 35.5 = 58.5.

### 9. Which equation is balanced?
**Correct answer:** A. N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃

Both sides contain two N and six H atoms.

### 10. Why must subscripts not be changed to balance an equation?
**Correct answer:** B. Changing a subscript changes the substance

A different subscript represents a different composition.

### 11. Which symbol identifies a solid?
**Correct answer:** C. (s)

(s) denotes solid.

### 12. A balanced equation demonstrates conservation of:
**Correct answer:** D. Each type of atom

Atom counts match across the reaction.

### 13. Which observation is strongest evidence for a precipitate reaction?
**Correct answer:** A. A new insoluble solid appears

A precipitate is an insoluble solid product.

### 14. Which reaction from the supplied table is endothermic?
**Correct answer:** B. CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂

The listed calcium-carbonate decomposition has positive ΔH.

### 15. On an energy diagram, activation energy is measured from:
**Correct answer:** C. Reactant energy to the pathway peak

The barrier is the difference between reactants and the transition-state peak.

### 16. What remains unchanged when a catalyst is added?
**Correct answer:** D. Overall ΔH

A catalyst lowers the barrier but does not change reactant/product energy difference.

### 17. Which change generally increases reaction rate for a solid reactant?
**Correct answer:** A. Increasing surface area

Greater surface area increases accessible collision sites.

### 18. A shorter completion time for the same amount of reaction usually indicates:
**Correct answer:** B. A faster average rate

The same change occurring in less time is faster.

### 19. Which statement is true at equilibrium?
**Correct answer:** C. Concentrations are constant but not necessarily equal

Equal forward and reverse rates keep concentrations constant.

### 20. Which activity is appropriate for home or ordinary classroom use with adult approval and goggles?
**Correct answer:** D. Timing an effervescent tablet in water

The other activities present serious hazards.

## Objective Unit Assessment

### 1. Which force is central to ionic bonding?
**Correct answer:** A. Electrostatic attraction between opposite charges

Ionic bonding is electrostatic attraction.

### 2. Which statement best describes a covalent bond?
**Correct answer:** B. A shared electron pair between atoms

The introductory covalent model uses shared electron pairs.

### 3. What formula results from K⁺ and O²⁻?
**Correct answer:** C. K₂O

Two K⁺ ions balance one O²⁻ ion.

### 4. What formula results from Al³⁺ and SO₄²⁻?
**Correct answer:** D. Al₂(SO₄)₃

Two Al³⁺ give +6 and three sulfate ions give −6.

### 5. Why are parentheses used in Ca(OH)₂?
**Correct answer:** A. Two hydroxide ions are present

The parentheses group the repeated polyatomic ion.

### 6. Which substance is expected to conduct when molten but not when solid?
**Correct answer:** B. Sodium chloride

Molten ionic compounds have mobile ions.

### 7. Which property is characteristic of many simple molecular substances?
**Correct answer:** C. Low melting and boiling points

Weak intermolecular attractions often give low phase-change temperatures.

### 8. Why is diamond hard?
**Correct answer:** D. Its atoms form a three-dimensional covalent network

Many strong bonds extend through the network.

### 9. Why is copper conductive?
**Correct answer:** A. Delocalised electrons can move through the metallic structure

Mobile delocalised electrons carry charge.

### 10. Which statement distinguishes bond polarity from molecular polarity?
**Correct answer:** B. A molecule’s shape can cause bond dipoles to cancel or reinforce

Whole-molecule polarity depends on both bond dipoles and arrangement.

### 11. Using C=12 and O=16, Mr of CO₂ is:
**Correct answer:** C. 44

12 + 2(16) = 44.

### 12. In 2H₂O, the coefficient 2 indicates:
**Correct answer:** D. Two water molecules or formula units in the represented ratio

The coefficient multiplies the entire formula.

### 13. Which equation balances aluminium oxide formation?
**Correct answer:** A. 4Al + 3O₂ → 2Al₂O₃

Four Al and six O atoms appear on both sides.

### 14. Which equation balances iron(III) oxide formation?
**Correct answer:** B. 4Fe + 3O₂ → 2Fe₂O₃

The coefficients 4:3:2 balance Fe₂O₃ formation.

### 15. Why is mass conserved in a closed ordinary chemical reaction?
**Correct answer:** C. The same atoms are rearranged into products

Chemical reactions rearrange existing atoms.

### 16. A gas-producing reaction loses measured mass in an open beaker. The best explanation is:
**Correct answer:** D. Gas left the balance system

The escaped product is no longer included in the measured system.

### 17. Which observation alone is least sufficient to prove a chemical reaction?
**Correct answer:** A. A temperature change without a control

Physical processes can also change temperature.

### 18. CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O is classified as:
**Correct answer:** B. Combustion

A hydrocarbon reacting with oxygen to form CO₂ and water is combustion.

### 19. Which sign belongs to an exothermic ΔH?
**Correct answer:** C. Negative

Products are lower in enthalpy than reactants under this convention.

### 20. Which process requires energy input?
**Correct answer:** D. Breaking a chemical bond

Bond breaking is endothermic.

### 21. Which statement about a catalyst is accurate?
**Correct answer:** A. It lowers activation energy but does not change ΔH

Catalysts alter the pathway, not overall energy difference.

### 22. Increasing concentration usually increases rate because:
**Correct answer:** B. More reactant particles occupy a given volume, increasing collision frequency

More particles per volume generally means more collisions.

### 23. Why can higher temperature increase rate?
**Correct answer:** C. More particles exceed activation energy

The energy distribution shifts so more collisions can succeed.

### 24. Which design best isolates the effect of surface area?
**Correct answer:** D. Use equal tablet masses, equal water volume and temperature, changing only tablet size

A fair comparison changes one factor while controlling others.

### 25. For equal reaction amounts, trial A finishes in 20 s and trial B in 50 s. Which is faster?
**Correct answer:** A. Trial A

The same extent of reaction occurs sooner in A.

### 26. At dynamic equilibrium:
**Correct answer:** B. Forward and reverse rates are equal

Equal rates maintain constant concentrations.

### 27. What does a catalyst do to an equilibrium system?
**Correct answer:** C. It helps both directions reach equilibrium faster without changing the equilibrium position

The catalyst lowers barriers for both directions.

### 28. Why is a compromise temperature used in the Haber process?
**Correct answer:** D. It balances reaction rate, equilibrium yield, energy use, and safety/economics

Industrial conditions balance multiple constraints.

### 29. Which statement is scientifically responsible?
**Correct answer:** A. Ball-and-stick models show connectivity and shape but distort size and electron density

Models are useful but simplified.

### 30. Which action is prohibited?
**Correct answer:** B. Mixing household cleaners to look for a reaction

Household cleaners can generate toxic gases or corrosive mixtures.

## Constructed Response 1 — Essential Evidence

- NaCl: giant ionic lattice; high melting point; solid poor conductor; molten/aqueous conductor; formula is a ratio.
- CO₂: discrete covalent molecules; low phase-change temperature; poor conductor.
- Graphite: layered giant covalent structure with delocalised electrons; conductive and soft between layers.
- Copper: metallic lattice with delocalised electrons; conductive, malleable, and ductile.
- Model limitations must address scale, electron density, phase/pressure, impurities, anisotropy, or category boundaries.

## Constructed Response 2 — Essential Evidence

- Methane combustion: CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O; C 1→1, H 4→4, O 4→4; exothermic, ΔH approximately −890.3 kJ mol⁻¹.
- Calcium carbonate: CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂; Ca 1→1, C 1→1, O 3→3; endothermic, ΔH approximately +178.3 kJ mol⁻¹.
- Closed-system conservation follows from unchanged atom counts.
- Higher temperature can increase the fraction of collisions above activation energy.
- A catalyst lowers activation energy but does not alter ΔH or atom conservation.
- Valid limitations include heat loss, phase assumptions, incomplete reaction, gas escape, measurement precision, and data-source conditions.
