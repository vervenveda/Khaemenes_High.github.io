# Unit 07 Laboratory Safety and Materials Guidance

## Prohibited Unsupervised Activities

- Mixing household cleaners
- Strong acids or bases
- Open flames or combustion
- Burning magnesium or hydrocarbons
- Heating sealed containers
- Gas collection in sealed or pressure-building vessels
- Unknown powders, salts, or metals
- Concentrated solutions
- Toxic, corrosive, oxidizing, or flammable reagents
- Tasting any laboratory or activity material

## Approved Low-Risk Option

With adult approval, properly fitting splash goggles, an open cup, water, and a commercially manufactured effervescent tablet may be used to compare temperature or surface area. Use warm tap water rather than boiling water. Change only one variable at a time. Do not ingest the tablet or reaction mixture.

## Safer Alternatives

- Published material-property data
- Printed or digital particle models
- Formula and equation calculations
- NIST thermochemical records
- Qualified demonstration videos
- Student-designed investigations that remain on paper
- Low-voltage commercial conductivity equipment used according to instructions

Stop immediately if a container warms unexpectedly, pressure builds, a substance is unidentified, ventilation is poor, goggles do not fit, or qualified supervision is unavailable.
