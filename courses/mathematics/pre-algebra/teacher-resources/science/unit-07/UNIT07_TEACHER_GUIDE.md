# Unit 07 Teacher and Parent-Educator Guide

## Unit Identity

**Course:** International Integrated Science 9 (`KH-SCI-IIS9`)  
**Unit:** 07 — Bonding, Chemical Reactions & Matter  
**Duration:** Three weeks, fifteen lessons, Weeks 19–21

## Purpose

Students connect outer-electron patterns to ionic, covalent, giant covalent, and metallic bonding models; use structure to explain real material properties; derive formulae from charge; balance equations; demonstrate atom and mass conservation; interpret energy pathways; analyze rate factors; and evaluate equilibrium tradeoffs.

## Required Evidence

- Fifteen daily lessons
- Bonding and Formula Check, 7/8
- Structure–Property Check, 7/8
- Equation and Conservation Check, 7/8
- Energy, Rate and Equilibrium Check, 7/8
- Real Materials and Reaction Evidence Case
- Mastery Quiz, 16/20
- Objective Assessment, 24/30
- Two constructed responses
- Corrections and final reflection

## Concept Safeguards

- “Atoms want full shells” is not a literal causal explanation.
- Ionic solids are lattices; their formulae show ratios, not discrete molecules.
- Ionic and covalent character are not perfectly binary categories.
- Bond polarity and whole-molecule polarity are different.
- Melting molecular substances overcomes intermolecular attractions, not necessarily covalent bonds.
- Coefficients may change during balancing; subscripts may not.
- A balanced equation demonstrates atom conservation but does not by itself prove reaction mechanism.
- A catalyst lowers activation energy but does not change ΔH or equilibrium position.
- Equilibrium means equal forward/reverse rates, not equal concentrations or stopped reactions.

## Assessment Boundary

Required work emphasizes main-group bonding, common ion charges, relative formula mass, qualitative energy and rate reasoning, and balancing equations. Advanced mole stoichiometry, quantitative bond-energy sums, detailed intermolecular-force taxonomy, formal charge, orbital hybridization, equilibrium constants, acids/bases, and electrochemistry are optional or reserved for later study.

## Accessibility

Permit charge tables, enlarged formula cards, tactile/physical models, calculators, read-aloud support, oral explanations, keyboard entry, alternate color-independent labels, extended time, and scaffolded atom-audit tables.
