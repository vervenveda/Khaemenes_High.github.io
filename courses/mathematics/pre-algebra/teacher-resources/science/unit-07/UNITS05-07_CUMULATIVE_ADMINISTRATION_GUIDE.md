# Units 05–07 Cumulative Benchmark Administration Guide

## Purpose

This benchmark measures cumulative mastery after Unit 07 and provides the transition checkpoint before Unit 08: Motion, Forces & Energy.

## Suggested Administration

- Objective section: 75–100 minutes
- Constructed responses: 75–100 minutes
- One extended session or two to three shorter sessions
- Calculator, periodic table and blank paper permitted
- Accessibility supports may include read-aloud, additional time, enlarged print, keyboard input, breaks, translated directions and language scaffolds that do not change the scientific reasoning assessed

## Security

Keep the answer key outside student-facing directories. Student pages contain no educator-key links.

## Scoring

- Objective: 60 points
- Constructed: four responses at 10 points each
- Recommended thresholds: 48/60 objective, 16/20 diagnostic per unit and 80/100 overall
- Permit corrections and retakes
- Record all three unit diagnostic scores

## Unit 05 Origins and Worldview Fairness

The assessment distinguishes empirical observations, measurements, classifications, scientific models, historical inferences, methodological naturalism, philosophical claims and theological/worldview conclusions.

A student's personal belief is not a scoring criterion. Students may accurately discuss creation, intelligent design, evolutionary creation, naturalistic evolution, or an undecided position. Evaluate definitions, evidence classification, source fairness, reasoning, uncertainty and respectful communication.

## Safety and Privacy

- No personal genetic, ancestry, paternity, reproductive, medical or health disclosure is required.
- No radioactive materials, reactive metals, elemental halogens, flames, strong acids/bases, unknown chemicals or household-cleaner mixing is required.
- All chemical and ecological calculations use supplied records or safe observations.
