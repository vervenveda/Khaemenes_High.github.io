Use the current Year 10 universe background for galaxies, stars, solar systems, Big Bang evidence and respectful engagement with First Nations astronomical knowledge.

## Evidence Classification

Identify:
- direct photon or particle detection
- raw detector record
- calibrated or background-subtracted data
- processed composite or false-color image
- calculated quantity
- physical or statistical model
- historical inference
- simulation
- artist concept
- projection or mission scenario
- uncertainty, bias and alternative explanation
