# Unit 11 Safety and Materials Guidance

## Prohibited Activities

- Direct or optical viewing of the Sun without a certified solar system used exactly as designed
- Sunglasses, smoked glass, exposed film, improvised filters or damaged eclipse viewers
- Binocular, telescope, camera or viewfinder solar use without approved front-aperture filtering and qualified supervision
- Laser pointers aimed at the sky, aircraft, people, animals or reflective surfaces
- Rooftop, cliff, roadside, rail, construction-site or restricted-land observation
- Night observation alone or during severe weather, wildfire smoke or unsafe temperatures
- Improvised radio transmitters, radar, high-voltage equipment, rocket motors, pyrotechnics, drones or launch devices
- Tracking or publishing private individual locations, schedules or sensitive infrastructure
- Publishing protected cultural sky knowledge without permission
- Handling meteorites or samples with unknown contamination or legal status

## Approved Low-Risk Approaches

- Official telescope and spacecraft archives
- Supplied spectra, light curves and catalogs
- Night observation from stable permitted locations with adult supervision
- Certified solar viewers and supervised projection systems used exactly as designed
- Indoor scale models, calculations and simulations
- Public citizen-science projects with privacy settings reviewed
- Cultural astronomy resources used with consent, attribution and appropriate boundaries

Stop if solar equipment is damaged, weather changes, visibility becomes unsafe, aircraft are nearby, the site is unpermitted, wildlife is disturbed, or adult supervision is unavailable.
