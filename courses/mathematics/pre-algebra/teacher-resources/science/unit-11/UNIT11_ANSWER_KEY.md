Course: International Integrated Science 9 (`KH-SCI-IIS9`)

## Solar-System Scale & Orbit Check

### 1. The apparent daily motion of most stars across the sky is mainly caused by:
**Correct answer:** A. Earth’s rotation

Earth rotates west-to-east, making the sky appear to move east-to-west.

### 2. Earth’s seasons are caused primarily by:
**Correct answer:** B. Earth’s axial tilt changing sunlight angle and day length

Axial tilt changes solar angle and day length through the year.

### 3. Light travels 3.0×10⁸ m/s. Time to cross 3.0×10¹¹ m is:
**Correct answer:** C. 1000 s

t=d/c=(3.0×10¹¹)/(3.0×10⁸)=1000 s.

### 4. One astronomical unit is approximately:
**Correct answer:** D. The average Earth–Sun distance

The astronomical unit is based on the average Earth–Sun distance.

### 5. For a circular approximation, average orbital speed is:
**Correct answer:** A. 2πr/T

Average speed equals orbital circumference divided by period.

### 6. In a nearly circular orbit, gravitational acceleration points:
**Correct answer:** B. Toward the central body

The inward acceleration changes velocity direction.

### 7. An object in an elliptical solar orbit generally moves fastest when:
**Correct answer:** C. Closest to the Sun

It moves faster near perihelion as potential energy decreases and kinetic energy increases.

### 8. Which is safe?
**Correct answer:** D. Using official solar images or a certified supervised solar viewer

Direct solar viewing requires certified equipment and proper supervision; official data are safest.

## Stellar Spectra & Light Check

### 1. Dark lines in a stellar absorption spectrum can reveal:
**Correct answer:** B. Elements absorbing characteristic wavelengths

Characteristic spectral lines help identify composition.

### 2. A line shifts from 500.0 nm to 501.0 nm. Redshift z is:
**Correct answer:** C. 0.002

z=(501.0−500.0)/500.0=0.002.

### 3. For z=0.002 and c=300,000 km/s, v≈zc is:
**Correct answer:** D. 600 km/s

v≈0.002×300,000=600 km/s.

### 4. Apparent brightness depends on:
**Correct answer:** A. Luminosity, distance, extinction, and instrument response

Several physical and observational factors affect measured brightness.

### 5. Why are many X-ray telescopes placed in space?
**Correct answer:** B. Earth’s atmosphere absorbs most cosmic X rays

The atmosphere blocks most incoming cosmic X rays.

### 6. The Sun’s long-term energy source is primarily:
**Correct answer:** C. Hydrogen fusion in the core

Core fusion converts hydrogen into helium and releases energy.

### 7. A coronal mass ejection is best described as:
**Correct answer:** D. A large release of magnetized plasma from the Sun

CMEs eject magnetized solar plasma and can disturb near-Earth space.

### 8. A false-color astronomical image is:
**Correct answer:** A. A processed representation mapping measured data to displayed colors

False color can encode wavelengths or intensity and must be labeled.

## Exoplanet Transit & Evidence Check

### 1. A transit depth of 1% gives Rp/Rs≈:
**Correct answer:** C. 0.10

Rp/Rs≈√0.01=0.10.

### 2. A planet radius is half its star’s radius in a simple model. Transit depth is:
**Correct answer:** D. 25%

Depth=(0.5)²=0.25, or 25%.

### 3. Repeated transits separated by 8 days most directly estimate:
**Correct answer:** A. Orbital period

Consistent spacing estimates the orbital period.

### 4. Transit surveys are especially sensitive to:
**Correct answer:** B. Large planets in short-period, edge-on orbits

Large, frequent, aligned transits are easier to detect.

### 5. Which can imitate a planetary transit?
**Correct answer:** C. A grazing eclipsing binary

Eclipsing stellar systems can create transit-like dips.

### 6. The radial-velocity method detects:
**Correct answer:** D. A star’s line-of-sight motion caused by an orbiting companion

An orbiting planet can make its star move toward and away from us.

### 7. A planet in a habitable zone is:
**Correct answer:** A. At a distance where surface liquid water could be possible under suitable conditions

Habitable-zone location is only one condition and does not establish habitability or life.

### 8. A strong exoplanet claim should report:
**Correct answer:** B. Method, signal, uncertainty, false-positive tests, and confirmation status

Transparent methods and uncertainty are required.

**Correct answer:** A. Large-scale cosmic expansion

### 7. The cosmic microwave background is:

The CMB is relic radiation from an early hot phase.

### 8. The Big Bang model directly explains:

It models cosmic evolution from an early hot dense state, with important open questions remaining.

## Constructed Response 1 — Scale, Orbit, Spectra, and Model Limits

- One-way light time: `(1.80 × 10¹¹ m)/(3.00 × 10⁸ m/s) = 600 s = 10.0 min`.
- Circular-average orbital speed: `v = 2πr/T = 2π(2.00 × 10¹¹)/(5.00 × 10⁷) ≈ 2.51 × 10⁴ m/s = 25.1 km/s`.
- Spectral redshift: `z = (657.0 − 656.3)/656.3 ≈ 0.001067`.
- Small-shift speed approximation: `v ≈ zc ≈ 320.0 km/s`.
- In a circular orbit, velocity is tangent to the path while gravitational acceleration points toward the central body.
- Valid limitations include circular rather than elliptical orbit, average radius, constant period, point-mass approximation, no perturbations, no relativity, vacuum light speed, uncertain wavelength calibration, line blending, and the limited range of v≈zc.
- Safety boundaries include no unfiltered solar viewing, no lasers, no rooftop or roadside observation, no observation alone, no improvised radio transmitter, and no restricted-site access.

## Constructed Response 2 — Transit, Galaxies, Expansion, and Evidence

- Fractional transit depth: `0.81% = 0.0081`.
- Radius ratio: `Rp/Rs = √0.0081 = 0.090`.
- Transit intervals: `6.2−1.2 = 5.0 days` and `11.2−6.2 = 5.0 days`; modeled period `5.0 days`.
- Hubble-law speed: `v = H₀d = 70 × 80 = 5600 km/s`.
- Hubble time for the selected classroom value: approximately `13.97 billion years`.
- A transit can be imitated by eclipsing binaries, blended stars, starspots, instrumental systematics, or grazing events. Repetition and independent confirmation are required.
- Habitable-zone position concerns possible surface liquid-water conditions under suitable atmospheric assumptions; it does not establish atmosphere, oceans, biology, or inhabitants.
- Big Bang evidence should combine galaxy redshift trends, the cosmic microwave background, and primordial light-element abundance. Observations are measured signals; calculations transform measurements; models connect mechanisms and predictions; extrapolations extend beyond directly sampled ranges; open questions remain.
