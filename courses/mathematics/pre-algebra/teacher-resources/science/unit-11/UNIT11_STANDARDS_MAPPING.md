## Alignment Boundaries

Required assessment uses apparent sky motion, seasons and lunar phases, scientific notation, astronomical units, light-time, circular-average orbital speed, qualitative elliptical-orbit energy, solar-system formation, introductory solar fusion, spectra, small-redshift calculations, stellar life cycles, transit depth, detection bias, cosmic scale, Hubble-law calculations and major Big Bang evidence.

Detailed celestial-coordinate transformations, full Kepler derivations, Newtonian universal-gravitation calculations with G, radiative transfer, stellar-structure equations, general relativity, advanced spectroscopy, orbital determination, cosmological distance metrics and precision-parameter estimation are outside the required Grade 9 boundary.

Khaemenes Academy uses these frameworks as planning references and does not claim endorsement, examination registration or automatic credit equivalence.
