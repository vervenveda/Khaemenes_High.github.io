| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Question, target, frame and scale | Missing | Confused | Partial | Clear | Clear and independently verified |
| Direct evidence and sources | Missing | Unsupported | One record | Several authoritative records | Processing and uncertainty evaluated |
| Physics calculation and units | Missing | Major errors | Partial | Correct | Correct with scale and reasonableness checks |
| Instrument and data pathway | Missing | Incorrect | Basic | Accurate | Accurate with calibration, cadence and resolution |
| Evidence classification | Missing | Confused | Partial | Clear | Raw, processed, modeled and inferred layers distinguished |
| Alternatives and bias | Missing | One-sided | Partial | Balanced | False positives and selection effects evaluated |

| Decision and communication | Incomplete | Confusing | Understandable | Clear | Clear, cited, accessible and measurable |
