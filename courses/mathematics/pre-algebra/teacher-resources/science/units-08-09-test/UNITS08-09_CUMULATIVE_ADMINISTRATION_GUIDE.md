# Units 08–09 Cumulative Benchmark Administration Guide

## Purpose

This benchmark measures cumulative mastery after Unit 09 and establishes the transition checkpoint before Unit 10: Earth Systems & Biogeochemical Cycles.

## Suggested Administration

- Objective section: 75–100 minutes
- Constructed responses: 75–100 minutes
- One extended session or two to three shorter sessions
- Calculator, blank paper and supplied constants permitted
- Accessibility supports may include read-aloud, additional time, enlarged graphs, keyboard input, breaks, captions, color-independent diagrams, equation reference cards and language scaffolds that do not change the scientific reasoning assessed

## Security

Keep the answer key outside student-facing directories. Student pages contain no educator-key links.

## Scoring

- Objective: 60 points
- Constructed: four responses at 10 points each
- Recommended thresholds: 48/60 objective, 24/30 diagnostic per unit and 80/100 overall
- Permit corrections and retakes
- Record both unit diagnostic scores

## Earth-Science Bridge

The final response assesses physics and information-literacy skills in an Earth-observation setting. Students are not expected to know Unit 10 content in advance. Score their ability to apply energy, circuit, wave, signal, uncertainty, model and inference concepts to a new system.

## Safety and Privacy

- No mains electricity, opened devices, battery shorting, laser exposure, loud-sound exposure, strong-magnet testing, road testing, human collision testing or improvised transmitters are required.
- Earth-observation examples may use supplied or published data.
- Do not require students to reveal private location, health, family, biometric or device information.
