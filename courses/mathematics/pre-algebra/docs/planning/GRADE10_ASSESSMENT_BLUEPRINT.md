# Grade 10 Assessment Blueprint

## Diagnostic
- 36 definitive-answer prerequisite questions
- balanced correct-answer positions: 9 A, 9 B, 9 C, 9 D

## Unit mastery
- 20 questions per unit
- 80% mastery target
- best score retained
- correction and retest opportunities

## Midterm
- 60 questions
- Units 01–06
- end of Week 18
- balanced: 15 A, 15 B, 15 C, 15 D

## Final
- 100 questions
- complete 36-week course
- balanced: 25 A, 25 B, 25 C, 25 D

## Capstone
- 100-point rubric
- written portfolio
- validation and sensitivity analysis
- oral defence
- readiness reflection
