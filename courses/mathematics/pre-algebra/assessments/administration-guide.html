Administration & Scoring Guide

A consistent, transparent protocol for using the midterm and final examination as homeschool portfolio evidence.

Suggested conditions

  • Use a quiet setting and identify permitted tools before beginning.
  • The midterm may be completed in one session with a short break. The final may be split into two supervised sessions.
  • Save a draft before a break or device change.
  • Do not provide content hints during scored administration.
  • Allow ordinary accessibility supports that do not supply answers.

Definitive-answer policy

  • Each scored question has exactly one correct option.
  • No answer choice duplicates an equivalent numerical or algebraic form.
  • No item depends on personal preference or an undefined meaning of “best.”
  • When a convention matters, the item states it explicitly.
  • Answer positions are balanced to reduce pattern guessing.

Suggested grading

Use the percentage shown by the assessment engine. A conventional scale is A=90–100, B=80–89.9, C=70–79.9, D=60–69.9, F below 60. The parent or program administrator remains responsible for the final course-grade and credit decision.

A suggested course formula is: unit/coursework average 40%, midterm 20%, final exam 30%, capstone 10%.

Corrections and retesting

After scoring, review explanations and require written corrections for missed items. A retest may be administered after additional instruction. Preserve both attempts or clearly label which attempt is the official record.

Portfolio evidence

Retain the printed or exported score, selected student work, the capstone, activity logs, and reading-material titles. The certificate is a parent-issued course record, not a government-issued credential or annual evaluation.

Florida note: Florida Statutes §1002.41 requires a contemporaneous activity log naming reading materials, work samples, two-year portfolio retention, and an annual educational evaluation. A notice of termination is filed with the district when the home education program ends.

Privacy and integrity

All assessment data remains in the current browser unless exported. Avoid entering unnecessary sensitive information on shared devices. Keep exported records in the family’s chosen secure portfolio location.
