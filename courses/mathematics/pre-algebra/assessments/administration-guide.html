Administration & Scoring Guide

Pre-Algebra uses aligned weekly checks, cumulative unit evidence, and mixed-evidence midterm/final assessments so an 80% mastery result reflects the depth of the instruction rather than topic recognition alone.

Assessment Alignment v2

What Mastery Must Demonstrate

Assessment evidence should sample the mathematics at the level it was taught. Across the course, checks deliberately include procedural fluency, multi-step application, representation and interpretation, error analysis, and transfer or reasoning.

Weekly ruleEach instructional week has a distinct 10-question mastery check aligned to that week’s focus. Eight correct answers are required to reach the 80% threshold. A generic unit quiz may not be reused as proof of mastery for a different week.

Weekly Learning Gate

10 Questions · 8 Required

Weekly checks are designed to reveal whether the learner can use the week’s ideas, not merely recognize vocabulary.

  • 10 unique questions for each of 36 weeks.
  • 80% means at least 8 of 10 correct.
  • Questions include context, interpretation, error analysis, or reasoning in addition to direct skill checks.
  • Scores below 80% trigger review, corrections, and retesting rather than erasing completed work.

Unit Evidence

Cumulative Mastery Checks

Unit mastery checks sample the full lesson sequence and retain written reasoning prompts alongside the scored items.

  • At least 20 scored items per unit mastery check.
  • At least two written reasoning/reflection prompts.
  • 80% remains the unit mastery target.
  • Review written work when the selected-response score and demonstrated reasoning appear inconsistent.

Midterm & Final

Mixed-Evidence Cumulative Mastery

The public midterm and final now combine auto-scored definitive-answer work with required constructed responses. The midterm contains seven constructed-response tasks across Units 01–07; the final contains ten cross-domain tasks covering reasoning, modelling, verification, interpretation, and Algebra I readiness.

70/30 evidence modelSelected-response mathematics contributes 70% of the mixed-evidence score and evaluator-reviewed constructed responses contribute 30%.
Three-part 80% ruleFull cumulative mastery is recorded only when the learner earns at least 80% on the selected-response portion, at least 80% on the constructed-response rubric, and at least 80% overall.

Human Review

Constructed-Response Rubric

Use the 0–4 rubric shown inside the cumulative assessment. Review the mathematics, not writing style.

  1. 4: complete/correct reasoning with relevant representation, units, verification, and interpretation.
  2. 3: substantially correct with a minor omission or non-conceptual error.
  3. 2: partial understanding with a conceptual gap or incomplete verification.
  4. 1: limited relevant work with major misconceptions or unsupported conclusions.
  5. 0: blank, unrelated, or no assessable reasoning.

Public Self-Check

Learning Evidence, Not Authentication

Public digital assessments support practice, correction cycles, retesting, and portfolio evidence. Browser-local scores and evaluator entries remain editable and are not confidential, digitally signed, or independently authenticated.

  • Keep representative scratch work or written reasoning.
  • Retain exported results when useful.
  • Have a parent, teacher, or program administrator review evidence used for an official grade.

Summative Security

Confidential Administration Is Separate

When confidential summative testing is required, use a separately issued supervised assessment whose questions and answer key have not been published in the learner-facing repository. Previously public questions are not eligible for confidential reuse. Supervision of a public form can improve administration consistency, but it does not make the form confidential.

Portal Display

Current Mastery Weighting

The course portal’s running mastery display uses the evidence available in the local course record. It is not itself an official transcript grade.

  • Weekly quiz mastery: 55%
  • Midterm evidence: 15%
  • Final cumulative evidence: 20%
  • Mathematical modelling capstone: 10%

Course Grade

Administrator Judgment

A parent, teacher, school, or program administrator determines the official course grade and any credit awarded from the evidence they retain and review. The portal mastery display is one source of evidence, not a replacement for local academic judgment or jurisdictional requirements.

Mastery

Corrections & Retesting

Assessment should support learning as well as measurement. After a score below 80%, identify the weak domain, complete meaningful corrections, revisit the relevant lesson, and retest. Preserve attempts so growth remains visible.

Record Integrity

Know the Evidence Source

Browser-local result files may include provenance and review status, but an unsigned export is not proof of authorship or authenticity. Parent or administrator attestation remains required until a separately signed verification record is issued.

Student Record

Portfolio Evidence

Retain the materials that best demonstrate the learner’s progress and achievement.

  • Weekly and unit mastery results
  • Representative lessons and practice
  • Written corrections and constructed responses
  • Projects and modelling capstone evidence
  • Midterm/final auto-score plus evaluator review

Privacy & Stewardship

Keep Records Purposeful

Use only the student information needed for the academic record. Keep exported or printed records in the family’s, school’s, or program’s chosen secure portfolio location and follow applicable recordkeeping requirements.

Administration Sequence

Recommended Cumulative Assessment Flow

  1. Identify permitted tools and accessibility supports before beginning.
  2. Complete the selected-response and constructed-response sections without content hints.
  3. Submit the assessment to obtain the auto-scored result.
  4. Review written responses using the 0–4 rubric.
  5. Save the evaluator review; the engine then calculates the mixed-evidence overall result.
  6. If any mastery component is below 80%, review, correct, and retest rather than treating the first attempt as final.
  7. Export or print the evidence chosen for the learner’s portfolio.
