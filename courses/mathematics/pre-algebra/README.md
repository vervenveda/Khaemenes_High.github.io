# Khaemenes Global Pre-Algebra

**Course Code:** KH-MATH-PA  
**Duration:** 36 weeks  
**Level:** Secondary readiness / open-age bridge  
**Delivery:** Self-paced · Teacher-led · Blended · Summer bridge  
**Pathways:** Foundation · Core · Extended  

Khaemenes Global Pre-Algebra is a complete 36-week bridge course designed to build mathematical fluency, reasoning, confidence, and Algebra I readiness through a structured sequence of number, algebra, geometry, data, and modelling experiences.

The course includes a readiness diagnostic, 13 instructional units, mastery tracking, differentiated practice pathways, family and teacher support materials, public self-check assessments, records/certificate support, offline capability, and built-in audit tooling.

---

## Course Philosophy

Students are expected to understand mathematical structure, explain reasoning, compare representations, estimate before calculating, check reasonableness, apply mathematics in unfamiliar contexts, and connect mathematics to practical, scientific, financial, and civic situations.

The readiness diagnostic is intended to guide support, not label or punish a learner.

---

## Mastery Model

| Level | Label | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Beginning | Needs direct instruction and supported practice. |
| 2 | Developing | Shows partial understanding with inconsistent application. |
| 3 | Proficient | Applies the skill accurately in familiar contexts. |
| 4 | Mastered | Explains, transfers, and applies the skill in unfamiliar contexts. |

Lesson completion is mastery-based rather than checkbox-based. The shared lesson engine uses an **80% mastery threshold** and reconstructs completion from actual lesson scores so stale browser state does not override current mastery evidence.

## 36-Week Course Map

### Unit 00 — Mathematical Readiness & Diagnostic
**1 week**

Essential arithmetic and notation review, identification of strengths and unfinished learning, support selection by skill, and personal goal setting.

### Unit 01 — Number Systems, Factors & Estimation
**2 weeks**

Number classification, factors and multiples, primes and divisibility, GCF/LCM, estimation, reasonableness, and order of operations.

### Unit 02 — Integers & Absolute Value
**3 weeks**

Signed numbers, comparison and ordering, absolute value, integer operations, and number-line modelling.

### Unit 03 — Fractions, Decimals & Rational Numbers
**3 weeks**

Equivalent fractions, rational-number operations, fraction/decimal/percent conversion, estimation, and multistep applications.

### Unit 04 — Ratios, Rates & Proportional Reasoning
**3 weeks**

Ratios, unit rates, tables, graphs, equations, double number lines, direct proportion, scale drawings, and proportional vs. nonproportional relationships.

### Unit 05 — Percent, Financial Mathematics & Change
**3 weeks**

Percent of a quantity, increase/decrease, discounts, tax, tip, markup, simple interest, financial comparisons, percentage points, and relative change.

### Unit 06 — Powers, Roots & Scientific Notation
**2 weeks**

Integer exponents, exponent properties, square roots, scientific notation, and powers of ten.

### Unit 07 — Variables, Expressions & Algebraic Structure
**3 weeks**

Terms, factors, coefficients, constants, verbal-to-algebraic translation, substitution, equivalent expressions, and combining like terms.

### Unit 08 — Equations, Formulas & Inverse Operations
**3 weeks**

One-step and multistep equations, balance models, inverse operations, checking solutions, rearranging formulas, and writing equations from context.

### Unit 09 — Inequalities & Constraints
**2 weeks**

Inequality symbols, graphing solution sets, one-step and multistep inequalities, reversal with negative multiplication/division, and contextual constraints.

### Unit 10 — Coordinates, Patterns, Functions & Linear Relationships
**4 weeks**

Four-quadrant coordinate plane, independent/dependent variables, tables, graphs, slope as rate of change, linear and nonlinear patterns, and informal function notation.

### Unit 11 — Geometry, Measurement & Scale
**3 weeks**

Perimeter, area, surface area, volume, angle relationships, scale, similarity, conceptual Pythagorean theorem, measurement conversion, and coordinate geometry.

### Unit 12 — Statistics, Probability & Data Decisions
**3 weeks**

Distributions, measures of center, variability, data displays, simple and compound probability, sampling, and misleading representations.

### Unit 13 — Mathematical Modelling Capstone
**1 week**

Students define a personal, scientific, environmental, financial, or civic problem, select representations, calculate and check results, explain assumptions and limitations, present a defensible conclusion, and reflect on Algebra I readiness.

## Assessment Policy

The course separates **public practice evidence** from any future confidential testing process.

### Public Assessments

The public midterm and final are classified as:

**public self-check · nonconfidential · non-authoritative**

They support practice, correction, retesting, progress evidence, portfolio evidence, and parent/administrator review.

| Assessment | Scope | Questions |
|---|---|---:|
| Midterm Public Self-Check | Units 01–07 | 56 |
| 36-Week Final Public Self-Check | Units 01–13 | 80 |

Browser-local scores are editable and are **not** confidential, digitally signed, independently authenticated, or authoritative. The parent, teacher, or program administrator determines the official course grade and any credit awarded from retained evidence.

### Confidential Assessment Rule

If confidential Midterm or Final versions are later issued:

- use newly authored questions;
- do not reuse previously public questions;
- hold answer keys separately;
- keep confidential assessment materials separate from learner-facing materials.

### Definitive Answer Policy

Scored items should have one correct answer, unique choices, stated conventions when material, no subjective “best answer” without a defined criterion, and explanations for scored lesson-bank items.
