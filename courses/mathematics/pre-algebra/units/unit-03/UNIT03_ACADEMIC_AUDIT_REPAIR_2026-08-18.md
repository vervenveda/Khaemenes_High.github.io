# Unit 03 Academic Audit + Repair

## Scope
Fractions, Decimals & Rational Numbers — 8 lessons, Foundation/Core/Extended practice, 24-question cumulative mastery check, answer key, unit fallbacks, and open-age presentation.

## Findings and repairs
- Verified the mathematics in all 160 lesson questions and the existing pathway/mastery banks.
- Repaired Lesson 05 Question 17, which used two mathematically equivalent answer choices (`−1/2` and `−4/8`) plus `Both A and C`. It now asks for simplest form with one unambiguous correct answer.
- Expanded Foundation practice from 15 to 20 questions.
- Expanded Core practice from 18 to 20 questions.
- Expanded Extended practice from 18 to 20 questions.
- Changed all pathway-practice mastery thresholds from 75% to 80%.
- Rebalanced every 20-question lesson and practice bank to exactly 5 A / 5 B / 5 C / 5 D correct-answer positions.
- Rebalanced the 24-question cumulative mastery check to exactly 6 A / 6 B / 6 C / 6 D.
- Preserved the 24-question cumulative mastery check for broader strand coverage and labeled its passing requirement explicitly: at least 20/24 correct.
- Resynchronized `assessment/answer-key.json` after answer-choice reordering.
- Resynchronized all embedded `window.UNIT_MAP_FALLBACK` copies with the canonical 80% unit map.
- Reframed Unit 03 architecture as open-age Pre-Algebra and removed Grade 9 breadcrumb labeling from the two unit landing variants.

## Academic verdict
Unit 03 is suitable as an open-age Pre-Algebra rational-number unit after these repairs. The progression remains:
fraction meaning → equivalence/simplifying → compare/order → add/subtract → multiply → divide → decimal operations/repeating decimals → conversions/estimation/applications.
