Lesson 04 · Rational Reasoning Studio

Ungraded investigations: Show models, equations, and labelled units. Explain the reasoning for your answers, not only the arithmetic. These extensions do not alter the ten original questions.

A · Community kitchen inventory

A community kitchen has 3 1/2 kg of flour. It uses 1 3/4 kg for bread and 5/8 kg for biscuits, then receives 2/3 kg from a neighbor. Write a single expression and calculate the final amount, keeping all units.

B · Hiking altitude log

A student hikes an elevation change of +3/4 km, then −5/6 km, then +1/3 km. Find the net signed change, choose a common denominator, and explain whether the final elevation is above or below the starting point.

C · Design a school garden border

A garden border is made from three lengths: 2 2/5 m, 1 3/4 m, and 7/10 m. Calculate the total length required. If the class has 5 m of material, determine the amount left over or still needed.

D · Budget with decimals and fractions

A club starts with $12.50, buys supplies for $4 3/4, and receives $2.25 in donations. Find the final balance using both decimals and fractions, then explain why the results match.

E · Investigate an incorrect sum

A student claims 1/3 + 1/4 = 2/7 by adding numerators and denominators. Show a correct area or fraction-strip model, calculate the correct sum, and explain why like units are required.
