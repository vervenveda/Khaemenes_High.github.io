Lesson 07 · Rational Reasoning Studio

Ungraded investigations: Show models, equations, units, and reasoning. These five activities supplement rather than replace the original ten questions.

A · Aquarium water experiment

A 6.4-liter aquarium container holds 1.75 liters before three equal 0.85-liter pours are added. Find the total water volume and remaining capacity. Explain your order of operations and whether any water spills.

B · Read the repeating pattern

Express 1/3 and 2/9 as repeating decimals, then estimate their sum to the nearest hundredth. Give the exact sum as a fraction and explain why a rounded decimal is not an exact substitute.

C · Robotics battery readings

A student's robot begins at 9.6 volts and loses 0.35 volt during each of 8 trials. Calculate the total loss and final reading. Another battery loses 1.8 volts over 6 equal trials; find its loss per trial.

D · Build a termination test

Convert 3/8, 7/20, 5/12 and 2/11 into decimals. Identify which terminate and which repeat. Explain how a reduced denominator's prime factors help predict the decimal form.

E · Find the decimal-place error

A student claims 0.7 × 0.06 = 0.42 because 7 × 6 = 42. Calculate the correct result, use an estimate to show why 0.42 is unreasonable, and explain decimal place value.

Reflection

When does rounding help communication, and when can it hide important mathematical information?
