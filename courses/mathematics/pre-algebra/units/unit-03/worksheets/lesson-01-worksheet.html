Lesson 01 · Rational Reasoning Studio

Ungraded investigations: Show models, equations, and labelled units. Explain the reasoning for your answers, not only the arithmetic. These extensions do not alter the ten original questions.

A · The community garden

A garden has 12 equal planting plots. Three plots grow herbs, five grow vegetables, and four grow flowers. Express each share as a fraction and decimal, identify which fraction is greatest, and explain what the denominator describes.

B · Map the trail

A walking trail is 3 1/4 miles long. One family walks 1 3/4 miles while another walks 2.5 miles. Locate all three quantities on a number line, convert the mixed numbers to improper fractions, and identify who has covered more distance.

C · Arctic laboratory samples

Scientists compare −1 1/2°C, −0.75°C, 0°C, and +1/4°C. Order them, identify the coldest sample, and explain which values are rational and why zero is a valid reference point.

D · Build a fraction museum

Create three different representations of 7/4 using an area model, an improper fraction, and a mixed number. Explain why they all represent the same amount and where the value lies between whole-number benchmarks.

E · Correct the denominator mistake

A student says 5/0 should be treated as 5 because zero means there are no extra pieces. Explain why a fraction with denominator zero is undefined, and contrast it with 0/5 using an equal-sharing model.

Reflection

Which representation helps you understand a rational value most clearly, and when is another representation more useful?
