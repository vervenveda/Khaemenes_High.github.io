Lesson 06 · Rational Reasoning Studio

Ungraded investigations: Show models, equations, units, and reasoning. These five activities supplement rather than replace the original ten questions.

A · Cut the science ribbons

A classroom has 7 1/2 meters of ribbon and cuts it into sections 3/8 meter long. How many full sections are possible? Confirm using multiplication and explain why the quotient is greater than 7 1/2.

B · Bike tour speed puzzle

A cyclist rides 5/6 mile in 1/4 hour at a constant pace. Find the rate in miles per hour, then estimate distance in 3/5 hour. Show division followed by multiplication, and label units.

C · Share the community harvest

A garden yields 4 1/2 kilograms of strawberries. Families each receive 3/4 kilogram. Determine the number of full shares, and explain whether any fruit remains. Verify by multiplication.

D · Design a miniature bridge

A bridge blueprint shows a beam 2 2/5 inches long. Each miniature segment should be 3/10 inch. How many segments will fit? If students use 3/4 of them, how many segments are used? Explain both operations.

E · Audit the reciprocal

A student calculates (3/4) ÷ (2/5) by multiplying 3/4 × 2/5 and gets 3/10. Identify the error, solve correctly, verify by multiplication, and explain the difference between dividing by 2/5 and multiplying by 2/5.

Reflection

How does the question 'how many groups fit?' make fraction division more understandable?
