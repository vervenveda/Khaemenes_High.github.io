Lesson 03 · Rational Reasoning Studio

Ungraded investigations: Show models, equations, and labelled units. Explain the reasoning for your answers, not only the arithmetic. These extensions do not alter the ten original questions.

A · Mountain weather ranking

Four mountain stations report −3/4°C, −0.6°C, +1/8°C, and −1.1°C. Rank them from coldest to warmest, convert as needed, and justify at least two comparisons using a number line.

B · Race results detective

Four runners completed 5/8, 0.58, 3/5, and 0.7 of a course. Rank their progress, determine the leader, and explain why comparing the numerators alone would give a misleading result.

C · Community survey chart

Three clubs report participation rates of 7/12, 0.6, and 5/9. Convert all to a common representation, rank them, and discuss whether rounding to one decimal place could hide a difference.

D · Invent a close comparison

Construct two distinct rational numbers strictly between 1/2 and 3/4. Write each as a fraction and decimal and prove the required inequalities.

E · Correct the magnitude claim

A student argues −0.9 is greater than −0.45 because 0.9 is greater than 0.45. Correct the comparison, distinguish numerical order from absolute value, and show the values on a number line.

Reflection

Which comparison strategy saves you the most work, and what clues tell you to choose it?
