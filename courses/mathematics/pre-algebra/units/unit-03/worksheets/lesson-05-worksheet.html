Lesson 05 · Rational Reasoning Studio

Ungraded investigations: Show models, equations, units, and reasoning. These five activities supplement rather than replace the original ten questions.

A · Design a nature trail

A 3/4-mile nature trail has 2/3 of its length through forest. Find the forest distance and the remaining open-land distance. Draw a fraction model and explain why multiplying is appropriate.

B · Community garden beds

A school has 2 1/2 garden beds, each with 3/4 square meter of planting area per bed unit. Calculate the total planting area and explain how to represent 2 1/2 as an improper fraction.

C · Robotics scale model

A robotics team builds a model whose length is 3/5 that of a 2 1/4-foot prototype. Find the model's length in feet and inches. Explain how you know the model must be shorter.

D · Inventory discount detective

An art club orders six packets, each containing 3/8 kilogram of modelling clay. It uses 2/3 of the clay for sculpture. Find the original mass, mass used and mass left over; show intermediate steps.

E · Correct the multiplication trick

A student claims 2/3 × 4/5 = 6/8 by adding across the fractions. Find the correct product, draw an area model showing parts of parts, and describe how a product of two positive fractions less than 1 should compare with each factor.

Reflection

How does thinking of multiplication as scaling help you predict whether an answer is reasonable?
