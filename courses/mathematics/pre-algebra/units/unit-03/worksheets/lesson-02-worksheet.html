Lesson 02 · Rational Reasoning Studio

Ungraded investigations: Show models, equations, and labelled units. Explain the reasoning for your answers, not only the arithmetic. These extensions do not alter the ten original questions.

A · Design a mosaic

A mosaic has 48 equal tiles, 18 of which are blue. Write the fraction of blue tiles, simplify it to lowest terms, create two other equivalent fractions, and explain why the color proportion remains unchanged.

B · Recipe ratio rescue

One batch of a school-cafe drink uses 6/8 cup of juice. Another recipe lists 9/12 cup. Determine whether the two amounts are equivalent, then scale both recipes for four batches and explain a reliable comparison method.

C · Estimate the hike

A hiker has completed 17/36 of a trail. Decide whether this is closer to 0, 1/2, or 1, and justify the benchmark using an equivalent fraction or inequality. Predict whether another hiker at 5/9 has gone farther.

D · Make a scale blueprint

A design uses 3/5 inch to represent 12 feet. Find an equivalent ratio for 2 2/5 inches on the drawing, using consistent scaling. Explain how you can check that the scale stayed proportional.

E · Repair the shortcut

A student simplifies 24/36 to 12/18 and stops, claiming it is simplest form. Find the greatest common factor, simplify completely, and explain how to verify equivalence without changing the fraction's value.

Reflection

How can you tell that simplifying a fraction changes its appearance but not its value?
