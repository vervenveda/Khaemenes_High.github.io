Lesson 08 · Rational Reasoning Studio

Ungraded investigations: Show models, equations, units, and reasoning. These five activities supplement rather than replace the original ten questions.

A · The school market challenge

A club sells a craft for $24.80 and offers a 25% discount. Find the discount and sale price. If materials cost $11.35, find the profit on one discounted sale and explain why the discount is based on the original price.

B · Science fair mixture

A science club has 3 1/2 liters of solution and uses 2/5 of it for an experiment. It divides the remaining solution equally among 6 jars. Find the amount used and the volume in each jar in fraction and decimal form.

C · Map the hiking expedition

A team hikes 2.4 miles in the morning, 1 3/5 miles in the afternoon, and then walks back 0.75 mile. Calculate the net distance from the start along a straight trail and total distance traveled. Explain why the two answers differ.

D · Create an event budget

A student council starts with $60, spends 35% of that amount on supplies, and then receives three donations of $8.50 each. Find the final balance. If tickets cost $4.25 each, how many whole tickets could be purchased with the final balance? Explain any remainder.

E · Investigate estimation

A student estimates 19.8 × 0.48 as about 100 and concludes the exact answer 9.504 must be wrong. Re-estimate by rounding sensibly, compute the product, and explain why multiplying by a number below 1 should reduce a positive starting value.

Reflection

How does choosing the right representation make a multi-step rational-number problem easier or more trustworthy?
