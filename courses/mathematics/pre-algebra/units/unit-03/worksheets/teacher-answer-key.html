Lesson 01 · Rational Numbers & Fraction Meaning

1. A — 3 equal parts selected from a whole divided into 5 equal parts
The denominator names fifths; the numerator counts three.

2. A — The whole is partitioned into 11 equal parts
The denominator names the equal partition.

3. C — 2 3/4

4. C — −7/3
2×3+1=7, then apply the sign.

5. D — 3/4
3/4 is greatest.

6. A — It is rational because −6=−6/1
Every integer n equals n/1.

7. A — 0.272727…
Repeating decimals are rational.

8. A — −7/4
7/4=1.75.

9. A — Division by zero is undefined
a/0 is undefined.

10. C — −5/4
−1.25=−125/100=−5/4.
