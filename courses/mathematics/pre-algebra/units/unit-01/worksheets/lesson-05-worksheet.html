Lesson 05 · Reasoning Workshop

Ungraded extension: Show every stage of your reasoning, including corrections. These activities do not change the original ten-question assignment or recorded score.

A · Equal-priority operations

Evaluate 48 ÷ 6 × 2 and 48 ÷ (6 × 2). Write one line for every operation. Explain why the two answers differ and why multiplication and division must be handled left to right when no grouping changes their order.

B · Add and subtract in order

Evaluate 30 − 12 + 5 and 30 − (12 + 5). Explain why addition does not always happen before subtraction and how parentheses change the meaning.

C · Nested grouping and exponents

Evaluate 2[3 + (5 − 1)²] − 6. Show the nested grouping, exponent, multiplication, and subtraction in the correct sequence.

D · Substitute with care

Evaluate 3n² − 2n + 1 for n = 3 and n = −2. Substitute the values using parentheses and explain why (−2)² differs from −2².

E · Diagnose and repair

A student evaluates 24 ÷ 4 × 3 as 24 ÷ 12 = 2. Identify the precise mistake, write the correct steps, and create one example in which parentheses would make the student's method valid.

Reflection

Choose one problem and explain how you verified that your answer is reasonable.
