Family Resource

Unit 12 Family Guide

Support data literacy, probability reasoning, and careful interpretation without turning everyday life into a test.

Helpful questions

  • Who or what does this data represent?
  • How was the sample selected?
  • What do center and spread each tell us?
  • Could the graph design exaggerate a difference?
  • Is this a probability model or an observed relative frequency?
  • Does the evidence show association or justify a causal claim?

Everyday connections

Compare weather summaries, sports statistics, household budgets, transit times, environmental measurements, polls, product reviews, and game probabilities. Protect privacy and use fictional data when appropriate.

Encouragement

Ask learners to explain what a statistic means in context, not just calculate it. Praise careful uncertainty language and the willingness to question a graph or claim.
