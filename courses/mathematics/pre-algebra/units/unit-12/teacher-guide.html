Unit 12 Teacher Guide

Three-week pacing, misconceptions, differentiation, ethical data routines, probability simulation, and assessment guidance.

Pacing

Week 1: questions, sampling, displays, and center. Week 2: variability, comparisons, misleading graphs, and simple probability. Week 3: compound probability, simulation, bias, inference, mastery, and project.

High-priority misconceptions

  • Mean is not always the best “typical” value.
  • Range and IQR describe different parts of spread.
  • Histogram bars touch because intervals are continuous; bar-graph bars represent separate categories.
  • Experimental probability need not equal theoretical probability in a small number of trials.
  • Association does not by itself establish causation.
  • Random selection and random assignment serve different purposes.

Assessment use

Use static items for formative review. Vary values and contexts for high-stakes use. Require written interpretation, source checks, denominators, and uncertainty—not only calculations.

Ethics and privacy

Prefer public, fictional, aggregated, or anonymous data. Avoid collecting sensitive personal data. Discuss consent, data minimization, misleading displays, accessibility, and limitations.
