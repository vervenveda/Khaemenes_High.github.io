Lesson 08 · Data & Evidence Studio

Five ungraded investigations: analyze data, explain choices, and avoid claims stronger than the evidence supports.

A · Convenience sample

A school seeks opinions on bus safety but surveys only drivers who arrive by car. Explain the bias and propose a fairer plan.

B · Association and causation

Ice-cream sales and swimming attendance rise together in summer. Explain a lurking variable and why the relationship is not proof of causation.

C · Transparent headline

A poll finds 60% approval among 40 volunteers. Explain what cannot be claimed and write a cautious summary.

D · Response-rate issue

A survey contacts 500 people and receives 125 replies. Calculate response rate and discuss nonresponse risk.

E · Evidence-based decision

Two clubs report average waiting time 8 minutes (n=5) and 9 minutes (n=100). List other evidence needed before declaring the first faster.

Reflection / Evidence Audit

Choose one data or probability claim from this lesson. Identify the evidence source, show the relevant calculation/display reasoning, name one possible bias or uncertainty, and write a conclusion that does not overstate what the evidence proves.
