Lesson 01 · Data & Evidence Studio

Five ungraded investigations: analyze data, explain choices, and avoid claims stronger than the evidence supports.

A · School commute survey

A school has 1,200 students. Investigators randomly survey 90 about commute minutes. Identify population, sample and variable; explain what random sampling helps and cannot guarantee.

B · Biased lunch poll

A cafeteria polls only students purchasing salads to decide whether all students want more salads. Explain the selection bias and design an improved method.

C · Statistical question design

Compare 'What time does one student arrive?' with 'How do arrival times vary among students?' Explain which is statistical and propose data to collect.

D · Protecting student privacy

A club wants to publish each child's name, address and sleep hours to study average sleep. Propose a privacy-preserving plan.

E · Sample-size reasoning

Two independent, unbiased surveys of the same school ask 12 students and 240 students. Which tends to give a more stable estimate? What important limitation remains?

Reflection / Evidence Audit

Choose one data or probability claim from this lesson. Identify the evidence source, show the relevant calculation/display reasoning, name one possible bias or uncertainty, and write a conclusion that does not overstate what the evidence proves.
