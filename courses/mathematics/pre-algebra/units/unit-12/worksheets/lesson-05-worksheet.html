Lesson 05 · Data & Evidence Studio

Five ungraded investigations: analyze data, explain choices, and avoid claims stronger than the evidence supports.

A · Truncated axis

A bar chart compares 98 and 100 but begins its vertical axis at 97. Explain how this could distort impressions and suggest a fair display.

B · Compare medians and spread

Group A: 4,5,6,7,8. Group B: 1,4,6,8,11. Find medians and ranges.

C · Which class is consistent?

Class X times: 12,12,13,13,14. Class Y: 7,10,13,16,19. Compare centers and variability.

D · Graph label audit

A graph compares monthly attendance for two years but provides no units, month labels or sample counts. Name three ways to make it interpretable.

E · Fair evidence claim

One class scored 2 points higher on average than another. Explain why the difference alone cannot prove a teaching method caused it.

Reflection / Evidence Audit

Choose one data or probability claim from this lesson. Identify the evidence source, show the relevant calculation/display reasoning, name one possible bias or uncertainty, and write a conclusion that does not overstate what the evidence proves.
