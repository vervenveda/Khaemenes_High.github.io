Lesson 02 · Data & Evidence Studio

Five ungraded investigations: analyze data, explain choices, and avoid claims stronger than the evidence supports.

A · Make a frequency table

Daily book counts for 10 students are 1,2,2,3,3,3,4,4,4,5. Construct a frequency table and identify the mode.

B · Histogram bin rules

Travel times (minutes) are 4,7,9,12,16,18,21,24. Count observations in bins 0–<10, 10–<20, 20–<30.

C · Box-plot landmarks

For 2,4,5,7,9,10,12, find median, lower-half median, upper-half median and full range.

D · Misleading dot plot

A dot plot shows one dot per five students but its caption says each dot equals one student. There are nine dots. Compare claimed and actual representation.

E · Choose a display

A school wants to show favorite genres (categories) and then daily reading minutes (numerical). Recommend a display for each and explain why.

Reflection / Evidence Audit

Choose one data or probability claim from this lesson. Identify the evidence source, show the relevant calculation/display reasoning, name one possible bias or uncertainty, and write a conclusion that does not overstate what the evidence proves.
