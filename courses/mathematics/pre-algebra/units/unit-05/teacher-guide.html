Teacher Guide

A three-week plan for percent reasoning, financial mathematics, decision quality, and responsible interpretation.

Mastery Check

Suggested pacing

  • Sessions 1–2: meaning, equivalence, and benchmarks
  • Sessions 3–4: part, rate, and whole
  • Sessions 5–6: percent change and multipliers
  • Sessions 7–9: discounts, tax, tips, commission, markup, profit
  • Sessions 10–11: simple interest and fees
  • Sessions 12–13: percentage points and repeated change
  • Sessions 14–15: project, review, and mastery check

Priority misconceptions

  • Using the new value instead of the original for percent change
  • Confusing the percent amount with the final total
  • Treating opposite percent changes as cancelling
  • Confusing percentage points with relative percent change
  • Ignoring fees or mismatched time units
  • Assuming markup and profit margin use the same base

Representation sequence

Move from hundred grids and benchmark percents to equations, proportions, multipliers, before-change-after tables, and complete financial comparisons. Require units and reference bases in explanations.

Differentiation

Foundation: money models, percent bars, benchmark decomposition, and calculators after estimation.

Core: multiple solution methods and contextual interpretation.

Extended: reverse percent, repeated change, margin, break-even, and critique of claims.

Assessment use

The browser-based mastery check is formative. Review domain summaries, written reasoning, and project evidence. Use alternate forms or supervised delivery for protected assessments.

Ethics and safety

Keep examples age-appropriate and educational. Do not ask students to disclose family income, debt, account numbers, or private financial records. Use fictional or public datasets.
