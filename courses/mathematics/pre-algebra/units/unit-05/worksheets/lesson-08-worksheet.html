Lesson 08 · Percent & Financial Reasoning Studio

Five ungraded missions: model each situation before calculating. Show your steps, label units, and explain any assumptions.

A · Percentage-point shift

Survey approval grows from 42% to 54%. Find the change in percentage points and the relative percent increase.

B · Two discounts

A $150 item is discounted 20%, then 15%. Calculate final price and effective single discount.

C · Repeated growth

A tree grows 10% in height each year. Starting at 2 meters, estimate height after 2 years and compare with adding 20%.

D · Budget recovery

A club's $1,000 budget drops 20%. What percent increase is needed from the reduced budget to return to $1,000?

E · Critique a headline

A statistic rises from 2% to 3%. A headline calls this a 50-percentage-point increase. Explain the mistake and state both changes.

Reflection

Explain what you learned about percentage points, repeated change, and transparent financial decisions. Include one worked example and explain how you checked that your result was reasonable.
