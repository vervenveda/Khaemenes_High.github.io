Lesson 01 · Percent & Financial Reasoning Studio

Five ungraded missions: model each situation before calculating. Show your steps, label units, and explain any assumptions.

A · Mural progress

A mural has 240 equal tiles. Volunteers paint 37.5% of them. How many tiles are painted, and what percent remain?

B · Library collection

A library display includes 48 science books, representing 30% of all displayed books. Find the total and describe 30% as a fraction.

C · More than a whole

An artist increases a 60 cm design to 125% of its original size. Find the new dimension and explain why it exceeds 60 cm.

D · Correct the estimate

A student claims 0.8% of 1,000 visitors is 800 visitors. Find the correct count and identify the place-value mistake.

E · Create a percent model

Design a 100-square grid showing 12.5% shaded. Explain how you would represent half of one square and the equivalent fraction.

Reflection

Explain what you learned about percent as a per-hundred relationship and equivalent representations. Include one worked example and explain how you checked that your result was reasonable.
