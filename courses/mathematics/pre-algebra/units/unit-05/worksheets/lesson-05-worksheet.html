Lesson 05 · Percent & Financial Reasoning Studio

Five ungraded missions: model each situation before calculating. Show your steps, label units, and explain any assumptions.

A · Tax and discount order

A $120 jacket receives a 25% discount, then 8% sales tax. Calculate the final total and show both steps.

B · Restaurant bill

Four friends share a $68 food bill before a 7% tax and a 18% tip on the pretax food amount. Find total and equal shares.

C · Commission contract

A salesperson earns a $450 base plus 6% commission on $8,500 sales. Find gross pay.

D · Compare sale signs

Store A sells a $75 backpack for 20% off. Store B charges $62 with an additional 5% discount. Before tax, which is cheaper?

E · Challenge the wording

An advertisement offers 40% off then an extra 10% off. Is the combined discount 50%? Calculate the final price of a $200 item.

Reflection

Explain what you learned about discounts, taxes, tips, commissions, and order of operations. Include one worked example and explain how you checked that your result was reasonable.
