Lesson 02 · Percent & Financial Reasoning Studio

Five ungraded missions: model each situation before calculating. Show your steps, label units, and explain any assumptions.

A · Rainwater storage

A tank holds 360 liters and is 65% full. Find the water volume and remaining capacity.

B · Community garden harvest

The garden harvests 840 kg of food. A pantry receives 17.5%. How many kilograms does it receive?

C · Budget planning

A club has $480. It allocates 35% to supplies and 22.5% to transport. Find both amounts and money left.

D · Spot the estimate error

A student calculates 12% of $75 as $900. Explain why the answer is impossible and solve accurately.

E · Build a real scenario

Invent a situation in which 2.5% of a quantity equals 15. Determine the whole and explain units.

Reflection

Explain what you learned about finding a percent of a quantity with estimation. Include one worked example and explain how you checked that your result was reasonable.
