Lesson 04 · Percent & Financial Reasoning Studio

Five ungraded missions: model each situation before calculating. Show your steps, label units, and explain any assumptions.

A · Visitor increase

Museum visitors rise from 320 to 400 in one month. Find the absolute and percentage increase.

B · Water conservation

A school cuts weekly water use from 2,400 L to 1,860 L. Find the percentage decrease.

C · Asymmetric change

A price climbs from $80 to $100 then returns to $80. Are the increase and decrease both 25%? Explain.

D · Reading challenge

A learner reads 18 books one year and 27 the next. Calculate absolute and relative improvement.

E · Write a counterexample

Invent a situation showing that a 10% decrease does not undo a previous 10% increase.

Reflection

Explain what you learned about absolute versus relative change and the original-value base. Include one worked example and explain how you checked that your result was reasonable.
