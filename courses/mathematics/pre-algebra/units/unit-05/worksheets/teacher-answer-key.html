Unit 05 · Financial Reasoning Studio Worked Solutions

These explanations support the 40 ungraded extended missions. Credit mathematically equivalent methods, properly labelled units, and defensible assumptions; do not change original mastery scoring.

Lesson 01

A · Mural progress: 0.375 × 240 = 90 painted. The remaining 150 tiles are 62.5%.

B · Library collection: 30% = 3/10; 48 ÷ 0.30 = 160 books.

C · More than a whole: 1.25 × 60 = 75 cm, an increase of 15 cm (25%).

D · Correct the estimate: 0.8%=0.008; 0.008×1000=8 visitors. The student confused 0.8% with 80%.

E · Create a percent model: Shade 12 full squares and half of another; 12.5%=1/8=0.125.

Lesson 02

A · Rainwater storage: 0.65×360=234 L; 360−234=126 L remain.

B · Community garden harvest: 0.175×840=147 kg.

C · Budget planning: Supplies $168; transport $108; remainder $204.

D · Spot the estimate error: 12% of a positive amount is less than the whole; 0.12×75=$9.

E · Build a real scenario: Whole=15/0.025=600 units; context and units vary.

Lesson 03

A · School survey: 45/0.18=250 surveyed.

B · Reconstruct the original: The sale price is 70%; 63/0.70=$90.

C · Find the unknown rate: 72/240=0.30=30%.

D · Base confusion: 20/60=1/3≈33.33%. The whole is 60.

E · Reverse-engineer a goal: Goal=$84/0.35=$240; remaining $156.

A · Visitor increase: Increase 80 visitors; 80/320=25%.

D · Reading challenge: 9 more books; 9/18=50% increase.
