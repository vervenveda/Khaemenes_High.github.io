Lesson 06 · Percent & Financial Reasoning Studio

Five ungraded missions: model each situation before calculating. Show your steps, label units, and explain any assumptions.

A · Art market pricing

An artist spends $28 on materials and $12 on booth fees allocated to one artwork. If sold for $65, find profit and profit margin on revenue.

B · Bakery break-even

A baker has $150 fixed costs and $2 variable cost per loaf. Each loaf sells for $5. Find break-even quantity.

C · Loss analysis

A handmade item costs $80 to produce and sells for $68. Find loss amount and percent loss based on cost.

D · Markup versus margin

A shop buys a book for $20 and marks it up 25% on cost. Find selling price, profit, and profit margin on sale.

E · Decision scenario

A vendor pays $90 to rent a stall, spends $3 per drink, and charges $6.50 per drink. How many whole drinks must be sold to make a profit?

Reflection

Explain what you learned about markup, profit, loss, margin, and break-even reasoning. Include one worked example and explain how you checked that your result was reasonable.
