# Unit 5 — Percent, Financial Mathematics & Change

**Course:** Khaemenes Global Pre-Algebra  
**Unit ID:** `KH-MATH-PA-U05`  
**Duration:** 3 weeks / approximately 15 sessions  
**Status:** Open

## Contents

- Eight complete lessons
- Foundation, Core, and Extended practice pathways
- 24-question mastery check and answer key
- Multilingual vocabulary bridge (English, Spanish, French, Arabic, Portuguese)
- Financial Change Lab
- Consumer Financial Decision Investigation project
- Teacher and family guides
- Local-only progress, reflections, drafts, and exports
- Printable, responsive, reduced-motion-aware interface

## Instructional sequence

1. Percent Meaning, Benchmarks & Equivalent Forms
2. Finding a Percent of a Quantity
3. Finding the Percent Rate or the Whole
4. Percent Increase, Decrease & Relative Change
5. Discounts, Tax, Tips & Commission
6. Markup, Profit, Loss & Break-Even
7. Simple Interest, Fees & Financial Comparisons
8. Percentage Points, Repeated Change & Financial Decisions
