    Financial Change Lab

    Apply a percent change and inspect every component.

    Choose an original amount, rate, and change type. The lab calculates the percent amount, multiplier, and updated total.

    Original whole $200.00 100% reference amount
    Rate as decimal 0.15 15/100
    Percent amount $30.00 0.15 × $200.00
    Signed change +$30.00 increase
    Multiplier 1.15 115% of original
    New value $230.00 $200.00 × 1.15
