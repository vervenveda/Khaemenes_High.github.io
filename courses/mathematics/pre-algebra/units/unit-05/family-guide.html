Family Guide

How percent and financial mathematics describe everyday choices—and how families can support reasoning without taking over the work.

Foundation Practice

What this unit teaches

Students connect fractions, decimals, and percents; solve for parts, rates, and wholes; measure increase and decrease; calculate discounts, tax, tips, markup, profit, and simple interest; and compare options using complete costs and assumptions.

Helpful questions

What quantity represents 100%?
Are we finding the percent amount or the final total?
What is the original value?
Which fees or time periods matter?
Does this claim use percentage points or relative change?

Practice naturally

  • Estimate discounts while shopping.
  • Read receipts and separate subtotal, tax, tip, and total.
  • Compare unit prices and subscription totals.
  • Track a savings goal as a percent.
  • Discuss how ads present rates and fees.

Help without giving answers

  • Ask the learner to identify part, rate, and whole.
  • Use benchmark percents before a calculator.
  • Circle the original value in change problems.
  • Write all time units and fees.
  • Ask for an estimate and a reasonableness check.

Understanding pathways

Foundation adds hundred grids, benchmark percents, and guided tables.

Core contains the full expected sequence.

Extended adds reverse percent, repeated change, margin analysis, and claim critique.

Consumer-safety habits

  • Compare total cost, not only monthly payment.
  • Read whether a rate is annual or per period.
  • Look for fixed fees and changing rates.
  • Protect personal financial information.
  • Ask a trusted adult before entering contracts or sharing account details.
