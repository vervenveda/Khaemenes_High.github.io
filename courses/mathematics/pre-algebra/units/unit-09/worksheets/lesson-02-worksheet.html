Lesson 02 · Inequality & Constraints Studio

Five ungraded investigations. Translate each scenario, distinguish strict versus inclusive boundaries, show work, and test both an allowed value and a disallowed value when possible.

A · Trail altitude chart

A trail must stay above 120 m altitude. Describe the graph of a>120, distinguish the open boundary, and test a=120 and a=121.

B · Below-freezing reading

Temperatures are at most −4°C. Describe the number line and give three numbers that satisfy the rule, including the boundary.

C · Interval detective

A graph includes 2 and every number greater than 2. Write the inequality, interval notation, and a non-solution.

D · Fix the misplaced arrow

A student graphs x<−3 by shading to the right from a filled point at −3. Identify both mistakes and repair the graph.

E · Create matching representations

Choose an inequality whose boundary is 7 and excludes 7. Write it in words and interval notation, and explain the graph.

Reflection / Error Analysis

Choose one inequality from this lesson. Explain its boundary, solve or interpret it, describe the graph, identify one likely error, and verify a solution and a non-solution.
