Lesson 05 · Inequality & Constraints Studio

Five ungraded investigations. Translate each scenario, distinguish strict versus inclusive boundaries, show work, and test both an allowed value and a disallowed value when possible.

A · Community market budget

A market charges a $35 stall fee and $6 per item sold. A vendor can spend no more than $155. Write and solve for whole items n.

B · Two gym plans

Plan A costs $15 plus $4 per class, and Plan B costs $9 per class. For which nonnegative whole numbers of classes is A no more expensive?

C · Distribution sign check

Solve 3(2x−5)−4≤17 and verify one boundary value.

D · Variable both sides

A delivery route takes 7x+9 minutes in one plan and 4x+30 in another. Find the x-values where the first route is shorter.

E · Fix an equation swap

A student solves 2x+5≥17 by writing 2x+5=17 and concludes x=6 is the only solution. Correct the reasoning.

Reflection / Error Analysis

Choose one inequality from this lesson. Explain its boundary, solve or interpret it, describe the graph, identify one likely error, and verify a solution and a non-solution.
