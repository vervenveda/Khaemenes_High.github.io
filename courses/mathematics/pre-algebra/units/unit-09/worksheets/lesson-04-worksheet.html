1. Solve −5x>30.

(A) x>−6   (B) x<−6   (C) x>6   (D) x<6

(A) wrong arithmetic only   (B) failure to reverse the inequality   (C) using subtraction   (D) including equality

(A) x<−15   (B) x≤15   (C) x≤−15   (D) x≥−15

(A) addition/subtraction does not reverse order   (B) all negatives reverse   (C) only addition reverses   (D) symbols never reverse
