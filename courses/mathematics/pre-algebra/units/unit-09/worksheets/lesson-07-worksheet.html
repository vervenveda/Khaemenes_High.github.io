Directions: Show transformations, boundary inclusion, solution direction, graph when useful, and contextual constraints. Verify in the original inequality.

1. A $20 fee plus $6 per item must be at most $80.

(A) 20+6n≤80   (B) 20+6n≥80   (C) 6n−20≤80   (D) 20n+6≤80

2. Boxes hold 24 books. At least 130 books require:

(A) 5 boxes   (B) 5.42 boxes   (C) 6 boxes   (D) 7 boxes

3. A contextual answer should be checked for:

(A) symbolic truth only   (B) units, domain, boundary, and reasonableness   (C) font size   (D) whether it is positive only

4. For 8n≤60 where n is whole notebooks, maximum n is:

(A) 7.5   (B) 8   (C) 6   (D) 7

5. A physical length L usually requires:

(A) L<0   (B) L≤−1   (C) all real values   (D) L≥0

6. You need at least 75 volunteer hours h.

(A) h≤75   (B) h<75   (C) h≥75   (D) h>75

7. If 3 vans carry at most 8 people each and 21 people travel, vans needed:

(A) 2.625   (B) 2   (C) 4   (D) 3

8. A tank has 90 L and loses 6 L/min. Nonnegative volume requires:

(A) 90−6t≥0   (B) 90−6t≤0   (C) 6t≥90 only for all times   (D) 90+6t≥0

9. A student reports 6.25 buses. Best correction:

(A) drop units   (B) round down automatically   (C) bus counts are discrete; determine the required whole number from the inequality   (D) 6.25 buses is always valid

10. A maximum-capacity problem produces a negative item count. Response:

(A) change units randomly   (B) recheck model/solution and apply nonnegative constraint   (C) accept it   (D) remove the inequality
