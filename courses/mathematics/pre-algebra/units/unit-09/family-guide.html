Family Guide

Guidance for teaching, supporting, and discussing inequalities and constraints with precision and care.

Big idea

Inequalities describe many possible answers

An equation often identifies a value. An inequality describes a range, such as “at most,” “at least,” “less than,” or “between.”

Ask at home

Helpful questions

  • Is the boundary included?
  • Which direction should the graph shade?
  • Did a negative multiplication or division reverse the symbol?
  • Does the situation require whole numbers or nonnegative values?
  • Can you test one solution and one non-solution?

Everyday examples

Notice constraints

Look for capacity limits, age ranges, budgets, temperatures, schedules, package sizes, safety thresholds, and minimum requirements.

Support without giving answers

Encourage explanation

Ask the learner to read the inequality in words, draw a number line, identify the boundary, and test a value. Praise careful checking rather than speed.
