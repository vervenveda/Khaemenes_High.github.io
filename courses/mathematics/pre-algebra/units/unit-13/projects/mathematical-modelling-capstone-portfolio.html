Mathematical Modelling Capstone Portfolio

Complete one safe, meaningful investigation that integrates mathematics from the course and communicates a recommendation without overstating evidence.

Required sequence

1. Frame

Question, purpose, audience, stakeholders, scope, privacy, ethics, criteria, and constraints.

2. Plan

Variables, units, sources or fictional inputs, assumptions, mathematics, and at least three connected representations.

3. Build and verify

Calculations, formulas, tables, graphs or diagrams, unit checks, estimates, independent verification, and revisions.

4. Test

Low–baseline–high scenarios, sensitivity, uncertainty, fairness, accessibility, trade-offs, and limitations.

5. Communicate and defend

Executive summary, evidence-aware conclusion, citations, three-to-five-minute defence, responses to questions, and one revision from feedback.

6. Reflect

Evidence of growth, strongest modelling practice, unfinished learning, and a specific Algebra I next step.
