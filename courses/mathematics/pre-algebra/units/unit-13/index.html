Evidence-aware conclusion

The baseline plan is feasible under the stated assumptions.

At 60 participants, the model projects a total cost of $1,320, leaving $180 in the budget and 40 places below capacity. The tested low and high scenarios also remain feasible.

This recommendation is conditional on the fixed cost, constant per-participant cost, capacity, budget, and selected scenario range.
