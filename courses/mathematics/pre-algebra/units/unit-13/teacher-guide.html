Unit 13 Teacher Guide

A one-week culminating capstone emphasizing authentic modelling evidence, transparent limitations, revision, defence, and Algebra I transition.

Five-session pacing

  1. Frame question and approve safe scope.
  2. Plan variables, sources, mathematics, and representations.
  3. Build, verify, and revise.
  4. Run sensitivity/fairness checks and prepare communication.
  5. Present defence, revise from feedback, and complete reflection.

Evidence hierarchy

The portfolio and defence are primary evidence. Static practice and readiness questions are formative supports, not substitutes for original modelling work.

Safety and ethics

Approve public, fictional, aggregated, or anonymous data. Reject projects involving private addresses, credentials, sensitive medical records, unsafe surveillance, or publication of identifiable minors.

Assessment

Use the 100-point rubric, allow pathway-specific scaffolds, require one revision, and evaluate claim strength, mathematical accuracy, verification, sensitivity, fairness, communication, and reflection.
