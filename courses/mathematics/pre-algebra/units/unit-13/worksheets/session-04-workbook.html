Session 04 · Capstone Investigation Studio

Five substantial ungraded project scenarios. Show all reasoning, choose a defensible model, and record what evidence could change your conclusion.

A · Sensitivity to price

A project sells 50 tickets. Revenue model is R=pt where p is ticket price. Compare $6 and $7 tickets, and state revenue sensitivity to a $1 price change.

B · Measurement uncertainty

A rectangular site is measured 10±0.2m by 6±0.1m. Compute minimum and maximum possible area.

C · Equity in transportation

A plan reduces average walking time from 15 to 12 minutes, but one neighborhood rises from 10 to 18 minutes. Explain why reporting only the total average may be unfair.

D · Confidence without overclaim

A poll of 24 volunteers finds 18 prefer Option A. Compute sample percent, then write a carefully limited conclusion.

E · Stress-test a decision

A student proposes a $500 project with a $520 budget. Show whether a 5% cost increase preserves feasibility, and explain what should change.

Reflection

Document one capstone decision: evidence used, mathematics performed, assumption involved, alternative considered, and what could cause revision.
