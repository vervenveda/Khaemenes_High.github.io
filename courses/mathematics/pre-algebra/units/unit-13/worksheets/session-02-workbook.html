Session 02 · Capstone Investigation Studio

Five substantial ungraded project scenarios. Show all reasoning, choose a defensible model, and record what evidence could change your conclusion.

A · Market budget model

A market charges $25 stall rent and $4 materials per item sold. Let n be items and selling price $9. Write revenue, cost, profit and break-even quantity.

B · Graph the alternatives

Two delivery offers charge A=$12+$3/km and B=$20+$2/km. Make a table at 0,5,8,10 km; find where the options cost the same.

C · Choose a representative data set

A community survey asks how many minutes people walk daily. A sample includes 6,8,10,12,64 minutes. Calculate mean and median; state why two summaries help.

D · Scale an art installation

A sculpture drawing is 1:25. A side is 7 cm on the sketch. Compute real length in meters; explain the measurement chain.

E · Variable definitions audit

A model writes C=5x+30 without explaining variables. Propose a valid context, interpret units and identify the sensible domain.
