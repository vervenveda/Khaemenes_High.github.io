Session 05 · Capstone Investigation Studio

Five substantial ungraded project scenarios. Show all reasoning, choose a defensible model, and record what evidence could change your conclusion.

A · Defend a design choice

Choose between Garden A (35m² for $180) and Garden B (40m² for $260) under a $220 budget. State claim, evidence and limitation.

B · Transparent model poster

Prepare a six-part outline for presenting a mathematical model: question, data, variables, method, results, limitations. Illustrate with a ticket-pricing model.

C · Answer the skeptic

A teammate says an average of 14 minutes proves everyone waits 14 minutes. Explain the misunderstanding and propose one additional statistic.

D · Algebra readiness reflection

Solve 3(x+4)=2x+29. Explain each transformation and substitute your answer to verify.

E · Final revision commitment

A model predicts $240 revenue, but actual revenue is $204. Calculate absolute and percent prediction error relative to predicted; write a revision note.

Reflection

Document one capstone decision: evidence used, mathematics performed, assumption involved, alternative considered, and what could cause revision.
