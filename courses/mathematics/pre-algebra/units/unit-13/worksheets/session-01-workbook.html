Session 01 · Capstone Investigation Studio

Five substantial ungraded project scenarios. Show all reasoning, choose a defensible model, and record what evidence could change your conclusion.

A · Community garden decision

A community has 24 meters of fence for a rectangular garden. Compare three options with whole-meter sides: 4 by 8, 5 by 7, and 6 by 6. All use the full perimeter. Which has greatest area? Define the decision rule and one limitation.

B · Safe school water plan

A school can collect at most 180 liters of rainwater. It wants at least 120 liters for a garden. Frame an answerable question comparing two storage tanks of 125 and 160 liters; identify constraints and what additional evidence is needed.

C · Library waiting time study

Write a measurable one-week question about library checkout waiting times. Specify the population, sample, unit and method that avoids gathering students' names.

D · Identify a hidden preference

An organizer declares 'The colorful, expensive plan is obviously best.' Rewrite the decision as a neutral question with at least two success criteria.

E · Project scope repair

An idea proposes proving which transportation system will be best worldwide in 2050. Narrow it to a safe one-week modeling task with one numerical outcome and clear limitations.

Reflection

Document one capstone decision: evidence used, mathematics performed, assumption involved, alternative considered, and what could cause revision.
