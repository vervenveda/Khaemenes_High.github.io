1. Which is most answerable in one week?

A) Which of three fictional garden layouts provides the most planting area under a 24 m fence limit? · B) How can everything improve? · C) Why is mathematics important? · D) What will happen forever?

2. Which choice best narrows scope?

A) study every city in the world · B) use all possible variables · C) define one school term, one population, and two measurable outcomes · D) avoid a time frame

3. A success criterion is:

A) a measurable condition used to judge a solution · B) a decorative title · C) a hidden assumption · D) an unrelated fact

4. What is data minimization?

A) Collecting every available identifier. · B) Publishing raw personal records. · C) Removing units from data. · D) Collecting only information needed to answer the question.

5. Which is a constraint?

A) The plan must cost no more than $600. · B) The graph is blue. · C) The mean is 12. · D) The sample has a median.

6. A feasible one-week project should use:

A) a bounded question and obtainable evidence. · B) every possible variable. · C) a nationwide census. · D) an unlimited time horizon.

7. A modelling question should connect mathematics to:

A) the longest formula. · B) a decorative graph. · C) a clearly stated purpose or decision. · D) a preferred answer.

8. A question framed to force a preferred answer risks:

A) confirmation bias. · B) unit conversion. · C) random assignment. · D) a larger IQR.

9. A safe school capstone should avoid:

A) public datasets. · B) fictional scenarios. · C) aggregated totals. · D) unnecessary sensitive or identifying information.

10. The first entry in a model audit trail should include:

A) the question, purpose, scope, criteria, and constraints. · B) only the final answer. · C) only calculator output. · D) only a title.
