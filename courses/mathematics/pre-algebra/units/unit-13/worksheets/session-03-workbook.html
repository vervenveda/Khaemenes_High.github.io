Session 03 · Capstone Investigation Studio

Five substantial ungraded project scenarios. Show all reasoning, choose a defensible model, and record what evidence could change your conclusion.

A · Build and validate a fence model

A rectangular animal enclosure has 50m of fencing. Let width w and length 25−w. Build its area model and compare w=5,10,12.5.

B · Fix the spreadsheet error

A student models total cost for q kits as 15q+40 but a spreadsheet computes (15+40)q. At q=6 compare results and explain the mistake.

C · Check a prediction against data

A linear model predicts 14,18,22 units for weeks 1–3. Observations are 13,20,21. Find residuals observed minus predicted and largest error.

D · Verify a proportional model

A baker predicts flour use F=0.3b kg for b loaves. Check 12 loaves and determine if 4.2 kg for 12 loaves contradicts the model.

E · Revision log

A tank-model prediction uses a filling rate of 8 L/min for 10 minutes, but a timed test shows only 72 L added. Estimate actual rate and revise the model.
