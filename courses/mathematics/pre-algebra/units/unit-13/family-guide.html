Unit 13 Family Guide

Support the learner by asking questions, protecting privacy, and encouraging explanation—not by supplying the model.

Helpful questions

  • What decision will this model help someone make?
  • What do the variables and units mean?
  • How did you check the result?
  • Which assumption changes the conclusion most?
  • Who benefits or may be burdened?
  • What can the model not prove?

Privacy

Encourage fictional, public, aggregated, or anonymous data. Do not include names, exact home locations, passwords, or sensitive personal records.

Celebrate growth

A successful capstone may include revisions and acknowledged limitations. Those are signs of mature mathematical thinking.
