7. Revision Studio
8. Communicate, Defend, Reflect & Demonstrate Readiness

The second week does **not** add five more content-heavy lessons. It protects time for authentic modelling, verification, revision, presentation, defence, and reflection.

NAIB may use the final readiness evidence to inform a separate **Readiness** profile for the next course. That result must never erase or downgrade the learner's completed Pre-Algebra **Course Mastery** record.
