Family Guide

How signed numbers describe real life and how families can support reasoning without taking over the work.

Foundation Practice

What this unit teaches

Students interpret, compare, and operate with positive and negative integers. They study opposites, absolute value, distance, number-line movement, the coordinate plane, and multi-step applications.

The deeper goal is to understand what a sign means, predict the direction and magnitude of a result, and use models to explain operation rules.

Helpful questions

What is the reference point?
What does the sign mean in this situation?
Can you show the change on a number line?
Which value has the greater magnitude?
Should the result be positive or negative?
Are we finding signed change or distance?

Practice naturally

  • Track daily high and low temperatures.
  • Use deposits, withdrawals, credits, and debts.
  • Compare elevations above and below sea level.
  • Record game bonuses and penalties.
  • Map movement on a coordinate grid.
  • Use a running total for multi-stage changes.

Help without giving the answer

  • Ask the learner to name the starting value and change.
  • Return to counters or a number line.
  • Encourage rewriting subtraction as addition.
  • Separate magnitude calculation from sign choice.
  • Praise explanation and correction rather than speed.
  • Pause when frustration rises.

Understanding pathways

Foundation adds number-line models, zero pairs, hints, and smaller steps.

Core contains the complete expected learning.

Extended adds counterexamples, coordinate transformations, long products, and unfamiliar models.

Pathways can differ by skill and are not permanent labels.

When extra support may help

Consider targeted review when a learner consistently struggles to:

  • Distinguish a negative sign from a subtraction sign
  • Order negative values
  • Interpret absolute value as distance
  • Rewrite subtraction as addition of the opposite
  • Track signs in multiplication and division
  • Move x first and y second on a coordinate plane
