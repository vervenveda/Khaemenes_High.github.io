Lesson 05 · Signed-Difference Story Lab

Ungraded challenge: Read the full situation first. Define your reference point and units, write an equation, show a model or intermediate steps, and explain what your signed answer means. These tasks are separate from the ten original worksheet questions.

A · Weather station mystery

A class records the temperature outside its school at −7°C at sunrise, +4°C at lunchtime, and −2°C after sunset. Find the signed temperature change from sunrise to lunch, then from lunch to sunset. Which interval warmed or cooled, and by how many degrees? Finally, compare the sunset temperature directly with the sunrise temperature. Explain why the changes do not all have the same sign.

B · Museum elevator challenge

A museum uses level 0 for its main entrance. Its fossil archive is on level −3, its art gallery is on level +5, and the underground theater is on level −2. A student begins in the fossil archive, visits the art gallery, and then travels to the theater. Calculate the signed change for each elevator trip using destination minus starting level. Find the net change from the first location to the last, and compare that with the total number of levels the elevator actually travels. Why are those numbers different?

C · School festival budget

A student committee begins the day with $18 in its event cash box. It pays a $31 supply bill, receives a $24 donation, and then pays $16 for decorations. Record every signed change and calculate the running balance after each transaction. How much additional money must the committee raise to finish at exactly $0? Write an equation that verifies your answer and explain why a negative balance represents money owed rather than money available to spend.

D · Design a mountain-adventure game

In a game you are designing, the player starts at an elevation of −4 meters relative to base camp, then reaches a bridge at +9 meters, drops to a cave at −6 meters, and finally finishes at +2 meters. Find each signed elevation change, the net elevation change from start to finish, and the total vertical distance traveled. Sketch a number line or simple level map. Explain why the net change and total travel distance measure different things.

E · Investigate the sign error

A classmate says: “The temperature went from −8°C to −3°C, so the change was −8 − (−3) = −5°C. That means it became colder.” Examine the classmate's calculation and the wording of the question. Write the correct expression for final minus initial, calculate the signed change, show the movement on a number line, and write a friendly explanation that would help the classmate fix the mistake.

Reflection

Choose one problem and explain how you verified that your answer is reasonable.
