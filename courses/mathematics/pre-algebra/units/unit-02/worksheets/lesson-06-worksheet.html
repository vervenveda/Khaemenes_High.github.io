Lesson 06 · Signed Rates & Operations Lab

Ungraded challenge: Read each situation carefully. Draw a model or table, label units, write a multiplication or division equation, and explain your answer's sign. These five challenges do not change the original ten scored-style worksheet questions.

A · Arctic research expedition

A research station records a temperature drop of 3°C per hour for 6 hours. Its temperature at the start is +4°C. Calculate the total signed temperature change and the final temperature. Then a different station reports a 24°C drop over 8 equal hours. Determine its signed hourly change using division. Explain why both rates and total changes are negative.

B · Create a game-score system

In an adventure game, each trap costs 7 points and each treasure chest adds 12 points. A player hits 5 traps and opens 3 chests. Calculate the net score change. Next, a second player loses 42 points from equally costly traps worth −7 points each: how many traps did that player hit? Verify using multiplication. Decide which player finishes ahead if both started at 0 points, and by how much.

C · Submarine mission log

A small research submarine starts at −18 meters relative to sea level. It descends 6 meters per minute for 4 minutes and then rises 5 meters per minute for 3 minutes. Find each signed change, the final depth, and the net change. For a separate constant-rate ascent, a submarine rises a total of 36 meters in 6 minutes: find the signed rate and verify with the inverse operation. Explain the difference between a negative position and a positive movement.

D · School makerspace budget

A student club orders 8 identical robotics components costing $9 each. Treat each purchase as a −$9 account change. The club receives 5 donations of +$15 each. Calculate the total expenses, total donations, and net change. If another class has a −$63 account change from 7 equal purchases, determine the signed amount of each purchase with division and check by multiplication. Explain whether the first club gained or lost money overall.

E · Diagnose the missing sign

A student writes: “(−48) ÷ (−6) = −8 because a negative number is involved. My check is (−8)(−6) = −48.” Identify every mathematical error, find the correct quotient, and check it by multiplying the quotient by the divisor. Then compare (−48) ÷ 6 and 0 ÷ (−6), explaining how their signs are determined. What would make division undefined?
