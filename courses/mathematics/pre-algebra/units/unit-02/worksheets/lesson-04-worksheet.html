Lesson 04 · Adding Integers Workshop

Ungraded reasoning extension: Show your number-line movements, zero pairs, and calculations. These exercises support explanation and do not change the original ten assignment questions.

A · Move along a number line

Draw a number line from −15 to +10. Start at −8, add +13, then add −6. Mark each movement and label the final position. Write an equation for the two changes.

B · Make and remove zero pairs

Use drawn positive and negative counters to model −7 + 5. Circle each positive-negative zero pair. Explain which counters remain and why the sum has that sign.

C · Explain two strategies

Find −14 + 9 and 14 + (−9). For each, show a number-line method and a magnitude-comparison method. Explain why the answers have different signs.

D · Track a changing balance

A school club starts with $18. It spends $25, receives a $12 donation, then spends $9. Write the signed changes and a running balance after each event. Explain whether the final balance is a credit or a debt.

E · Diagnose the mistake

A student says −6 + (−4) = −2 because 6 − 4 = 2. Identify the sign error, correct the sum, and justify your answer using counters or a number line.

Reflection

Choose one problem and explain how you verified that your answer is reasonable.
