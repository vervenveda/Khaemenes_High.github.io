1. What is the opposite of +6?

(A) −6    (B) +6    (C) 0    (D) 12

Answer: __________

2. What is the opposite of 0?

(A) −1    (B) +1    (C) 0    (D) Undefined

Answer: __________

3. Evaluate |17|.

(A) −17    (B) 0    (C) 17    (D) 34

Answer: __________

4. Which pair contains opposite integers?

(A) −4 and 4    (B) −4 and −4    (C) 4 and 8    (D) 0 and 4

Answer: __________

5. Which statement is true?

(A) Absolute value can be negative.    (B) Opposites always have different absolute values.    (C) Absolute value measures distance from zero.    (D) Every number has absolute value 0.

Answer: __________

6. Which two integers are equally far from zero?

(A) −7 and +7    (B) −7 and +6    (C) 0 and +7    (D) −8 and +7

Answer: __________

7. What is the value of −|8|?

(A) −8    (B) 0    (C) 8    (D) 16

Answer: __________

8. A temperature is −5°C relative to zero. How far is it from 0°C?

(A) −5°C    (B) 0°C    (C) 5°C    (D) 10°C

Answer: __________

9. Two elevators are at floors −4 and +4 relative to the lobby. Which statement is true?

(A) They are opposites and equally far from the lobby.    (B) They are on the same floor.    (C) Floor −4 is farther from the lobby.    (D) Floor +4 has absolute value −4.

Answer: __________

10. If an integer and its opposite are equal, what must the integer be?

(A) −1    (B) 0    (C) 1    (D) Any integer

Answer: __________

Reflection

Explain the difference between the opposite of a number and the absolute value of a number. Use −8 as your example.
