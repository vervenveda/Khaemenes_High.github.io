1. Which value is an integer?

(A) 3/4    (B) −8    (C) 2.5    (D) √2

Answer: __________

2. A temperature of 6 degrees below zero is represented by which integer?

(A) −6    (B) 0    (C) +6    (D) 60

Answer: __________

3. A withdrawal of $28 is represented by which signed change?

(A) +28    (B) −28    (C) 0    (D) 28/100

Answer: __________

4. A submarine is 75 meters below sea level. Which integer represents its position?

(A) +75    (B) −75    (C) 0    (D) 750

Answer: __________

5. Which statement about zero is correct?

(A) Zero is positive.    (B) Zero is negative.    (C) Zero is neither positive nor negative.    (D) Zero is not an integer.

Answer: __________

6. A player gains 14 yards. Which signed value represents the gain?

(A) −14    (B) +14    (C) 0    (D) −1.4

Answer: __________

7. A climber moves upward 24 meters. Which signed change represents the movement?

(A) −24    (B) +24    (C) 0    (D) 2.4

Answer: __________

8. In the statement 'the temperature changed by −5°C,' what does 5 describe?

(A) The direction only    (B) The magnitude of the change    (C) The reference point    (D) A positive temperature

Answer: __________

9. Which phrase is best represented by +22?

(A) a decrease of 22    (B) 22 below zero    (C) no change    (D) an increase of 22

Answer: __________

10. A parking garage labels street level as 0. Level −3 means what?

(A) Three levels above street level    (B) Three levels below street level    (C) Thirty levels below street level    (D) The garage has no levels

Answer: __________

Reflection

Choose one integer from today's lesson. Explain what its sign means, what its magnitude means, and what zero represents in the same context.
