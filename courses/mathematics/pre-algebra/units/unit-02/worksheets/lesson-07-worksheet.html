Lesson 07 · Division Detective Lab

Ungraded investigation: Read the entire challenge. Identify which number represents the total and which represents the equal rate or group. Write and solve a division equation, check it with multiplication, include units, and explain whether a positive, negative, or zero result makes sense. These investigations supplement the original ten questions.

A · Ocean explorer's missing mission log

An autonomous underwater robot starts at −12 meters relative to sea level. Over 8 equal minutes, it changes elevation by −56 meters. Calculate its signed elevation change per minute and its final position. During a separate 6-minute ascent, the robot changes elevation by +42 meters. Find that ascent rate. Explain why the robot can have a negative depth but a positive ascent rate, and verify both rates with multiplication.

B · Solve the missing purchases

A school robotics club records a total account change of −$81 from purchasing identical sensors. Each sensor produces a change of −$9. How many sensors were bought? Show the equation that makes the number of sensors positive, then verify it by multiplication. Later, a sponsor donates $54 in 6 equal installments. Find the signed change per installment and the combined net change of the purchases and donations. Explain the meaning of the remaining balance change.

C · Zero-case science lab

During an experiment, a thermometer's temperature changes by 0°C over 5 equal hours. Calculate the signed average change per hour and check it using multiplication. A student then tries to calculate a rate using a 12°C change divided by 0 hours. Explain why this division has no defined answer and why it is not equivalent to 0 ÷ 5. Create your own example that makes this difference clear without a calculator.

D · Game designer's score puzzle

In a strategy game, an equal point penalty is applied once in each of 8 rounds, creating a total score change of −96 points. Find the signed penalty per round. A special bonus then adds 6 identical rewards for a total of +90 points. Find the reward per event. Starting at zero, what is the player's final score after both sets of events? How many extra +15-point rewards would the player need to finish at exactly +24 points? Explain each decision and verify your answers.

E · Rescue the incorrect quotient

A classmate claims, “(−72) ÷ (−9) = −8, because negative numbers make a negative answer. Also, 0 ÷ (−9) is undefined, since it contains a zero.” Analyze each claim. Correct both quotients using multiplication, and explain what changes if the second expression is written (−9) ÷ 0. Finish by writing one reliable rule for quotient signs and one for division involving zero.

Reflection

Choose one problem and explain how you verified that your answer is reasonable.
