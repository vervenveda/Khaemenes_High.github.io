1. Which statement is true?

(A) −3 > −8    (B) −3 < −8    (C) −3 = −8    (D) Neither can be compared

Answer: __________

2. Choose the correct symbol: 7 __ −2.

(A) >    (B) <    (C) =    (D) −

Answer: __________

3. Which integer is greatest?

(A) −11    (B) −2    (C) −7    (D) −5

Answer: __________

4. Which list is ordered from least to greatest?

(A) −8, −3, 0, 5    (B) −3, −8, 0, 5    (C) 5, 0, −3, −8    (D) 0, −8, −3, 5

Answer: __________

5. Which value belongs first when −4, 3, −10, 0 are ordered least to greatest?

(A) 3    (B) 0    (C) −4    (D) −10

Answer: __________

6. Which integer lies between −6 and −2?

(A) −8    (B) −4    (C) 0    (D) 3

Answer: __________

7. Which integer has the greatest absolute value?

(A) −12    (B) 7    (C) −9    (D) 4

Answer: __________

8. A diver at −25 m and a diver at −8 m are compared relative to sea level. Who is higher?

(A) The diver at −25 m    (B) The diver at −8 m    (C) They are at the same height    (D) Neither position is an integer

Answer: __________

9. A student says −15 > −6 because 15 > 6. What is the best correction?

(A) The student is correct.    (B) For negative integers, −15 lies farther left, so −15 < −6.    (C) Negative integers cannot be ordered.    (D) Change both values to positive before comparing.

Answer: __________

10. If a < b and both a and b are integers, which number appears farther right on a number line?

(A) a    (B) b    (C) 0 always    (D) Their opposites are equal

Answer: __________

Reflection

Why is −4 greater than −10 even though 10 has the larger magnitude? Explain using a number line or reference point.
