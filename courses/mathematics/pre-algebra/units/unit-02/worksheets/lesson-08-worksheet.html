Lesson 08 · Coordinate Mission Studio

Ungraded capstone challenges: Plot each route, show signed calculations, state your assumptions, and explain why your answer makes sense. These activities complement the ten original questions.

A · Rescue drone route

A rescue drone starts at coordinate (−6, +3) on a grid where each unit represents one kilometer. It flies 9 km east, 7 km south, and 4 km west. Plot the starting point and every stop. Give its final ordered pair, net horizontal and vertical changes, and total distance flown. Explain why total distance is not the same as net movement.

B · Map a mystery museum

A museum map puts the front desk at (0,0), a fossil exhibit at (−4,+5), and a planetarium at (+3,−2). Draw the three locations on a coordinate plane. For a route that travels horizontally first and then vertically, calculate the number of grid units from the fossil exhibit to the planetarium. Describe both signed coordinate changes and explain why you cannot determine a walking route from the straight-line diagonal alone.

C · School festival finances

A school festival begins with $45 in its cash box, pays $68 for supplies, receives four donations of $12 each, then pays another $17. Write an expression with signed numbers, find the final balance, and calculate how much money would be needed to reach a positive balance of $30. Verify your answer with a second strategy.

D · Invent a strategy-game level

A player starts at (−2,−3), collects 8 points, loses 13 points, and then gains three bonuses worth 6 points each. The player moves 7 units right and 5 units up. Design a clear map and score log. State the new coordinate, net score change, and how much additional score is required to reach +20 overall, assuming the score began at 0. Show the operations in order.

E · Find the misplaced coordinate

A classmate claims that starting at (−5,+4) and moving 8 units right and 6 units down leads to (−13,+10). Determine the correct endpoint and identify both sign mistakes. Check using the difference between final and initial coordinates. Explain your correction in a way another student could follow.

Reflection

Choose one problem and explain how you verified that your answer is reasonable.
