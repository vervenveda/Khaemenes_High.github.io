01

How this works

NAIB advises. Your parent or guardian decides.

This gateway combines available Unit 01 evidence with a short readiness challenge. It may recommend targeted preparation, continued Pre-Algebra, an accelerated Pre-Algebra route, or a higher-level readiness check. A recommendation never changes course placement by itself.

Student-respect rule: Preparation is presented as strengthening foundations, never as being “behind.” Advanced readiness is presented as an opportunity for challenge, never as pressure to skip learning.
