      Concept study guide

      How to read signed-number situations

      Before choosing a positive or negative integer, slow down and identify the structure of the situation. A signed number has two jobs: the sign communicates direction, and the magnitude communicates size.

      1 · Name the reference point

      Ask what zero means. It might be sea level, street level, no change, a starting balance, or the current position.

      2 · Identify the direction

      Look for words such as above, below, gained, lost, deposit, withdrawal, forward, backward, up, down, or no change.

      3 · Separate sign from magnitude

      The sign tells direction relative to zero. The number part tells distance or size. In −18, the direction is negative and the magnitude is 18.

      4 · Explain the integer in context

      Do not stop at the symbol. Write a sentence: “−18 means 18 feet below sea level,” or “+45 means an account increase of 45 dollars.”
