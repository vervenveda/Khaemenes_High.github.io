## Instructional sequence

1. Integers in Context & on the Number Line
2. Opposites, Absolute Value & Distance
3. Comparing & Ordering Integers
4. Adding Integers with Models & Meaning
5. Subtracting Integers: Add the Opposite
6. Multiplying Integers & Sign Patterns
7. Dividing Integers & Inverse Reasoning
8. Coordinate Plane & Multi-Step Integer Applications

Lesson 6 still connects multiplication to division because the operations are inverses, but multiplication is the primary instructional focus. Lesson 7 develops division explicitly through inverse multiplication, signed rates, and division-by-zero reasoning. Lesson 8 consolidates coordinate reasoning and multi-step integer applications.
