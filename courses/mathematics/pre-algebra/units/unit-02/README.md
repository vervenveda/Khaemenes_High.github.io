## Instructional sequence

1. Integers in Context & on the Number Line
2. Opposites, Absolute Value & Distance
3. Comparing & Ordering Integers
4. Adding Integers with Models & Meaning
5. Subtracting Integers: Add the Opposite
6. Multiplying & Dividing Integers
7. Dividing Integers — Inverse Reasoning, Rates & Zero Cases
8. Coordinate Plane & Multi-Step Integer Applications

Lesson 6 develops the shared sign structure of multiplication and division together and uses inverse relationships as a check. Lesson 7 deliberately revisits division at greater depth through missing-factor reasoning, signed rates, equal-group interpretations, equations, and division-by-zero distinctions. Lesson 8 then consolidates coordinate reasoning and multi-step integer applications.
