Directions: Show algebraic structure and steps. Define variables and units in context. Verify equivalent forms or numerical results.

1. Expand 4(x+7).

(A) 4x+7   (B) 4x+28   (C) x+28   (D) 11x

2. Factor 15x+20 using the greatest integer common factor.

(A) 5(3x+4)   (B) 10(1.5x+2)   (C) 15(x+20)   (D) 5(3x+20)

3. A rectangle with width 3 and length x+4 has area:

(A) 3x+4   (B) 3x+7   (C) 3x+12   (D) x+12

4. Expand 4(2x−3).

(A) 6x−12   (B) 8x+12   (C) 8x−12   (D) 8x−3

5. Expand −3(2x−5).

(A) −6x−15   (B) 6x−15   (C) −3x+5   (D) −6x+15

6. Factor 12y−8.

(A) 12(y−8)   (B) 4(3y−2)   (C) 2(6y−8)   (D) 4(3y+2)

7. A rectangle with sides 5 and x+4 has area:

(A) 5x+20   (B) 5x+4   (C) x+9   (D) 20x

8. Factoring is the reverse of:

(A) substitution   (B) division only   (C) evaluation   (D) distributing

9. Expand −(x−4).

(A) x+4   (B) −x+4   (C) −x−4   (D) x−4

10. Which form makes the common factor most visible?

(A) 10x+15   (B) 10x+10+5   (C) 2x+3+5   (D) 5(2x+3)
