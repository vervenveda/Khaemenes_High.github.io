1. Which pair is like?

(A) 3x and 3x²   (B) 4a and −7a   (C) 2m and 2n   (D) 5 and 5x

2. Simplify 2y+8−5+y.

(A) 3y+3   (B) 3y+13   (C) 2y+4   (D) y+3

3. Why can 4x and 4x² not combine?

(A) Their coefficients differ   (B) Their variable parts differ   (C) They have no variables   (D) They are constants

4. Simplify 4x+7x.

(A) 11x²   (B) 28x   (C) 3x   (D) 11x

5. Simplify 3x+4+6x−9.

(A) 9x+13   (B) 3x+1   (C) 18x−5   (D) 9x−5

6. Simplify 2(x+3)+4x.

(A) 6x   (B) 6x+6   (C) 6x+3   (D) 8x+6

7. Simplify 3(2x−1)+4(x+2).

(A) 10x+11   (B) 6x+5   (C) 10x−5   (D) 10x+5

8. How can substitution verify equivalence?

(A) round coefficients   (B) evaluate both expressions at the same input and compare   (C) change inputs between forms   (D) compare term counts only

9. Simplify 4−(2x−3).

(A) 1−2x   (B) 4−2x−3   (C) 2x+7   (D) 7−2x

10. Simplify 2(3x+5)−(x−4).

(A) 7x+14   (B) 5x−14   (C) 5x+14   (D) 5x+6
