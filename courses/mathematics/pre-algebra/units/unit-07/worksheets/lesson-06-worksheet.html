Lesson 06 · Algebraic Structure Studio

Five ungraded missions. Model each situation, define variables and units, show mathematical reasoning, and critique incorrect methods when asked.

A · Farmers market bags

A vendor packs 3 apples and 2 oranges per bag, but adds 4 extra apples and 5 extra oranges. For b bags, write and simplify a total-fruit expression.

B · Fundraiser revenue

Tickets cost $7 and raffle entries $3. A group sells x of each, then pays $25 expenses. Model and simplify net proceeds.

C · Repair combining terms

A student says 4x+3x²=7x³. Explain why this is invalid and give a numerical counterexample.

D · Track water use

Water usage is 2.5t+8−0.5t+4 liters for t minutes. Simplify and interpret the resulting constant.

E · Compare two plans

Plan A costs 4x+18 dollars; Plan B costs 7x+6 dollars. Write the difference A−B in simplest form and find when it is zero.

Reflection

Choose one expression from this lesson. Describe its structure, complete a valid transformation or evaluation, identify a common error, and explain how you verified your result.
