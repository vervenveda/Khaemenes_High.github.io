Lesson 04 · Algebraic Structure Studio

Five ungraded missions. Model each situation, define variables and units, show mathematical reasoning, and critique incorrect methods when asked.

A · Community fundraiser

A booth sells 3 snacks and 5 drinks in one bundle. Let snack price be s and drink price be d. Write bundle revenue and show a valid regrouping for two bundles.

B · Order doesn't change

A student has expenses $15, $23, and $12. Explain why changing addition order does not change total; name the property.

C · Mistaken distribution

A student simplifies 6(x+4) to 6x+4. Show the error and compare both expressions at x=3.

D · Area two ways

A rectangle is x+7 meters long and 4 meters wide. Give two equivalent area expressions and describe why they agree.

E · Is subtraction commutative?

Choose two different numbers and test a−b=b−a. Explain what conclusion can and cannot be drawn.

Reflection

Choose one expression from this lesson. Describe its structure, complete a valid transformation or evaluation, identify a common error, and explain how you verified your result.
