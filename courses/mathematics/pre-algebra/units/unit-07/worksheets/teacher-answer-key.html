Unit 07 · Algebraic Structure Studio Worked Solutions

Guidance for the 40 ungraded extended missions. Accept equivalent expressions, mathematically justified alternative solutions, and appropriate units. Original assessment scoring remains unchanged.

Lesson 01

A · School theater seats: S=18r+12. Coefficient 18, constant 12. S(14)=18×14+12=264 seats.

B · Artists' supply budget: C=7d+9. d is dollars per kit. Increasing d by $2 increases total by $14.

C · Negative terms in context: V=250−qt. Constant 250 and variable term −qt, with q measured in liters/minute and t in minutes.

D · Expression or equation?: 5x+20 is an expression; adding =70 creates an equation. 5x=50, x=10 tickets.

E · Fix a coefficient: Terms are 3ab, −8a, and 11. Variable-term coefficients 3 and −8; 11 is a constant.

Lesson 02

A · Game token packs: C=5p+12; for p=9, C=$57. Fixed cost does not grow with p.

B · Three less than triple: First 3x−3 gives 21 at x=8. Second 3(x−3) gives 15 at x=8.

C · Travel to a museum: D=55h+12; D(2.5)=137.5+12=149.5 km.

D · Recipe batch change: F=2.5b+0.75 cups. 2.5b is batch flour; 0.75 is fixed starter flour.

E · Translate and diagnose: Correct (n+6)/2, which at n=10 is 8. Incorrect n/2+6=11.

Lesson 03

A · Drone flight time: t=2.4(7.5)+3=21 minutes; 3 minutes is fixed preparation time.

B · Temperature model: T(−10)=14°F, T(25)=77°F; 14°F is colder.

C · Park fence estimate: P=2((2w+7)+w)=6w+14; P(11)=80 m.

D · Substitution mistake: 4(−3)²=4×9=36; only x is squared, not the coefficient 4.

E · Create and test: Example C=8a+5c dollars, a adult tickets, c child tickets. At a=6,c=4, C=$68; alternatives valid.

Lesson 04

A · Community fundraiser: One bundle 3s+5d. Two bundles 2(3s+5d)=6s+10d; distributive property.

B · Order doesn't change: 15+(23+12)=(15+23)+12=50. Associative property; commutative can also reorder.

C · Mistaken distribution: Correct 6x+24=42 at x=3; incorrect 6x+4=22. Both terms must be multiplied by 6.

D · Area two ways: A=4(x+7)=4x+28 square meters; split into widths x and 7.

E · Is subtraction commutative?: For 8 and 3: 8−3=5, 3−8=−5. Subtraction is not generally commutative.

Lesson 05

A · Concert poster packages: C=n(4+3)=4n+3n=7n dollars.

B · School transport: P=v(8+2)=8v+2v=10v; P(6)=60 people.

C · Reverse engineer area: 12x+18=6(2x+3), so a rectangle might have sides 6m and (2x+3)m, with appropriate units for x.

D · Negative distribution: Correct −6y+15. At y=2, expression equals 3; incorrect form equals −27.

E · Build two equivalent plans: Example 5 boxes each with a pencils and 2 pens: 5a pencils plus 10 pens. Both count all items.

Lesson 06

A · Farmers market bags: 3b+2b+4+5=5b+9 pieces of fruit.

B · Fundraiser revenue: N=7x+3x−25=10x−25 dollars.

C · Repair combining terms: Unlike terms cannot combine: at x=2, left=8+12=20 while 7×2³=56.

D · Track water use: 2t+12 liters. Variable rate net 2 L/min; initial or fixed quantity 12 L.

E · Compare two plans: A−B=−3x+12; zero when x=4.

Lesson 07

A · Cylindrical tank: V≈3.14×4×5=62.8 m³.

B · Energy use: E=1.25×6.4=8 kWh.

C · Combined travel: D=vt+12=48×1.5+12=84 km.

D · Formula mistake: Area A=w(3w+2)=3w²+2w; at w=4, A=56 square units. Student computed perimeter-like sum, not area.

E · Plan a garden: Example C=18s+7p dollars; at s=4 bags,p=10 plants, C=72+70=$142.

Lesson 08

A · Growing border tiles: New area (n+2)²; extra (n+2)²−n²=4n+4. For n=5, 24 extra tiles.

B · Stage seating pattern: a(n)=6+4(n−1)=4n+2. Row 20 has 82 seats.

C · Compare models: A=12+4s; B=7s. 12=3s gives s=4 sessions, both $28.

D · Catch the pattern error: 5n gives 5,10,... but actual second term 9. Correct 4n+1.

E · Create an algebra mission: Example ticket sales 6t−40=200 -> 6t=240 -> t=40 tickets, assuming all tickets have identical price and fixed costs.
