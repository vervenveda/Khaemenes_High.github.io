Lesson 07 · Algebraic Structure Studio

Five ungraded missions. Model each situation, define variables and units, show mathematical reasoning, and critique incorrect methods when asked.

A · Cylindrical tank

A cylindrical tank has radius r meters and height h meters. Write V=πr²h and evaluate for r=2,h=5 using π≈3.14.

B · Energy use

A device consumes p kilowatts for t hours; energy is E=pt. Find usage at p=1.25 and t=6.4 and give correct units.

C · Combined travel

A delivery route has speed v km/h for t hours plus a fixed 12-km detour. Write a distance formula and evaluate at v=48,t=1.5.

D · Formula mistake

A rectangle has width w and length 3w+2. A student says its area is 3w+2+w. Correct the model and calculate for w=4.

E · Plan a garden

Create a formula with two variables for the cost of soil and plants; give prices and quantities and calculate a realistic total.

Reflection

Choose one expression from this lesson. Describe its structure, complete a valid transformation or evaluation, identify a common error, and explain how you verified your result.
