Directions: Show algebraic structure and steps. Define variables and units in context. Verify equivalent forms or numerical results.

1. A pattern uses 5n+2 objects at stage n. Stage 4 uses:

(A) 11   (B) 20   (C) 22   (D) 28

2. Which equals 4(2x+3)?

(A) 8x+3   (B) 8x+7   (C) 8x+12   (D) 6x+12

3. For n counting people, a reasonable domain is often:

(A) all complex numbers   (B) nonnegative whole numbers   (C) negative fractions only   (D) irrational numbers only

4. For 5,8,11,... with n=1 giving 5, a rule is:

(A) 3n+2   (B) 5n+3   (C) 3n+5   (D) 2n+3

5. A gym charges $20 plus $8 per visit v. Model:

(A) 20v+8   (B) 8(v+20)   (C) 20+8v   (D) 28v

6. In 20+8v, 8 represents:

(A) total cost   (B) cost per visit   (C) fixed fee   (D) visit count

7. A student simplifies 2(x+4)+x to 3x+4. Correct:

(A) 3x+4   (B) 2x+8   (C) 3x+6   (D) 3x+8

8. Why state model assumptions?

(A) to avoid data   (B) to clarify when the relationship is expected to apply   (C) to make all models exact   (D) to eliminate variables

9. If a table's output increases by 6 whenever input increases by 1, the coefficient in a linear expression is likely:

(A) 1   (B) 0   (C) −6   (D) 6

10. A complete algebraic model should include:

(A) answer only   (B) calculator display   (C) variable definitions, expression/formula, units, and interpretation   (D) symbols only
