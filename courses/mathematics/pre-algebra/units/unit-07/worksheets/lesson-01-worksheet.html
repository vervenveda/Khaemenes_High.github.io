Lesson 01 · Algebraic Structure Studio

Five ungraded missions. Model each situation, define variables and units, show mathematical reasoning, and critique incorrect methods when asked.

A · School theater seats

A theater has r rows with 18 seats in each row and 12 accessible seats near the stage. Write a total-seat expression, identify the coefficient and constant, then calculate when r=14.

B · Artists' supply budget

Each art kit costs d dollars; a delivery charge is $9. Write the price of 7 kits, explain the variable, and describe what changes when d increases by $2.

C · Negative terms in context

A water tank begins with 250 liters and loses q liters per minute. Write its volume after t minutes and identify which terms are variable.

D · Expression or equation?

Compare 5x+20 and 5x+20=70. Explain why one is an expression and the other is an equation. Solve the equation and interpret x as ticket count.

E · Fix a coefficient

A student describes 3ab−8a+11 as three terms with coefficients 3, 8, and 11. Correct the term and coefficient classifications.

Reflection

Choose one expression from this lesson. Describe its structure, complete a valid transformation or evaluation, identify a common error, and explain how you verified your result.
