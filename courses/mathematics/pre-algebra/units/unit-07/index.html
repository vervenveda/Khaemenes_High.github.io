    Expression Structure Lab

    Change coefficients and constants, then compare equivalent forms.

    Build a linear expression, evaluate it, distribute a grouped form, and inspect how coefficients and constants control output.

    Standard form 3x + 4 two-term linear expression
    Structure map 3x │ +4 coefficient 3 · constant 4
    Substitution 3(5) + 4 replace x with 5
    Output 19 15 + 4 = 19
    Coefficient-factored form 3(x + 4/3) distribution returns 3x + 4
    Integer GCF form 1(3x + 4) greatest common factor = 1
    Change when x rises by 1 +3 constant first difference
    Output at x = 0 4 constant term / vertical intercept
    Term count 2 one variable term and one constant term
    Equivalence check 19 = 19 both forms agree at x = 5
    InputSubstitutionOutputFirst difference

    The coefficient controls constant change; the constant is the output when x=0.
