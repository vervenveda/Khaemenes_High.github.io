Algebraic Model & Design Investigation

Create and test an algebraic model for a public, fictional, measured, or designed situation; compare equivalent forms and communicate assumptions, constraints, units, and limitations.

Open Project Workspace

Project sequence

Build an evidence-aware algebraic model.

Select a safe context or design

Examples: pricing, garden layout, art installation, transportation, energy use, event planning, production, or a visual pattern. Use public, fictional, or measured data.

Define variables and constraints

State every variable, unit, allowed input, constant, coefficient, and assumption.

Build multiple representations

Create a diagram or table, verbal rule, algebraic expression or formula, and at least two equivalent forms.

Evaluate and test

Use at least six input values, verify equivalence by structure and substitution, and test one boundary or counterexample.

Communicate a conclusion

Explain which form is most useful, what the model predicts, and where the model stops being trustworthy.

Rubric

DimensionProficient evidence
DefinitionsVariables, units, constants, coefficients, constraints, and assumptions are explicit.
Algebraic accuracyTranslation, substitution, distribution, factoring, and simplification are correct.
EquivalenceEquivalent forms are justified structurally and checked numerically.
Model testingInputs, boundaries, and counterexamples are used appropriately.
Design and evidenceThe model answers a meaningful question using public, fictional, or measured evidence.
CommunicationConclusions and limitations are clear and audience-appropriate.
