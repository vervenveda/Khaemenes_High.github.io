Teacher Guide

A three-week sequence for building algebraic language, equivalence, and responsible modelling.

Suggested pacing

Week 1: Lessons 1–3. Week 2: Lessons 4–6. Week 3: Lessons 7–8, project, review, and mastery check.

High-leverage routines

  • Ask learners to name the outer operation before simplifying.
  • Use substitution to verify, not replace, structural reasoning.
  • Require variable definitions and units in every model.
  • Compare expanded and factored forms for purpose.

Common misconceptions

  • A sign is separated from its term.
  • Unlike terms are combined.
  • Distribution reaches only the first term.
  • Negative inputs are substituted without parentheses.
  • Equivalent expressions are assumed from appearance alone.

Differentiation

Foundation learners use color coding, tiles, area models, and sentence frames. Core learners move among verbal, symbolic, numerical, and contextual representations. Extended learners prove equivalence, analyze parameters, construct counterexamples, and critique model domains.

Assessment use

The public mastery check is formative. Use protected alternate forms for high-stakes assessment, and review written reasoning, units, and assumptions alongside scores.
