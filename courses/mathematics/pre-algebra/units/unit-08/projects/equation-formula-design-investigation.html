Equation & Formula Design Investigation

Create and solve an equation model for a public, fictional, measured, or designed situation; rearrange a related formula and communicate equality, units, assumptions, constraints, and verification.

Open Project Workspace

Project sequence

Build and verify an evidence-aware equation model.

Select a safe context or design

Examples: event pricing, transportation, garden dimensions, art fabrication, energy use, production, temperature conversion, or a science formula. Use public, fictional, or measured data.

Define the unknown and conditions

State the variable, units, fixed quantities, rates, domain, assumptions, and the question the equation must answer.

Write and solve an equation

Build a contextual equation, show equivalent transformations, solve it, and check the solution in both the original equation and the situation.

Rearrange a related formula

Select or create a formula with at least three variables. Isolate a different variable, state denominator restrictions, and verify units.

Test and communicate

Use at least five input cases, analyze one boundary or impossible value, and explain limitations and one common error to avoid.

Rubric

DimensionProficient evidence
Definitions and equalityVariables, units, both sides of the equation, and the meaning of equality are explicit.
Solution processEvery transformation preserves equality and the arithmetic is accurate.
VerificationThe solution checks in the original equation and satisfies context constraints.
Formula reasoningA related formula is rearranged correctly with restrictions and units.
Model testingInputs, boundaries, assumptions, and limitations are examined.
CommunicationThe final recommendation or design conclusion is clear and evidence-based.
