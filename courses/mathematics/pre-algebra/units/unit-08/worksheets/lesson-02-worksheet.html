Lesson 02 · Equation Investigation Studio

Five ungraded investigations. Define variables and units, explain inverse operations, and verify results.

A · Lake temperature

Water temperature rises 7°C to reach 18°C. Write and solve an equation for its starting temperature, then check.

B · Library inventory

A shelf held b books before 23 were loaned. It now holds 41. Form an equation and find the original inventory.

C · Repair a sign mistake

A student solves x−(−8)=15 by subtracting 8 and concludes x=7. Explain the double-negative rule and solve.

D · Compare inverse moves

Two students solve n+14=−5. One subtracts 14 on both sides, while another adds 14. Determine which method preserves equality and solve.

E · Create a negative solution

Make a real-world story represented by x+12=4. Interpret the negative solution in its context.

Reflection / Error Analysis

Choose one equation from this lesson. Explain the structure, justify each inverse operation, identify one likely error, and show a verification in the original equation.
