Unit 08 · Equation Investigation Studio Worked Solutions

Guidance for 40 ungraded applied investigations. Accept equivalent valid methods, clear variable definitions and checks. Do not change mastery scoring.

Lesson 01

A · Balance the museum crates: 3x+12=87; 3x=75; x=25 kg. Check: 3(25)+12=87.

B · Test a proposed answer: At x=6, left=15, not 19. 4x=28, so x=7; check 28−9=19.

C · Design a balancing move: 2x+5=17; subtract 5 on both sides to get 2x=12, then x=6. Altering one side breaks equality.

D · Detect the false equality: Subtract 4, not add: 8x=32 and x=4. Check 8(4)+4=36.

E · Build your own balance: Example 2x+9=3 gives x=−3. Substitute 2(−3)+9=3.

Lesson 02

A · Lake temperature: t+7=18, so t=11°C. Check 11+7=18.

B · Library inventory: b−23=41, hence b=64 books. Check 64−23=41.

C · Repair a sign mistake: x+8=15, so x=7. The numerical result happens to be correct, but the justification must recognize adding 8; 7−(−8)=15.

D · Compare inverse moves: Subtract 14 from both sides to obtain n=−19. Check −19+14=−5.

E · Create a negative solution: x=−8. Example temperature starts at −8°C and rises 12°C to 4°C.

Lesson 03

A · Garden share: 8s=96; s=12 seedlings per tray. Check 8×12=96.

B · Cycling distance: 2.5v=67.5; v=27 km/h. Check 2.5×27=67.5.

C · Catch the divide error: Multiply by 6: x=−24. Check −24/6=−4.

D · Fraction coefficient: x=18×4/3=24. Multiplying by reciprocal 4/3 cancels 3/4; check (3/4)×24=18.

E · Unusual measurement: 0.2n=3.6; n=18 loaves. Check 0.2×18=3.6 kg.

Lesson 04

A · Community ticket booth: 6t+18=90; 6t=72; t=12 tickets. Check 72+18=90.

B · Water station setup: 15+4.5t=69; t=54/4.5=12 minutes. Check 15+54=69.

C · Reverse the steps: Multiply both sides by 3: x−7=33; add 7: x=40. Check (40−7)/3=11.

D · Phone plan error: Subtract $9 first: 3g=36; g=12 GB. Check 3×12+9=45.

E · Design a two-step equation: x=(18+7)/2.5=10. Example $2.50 per item minus $7 coupon yields $18 total for 10 items.

Lesson 05

A · Distribute the supply bill: 4(x+7)+12=88; 4x+40=88; 4x=48; x=$12 per paint set.

B · Simplify concert revenue: 8t−26=118; 8t=144; t=18 (matching units sold in both revenue streams).

C · Distributive sign trap: −6x+8+5=−11; −6x=−24; x=4. Check −2(12−4)+5=−11.

D · Two routes same distance: 6x+12=5x+23; x=11. Both routes are 78 km.

E · Spot lost terms: Distribution requires 2x+10+3x=35; 5x=25; x=5.

Lesson 06

A · Competing memberships: 12+5m=30+2m; 3m=18; m=6 months. Both cost $42.

B · Identity inspection: 3x+6=3x+6, true for every real x. Infinitely many solutions.

C · Impossible tickets: 2x+10=2x+7 implies 10=7, false. No solution.

D · Both-sides sign error: 7−2x=3x−8 gives 15=5x and x=3. Substitute: left 7−6=1 and right 9−8=1, so both sides agree.

E · Invent three outcomes: Examples: x+2=5 -> x=3; 2(x+1)=2x+2 -> all x; x+2=x+3 -> no x.

Lesson 07

A · Rearrange a perimeter: W=(P−2L)/2=P/2−L. W=34−21=13m.

B · Solve for time: t=d/v for v≠0; t=135/45=3 hours.

C · Temperature formula: C=(5/9)(F−32); C=(5/9)×36=20°C. Check 9/5×20+32=68.

D · Triangle height: h=2A/b for b≠0; h=108/12=9m.

E · Interest formula: r=I/(Pt), assuming Pt≠0. r=84/(700×3)=0.04=4% annually.

Lesson 08

A · Budgeting for a science fair: 12k+28=220; 12k=192; k=16 kits. Check 192+28=220.

B · Find missing distance: x+2x+(x+3)=18; 4x=15; x=3.75km, then 7.5km and 6.75km. Sum 18km.

C · Repair a shipping calculation: 51/3=17; 5x+2=17; 5x=15; x=3kg per contents unit. Check 3(5×3+2)=51.

D · Ticket mix: Let a be adults; students=20−a. 12a+7(20−a)=185; 5a=45; a=9, students=11.

E · Model constraints: 75+8n≤251; 8n≤176; n≤22. At most 22 attendees; verify 75+8×22=$251.
