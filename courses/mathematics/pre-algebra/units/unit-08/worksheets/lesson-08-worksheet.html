Directions: Show equivalent operations on both sides. Preserve signs, grouping, units, and constraints. Check solutions in the original equation.

1. A $25 fee plus $6 per session s totals $73. Equation:

(A) 25s+6=73   (B) 6s+25=73   (C) 31s=73   (D) 6(s+25)=73

2. Perimeter 38, length 11. Width:

(A) 4   (B) 8   (C) 16   (D) 27

3. A model gives −4 tickets. Best conclusion:

(A) sell −4 tickets   (B) round to 0 automatically   (C) review equation and context because ticket count cannot be negative   (D) the algebra must always be wrong

4. For 15+6c=63, c=

(A) 48   (B) 8   (C) 7   (D) 13

5. Five times a number plus 2 is 37:

(A) 5x+2=37   (B) 5(x+2)=37   (C) 2x+5=37   (D) 5x−2=37

6. A distance equation should report a solved distance with:

(A) distance units   (B) time units   (C) rate units only   (D) no units

7. A student models '12 less than 3x' as 12−3x. Correct:

(A) 3x−12   (B) 12−3x   (C) 3(x−12)   (D) 12+3x

8. A constant-rate equation assumes:

(A) the rate doubles each step   (B) all values are integers   (C) units are optional   (D) the rate remains constant over the modelled interval

9. A complete contextual solution includes:

(A) equation without meaning   (B) variable definition, equation, steps, check, units, interpretation   (C) answer only   (D) calculator screen

10. For V=100−4m, physically meaningful m before emptying satisfies:

(A) 0≤m≤25   (B) m≥100   (C) m<0   (D) m≥25 only
