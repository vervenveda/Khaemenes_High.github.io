Lesson 08 · Equation Investigation Studio

Five ungraded investigations. Define variables and units, explain inverse operations, and verify results.

A · Budgeting for a science fair

A team has $220. It spends $28 on signage and $12 on each experiment kit. How many kits can it afford if it spends exactly the budget? Check.

B · Find missing distance

A hiker covers 18 km over three days. Day two is twice day one's distance, and day three is 3 km more than day one. Determine each day.

C · Repair a shipping calculation

A box and its contents weigh 5x+2 kg. Three such boxes together weigh 51 kg. A student writes 5x+2=51/3 then concludes x=3. Explain and check.

D · Ticket mix

A museum sells adult tickets for $12 and student tickets for $7. It sells 20 tickets totaling $185. Find the counts with an equation.

E · Model constraints

A club's venue charges $75 plus $8 per attendee. It has a $251 budget. Write the inequality for affordability, solve, and state the maximum whole number of attendees.

Reflection / Error Analysis

Choose one equation from this lesson. Explain the structure, justify each inverse operation, identify one likely error, and show a verification in the original equation.
