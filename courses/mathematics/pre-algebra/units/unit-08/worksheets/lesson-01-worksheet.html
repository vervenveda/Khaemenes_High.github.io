Lesson 01 · Equation Investigation Studio

Five ungraded investigations. Define variables and units, explain inverse operations, and verify results.

A · Balance the museum crates

A museum ships identical crates, each weighing x kg, plus a 12 kg pallet. Three crates and the pallet weigh 87 kg. Write the equation, solve, and check the load.

B · Test a proposed answer

A learner claims x=6 solves 4x−9=19. Substitute directly, assess the claim, and find the actual solution.

C · Design a balancing move

A balance shows 2x+5 units on the left and 17 on the right. Explain which operation to do first, and why subtracting 5 on only one side is invalid.

D · Detect the false equality

A student rewrites 8x+4=36 as 8x=40. Diagnose the mistaken operation, correct the steps, and verify.

E · Build your own balance

Invent an equation with the unique solution x=−3, including a constant on each side. Demonstrate the check.

Reflection / Error Analysis

Choose one equation from this lesson. Explain the structure, justify each inverse operation, identify one likely error, and show a verification in the original equation.
