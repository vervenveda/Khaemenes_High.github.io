Lesson 03 · Equation Investigation Studio

Five ungraded investigations. Define variables and units, explain inverse operations, and verify results.

A · Garden share

A group divides 96 seedlings into 8 identical trays. Represent seedlings per tray with a one-step equation and verify.

B · Cycling distance

A rider travels 67.5 km in 2.5 hours at a constant speed. Write an equation for speed and solve with units.

C · Catch the divide error

A learner solves x/6=−4 as x=−4/6. Identify the inverse operation and correct the answer.

D · Fraction coefficient

Solve (3/4)x=18 using inverse operations. Explain why multiplying by 4/3 works.

E · Unusual measurement

A recipe uses 0.2 kg of flour for each loaf, totaling 3.6 kg. Write the equation and calculate the number of loaves.

Reflection / Error Analysis

Choose one equation from this lesson. Explain the structure, justify each inverse operation, identify one likely error, and show a verification in the original equation.
