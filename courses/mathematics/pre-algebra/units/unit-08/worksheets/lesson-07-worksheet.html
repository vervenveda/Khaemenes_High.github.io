Directions: Show equivalent operations on both sides. Preserve signs, grouping, units, and constraints. Check solutions in the original equation.

1. Solve d=rt for r.

(A) r=d/t   (B) r=t/d   (C) r=dt   (D) r=d−t

2. Solve P=2l+2w for l.

(A) l=P−2w   (B) l=(P−2w)/2   (C) l=P/2+2w   (D) l=2P−w

3. In t=d/r, required restriction:

(A) d≠0   (B) t≠0   (C) r≠0   (D) r>0 only

4. Solve d=rt for t.

(A) t=dr   (B) t=r/d   (C) t=d−r   (D) t=d/r

5. Solve C=5(F−32)/9 for F.

(A) F=5C/9+32   (B) F=9C/5+32   (C) F=5C/9−32   (D) F=9(C+32)/5

6. When solving a formula for one variable, other variables are treated as:

(A) known quantities/parameters during the rearrangement   (B) zero   (C) errors   (D) equal to each other

7. To isolate h in V=lwh, divide by:

(A) l+w   (B) lw   (C) V   (D) h

8. Solve y=mx+b for x.

(A) x=m/(y−b)   (B) x=y−b−m   (C) x=(y−b)/m   (D) x=(y+b)/m

9. How can a rearranged formula be checked?

(A) set every variable to zero   (B) substitute reasonable values and confirm both forms agree   (C) count letters   (D) remove units

10. Solve A=(1/2)bh for h.

(A) h=A/(2b)   (B) h=Ab/2   (C) h=2Ab   (D) h=2A/b
