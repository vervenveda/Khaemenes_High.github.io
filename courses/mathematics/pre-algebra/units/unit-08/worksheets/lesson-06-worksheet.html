Lesson 06 · Equation Investigation Studio

Five ungraded investigations. Define variables and units, explain inverse operations, and verify results.

A · Competing memberships

Club A charges $12 plus $5 per month; Club B charges $30 plus $2 per month. Find the break-even month.

B · Identity inspection

Solve 3(x+2)=3x+6 and explain the number of solutions using the equivalent forms.

C · Impossible tickets

Solve 2(x+5)=2x+7. Explain in words why no ticket quantity can satisfy the equation.

D · Both-sides sign error

Solve 7−2x=3x−8, carefully accounting for negative terms, and check the result.

E · Invent three outcomes

Write one linear equation with one solution, one identity, and one contradiction. Explain how algebra reveals each type.

Reflection / Error Analysis

Choose one equation from this lesson. Explain the structure, justify each inverse operation, identify one likely error, and show a verification in the original equation.
