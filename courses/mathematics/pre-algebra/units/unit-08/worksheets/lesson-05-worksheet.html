Lesson 05 · Equation Investigation Studio

Five ungraded investigations. Define variables and units, explain inverse operations, and verify results.

A · Distribute the supply bill

An art class orders 4 supply bundles, each with a $7 frame and x-dollar paints, plus $12 delivery. Total is $88. Write and solve.

B · Simplify concert revenue

A school collects 5t+3t−26 dollars after expenses and reports $118 net. Find t, stating what the variable represents.

C · Distributive sign trap

Solve −2(3x−4)+5=−11. Show each transformed equation and substitution check.

D · Two routes same distance

Route A has distance 3(2x+4) km. Route B is 5x+23 km. Find x when distances match and validate.

E · Spot lost terms

A learner rewrites 2(x+5)+3x=35 as 2x+5+3x=35. Diagnose and solve correctly.

Reflection / Error Analysis

Choose one equation from this lesson. Explain the structure, justify each inverse operation, identify one likely error, and show a verification in the original equation.
