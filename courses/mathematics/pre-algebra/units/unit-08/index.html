    Equation Balance Lab

    Change a linear equation and inspect the solution and verification.

    Adjust a, b, and c in ax+b=c. The lab isolates x, displays inverse steps, and checks both sides. Cases with a=0 reveal identities or contradictions.

    Original equation 3x + 4 = 19 two-step linear equation
    Inverse addition step 3x = 15 subtract 4 from both sides
    Inverse multiplication step x = 15/3 divide both sides by 3
    Solution x = 5 solution set: {5}
    Classification one solution nonzero coefficient
    Equivalent equation 3x = 15 same solution set preserved
    Substitution check 3(5) + 4 = 19 replace x with the proposed solution
    Left-side value 19 LHS after substitution
    Right-side value 19 RHS from the original equation
    Truth statement 19 = 19 verified
    Left side 3x + 4
    △
    Right side 19

    Subtract 4 from both sides, then divide by 3. Both sides evaluate to 19.
