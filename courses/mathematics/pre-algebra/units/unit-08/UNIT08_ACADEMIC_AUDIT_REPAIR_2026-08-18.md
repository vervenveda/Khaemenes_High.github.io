# Unit 08 Academic Audit + Repair
## Equations, Formulas & Inverse Operations

### Academic review
Reviewed all 160 lesson questions, Foundation/Core/Extended practice, and the 24-question cumulative mastery assessment.

The lesson and cumulative mastery mathematics were correctly keyed. One assessment-quality defect was found in Foundation practice: a yes/no item had only two answer choices while the rest of the course uses four-choice banks. It was rewritten as a four-choice verification item.

### Repairs
- Foundation practice expanded from 15 to 20 questions.
- Core practice expanded from 18 to 20 questions.
- Extended practice expanded from 18 to 20 questions.
- All pathway practice thresholds raised from 75% to 80%.
- Every 20-question lesson/practice bank balanced to 5 A / 5 B / 5 C / 5 D.
- 24-question cumulative mastery balanced to 6 A / 6 B / 6 C / 6 D.
- 20/24 explicitly identified as the cumulative mastery minimum.
- Teacher answer key and embedded unit-map fallbacks synchronized.
- Unit labels and architecture updated for open-age Pre-Algebra.

### Sequence retained
equality/balance → one-step additive equations → one-step multiplicative equations → two-step equations → multistep equations → variables on both sides/identity/contradiction → literal equations → contextual modelling/verification.
