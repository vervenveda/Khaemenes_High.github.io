Lesson 01 · Powers, Roots & Scale Studio

Five ungraded missions. Explain why your mathematical rule works, and use a calculator only to check an established model.

A · Chessboard doubles

A game board has 8 squares in a row. Place 1 token on the first square and double the number on each successive square. How many tokens are on the eighth square and across the whole row?

B · Lighting installation

An art installation has 4 sections, each containing 4 panels, each containing 4 lights. Represent the total with a power and evaluate.

C · Parentheses matter

Compare −5^2 and (−5)^2. Give a context in which confusing their signs would matter.

D · Population game

A simulated colony triples every round, starting with 2 colonies. How many are present after four completed rounds? Explain the exponent.

E · Incorrect exponent shortcut

A classmate claims 3^4 means 3×4. Explain the misunderstanding and compare both values.

Reflection

Explain what you learned about exponent meaning, grouping, and the sign of powers. Include one worked example, one common error to avoid, and one reasonableness check.
