Lesson 02 · Powers, Roots & Scale Studio

Five ungraded missions. Explain why your mathematical rule works, and use a calculator only to check an established model.

A · Fraction patterns

Starting from 2^3, divide by 2 repeatedly to find 2^2, 2^1, 2^0, 2^(−1), and 2^(−2). Explain the rule.

B · Microscope zoom

A scale is multiplied by 10^(−3). Express the multiplier as a decimal and a fraction, and explain what happens to a 4-meter distance.

C · Correct zero-power confusion

A student says 7^0=0 because zero means nothing. Use a pattern to refute the statement.

D · Negative-exponent order

Compare 3^(−2) and 2^(−3). Which quantity is greater? Show exact fractions.

E · Design a conversion rule

Write an example where a power of 10 with a negative exponent represents a real measurement. Convert it to ordinary decimal form.

Reflection

Explain what you learned about zero and negative exponent patterns and restrictions. Include one worked example, one common error to avoid, and one reasonableness check.
