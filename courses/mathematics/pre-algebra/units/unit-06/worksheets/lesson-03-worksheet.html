Directions: Show exponent/root structure, preserve units, estimate magnitude, and use a scientific calculator only as a check after modelling.

1. Simplify a^4×a^7.

(A) a^11   (B) a^28   (C) 2a^11   (D) a^3

2. Simplify (m^3)^5.

(A) m^8   (B) m^15   (C) 5m^3   (D) m^243

3. Simplify z^2÷z^6 with positive exponents.

(A) z^4   (B) 1/z^4   (C) -z^4   (D) 1/z^3

4. Simplify a^3·a^5.

(A) a^8   (B) a^15   (C) a^2   (D) 2a^8

5. Simplify m^6/m^8.

(A) m^14   (B) 1/m^14   (C) 1/m^2   (D) m^2

6. Simplify (2x)^3.

(A) 8x   (B) 8x^3   (C) 2x^3   (D) 6x^3

7. Simplify (p^2)^3·p^4.

(A) p^24   (B) p^5   (C) p^10   (D) p^9

8. Why does x^2·x^3=x^5?

(A) bases are added   (B) exponents disappear   (C) there are five total factors of x   (D) 2×3=5

9. A student writes x^3+x^4=x^7. What is wrong?

(A) the product rule does not apply to addition   (B) 3+4 is not 7   (C) x cannot be repeated   (D) all sums equal products

10. Which expression equals 2^7?

(A) 2^3·2^4   (B) 2^3+2^4   (C) (2^3)^4   (D) 2^4/2^3
