Lesson 07 · Powers, Roots & Scale Studio

Five ungraded missions. Explain why your mathematical rule works, and use a calculator only to check an established model.

A · Star archive

A survey catalogs 3.2×10^6 stars in each of 4.5×10^2 sectors. Estimate the total number of stars in scientific notation.

B · Microchip region

A tiny circuit covers 6×10^(−4) m² and is divided into 3×10² equal regions. Find each region's area.

C · Align before adding

A student adds 2.5×10^5 and 3×10^4 to get 5.5×10^9. Identify two errors and give the correct sum.

D · Scale comparison

A bacterium is 2×10^(−6)m long and a grain of sand is 4×10^(−4)m long. How many times longer is the grain?

E · Design an astronomy estimate

Invent two scientific-notation distances, calculate their ratio, and explain how exponents affect order of magnitude.

Reflection

Explain what you learned about operations with scientific notation and real-world scale. Include one worked example, one common error to avoid, and one reasonableness check.
