    Exponent & Scale Lab

    Move between powers, reciprocals, roots, and scientific scale.

    Change a base and integer exponent, then inspect the expanded meaning, exact value, reciprocal form, and power-of-ten scale.

    Power notation 2⁵ two to the fifth power
    Exact value 32 whole number
    Equivalent form 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 positive exponent
    Scientific notation 3.2 × 10¹ order of magnitude: 10¹
    Power of ten 10⁵ = 100,000 place-value scale
    Reciprocal pair 2⁻⁵ = 1/32 = 0.03125
    Principal square root √50 ≈ 7.071 rounded to the nearest thousandth
    Perfect-square bounds 7² < 50 < 8² 7 < √50 < 8
    Number classification irrational 50 is not a perfect square
    Approximation check 7.071² ≈ 49.999 absolute error ≈ 0.001

    A positive exponent counts repeated factors. The root estimate is bracketed by nearby perfect squares.

    Scientific Scale Investigation

    Begin with the Unit 06 project, choose a consistent model scale, and keep every original measurement, conversion, unit, and assumption visible in the drawing.

    1. Record each real quantity and unit.
    2. Rewrite the quantity in scientific notation.
    3. Choose one defensible scale factor.
    4. Convert each real quantity to a drawing length.
    5. Draw and dimension the model in OHMIC.
    6. Explain precision, rounding, and limits of the model.
