Scale of the Universe Investigation

Investigate a set of quantities spanning several powers of ten, connect roots or powers to a meaningful model, and communicate scale without misleading precision.

Open Project Workspace

Project sequence

Build an evidence-aware scale story.

Select a safe public topic

Examples: planetary distances, microscopic life, ocean depth, energy use, computer storage, population, environmental measurements, architecture, or material thickness.

Collect and document quantities

Use at least twelve sourced values with units. Include values from at least four different orders of magnitude and record measurement precision.

Represent powers and roots

Convert all values to scientific notation. Include at least two exponent-property calculations and one root or geometric scaling calculation.

Compare and model scale

Calculate ratios between selected values, create a logarithmic-style power-of-ten timeline or scale model, and explain where ordinary linear drawings fail.

Communicate limits and conclusions

State assumptions, rounding, significant digits, source limitations, and one claim that could be misleading without scale context.
