Foundations & Readiness · Lesson 02

Fractions, Decimals, Percents & Ratios

Move flexibly among equivalent representations and use proportional reasoning in practical situations.

Math Word: Equivalent

Two representations are equivalent when they have the same value even though they look different.

3/4 = 0.75 = 75%

Guided refresh

  • Use common denominators to add/subtract fractions.
  • Convert fractions to decimals by division.
  • Convert decimals to percents by multiplying by 100%.
  • A unit rate compares a quantity to exactly one unit of another quantity.

Example: 5 notebooks cost $12.50, so the unit price is $12.50÷5=$2.50 per notebook.

Independent readiness checks

1. Simplify 18/24.
2. Compute 2/3+1/6.
3. Convert 0.375 to a percent and a simplified fraction.
4. Find 35% of 80.
5. A car travels 180 miles on 6 gallons. Find the unit rate in miles per gallon.
6. A recipe uses 3 cups of flour for 2 loaves. How much flour is needed for 5 loaves at the same ratio?

Application

A jacket is discounted 20% from $75, then a 6% sales tax is applied to the discounted price. Show both stages and explain why the order matters.

Week 2 evidence: represent the same quantity in more than one form and solve at least one proportional application.
