Variables & Mathematical Language

Translate between words and symbols, evaluate expressions, and recognize algebraic structure.

Math Word: Coefficient

A coefficient is the numerical factor multiplying a variable. In 7x, the coefficient is 7.

Guided refresh

  • A term is separated by addition or subtraction.
  • A constant has no variable.
  • Substitution means replacing a variable with a value.
  • Equivalent expressions have the same value for all allowed inputs.

Example: “Five more than twice a number” becomes 2x+5, not 2(x+5).

Independent readiness checks

1. Identify the coefficient and constant in 6x−11.
2. Evaluate 3a+4 when a=5.
3. Translate: “four less than three times n.”
4. Simplify 5x+2+3x−7.
5. Are 2(x+3) and 2x+3 equivalent? Explain.
6. A phone plan costs $18 plus $4 per gigabyte. Write an expression for g gigabytes.

Application

Create one real-world situation represented by 12+3x. Label the meaning of 12, 3, and x.

Week 3 evidence: translate, evaluate, simplify, and explain the meaning of algebraic parts.
