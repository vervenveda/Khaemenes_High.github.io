Foundations & Readiness · Lesson 04

Equations & Inequalities

Solve by preserving balance, verify solutions, and recognize when a step changes the meaning of an inequality.

Math Word: Inverse

Inverse operations undo one another: addition/subtraction and multiplication/division.

Whatever valid operation you perform on one side of an equation, perform on the other side.

Guided refresh

  • Undo operations in reverse order.
  • Check a solution by substitution.
  • When multiplying or dividing an inequality by a negative number, reverse the inequality symbol.
  • A correct-looking answer reached through an invalid step still needs repair.

Independent readiness checks

1. Solve x+9=21 and check.
2. Solve 4x−7=29.
3. Solve 3(x+2)=24.
4. Solve −2x>10 and explain what happens to the inequality sign.
5. A learner changes 5x+3=18 into 5x=21. Identify and correct the invalid step.
6. Write and solve: “A number decreased by 8 is 15.”

Application

A budget allows at most $60. A fixed fee is $12 and each activity costs $8. Write and solve an inequality for the maximum number of activities.

Week 4 evidence: solve, check, and diagnose an invalid equation or inequality step.
