Educator planning

Teacher Guide

A pacing, differentiation, assessment, and modelling guide for Unit 10.

Pacing

Four-week sequence

Use approximately two sessions per lesson, with additional sessions for representation workshops, the project, review, and mastery assessment.

Core routines

Connect representations

Ask students to move repeatedly among words, tables, ordered pairs, graphs, equations, and function notation. Require units and domain statements.

Misconceptions

Watch closely

Address reversed ordered pairs, scale errors, confusing f(x) with multiplication, rise/run sign errors, treating every trend as exact, and assuming extrapolation is automatically reliable.

Differentiation

Adjust by skill

Foundation learners use grids, mapping diagrams, and change arrows. Extended learners compare candidate models, transformations, parameters, and residual patterns.

Assessment use

Require reasoning

Use lesson items formatively. For mastery evidence, require students to explain coordinates, function tests, slope units, model domain, and limitations in addition to selecting answers.

Technology and privacy

Keep data safe

The unit works locally without accounts. Projects should use public, fictional, or non-sensitive measured data and should document sources without collecting private identifiers.
