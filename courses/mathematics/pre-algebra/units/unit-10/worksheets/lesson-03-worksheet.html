Lesson 03 · Functions & Linear Modelling Studio

Five ungraded investigations. Model the scenario, show a table, graph or equation when useful, explain your rate or pattern, and identify limitations.

A · Library checkout function

Each library card ID is assigned exactly one current borrowing limit. Explain why this is a function even if two cards share the same limit.

B · Not a function

A relation contains (1,4),(2,6),(1,7),(3,6). Explain the violation and repair one ordered pair.

C · Water dispenser table

A dispenser fills 2.5 liters per minute. List the outputs for inputs 0, 2, 5, and 8 minutes and give its rule.

D · Inverse ambiguity

A family relation maps a child to their birth month; its reverse maps each month to its children. Which direction is guaranteed to be a function?

E · Build a domain

A snack stand charges $3 per snack and has only 12 snacks. Define a realistic domain for number sold and list the possible outputs.
