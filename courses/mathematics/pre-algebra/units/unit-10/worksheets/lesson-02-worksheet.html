Lesson 02 · Functions & Linear Modelling Studio

Five ungraded investigations. Model the scenario, show a table, graph or equation when useful, explain your rate or pattern, and identify limitations.

A · Festival seating pattern

Rows have 8, 13, 18, 23 seats. Find the nth-row rule and predict row 15.

B · Garden tile border

A growing design uses 5 tiles in stage 1, 9 in stage 2, and 13 in stage 3. Write a rule for stage n and explain the initial value.

C · Repair a prediction

A student assumes 2, 4, 8, 16 grows by adding 2 and predicts 18 next. Determine the correct rule and next two terms.

D · Two growing savings

Plan A saves $12 initially and $4 each week; Plan B saves $2 initially and $6 each week. Write rules and find when amounts match.

E · Design a counterexample

Give two sequences with the same first two terms but different third terms. Explain why two points cannot establish the rule uniquely.
