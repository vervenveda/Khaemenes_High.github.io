Lesson 04 · Functions & Linear Modelling Studio

Five ungraded investigations. Model the scenario, show a table, graph or equation when useful, explain your rate or pattern, and identify limitations.

A · Bicycle rental four ways

A bicycle rental charges $9 fixed plus $4 an hour. Give an equation, a three-row table, and a verbal description; identify a point to graph.

B · Spot inconsistent row

A table shows (0,6),(1,10),(2,14),(3,19). Determine the likely rule and repair the inconsistent point.

C · Graph to words

A line passes through (0,12) and (4,28). Explain its starting value, rate of change, and equation.

D · Which representation?

A charity collects $8 for every shirt sold with a one-time $25 donation. Give a graph intercept and tell whether the relation is proportional.

E · Create a matching model

Invent a real-world situation for y=2.5x−5, state a sensible domain, and list two matching table entries.

Reflection / Error Analysis

Choose one relationship from this lesson. Represent it in at least two forms, explain the input/output meaning, identify rate or pattern evidence, verify a point, and state one limitation or likely error.
