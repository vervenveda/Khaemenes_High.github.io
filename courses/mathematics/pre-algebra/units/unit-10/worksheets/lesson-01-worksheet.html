Lesson 01 · Functions & Linear Modelling Studio

Five ungraded investigations. Model the scenario, show a table, graph or equation when useful, explain your rate or pattern, and identify limitations.

A · Rescue-drone navigation

A rescue drone begins at the origin, flies 4 units west and 3 north, then 6 east and 5 south. Plot its destination and name the quadrant.

B · Museum map reflection

A sculpture is at (−5,2). Its mirror across the y-axis is where? Explain which coordinate changes and why.

C · Robotics checkpoint

A robot moves from (−2,−3) to (4,−3). Find the horizontal distance and explain whether it crosses an axis.

D · Confused coordinates

A student locates (3,−6) at (−6,3). Identify the mistake and describe the correct movement from the origin.

E · Create a treasure map

Create four landmarks, one in each quadrant, and identify one point that lies on an axis rather than in a quadrant.

Reflection / Error Analysis
