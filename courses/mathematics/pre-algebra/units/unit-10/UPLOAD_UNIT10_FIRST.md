# Upload Unit 10 First

1. Unzip this package.
2. Upload the complete `courses/mathematics/pre-algebra/units/unit-10/` folder beside Units 1–9.
3. Replace `courses/mathematics/pre-algebra/index.html` and `course-map.json`.
4. Replace root `README.md`, `manifest.webmanifest`, and `service-worker.js`.
5. Add `docs/releases/UNIT10_RELEASE.md` and `docs/audits/UNIT10_VALIDATION.md`.

Do not move or replace Units 1–9, Grade 9, or the diagnostic.
