Family Guide

A plain-language guide to coordinates, functions, slope, graphs, and responsible prediction.

Big picture

What students are learning

Students learn how locations, patterns, input-output rules, graphs, and equations connect. They also learn how a line can describe a rate and starting value.

Helpful questions

Ask for meaning

What does this point represent? Which variable is the input? What are the units of the slope? Does the prediction stay inside the observed data range?

Everyday examples

Notice relationships

Look for fixed fees plus rates, travel distance over time, temperature trends, savings patterns, recipes, art scaling, and resource use.

Graph reading

Check axes and scale

Before interpreting a graph, identify the axes, units, origin, tick scale, and whether the visual starts at zero or uses a restricted range.

Support without giving answers

Encourage checking

Ask the learner to test a point in the equation, compare two table rows, draw a slope triangle, or explain why an extrapolation may be uncertain.

Project safety

Use appropriate data

Choose public, fictional, or non-sensitive family measurements. Avoid names, exact locations, health records, account information, or other private data.
