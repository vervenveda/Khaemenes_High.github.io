Lesson 03 · Proportional Reasoning Studio

Explore all five ungraded missions. Label units, write a mathematical model, show calculations, and check whether the result makes sense.

A · Farmers market comparison

A stall sells 6 apples for $4.50; another sells 10 for $7.20. Which is cheaper per apple, and what would 30 apples cost at that rate?

B · Delivery speed

A bicycle courier rides 42 km in 2.5 hours. Calculate average speed and estimate distance in 4 hours at the same speed.

C · Detect the misleading label

A shop advertises 12 pens for $8.40 as a better deal than 5 for $3.25. Test the advertisement with unit prices.

D · Recipe serving price

A soup recipe costing $13.50 serves 6. Find the cost per serving and the estimated cost for 14 servings at unchanged prices.

E · Design a fair test

Two streaming plans charge different prices for different numbers of films. Invent valid prices and compare the cost per film; explain a limit of this comparison.

Reflection

Explain what you learned about unit rates and fair comparisons. Include one example and one reasonableness check.
