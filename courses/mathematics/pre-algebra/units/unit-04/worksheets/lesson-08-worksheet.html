Lesson 08 · Proportional Reasoning Studio

Explore all five ungraded missions. Label units, write a mathematical model, show calculations, and check whether the result makes sense.

A · Water tank conversion

A tank holds 18 gallons. Using 1 gallon ≈ 3.785 liters, estimate its volume in liters and assess whether 70 liters is reasonable.

B · Marathon hydration

Runners need 0.6 L per hour. For 18 runners averaging 2.5 hours, calculate liters needed, then add a 10% reserve.

C · Scale and units

A trail map uses 2 cm for 7.5 km. A route is 12 cm on the map. Find real distance in kilometers and meters.

D · Budget and portions

A class buys snacks at $2.40 for 3 servings. There are 35 students who each need 2 servings. Find needed servings and total budget.

E · Plan an event

Create a multistep proportional model for supplies at a school event. Include a unit conversion, a numerical estimate, and a note about fixed costs.

Reflection

Explain what you learned about units, estimation, and modelling. Include one example and one reasonableness check.
