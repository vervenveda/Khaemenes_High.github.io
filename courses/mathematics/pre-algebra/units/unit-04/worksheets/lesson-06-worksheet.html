Lesson 06 · Proportional Reasoning Studio

Explore all five ungraded missions. Label units, write a mathematical model, show calculations, and check whether the result makes sense.

A · Museum map

On a map 1.5 cm represents 6 km. A walking route measures 8.25 cm. Find its actual length with units.

B · Model village

A model barn is 18 cm tall at scale 1:50. Find actual height in meters and explain conversion.

C · Photo enlargement

A photo 12 cm by 16 cm is enlarged by scale factor 1.75. Find new dimensions and compare the areas.

D · Fix the blueprint

A student enlarges a 4-by-7 rectangle to 12-by-18. Explain why the figures are not similar and correct one dimension.

E · Design a park map

Design a 60 m by 40 m rectangular park using scale 1 cm : 10 m. Give drawing dimensions and perimeter in real units.

Reflection

Explain what you learned about scale factors, similarity, and units. Include one example and one reasonableness check.
