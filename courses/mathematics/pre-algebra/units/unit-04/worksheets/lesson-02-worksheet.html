Lesson 02 · Proportional Reasoning Studio

Explore all five ungraded missions. Label units, write a mathematical model, show calculations, and check whether the result makes sense.

A · Stage lighting schedule

Four lights need 6 minutes to check. At the same steady per-light rate, how long for 18 lights? Make a ratio table.

B · Bike route milestones

A rider covers 15 km in 50 minutes at a constant pace. Locate distances at 10, 30, and 80 minutes on a double number line.

C · Catch the table error

A table pairs 2 notebooks with $7, 4 with $14, and 8 with $30. Identify the inconsistent pair and correct it.

D · Water station model

A refill station provides 9 liters in 3 minutes. Build 3 rows of a ratio table, including the time for 24 liters.

E · Create and verify

Invent a real-life ratio table with four rows, then explain the multiplication rule connecting every row.

Reflection

Explain what you learned about ratio tables and double number lines. Include one example and one reasonableness check.
