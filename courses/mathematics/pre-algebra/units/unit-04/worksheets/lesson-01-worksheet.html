Lesson 01 · Proportional Reasoning Studio

Explore all five ungraded missions. Label units, write a mathematical model, show calculations, and check whether the result makes sense.

A · Festival supply ratios

For every 3 red flags, a festival uses 5 gold flags. The team has 72 flags total. How many of each color are needed? Explain why your ratio is equivalent.

B · Garden planning

A garden uses 4 herb plants for every 7 vegetable plants. If there are 33 plants, calculate each group. Show an equivalent ratio.

C · Repair the claim

A student says 6:9 becomes 10:13 when 4 is added to each term. Test the claim with quotients and correct it.

D · Design a game

A strategy game rewards 2 stars for every 9 tokens. Plan a level that awards 18 stars. Determine tokens and justify using a ratio table.

E · Write a counterexample

Is the ratio of two quantities unchanged when the same positive amount is added to both? Provide a numerical counterexample.

Reflection

Explain what you learned about multiplicative vs additive thinking. Include one example and one reasonableness check.
