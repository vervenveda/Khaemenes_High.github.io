Lesson 07 · Proportional Reasoning Studio

Explore all five ungraded missions. Label units, write a mathematical model, show calculations, and check whether the result makes sense.

A · Taxi fare puzzle

A taxi charges $4 to start plus $2.20 per kilometer. Is total fare proportional to distance? Write equation and find fare for 8 km.

B · Club dues comparison

Club A charges $6 monthly; Club B charges a $15 joining fee plus $3 monthly. Which total is proportional to months, and when do costs match?

C · Table investigation

For x values 1, 2, 3, 4, the y values are 4, 8, 12, 17. Find the outlier and describe a proportional rule.

D · Origin check

A line has points (0,5), (2,9), (4,13). Explain whether it represents direct variation, and find its equation.

E · Invent two businesses

Create a proportional cost model and a cost model with a fixed fee. Give equations and explain how a customer could distinguish them.

Reflection

Explain what you learned about evidence for proportionality. Include one example and one reasonableness check.
