Lesson 05 · Proportional Reasoning Studio

Explore all five ungraded missions. Label units, write a mathematical model, show calculations, and check whether the result makes sense.

A · Costume fabric

Seven costumes require 10.5 meters of fabric. How much fabric for 18 similar costumes? Solve by two methods.

B · Solar charging

A solar battery gains 18 percentage points of charge in 45 minutes at a constant rate. How many points in 80 minutes? Explain assumptions.

C · Spot the invalid move

To solve x/12 = 5/8, a student adds 12 and 8 and concludes x=25. Repair the method and verify.

D · Direct variation mission

The amount of paint P varies directly with mural area A. When A=8 m², P=1.6 L. Find the equation and paint for 23 m².

E · Choose a method

A fundraiser collects $84 for 12 identical kits. Predict revenue from 35 kits; show a proportion and an equivalent unit-rate method.

Reflection

Explain what you learned about strategy choice and verification. Include one example and one reasonableness check.
