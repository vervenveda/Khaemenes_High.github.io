    Proportion Lab

    Scale one ratio and inspect its constant rate.

    Choose a base pair and scale factor. The lab preserves the ratio, calculates both unit rates, and writes the proportional equation.

    Base ratio 3:5 3/5
    Equivalent ratio 12:20 × 4
    Constant k = y/x 5/3 ≈ 1.667
    Second per first 1.667 y-units per x-unit
    First per second 0.6 x-units per y-unit
    Equation y = 5/3x y ≈ 1.667x

    Both terms are multiplied by 4, so the quotient y/x remains constant.
