Teacher Guide

Pacing, misconceptions, differentiation, discussion prompts, and assessment guidance for Unit 4.

Open Core Practice

Suggested pacing

  • Week 1: Lessons 1–3—ratio meaning, representations, and unit rates.
  • Week 2: Lessons 4–6—proportional equations, proportions, and scale.
  • Week 3: Lessons 7–8—classification, conversions, modelling, project, and mastery check.

High-value questions

What is being compared, and in what order?
What stays constant?
What does one unit represent?
Does the graph pass through the origin?
Are the units compatible?

Common misconceptions

  • Treating ratios as differences.
  • Scaling only one term.
  • Comparing totals instead of unit rates.
  • Assuming every straight line is proportional.
  • Ignoring fixed fees or starting values.
  • Using map scales without converting units.

Differentiation

Foundation: concrete counters, repeated addition, double number lines, labels, and one-step scale factors.

Core: complete representations and multistep applications.

Extended: proof, negative constants, precision, area scaling, and competing models.

Assessment use

Use lesson practice formatively. The three pathway practices support targeted review. The mastery check reports domain performance but should not be treated as secure high-stakes testing because answer data is delivered to the browser.

Project guidance

Require explicit units, source notes, a defensible proportional assumption, and at least one limitation. Encourage students to compare a proportional model with a possible nonproportional alternative.
