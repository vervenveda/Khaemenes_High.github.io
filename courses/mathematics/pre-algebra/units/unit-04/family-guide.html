Family Guide

How ratios and rates describe everyday comparisons, and how families can support reasoning without taking over the work.

What this unit teaches

Students learn to compare quantities with ratios, find per-one rates, recognize proportional relationships, solve proportions, interpret scale drawings, and decide when constant scaling is a reasonable model.

Helpful questions

What are the two quantities and units?
What does “per one” mean here?
Did both quantities scale by the same factor?
What is the starting value?
Would doubling one quantity double the other?

Practice naturally

  • Compare grocery unit prices.
  • Scale a recipe.
  • Estimate travel speed.
  • Use a map scale.
  • Compare game scoring rates.
  • Track water or energy use per day.

Help without taking over

  • Ask the learner to label units.
  • Return to a table or double number line.
  • Encourage finding the unit rate.
  • Check whether the graph should begin at zero.
  • Ask for an estimate before exact calculation.

Understanding pathways

Foundation provides models and guided steps. Core is the complete expected learning. Extended adds proof, model comparison, and unfamiliar applications. A learner may use different pathways for different skills.

When extra support may help

  • Ratio order changes unintentionally.
  • Only one term is scaled.
  • Units disappear during rate calculations.
  • Fixed fees are mistaken for proportions.
  • Corresponding sides are mismatched.
