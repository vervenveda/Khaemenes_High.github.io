Educator planning

Teacher Guide

A pacing, differentiation, assessment, precision, and modelling guide for Unit 11.

Pacing

Three-week sequence

Use roughly one to two sessions per lesson, then reserve sessions for the design studio, project workshop, review, and mastery assessment.

Core routines

Draw, label, calculate, interpret

Require a labelled diagram, formula or relationship, substitution with units, calculation, and contextual interpretation. Ask learners to estimate before calculating.

Misconceptions

Watch closely

Address radius/diameter confusion, slanted versus perpendicular height, shared boundary counting, linear-versus-area scale, square/cubic units, hypotenuse identification, and premature rounding.

Differentiation

Adjust by representation

Foundation learners use physical tools, grids, nets, colour-coded formulas, and integer triples. Extended learners derive relationships, optimize, use radicals, and critique tolerance and model assumptions.

Assessment use

Require evidence

For mastery evidence, require correct units, diagrams, scale interpretation, and explanation of precision or constraints—not answer selection alone.

Safety and privacy

Use appropriate measurements

Projects should use public, fictional, or non-sensitive dimensions. Avoid publishing exact private home layouts, addresses, security details, or personally identifying location data.
