Accessible Space & Scale Design Investigation

Create a safe fictional, public, or non-sensitive geometric design that connects measurement, scale, perimeter, area, right-triangle distance, material estimates, tolerance, and limitations.

Open Project Workspace

Project sequence

Build and verify an evidence-aware geometric design.

Select a safe design question

Examples: accessible reading area, community garden, exhibit booth, fictional room, package, habitat feature, public event layout, or art installation. Do not publish exact private home or security layouts.

Define units, scale, constraints, and tolerance

State actual and drawing units, scale factor, required clearances or capacity, measurement precision, assumptions, and acceptable variation.

Create connected representations

Produce a labelled scale drawing, dimension table, formulas, perimeter or circumference, area, and at least one coordinate or right-triangle calculation.

Estimate materials or capacity

Calculate surface area, volume, boundary material, flooring, paint, soil, storage, or another meaningful quantity. Include a justified allowance when appropriate.

Verify and communicate limitations

Check unit consistency, scale, reasonableness, rounding, constraints, and tolerance. Explain what the model can and cannot guarantee.
