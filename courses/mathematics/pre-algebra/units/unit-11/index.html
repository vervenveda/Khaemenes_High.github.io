    Change dimensions, scale, and unit—then compare perimeter, area, surface area, and volume.

    OHMIC Accessible Space Studio

    The mathematical work remains primary. OHMIC provides a place to draw, dimension, revise, layer, and export the student’s geometric model.

    1. Measure or define safe, fictional dimensions and units.
    2. Select a scale and convert every actual length to a drawing length.
    3. Draw the boundary and label doors, paths, zones, or fixtures.
    4. Add dimensions for lengths, widths, clearances, and diagonals.
    5. Calculate perimeter, area, material quantities, and allowances.
    6. Document tolerance, accessibility assumptions, and model limits.
