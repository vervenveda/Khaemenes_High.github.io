Unit 11 · Geometry & Design Studio — Worked Solutions

Teacher guidance for 40 ungraded investigations. Accept mathematically sound alternatives, properly labelled dimensions and stated assumptions. Keep assessment scoring independent.

Lesson 01

A · Garden measurements: P=2(12.4+8.75)=42.3 m=4230 cm.

B · Cabinet tolerance: Opening 84.8–85.2 cm and cabinet 84.7–85.1 cm overlap; a fit is not guaranteed.

C · Hallway scale: 4.2÷0.6=7 cm.

D · Accuracy investigation: Error=+0.200 m. Additional decimal places do not guarantee accuracy.

E · Choosing tools: Surveyed route/GPS or wheel for trail; caliper/micrometer for wire. Accuracy depends on tool calibration and conditions.

Lesson 02

A · Transversal challenge: Corresponding acute angles 63°; adjacent obtuse angles 117°.

B · Nine-sided polygon: Sum=(9−2)×180=1260°; each 140°.

C · Triangle detective: Third=75°. All angles acute.

D · Invalid quadrilateral: Sum=365°, but should be 360°. For example, replace 95° with 90°.

E · Exterior angles: Sides=360÷24=15. Interior angle=156°.

Lesson 03

A · Playground fence: 2(18+12)−2.5=57.5 m.

B · Circular pond: 2πr=9π≈28.26 m; circumference measures boundary length.

C · Notched courtyard: 32 m. Two removed outer edges of length 2 m are replaced by two equal inner edges.

D · Racetrack scaling: C=πd doubles, from 20π to 40π m.

E · Hexagonal pen: Perimeter=45m. Total=45×18+90=$900.

Lesson 04

A · Triangle banner: Area=0.5×6×3.5=10.5 m².

B · Paved courtyard: 240−28.26=211.74 m².

C · L-shaped room: 96−15=81 m².

D · Enlarged triangle: Area scales by 9. Example base 2,height 4 gives 4 m²; scaled base 6,height 12 gives 36 m².

E · Wall painting: Paintable area=19m²; 19÷5=3.8L. Buy at least 4L.

Lesson 05

A · Bridge scale: Each cm represents 4m; 7.5×4=30m.

B · Poster enlargement: Factor=2.5. Height=75cm; area multiplier=2.5²=6.25.

C · Miniature tank: Real dimensions 0.8,1.2,2m. Volume=1.92m³.

D · Area scaling error: Original 9cm²; new 36cm². Area quadruples.

E · Blueprint conversion: 900÷150=6cm; 600÷150=4cm.

Lesson 06

A · Ladder length: √(12²+5²)=13ft.

B · Park shortcut: Diagonal=26m; side route=34m; save 8m.

C · Missing leg: √(17²−8²)=15cm; 64+225=289.

D · Right-angle test: 7²+10²=149, unlike 12²=144; not right angled.

E · Sensor distance: √(8²+6²)=10 coordinate units.

Lesson 07

A · Shipping box: Volume=30000cm³=30L.

B · Wrap a cube: SA=6×36=216cm²; V=216cm³. Wrapping depends on area.

C · Open-top planter: Base 0.6m²; four walls 0.96+0.4=1.36m²; total 1.96m².

D · Missing cube face: Six faces total 6×16=96cm²; missing face 16cm².

E · Scale a container: Area factor 9; volume factor 27. SA 24→216cm²; V 8→216cm³.

Lesson 08

A · Coordinate garden: Dimensions 8m by 5m; perimeter 26m; area 40m².

B · Ramp grade: 0.75÷9×100≈8.33%. Other conditions and applicable codes matter.

C · Area uncertainty: Min 5.9×3.9=23.01m²; max 6.1×4.1=25.01m².

D · Fixed fence length: 2(L+10)=60, so L=20m. Area=200m².

E · Scale unit error: 7×200=1400cm=14m, not 1400m.
