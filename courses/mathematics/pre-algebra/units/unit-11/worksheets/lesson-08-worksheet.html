Lesson 08 · Geometry & Design Studio

Five ungraded investigations: use drawings, formulas, unit conversions and reasonableness checks.

A · Coordinate garden

A rectangle has vertices (1,2),(9,2),(9,7),(1,7), measured in meters. Find perimeter and area.

B · Ramp grade

A ramp rises 0.75m over 9m horizontal. Calculate slope percent; explain why this alone cannot prove code compliance.

C · Area uncertainty

A floor is measured 6.0±0.1m by 4.0±0.1m. Compute minimum and maximum possible areas.

D · Fixed fence length

A rectangular enclosure's perimeter is 60m and width is 10m. Find its length and area.

E · Scale unit error

A 1:200 drawing has a 7cm segment. A student claims it represents 1400m. Correct the units and answer.

Reflection / Error Analysis

Choose one geometry problem from this lesson. Draw and label it, show the governing relationship, calculate with units, identify one likely measurement or reasoning error, and verify the result.
