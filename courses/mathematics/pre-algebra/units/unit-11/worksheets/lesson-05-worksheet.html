1. At 1 cm:50 cm, 6 cm on a drawing represents:

(A) 300 cm   (B) 56 cm   (C) 500 cm   (D) 3,000 cm

2. 4/10=x/25. The value of x is:

(A) 8   (B) 12.5   (C) 10   (D) 15

3. Which pair of corresponding side ratios proves similarity?

(A) 3/5 and 6/10   (B) 2/3 and 4/5   (C) 4/7 and 8/15   (D) 5/8 and 10/18

4. A dilation with scale factor 2 makes lengths:

(A) four times as long   (B) unchanged   (C) twice as long   (D) half as long

5. Similar figures have:

(A) equal corresponding angles and proportional corresponding sides   (B) equal area always   (C) equal side lengths always   (D) different angle measures

6. Map scale 1 in:20 mi; 2.5 in represents:

(A) 50 mi   (B) 8 mi   (C) 22.5 mi   (D) 200 mi

7. Scale factor 0.5 is a:

(A) enlargement   (B) translation   (C) rotation   (D) reduction

8. Why state scale on a drawing?

(A) to guarantee angles are 90°   (B) to remove units   (C) so drawing measurements can be converted to actual dimensions   (D) to determine color

9. A student compares non-corresponding sides in a similarity proportion. Issue:

(A) units must differ   (B) ratios must pair corresponding dimensions   (C) all side ratios work   (D) angles determine length directly

10. When using scale ratios with different units, first ensure:

(A) the ratio meaning and units are consistent   (B) units are ignored   (C) all values are squared   (D) angles replace lengths
