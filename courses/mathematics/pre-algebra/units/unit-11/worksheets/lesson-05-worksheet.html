Lesson 05 · Geometry & Design Studio

Five ungraded investigations: use drawings, formulas, unit conversions and reasonableness checks.

A · Bridge scale

A map shows 3 cm for 12 m. A bridge measures 7.5 cm. Find real length.

B · Poster enlargement

A 20×30 cm poster is enlarged to 50 cm wide. Find new height and area factor.

C · Miniature tank

Model dimensions 4,6,10 cm have scale 1:20. Find real dimensions in meters and volume.

D · Area scaling error

A student doubles a 3 cm square's side and says area doubles. Calculate original and enlarged areas.

E · Blueprint conversion

A 9m by 6m garden is drawn at scale 1:150. Find dimensions in centimeters.

Reflection / Error Analysis

Choose one geometry problem from this lesson. Draw and label it, show the governing relationship, calculate with units, identify one likely measurement or reasoning error, and verify the result.
