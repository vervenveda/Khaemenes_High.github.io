Lesson 02 · Geometry & Design Studio

Five ungraded investigations: use drawings, formulas, unit conversions and reasonableness checks.

A · Transversal challenge

Parallel lines meet a transversal; an acute angle is 63°. Find corresponding angles and adjacent obtuse angles.

B · Nine-sided polygon

Find the interior-angle sum and each interior angle in a regular nine-sided polygon.

C · Triangle detective

A triangle has angles 38° and 67°. Find its missing angle and classify by angles.

D · Invalid quadrilateral

A quadrilateral is marked 90°,100°,80°,95°. Explain whether the drawing can be correct and repair it.

E · Exterior angles

A regular polygon has exterior angles of 24°. Find number of sides and an interior angle.

Reflection / Error Analysis

Choose one geometry problem from this lesson. Draw and label it, show the governing relationship, calculate with units, identify one likely measurement or reasoning error, and verify the result.
