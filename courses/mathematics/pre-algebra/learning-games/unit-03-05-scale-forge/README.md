This companion follows the lesson’s actual structure: fraction multiplication as scaling and “part of a part,” signed fraction products, mixed-number conversion, cross-cancellation, and estimation as a reasonableness check.

The Animated Instruction Studio includes Part of a Part, with an animated 4 × 5 area model showing why

3/4 × 2/5 = 6/20 = 3/10

plus Cross-Cancel, demonstrating

15/28 × 14/25 → 3/10;

Mixed → Single Factor, showing

−2 1/3 × 3/7 → −7/3 × 3/7 → −1;

and Scale & Sign Prediction, where students first predict that

7 1/2 × 1/4

must remain positive but become much smaller before confirming the exact result 1 7/8.

That predictive layer is especially important because the source lesson explicitly frames multiplication as scaling and asks students to estimate whether products should be smaller, similar, or larger than their starting quantities.

The mission bank covers the lesson’s 20 skills and adds Extended reasoning such as:

21/32 × 16/49 = 3/14
2 3/5 × 1 1/4 = 3 1/4
2/3 of 3/5 of 10 = 4
predicting the effect of factors greater than 1, between 0 and 1, and negative scale factors
distinguishing legitimate cross-cancellation from incorrect fraction manipulation

The established architecture remains intact: Foundation / Core / Extended, 12/16/20 mission rounds, score/streak/accuracy tracking, immediate explanations, hints, missed-item replay, local best score, print support, keyboard controls, responsive design, and the 80% mastery target.
