Divisibility Field Guide

Divisible by 2The last digit is even: 0, 2, 4, 6, or 8.
Divisible by 3The sum of the digits is divisible by 3.
Divisible by 5The last digit is 0 or 5.
Divisible by 7No short universal classroom rule is needed here—test the quotient directly.
Divisible by 11For these small numbers, test the quotient or use the alternating-sum rule.
Prime reasoning

To prove a number prime, you only need to test possible prime divisors up to its square root.
