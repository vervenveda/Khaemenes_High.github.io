Opposites are mirror positions.

−4 and +4 lie on different sides of zero but are the same distance from zero.

Opposite numbers have equal absolute value and opposite signs.

Measure the distance from −12 to 0.

Absolute value measures distance.

The bars do not mean “make it positive.” They ask for the nonnegative distance from zero.

  • |−12| = 12
  • |12| = 12
  • |0| = 0
Distance cannot be negative.

Zero is its own mirror image.

Zero is the only integer whose opposite is itself, and its distance from zero is also zero.

Opposite(0) = 0 and |0| = 0.
distance from −4 to +7
Difference: 7 − (−4) = 11
Distance form: |7 − (−4)| = 11
Reverse order: |−4 − 7| = 11

Distance is independent of direction.

The Core and Extended pathways connect absolute value to distance between positions. Reversing the subtraction changes the sign inside the bars but not the final distance.

|a − b| = |b − a| because both expressions measure the same separation.
