Zero is neither positive nor negative.

Zero tells us where the measurement starts. A sign only makes sense after that reference is known.

Ask first: “What does zero mean here?”

The sign and magnitude do different jobs.

For −12, the negative sign gives direction. The 12 gives the size of the position or change.

Sign = direction. Magnitude = size or distance.
Temperature6° below zero → −6
Money$45 deposit → +45
Movementdown 9 floors → −9
Elevation320 ft above sea level → +320
Sportslose 7 yards → −7
Net changeno increase or decrease → 0

The context determines what the sign means.

Positive can mean above, gain, increase, forward, or deposit. Negative can mean below, loss, decrease, backward, or withdrawal.

Do not memorize “negative = bad.” Read the direction defined by the situation.
Sea level = 0A platform 8 m above sea level is +8.
Deck level = 0If the same platform is 3 m below the deck, it is −3 relative to the deck.
Same physical placeThe reference changed—not the platform.

Signed values depend on the chosen reference.

A physical position can have different signed values when zero changes.
