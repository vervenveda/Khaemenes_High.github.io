GCF & LCM Reference

GCF: factor viewList the factors of both numbers, identify the common factors, then choose the greatest one.
LCM: multiple viewList positive multiples of both numbers and stop at the first value that appears in both lists.
Prime-factor shortcutGCF uses shared prime factors at their smallest exponents; LCM uses all prime factors at their greatest exponents.
Useful relationshipFor positive integers a and b: GCF(a,b) × LCM(a,b) = a × b.
Strategy

For GCF, think “largest shared divisor.” For LCM, think “first shared landing point.”
