Subtracting a negative moves right.

−4 − (−5) becomes −4 + 5. Adding +5 means move five units to the right.

Subtract a negative → add a positive.
Initial temperature: −6°C
Final temperature: +4°C
final − initial = 4 − (−6)

Signed difference answers “how did the value change?”

Final minus initial gives direction as well as size. A positive difference means the final value is higher than the initial value.

Signed change = final − initial.
Signed change: −5 − 3 = −8This says the value moved downward by 8.
Distance: |−5 − 3| = 8This says the positions are 8 units apart.
Same size, different meaningSigned difference keeps direction; absolute distance does not.

Subtraction and absolute distance answer different questions.

This is the lesson’s Extended pathway: `a − b` can be negative, but `|a − b|` is always nonnegative.

Difference can have direction. Distance cannot.
