Reasonableness Toolkit

Round strategicallyUse easy nearby numbers: 398 ≈ 400, 49 ≈ 50, 1.98 ≈ 2.
Use benchmarksFractions near 0, 1/2, and 1 can quickly reveal whether a result makes sense.
Watch operation sizeMultiplying by a number greater than 1 increases magnitude; dividing by a number greater than 1 decreases it.
Check unitsWord problems should produce an answer in the expected unit and scale.
Strategy

Do a rough calculation first. Exact arithmetic is not needed to reject wildly unreasonable answers.
