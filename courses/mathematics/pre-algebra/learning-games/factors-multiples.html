Factor Laboratory Notes

Factor pair Two whole numbers whose product equals the target: 3 × 8 = 24.
Array A rectangle made from equal rows and columns. Turning it 90° does not create a new factor pair.
Perfect square A number with a square factor pair such as 6 × 6 = 36.
Prime number A number with exactly one unique factor pair: 1 × itself.
Completeness strategy

Test possible row lengths from 1 upward. Once the first number in the pair passes the square root of the target, any new rectangle would only repeat one you already found.
