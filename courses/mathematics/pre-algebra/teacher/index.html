Pre-Algebra Teacher & Records Center

Canonical educator resources for the 36-week open-age Pre-Algebra course, including weekly mastery, unit evidence, mixed-evidence cumulative examinations, investigations, records, and Algebra I readiness.

Course HomeAssessment CenterCompletion Record
Exceptional evidence standard: weekly mastery uses 10 questions with five reasoning dimensions; Weeks 2–36 include 8 current + 2 delayed-retrieval items. Legacy five-question planner scores remain historical evidence only. Unit mastery remains 20–24 items with an 80% threshold, and cumulative exams combine selected response with evaluator-reviewed constructed reasoning.
