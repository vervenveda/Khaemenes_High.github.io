About Pre-Algebra

Pre-Algebra builds the mathematical habits students need before formal algebra: fluent number reasoning, accurate computation, clear representation, multi-step problem solving, mathematical communication, and confident transition into Algebra I.

Course Features

36-Week PathwayA complete year of structured instruction with readiness, lessons, practice, review, mastery checks, and cumulative evidence.
80% MasteryProgression is mastery-based. Students review, correct, and retry when needed before advancing.
Applied MathematicsFinancial mathematics, proportional reasoning, modelling, statistics, geometry, probability, and real-world problem solving are integrated throughout.
Algebra I ReadinessThe course ends with cumulative preparation for equations, functions, graphing, modelling, and formal Algebra I reasoning.
