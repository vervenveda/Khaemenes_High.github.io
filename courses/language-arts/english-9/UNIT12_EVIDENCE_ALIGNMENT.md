# English 9 · Unit 12 Evidence Alignment
## Capstone Portfolio & Oral Defense

**Upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Unit 12 propagation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade-level rhythm

**Curate Evidence → Revise → Synthesize → Demonstrate → Defend**

Grade 9 closes with visible evidence of growth: students curate work, revise major pieces, synthesize learning across the year, complete the cumulative assessment, and defend their choices orally.

## Three-week architecture

### Week 34 · Portfolio Curation & Reflection
1. **Curate Evidence:** Select artifacts that demonstrate growth across reading, writing, speaking, research, and language—not simply the highest scores.
2. **Map Standards:** Connect each selected artifact to the skill or standard it demonstrates and cite the specific evidence within the work.
3. **Compare:** Place an early and later artifact side by side and identify the most important change in reasoning, craft, evidence use, or independence.
4. **Revise:** Choose one major artifact for substantive revision based on current skill rather than cosmetic correction alone.
5. **Reflect:** Write a portfolio reflection naming strongest growth, persistent challenge, most important revision, and evidence supporting each judgment.

### Week 35 · Capstone Synthesis Project
1. **Frame:** Choose a capstone question that allows meaningful connection across at least two major Grade 9 English domains.
2. **Gather:** Select evidence from literature, research, writing, speaking, or portfolio artifacts that genuinely contributes to the synthesis.
3. **Synthesize:** Build a claim that connects evidence across domains rather than presenting separate summaries.
4. **Test:** Identify one contradiction, limitation, or piece of evidence that complicates the capstone claim and revise accordingly.
5. **Present:** Complete a capstone statement explaining claim, strongest evidence, synthesis move, limitation, and what the project demonstrates about readiness.

### Week 36 · Cumulative Examination & Oral Defense
1. **Retrieve:** Use portfolio and prior assessment evidence to identify the highest-value skills to retrieve before the cumulative examination.
2. **Demonstrate:** Complete the final under existing assessment rules without changing the distinction between completion and mastery.
3. **Correct:** Analyze errors by skill or reasoning pattern and write a correction rationale for significant misunderstandings.
4. **Prepare Defense:** Choose portfolio evidence for claims about growth, independence, revision, and readiness; prepare for follow-up questions.
5. **Defend:** Complete the oral defense with claim, evidence, reflection, correction, limitation, and one concrete goal for English 10.

## Full-year retrieval and transfer

The Unit 12 evidence layer uses a 15-day map across Weeks 34–36. Retrieval deliberately returns to:
- full-year reading, writing, research, rhetoric, media, speaking, publication, and revision habits;
- earlier portfolio artifacts;
- prior assessments and corrections;
- long-form or capstone evidence;
- a cumulative final, oral defense, and next-stage literacy plan.

Each prompt requires students to demonstrate growth with evidence rather than rely on completion, self-description, or score alone.

## Reading/source custody

Required texts and evidence remain in the existing unit/week pages, reading dossier, student portfolio, approved authentic works, research sources, capstone materials, and assessment records. Exact source, author/creator, edition, translator, publication date, provenance, and citation records remain required where applicable.

## Mastery boundary

The Unit 12 evidence layer:
- writes no learner data;
- changes no storage keys;
- changes no final-exam answers or thresholds;
- changes no assessment authority;
- does not convert completion, portfolio presence, or oral participation into mastery;
- adds curation, revision, synthesis, cumulative retrieval, corrections, oral defense, readiness evaluation, transfer planning, and exit-audit prompts only.

## Release criterion

Unit 12 is ready when:
1. all 15 instructional days receive the grade-specific evidence layer;
2. the grade-level scaffold fade is preserved through final defense;
3. portfolio and capstone claims are tied to concrete evidence;
4. corrections remain separate from simple completion;
5. no duplicate generic prompts appear;
6. JavaScript parses cleanly;
7. storage/assessment authority remains unchanged;
8. the unit landing page communicates the final learning progression;
9. the learner exits with a documented next-stage literacy plan.
