Local research simulation · Rivermark Learning Annex

Common case file

Decision: Rivermark must decide whether to convert an unused municipal annex into an evening study center for students ages 13–18.

Background brief: The annex has four rooms, basic internet wiring, and a bus stop nearby. Renovation, supervision, accessibility, transportation, operating cost, demand, and neighborhood impact all require investigation. No conclusion is supplied.

Evidence boundary: Rivermark and every document in this packet are course-created simulations. Students may cite them for this instructional case but must never present them as evidence about a real community.

Week 21 evidence packet

Read before drawing conclusions

Additional record E · Facilities estimate

Prepared by licensed municipal engineer Tomas Adey, 20 March 2026. Estimates $186,000 for code, accessibility, lighting, and restroom work, with a ±20% early-design range. Staffing and yearly operation are excluded.

Additional record F · Student listening session

Facilitator notes from 22 students recruited through two school clubs. Participants frequently requested quiet space, device charging, and later bus service. The notes capture priorities of attendees but are not representative of every student.

Citation record

Each entry must identify creator, title or document description, organization, date, document type, and local course packet. When data are incomplete, say so rather than inventing details.

Day 101 · Required lesson

Build a synthesis matrix

Organize by ideas, not one paragraph per source.

Direct instruction and model

Rows represent subquestions; columns represent sources. Record agreement, tension, distinct contribution, and limitation. Synthesis begins where sources meet.

Independent evidence

Complete matrix rows for demand, access, cost, neighborhood impact, and missing evidence. Draft one cross-source inference.

Research integrity check

  • What is known?
  • What is inferred?
  • What remains unknown?
  • Which source supports the statement?
  • What limitation must be disclosed?

Day 102 · Required lesson

Quote, paraphrase, attribute

Preserve meaning and make source ownership visible.

Direct instruction and model

Quote only wording whose exact form matters. A paraphrase changes both wording and structure without changing meaning. Both require attribution and citation.

Independent evidence

Write one accurate quotation integration and one genuine paraphrase from the packet. Highlight attribution, evidence, and your explanation.

Research integrity check

  • What is known?
  • What is inferred?
  • What remains unknown?
  • Which source supports the statement?
  • What limitation must be disclosed?

Day 103 · Required lesson

Reason through conflict

Use warrants, counterevidence, and qualification.

Direct instruction and model

A warrant explains why evidence supports a claim. Responsible writers narrow conclusions when the evidence is partial or conflicting.

Independent evidence

Draft a claim about feasibility, a warrant, the strongest counterevidence, and a qualified revised claim.

Research integrity check

  • What is known?
  • What is inferred?
  • What remains unknown?
  • Which source supports the statement?
  • What limitation must be disclosed?

Day 104 · Required lesson

Draft and document

Compose a report whose structure follows the inquiry.

Direct instruction and model

Use an introduction with question and provisional thesis; sections organized by subquestion; source synthesis; limitations; conclusion; and annotated bibliography.

Independent evidence

Draft 900–1,200 words. Annotate each bibliography entry with usefulness, limitation, and the claim it can responsibly support.

Research integrity check

  • What is known?
  • What is inferred?
  • What remains unknown?
  • Which source supports the statement?
  • What limitation must be disclosed?

Day 105 · Required lesson

Audit and defend

Check accuracy before style and completion.

Direct instruction and model

Verify every factual statement against the packet, every borrowed idea against attribution, and every conclusion against the stated limitations.

Independent evidence

Submit report, synthesis matrix, citation audit, revision note, and a 150–200 word defense explaining the strongest conclusion and most important uncertainty.

Research integrity check

  • What is known?
  • What is inferred?
  • What remains unknown?
  • Which source supports the statement?
  • What limitation must be disclosed?
