Weekly Targets

By the end of this week, the student can…

  • Read the assigned text family closely and cite precise evidence.
  • Answer the essential question with a defensible claim and reasoning.
  • Apply numbers, units, captions, and consistent terminology in original writing.
  • Use the vocabulary accurately: visual rhetoric, hierarchy, caption, alt text, accessibility, attribution.
  • Complete and preserve: Final explanatory article and infographic.

Materials

  • Reading notebook or annotation copy

  • Dictionary and vocabulary record

Five-Day Learning Cycle

One coherent week, not five disconnected worksheets.

Day 1

Read & Investigate

Preview the essential question. Read and annotate: Charts, captions, infographics, diagrams, and accessible design examples. Record three observations, two questions, and one possible pattern.

  • Read or review
  • Make evidence visible
  • Save one artifact
Day 2

Analyze & Discuss

Use textual evidence to answer the week’s question: How should words and visuals divide the work of explanation? Compare interpretations and identify the strongest warrant.

  • Read or review
  • Make evidence visible
  • Save one artifact
Day 3

Language Laboratory

Study numbers, units, captions, and consistent terminology. Complete a correction set, then revise five sentences from current writing. Practice vocabulary: visual rhetoric, hierarchy, caption, alt text, accessibility, attribution.

  • Read or review
  • Make evidence visible
  • Save one artifact
Day 4

Compose & Create

Integrate verified data, captions, alt text, visual hierarchy, and source credit. Draft in PROSE, preserving a clear planning note and at least one meaningful revision.

  • Read or review
  • Make evidence visible
  • Save one artifact
Day 5

Assess & Reflect

Submit or present: Final explanatory article and infographic. Complete the exit reflection and mark the week complete only after evidence is saved.

  • Read or review
  • Make evidence visible
  • Save one artifact
