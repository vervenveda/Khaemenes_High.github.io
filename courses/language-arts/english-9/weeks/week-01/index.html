Weekly Targets

By the end of this week, the student can…

  • Read a diagnostic passage closely and cite precise evidence for an interpretation.
  • Identify current reading and writing habits through a literacy inventory and reflection.
  • Apply sentence boundaries and paragraph unity in original writing.
  • Use accurately: annotation, inference, thesis, evidence, revision.
  • Draft and revise a baseline essay while preserving visible revision evidence.
  • Set one measurable English I growth goal for the next instructional cycle.

Materials

  • Reading notebook or annotation copy
  • PROSE Editorial Suite
  • Optional PROSE Handwriting Lab
  • Dictionary and vocabulary record
  • Portfolio folder or local export

Five-Day Diagnostic Cycle

Baseline evidence before judgment.

Day 1

Read & Investigate

Complete the literacy inventory, then read and annotate the diagnostic passage. Record three observations, two questions, and one emerging pattern.

  • Mark one inference and its evidence.
  • Preserve the literacy inventory.
Day 2

Analyze & Discuss

Use textual evidence to answer what your present reading and writing habits reveal. Compare two interpretations and decide which is better supported.

Day 3

Language Laboratory

Study sentence boundaries and paragraph unity. Correct a short error set, then revise five sentences from your own writing.

Day 4

Compose in PROSE

Draft the controlled baseline essay in PROSE. Preserve planning, first draft, and at least one meaningful revision.

Day 5

Assess, Mentor & Reflect

Use the essay, notes, and literacy inventory to identify one strength, one recurring challenge, and one measurable growth goal. Consult Eiren after forming your own evidence-based judgment.
