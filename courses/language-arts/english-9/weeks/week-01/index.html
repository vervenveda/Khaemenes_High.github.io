Weekly Targets

By the end of this week, the student can…

  • Read a diagnostic passage closely and cite precise evidence for an interpretation.
  • Identify current reading and writing habits through a literacy inventory and reflection.
  • Apply sentence boundaries and paragraph unity in original writing.
  • Use the vocabulary accurately: annotation, inference, thesis, evidence, revision.
  • Draft and revise a baseline essay while preserving visible revision evidence.
  • Set one measurable English I growth goal for the next instructional cycle.

Materials

  • Reading notebook or annotation copy
  • PROSE Editorial Suite drafting workspace
  • Optional PROSE Handwriting Lab
  • Dictionary and vocabulary record
  • Portfolio folder or local export

Five-Day Learning Cycle

One coherent diagnostic week.

Week 1 establishes a baseline for reading, writing, language, revision, and reflection so later growth can be measured against real evidence.

Day 1

Read & Investigate

Complete the literacy inventory, then read and annotate the diagnostic passage. Record three observations, two questions, and one emerging pattern about how meaning is built.

  • Annotate directly or in a notebook
  • Mark one inference and the evidence behind it
  • Preserve the literacy inventory
Day 2

Analyze & Discuss

Use textual evidence to answer: What do my present reading and writing habits reveal? Compare two possible interpretations and decide which is better supported.

  • Write a claim
  • Attach exact evidence
  • Explain the reasoning
Day 3

Language Laboratory

Study sentence boundaries and paragraph unity. Correct a short error set, then revise five sentences from your own baseline writing. Practice annotation, inference, thesis, evidence, and revision in context.

  • Correct and explain
  • Revise your own sentences
  • Optional handwriting practice in PROSE Handwriting Lab
Day 4

Compose in PROSE

Draft the controlled baseline essay in the PROSE Editorial Suite. Preserve a planning note, first-draft state, and at least one meaningful revision so growth is visible rather than merely claimed.

  • Draft independently first
  • Revise for clarity and unity
  • Export or preserve the revised artifact
Day 5

Assess, Mentor & Reflect

Use the revised essay, reading notes, and literacy inventory to identify one strength, one recurring challenge, and one measurable growth goal. Consult Eiren if you need help interpreting the evidence or choosing a next step.

  • Self-assess with evidence
  • Set one measurable goal
  • Preserve the final diagnostic artifact

Language Arts Mentor

Work with Eiren after you have evidence.

Eiren can help you examine a difficult passage, test an inference, identify a revision priority, or turn your Week 1 evidence into a realistic growth goal. Bring the passage, claim, draft, or reflection to the conversation.

  • Reading conference: “Here is my inference and the evidence I used. What should I re-check?”
  • Writing conference: “Here is my draft and one revision I made. What pattern should I investigate next?”
  • Goal conference: “Here is my Week 1 evidence. Help me turn it into a measurable goal.”
