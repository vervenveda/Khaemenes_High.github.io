Comparative Myth Synthesis

How can two traditions illuminate one another without being flattened into sameness?

Reading: Paired authentic passages with named editions, translation limits, and cultural context.
Writing: Develop, revise, and defend a comparative thesis organized by meaningful criteria.

Weekly Targets

By the end of this week, the student can…

  • Read the assigned text family closely and cite precise evidence.
  • Answer the essential question with a defensible claim and reasoning.
  • Apply parallel structure and comparison signals in original writing.
  • Use the vocabulary accurately: synthesis, transformation, allusion, context, nuance, universality.
  • Complete and preserve: Comparative literary essay and visual archetype map.

Materials

Five-Day Learning Cycle

One coherent week, not five disconnected worksheets.

Day 1

Read & Investigate

Preview the essential question. Read and annotate: Paired myths with attention to historical and cultural context. Record three observations, two questions, and one possible pattern.

  • Read or review
  • Make evidence visible
  • Save one artifact
Day 2

Analyze & Discuss

Use textual evidence to answer the week’s question: How can two traditions illuminate one another without being flattened into sameness? Compare interpretations and identify the strongest warrant.

  • Read or review
  • Make evidence visible
  • Save one artifact
Day 3

Language Laboratory

Study parallel structure and comparison signals. Complete a correction set, then revise five sentences from current writing. Practice vocabulary: synthesis, transformation, allusion, context, nuance, universality.

  • Read or review
  • Make evidence visible
  • Save one artifact
Day 4

Compose & Create

Write a comparative thesis and organize evidence by meaningful criteria. Draft in PROSE, preserving a clear planning note and at least one meaningful revision.

  • Read or review
  • Make evidence visible
  • Save one artifact
Day 5

Assess & Reflect

Submit or present: Comparative literary essay and visual archetype map. Complete the exit reflection and mark the week complete only after evidence is saved.

  • Make evidence visible
