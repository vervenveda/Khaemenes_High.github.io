Unseen transfer packet · The open workshop notice

New passage. “Beginning Monday, the school workshop will open after regular classes for independent project time. Students should arrive prepared, use tools responsibly, and make good choices about transportation. Space may be limited. Ask a supervisor if you have questions.”

Related planning note. The draft schedule lists Monday and Wednesday, 4:00–6:00 p.m., with 18 workstations and two supervisors. It also states that students must complete a safety orientation before using powered equipment. Transportation arrangements are marked “family responsibility; late bus not confirmed.”

Transfer task. Without copying an earlier model, analyze the public notice for audience, purpose, evidence, missing information, tone, and accessibility. Use the planning note to distinguish what can be added as verified information from what must remain unresolved.

Worked method, not answer. First identify the decision a reader must make. Then locate exact evidence, test whether the evidence supports the claim, note uncertainty, and revise for the actual audience. Do not invent a late bus, open additional days, or guarantee space merely because those additions would make the notice more convenient.

Defense preparation. Be ready to explain one revision choice, one limitation you preserved, and one alternative interpretation a careful reader could make.

Assessment boundary. This packet is practice for transfer and oral defense. The standalone cumulative final remains the authoritative supervised assessment when an evaluator uses it under the course rules.

Evidence-Based English Architecture · Unit 12

Cumulative Examination & Oral Defense · Retrieval & Transfer

Grade 9 closes with visible evidence of growth: students curate work, revise major pieces, synthesize learning across the year, complete the cumulative assessment, and defend their choices orally.

Day 1 · Retrieve

Use portfolio and prior assessment evidence to identify the highest-value skills to retrieve before the cumulative examination.

Day 2 · Demonstrate

Complete the final under existing assessment rules without changing the distinction between completion and mastery.

Day 3 · Correct

Analyze errors by skill or reasoning pattern and write a correction rationale for significant misunderstandings.

Day 4 · Prepare Defense

Choose portfolio evidence for claims about growth, independence, revision, and readiness; prepare for follow-up questions.

Day 5 · Defend

Complete the oral defense with claim, evidence, reflection, correction, limitation, and one concrete goal for English 10.

Mastery boundary: These prompts strengthen portfolio curation, capstone synthesis, cumulative transfer, corrections, oral defense, readiness planning, and reflection only. They do not change assessment answers, thresholds, learner-record authority, or completion rules.
