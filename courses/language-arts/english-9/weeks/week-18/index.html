COURSE SPEECH SEED
A school tool should not merely be impressive when demonstrated. It should remain understandable when the network is unavailable, usable when a learner needs more time, and honest about what it records. Reliability is not decoration. Accessibility is not an afterthought. Privacy is not a promise hidden at the bottom of a page. These are conditions of trust.

Day 86

Write for the Ear

Recognize differences between readable prose and listenable speech.

Learn and model

Mark the speech seed for breath groups, signposts, sentence length and information density. Read it aloud once and record where meaning is lost.

Independent evidence

Revise one dense paragraph for one-hearing comprehension.

Day 87

Pattern With Purpose

Use parallelism and repetition to organize meaning rather than imitate importance.

Learn and model

Analyze the repeated “is not” structure. Test a version with random repetition and explain why pattern must carry a real relationship.

Independent evidence

Create one three-part parallel sequence and justify its order.

Day 88

Evidence in Oral Form

Integrate evidence so a listener can identify source, number, meaning and limitation.

Learn and model

Transform a small local data table into two spoken sentences. State the comparison base and avoid reading a citation string aloud.

Independent evidence

Write and annotate a 90-second evidence section.

Day 89

Delivery Is Interpretation

Plan pace, pause, emphasis, gesture or an approved accessible equivalent.

Learn and model
