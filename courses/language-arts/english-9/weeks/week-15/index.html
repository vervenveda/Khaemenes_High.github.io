Unit 05 · Week 15

Seminar & Dramatic Interpretation

How can interpretation remain creative while accountable to evidence?

Reading: Key scenes, critical questions, and defensible alternate stagings.
Writing, Discussion & Performance: Revise the drama analysis, participate in an evidence-based seminar, stage a scene, and defend the interpretation.

Weekly Targets

By the end of this week, the student can…

  • Develop a defensible dramatic interpretation and distinguish it from unsupported invention.
  • Compare a counterreading fairly and explain what evidence strengthens or weakens each interpretation.
  • Participate in seminar by citing evidence, asking genuine questions, building on ideas, challenging reasoning respectfully, and revising a position when warranted.
  • Use formal register without confusing formality with inflated vocabulary or artificial speech.
  • Translate an interpretive claim into playable blocking, voice, movement, pause, and spatial choices.
  • Use accurately: interpretation, staging, blocking, seminar, counterreading, evidence.
  • Complete the final drama essay, seminar evidence, staged interpretation, reflection, and defined Unit 5 scored evidence at 80% or higher.

Five-Day Interpretation & Seminar Laboratory

Question → counterread → discuss → stage → defend.

Day 1 · Interpretive Question Workshop

Questions That Can Sustain Inquiry

Convert textual tensions into questions with multiple evidence-based answers, attach passages, write a provisional answer, and identify evidence that could change it.

Day 2 · Counterreading Laboratory

More Than One Reading Can Be Defensible

State Interpretation A and Counterreading B fairly, identify shared evidence both must explain, compare inference load, and revise the essay to address the strongest counterreading.

Day 3 · Seminar & Language Lab

Listen to Think, Not Merely to Reply

Practice evidence-based discussion moves, track an idea another participant genuinely changed or complicated, revise vague/inflated statements into precise formal register, and meet the 80% seminar/language check.

Day 4 · Staging Interpretation Studio

Make the Reading Visible

Create a staging claim and blocking map, assign movement/stillness/proximity/pause/pace/vocal emphasis intentionally, connect performance choices to the script, test alternate versions, and revise from observer feedback.

Day 5 · Dramatic Colloquium & Unit Defense

Interpret, Stage, Defend, Revise

Present the staged interpretation and final analysis, defend a creative choice with precise evidence, answer a counterreading fairly, explain one substantive revision, and enter corrective learning/reassessment if any defined criterion remains below 80%.
