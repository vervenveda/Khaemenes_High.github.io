Unit 03 Narrative Demonstration

Today’s Question: Can I make purposeful narrative choices and explain what they accomplish?

Assessment Package
  1. Polished original short story: approximately 900–1,400 words.
  2. Craft map: annotate at least six deliberate choices across the story.
  3. Craft commentary: 250–350 words explaining how at least three choices shape the reader’s experience.
  4. Revision evidence: Draft 1 + revision note + final.
  5. Self-score: use the rubric before submitting.
Craft Map Categories

Your six annotations may include point of view, characterization, motivation, setting, internal/external conflict, scene/summary, pacing, dialogue/subtext, sensory detail, flashback/foreshadowing, suspense/irony, sentence rhythm, or another defensible craft decision. Do not force six different devices. Annotate choices that actually matter.

Craft Commentary Prompt

How did your most important narrative choices help the story become purposeful rather than merely eventful? Discuss at least three choices, quote or point to specific moments in your own story, and explain one meaningful revision between Draft 1 and final.

Final Read

Read the story aloud or use silent line-by-line tracking. Check speaker changes, dialogue punctuation, pronoun consistency, accidental tense shifts, repeated words, missing words, sentence boundaries, and paragraphing.

Mastery & Corrective Learning

8/10 = mastery. Below 8/10: identify the lowest rubric row, revise that exact dimension, explain the change, and resubmit. Do not start over unless the writer chooses to; correction targets the weakest craft decision while preserving the same standard.
