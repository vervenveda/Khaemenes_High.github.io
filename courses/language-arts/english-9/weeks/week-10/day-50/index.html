Week 10 Poetry Demonstration

Today’s Question: Can I explain how poetic language creates meaning and make those choices deliberately in my own writing?

Part A · Unfamiliar Poem

Read the original transfer poem below independently. Annotate before drafting your claim.

Inventory at Dusk

One bent spoon in the sink. Two envelopes unopened. The basil on the sill leans hard toward the glass. Downstairs, a bicycle bell rings once, then disappears. I write milk on the list, then cross it out. Outside, the first streetlight clicks awake in an empty car.

Analysis Prompt · 300–450 Words

Develop a defensible interpretation of the speaker’s experience in Inventory at Dusk. Analyze how at least two precise diction or imagery choices and one structural or figurative feature create tone and meaning. Distinguish textual evidence from inference. Do not assume the speaker is the poet.

Part B · Original Poetry Portfolio

Submit both Day 49 poems in revised form. For each, preserve or describe one meaningful revision. You may not receive extra points for using a tool, a particular form, rhyme, or a certain number of literary devices.

Part C · Craft Note · 120–180 Words

Choose one poem and explain three deliberate choices: one in image/diction, one in speaker/tone or perspective, and one in structure/line/rhythm. Name one choice you removed or resisted because it did not serve the poem.
