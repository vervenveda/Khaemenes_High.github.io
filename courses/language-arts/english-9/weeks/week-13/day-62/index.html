Course-created scene · Mara & Elias

Course-created practice text

Evidence available to every learner

MARA: You moved the chair.
ELIAS: It was blocking the door.
[Mara sets a sealed envelope where the chair had been.]
MARA: Nothing was blocking the door yesterday.
ELIAS: Yesterday no one was leaving.
[A train sounds in the distance. Elias reaches for the envelope, then stops.]
MARA: You said the station was closed.
ELIAS: I said the last train had gone.
[Mara looks toward the audience rather than the window.]
MARA: Those are not the same sentence.

This original instructional text may be annotated, printed, performed and revised inside the course.

Day 62Conflict, Objective, and Obstacle

Conflict, Objective, and Obstacle

Infer what each character wants and what prevents it.

Learning target

Infer what each character wants and what prevents it.

Learn and model

Build an objective–obstacle–tactic chart. For every inference, attach a word, action, pause, entrance, or exit from the scene. Compare two plausible readings and decide which has stronger evidence.

Independent evidence

Write a claim explaining how one character’s tactic changes during the scene.

Student response · handwriting or typing

Evidence check

  • Claim answers today’s target
  • Specific words or stage actions are cited
  • Reasoning explains how evidence supports the claim
  • A limitation or alternate reading is considered

Language focus: verbals and concise stage directions.
