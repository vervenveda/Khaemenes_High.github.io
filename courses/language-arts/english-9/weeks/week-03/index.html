Revision, Style & Academic Integrity

What changes make writing clearer, stronger, and more trustworthy?

Reading: Before-and-after drafts, paraphrases, summaries, quotations, and source notes.
Writing: Revise for focus, coherence, sentence control, attribution, citation, and intellectual honesty.

Weekly Targets

By the end of this week, the student can…

  • Distinguish revision from editing and explain why substantial revision improves meaning.
  • Paraphrase and summarize source material without distorting the source or disguising borrowed ideas.
  • Integrate quotations and attribution so a reader can tell which ideas belong to the writer and which come from a source.
  • Repair fragments, run-ons, and comma splices and use coordination deliberately.
  • Use accurately: paraphrase, summary, plagiarism, attribution, coherence, syntax.
  • Complete the Unit 1 mastery task and conventions/integrity check at 80% or higher before Unit 2 progression.

Five-Day Learning Cycle

Revision is thinking again—not merely fixing commas.

Day 1 · Revision Laboratory

Diagnose Before You Rewrite

Compare an earlier draft and revision. Identify changes to claim, organization, evidence, explanation, word choice, and sentence structure, then explain which change most improves meaning.

Day 2 · Integrity Laboratory

Quote, Paraphrase, Summarize

Create a faithful quotation, paraphrase, and summary from one short source passage. Compare each against the original for accuracy, independence of wording, and attribution.

Day 3 · Language Laboratory

Control the Sentence

Study fragments, run-ons, comma splices, and coordination as meaning problems. Diagnose ten sentences, repair them, then revise five sentences from your own writing. Reteach and retry scored practice below 80%.

Day 4 · Editorial Studio

Build a Trustworthy Revision

Revise a Week 1 or Week 2 composition in PROSE using passes for purpose, organization, evidence/reasoning, attribution, sentence style, and conventions. Preserve the earlier draft.

Day 5 · Unit 1 Mastery

Demonstrate, Review, Retry

Complete the Unit 1 mastery task: evidence-based analytical paragraph, revised diagnostic writing, and conventions/integrity check. Below 80%, diagnose the missed competency, complete corrective learning, and reassess with corrected or meaningfully changed evidence.
