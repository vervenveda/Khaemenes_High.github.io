Weekly Targets

By the end of this week, the student can…

  • Distinguish revision from editing and explain why substantial revision improves meaning.
  • Paraphrase and summarize source material without distorting the source or disguising borrowed ideas.
  • Integrate quotations and attribution so a reader can distinguish writer language from source language.
  • Repair fragments, run-ons, and comma splices and use coordination deliberately.
  • Use accurately: paraphrase, summary, plagiarism, attribution, coherence, syntax.
  • Assemble Unit 1 authoritative evidence: analytical paragraph, revised diagnostic essay, language/integrity check, and fresh reading transfer.
  • Reach at least 80% on every required scored component and at least 80% on the weighted Unit 1 result before Unit 2 progression.

Five-Day Learning Cycle

Revision is thinking again—not merely fixing commas.

Day 1 · Revision Laboratory

Diagnose Before You Rewrite

Compare an earlier draft and revision. Identify changes to claim, organization, evidence, explanation, word choice, and sentence structure, then explain which change most improves meaning.

Day 2 · Integrity Laboratory

Quote, Paraphrase, Summarize

Create a faithful quotation, paraphrase, and summary from one short source passage. Compare each against the original for accuracy, independence of wording, and attribution.

Day 3 · Language Laboratory

Control the Sentence

Study fragments, run-ons, comma splices, and coordination as meaning problems. Diagnose ten sentences, repair them, then revise five sentences from your own writing. Scored practice below 80% triggers targeted reteaching and a fresh retry.

Day 4 · Editorial Studio

Build a Trustworthy Revision

Revise a Week 1 or Week 2 composition in PROSE using passes for purpose, organization, evidence/reasoning, attribution, sentence style, and conventions. Preserve the earlier draft and record consequential changes.

Day 5 · Unit 1 Mastery Assembly

Demonstrate, Review, Retry

Submit the four required components defined by the Unit 1 Mastery Evidence Contract. The fresh reading-transfer task must use unrehearsed material. Below 80% on any required component, diagnose that competency, complete corrective learning, and reassess with corrected or meaningfully changed evidence.
