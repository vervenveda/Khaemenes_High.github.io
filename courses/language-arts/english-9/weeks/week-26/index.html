Weekly Targets

By the end of this week, the student can…

  • Read the assigned text family closely and cite precise evidence.
  • Answer the essential question with a defensible claim and reasoning.
  • Apply sentence combining for analytical flow in original writing.
  • Use the vocabulary accurately: arc, motif, foil, theme, tension, complexity.
  • Complete and preserve: Pattern tracker and analytical notebook entry.

Five-Day Learning Cycle

One coherent week, not five disconnected worksheets.

Day 1

Read & Investigate

Preview the essential question. Read and annotate: Middle sections of the selected novel and related context. Record three observations, two questions, and one possible pattern.

  • Read or review
  • Make evidence visible
  • Save one artifact
Day 2

Analyze & Discuss

Use textual evidence to answer the week’s question: How do repeated details gather meaning across a novel? Compare interpretations and identify the strongest warrant.

  • Read or review
  • Make evidence visible
  • Save one artifact
Day 3

Language Laboratory

Study sentence combining for analytical flow. Complete a correction set, then revise five sentences from current writing. Practice vocabulary: arc, motif, foil, theme, tension, complexity.

  • Read or review
  • Make evidence visible
  • Save one artifact
Day 4

Compose & Create

Track changes, patterns, contradictions, and competing interpretations. Draft in PROSE, preserving a clear planning note and at least one meaningful revision.

  • Read or review
  • Make evidence visible
  • Save one artifact
Day 5

Assess & Reflect

Submit or present: Pattern tracker and analytical notebook entry. Complete the exit reflection and mark the week complete only after evidence is saved.

  • Make evidence visible
