Course-created source

Shared evidence text

PROPOSAL — EXTEND THE LIBRARY HOUR
During a four-week trial, the library remained open one additional hour on Tuesdays. Entry counts averaged 18 students during that hour, compared with 11 during the final regular hour. A voluntary exit card received 22 responses; 17 respondents said the later hour helped them finish schoolwork. The proposal recommends a twelve-week extension while collecting attendance, staffing cost, transportation, and student-access data. The trial does not establish that every student benefited or that the schedule caused the higher entry count.

This original instructional source may be printed, annotated, quoted and adapted for course work.

Day 82

Test Evidence Quality

Evaluate relevance, sufficiency, representativeness and credibility.

Learn and model

Audit sample size, voluntary response, comparison period, missing cost data and causal limits without dismissing all evidence.

Independent evidence

Rate each evidence item and justify the rating in two sentences.

Day 83

Name the Error Precisely

Identify reasoning errors without using fallacy labels as insults.

Learn and model

Distinguish overgeneralization, false dilemma, circular reasoning, irrelevant appeal, post hoc reasoning and straw person. Repair a flawed argument after naming its exact broken step.

Independent evidence

Correct six flawed claims and explain the repaired logic.

Day 84

Counterclaim as Quality Control

Represent the strongest opposing concern fairly.

Learn and model

Evidence Audit
