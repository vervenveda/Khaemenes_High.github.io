# English 9 · Unit 08 Evidence Alignment
## Informational & Technical Communication

**Upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Unit 08 propagation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade-level rhythm

**Define Need → Organize → Test Usability → Integrate Visuals → Audit Clarity**

Grade 9 treats informational writing as a design problem: learners choose structures, test procedures, interpret data, and divide explanatory work between words and visuals.

## Three-week architecture

### Week 22 · Structures of Explanation
1. **Define Need:** Identify what the reader must understand and what prior knowledge can reasonably be assumed.
2. **Organize:** Choose the explanatory structure—definition, classification, sequence, comparison, cause/effect, or problem/solution—that best matches the idea.
3. **Compare:** Test two possible structures for the same content. Which makes relationships easier to understand, and why?
4. **Draft:** Write a short explanation using explicit transitions and precise terms. Mark where each section serves the reader’s understanding.
5. **Audit Clarity:** Ask a reader to identify the main idea, structure, and one confusing point. Revise based on evidence from that test.

### Week 23 · Technical Reading & Procedural Writing
1. **Define Need:** State the task, intended user, required materials, safety constraints, and successful outcome before writing the procedure.
2. **Sequence:** Break the process into actions that are observable, ordered, and reproducible. Remove hidden assumptions.
3. **Test Usability:** Have another person follow the procedure exactly as written. Record where they hesitate, misinterpret, or need missing information.
4. **Revise:** Improve wording, order, warnings, definitions, or visuals based on the usability evidence rather than preference.
5. **Audit Clarity:** Document the procedure’s strongest step, most important revision, remaining risk, and evidence that the process is reproducible.

### Week 24 · Data, Visuals & Accessible Information
1. **Interpret:** Read a chart, diagram, table, or infographic and state the claim the visual actually supports rather than the impression it creates.
2. **Integrate:** Decide what information belongs in prose and what belongs in a visual. Avoid duplicating the same content without purpose.
3. **Test:** Check titles, labels, scales, captions, alt text, color dependence, and whether the visual can be understood without guessing.
4. **Revise:** Redesign one visual or caption to improve accuracy, accessibility, or reader comprehension.
5. **Audit Clarity:** Submit an information-design rationale explaining the visual choice, supported claim, accessibility features, and one limitation.

## Spacing and retrieval

The Unit 08 evidence layer uses a 15-day map across Weeks 22–24. Retrieval deliberately returns to:
- prior-unit evidence, synthesis, comparison, source evaluation, rhetoric, and qualification habits;
- prior-day concepts;
- approximately one-week-delayed concepts;
- earlier Unit 08 methods;
- a cumulative usability, adaptation, performance, multimedia, global-issue, or representation audit on the final instructional day.

Each prompt is tied to the actual intellectual work of the day rather than a generic recall routine.

## Reading/source custody

Required reading remains in the existing unit/week pages, reading dossier, locally embedded Academy texts, approved authentic works, and approved multimedia sources. Exact source, author/creator, publication date, edition, translator, cultural/religious context, medium, platform, and provenance remain required where applicable.

## Mastery boundary

The Unit 08 evidence layer:
- writes no learner data;
- changes no storage keys;
- changes no quiz answers or thresholds;
- changes no assessment authority;
- does not convert completion into mastery;
- adds retrieval, explanation, usability testing, adaptation comparison, cultural/contextual analysis, performance, multimedia interpretation, accessibility review, and exit-audit prompts only.

## Release criterion

Unit 08 is ready when:
1. all 15 instructional days receive the grade-specific evidence layer;
2. the grade-level scaffold fade is preserved;
3. authentic-source, cultural, translation, and multimedia custody remain visible;
4. no duplicate generic prompts appear;
5. JavaScript parses cleanly;
6. storage/assessment authority remains unchanged;
7. the unit landing page communicates the learning progression.
