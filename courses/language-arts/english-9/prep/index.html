English I Preparatory Refresher

This bridge rebuilds the foundations English I assumes. It is not a second English course and does not add unnecessary advanced work. Each week repairs one readiness domain and gradually combines the skills until the student can demonstrate independent readiness for official Unit 1.

Six-Week Sequence

One focused foundation at a time.

Prep Week 1

Read, Observe & Understand

Main idea, details, sequence, comparison, explicit information, inference, annotation, rereading, and the discipline of separating “the text says” from “I think.”

Prep Week 2

Vocabulary & Meaning in Context

Context clues, roots/affixes, morphology, denotation/connotation, tone clues, reference skills, and explaining why a meaning fits.

Prep Week 3

Sentence Foundations

Complete thoughts, fragments/run-ons, subjects/predicates, agreement, tense, pronouns, punctuation, capitalization, sentence combining, and editing for clarity.

Prep Week 4

Paragraphs & Evidence-Based Writing

Controlling idea, logical order, relevant evidence/examples, explanation, transitions, paragraph unity, short response structure, revision, and proofreading.

Prep Week 5

Evidence, Sources & Critical Thinking

Fact/inference/opinion, relevant evidence, author/source/purpose, basic credibility, corroboration, assumptions, alternative explanations, and conclusions proportional to evidence.

Prep Week 6

Integrated English I Readiness Lab

Fresh reading + vocabulary + language + writing + evidence/source judgment + critical-thinking tasks. Practice culminates in an independent prep mastery assessment rather than a completion check.

Mastery Exit

80% opens official Unit 1.

The exit assessment uses fresh material so students demonstrate transfer rather than memorizing practice. If the student is not yet at 80%, NAIB identifies the weak domain, assigns targeted corrective practice, and offers a fresh reassessment. Full six-week repetition is not automatic.

Progression: NAIB Readiness → Prep Weeks 1–6 → independent Prep Mastery ≥80% → official English I Unit 1 → Units 1–13 → 50-question capstone → separate final examination.

Design Boundary

Preparation supports the course; it does not dilute it.

The prep bridge teaches prerequisites. It does not preteach the official unit assessments, reveal answer keys, replace Unit 1 evidence, or lower the English I mastery standard. Students entering directly through NAIB readiness and students entering after prep meet the same official Unit 1 expectations.
