Text Selection Framework

Reading List

Whole works, world voices, informational depth, and student choice.

The curriculum specifies text families and skills rather than locking every learner into one commercial anthology. Teachers select lawful, accessible editions and preserve cultural and intellectual breadth.

Independent Reading

Choice with accountability.

Students complete at least four independent works across the year: one novel, one nonfiction work, one drama or poetry collection, and one student-choice work. Each receives a brief conference, written response, or creative demonstration.

Text Complexity

Choose works that offer meaningful challenge in language, structure, ideas, background knowledge, or length while remaining teachable and accessible.

Breadth

Include voices from multiple eras, regions, cultures, genres, social positions, and intellectual traditions. Avoid treating one tradition as the universal default.

Care

Preview mature content, historical prejudice, violence, trauma, or contested language. Provide context and reasonable alternate texts without lowering the skill goal.
