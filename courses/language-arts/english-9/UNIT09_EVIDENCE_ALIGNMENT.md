# English 9 · Unit 09 Evidence Alignment
## The Novel & Sustained Inquiry

**Upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Unit 09 propagation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade-level rhythm

**Track → Connect → Question → Interpret → Defend**

Grade 9 develops sustained attention across a full work by tracking patterns over time, testing interpretations against the whole text, and distinguishing contextual or critical lenses from the primary text itself.

## Three-week architecture

### Week 25 · Launching a Sustained Fiction Study
1. **Track:** Establish a reading log that records characters, setting shifts, unresolved questions, recurring details, and passages worth revisiting.
2. **Connect:** Link a new passage to an earlier detail or event. Explain whether the connection strengthens, complicates, or overturns your earlier understanding.
3. **Question:** Write one interpretive question that cannot be answered from a single chapter and identify what future evidence would help answer it.
4. **Monitor:** Check whether your reading pace and notes are sufficient to preserve continuity across the whole work. Adjust the plan if evidence is being lost.
5. **Defend:** State one provisional claim about the novel so far, support it with evidence from more than one section, and label what remains uncertain.

### Week 26 · Character Arcs, Motifs & Themes
1. **Track:** Trace one character choice, motif, image, or repeated phrase across multiple chapters rather than interpreting one isolated appearance.
2. **Connect:** Explain how a later event changes the meaning of an earlier detail or decision.
3. **Interpret:** Develop a thematic or character-arc claim using evidence from beginning, middle, and current reading.
4. **Test:** Identify one piece of evidence that resists your interpretation. Decide whether to revise, qualify, or reject the claim.
5. **Defend:** Write a sustained-reading claim that explains development over time and cites a pattern rather than a single moment.

### Week 27 · Critical Perspectives & Literary Analysis
1. **Question:** Choose a critical lens or contextual perspective and state what kind of question it helps you ask of the novel.
2. **Apply:** Use the lens on one passage, then return to the passage itself to verify that the interpretation remains textually grounded.
3. **Compare:** Compare the lens-based reading with a text-first reading. What does the lens reveal, and what might it obscure?
4. **Test:** Identify a passage that does not fit the critical interpretation neatly. Use it to qualify the argument.
5. **Defend:** Draft a developed literary analysis claim that integrates whole-text evidence, one critical perspective, and an explicit limitation.

## Spacing and retrieval

The Unit 09 evidence layer uses a 15-day map across Weeks 25–27. Retrieval deliberately returns to:
- prior-unit evidence, synthesis, source evaluation, contextualization, research, and qualification habits;
- prior-day concepts;
- approximately one-week-delayed concepts;
- earlier Unit 09 methods;
- a cumulative literary-analysis, research, synthesis, scholarly-argument, or limitation audit on the final instructional day.

Each prompt is tied to the actual intellectual work of the day rather than a generic recall routine.

## Reading/source custody

Required reading remains in the existing unit/week pages, reading dossier, locally embedded Academy texts, approved authentic works, and approved research sources. Exact source, author, publication date, edition, translator, database/archive location, research method, and provenance remain required where applicable.

## Mastery boundary

The Unit 09 evidence layer:
- writes no learner data;
- changes no storage keys;
- changes no quiz answers or thresholds;
- changes no assessment authority;
- does not convert completion into mastery;
- adds sustained-reading tracking, research design, source evaluation, lateral verification, synthesis, citation, scholarly integration, peer review, qualification, and exit-audit prompts only.

## Release criterion

Unit 09 is ready when:
1. all 15 instructional days receive the grade-specific evidence layer;
2. the grade-level scaffold fade is preserved;
3. primary-text, scholarly-source, and research custody remain visible;
4. no duplicate generic prompts appear;
5. JavaScript parses cleanly;
6. storage/assessment authority remains unchanged;
7. the unit landing page communicates the learning progression.
