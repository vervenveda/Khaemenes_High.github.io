# Protools to English 9 Audit

**Audit pass:** 4B source verification

**Repository:** `vervenveda/proresource_hub.github.io/Protools`

**Boundary:** Read-only review of public educational functionality. This report contains no private architecture, hidden locations, credentials, synchronization details, or mentor-chain internals.

## Executive finding

The directory contains 22 HTML interfaces. Three are immediately important to the English 9 template: PROSE Editorial Suite, Evidence & Citation Studio, and Daily Spark. Handwriting Lab is a strong optional accessibility and fluency support. The remaining tools have legitimate professional uses but should not become required Language Arts dependencies.

## Critical launch finding

`Protools/PROSE/Editorial Suite/editingsuite_index.html` currently requests `PROSE_editor_core.html`, but the inspected directory contains `editorial-suite_core.html`. The compatibility loader therefore cannot reliably launch the preserved editor from this revision. English 9 now uses the exact existing core file. ProResource itself was not changed.

The older `PROSE/editorial-gateway.html` also contains legacy filenames and should not be treated as a verified course launch path until its route table is repaired.

## Evidence & Citation Studio strengths

- It does not decide whether a claim is true.
- It preserves supporting, opposing, contextual, and insufficient evidence relationships.
- It records creator, publisher, date, access date, edition, language, jurisdiction, reliability notes, and source type.
- It can hash a selected local file without uploading or embedding the file.
- It audits unsupported claims, missing reasoning, incomplete source metadata, and orphaned evidence relationships.
- It exports evidence matrices, research reports, citation drafts, Markdown, provenance records, and workspace backups.
- Optional encryption is local; there is no password-recovery service.

Generated citations remain drafting aids. Laws, archives, datasets, institutional authors, audiovisual sources, translations, and incomplete records require manual verification.

## Daily Spark boundary

The transparent ranking logic is useful because it exposes urgency, value, due date, available time, and stated energy rather than presenting a mysterious recommendation. English 9 may use this for voluntary planning and task decomposition. Completion counts, XP, streaks, energy settings, and notification activity may not become grades or inferred learner traits.

## Template decision

The English 9 tool sequence will be:

1. discover a lawful source;
2. preserve source and evidence reasoning in Evidence & Citation Studio;
3. draft and revise in PROSE;
4. export a readable portfolio artifact;
5. retain a printable course-local fallback at every step.

No external tool may be the sole location of required instructions, evidence, grades, or learner records.
