# English 9 · Unit 05 Evidence Alignment
## Drama, Choice & Consequence

**Upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Unit 05 propagation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade-level rhythm

**Observe → Interpret → Weigh → Stage → Defend**

Grade 9 makes dramatic evidence visible by linking dialogue, stage direction, silence, blocking, motivation, and consequence before asking learners to defend an interpretation.

## Three-week architecture

### Week 13 · Reading Drama as Literature and Performance
1. **Observe:** Identify one moment where meaning depends on more than dialogue alone. Note the stage direction, pause, movement, silence, or spatial relationship that contributes to the scene.
2. **Interpret:** Explain how one performance choice could change the audience’s understanding of character, tension, or power while the words remain the same.
3. **Weigh:** Compare two plausible staging choices for the same moment. Which is better supported by the text, and what evidence keeps the alternative possible?
4. **Stage:** Design a brief blocking or delivery plan for one scene. Annotate where movement, pause, gesture, or tone carries meaning.
5. **Defend:** Make one claim about how the scene should be performed, cite dialogue and stage evidence, and acknowledge one reasonable alternative interpretation.

### Week 14 · Duty, Power and Consequence
1. **Observe:** Identify the competing duties, loyalties, rules, or values in a central conflict. Separate what each character wants from what each believes is required.
2. **Interpret:** Trace one decision to its immediate and later consequences. Explain which consequence the character could reasonably have predicted.
3. **Weigh:** Compare the strongest argument for two opposing choices. Avoid reducing either side to a simple villain/hero position.
4. **Transfer:** Write or discuss a decision memo for the character: choice, evidence, risk, competing duty, and likely consequence.
5. **Defend:** Defend a judgment about one character’s choice using textual evidence, while naming one circumstance that could make you revise the judgment.

### Week 15 · Seminar and Dramatic Interpretation
1. **Observe:** Select one unresolved interpretive question from the play and gather the strongest evidence on more than one side.
2. **Interpret:** Prepare a seminar claim that distinguishes textual fact, inference, and personal evaluation.
3. **Weigh:** Listen for a competing interpretation that uses valid evidence. Identify where it strengthens, complicates, or challenges your own.
4. **Stage:** Revise or rehearse a staged interpretation in response to evidence from discussion rather than preference alone.
5. **Defend:** Complete a dramatic interpretation defense: claim, strongest textual evidence, performance choice, counterreading, and one limitation.

## Spacing and retrieval

The Unit 05 evidence layer uses a 15-day map across Weeks 13–15. Retrieval deliberately returns to:
- prior-unit evidence, interpretation, structure, rhetoric, and qualification habits;
- prior-day concepts;
- approximately one-week-delayed concepts;
- earlier Unit 05 methods;
- a cumulative Unit 05 defense, deliberation, verification, or calibrated-judgment task on the final instructional day.

Each prompt is tied to the actual intellectual work of the day rather than a generic recall routine.

## Reading/source custody

Required reading remains in the existing unit/week pages, reading dossier, locally embedded Academy texts, and approved authentic works. Course-created practice texts remain clearly identified as instructional examples. Exact source, edition, author/speaker, publication date, historical context, data provenance, and translation records remain required where applicable.

## Mastery boundary

The Unit 05 evidence layer:
- writes no learner data;
- changes no storage keys;
- changes no quiz answers or thresholds;
- changes no assessment authority;
- does not convert completion into mastery;
- adds retrieval, transfer, fair representation, counterargument, verification, ethical evaluation, performance, deliberation, and exit-audit prompts only.

## Release criterion

Unit 05 is ready when:
1. all 15 instructional days receive the grade-specific evidence layer;
2. the grade-level scaffold fade is preserved;
3. authentic-source and data custody remain visible;
4. no duplicate generic prompts appear;
5. JavaScript parses cleanly;
6. storage/assessment authority remains unchanged;
7. the unit landing page communicates the learning progression.
