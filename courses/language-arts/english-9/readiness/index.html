NAIB English I Readiness Gateway

Readiness guides support; it does not label or punish a learner. NAIB records strand-level evidence and routes the learner either to Official Unit 1 or to conditional Unit 0 Foundations & Readiness.

Readiness ProfilePreview Unit 0

Canonical Route

Readiness Gateway → strand profile → advance OR Unit 0 → 80% + corrections → Official Unit 1.

Advance

Every prerequisite strand is ready

Students with at least 80% overall and at least 80% in every English I readiness strand enter Official Unit 1 without added workload.

Unit 0

Targeted Foundations & Readiness

If any prerequisite strand is below 80%, the student receives the six-lesson/six-week conditional bridge. Strong strands may use confirmation challenges while weak strands receive full refresher instruction.

Exit

80% + reasoning corrections

Unit 0 exits only after 80% mastery in every essential strand and required corrections. The learner then enters the same Official Unit 1 curriculum.

Readiness Strands

Seven English I prerequisites.

Reading Comprehension

Main idea, relevant detail, sequence, comparison, implication, and supported conclusion.

Vocabulary in Context

Context, morphology, tone, and sentence relationships.

Sentence & Grammar

Complete sentences, agreement, tense, pronouns, punctuation, capitalization, and readable syntax.

Paragraph & Writing

Controlling idea, organization, evidence/example, explanation, transitions, revision, and concise response.

Observation, Evidence & Inference

Separate what is observed from what is inferred and select evidence that genuinely supports a claim.

Source & Information Judgment

Source, purpose, relevance, credibility, corroboration, fact/opinion, and when verification is required.

Critical-Thinking Transfer

Explain reasoning, notice assumptions, consider alternatives, and calibrate conclusions.

NAIB Readiness Profile

Strand evidence—not only a total score.

This local profile models the academy record shape. The production assessment should populate it from stable-question-ID responses and attempt records rather than manual entry.

Not evaluated.
English I treats all seven readiness strands as essential prerequisites. Direct entry requires at least 80% overall and at least 80% in every strand; a high average cannot hide a foundational gap.

Four Separate NAIB Records

Never overwrite learning history.

Course Mastery

What the learner demonstrated during the completed course.

Retention

What remains fluent later.

Refresh

What now needs reinforcement.

Readiness

Whether the learner can successfully enter the next course.

A later readiness result must never erase or downgrade a prior completed-course mastery record.

Assessment Implementation Contract

The production test must be auditable and student-friendly.

Stable question IDs · attempt records · question navigator · Previous / Next · keyboard usability · responsive high-contrast layout · clear focus states · printability · no correct-answer disclosure before submission · strand reporting · reasoning corrections below mastery.
