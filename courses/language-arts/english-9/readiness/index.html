NAIB English I Readiness Gateway

This gateway does not label a student as “good” or “bad” at English. It identifies whether the foundations assumed by English I are already secure or whether six weeks of focused preparation would make the course more successful.

Placement Rule

Transparent evidence, not a hidden NAIB decision.

Ready

80%+ with foundations secure

NAIB produces the domain report and routes the learner directly into official Unit 1 and the standard 36-week curriculum.

Bridge

Below 80% or critical foundation gap

The learner receives a six-week preparatory bridge targeted to reading, language, writing, evidence, source judgment, and integrated readiness. After 80% prep mastery, Unit 1 opens.

Retry

Target the weakness

If prep mastery is not yet 80%, corrective work focuses on the weak domain followed by a fresh assessment. The student does not repeat six weeks simply to satisfy a timer.

Human-readable placement: NAIB must show the student/family the overall result, domain pattern, placement reason, and next step. A single opaque score is not enough.

Diagnostic Blueprint

Seven foundations sampled before placement.

The production assessment should use fresh, age-appropriate passages and items. This page establishes the authoritative placement domains and scoring contract.

Reading Comprehension

Main idea, relevant detail, sequence, comparison, implication, and conclusions supported by a passage.

Vocabulary in Context

Use context, morphology, tone, and sentence relationships rather than isolated memorization.

Sentence & Grammar Foundations

Complete sentences, agreement, tense, pronouns, punctuation, capitalization, and readable syntax.

Paragraph & Writing Foundations

Controlling idea, organization, evidence/example, explanation, transitions, revision, and concise written response.

Observation, Evidence & Inference

Distinguish what is explicitly observed from what is inferred; select evidence that genuinely supports a claim.

Source & Information Judgment

Author/source, purpose, relevance, basic credibility, corroboration, fact/opinion, and when verification is needed.

Critical-Thinking Transfer

Explain reasoning, notice assumptions, consider another plausible interpretation, and state conclusions proportionally.

Student Dignity & Access

The gateway measures readiness for this course—not intelligence or worth.

Provide normal accessibility supports and approved accommodations. Do not lower the reasoning construct because of accent, disability, cultural background, handwriting, speech style, or unfamiliarity with one narrow life experience. Writing mechanics should not erase demonstrated comprehension where mechanics are not the construct being measured.
