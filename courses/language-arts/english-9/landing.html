Pre-Algebra-Informed Architecture

A course portal, not a folder of pages.

English I uses the strongest structural ideas from the hardened Pre-Algebra course while preserving Language Arts pedagogy.

Portal

Students can enter the course, locate units and weeks, reach assessments, tools, portfolio records, mentor support, and the Beta path.

Mastery

Defined scored checks require at least 80%. Below mastery triggers targeted corrective learning and reassessment with fresh or meaningfully changed evidence.

Evidence

Progress comes from reading records, writing, research trails, speaking/listening evidence, revision, assessments, and portfolio artifacts—not a completion click.

Transfer

Students repeatedly apply skills in new contexts so the course can distinguish memorized completion from genuine independent capability.

Khaemenes Beta Learning Program

The improvement path stays visible all the way into the lesson.

English I now uses the same public Beta destination already surfaced by the High School and Grade 9 campus.

Report observable problems

Broken links, confusing instructions, inaccessible materials, factual concerns, mismatched tools, duplicated lesson patterns, assessment misalignment, or mastery behavior that does not work as intended.

Keep Beta separate from mastery

Beta participation never replaces coursework, assessment, mentor conferences, portfolio evidence, grading, or the 80% mastery requirement.

Canonical route

No internal feedback endpoint, implementation map, or secret is exposed on the student surface.

Open Beta Program

Public-Surface Rule

The classroom shows the learning—not the machinery.

Student-facing English I pages may identify approved public educational resources and explain their learning purpose. They must not expose credentials, tokens, private endpoints, internal repositories, protected routes, service topology, administrative maps, orchestration details, storage architecture, security implementation, or answer keys intended to remain protected.

Public destination + educational purpose = appropriate. Internal machinery + sensitive implementation = keep off the classroom surface.
