English 9 · Unit 01 · Day 15

Educator Review · Scholarly Demonstration

Score the independent writing with the original five-criterion, 10-point rubric. Record evidence-specific feedback, then preserve the evaluation in the Gradebook and as an exportable JSON file.

Local educator/parent-managed review: This publicly hosted, offline-capable tool does not authenticate reviewers or synchronize devices. Use the school’s authorized review process for official grade approval. Never use a shared public computer for private student evidence.

Open Day 15 General Literary Analysis Rubric

Day 15 Mastery Rubric · 10 Points

For each criterion, award 0 (missing), 1 (developing), or 2 (mastery). A total of 8/10 or higher meets the Day 15 threshold; below 8/10 requires targeted revision and re-evaluation.

CriterionEvidence to evaluateScore (0–2)
Inquiry & ClaimIs the claim specific, arguable, and responsive to both texts?
EvidenceAre quotations and paraphrases accurate, precise, and taken from both sources?
SynthesisDoes the response produce a new understanding of a relationship among sources?
ReasoningDoes analysis explain how the evidence supports or complicates the interpretation?
Clarity & ConventionsIs the argument coherent and responsibly attributed, with controlled conventions?
