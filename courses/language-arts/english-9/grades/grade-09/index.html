Course Contract

Learning evidence, not completion clicks.

  • 12 units · 36 weeks · 180 daily instructional blocks.
  • Defined mastery assessments require at least 80%.
  • Below mastery triggers targeted corrective learning and reassessment.
  • Eiren supports the learning process but cannot write assessed work, assign verified scores, or override mastery.
  • Beta feedback is separate from coursework, grading, and mastery.
  • Major work is preserved in the Grade 9 portfolio.
