02 · Daily Vocabulary — 10 Words

Read each definition. Write an original sentence for at least five words, and use two or more in today's journal or reading response.

1. AnalyzeTo examine something carefully by studying its parts.
2. InterpretTo explain the meaning of information or a text.
3. InferenceA reasoned conclusion based on evidence and knowledge.
4. EvidenceFacts, details, or examples that support an idea.
5. NuanceA small but meaningful difference in meaning or tone.
6. PerspectiveA particular point of view or way of seeing an issue.
7. ContextThe circumstances or surrounding text that clarify meaning.
8. AmbiguousOpen to more than one possible meaning.
9. SubstantiateTo support a claim with evidence or proof.
10. SynthesizeTo combine ideas or information into a new understanding.
03 · Daily Journal

Journal prompt: Think of a time when a first impression changed after you learned more information. What detail changed your understanding? Explain how you know.

Writing target: a clear idea, specific detail, explanation, and complete sentences.

04 · Daily Reading Assignment

Reading: “The First Annotation” — Khaemenes Academy original classroom passage.

Sela read the notice once and thought she understood it. On a second reading she circled the words “some routes” and asked whether the notice referred to every street. The difference between “some” and “all” changed the claim she was willing to make.

Her notebook separated the printed words from her interpretation. She could quote the notice, explain a possibility, and identify what the passage did not say.

Read twice, then complete these steps:

  1. Underline or record two details that matter.
  2. Identify the difference between what the notice states and what Sela infers.
  3. Explain how the words “some” and “all” change the meaning.
  4. Write a short evidence-based response: What makes Sela's second reading more careful?
