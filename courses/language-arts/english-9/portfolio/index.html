Cumulative Evidence

Grade 9 Portfolio

Growth made visible through selected, revised, and defended work.

The portfolio is not a folder containing everything. It is a curated argument demonstrating what the student can now do, how the work improved, where the evidence is located, and what remains to be learned.

Open PROSEAssessments

Required Artifacts

  • Baseline diagnostic and revised comparison
  • One literary analysis
  • One polished narrative or poetry portfolio
  • One speech, seminar, performance, or debate record
  • Research proposal, source audit, annotated bibliography, and report
  • One informational or technical composition with a visual
  • One argument with counterclaim and rebuttal
  • Publication or multimodal composition
  • Capstone synthesis project
  • Final reflection and oral-defense evidence map

Artifact Reflection

For each artifact, identify the task, date, competency demonstrated, exact evidence location, strongest revision, feedback used, counterevidence or limitation, and next-step skill.

Oral Defense

  1. State a narrow claim about growth as a reader, writer, researcher, and communicator.
  2. Present specific artifacts and exact evidence locations.
  3. Explain one consequential revision and why it improved the work.
  4. Respond to questions about source use, language choices, counterevidence, and independent judgment.
  5. Identify a realistic English 10 goal.

Evidence Readiness Check

  • Artifacts are dated and titled.
  • Draft and final versions are distinguishable.
  • Sources and permissions are recorded.
  • Rubrics or teacher feedback are attached where applicable.
  • Reflection addresses evidence rather than vague effort.
  • At least one limitation or unresolved growth area is acknowledged.
  • Files are exported beyond browser storage.

This records organizational readiness only. It does not satisfy the final English 9 mastery gate.

Eiren · Language Arts Mentor

Use Eiren to rehearse claims, not manufacture them.

Bring the artifact, growth claim, evidence location, revision history, counterevidence, or defense question first. Eiren can challenge whether a claim is too broad and whether the portfolio actually proves independence.

Practice with Eiren
Eiren cannot invent missing artifacts, fabricate feedback, write the assessed reflection, certify mastery, or participate in independently scored defense portions unless explicitly permitted as an accommodation/support.

Beta Program

Audit the portfolio for authenticity, access, and evidence quality.

Report broken artifact links, inaccessible portfolio materials, vague reflections accepted as proof, incomparable before/after evidence, cosmetic edits presented as substantial revision, missing source/rights records, or any path that confuses portfolio readiness with verified mastery.
