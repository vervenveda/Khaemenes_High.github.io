Canonical Mastery Contract

Unit 5 · Drama, Choice & Consequence

Mastery requires literary reasoning, collaborative listening, evidence-based staging, and independent transfer. Performance polish, participation alone, or a single local score cannot substitute for the reviewed evidence set.

Authoritative Evidence Set

Four components establish Unit 5 mastery.

40%

Dramatic Literary Analysis

A sustained evidence-based interpretation of character, conflict, dramatic structure, language, stagecraft, competing duties, or consequence. The analysis must address a serious counterreading and preserve a meaningful revision trail.

Literary Analysis Rubric
20%

Socratic Seminar

Reviewed evidence of preparation, accurate listening, textual support, genuine questioning, building or challenging ideas responsibly, and revising or qualifying a position when warranted. Quantity of speech is not mastery.

Speaking & Listening Rubric
25%

Staged-Interpretation Rationale

A staging claim plus blocking, voice, movement, pause, spatial, or visual choices explicitly connected to the dramatic text. The student must explain how the choices make an interpretation visible and respond to feedback or an alternate staging.

15%

Unfamiliar-Scene Transfer

Analyze an unrehearsed dramatic scene or excerpt for conflict, motivation, stagecraft, structure, or interpretive possibility using precise textual and stage evidence without relying on a memorized classroom reading.

Access & Fairness

Drama assessment measures interpretation—not theatrical conformity.

Approved accessible equivalents may be used for seminar or staged-performance evidence when they preserve the underlying targets: evidence, listening/response, interpretive reasoning, and purposeful staging decisions. Accent, disability, charisma, eye-contact convention, volume norm, mobility, or theatrical confidence cannot determine mastery.

A student may demonstrate staging reasoning through live performance, recorded performance, director's presentation, annotated blocking plan with oral/written defense, or another approved equivalent when necessary. The evidence standard remains the same.

80% Decision Rule

Every component and the weighted result must reach mastery.

All four components must be present. Each required scored component must reach at least 80%, and the weighted Unit 5 result must also reach at least 80%.

Corrective Learning & Reassessment

Repair the missed dramatic competency.

Analysis Below 80%

Return to claim scope, scene evidence, warrants, dramatic form, counterreading, and substantive revision. Reassess the weak criteria with revised or meaningfully changed evidence.

Seminar Below 80%

Practice paraphrasing, evidence-based questioning, listening notes, respectful challenge, and qualification. Reassess through a new discussion, conference, or approved equivalent that requires real response to another interpretation.

Staging Below 80%

Repair unsupported blocking or performance choices by connecting each major choice to language, stage direction, conflict, or structure; then test and defend a revised staging.

Transfer Below 80%

Practice observation → inference → stage/text evidence → warrant on different scenes, then reassess with a fresh unrehearsed dramatic passage.

Eiren · Language Arts Mentor

Eiren may rehearse challenge, but cannot become the student's performance.

Eiren may pressure-test an interpretation, counterreading, seminar question, staging rationale, or revision before assessment. During scored seminar or unfamiliar-scene transfer, Eiren cannot supply the student's contribution, interpretation, answer, or verified score.

Open Eiren

Beta Program

Audit drama assessment for access, fairness, and evidence integrity.

Report inaccessible scripts, seminar structures that reward domination over listening, performance requirements that confuse theatrical style with mastery, unclear scoring, broken tools, or any progression path that appears to bypass the evidence contract.

Open Beta Program
Beta participation is never assessment evidence. Public surfaces must not expose protected answer material, credentials, tokens, private endpoints, internal repository machinery, infrastructure topology, or network mapping.
