Assessment System

Demonstrate what language can do.

Practice, performance, revision, transfer, and verified mastery are different kinds of evidence.

English 9 is a public static course. Public forms can support practice and formative feedback, while writing, research, speaking, portfolio, and complex reasoning require human review. A local browser score is a learning record—not tamper-resistant institutional verification.

Evidence Classes

Not every score means the same thing.

Practice

Weekly checks, games, self-checks, drafting tools, mentor rehearsal, and evidence-record buttons support learning. They do not independently satisfy a mastery gate.

Human-Reviewed Performance

Essays, research, speeches, debates, projects, portfolio evidence, oral defense, and complex reasoning are evaluated with criterion-specific rubrics and retained evidence.

Verified Mastery

A unit or course mastery decision combines the defined evidence for that unit. Progression requires at least 80%. Corrective learning targets the weak competency before reassessment.

Public Assessment Forms

These forms are released and open.

The diagnostic, midterm, and final have existed in public Git history with client-side answer keys. They remain useful as practice, familiarization, accessibility checks, and formative review, but they are not secure hidden-key examinations.

80% Mastery Model

Mastery is evidence-based, not click-based.

  • Each defined unit mastery decision requires at least 80%.
  • Below 80% triggers targeted corrective learning and reassessment.
  • A later lower attempt does not erase prior demonstrated mastery.
  • Fresh or meaningfully changed evidence is used when memorization could invalidate a retake.
  • Weekly evidence completion, attendance, games, Beta participation, Eiren interaction, or tool use cannot override mastery.
  • Human review remains authoritative for essays, research, speaking, debate, portfolio, projects, oral defense, and complex reasoning.

Assessment Integrity

A public course should be transparent about what it can and cannot secure.

Do not treat an answer key embedded in public HTML/JavaScript as hidden. Future canonical objective forms require an educator-administered fresh form, an authorized non-public scoring path, a generated fresh-transfer form with verification, or an intentionally open-book/open-source design where secrecy is not part of validity.

The current released forms are preserved for learning value. They are not used as the sole evidence for secure high-stakes mastery.

Eiren · Language Arts Mentor

Prepare with Eiren; demonstrate independently where required.

Eiren may support questioning, corrective learning, source skepticism, rehearsal, revision, and reflection when permitted. Eiren cannot write assessed work, fabricate evidence, supply protected answers, assign verified scores, alter mastery records, or participate in independently scored portions unless explicitly allowed as an accommodation/support.

Beta Program

Report assessment defects without changing the student's score.

Report ambiguous questions, multiple defensible answers, inaccessible forms, scoring defects, broken routes, unclear rubric criteria, stale source claims, or progression behavior that does not match the 80% model.

Open Beta Program
Beta participation is separate from grading and mastery. Public assessment surfaces expose no credentials, protected endpoints, private infrastructure, or hidden answer material represented as secure.
