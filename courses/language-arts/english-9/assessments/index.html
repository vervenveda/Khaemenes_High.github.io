Assessment System · 12 Canonical Mastery Gates

Demonstrate what language and reasoning can do.

Practice, performance, revision, transfer, independence, and verified mastery are different kinds of evidence.

English 9 is a public static course. Public forms can support practice and formative feedback, while writing, research, speaking, portfolio, critical thinking, and complex transfer require reviewed evidence. A local browser score is a learning record—not tamper-resistant institutional verification.

Evidence Classes

Not every score means the same thing.

Practice

Weekly checks, LearnA vocabulary, games, self-checks, drafting tools, mentor rehearsal, and evidence-record buttons support learning. They do not independently satisfy a mastery gate.

Reviewed Performance

Essays, research, speeches, debates, projects, procedures, visual communication, portfolio evidence, oral defense, and complex reasoning are evaluated with criterion-specific evidence.

Independent Transfer

Fresh or unfamiliar material tests whether a skill can travel beyond rehearsed texts and previously supplied interpretations. Transfer is separately protected from preparation support where required.

Verified Mastery

A unit mastery decision combines that unit's canonical evidence. Each required component and the weighted result must reach at least 80%.

Critical-Thinking Continuum

The reasoning burden increases across the year.

Units 1–3: observation → inference → evidence → craft decision → revision.

Units 4–6: interpretation → alternatives → reasoning → audience → public communication.

Units 7–9: provenance → verification → failure testing → hypothesis revision → counterreading.

Units 10–12: steelmanning → adversarial reasoning → editorial judgment → evidence-based metacognition → independent transfer.

The final standard is not memorization. It is the ability to make a claim, locate evidence, expose the warrant, countertest the claim, recalibrate, and transfer the underlying skill to unfamiliar material.

Assessment Integrity

A public course should be explicit about what it can and cannot secure.

Do not treat an answer key embedded in public HTML/JavaScript as hidden. Future objective mastery forms require an educator-administered fresh form, an authorized non-public scoring path, a generated fresh-transfer form with verification, or an intentionally open-book/open-source design where secrecy is not part of validity.

Corrective learning is targeted. Reassessment uses revised, fresh, or meaningfully changed evidence when repeating identical material would reward memorization rather than competence.

Eiren · Language Arts Mentor

Prepare with Eiren; demonstrate independently where required.

Eiren may support questioning, corrective learning, source skepticism, counterreading, rehearsal, revision, and reflection when permitted. Eiren cannot write assessed work, fabricate evidence, supply protected answers, assign verified scores, alter mastery records, or participate in independently scored portions unless explicitly allowed as an accommodation/support.

Beta Program

Report assessment defects without changing the student's score.

Report ambiguous questions, multiple defensible answers, inaccessible forms, scoring defects, broken routes, unclear criteria, stale source claims, missing unit contracts, weak reassessment, or progression behavior that does not match the component-level 80% model.

Open Beta Program
Beta participation, LearnA vocabulary completion, games, and tool use are separate from grading and mastery. Public assessment surfaces expose no credentials, protected endpoints, private infrastructure, network mapping, or hidden answer material represented as secure.
