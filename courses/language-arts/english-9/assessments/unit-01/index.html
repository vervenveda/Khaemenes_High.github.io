Authoritative Evidence

Four components establish Unit 1 mastery.

Human review remains authoritative for complex writing and reasoning. This public page records reviewed scores locally after the evidence has actually been evaluated.

35% · Analytical Paragraph

Evidence-based analytical paragraph demonstrating a defensible claim, precise textual evidence, explicit reasoning/warrant, coherent organization, and controlled language.

Authority: teacher/educator review using the Literary Analysis Rubric. Minimum: 16/20 or equivalent 80%.

30% · Revised Diagnostic Essay

A substantially revised version of the Week 1 baseline writing, with earlier draft preserved and a brief explanation of consequential revision decisions.

Authority: teacher/educator review. Minimum: 80% on the defined writing criteria.

20% · Language & Integrity Check

Fresh or meaningfully changed evidence covering sentence boundaries, fragments/run-ons/comma splices, quotation integration, paraphrase/summary accuracy, attribution, and source boundaries.

Authority: reviewed scored check. Minimum: 80%. Released diagnostic answers do not satisfy this component.

15% · Reading Evidence Transfer

A fresh short literary or informational passage requiring the student to distinguish detail from inference and produce a claim → evidence → warrant explanation without relying on a rehearsed answer.

Authority: teacher/educator reviewed transfer response. Minimum: 80%.

Weeks 1–3 Classification

Practice, process, and mastery are not interchangeable.

Week 1 · Formative Baseline

Diagnostic reading, literacy inventory, baseline essay, and growth goal are formative evidence. The released public diagnostic routes instruction and does not lock or unlock progression.

Week 2 · Teacher-Reviewed Checkpoint

Annotation trail, evidence ranking, language revision, and two claim–evidence–reasoning paragraphs are instructional checkpoints. They prepare and may contribute evidence to the analytical-paragraph component, but the weekly record itself is not mastery.

Week 3 · Mastery Assembly

Revision, academic-integrity work, language evidence, and fresh transfer are assembled into the authoritative Unit 1 evidence set. Scored mastery evidence below 80% triggers corrective learning and reassessment.

Eiren · Language Arts Mentor

Preparation support ends where independent evidence begins.

Eiren may help the student identify a weak competency, question an inference, compare revision options, or rehearse explanation before reassessment. Eiren cannot write the assessed response, supply answers during independent scored work, assign the verified score, or override the gate.
