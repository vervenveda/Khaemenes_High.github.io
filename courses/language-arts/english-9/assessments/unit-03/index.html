Unit 3 · Short Fiction & Narrative Craft

Mastery is demonstrated through original narrative craft, explicit craft reasoning, fresh reading transfer, and visible revision—not by merely producing a story.

Authoritative Evidence Set

Four components establish Unit 3 mastery.

55%

Polished Short Story

An original short story demonstrating purposeful character pressure, consequential choice, structure, pacing, perspective, dialogue, detail, and language control. Score with the Narrative rubric.

Narrative Rubric
20%

Craft Commentary

A concise analytical commentary that identifies specific craft decisions, cites passages from the student's own story, and explains intended effects without merely summarizing the plot.

15%

Fresh Narrative-Reading Transfer

Analyze an unrehearsed short-fiction passage for character, perspective, structure, irony, pacing, dialogue, or another relevant craft feature using precise textual evidence.

10%

Revision Trail

Preserve planning, an earlier complete draft, feedback evidence, and at least three substantive before → after changes with reasons showing how revision improved meaning or craft.

Student Authorship

The story must remain inspectably the student's own work.

PROSE, read-aloud support, peer feedback, Eiren questioning, and approved creative tools may assist process. They cannot generate the assessed story, replace the student's narrative decisions, or obscure who authored the submitted language.

A polished result without inspectable student planning, drafting, revision, and decision-making is insufficient evidence of Unit 3 mastery.

Independent Transfer

Reading craft must transfer beyond the student's own story.

The 15% transfer task uses a fresh narrative passage for which the target interpretation has not been taught. The student independently identifies a craft feature, selects evidence, and explains its effect. Approved accessibility support may change access, not the reasoning target.

80% Decision Rule

Every required component and the weighted result must reach mastery.

All four components must be present. Each required scored component must reach at least 80%, and the weighted Unit 3 result must also reach at least 80%.

Corrective Learning & Reassessment

Repair the missed craft competency.

Story Below 80%

Target the weak criterion: character pressure, structure, POV, pacing, dialogue, detail, language control, or coherence. Revise substantively before reassessment.

Commentary Below 80%

Replace plot summary or vague intention statements with specific passage evidence and explanation of the craft decision's effect.

Transfer Below 80%

Practice craft analysis on different short passages, then reassess with a fresh unrehearsed narrative selection.

Revision Trail Below 80%

Recover or recreate visible draft-state comparison and demonstrate substantive changes rather than proofreading-only edits.

Eiren · Language Arts Mentor

Eiren is a craft examiner and conference partner, not a ghostwriter.

Eiren may ask whether a climax grows from character choice, pressure-test POV, identify places a reader becomes confused, or compare revision options. Eiren cannot write the assessed story, craft commentary, or fresh transfer answer.
