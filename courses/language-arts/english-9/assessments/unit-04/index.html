Authoritative Evidence Set

Four components establish Unit 4 mastery.

35%

Four-Poem Portfolio

Four revised original poems demonstrating purposeful choices in image, diction, figurative language, sound, syntax, structure, or form. Earlier versions and meaningful revisions must remain visible.

30%

Comparative Poetry Analysis

An evidence-based analysis comparing poetic or translation choices across texts. The analysis must distinguish observable language differences from unsupported claims about authorial or translator intent.

Literary Analysis Rubric
15%

Oral Reading / Accessible Equivalent

A prepared interpretation demonstrating deliberate pace, pause, emphasis, tone, phrasing, or another competency-appropriate delivery mode. The score measures interpretation and clarity rather than charisma, accent, disability, or theatricality.

Speaking & Listening Rubric
20%

Fresh Unfamiliar-Poetry Transfer

Analyze an unrehearsed poem or poetic passage for image, diction, sound, syntax, figurative language, structure, ambiguity, or form using precise textual evidence.

Independent Transfer

The 20% transfer component must be meaningfully fresh.

The poem or passage should not be one for which the learner has already received the target interpretation or answer. The student must independently form and defend an interpretation from the language on the page.

During the scored transfer portion, Eiren, PROSE, generated interpretation, answer keys, and unapproved collaboration may not supply the interpretation being evaluated. Approved accessibility supports may change access without lowering the reading-and-reasoning target.

Performance Access

Performance demonstrates interpretation, not conformity.

Students may use an approved accessible equivalent when the course setting or learner needs require one, provided the response still demonstrates deliberate interpretive choices tied to the poem. Scoring must not reward eye-contact convention, theatrical style, accent, or disability.

80% Decision Rule

Every component and the weighted result must reach mastery.

The software gate mirrors the written contract. Enter all four educator-reviewed component scores; a single typed weighted score cannot create Unit 4 mastery.

Corrective Learning & Reassessment

Repair the specific missed competency.

Portfolio Below 80%

Return to image, diction, form, sound, syntax, intentional deviation, and revision evidence. Reassess the weak criterion through substantive revision, not cosmetic polishing.

Analysis Below 80%

Return to exact language, comparison criteria, warrants, translation evidence, ambiguity, and qualification. Reassess with revised analysis or a meaningfully changed task.

Performance Below 80%

Use annotated performance planning, read-aloud rehearsal, and listener feedback to repair interpretive clarity. Reperform using an approved accessible mode where appropriate.

Transfer Below 80%

Practice close reading on different poems, then reassess with a fresh unrehearsed selection.

Eiren · Language Arts Mentor

Eiren can challenge interpretation and craft without becoming the author.

Eiren may question a diction choice, test a translation claim, challenge a performance decision, or help identify the missed criterion. During independent transfer, Eiren cannot provide the interpretation being scored.
