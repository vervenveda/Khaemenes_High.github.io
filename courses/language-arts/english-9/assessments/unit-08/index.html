Unit 8 · Informational & Technical Communication

Students must not merely explain information. They must test whether explanations work, procedures reproduce, visuals represent evidence faithfully, accessibility survives presentation changes, and conclusions remain proportionate to what is known.

Authoritative Evidence Set

Four components establish Unit 8 mastery.

30%

Explanatory Article

Define the problem or concept, choose an explanatory architecture, distinguish facts from inference, expose necessary assumptions, explain mechanisms or relationships accurately, preserve provenance, acknowledge meaningful uncertainty, and revise for reader comprehension.

Research Rubric
30%

Tested Technical Procedure

Produce a procedure that another user can execute. Preserve test evidence, failure points, ambiguity, safety constraints, prerequisite knowledge, user feedback, revision, and retest. A plausible-looking procedure without testing cannot satisfy mastery.

25%

Accessible Infographic

Represent verified information/data with traceable sources, appropriate scale and units, honest visual framing, useful caption and alt text, clear hierarchy, redundant cues, and an explicit statement of what the visual does and does not establish.

15%

Fresh Informational/Technical Transfer

On unfamiliar material, diagnose an explanation, procedure, or visual for hidden assumptions, missing steps/context, misleading representation, accessibility barriers, unsupported inference, and likely failure modes; then propose and defend a repair.

Critical-Thinking Protocol

Define → model → test → fail → inspect → revise → retest.

Students identify the intended user and problem, state assumptions, explain why the proposed structure/process/visual should work, test it against evidence or actual use, inspect failure modes and alternative interpretations, revise, and retest. Attractive presentation cannot substitute for successful reasoning.

80% Decision Rule

Every component and the weighted result must reach 80%.

Corrective Learning

Repair the actual failure mode.

Explanation Below 80%

Identify the reader's comprehension failure: structure, definition, mechanism, evidence, assumption, context, uncertainty, or language. Revise and test with a reader.

Procedure Below 80%

Locate the step, prerequisite, condition, safety issue, branch, measurement, or ambiguity that caused failure. Revise and retest with fresh execution evidence.

Visual Below 80%

Repair data provenance, scale, denominator, unit, hierarchy, caption, alt text, redundant cue, or interpretive overreach and repeat the access/interpretation audit.

Transfer Below 80%

Practice diagnosis on different material, then reassess with a fresh explanation/procedure/visual and no supplied repair.

Eiren · Failure-Mode Examiner

Eiren asks where the explanation breaks.

Eiren may probe assumptions, missing prerequisites, edge cases, causal overreach, ambiguous instructions, denominators, scale, alternative interpretations, accessibility, and likely user failure. Eiren cannot invent technical facts, substitute for actual procedure testing, certify accessibility compliance, create assessed products, assign scores, or override mastery.
