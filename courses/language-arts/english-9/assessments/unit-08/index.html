Open Week 24 Mastery AssessmentUnit Overview

Assessment Authority

Mastery is awarded from the existing evaluator-reviewed demonstration.

This route creates a stable assessment address without replacing the original scholarly work. The student completes the existing Unit 08 demonstration and an evaluator applies its rubric.

  • Mastery threshold: 8/10 (80%).
  • Authority: human evaluator review.
  • Correction: revise the weakest rubric criterion and resubmit.
  • Completion: submitting work or pressing a completion control does not grant mastery.
  • Evidence: preserve drafts, source records, revision history, reflection, and required performance/product evidence.

Unit Record

Weeks: 22–24

Assessment type: Final-week mastery demonstration

Retake model: corrective revision of the same intellectual work; no automatic answer reshuffle is introduced here.
