Authoritative Evidence Set

Four components establish Unit 2 mastery.

The canonical course mastery map assigns the following weights. Each component must be reviewed as authentic evidence of the named competency.

55%

Comparative Literary Analysis

A sustained analysis comparing two mythic or epic traditions through meaningful analytical criteria. The essay must integrate evidence from both texts, preserve contextual difference, explain warrants, and avoid superficial universality.

Literary Analysis Rubric
25%

Fresh Close-Reading Transfer

Analyze an unrehearsed mythic, epic, or culturally situated passage. Distinguish observation from inference, identify relevant textual evidence, and defend an interpretation without relying on a memorized classroom reading.

10%

Visual Archetype Map

An analytical visual model showing pattern, evidence, difference, transformation, and context. Connections must be labeled so the visual communicates reasoning rather than decoration.

10%

Revision Reflection

Use draft history or feedback evidence to identify a consequential revision or qualification, explain why it was necessary, and show how the change improved analytical accuracy or nuance.

Independent Transfer

The 25% transfer component must be meaningfully fresh.

The passage should not be one for which the learner has already received the target interpretation, answer, or completed analysis. The student must independently establish a defensible reading from textual evidence. Approved accessibility supports may change access to the task without lowering the reading-and-reasoning target.

Eiren, PROSE, games, answer keys, generated interpretations, and unapproved collaboration cannot supply the scored interpretation during the independent transfer portion.

80% Decision Rule

Mastery requires component competence and an 80% weighted result.

All four required components must be present. Each required component must reach at least 80%, and the weighted Unit 2 result must also reach at least 80%. The local mastery record cannot be created from a single typed weighted score.

Corrective Learning & Reassessment

Repair the competency that missed mastery.

Comparative Analysis Below 80%

Return to comparison criteria, paired evidence, warrants, contextual qualification, counterexamples, organization by idea, and substantive revision. Reassess the weak criterion with revised or meaningfully changed evidence.

Transfer Below 80%

Practice observation → inference → evidence → warrant on different passages, then reassess with a fresh unrehearsed passage.

Visual Reasoning Below 80%

Repair unlabeled connections, unsupported archetype claims, false equivalence, missing textual evidence, or decorative elements that do not communicate reasoning.

Revision Reflection Below 80%

Compare actual draft states, identify a substantive change, and explain the reasoning and consequence of that change rather than merely listing edits.

Eiren · Language Arts Mentor

Use Eiren during learning and revision—not as hidden assessment evidence.

Eiren may challenge false equivalence, ask for missing evidence, question overgeneralization, help identify a weak criterion, or examine revision options. During independent transfer, Eiren cannot provide the interpretation or answer being scored.

Open Eiren

Beta Program

Audit the assessment without changing the score.

Report inaccessible passages, cultural flattening, ambiguous directions, broken tools, scoring behavior that does not match the evidence contract, or any route that appears to bypass the 80% requirement. Beta participation is never assessment evidence.

Open Beta Program
Public assessment pages contain learning criteria and released instructions only. They must not expose protected answer material, credentials, tokens, private endpoints, internal repository machinery, infrastructure topology, or network mapping.
