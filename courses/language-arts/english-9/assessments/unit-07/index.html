Unit 7 · Research, Evidence & Media Literacy

Students make reasoning inspectable: claim → evidence → warrant → assumptions → counterevidence → alternative explanation → provenance → uncertainty → conclusion → revision.

Authoritative Evidence Set

Four independently reviewed components establish mastery.

45%

Research Report

A 900–1,200-word synthesis organized around the inquiry and relationships among evidence. It distinguishes evidence from inference, represents disagreement honestly, exposes consequential warrants/assumptions, addresses counterevidence or alternatives, calibrates certainty, and revises when evidence requires it.

Research Rubric
30%

Source Audit & Annotated Bibliography

Trace provenance, expertise, evidence, incentives, lineage, context, corroboration, limitations, and missing information. Judge what particular claims each source can responsibly support.

15%

Fresh Source-Verification Transfer

Investigate an unrehearsed claim/source cluster. Separate observation from inference, trace origin, compare genuinely independent evidence, test alternatives, identify uncertainty, and state what would change the conclusion.

10%

Research Trail & Reflection

Preserve question mutations, search decisions, dead ends, source changes, rejected evidence, draft revisions, and a documented moment when stronger evidence caused qualification, revision, or abandonment of an earlier belief.

Reading Hall boundary: the Unit 7 Necklace critical review is valuable portfolio and transfer practice. It may contribute evidence only where a rubric criterion genuinely overlaps. Reading completion or a polished review cannot substitute for the fresh source-verification transfer, source audit, or other independent evidence required here.

Critical-Thinking Standard

Neutral inquiry means disciplined bias management—not pretending to have no viewpoint.

Students distinguish observation from inference, identify assumptions, investigate source and personal bias, seek serious counterevidence, avoid false balance, represent opposing interpretations accurately, and change their minds when warranted. Automatic skepticism and automatic agreement are both insufficient.

80% Decision Rule

Every component and the calculated weighted result must reach 80%.

No manually entered overall score can create mastery.

Corrective Learning

Repair the specific reasoning failure, then reassess.

Research Report Below 80%

Rebuild synthesis, evidence relationships, warrants, counterevidence/alternatives, source boundaries, and certainty language; revise affected sections.

Source Audit Below 80%

Repeat provenance tracing and lateral reading with emphasis on claim-level support, independence, source lineage, incentives, context, and limitations.

Fresh Transfer Below 80%

Practice the verification process on a different cluster, then reassess with new sources and no supplied conclusion.

Research Trail Below 80%

Use actual search/draft history to document consequential decisions, rejected hypotheses, uncertainty, and revisions caused by evidence.

Eiren · Critical-Thinking Examiner

Eiren may challenge reasoning but cannot become the evidence.

Eiren may probe assumptions, missing evidence, source independence, alternative explanations, causal claims, uncertainty, and revision thresholds. Eiren cannot become an uncited source, fabricate evidence/citations, write the assessed report or Reading Hall review, supply the scored transfer conclusion, assign the verified score, or override mastery.
