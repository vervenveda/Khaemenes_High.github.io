# Advanced Readiness Pathway

A learner enters the advanced pathway when diagnostic or unit evidence shows secure mastery and independent transfer.

## Challenge dimensions
- harder/less scaffolded text
- independent source selection
- comparative synthesis
- multiple perspectives
- counterargument
- authentic audience
- independent research
- leadership in seminar/debate
- publication-quality revision

## Advancement rule
Advanced learners do not skip essential evidence automatically. They demonstrate mastery, then receive complexity/transfer tasks.

## Examples
### Close reading
Core: identify evidence supporting an interpretation.
Advanced: compare two plausible interpretations and defend one.

### Argument
Core: develop a defensible claim.
Advanced: construct a claim, concession, counterclaim, rebuttal, and source-based synthesis.

### Research
Core: evaluate sources.
Advanced: reconcile conflicting sources and explain why their methods/contexts produce different conclusions.
