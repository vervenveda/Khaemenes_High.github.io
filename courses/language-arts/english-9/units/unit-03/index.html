Unit 03

Short Fiction & Narrative Craft

Study character, conflict, setting, point of view, irony, pacing, dialogue, and the deliberate revision of original fiction.

OverviewWeeksAssessmentStandards

Essential Question

How do writers shape a reader’s experience through character, structure, perspective, and detail?

Text and Source Family

Public-domain short fiction by writers such as Edgar Allan Poe, Kate Chopin, Anton Chekhov, Rabindranath Tagore, and Ryūnosuke Akutagawa.

Learning Priorities

  • Read closely and cite precise textual evidence.
  • Write through planning, drafting, feedback, revision, editing, and reflection.
  • Use language conventions and vocabulary as tools for meaning and style.
  • Discuss, present, listen, question, and respond with intellectual fairness.
  • Preserve major work in the Grade 9 portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: Polished short story with craft commentary and peer-review record.
