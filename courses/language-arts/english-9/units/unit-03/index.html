Essential Question

How do writers shape a reader's experience through character, structure, perspective, and detail?

Text and Source Family

Public-domain short fiction by writers such as Edgar Allan Poe, Kate Chopin, Anton Chekhov, Rabindranath Tagore, and Ryūnosuke Akutagawa.

Learning Priorities

  • Analyze character, conflict, setting, perspective, structure, irony, and pacing through precise textual evidence.
  • Transfer observed craft techniques into original fiction without copying source content.
  • Write through planning, drafting, feedback, revision, editing, and reflection.
  • Use language conventions and vocabulary as tools for meaning, voice, and style.
  • Preserve analytical and creative evidence in the Grade 9 portfolio.
