Essential Question

How do writers shape a reader's experience through character, structure, perspective, and detail?

Text and Source Family

Public-domain short fiction by writers such as Edgar Allan Poe, Kate Chopin, Anton Chekhov, Rabindranath Tagore, and Ryūnosuke Akutagawa.

Learning Priorities

  • Analyze character, conflict, setting, perspective, structure, irony, pacing, and dialogue through precise textual evidence.
  • Transfer observed craft techniques into original fiction without copying source content.
  • Write through planning, drafting, feedback, substantive revision, editing, and reflection.
  • Preserve student authorship while using tools and mentor feedback.
  • Demonstrate that narrative-reading skills transfer to unfamiliar fiction.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Canonical evidence: polished short story, craft commentary, fresh narrative-reading transfer, revision trail.

Mastery: every required component ≥80% and weighted Unit 3 result ≥80%.
