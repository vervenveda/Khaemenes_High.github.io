Poetry, Voice & Form

Analyze imagery, diction, figurative language, rhythm, meter, form, translation, and performance while composing an original portfolio.

OverviewWeeksAssessmentStandards

Essential Question

How can compressed language carry sound, image, argument, memory, and emotion?

Text and Source Family

Public-domain poetry by Shakespeare, Dickinson, Whitman, Dunbar, Bashō, Li Bai in approved translation, and other global poets.

Learning Priorities

  • Read closely and cite precise textual evidence.
  • Write through planning, drafting, feedback, revision, editing, and reflection.
  • Use language conventions and vocabulary as tools for meaning and style.
  • Discuss, present, listen, question, and respond with intellectual fairness.
  • Preserve major work in the Grade 9 portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: Four-poem portfolio, comparative analysis, and formal oral reading.
