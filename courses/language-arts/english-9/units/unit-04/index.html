Essential Question

How can compressed language carry sound, image, argument, memory, and emotion?

Text and Source Family

Public-domain poetry by Shakespeare, Dickinson, Whitman, Dunbar, Bashō, Li Bai in approved translation, and other global poets.

Learning Priorities

  • Analyze how image, diction, figurative language, sound, syntax, rhythm, and form create effects.
  • Compare poetic choices across texts, translations, and performances without treating one version as automatically definitive.
  • Compose original poetry through planning, experimentation, feedback, revision, and performance.
  • Use language conventions and vocabulary as tools for voice, precision, and intentional deviation.
  • Preserve analytical, creative, revision, and performance evidence in the Grade 9 portfolio.

Unit Record
