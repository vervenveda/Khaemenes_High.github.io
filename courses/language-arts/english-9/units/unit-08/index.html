Informational & Technical Communication

Study explanatory structures, definitions, classification, cause and effect, technical procedures, data displays, captions, and visual rhetoric.

Essential Question

How do writers make complex processes and ideas accurate, usable, and clear?

Text and Source Family

Science, environment, health, technology, and public-information texts drawn from the Verve N Veda learning network.

Learning Priorities

  • Read closely and cite precise textual evidence.
  • Write through planning, drafting, feedback, revision, editing, and reflection.
  • Use language conventions and vocabulary as tools for meaning and style.
  • Discuss, present, listen, question, and respond with intellectual fairness.
  • Preserve major work in the Grade 9 portfolio.
