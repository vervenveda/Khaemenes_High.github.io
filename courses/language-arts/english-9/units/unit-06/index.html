Essential Question

How do speakers use language, evidence, structure, and delivery to move an audience responsibly?

Text and Source Family

Public-domain speeches and essays by Frederick Douglass, Sojourner Truth, Abraham Lincoln, Theodore Roosevelt, and other civic voices.

Learning Priorities

  • Analyze audience, purpose, context, occasion, claims, evidence, reasoning, appeals, style, and delivery.
  • Distinguish persuasive effectiveness from factual accuracy, logical soundness, and ethical responsibility.
  • Write speeches through planning, research/evidence, drafting, feedback, revision, rehearsal, and reflection.
  • Discuss, present, listen, question, and respond with intellectual fairness.
  • Preserve rhetorical analysis, speech development, rehearsal, and performance evidence in the Grade 9 portfolio.

Unit Record
