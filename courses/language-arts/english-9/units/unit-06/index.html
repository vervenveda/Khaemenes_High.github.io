Unit 06

Rhetoric & Public Voice

Introduce rhetorical situation, ethos, pathos, logos, claims, reasoning, fallacies, style, speech writing, and purposeful delivery.

OverviewWeeksAssessmentStandards

Essential Question

How do speakers use language, evidence, structure, and delivery to move an audience?

Text and Source Family

Public-domain speeches and essays by Frederick Douglass, Sojourner Truth, Abraham Lincoln, Theodore Roosevelt, and other civic voices.

Learning Priorities

  • Read closely and cite precise textual evidence.
  • Write through planning, drafting, feedback, revision, editing, and reflection.
  • Use language conventions and vocabulary as tools for meaning and style.
  • Discuss, present, listen, question, and respond with intellectual fairness.
  • Preserve major work in the Grade 9 portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: Four-to-six-minute evidence-based speech with rhetorical analysis.
