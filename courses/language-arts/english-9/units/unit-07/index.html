Essential Question

How can researchers find, test, connect, and ethically present reliable information?

Text and Source Family

Current and historical informational sources selected through approved Academy research tools, ARSHIF, public archives, libraries, databases, and appropriate primary/secondary sources.

Learning Priorities

  • Develop focused, significant, researchable questions and flexible search strategies.
  • Investigate source provenance, expertise, evidence, context, incentives, corroboration, and limitations through lateral reading.
  • Distinguish source credibility from whether a particular claim is adequately supported.
  • Annotate, paraphrase, quote, synthesize, and cite sources ethically while preserving provenance.
  • Recognize uncertainty, conflicting evidence, missing information, manipulated context, and media-format effects.
  • Write through planning, research, drafting, feedback, revision, editing, and reflection.
  • Preserve the research trail and major work in the Grade 9 portfolio.

Eiren · Language Arts Mentor

Eiren becomes a research-method mentor, not a source of invisible evidence.

Bring a research question, search plan, source evaluation, synthesis problem, citation decision, or draft claim first. Eiren can ask what would count as evidence, expose gaps in provenance or corroboration, and challenge overconfidence. Any factual claim used in assessed work still needs a traceable source.

Open Eiren
Eiren cannot become an uncited source, fabricate citations, invent research findings, write the assessed report, assign verified scores, or override mastery.

Beta Program

Report broken research tools, inaccessible sources, misleading source labels, citation defects, weak provenance, unclear distinctions between evidence and generated assistance, inaccessible media, or any path that allows the 80% mastery requirement to be bypassed.
