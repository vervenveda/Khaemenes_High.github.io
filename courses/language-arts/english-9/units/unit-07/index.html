Research, Evidence & Media Literacy

Build research questions, advanced search plans, lateral-reading habits, source evaluations, annotations, citations, and synthesized reports.

OverviewWeeksAssessmentStandards

Essential Question

How can researchers find, test, connect, and ethically present reliable information?

Text and Source Family

Current and historical informational sources selected through The Verifier, PLERA Search, ARSHIF, and public archives.

Learning Priorities

  • Read closely and cite precise textual evidence.
  • Write through planning, drafting, feedback, revision, editing, and reflection.
  • Use language conventions and vocabulary as tools for meaning and style.
  • Discuss, present, listen, question, and respond with intellectual fairness.
  • Preserve major work in the Grade 9 portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: Annotated bibliography, source audit, and 900–1,200-word research report.
