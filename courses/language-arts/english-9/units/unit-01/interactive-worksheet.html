01 · Read and Annotate

The First Annotation

Original Khaemenes Academy Unit 01 reading. Read twice. Mark two details and explain why they matter.

Sela read the notice once and thought she understood it. On a second reading she circled the words 'some routes' and asked whether the notice referred to every street. The difference between 'some' and 'all' changed the claim she was willing to make.

Her notebook separated the printed words from her interpretation. She could quote the notice, explain a possibility, and identify what the passage did not say.

02 · Observe and Infer

What can the evidence support?

Observation names what is printed. Inference explains a possibility supported by that evidence.

The passage says Sela changed her claim after noticing the difference between “some” and “all.” Which conclusion is safest?

03 · Build an Argument

Claim → Evidence → Warrant

An evidence-based claim can be questioned, supported, and revised. A warrant explains how the evidence supports it.
