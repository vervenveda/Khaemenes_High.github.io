Foundations of Scholarly Reading & Writing

Establish annotation, inference, paragraph architecture, grammar control, academic integrity, and productive revision habits.

OverviewWeeksAssessmentStandards

Essential Question

How do careful readers turn observations into defensible ideas?

Text and Source Family

Original course texts, short public-domain essays, selected speeches, and teacher-approved informational passages.

Learning Priorities

  • Read closely and cite precise textual evidence.
  • Write through planning, drafting, feedback, revision, editing, and reflection.
  • Use language conventions and vocabulary as tools for meaning and style.
  • Discuss, present, listen, question, and respond with intellectual fairness.
  • Preserve major work in the Grade 9 portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: Evidence-based analytical paragraph, revised diagnostic essay, and language conventions check.
