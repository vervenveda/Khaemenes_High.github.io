WEEK 01 · SHORT FICTION

The Story of an Hour

Author: Kate Chopin · Published: 1894

Assigned span: Complete short story

Reading focus: observation versus inference; diction; reversal; evidence limits

Evidence task: Annotate three details whose meaning changes after the ending. Write a claim, identify one competing interpretation, and revise the claim after rereading.

Source custody: First published in Vogue, 1894. Public domain in the United States.

WEEK 02 · SPEECH

Gettysburg Address

Author: Abraham Lincoln · Published: 1863

Assigned span: Complete address

Reading focus: purpose; repetition; compression; claim and evidence

Evidence task: Trace how repeated words develop Lincoln's claim. Distinguish what the speech asserts from historical information it assumes.

Source custody: Speech delivered at Gettysburg, Pennsylvania, November 19, 1863. Public-domain U.S. government-era historical text.

Open Authentic Source

WEEK 03 · SHORT FICTION

The Open Window

Author: Saki (H. H. Munro) · Published: 1914

Assigned span: Complete short story

Reading focus: narrative reliability; inference; revision; academic integrity

Evidence task: Build two interpretations of Vera's story, identify the textual evidence available at each stage, then explain why a responsible reader revises rather than hides an earlier inference.

Source custody: Published in Beasts and Super-Beasts, 1914. Published before 1931; public domain in the United States.
