Essential Question

What responsibilities arise when our editorial choices shape what other people see, hear, understand, and believe?

Learning Priorities

  • Analyze audience, purpose, genre, medium, circulation context, and accessibility before composing or curating.
  • Identify what each mode contributes and whether modes reinforce, complicate, contradict, manipulate, or merely duplicate one another.
  • Interrogate hierarchy, crop, caption, typography, sequence, sound, motion, whitespace, and omission as rhetorical choices.
  • Ask what a reader is encouraged to notice, infer, trust, ignore, or misunderstand.
  • Compare competing editorial/design solutions rather than treating the first attractive option as correct.
  • Use house style consistently while preserving justified authorial exceptions.
  • Distinguish substantive editing from copyediting and preserve author approval for consequential changes.
  • Trace attribution, provenance, rights/permission, consent, privacy, and representation before publication.
  • Test whether essential meaning survives loss of color, image, audio, motion, fine motor precision, or other single access channels.
  • Document corrections and later versions rather than silently erasing publication history.

Evidence Contract

Critical-Thinking Spine

Every design decision is also an interpretive decision.

Interpret

Define audience, purpose, context, content, constraints, ethical boundaries, and what the audience needs to understand.

Design

Choose modes, hierarchy, sequence, typography, imagery, sound, interaction, and accessibility paths for defensible reasons.

Interrogate

Ask what is emphasized or omitted, what assumptions the design embeds, what alternate interpretation exists, and whether framing becomes misleading.

Test

Use real reader/accessibility feedback, proofing, source checks, and alternate-mode inspection rather than relying only on creator intention.

Decide & Account

Publish, revise, hold, decline, correct, or withdraw for documented reasons. Preserve responsibility for the decision after release.
