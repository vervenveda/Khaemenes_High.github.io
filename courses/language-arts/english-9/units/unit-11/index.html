Unit 11

Creative Media & Publication

Plan a publication, study visual rhetoric and editorial design, revise collaboratively, credit sources, and produce a literary magazine or exhibit.

OverviewWeeksAssessmentStandards

Essential Question

How do words, images, sound, layout, and sequence work together for a real audience?

Text and Source Family

Student-selected mentor texts, visual essays, poems, short nonfiction, art criticism, and multimedia compositions.

Learning Priorities

  • Read closely and cite precise textual evidence.
  • Write through planning, drafting, feedback, revision, editing, and reflection.
  • Use language conventions and vocabulary as tools for meaning and style.
  • Discuss, present, listen, question, and respond with intellectual fairness.
  • Preserve major work in the Grade 9 portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: Curated literary magazine, digital exhibit, or themed publication with editorial statement.
