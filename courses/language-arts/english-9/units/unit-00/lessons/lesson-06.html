Unit 0 · Lesson 6

Integrated English I Readiness Lab

Combine the foundations on fresh material before the official Unit 0 exit assessment.

Diagnostic Connection

Review the current NAIB refresh priorities. Strong strands receive confirmation tasks; weaker strands receive one final targeted reteach before integrated practice.

Integrated Practice

Fresh Reading

Main idea, evidence, inference, uncertainty, and one alternative interpretation.

Language

Vocabulary in context plus sentence editing for boundaries, agreement, punctuation, and clarity.

Writing

One concise evidence-based paragraph with claim, evidence, warrant, organization, and revision.

Source Judgment

Identify source/purpose, credibility limits, verification needs, and whether corroboration is independent.

Critical Thinking

State an initial conclusion, identify an assumption, consider a plausible alternative, and explain what would change the conclusion.

Independent Rehearsal

Complete one mixed set without coaching or answer disclosure. Use normal approved accessibility supports. After submission, review strand results and complete reasoning corrections for any missed competency.

Ready for the Exit Assessment?

The rehearsal is preparation, not the official mastery decision. The exit assessment must use fresh material, stable question IDs, attempt records, strand reporting, and the academy-wide 80% + corrections rule.
