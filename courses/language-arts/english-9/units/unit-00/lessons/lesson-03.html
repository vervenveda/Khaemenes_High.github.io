Unit 0 · Lesson 3

Sentence Foundations

Make meaning clear through complete sentences, agreement, tense control, pronoun clarity, punctuation, and deliberate sentence combining.

Diagnostic Connection

Use the NAIB sentence-and-grammar strand to choose a confirmation challenge or full reteach.

Teach / Reteach

Boundaries

Complete sentence, fragment, run-on, comma splice, and correct joining methods.

Control

Subject-verb agreement, consistent verb tense, pronoun agreement and clear antecedents.

Clarity

Punctuation, capitalization, modifier placement, and sentence combining for readable emphasis.

Guided Practice

Repair fragments and run-ons, correct agreement/tense problems, and explain why each revision improves clarity rather than merely naming a rule.

Independent Practice

Edit a fresh paragraph containing mixed sentence-boundary, pronoun, tense, agreement, punctuation, and capitalization issues. Preserve the writer's intended meaning.

Application

Combine three short sentences in two different ways. Explain how syntax changes emphasis or pace.

Mastery Evidence

12 fresh editing/application items plus one explanation. Target ≥80%; reasoning corrections are required on misses before fresh retry.
