Unit 0 · Lesson 4

Paragraphs & Evidence-Based Writing

Build a paragraph that makes one clear claim, supports it with relevant evidence, explains the connection, and revises for coherence.

Diagnostic Connection

Use the NAIB paragraph-and-writing strand to choose a confirmation challenge or full reteach.

Teach / Reteach

Claim

A focused controlling idea narrow enough to support.

Evidence

A detail, example, quotation, fact, or observation that actually bears on the claim.

Explanation

The reasoning that shows how the evidence supports the claim.

Guided Practice

Reorder a scrambled paragraph, remove irrelevant evidence, add a missing explanation, and strengthen transitions. Discuss why each change improves logic.

Independent Practice

Write one evidence-based paragraph from a fresh short passage or prompt. Then perform a revision pass for unity, sequence, support, explanation, and sentence clarity.

Application

Compare an unsupported opinion with a warranted paragraph on the same topic. Identify exactly what makes the second more defensible.

Mastery Evidence

One fresh paragraph plus 8 organization/evidence/revision items. Target ≥80%; below mastery, complete criterion-specific correction and revise before fresh reassessment.
