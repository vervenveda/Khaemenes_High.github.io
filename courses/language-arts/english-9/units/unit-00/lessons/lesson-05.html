Unit 0 · Lesson 5

Evidence, Sources & Critical Thinking

Think before you conclude: separate fact from inference, examine the source, compare evidence, notice assumptions, and communicate only as strongly as the evidence permits.

Diagnostic Connection

Use the NAIB source-judgment and critical-thinking strands to choose confirmation challenge or full reteach.

Teach / Reteach

Claim vs. Evidence

A claim is what someone says is true; evidence is what can support or challenge it.

Source Judgment

Ask who created it, for what purpose, when, with what evidence, and whether another independent source corroborates the claim.

Bias Management

Bias does not automatically make something false. Identify assumptions and incentives, then test the actual evidence.

Guided Practice

Classify statements as observation, fact claim, inference, opinion, or prediction. Match claims to relevant evidence and reject evidence that is merely related to the topic.

Independent Practice

Compare two short source descriptions about the same claim. Identify source/purpose, evidence offered, what remains unknown, and which claim requires verification.

Application

Write one conclusion, one credible alternative explanation, and one piece of evidence that would make you change your mind. Revise overconfident language if necessary.

Mastery Evidence

12 fresh items plus two short reasoning explanations. Target ≥80%; misses require written reasoning corrections before fresh retry.
