Unit 0 · Lesson 2

Vocabulary & Meaning in Context

Use surrounding language, word parts, tone, and sentence relationships to infer meaning responsibly.

Diagnostic Connection

Use the NAIB vocabulary-in-context strand to choose confirmation challenge or full reteach.

Teach / Reteach

Context

Definition, example, contrast, cause/effect, and restatement clues.

Morphology

Roots, prefixes, suffixes, and how word parts constrain plausible meanings.

Nuance

Denotation, connotation, register, and tone.

Guided Practice

Infer six unfamiliar words from context. For each, underline the clue and explain why one alternative meaning does not fit.

Independent Practice

Complete a fresh context-clue set including morphology and connotation. Then revise one sentence by replacing a vague word with a more precise choice and explain the difference.

Application

Take one word whose meaning changes by context and write two sentences demonstrating two different meanings or connotations.

Mastery Evidence

10 fresh items plus one precision explanation. Target ≥80%; missed items require a reasoning correction before fresh retry.
