Unit 0 · Lesson 1

Read, Observe & Understand

Build the habit that powers the whole course: notice first, infer second, explain why.

Diagnostic Connection

Review the NAIB readiness profile for reading comprehension and observation/evidence/inference. Strong strands may begin with the confirmation challenge; developing strands complete the full sequence.

Teach / Reteach

Observation

What the passage directly states or clearly shows.

Inference

A conclusion drawn from evidence plus reasoning.

Warrant

The explanation connecting the evidence to the inference.

Guided Practice

Read a short age-appropriate passage. Mark five direct observations. Turn two into inferences and write the warrant for each. Then identify one tempting conclusion the passage does not justify.

Independent Practice

On a fresh passage, identify main idea, two supporting details, sequence or relationship, one defensible inference, one piece of evidence, and one uncertainty.

Application

Explain one real-life situation where speaking too quickly could confuse observation with assumption. Rewrite the conclusion in language proportional to the evidence.

Mastery Evidence

Confirmation/mastery check: 10 fresh items plus one short explanation. Target: ≥80%. Below mastery, correct each missed item by stating the correct reasoning before retrying with fresh items.
