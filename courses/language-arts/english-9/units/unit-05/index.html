Unit 05

Drama, Choice & Consequence

Read drama as literature and performance, examining dramatic structure, stagecraft, character motivation, conflict, and interpretation.

Begin Week 13Unit OverviewAssessments
OverviewWeeksAssessmentStandards

Essential Question

How does drama place competing duties, values, and consequences into public view?

Text and Source Family

Sophocles’ Antigone or Shakespeare’s Julius Caesar, with shorter scenes from world drama.

Learning Priorities

  • Read closely and cite precise textual evidence.
  • Write through planning, drafting, feedback, revision, editing, and reflection.
  • Use language conventions and vocabulary as tools for meaning and style.
  • Discuss, present, listen, question, and respond with intellectual fairness.
  • Preserve major work in the Grade 9 portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: Literary analysis, Socratic seminar, and staged interpretation.
