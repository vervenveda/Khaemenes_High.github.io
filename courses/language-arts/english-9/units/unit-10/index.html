Unit 10

Argument, Debate & Civic Reasoning

Frame issues, distinguish claims from assumptions, test evidence, address counterclaims, rebut respectfully, debate, and revise position papers.

Begin Week 28Unit OverviewAssessments
OverviewWeeksAssessmentStandards

Essential Question

What makes an argument fair, rigorous, responsive, and persuasive?

Text and Source Family

Paired arguments, policy excerpts, civic documents, editorials, and evidence sets selected for balance and source transparency.

Learning Priorities

  • Read closely and cite precise textual evidence.
  • Write through planning, drafting, feedback, revision, editing, and reflection.
  • Use language conventions and vocabulary as tools for meaning and style.
  • Discuss, present, listen, question, and respond with intellectual fairness.
  • Preserve major work in the Grade 9 portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: 1,200–1,600-word position paper and structured debate.
