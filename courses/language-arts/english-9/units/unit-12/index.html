Unit 12 · Low-Load Transition

Reflection, Portfolio & Breathing Room

No new anchor book. No three-week book review. The reading load fades out here so students can organize what they already know, revisit work they care about, rest cognitively, and prepare calmly for the final capstone.

Course PortalEnter Unit 12Preview Capstone

Fade-Out Design

Consolidate. Reflect. Choose. Breathe.

Unit 12 deliberately reduces cognitive load. Students are not asked to absorb another classic or produce another major literary review. They revisit prior work and notice how their thinking changed across the year.

Portfolio Tidy

Select representative work, attach missing provenance/revision evidence, and organize artifacts for easy retrieval. No decorative scrapbook requirement.

Reasoning Retrospective

Compare an early claim with a later one. Identify growth in observation, evidence, warrants, counterreading, bias management, qualification, revision, or communication.

Choice & Recovery

Optional independent reading, ARSHIF browsing, vocabulary games, creative writing, rereading, or quiet catch-up may be offered without creating another required book review.

Capstone Familiarization

Students see the format of Unit 13 in advance: 50 mixed questions plus the separate final exam. They may review prior materials but receive no capstone answers.

What Unit 12 Is Not

The course should feel lighter now.

No required anchor classic · no new three-week book review · no major new research project · no artificial busywork · no extra mastery shortcut. Existing accommodations and opportunities for corrective learning remain available.

Transition Reflection

Think about the thinking.

Progression

Unit 13 is assessment-only.

After Unit 12's transition work is complete, students move into the capstone. Unit 13 contains no new lessons: it is reserved for cumulative demonstration and the final exam.
