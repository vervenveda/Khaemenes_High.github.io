Essential Question

How should an interpretation change as evidence accumulates across an entire novel?

Text Family

One teacher-approved full novel, with lawful and accessible selections chosen for student readiness and instructional purpose.

Learning Priorities

  • Sustain reading while preserving questions, evidence, vocabulary, uncertainty, and evolving interpretations.
  • Track character decisions, motifs, conflicts, symbols, setting, narration, chronology, and thematic development across separated passages.
  • Distinguish coincidence, correlation, motivation, and defensible causal interpretation when discussing character behavior and events.
  • Search deliberately for anomalies and passages a preferred interpretation struggles to explain.
  • Generate competing interpretations and test each against supporting and disconfirming evidence.
  • Use context to clarify or constrain claims without allowing context to dictate the reading.
  • Use critical perspectives as question sets rather than predetermined answers.
  • Revise or abandon hypotheses when later evidence warrants it.
  • Defend reading knowledge independently and transfer reasoning to fresh passages.

Evidence Contract

Critical-Thinking Spine

Hypothesize → accumulate → challenge → revise → defend → transfer.

Build a Provisional Reading

State what you currently think the novel is doing and identify the evidence and assumptions supporting that interpretation.

Accumulate Longitudinal Evidence

Track recurrence, change, contradiction, chronology, silence, narrative choice, and consequences across the whole work.

Search for Trouble

Find the strongest counterreading and the passage your interpretation has the hardest time explaining. Do not hide anomalies.

Revise the Hypothesis

Decide whether new evidence calls for rebuttal, qualification, narrower scope, reframing, or abandonment of the earlier interpretation.

Transfer

Meet fresh text without a prepared answer. Generate alternatives, test assumptions, and explain how new evidence affects the larger interpretation.
