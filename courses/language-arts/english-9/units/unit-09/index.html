Unit 09

The Novel & Sustained Inquiry

Sustain independent reading, track motifs and character arcs, build contextual knowledge, compare interpretations, and write a developed analysis.

Begin Week 25Unit OverviewAssessments
OverviewWeeksAssessmentStandards

Essential Question

What becomes visible when a reader follows characters, patterns, and ideas across an entire book?

Text and Source Family

One teacher-approved full novel, with public-domain choices including Frankenstein, Jane Eyre, The Time Machine, or The Call of the Wild.

Learning Priorities

  • Read closely and cite precise textual evidence.
  • Write through planning, drafting, feedback, revision, editing, and reflection.
  • Use language conventions and vocabulary as tools for meaning and style.
  • Discuss, present, listen, question, and respond with intellectual fairness.
  • Preserve major work in the Grade 9 portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: 1,200–1,500-word literary analysis and independent reading conference.
