Unit 02

Myth, Epic & Archetype

Compare origin stories, heroic patterns, archetypes, cultural values, and transformations of source material across civilizations.

Begin Week 4Unit OverviewAssessments
OverviewWeeksAssessmentStandards

Essential Question

How do cultures use stories to explain duty, identity, power, and the unknown?

Text and Source Family

The Epic of Gilgamesh, The Odyssey, Popol Vuh, and teacher-selected passages from African, Asian, Indigenous, and Near Eastern traditions.

Learning Priorities

  • Read closely and cite precise textual evidence.
  • Write through planning, drafting, feedback, revision, editing, and reflection.
  • Use language conventions and vocabulary as tools for meaning and style.
  • Discuss, present, listen, question, and respond with intellectual fairness.
  • Preserve major work in the Grade 9 portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: Comparative literary analysis with a visual archetype map.
