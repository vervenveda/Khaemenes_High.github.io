Unit 02

Myth, Epic & Archetype

Compare origin stories, heroic patterns, archetypes, cultural values, and transformations of source material across traditions while preserving context and meaningful difference.

Begin Week 4Unit OverviewMastery Gate
OverviewWeeksAssessmentMasteryStandards

Essential Question

How do cultures use stories to explain duty, identity, power, and the unknown?

Text and Source Family

The Epic of Gilgamesh, The Odyssey, Popol Vuh, and teacher-selected passages from African, Asian, Indigenous, and Near Eastern traditions.

Learning Priorities

  • Interpret myth and epic through precise textual evidence.
  • Analyze archetype, motif, symbol, transformation, and cultural value without flattening traditions into sameness.
  • Compare texts through meaningful criteria, counterexamples, context, and warrants.
  • Use language conventions and vocabulary as tools for analytical precision.
  • Preserve major work and revision evidence in the Grade 9 portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: Comparative literary analysis with a visual archetype map.

Mastery: 80% minimum on defined scored evidence.
