Unit 13

Show what you can carry with you.

The teaching load has faded out. Unit 13 introduces no new reading assignment, book review, research project, vocabulary list, or daily lessons. Students complete a 50-question mixed-format cumulative capstone and the separate English I final examination.

50-Question Mixed Capstone

Recall, application, judgment, and moral reasoning.

The capstone samples the year's core skills without becoming another instructional unit. Questions should be cumulative, balanced, age-appropriate, and based on skills students have already practiced.

20 questions

Multiple Choice

Reading comprehension, grammar/language, vocabulary in context, research/source literacy, rhetoric, reasoning, citation/provenance, and interpretation.

15 questions

Fill in the Blank / Short Completion

Terminology and concise application: inference, warrant, provenance, corroboration, synthesis, bias, counterclaim, qualification, citation, literary craft, and other taught concepts.

15 responses

Moral & Critical Explanation

Short constructed responses using compact scenarios or familiar literary dilemmas. Students explain a judgment with evidence/reasoning, identify another defensible perspective, distinguish fact from inference, and state uncertainty where appropriate. No teacher-approved ideology or predetermined moral is required.

Moral Explanation Standard

Score the reasoning—not agreement with the conclusion.

A response earns credit for accurately observing the situation, explaining the reasoning, using relevant evidence, recognizing assumptions or consequences, considering a credible alternative perspective, and communicating a proportionate conclusion. Religious, political, cultural, philosophical, or personal agreement with the evaluator cannot determine the score.

Final Examination

The final remains separate from the 50-question capstone.

The capstone provides a broad mixed-format demonstration; the final examination remains the course's formal cumulative assessment. No new material is introduced between them.

Student Load Protection

Finish strong without piling on.

Unit 13 contains assessment sessions, reasonable breaks, accommodations, corrective/reassessment routes where the course contract permits them, and quiet portfolio closure. There are no ordinary lesson pages to complete simply to fill time.
