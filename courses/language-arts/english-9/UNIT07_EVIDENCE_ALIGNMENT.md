# English 9 · Unit 07 Evidence Alignment
## Research, Evidence & Media Literacy

**Upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Unit 07 propagation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade-level rhythm

**Ask → Search → Investigate → Synthesize → Document**

Grade 9 makes the research process explicit: learners refine questions, investigate sources laterally, distinguish evidence from presentation, synthesize across sources, and document provenance.

## Three-week architecture

### Week 19 · Research Questions & Search Strategy
1. **Ask:** Turn a broad topic into a focused question that is significant, answerable, and narrow enough for the available time and source set.
2. **Search:** Generate search terms using synonyms, narrower concepts, broader concepts, dates, institutions, and exact phrases rather than repeating one query.
3. **Investigate:** Compare search results by source type and likely usefulness before opening them. Explain why one result deserves priority.
4. **Refine:** Revise the research question after early searching reveals that it is too broad, too narrow, already answered, or based on a weak assumption.
5. **Document:** Record the final question, search strategy, strongest early source, and one information gap that still needs investigation.

### Week 20 · Credibility, Bias & Lateral Reading
1. **Investigate:** Identify who created the source, when, where, for what purpose, and what evidence it claims to rely on.
2. **Lateral Read:** Leave the source and investigate the author, organization, publication, or claim using independent sources.
3. **Compare:** Compare how multiple sources describe the same event or claim. Separate factual agreement from differences in framing or emphasis.
4. **Test:** Identify one credibility concern, bias risk, missing context, or unsupported assertion and determine whether it weakens the whole source or only part of it.
5. **Document:** Complete a source audit with credibility strengths, limitations, corroborating evidence, and a justified decision about how the source may be used.

### Week 21 · Synthesis, Citation & Research Reporting
1. **Gather:** Group evidence by idea or relationship rather than by source order. Mark where sources agree, extend, qualify, or conflict.
2. **Synthesize:** Draft a paragraph that combines evidence from multiple sources to answer one part of the research question.
3. **Attribute:** Practice signal phrases, paraphrase, quotation, and citation so the reader can distinguish your reasoning from source material.
4. **Revise:** Audit for source dumping, patchwriting, unsupported synthesis, and missing attribution. Revise one weak passage.
5. **Document:** Submit a research evidence statement identifying the strongest conclusion, strongest source, unresolved question, and complete source trail.

## Spacing and retrieval

The Unit 07 evidence layer uses a 15-day map across Weeks 19–21. Retrieval deliberately returns to:
- prior-unit evidence, synthesis, interpretation, rhetoric, research, and qualification habits;
- prior-day concepts;
- approximately one-week-delayed concepts;
- earlier Unit 07 methods;
- a cumulative research, comparative, contextual, translation, or interpretation audit on the final instructional day.

Each prompt is tied to the actual intellectual work of the day rather than a generic recall routine.

## Reading/source custody

Required reading remains in the existing unit/week pages, reading dossier, locally embedded Academy texts, approved authentic works, and approved research sources. Exact source, author, publication date, edition, translator, archive/database location, historical context, and provenance remain required where applicable.

## Mastery boundary

The Unit 07 evidence layer:
- writes no learner data;
- changes no storage keys;
- changes no quiz answers or thresholds;
- changes no assessment authority;
- does not convert completion into mastery;
- adds retrieval, research, lateral reading, ambiguity testing, contextualization, cross-cultural comparison, translation/intertextual analysis, synthesis, and exit-audit prompts only.

## Release criterion

Unit 07 is ready when:
1. all 15 instructional days receive the grade-specific evidence layer;
2. the grade-level scaffold fade is preserved;
3. authentic-source, translation, and research custody remain visible;
4. no duplicate generic prompts appear;
5. JavaScript parses cleanly;
6. storage/assessment authority remains unchanged;
7. the unit landing page communicates the learning progression.
