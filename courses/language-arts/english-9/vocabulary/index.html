Daily Routine

Five minutes, one word, real intellectual work.

1 · Hear & Define

Listen to the pronunciation. Read the definition and simple definition. State the meaning in your own words without merely repeating the app.

2 · Inspect

Notice part of speech, morphology, origin, synonyms, register, and connotation. Ask where a near-synonym would change meaning.

3 · Apply

Use the word accurately in the day's Language Arts work: analysis, research, discussion, narrative, argument, technical explanation, editing, or reflection.

4 · Challenge

Test whether the word truly improves precision. If it adds only decoration or pretension, replace it. Explain why your final word choice is stronger.

5 · Retain

Complete LearnA's quick quiz and mark the word learned when appropriate. Reuse prior words naturally when they improve later writing or speaking.

Critical Thinking & Vocabulary

Vocabulary is not a contest to sound sophisticated.

A word earns its place when it improves accuracy, nuance, reasoning, tone, or audience comprehension. Students should be able to explain what distinction the chosen word makes and when a simpler word would actually be better.

Daily vocabulary is formative enrichment. Completing LearnA, earning favorites, marking words learned, or completing the one-question app quiz does not satisfy or bypass any 80% course mastery gate.

Mentor Integration

Eiren can become a precision examiner.

After using the daily word, ask Eiren to challenge whether the usage is accurate, natural, appropriately formal, and meaningfully more precise than a likely alternative. Eiren may question the choice but should not replace the student's sentence.

The vocabulary pathway remains public and low-risk.
