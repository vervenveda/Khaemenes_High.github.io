Course Rhythm

Build deeply, then fade the workload responsibly.

Units 1–11 · Instruction & Reading

Daily language, literature, research, writing, speaking/listening, critical thinking, Eiren mentorship, and integrated Reading Hall investigations.

Unit 12 · Reflection & Breathing Room

No new anchor classic or three-week book review. Students organize portfolio evidence, revisit selected skills, reflect on growth, and prepare calmly.

Unit 13 · Assessment Only

No lessons or new material. Complete the 50-question mixed capstone, then the separate comprehensive final examination. Neither assessment substitutes for the other.

Open Capstone Center
End-of-course mastery boundary: Unit 13's 50-question capstone and the final examination are separate cumulative demonstrations. A strong score on one does not erase failure on the other. Reading completion, tool use, games, Eiren interaction, portfolio decoration, or completion clicks cannot bypass the applicable 80% mastery requirements.
