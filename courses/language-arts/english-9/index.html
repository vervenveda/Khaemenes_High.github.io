About English 9

English 9 develops students as careful readers, purposeful writers, thoughtful speakers, and evidence-based thinkers. The year spirals literature, informational text, vocabulary, conventions, research, discussion, media literacy, revision, and portfolio reflection across a complete high-school Language Arts sequence.

Course Features

Close Reading & Evidence Students annotate, infer, compare interpretations, cite precise evidence, and explain how literary and informational details create meaning.
Writing & Revision Analytical, narrative, explanatory, argumentative, research, and multimodal writing develop through planning, drafting, feedback, editing, and revision.
Research & Communication Source credibility, synthesis, citation, discussion, speech, debate, media analysis, and presentation strengthen academic communication.
Mastery & Portfolio Weekly evidence, vocabulary, quizzes, reflection, revision cycles, and preserved work build toward cumulative mastery and a final portfolio.
