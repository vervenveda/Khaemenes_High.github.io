National Benchmarking

Standards Crosswalk

A complete floor with an advanced ceiling.

The course covers the Grade 9–10 Common Core domains and NCTE/IRA national standards, then extends toward ACT upper-band language expectations, college-level rhetorical analysis, digital research, publication, and oral defense.

Teacher Guide

Competency Architecture

Twelve integrated competencies.

Competency 01

Close Reading

Cite evidence, infer, analyze development, interpret language, structure, perspective, and transformation.

Competency 02

Literary Knowledge

Read fiction, poetry, drama, myth, epic, and a full novel across cultures and periods.

Competency 03

Informational Reading

Analyze central ideas, reasoning, evidence, rhetoric, visuals, and source relationships.

Competency 04

Argument Writing

Develop precise claims, logical organization, relevant evidence, counterclaims, rebuttal, and responsible style.

Competency 05

Informative Writing

Explain complex ideas through accurate structure, examples, definitions, data, visuals, and precise language.

Competency 06

Narrative Writing

Create purposeful narratives using character, structure, pacing, dialogue, detail, and reflection.

Competency 07

Research and Citation

Ask significant questions, search strategically, evaluate credibility, synthesize sources, and cite ethically.

Competency 08

Speaking and Listening

Discuss, question, collaborate, perform, debate, present, listen, and defend ideas with evidence.

Competency 09

Language and Style

Control conventions, syntax, vocabulary, register, tone, rhythm, cohesion, and purposeful variation.

Competency 10

Media and Digital Literacy

Evaluate platforms and visuals, verify claims, design accessible media, and understand rights and attribution.

Competency 11

Creative Publication

Compose, edit, curate, design, proof, credit, and publish work for authentic audiences.

Competency 12

Independence and Reflection

Set goals, sustain reading, revise from feedback, curate evidence, transfer learning, and defend growth.
