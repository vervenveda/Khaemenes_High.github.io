Performance Rubric

Speaking & Listening Rubric

Four levels · Five criteria · Observable evidence

Criterion4 · Advanced3 · Proficient2 · Developing1 · Beginning
Content & EvidenceCommunicates a precise purpose/claim with accurate, well-chosen evidence and explains the reasoning or significance clearly for listeners.Communicates a clear purpose/claim with relevant evidence and generally sufficient explanation.Purpose is partly clear but evidence, explanation, accuracy, or focus is uneven.Purpose is unclear or unsupported; evidence is missing, inaccurate, or largely unrelated.
OrganizationUses a deliberate opening, logical progression, listener signposts, transitions, and conclusion so the audience can follow the reasoning without rereading.Uses a clear beginning, sequence, transitions, and ending that make the presentation easy to follow.Organization is partly effective but transitions, sequencing, or signposting may leave listeners uncertain.Ideas are difficult to follow because structure, sequencing, or transitions are missing or confusing.
Delivery / Accessible CommunicationUses pace, pause, emphasis, articulation, volume, gesture/movement, visual/audio supports, or approved accessible equivalents purposefully to clarify meaning.Delivery is understandable and generally controlled; choices support communication with only minor lapses.Delivery is sometimes difficult to follow because pace, volume, articulation, supports, or rehearsal choices are inconsistent.Communication is frequently inaccessible or unclear because essential delivery/support choices are not yet controlled.
Audience AwarenessAdapts context, evidence, terminology, tone, examples, and emphasis to the actual audience without stereotyping or oversimplifying.Uses language, explanation, tone, and detail appropriate to the intended audience and purpose.Shows some audience awareness, but assumes too much/too little knowledge or uses inconsistent tone/detail.Shows little adaptation to audience or purpose; language/supports substantially limit audience access.
Listening & ResponseAccurately represents others' ideas, asks/answers substantive questions, uses evidence in response, and revises or qualifies a position when warranted.Listens accurately, responds to relevant questions or claims, and uses understandable reasoning in discussion.Responds to some ideas but may miss key points, repeat prepared material, or provide weakly connected answers.Does not demonstrate accurate listening or responds primarily to a different claim/question than the one presented.
Scoring: 20 points possible. A score of 16/20 equals 80% when this rubric is the defined authoritative evidence. Accent, disability, charisma, memorization style, eye contact conventions, or theatricality may not be used as substitutes for the actual communication competencies. Valid accommodations and alternate response modes preserve the target skill.
