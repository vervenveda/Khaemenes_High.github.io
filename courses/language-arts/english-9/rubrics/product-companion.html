Authentic Assessment

English I Product Companion Rubrics

Use these product-specific criteria beside the existing literary analysis, argument, research, speaking, and narrative rubrics. They sharpen evaluator attention without changing the 80% mastery threshold, assessment authority, or existing score records.

How to use: score the established mode rubric as usual. Then use the four companion criteria for targeted feedback and evidence review. A companion criterion does not independently award mastery and never converts completion into mastery.

Unit 01

Foundation portfolio

  • Evidence accuracy
  • Revision quality
  • Conventions applied in context
  • Reflection names a specific next step

Unit 02

Comparative myth synthesis

  • Comparison across traditions
  • Archetype map accuracy
  • Evidence-to-claim reasoning
  • Cultural context handled responsibly

Unit 03

Narrative craft

  • Narrative coherence
  • Craft choices are purposeful
  • Revision changes meaning or effect
  • Craft commentary explains decisions

Unit 04

Poetry portfolio & reading

  • Image/diction/form control
  • Comparison or translation insight
  • Oral delivery supports meaning
  • Portfolio revision is documented

Unit 05

Drama essay, seminar & performance

  • Textual interpretation
  • Seminar listening and response
  • Staging choices supported by text
  • Alternative interpretations acknowledged

Unit 06

Evidence-based speech

  • Rhetorical situation
  • Evidence and reasoning
  • Delivery and audience awareness
  • Reflection evaluates rhetorical choices

Unit 07

Research report

  • Question and search strategy
  • Source credibility and provenance
  • Synthesis rather than source stacking
  • Citation and attribution accuracy

Unit 08

Article, procedure & infographic

  • Explanation accuracy
  • Procedure is reproducible
  • Visual/data integrity
  • Accessibility and alt-text quality

Unit 09

Novel analysis & conference

  • Sustained pattern tracking
  • Interpretive complexity
  • Evidence across the whole work
  • Conference explanation shows independent reading

Unit 10

Position paper & debate

  • Fair issue framing
  • Evidence sufficiency
  • Counterclaim/rebuttal quality
  • Debate conduct and revision after opposition

Unit 11

Publication

  • Audience and editorial purpose
  • Editing and visual hierarchy
  • Rights/credits/accessibility
  • Publication readiness and editorial statement

Unit 12

Portfolio, capstone & defense

  • Artifact selection shows growth
  • Capstone synthesis across modes
  • Process record and revision history
  • Oral defense explains evidence of mastery
