# English 9 · Unit 03 Evidence Alignment
## Short Fiction & Narrative Craft

**Upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Unit 03 propagation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade-level rhythm

**Recall → Interpret → Compare → Revise → Synthesize**

Grade 9 keeps explicit evidence prompts, names the craft move being tested, and asks learners to connect interpretation to their own narrative decisions.

## Three-week architecture

### Week 7 · Character, Conflict & Setting
1. **Retrieve:** Name one Unit 02 evidence habit that can help you distinguish a claim about character from a guess. Then identify one observable detail that could support a character inference.
2. **Interpret:** Select one action, line of dialogue, or setting detail. Explain what it suggests about motivation or conflict and identify what remains uncertain.
3. **Compare:** Compare two details that point in different directions. Which interpretation is better supported, and what evidence keeps the weaker interpretation plausible?
4. **Transfer:** Revise a short scene so setting changes the pressure on the character without directly stating the emotion. Mark the craft choice that carries the inference.
5. **Audit:** State your strongest claim about character or conflict, cite the best evidence, and name one alternative explanation a careful reader should still consider.

### Week 8 · Point of View, Structure & Irony
1. **Retrieve:** Recall one character inference from Week 7. Explain how a different narrator could make the same evidence feel more or less trustworthy.
2. **Interpret:** Identify a passage where point of view controls what the reader knows. Separate what happened from what the narrator believes happened.
3. **Compare:** Compare two structural choices—such as chronological order, flashback, foreshadowing, or withheld information. Which more strongly shapes suspense or irony?
4. **Transfer:** Rewrite a brief moment from another point of view. Explain which facts stayed constant and which interpretations changed.
5. **Audit:** Make one claim about narrator reliability or irony. Support it with two details and identify what evidence would weaken your claim.

### Week 9 · Narrative Workshop
1. **Retrieve:** List two craft moves from Weeks 7–8 that you can deliberately use in your own story. For each, name the reader effect you intend.
2. **Plan:** Choose a central conflict and identify the scene evidence that will let a reader infer it rather than receive an explanation.
3. **Draft:** Draft one scene using purposeful dialogue, pacing, and sensory detail. Annotate where each choice advances character, conflict, or mood.
4. **Revise:** Use peer or self-review evidence to identify one passage that is unclear or over-explained. Revise it and explain why the revision is stronger.
5. **Synthesize:** Submit a craft commentary that makes one claim about your story’s effectiveness, cites specific passages, and names one next revision you would make.

## Spacing and retrieval

The Unit 03 evidence layer uses a 15-day map across Weeks 7–9. Retrieval deliberately returns to:
- Unit 01–02 evidence, qualification, and comparison habits;
- prior-day concepts;
- approximately one-week-delayed concepts;
- earlier Unit 03 methods;
- a cumulative Unit 03 transfer, defense, or audit task on the final instructional day.

Each prompt is tied to the actual intellectual work of the day rather than a generic recall routine.

## Reading/source custody

Required reading remains in the existing unit/week pages, reading dossier, locally embedded Academy texts, and approved authentic works. Course-created practice texts remain clearly identified as instructional examples. Exact source, edition, translator, and provenance records remain required where applicable.

## Mastery boundary

The Unit 03 evidence layer:
- writes no learner data;
- changes no storage keys;
- changes no quiz answers or thresholds;
- changes no assessment authority;
- does not convert completion into mastery;
- adds retrieval, transfer, counterreading, qualification, revision, and exit-audit prompts only.

## Release criterion

Unit 03 is ready when:
1. all 15 instructional days receive the grade-specific retrieval/transfer layer;
2. the grade-level scaffold fade is preserved;
3. authentic-source custody remains visible;
4. no duplicate generic prompts appear;
5. JavaScript parses cleanly;
6. storage/assessment authority remains unchanged;
