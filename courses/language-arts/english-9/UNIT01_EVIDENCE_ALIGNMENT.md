# English 9 · Unit 01 Evidence Alignment
## Foundations of Scholarly Reading & Writing

**Audit/upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Canonical Unit 01 laboratory for the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Why Unit 01 is preserved

The existing fifteen-day sequence already contains the strongest ingredients identified in the Academy research standard: local texts, explicit modeling, guided practice, independent writing, formative feedback, revision, transfer, learner evidence, and an evaluator-reviewed culminating demonstration. The upgrade therefore reorganizes and connects the existing instruction rather than replacing it.

## Reading custody

All core instructional texts in this unit are local Khaemenes Academy course texts and remain available without depending on an external reading service.

| 5 | *The Unanswered Question* | Unfamiliar Week 1 transfer |

Optional videos remain reinforcement only. The written/local lesson remains authoritative whenever captions, transcript, provenance, or availability is not fully verified.

## Fifteen-day spacing map

Retrieval prompts are deliberately cumulative rather than generic.

1. **Day 1:** prequestion — distinguish what is stated from what is inferred.
2. **Day 2:** retrieve Day 1 observation/inference distinction before rereading.
3. **Day 3:** retrieve Day 1–2 pattern finding; decide what annotation should record.
4. **Day 4:** retrieve observation → inference → evidence; transform into a defensible claim.
5. **Day 5:** closed-notes Week 1 transfer to a new text.
6. **Day 6:** retrieve Week 1 inference/claim distinctions after a delay; build a claim ladder.
7. **Day 7:** retrieve Day 4/6 claim quality; add evidence and warrant.
8. **Day 8:** retrieve paragraph architecture; integrate quotation without dropping evidence into prose.
9. **Day 9:** retrieve claim/evidence/warrant; revise for precision rather than surface correction.
10. **Day 10:** closed-notes Week 2 transfer using an unfamiliar text.
11. **Day 11:** retrieve Week 1 observation/inference + Week 2 defensible claims; generate evidence-demanding questions.
12. **Day 12:** retrieve claim/evidence/warrant and compare two defensible interpretations.
13. **Day 13:** retrieve interpretation comparison; accurately paraphrase another speaker before responding.
14. **Day 14:** retrieve evidence selection and fair comparison; synthesize two texts into a new conclusion.
15. **Day 15:** cumulative closed-notes preflight followed by independent paired-text demonstration and evaluator review.

## Feedback architecture

- Objective formative checks provide immediate item-level feedback.
- Written analysis is not auto-graded by completion controls.
- Revision asks what changed and why, not merely whether editing occurred.
- Day 15 preserves the independent draft separately from the revised final.
- Evaluator review remains the authority for Unit 01 mastery.

## Page organization

Daily lessons now receive a Unit 01 research layer that:
- places a concise **Retrieve Before Looking** card near the top of the lesson;
- states the delayed skill being brought forward;
- identifies the day's transfer target;
- adds an **Exit Retrieval** card before evidence completion;
- keeps reading width controlled and support optional;
- does not write learner data, alter quiz answers, or award mastery.

## Remaining content-level work after this laboratory

1. Build annotated contrasting mentor examples for the most demanding writing moves.
2. Add explicit delayed retrieval again in Unit 02 and later units so Unit 01 knowledge continues to recur.
3. Complete optional-media caption/transcript verification.
4. Use the same custody/spacing/evidence audit format for English 10–12 after Unit 01 validation.
