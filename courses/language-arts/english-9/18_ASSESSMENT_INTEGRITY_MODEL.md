# English 9 Assessment Integrity Model

Branch: `audit/language-arts-a-plus-plus-plus`

## Purpose

English 9 is intentionally public and free. A public static GitHub Pages course cannot honestly promise hidden client-side answer keys or tamper-resistant local mastery records. The assessment system therefore separates **practice**, **released forms**, **human-reviewed performance evidence**, and **verified mastery decisions**.

## Four evidence classes

### 1. Practice
Low-stakes checks, games, self-checks, mentor rehearsal, drafting tools, and weekly evidence records.

Practice may provide immediate feedback. Practice completion never unlocks a mastery gate by itself.

### 2. Released public forms
The current diagnostic, midterm, and final HTML forms have answer keys present in public Git history. They are therefore considered **released/open forms**.

They remain useful for:
- practice
- familiarization
- self-study
- teacher modeling
- accessibility testing
- formative review

They must not be represented as secure hidden-key high-stakes examinations.

### 3. Human-reviewed performance evidence
Writing, research, speech, debate, portfolio, oral defense, projects, and complex reasoning are scored with criterion-specific rubrics and retained evidence.

Human review remains authoritative for these competencies.

### 4. Verified mastery evidence
A unit or course mastery decision may combine objective evidence and human-reviewed performance evidence according to the defined unit assessment plan. The verified result is recorded at 80% or higher.

A browser/localStorage record is a convenience record, not tamper-resistant institutional proof.

## Public static assessment rule

Do not place any answer key in public HTML/JS and then describe that form as secure or hidden-key.

For future canonical objective forms, use one of these models:

1. educator-administered fresh form not published with its key;
2. authorized non-public scoring service;
3. generated fresh-transfer form with human verification;
4. open-book/open-source task where secrecy is not part of validity.

## Diagnostic

The diagnostic is formative. It routes instruction and never permanently labels or blocks the learner.

Required output:
- strengths
- priority skills
- recommended refresh work
- challenge options
- learner growth goal

## Midterm

The released public midterm is a practice/review form. A verified midyear mastery decision should use fresh or meaningfully changed evidence across Units 1–6 and should include human-reviewed writing/performance evidence.

## Final

The released public final is a practice/review form. Final course mastery must include independent transfer with unfamiliar or meaningfully changed material, portfolio evidence, human-reviewed writing/complex reasoning, and oral defense as defined by Unit 12.

## Retakes

- Corrective learning targets the failed skill, not the entire unit by default.
- Reassessment uses fresh or meaningfully changed evidence where memorization could invalidate the result.
- First, latest, and best score are retained.
- Later lower attempts do not erase established mastery.
- Attempts and mastered-at timestamp are preserved locally where supported.

## AI / mentor boundary

Eiren and other tools may support preparation, questioning, rehearsal, revision, and corrective learning when permitted.

They may not:
- write assessed work for the learner;
- fabricate quotations, evidence, or citations;
- supply protected answers;
- assign a verified score;
- alter mastery records;
- participate in independently scored portions unless explicitly allowed as an accommodation/support.

## Release criterion

No English 9 assessment should be labeled canonical/secure/high-stakes unless its validity, authority, scoring path, accessibility, corrective route, and public exposure model are explicitly documented.
