Connected tools and practice

Open a companion in a new tab so this lesson remains available. These optional tools may need internet access; all required core work can be completed in the lesson or on paper. First complete the lesson task, then use practice deliberately.

Apps do not automatically share learner records or award course mastery. Save or export your work in each app and return relevant evidence to the weekly notebook. Avoid entering private account or family information in a companion tool.

PROSE Writer’s Studio

Bring a working claim and two cited details. Draft, revise and export your own writing. Copy a before/after passage back into the weekly notebook.

Open PROSE Writer’s Studio in a new tab

Evidence & Citation Studio

Bring the source title, creator, date and locator. Record claim, evidence and limitation. Return the citation and one sentence explaining what the source cannot establish.

Open Evidence & Citation Studio in a new tab

AffixSix vocabulary game

Practice word parts. Return three words, their useful parts, provisional meanings and original sentences. Verify meanings against the lesson context; a game score is practice, not mastery.

Open AffixSix vocabulary game in a new tab

Learn a New Word

Practice vocabulary retrieval. Return one word, its meaning, a context sentence and a sentence of your own. Explain how context changes meaning.

Open Learn a New Word in a new tab
