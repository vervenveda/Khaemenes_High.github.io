# Student Mastery Panel

## Student sees
- Current unit
- Skills mastered
- Skills in progress
- Recommended correction
- Reassessment available
- Best demonstrated score
- Growth goal
- Challenge available

## Never expose
- punitive language
- permanent labels
- hidden ranking
- raw teacher-only notes

## Suggested status language
**Mastered**
You demonstrated this skill at the course mastery standard.

**Keep Practicing**
You are close. Try the targeted practice below.

**Correction Ready**
Review the short lesson, then reassess.

**Challenge Ready**
You have demonstrated mastery. Try the advanced transfer task.
