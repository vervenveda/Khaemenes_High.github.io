# Khaemenes English 9 - Global A+++ Standard

## Promise

Khaemenes English 9 is free universal education. It is designed for learners in varied countries, cultures, school structures, home-education settings, and language backgrounds. It does not belong to one state or one national examination system.

The course will exceed the shared academic expectations of major Grade 9 / upper-secondary foundation frameworks while remaining adaptable to local requirements. Jurisdictional recognition remains the decision of the relevant school, ministry, district, examination authority, or home-education authority.

## Graduate performance profile

By completion, a successful learner will be able to:

1. comprehend and evaluate complex literary, informational, visual, spoken, and multimodal texts;
2. distinguish observation, inference, interpretation, evaluation, and judgment;
3. support claims with precise evidence and explicit reasoning;
4. compare texts across cultures, periods, genres, media, and translations without flattening meaningful differences;
5. write controlled analytical, argumentative, informative, narrative, reflective, and creative texts for authentic audiences;
6. plan, draft, receive feedback, revise substantially, edit accurately, publish, and reflect;
7. listen closely, discuss fairly, present formally, answer questions, debate responsibly, and defend a body of work;
8. conduct ethical inquiry using multiple reliable sources, lateral reading, synthesis, citation, and transparent use of tools;
9. analyze rhetoric, bias, representation, visual design, algorithmic influence, and media construction;
10. use vocabulary, grammar, syntax, punctuation, voice, register, and style deliberately;
11. mediate ideas between texts, people, modes, and—where the learner can—languages;
12. curate a portfolio that demonstrates growth, mastery, independence, and intellectual integrity.

## Multilingual access contract

### Additive, never subtractive

The learner’s home language is an intellectual resource. The course may use it for directions, prior-knowledge activation, vocabulary bridges, planning, discussion, annotation, mediation, family participation, and comparison. Learning English must not require abandoning another language.

### English mastery remains visible

This is an English Language Arts course. Final English mastery evidence must include substantial reading, writing, speaking/listening or signed interaction, and presentation in English. Home-language supports clarify access; they do not disguise which language performance was assessed.

### Translation integrity

- Every translated page must identify the language and translation status.
- Human-reviewed translations are distinguished from machine-assisted drafts.
- Assessment translations preserve construct, difficulty, directions, and scoring intent.
- Literary translations name the translator and edition when known.
- A translated ancient work is not assumed free merely because the source is ancient.
- Right-to-left languages receive actual bidirectional layout testing, not mirrored decoration.
- Fonts, input fields, punctuation, line breaking, and print output must support the script being used.

### Planned access layers

1. translated navigation and safety language;
2. translated lesson directions;
3. bilingual vocabulary and morphology support;
4. concise home-language concept summaries;
5. optional parallel-text reading where legally available;
6. read-aloud and keyboard accessibility;
7. learner language-profile settings;
8. printable bilingual family/educator guidance.

The curriculum’s English source must be academically locked before full translation so that global learners do not receive an obsolete or weaker version.

## International benchmark families

| Framework | Required Khaemenes evidence |
|---|---|
| Cambridge IGCSE First Language English | Unseen reading, summary, analysis/evaluation, directed writing, extended composition, technical accuracy |
| Cambridge Literature in English | Close analysis, informed interpretation, literary form, comparison, personal critical response |
| IB MYP Language and Literature | Listening, speaking, reading, writing, viewing, presenting, inquiry, action, reflection |
| Australian Curriculum Year 9 English | Language/literature/literacy integration and spoken, written, visual, critical, creative, and multimodal texts |
| England KS3 / early KS4 English | Wide reading, comparison, critical reading, grammar/vocabulary, discussion, speech, accurate composition |
| U.S. Common Core / Florida B.E.S.T. | Evidence, complex text, argument, information, narrative, research, conventions, vocabulary, discussion |
| CEFR Companion Volume | Reception, production, interaction, mediation, plurilingual/pluricultural competence, online interaction |
| UNESCO multilingual guidance | Meaningful access through languages learners understand and respect for linguistic diversity |

## Evidence rule

No standard is counted because it appears in a list. It is counted only when the repository contains:

1. explicit instruction;
2. guided practice;
3. independent learner evidence;
4. a valid assessment or performance task;
5. a scoring criterion;
6. a review, revision, or retry pathway.

## Quality gates

A unit cannot be marked complete until it passes curriculum, content accuracy, source/rights, pedagogy, assessment, accessibility, multilingual readiness, interaction, print, navigation, evidence, and educator-key audits.
