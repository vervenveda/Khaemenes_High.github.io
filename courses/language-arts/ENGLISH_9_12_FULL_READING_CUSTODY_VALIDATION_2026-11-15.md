## Custody model
Every source now falls into one of these states:

- **PASS · FIRST-PARTY** — exact Academy-created text is local and tied to a task.
- **PASS · AUTHENTIC** — exact work/source, edition/translator where relevant, passage/span, provenance and access route are documented.
- **CONDITIONAL** — student/teacher/current/modern selection is allowed only after the exact item-level custody record is completed.
- **HOLD** — category-only source description is not sufficient for release.
- **BLOCK** — an outside source cannot enter an authoritative assessment/capstone path without lawful access and exact metadata.

## What changed
- Missing Unit 01 dossier routes were repaired across all four grades.
- Generic labels such as “teacher-selected Shakespearean scene,” “approved novel,” “selected criticism,” “public-domain poem,” “current source,” or “scholarly source” are no longer treated as complete custody by themselves.
- Public-domain literary paths were bound to exact Project Gutenberg, Wikisource, Library of Congress, or equivalent stable records.
- Translation-sensitive works record translator/edition where verified.
- Current statistical/web sources require retrieval date and source-year/context.
- Research units treat the bibliography/search log as a custody ledger.
- Multimedia publication treats each external media asset as its own custody object.
- Portfolios/capstones inherit source provenance rather than stripping citations from finished artifacts.

## Intentional CONDITIONAL areas
Conditional markers remain where the curriculum is designed to permit:
- student-selected mentor texts;
- current news/social/fact-check material;
- contemporary/postcolonial literature;
- modern film/audio/performance/media;
- student-selected research/capstone sources;
- current application/admissions prompts;
- global-issue literary/nonliterary pairings;
- translated works whose specific translator/edition has not yet been selected.

These are not failures. They are fail-closed release gates.

## Final integrity rule
A beautiful reading dossier is not enough. Release requires:
1. exact source identity;
2. lawful/stable access;
3. edition/translator where relevant;
4. exact assigned span;
5. provenance/rights context;
6. student task tied to that source;
7. citation/attribution expectation;
8. evaluator boundary where needed.
