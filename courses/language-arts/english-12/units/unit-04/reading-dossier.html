WEEK 10 · UNIT 04

Rhetorical Situation, Purpose & Ethical Appeals

Original public statement

We can publish the full inspection report today, including names of witnesses, or release a redacted version while privacy review continues. Transparency matters; so does the safety of people who gave evidence under confidentiality.

Senior model

Ethical persuasion requires more than effectiveness. Analyze accuracy, omission, audience vulnerability, evidence quality, privacy, uncertainty and the consequences of framing.

Independent task

Use an approved speech, letter, testimony or civic document. Record full source custody, then evaluate purpose, audience, evidence, ethical appeal and one legitimate competing obligation.

WEEK 11 · UNIT 04

Rhetorical Analysis Across Texts

Comparative problem

Two speakers advocate the same policy. One addresses lawmakers with cost and legal precedent; another addresses affected families with testimony and practical consequences.

Model

Cross-text rhetorical analysis should compare choices in relation to audience and occasion, not rank texts by which uses more devices. Ask why different strategies are reasonable—or misleading—under different constraints.

Independent task

Compare two approved speeches/essays with differing audiences or occasions. Build a strategy/audience/evidence/ethics matrix and draft a timed rhetorical analysis with one counterinterpretation.

Return to Week 11 classroom and saved work

WEEK 12 · UNIT 04

Public Speaking, Debate & Civic Discourse

Practice issue brief

A fictional city proposes closing one downtown street to private cars on weekends. Evidence includes pedestrian counts, emergency-access concerns, business surveys with mixed response rates, and transit-capacity limits.

Civic model

Responsible public discourse distinguishes facts, projections, values and assumptions. Debate should answer the strongest opposing evidence and state what additional information could change a recommendation.

Independent task

Prepare an evidence-based speech or debate response using an approved real issue brief or transparent classroom simulation. Record source custody, identify uncertainty, anticipate questions, and write a revision memo after the question period.
