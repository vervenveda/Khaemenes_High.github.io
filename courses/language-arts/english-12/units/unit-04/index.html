Essential Question

How do language, structure, credibility, emotion, timing, and evidence shape public judgment?

Learning Architecture

Evaluate rhetorical situations, classical and global rhetorical traditions, speeches, essays, public documents, civic discourse, oral delivery, and the ethics of persuasion.

Universal Course Expectations

  • Use complex literary and informational texts from varied periods, cultures, disciplines, and media.
  • Distinguish evidence, inference, interpretation, evaluation, and synthesis.
  • Write through planning, drafting, feedback, revision, editing, publication, and reflection.
  • Practice oral communication, active listening, intellectual fairness, and responsible source use.
  • Preserve major products and process evidence in the English IV portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: Rhetorical analysis, public-address performance, and annotated evaluation of rhetorical choices across texts.

Senior required reading · The Translator's Choice

Original Academy close-reading source, embedded for offline use.

A translator rendered the phrase as 'I belong here'; another chose 'Here I remain.' Without access to every nuance of the source language, the seminar examined what each English choice emphasized and what might be lost.

Senior analysis: Develop a nuanced interpretation with at least two textual details, address a competing reading and a limitation in your claim, and outline how additional primary or scholarly sources could test it. Revisit during Weeks 10–12. Preserve work in the existing portfolio; this microtext complements longer advanced literature.
