Unit 10

College, Career, Civic & Digital Communication

Produce college or scholarship writing, professional correspondence, proposals, résumés, civic communication, explanatory writing, and accessible digital publication while reflecting on ethical technology use.

Begin Week 28Unit OverviewUnit Assessment
OverviewWeeksAssessmentStandards

Essential Question

How should a graduate adapt voice, evidence, structure, and medium for consequential real-world audiences?

Learning Architecture

Produce college or scholarship writing, professional correspondence, proposals, résumés, civic communication, explanatory writing, and accessible digital publication while reflecting on ethical technology use.

Universal Course Expectations

  • Use complex literary and informational texts from varied periods, cultures, disciplines, and media.
  • Distinguish evidence, inference, interpretation, evaluation, and synthesis.
  • Write through planning, drafting, feedback, revision, editing, publication, and reflection.
  • Practice oral communication, active listening, intellectual fairness, and responsible source use.
  • Preserve major products and process evidence in the English IV portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: Postsecondary communication portfolio with personal statement, résumé, professional correspondence, proposal, and accessible multimedia publication.

Three-Week Sequence

Read, evaluate, compose, revise, and demonstrate.
