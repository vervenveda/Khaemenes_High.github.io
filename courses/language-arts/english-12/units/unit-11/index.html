Essential Question

How can sustained reading and critical lenses deepen or challenge an interpretation of a complete work?

Learning Architecture

Complete a demanding novel or drama, trace motifs and structural development, compare interpretations, apply critical lenses responsibly, and lead an evidence-based seminar.

Universal Course Expectations

  • Use complex literary and informational texts from varied periods, cultures, disciplines, and media.
  • Distinguish evidence, inference, interpretation, evaluation, and synthesis.
  • Write through planning, drafting, feedback, revision, editing, publication, and reflection.
  • Practice oral communication, active listening, intellectual fairness, and responsible source use.
  • Preserve major products and process evidence in the English IV portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: Long-form reading dossier, independent literary argument, seminar leadership, and oral defense.

Senior required reading · The Capstone Abstract

Original Academy scholarly microtext, included offline.

The first abstract announced that a policy had solved a local problem. The final abstract stated that participants reported improvement during a limited trial, while longer-term outcomes remained unknown. Precision made the claim stronger, not weaker.

Senior response: Cite two precise details, articulate a defensible thesis, develop a substantial counterinterpretation, and explain what additional sources would be needed to advance the inquiry. Revisit during Weeks 31–33; preserve your draft and reflection in the existing portfolio. Full-length literature remains part of the course.
