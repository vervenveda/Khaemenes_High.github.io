Unit 09

Advanced Research & Academic Writing

Conduct sustained research, use databases and advanced search, evaluate scholarship, synthesize disagreements, document sources, analyze data, and draft an academic paper or literary inquiry.

OverviewWeeksAssessmentStandards

Essential Question

What makes a senior research claim original, responsible, credible, and useful?

Learning Architecture

Conduct sustained research, use databases and advanced search, evaluate scholarship, synthesize disagreements, document sources, analyze data, and draft an academic paper or literary inquiry.

Universal Course Expectations

  • Use complex literary and informational texts from varied periods, cultures, disciplines, and media.
  • Distinguish evidence, inference, interpretation, evaluation, and synthesis.
  • Write through planning, drafting, feedback, revision, editing, publication, and reflection.
  • Practice oral communication, active listening, intellectual fairness, and responsible source use.
  • Preserve major products and process evidence in the English IV portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: Research question, proposal, annotated bibliography, synthesis matrix, literature review, conference, and documented draft.

Three-Week Sequence

Read, evaluate, compose, revise, and demonstrate.

Week 25

Advanced Search, Databases & Research Methods

How do search methods, source types, and research design affect what a researcher can responsibly conclude?

search strings, methodology, source-type labels, citation, and limitations language
Open Week
Week 26

Literature Review & Synthesis of Scholarship

How can a researcher map agreement, disagreement, methods, gaps, and significance across sources?

source relationships, synthesis transitions, attribution, hedging, and research-gap language
Open Week
Week 27

Academic Argument Draft, Citation & Peer Review

How does a writer turn research into an original argument while documenting evidence and responding to limitations?

academic tone, citation, paragraph coherence, counterargument, and revision priorities
Open Week

Performance Evidence

Research question, proposal, annotated bibliography, synthesis matrix, literature review, conference, and documented draft.
