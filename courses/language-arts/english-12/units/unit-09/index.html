Evidence-Based Senior Learning Rhythm

Design Method → Search Systematically → Review Scholarship → Argue → Audit

Unit 09 develops advanced academic research through method selection, database/archive search, literature-review synthesis, defensible gap claims, documented argument, citation audit, and evidence-based peer review.

Week 25 · Advanced Search, Databases & Research Methods

Search pathways, source taxonomy, methodological models, assumptions, limitations, search logs, and research design.

Week 26 · Literature Review & Synthesis of Scholarship

Methods, findings, agreement, disagreement, genuine gaps, significance, and relational synthesis.

Week 27 · Academic Argument, Citation & Peer Review

Original contribution, claim/evidence/warrant, citation audit, limitations, peer-review judgment, and revision planning.

Senior research rule: a gap is defensible only when supported by the search record and literature reviewed—not merely because the researcher has not yet located an answer.

Essential Question

What makes a senior research claim original, responsible, credible, and useful?

Learning Architecture

Conduct sustained research, use databases and advanced search, evaluate scholarship, synthesize disagreements, document sources, analyze data, and draft an academic paper or literary inquiry.

Universal Course Expectations

  • Use complex literary and informational texts from varied periods, cultures, disciplines, and media.
  • Distinguish evidence, inference, interpretation, evaluation, and synthesis.
  • Write through planning, drafting, feedback, revision, editing, publication, and reflection.
  • Practice oral communication, active listening, intellectual fairness, and responsible source use.
  • Preserve major products and process evidence in the English IV portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: Research question, proposal, annotated bibliography, synthesis matrix, literature review, conference, and documented draft.

Senior required reading · From Page to Stage

Original Academy scholarly microtext, included offline.

On the page, a character's refusal appeared final. In performance, a long hesitation preceded the words. The adaptation did not change the sentence, but the pause created space for a different interpretation.

Senior response: Cite two precise details, articulate a defensible thesis, develop a substantial counterinterpretation, and explain what additional sources would be needed to advance the inquiry. Revisit during Weeks 25–27; preserve your draft and reflection in the existing portfolio. Full-length literature remains part of the course.

Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1

Unit 09 Learning Progression

Design Method → Map Scholarship → Identify Gap → Build Original Claim → Audit Limitations

Grade 12 treats research as an independent scholarly process: method choice, literature review, gap identification, original argument, citation discipline, peer review, and explicit limitation analysis.

Week 25

Advanced Search, Databases & Research Methods

Produce a methods note stating what the chosen design can answer, what it cannot answer, and one likely source of bias or blind spot.

Week 26

Literature Review & Synthesis of Scholarship

Draft a literature-review synthesis that shows the state of the conversation, the proposed contribution, and the boundaries of the claim.

Week 27

Academic Argument Draft, Citation & Peer Review

Complete a draft audit: strongest contribution, weakest inferential step, source limitation, counterevidence, citation risk, and next revision.

The 15-day sequence revisits prior evidence habits while increasing independence in sustained inquiry, source selection, synthesis, academic argument, and limitation analysis.
