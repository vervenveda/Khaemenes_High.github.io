# English 12 · Unit 08 Evidence Alignment
## Language, Power, Culture & Global Issues

**Upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Unit 08 propagation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade-level rhythm

**Define Context → Analyze Representation → Evaluate Medium → Connect Global Issue → Audit Power**

Grade 12 independently analyzes language variation, representation, multimodal rhetoric, accessibility, and global issues while explicitly examining who gains authority and who may be excluded.

## Three-week architecture

### Week 22 · Language, Identity, Dialect & Code-Switching
1. **Define Context:** Identify the discourse community, audience, situation, and language variety without treating standard language as inherently superior.
2. **Analyze Representation:** Examine how dialect, register, code-switching, multilingualism, or silence communicates identity, belonging, resistance, or strategic audience awareness.
3. **Trace Power:** Identify where institutions or audiences reward, stigmatize, authorize, or misunderstand particular language practices.
4. **Counter:** Test a reading focused on personal choice against one focused on social constraint. Where does each explanation overreach?
5. **Audit Power:** State a calibrated claim distinguishing linguistic evidence, social context, interpretive inference, and unresolved complexity.

### Week 23 · Visual Rhetoric, Film & Digital Texts
1. **Define Context:** Identify creator, audience, platform, genre, purpose, publication conditions, and any relevant interface or algorithmic context.
2. **Analyze Representation:** Examine how image, sound, editing, typography, sequence, framing, and omission shape what becomes visible or credible.
3. **Evaluate Medium:** Determine what the medium enables that prose alone would not, and what distortions or accessibility barriers it may introduce.
4. **Test:** Compare two representations of the same issue using common criteria for evidence, framing, emotional force, and omission.
5. **Audit Power:** Produce a media-rhetoric judgment that separates factual reliability, persuasive effectiveness, representation, and ethical concern.

### Week 24 · Global Issue Comparative Oral
1. **Define Global Issue:** Narrow the issue to a focused, transnationally significant problem that is demonstrably present in both literary and nonliterary evidence.
2. **Select Evidence:** Choose passages or media moments that reveal authorial choices rather than merely mention the issue.
3. **Connect:** Explain how form, language, image, structure, or perspective constructs the issue differently across the two works.
4. **Defend:** Respond to questions about scope, evidence, context, and why the issue qualifies as global rather than merely broad.
5. **Audit Power:** Complete the oral with a self-audit of strongest evidence, weakest link, representation risk, cultural limitation, and next revision.

## Spacing and retrieval

The Unit 08 evidence layer uses a 15-day map across Weeks 22–24. Retrieval deliberately returns to:
- prior-unit evidence, synthesis, comparison, source evaluation, rhetoric, and qualification habits;
- prior-day concepts;
- approximately one-week-delayed concepts;
- earlier Unit 08 methods;
- a cumulative usability, adaptation, performance, multimedia, global-issue, or representation audit on the final instructional day.

Each prompt is tied to the actual intellectual work of the day rather than a generic recall routine.

## Reading/source custody

Required reading remains in the existing unit/week pages, reading dossier, locally embedded Academy texts, approved authentic works, and approved multimedia sources. Exact source, author/creator, publication date, edition, translator, cultural/religious context, medium, platform, and provenance remain required where applicable.

## Mastery boundary

The Unit 08 evidence layer:
- writes no learner data;
- changes no storage keys;
- changes no quiz answers or thresholds;
- changes no assessment authority;
- does not convert completion into mastery;
- adds retrieval, explanation, usability testing, adaptation comparison, cultural/contextual analysis, performance, multimedia interpretation, accessibility review, and exit-audit prompts only.

## Release criterion

Unit 08 is ready when:
1. all 15 instructional days receive the grade-specific evidence layer;
2. the grade-level scaffold fade is preserved;
3. authentic-source, cultural, translation, and multimedia custody remain visible;
4. no duplicate generic prompts appear;
5. JavaScript parses cleanly;
6. storage/assessment authority remains unchanged;
