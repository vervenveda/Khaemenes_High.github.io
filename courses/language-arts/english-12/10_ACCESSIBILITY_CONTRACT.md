# Accessibility Contract — English 12

The canonical accessibility requirements are defined in:

`../ENGLISH_9_12_A_PLUS_PLUS_PLUS_CANONICAL_CONTRACT.md#6-accessibility-contract`

English 12 may add supports, but may not weaken the canonical requirements. Alternate response modes must preserve the competency being measured. Minimum body and button text is 16px; the standard eyebrow/kicker size is 14px.
