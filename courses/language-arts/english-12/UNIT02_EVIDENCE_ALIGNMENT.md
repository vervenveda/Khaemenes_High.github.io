# English 12 · Unit 02 Evidence Alignment
## Epic, Tragedy & World Literary Foundations

**Upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Unit 02 propagation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade-level rhythm

**Set Inquiry → Choose Method → Test Relationship → Defend → Audit**

Grade 12 places inquiry design, method choice, source relationship, falsification conditions, and defense with the learner.

## Three-week architecture

### Week 4 · Epic, Myth, Archetype & Allusion
Foundational patterns, allusion, evidence of influence, transformation, and alternative explanations.

### Week 5 · Tragedy, Character & Moral Choice
Responsibility, fate, power, causal reasoning, conflicting perspectives, and moral counterargument.

### Week 6 · Comparative Foundations & Contemporary Adaptation
Preserve/revise/resist/transform, influence versus resemblance, contextual change, and senior comparative defense.

## Spacing and retrieval

The Unit 02 evidence layer uses a 15-day map across Weeks 4–6. Retrieval deliberately returns to:
- Unit 01 evidence/qualification habits;
- prior-day concepts;
- approximately one-week-delayed concepts;
- earlier Unit 02 comparison methods;
- a cumulative Unit 02 transfer task on the final instructional day.

Each prompt is tied to the actual intellectual work of the day rather than a generic recall routine.

## Reading/source custody

Required reading remains in the existing unit/week pages, reading dossier, locally embedded Academy texts, and approved authentic works. Course-created practice texts remain clearly identified as instructional examples. When authentic literature or living/sacred traditions are assigned, exact source/edition/translator/provenance records remain required where applicable.

## Mastery boundary

The Unit 02 evidence layer:
- writes no learner data;
- changes no storage keys;
- changes no quiz answers or thresholds;
- changes no assessment authority;
- does not convert completion into mastery;
- adds retrieval, transfer, counterreading, qualification, and exit-audit prompts only.

## Release criterion

Unit 02 is ready when:
1. all 15 instructional days receive the grade-specific retrieval/exit layer;
2. the grade-level scaffold fade is preserved;
3. authentic-source custody remains visible;
4. no duplicate generic prompts appear;
5. JavaScript parses cleanly;
6. storage/assessment authority remains unchanged;
7. the unit landing page communicates the learning progression.
