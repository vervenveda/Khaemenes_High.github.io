# English 12 · Unit 10 Evidence Alignment
## College, Career, Civic & Digital Communication

**Upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Unit 10 propagation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade-level rhythm

**Define Audience → Select Evidence → Adapt Voice → Deliver Action → Audit Consequence**

Grade 12 shifts English toward consequential real-world communication where audience, hierarchy, evidence, risk, accessibility, and ethical technology use directly affect outcomes.

## Three-week architecture

### Week 28 · College, Scholarship & Reflective Narrative
1. **Define Audience:** Identify the actual audience, prompt, selection criteria, and purpose before deciding what experience to narrate.
2. **Select Evidence:** Choose concrete details and moments that demonstrate character, growth, or fit rather than simply claim qualities.
3. **Adapt Voice:** Revise for specificity, honesty, tone, and rhetorical restraint; remove cliché, inflated claims, and generic inspiration language.
4. **Test:** Ask whether the narrative represents the experience fairly and whether any omission changes the meaning materially.
5. **Audit Consequence:** Complete a submission audit covering authenticity, relevance, specificity, privacy, audience fit, and one remaining weakness.

### Week 29 · Workplace, Civic & Professional Communication
1. **Define Audience:** Identify recipient, hierarchy, decision authority, risk, desired action, and constraints before drafting.
2. **Select Evidence:** Include only the evidence, context, or documentation necessary for the reader to act responsibly.
3. **Adapt Voice:** Adjust tone, structure, directness, and formality for professional, civic, or institutional context without becoming vague or submissive.
4. **Deliver Action:** Make the requested action, deadline, responsibility, and next step unmistakable while preserving courtesy and accuracy.
5. **Audit Consequence:** Review for ambiguity, legal/ethical risk, unsupported claims, privacy concerns, record value, and likely reader interpretation.

### Week 30 · Accessible Multimedia Publication & Digital Citizenship
1. **Define Audience:** Specify real audience, platform, purpose, accessibility needs, and permanence or redistribution risk.
2. **Select Evidence:** Verify claims, permissions, attribution, media provenance, and whether automated or AI-assisted content requires disclosure or correction.
3. **Adapt Medium:** Choose text, image, audio, video, data display, or interactive elements based on communication need rather than novelty.
4. **Test:** Evaluate accessibility, privacy, misinformation risk, security, audience interpretation, and whether the medium changes the evidentiary claim.
5. **Audit Consequence:** Complete a digital-publication audit: accuracy, attribution, accessibility, consent/privacy, ethical technology use, audience impact, and revision threshold.

## Spacing and retrieval

The Unit 10 evidence layer uses a 15-day map across Weeks 28–30. Retrieval deliberately returns to:
- prior-unit argument, research, source evaluation, synthesis, publication, and qualification habits;
- prior-day concepts;
- approximately one-week-delayed concepts;
- earlier Unit 10 methods;
- a cumulative debate, publication, professional-communication, accessibility, or consequence audit on the final instructional day.

Each prompt is tied to the actual intellectual work of the day rather than a generic recall routine.

## Reading/source custody

Required reading and research remain in the existing unit/week pages, reading dossier, locally embedded Academy texts, approved authentic works, civic/professional documents, and approved multimedia sources. Exact source, author/creator, publication date, edition, platform, media provenance, permissions, and citation records remain required where applicable.

## Mastery boundary

The Unit 10 evidence layer:
- writes no learner data;
- changes no storage keys;
- changes no quiz answers or thresholds;
- changes no assessment authority;
- does not convert completion into mastery;
- adds argument framing, evidence testing, research writing, counterclaim, publication design, accessibility, professional/civic communication, ethical technology review, and exit-audit prompts only.

## Release criterion

Unit 10 is ready when:
1. all 15 instructional days receive the grade-specific evidence layer;
2. the grade-level scaffold fade is preserved;
3. source, media, attribution, and publication custody remain visible;
4. no duplicate generic prompts appear;
5. JavaScript parses cleanly;
6. storage/assessment authority remains unchanged;
7. the unit landing page communicates the learning progression.
