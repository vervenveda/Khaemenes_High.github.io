About English 12

English 12 is the culminating Language Arts course of the High School program. Students are expected to read independently at the high end of text complexity, evaluate evidence and uncertainty, synthesize across sources and traditions, communicate for real audiences, document authorship and ethical source use, and defend a body of work that demonstrates readiness for college, career, civic life, and lifelong literacy.

Course Features

Independent Reading & World Literature Students work with demanding literary, informational, civic, historical, visual, and global texts while managing uncertainty, interpretation, translation, and competing perspectives.
Rhetoric, Argument & Media Literacy Advanced claims, counterclaims, source limits, rhetorical choices, digital media, data, civic communication, and ethical persuasion develop mature public reasoning.
Research & Postsecondary Communication Literature review, synthesis, citation, proposals, professional correspondence, personal statements, résumés, accessible publication, and responsible technology use prepare students for consequential audiences.
Capstone & Graduation Portfolio Long-form study, seminar leadership, senior capstone inquiry, cumulative assessment, substantive revision, portfolio curation, oral defense, and a postsecondary literacy plan document independent readiness.
