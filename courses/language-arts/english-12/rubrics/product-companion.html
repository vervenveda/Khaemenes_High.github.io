Authentic Assessment

English IV Product Companion Rubrics

Use these product-specific criteria beside the existing literary analysis, argument, research, speaking, and narrative rubrics. They sharpen evaluator attention without changing the 80% mastery threshold, assessment authority, or existing score records.

How to use: score the established mode rubric as usual. Then use the four companion criteria for targeted feedback and evidence review. A companion criterion does not independently award mastery and never converts completion into mastery.

Unit 01

Senior scholar portfolio

  • Independent planning
  • Evidence and uncertainty management
  • Authorship transparency
  • Revision decisions are documented

Unit 02

Epic/tragedy synthesis

  • Comparative literary judgment
  • Historical/cultural context
  • Theory or lens used responsibly
  • Interpretive limits acknowledged

Unit 03

Advanced poetry interpretation

  • Formal analysis
  • Voice/style judgment
  • Translation/context awareness
  • Independent interpretive defense

Unit 04

Public discourse & ethical persuasion

  • Ethical rhetorical choices
  • Audience/stakeholder analysis
  • Evidence and reasoning
  • Limits and consequences addressed

Unit 05

Logic, media & information literacy

  • Claim verification
  • Logical analysis
  • Media/source provenance
  • Correction of misinformation or overclaim

Unit 06

Research design & midyear mastery

  • Methodological fit
  • Source strategy
  • Synthesis and limitations
  • Defense of research decisions

Unit 07

Contemporary world literature

  • Global/contextual literacy
  • Complex interpretation
  • Comparative synthesis
  • Ethical representation of perspectives

Unit 08

Language, power & global issues

  • Power/language analysis
  • Source diversity
  • Cultural and ethical nuance
  • Argument remains evidence-bound

Unit 09

Advanced academic research

  • Research significance
  • Method/source rigor
  • Scholarly synthesis
  • Citation, attribution and limitations

Unit 10

College/career/civic/digital communication

  • Real audience fit
  • Genre and platform control
  • Accessibility and professionalism
  • Evidence of revision after feedback

Unit 11

Theory & independent seminar

  • Independent reading depth
  • Theoretical application
  • Seminar leadership/listening
  • Counterreading and uncertainty

Unit 12

Capstone & graduation defense

  • Inquiry originality
  • Cross-mode synthesis
  • Process/authorship record
  • Oral defense and postsecondary transfer plan
