# English 12 · Unit 12 Evidence Alignment
## Capstone Inquiry, Graduation Portfolio & Final

**Upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Unit 12 propagation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade-level rhythm

**Contribute → Curate Mastery → Defend Independence → Demonstrate Transfer → Launch Postsecondary Plan**

Grade 12 culminates in independent contribution, graduation-level portfolio curation, oral defense, cumulative transfer, correction analysis, and a postsecondary literacy plan grounded in evidence rather than ceremony alone.

## Three-week architecture

### Week 34 · Senior Capstone: Inquiry, Creation & Contribution
1. **Define Contribution:** State the real audience, need, question, or problem the capstone addresses and what meaningful contribution the work intends to make.
2. **Integrate Evidence:** Use research, analysis, creation, or communication methods appropriate to the capstone rather than forcing one academic format onto every project.
3. **Test Value:** Gather evidence from users, readers, listeners, experts, or source comparison to evaluate whether the contribution actually meets its purpose.
4. **Revise:** Make a substantive revision based on evidence of effectiveness, limitation, accessibility, or ethical concern.
5. **Contribute:** Complete a capstone rationale covering purpose, audience, method, evidence, contribution, limitation, and what would be done next.

### Week 35 · Graduation Portfolio & Oral Defense
1. **Curate Mastery:** Select representative evidence of growth, mastery, independence, revision, research, communication, and transfer across the senior year and earlier high-school work where useful.
2. **Map Evidence:** Tie each readiness claim to a concrete artifact, revision, assessment, performance, or capstone decision.
3. **Prepare Defense:** Anticipate questions about limitations, failures, revisions, source choices, ethics, and independent decision-making.
4. **Defend Independence:** Complete the oral defense by explaining not only what was accomplished but how judgments were made and revised.
5. **Recalibrate:** After defense, record the strongest demonstrated competency, most important remaining weakness, and one postsecondary literacy risk to monitor.

### Week 36 · Cumulative Final & Postsecondary Literacy Plan
1. **Retrieve:** Use four-year and senior-year evidence to identify the highest-value concepts and skills for cumulative retrieval before the final.
2. **Demonstrate Transfer:** Complete the cumulative final with unfamiliar texts, evidence, and communication demands under established assessment authority.
3. **Correct:** Document corrections by knowledge gap, reasoning error, source/evidence problem, communication failure, or execution mistake.
4. **Plan Transfer:** Translate senior English skills into specific college, career, civic, and independent-learning contexts rather than generic future goals.
5. **Launch Postsecondary Plan:** Complete a literacy plan with reading, research, writing, communication, media-verification, and lifelong-learning practices plus evidence that will signal when support or revision is needed.

## Full-year retrieval and transfer

The Unit 12 evidence layer uses a 15-day map across Weeks 34–36. Retrieval deliberately returns to:
- full-year reading, writing, research, rhetoric, media, speaking, publication, and revision habits;
- earlier portfolio artifacts;
- prior assessments and corrections;
- long-form or capstone evidence;
- a cumulative final, oral defense, and next-stage literacy plan.

Each prompt requires students to demonstrate growth with evidence rather than rely on completion, self-description, or score alone.

## Reading/source custody

Required texts and evidence remain in the existing unit/week pages, reading dossier, student portfolio, approved authentic works, research sources, capstone materials, and assessment records. Exact source, author/creator, edition, translator, publication date, provenance, and citation records remain required where applicable.

## Mastery boundary

The Unit 12 evidence layer:
- writes no learner data;
- changes no storage keys;
- changes no final-exam answers or thresholds;
- changes no assessment authority;
- does not convert completion, portfolio presence, or oral participation into mastery;
- adds curation, revision, synthesis, cumulative retrieval, corrections, oral defense, readiness evaluation, transfer planning, and exit-audit prompts only.

## Release criterion

Unit 12 is ready when:
1. all 15 instructional days receive the grade-specific evidence layer;
2. the grade-level scaffold fade is preserved through final defense;
3. portfolio and capstone claims are tied to concrete evidence;
4. corrections remain separate from simple completion;
5. no duplicate generic prompts appear;
6. JavaScript parses cleanly;
7. storage/assessment authority remains unchanged;
8. the unit landing page communicates the final learning progression;
9. the learner exits with a documented next-stage literacy plan.
