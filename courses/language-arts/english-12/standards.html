Senior graduation competency profile

Independent Reading

Read demanding literature and literary nonfiction independently; manage ambiguity; evaluate evidence, structure, perspective, rhetoric, context, and uncertainty.

Advanced Writing

Produce in-depth literary and rhetorical analysis, argument, synthesis, research, explanatory writing, complex narrative, timed writing, revision, and publication.

Research & Information

Develop significant questions; use primary, secondary, scholarly, visual, and quantitative sources; verify media; synthesize disagreement; cite accurately; state limitations.

Communication & Capstone

Lead seminars, deliver evidence-based presentations, publish accessible multimedia, complete a capstone, and defend a standards-based graduation portfolio.

  • Common Core State Standards — English Language Arts — Grade 11–12 college- and career-readiness reading, writing, speaking/listening, language, and disciplinary literacy reference.
  • Florida B.E.S.T. Standards — English Language Arts — Current Florida Grade 12 reading, communication, vocabulary, and research benchmark reference, verified through CPALMS.
  • NAEP 2026 Reading Framework — National assessment reference for Grade 12 literary and informational reading performance, including advanced interpretation and analysis.
  • SAT Reading and Writing Content Domains — Information and Ideas, Craft and Structure, Expression of Ideas, and Standard English Conventions.
  • AP English Language and Composition — College-level rhetoric, source evaluation, synthesis, evidence-based argument, organization, style, drafting, and revision reference. This package is not AP-authorized.
  • AP English Literature and Composition — College-level close reading, literary interpretation, character, setting, structure, perspective, figurative language, comparison, and literary argument reference. This package is not AP-authorized.
  • ACT College and Career Readiness Standards — English and reading college-readiness reference for conventions, production of writing, knowledge of language, key ideas, craft, structure, and integration.
  • International Baccalaureate — Language A: Language and Literature — International reference for literary and nonliterary analysis, varied cultures and periods, global issues, written communication, and oral interpretation. This package is not IB-authorized.
  • Cambridge International AS & A Level English Language — International reference for critical response to varied texts, context, style, audience, language change, research, and effective communication. This package is not a Cambridge qualification.
