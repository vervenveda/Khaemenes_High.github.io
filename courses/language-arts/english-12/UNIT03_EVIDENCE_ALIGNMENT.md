# English 12 · Unit 03 Evidence Alignment
## Poetry, Voice, Form & Advanced Interpretation

**Upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Unit 03 propagation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade-level rhythm

**Set Inquiry → Choose Method → Test Relationship → Defend → Audit**

Grade 12 places inquiry design, method choice, falsification conditions, contextual judgment, and defense with the learner.

## Three-week architecture

### Week 7 · Poetic Speaker, Situation & Form
1. **Set Inquiry:** Formulate a question about speaker, situation, and form that cannot be answered by biography alone. Define what textual evidence would count.
2. **Choose Method:** Select a formal feature—structure, lineation, syntax, stanza pattern, or another defensible feature—and justify why it is the best method for the inquiry.
3. **Test Relationship:** Test whether the speaker/form relationship holds across the entire poem or only in selected passages. Record disconfirming evidence.
4. **Defend:** Defend your interpretation against a reading that treats speaker and author as equivalent. Explain which textual facts require the distinction.
5. **Audit:** State your current interpretation, its strongest evidence, its weakest assumption, and the evidence that would cause you to revise it.

### Week 8 · Figurative Language, Sound, Ambiguity & Image
1. **Set Inquiry:** Identify an ambiguity worth investigating and frame an inquiry that allows more than one initially plausible answer.
2. **Choose Method:** Choose whether sound, imagery, figurative language, syntax, or pattern is the most revealing method. Explain the opportunity cost of that choice.
3. **Test Relationship:** Test two interpretations against the same evidence. Track where each explains the poem well and where it produces strain.
4. **Defend:** Defend the stronger interpretation while preserving the textual fact that makes the weaker interpretation possible.
5. **Audit:** Specify the claim, evidence chain, unresolved ambiguity, and falsification condition for your reading.

### Week 9 · Major Poets in Historical Context
1. **Set Inquiry:** Frame a question about the relationship between poem and historical context without assuming context determines meaning.
2. **Choose Method:** Decide which contextual evidence is necessary, which is merely interesting, and which risks replacing close reading.
3. **Test Relationship:** Compare a text-first interpretation with a context-enriched interpretation. Identify exactly what the contextual evidence changes.
4. **Defend:** Defend a contextual claim against an alternative explanation based solely on form, language, or literary convention.
5. **Audit:** Conclude with a calibrated claim that distinguishes evidence of influence, plausible context, coincidence, and uncertainty.

## Spacing and retrieval

The Unit 03 evidence layer uses a 15-day map across Weeks 7–9. Retrieval deliberately returns to:
- Unit 01–02 evidence, qualification, and comparison habits;
- prior-day concepts;
- approximately one-week-delayed concepts;
- earlier Unit 03 methods;
- a cumulative Unit 03 transfer, defense, or audit task on the final instructional day.

Each prompt is tied to the actual intellectual work of the day rather than a generic recall routine.

## Reading/source custody

Required reading remains in the existing unit/week pages, reading dossier, locally embedded Academy texts, and approved authentic works. Course-created practice texts remain clearly identified as instructional examples. Exact source, edition, translator, and provenance records remain required where applicable.

## Mastery boundary

The Unit 03 evidence layer:
- writes no learner data;
- changes no storage keys;
- changes no quiz answers or thresholds;
- changes no assessment authority;
- does not convert completion into mastery;
- adds retrieval, transfer, counterreading, qualification, revision, and exit-audit prompts only.

## Release criterion

Unit 03 is ready when:
1. all 15 instructional days receive the grade-specific retrieval/transfer layer;
2. the grade-level scaffold fade is preserved;
3. authentic-source custody remains visible;
4. no duplicate generic prompts appear;
5. JavaScript parses cleanly;
6. storage/assessment authority remains unchanged;
7. the unit landing page communicates the learning progression.
