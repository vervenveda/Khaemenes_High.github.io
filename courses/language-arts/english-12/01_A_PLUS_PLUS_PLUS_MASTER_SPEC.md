## English 12 emphasis

English 12 operates at Senior Scholar level: learners frame inquiry, choose method, test scope and falsification conditions, audit limitations, defend independent judgment, and transfer literacy into postsecondary contexts.

## Non-negotiable local rules

- Mastery threshold: 80%.
- Completion, participation, practice, games, or viewing do not grant mastery.
- Human review remains required for complex performance.
- Prior mastery is preserved after a lower voluntary retake.
- Corrections target demonstrated skill needs.
- The course remains 12 units, 36 weeks, and 180 daily blocks.
