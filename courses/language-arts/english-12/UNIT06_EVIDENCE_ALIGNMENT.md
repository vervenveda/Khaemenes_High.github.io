# English 12 · Unit 06 Evidence Alignment
## Synthesis, Research Design & Midyear Mastery

**Upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Unit 06 propagation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade-level rhythm

**Frame Inquiry → Evaluate Sources → Synthesize Tension → Test Scope → Recalibrate**

Grade 12 places research design, source authority, conflicting evidence, scope control, and evidence-based self-assessment under independent learner control.

## Three-week architecture

### Week 16 · Source-Based Synthesis & Cross-Text Reasoning
1. **Frame Inquiry:** State the question the source set can actually answer and identify any part of the larger issue it cannot resolve.
2. **Evaluate Sources:** Rank sources by authority, relevance, method, recency, and limitation rather than prestige alone.
3. **Synthesize Tension:** Build a synthesis around relationships among sources—agreement, contradiction, qualification, causal difference, or scope difference.
4. **Test Scope:** Identify where your synthesis overgeneralizes beyond the evidence. Narrow or qualify the claim.
5. **Recalibrate:** Write a defensible synthesis claim with explicit uncertainty, strongest evidence, strongest conflict, and what additional evidence is needed.

### Week 17 · Research Question, Scope & Annotated Bibliography
1. **Frame Inquiry:** Turn a broad concern into a researchable question with defined population, context, time frame, concepts, or text set where appropriate.
2. **Evaluate Sources:** For each major source, record authority, method or basis, contribution, limitation, and relationship to the inquiry.
3. **Test Scope:** Check whether the question is feasible, significant, ethically appropriate, and answerable with available evidence.
4. **Refine:** Revise the question after source review so that it reflects what the evidence can support rather than the answer you hoped to prove.
5. **Recalibrate:** Produce a research prospectus stating question, rationale, scope, source strategy, likely limitations, and criteria for changing direction.

### Week 18 · Midterm Examination & Midyear Conference
1. **Retrieve:** Construct a first-semester evidence map across reading, rhetoric, argument, media literacy, synthesis, and research design.
2. **Diagnose:** Identify the largest gap between perceived competence and demonstrated performance, using specific artifacts or assessment evidence.
3. **Demonstrate:** Complete the cumulative midterm under the established assessment rules and preserve the distinction between completion and mastery.
4. **Analyze:** Perform a corrections analysis that distinguishes knowledge gaps, reasoning errors, evidence problems, scope errors, and execution mistakes.
5. **Recalibrate:** Conduct the midyear conference with one evidence-backed strength, one priority weakness, one falsifiable improvement goal, and the artifact that will demonstrate progress.

## Spacing and retrieval

The Unit 06 evidence layer uses a 15-day map across Weeks 16–18. Retrieval deliberately returns to:
- first-semester evidence, interpretation, rhetoric, argument, synthesis, and language habits;
- prior-day concepts;
- approximately one-week-delayed concepts;
- earlier Unit 06 methods;
- cumulative midyear assessment evidence, corrections, and a next-step improvement plan.

Each prompt is tied to the actual intellectual work of the day rather than a generic review routine.

## Reading/source custody

Required reading remains in the existing unit/week pages, reading dossier, locally embedded Academy texts, approved authentic works, and approved source sets. Course-created practice texts remain clearly identified as instructional examples. Exact source, author/speaker, publication date, edition, translator, data provenance, and research limitations remain required where applicable.

## Mastery boundary

The Unit 06 evidence layer:
- writes no learner data;
- changes no storage keys;
- changes no quiz or midterm answers or thresholds;
- changes no assessment authority;
- does not convert completion into mastery;
- adds retrieval, synthesis, rhetorical analysis/composition, source evaluation, corrections analysis, reflection, and evidence-based planning prompts only.

## Release criterion

Unit 06 is ready when:
1. all 15 instructional days receive the grade-specific evidence layer;
2. the grade-level scaffold fade is preserved;
3. authentic-source and research custody remain visible;
4. no duplicate generic prompts appear;
5. JavaScript parses cleanly;
6. storage/assessment authority remains unchanged;
7. the unit landing page communicates the learning progression;
8. midyear reflection is tied to demonstrated evidence rather than self-report alone.
