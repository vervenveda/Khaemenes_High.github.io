# English 12 · Unit 11 Evidence Alignment
## Long-Form Literature, Theory & Independent Seminar

**Upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Unit 11 propagation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade-level rhythm

**Sustain Inquiry → Select Lens → Test Whole-Text Fit → Lead Seminar → Defend & Recalibrate**

Grade 12 places sustained reading, critical-lens choice, whole-text pattern testing, seminar leadership, and oral defense under independent learner control.

## Three-week architecture

### Week 31 · Long-Form Novel or Drama: Independent Reading
1. **Sustain Inquiry:** Establish an independent reading/annotation system that tracks character, setting, conflict, motif, structure, and unresolved questions across the complete work.
2. **Trace Development:** Follow one pattern across multiple sections and distinguish recurrence from genuine development.
3. **Test Whole-Text Fit:** Identify a passage that challenges your emerging interpretation and decide whether to revise, qualify, or reject the claim.
4. **Connect:** Relate structural or character development to the work’s larger meaning without reducing the entire text to one theme.
5. **Recalibrate:** Write a provisional whole-text claim with strongest evidence, strongest counterevidence, unresolved question, and next reading target.

### Week 32 · Critical Lenses & Competing Interpretations
1. **Select Lens:** Choose a critical lens because it fits the text and inquiry, not because it is familiar. State what the lens helps reveal and what it risks obscuring.
2. **Apply:** Use the lens on multiple passages and verify that the interpretation remains textually grounded across the work.
3. **Compare:** Compare the lens-based reading with at least one competing interpretation grounded in different evidence or method.
4. **Test:** Identify where the chosen lens begins to force the text into a formula or ignore contradictory evidence.
5. **Recalibrate:** Revise the interpretation so the lens remains a tool rather than the authority, explicitly naming limits and counterevidence.

### Week 33 · Independent Seminar & Literary Argument
1. **Frame Seminar:** Develop a discussion question with enough complexity, textual evidence, and openness to sustain genuine inquiry rather than produce one expected answer.
2. **Lead:** Guide discussion by inviting evidence, clarifying claims, surfacing disagreement, and preventing unsupported assertions from becoming consensus.
3. **Respond:** Revise or defend your own interpretation in response to strong seminar evidence rather than authority or popularity.
4. **Oral Defense:** Present the whole-text argument, method, strongest evidence, counterinterpretation, limitation, and what would change your conclusion.
5. **Recalibrate:** Complete an independent-scholar audit of reading method, discussion leadership, argument quality, evidence gaps, and next intellectual step.

## Spacing and retrieval

The Unit 11 evidence layer uses a 15-day map across Weeks 31–33. Retrieval deliberately returns to:
- prior-unit evidence, publication, rhetoric, interpretation, accessibility, and long-form reading habits;
- prior-day concepts;
- approximately one-week-delayed concepts;
- earlier Unit 11 methods;
- a cumulative publication, performance, long-form inquiry, seminar, or oral-defense audit on the final instructional day.

Each prompt is tied to the actual intellectual work of the day rather than a generic recall routine.

## Reading/source custody

Required reading remains in the existing unit/week pages, reading dossier, locally embedded Academy texts, approved authentic works, dramatic texts, long-form works, mentor texts, and approved multimedia sources. Exact source, edition, translator, creator, performance version, publication venue, media provenance, and citation records remain required where applicable.

## Mastery boundary

The Unit 11 evidence layer:
- writes no learner data;
- changes no storage keys;
- changes no quiz answers or thresholds;
- changes no assessment authority;
- does not convert completion into mastery;
- adds publication planning, source credit, editing, accessibility review, dramatic/performance analysis, sustained-reading tracking, critical-lens evaluation, seminar leadership, oral defense, and exit-audit prompts only.

## Release criterion

Unit 11 is ready when:
1. all 15 instructional days receive the grade-specific evidence layer;
2. the grade-level scaffold fade is preserved;
3. source, performance, publication, and long-form reading custody remain visible;
4. no duplicate generic prompts appear;
5. JavaScript parses cleanly;
6. storage/assessment authority remains unchanged;
7. the unit landing page communicates the learning progression.
