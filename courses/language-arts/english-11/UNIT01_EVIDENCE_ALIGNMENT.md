# English 11 · Unit 01 Evidence Alignment
## Advanced Foundations of Reading, Writing & Language

**Audit/upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Canonical Grade 11 implementation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade 11 instructional difference

English 11 keeps the same stable cognitive rhythm while shifting control toward the learner. Students are expected to choose the relevant strategy, evaluate provenance and rhetoric, compare competing interpretations, preserve uncertainty, synthesize across sources, and defend revisions with explicit reasoning.

The stable rhythm remains:
**Retrieve → Select Strategy → Evaluate → Synthesize → Counter → Revise → Reflect**

## Fifteen-step retrieval map

1. W1D1: retrieve claim/evidence/qualification; choose a rhetorical lens.
2. W1D2: retrieve audience/purpose; add provenance and editorial framing.
3. W1D3: retrieve provenance; preserve conflicting testimony and identify corroboration needs.
4. W1D4: retrieve source ownership; distinguish quotation, paraphrase, patchwriting, inference.
5. W1D5: retrieve all four methods; build a qualified thesis with counterpoint and limitation.
6. W2D1: delayed retrieval of audience/provenance; evaluate a committee claim.
7. W2D2: retrieve corroboration; compare witness reliability without flattening disagreement.
8. W2D3: retrieve editorial framing; separate source wording from later interpretation.
9. W2D4: retrieve paraphrase fidelity; preserve meaning while changing language/structure.
10. W2D5: cumulative transfer; write a conditional conclusion with explicit uncertainty.
11. W3D1: retrieve attribution and rhetorical effect; evaluate diction and implied stance.
12. W3D2: retrieve qualification; analyze how syntax and modality limit a claim.
13. W3D3: retrieve provenance/context; distinguish denotation, connotation, and historical inference.
14. W3D4: retrieve counterreading; revise a thesis to fit all relevant evidence.
15. W3D5: cumulative Unit 01 retrieval; synthesize diction, syntax, attribution, provenance, counterinterpretation, and limitation before assessment.

## Grade 11 mastery boundary

Completion records workflow only. Objective checks provide formative evidence. Advanced interpretation, synthesis, source evaluation, and revision require preserved student artifacts and evaluator judgment.

## Promotion criterion

Grade 11 is ready to promote when each day requires a learner to:
1. retrieve prior knowledge without notes;
2. choose or justify a strategy;
3. evaluate source/rhetorical conditions;
4. preserve counterreading or uncertainty;
5. apply independently;
6. explain a revision or methodological choice;
7. exit with a corrected reconstruction.
