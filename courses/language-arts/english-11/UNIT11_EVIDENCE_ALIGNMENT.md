# English 11 · Unit 11 Evidence Alignment
## Drama, Performance & Long-Form Reading

**Upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Unit 11 propagation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade-level rhythm

**Analyze Contrast → Stage Interpretation → Evaluate Audience Effect → Launch Sustained Inquiry → Track Pattern**

Grade 11 combines advanced drama analysis with the launch of sustained long-form reading, requiring students to move from performance evidence into independent pattern tracking and seminar-ready inquiry.

## Three-week architecture

### Week 31 · Drama Study: Juxtaposition, Rhetoric, and Conflict
1. **Analyze Contrast:** Identify a meaningful juxtaposition in character, rhetoric, setting, or action and explain what interpretive tension it creates.
2. **Select Lens:** Choose whether rhetoric, conflict structure, perspective, or stagecraft best explains the scene and justify the choice.
3. **Evaluate:** Assess how the juxtaposition shapes audience judgment rather than simply describing the contrast.
4. **Counter:** Develop an alternative reading of the same dramatic contrast and identify the textual evidence that keeps it viable.
5. **Synthesize:** Write a qualified claim connecting juxtaposition, rhetoric, conflict, and audience interpretation.

### Week 32 · Drama Performance and Oral Interpretation
1. **Frame Interpretation:** State the interpretation that performance choices should communicate and identify the textual evidence behind it.
2. **Stage:** Choose delivery, blocking, pacing, gesture, and emphasis based on interpretive purpose.
3. **Evaluate Audience Effect:** Collect audience evidence about what relationship, motive, conflict, or theme became clearer or more distorted.
4. **Revise:** Change one performance choice in response to textual or audience evidence and explain the revision.
5. **Defend:** Complete an oral-interpretation defense covering evidence, performance choice, audience effect, counterreading, and limitation.

### Week 33 · Long-Form Novel Study Launch
1. **Launch Inquiry:** Choose a demanding long-form work and establish a reading plan, annotation method, recurring-question log, and evidence-capture routine.
2. **Track Pattern:** Identify an early character, motif, structural, or thematic pattern and record evidence without turning it into a fixed conclusion too soon.
3. **Connect:** Link one new passage to an earlier moment and explain whether the connection strengthens or complicates the emerging interpretation.
4. **Question:** Formulate a seminar-worthy question that requires evidence from multiple points in the work rather than one chapter.
5. **Reflect:** Submit a launch dossier with reading plan, initial pattern, strongest passage, interpretive question, and one risk to sustained attention.

## Spacing and retrieval

The Unit 11 evidence layer uses a 15-day map across Weeks 31–33. Retrieval deliberately returns to:
- prior-unit evidence, publication, rhetoric, interpretation, accessibility, and long-form reading habits;
- prior-day concepts;
- approximately one-week-delayed concepts;
- earlier Unit 11 methods;
- a cumulative publication, performance, long-form inquiry, seminar, or oral-defense audit on the final instructional day.

Each prompt is tied to the actual intellectual work of the day rather than a generic recall routine.

## Reading/source custody

Required reading remains in the existing unit/week pages, reading dossier, locally embedded Academy texts, approved authentic works, dramatic texts, long-form works, mentor texts, and approved multimedia sources. Exact source, edition, translator, creator, performance version, publication venue, media provenance, and citation records remain required where applicable.

## Mastery boundary

The Unit 11 evidence layer:
- writes no learner data;
- changes no storage keys;
- changes no quiz answers or thresholds;
- changes no assessment authority;
- does not convert completion into mastery;
- adds publication planning, source credit, editing, accessibility review, dramatic/performance analysis, sustained-reading tracking, critical-lens evaluation, seminar leadership, oral defense, and exit-audit prompts only.

## Release criterion

Unit 11 is ready when:
1. all 15 instructional days receive the grade-specific evidence layer;
2. the grade-level scaffold fade is preserved;
3. source, performance, publication, and long-form reading custody remain visible;
4. no duplicate generic prompts appear;
5. JavaScript parses cleanly;
6. storage/assessment authority remains unchanged;
7. the unit landing page communicates the learning progression.
