English III Scoring Guide

Literary Analysis Rubric

Score each dimension from 4 to 1. Multiply or convert the total according to the teacher’s grading plan.

Dimension4 · Advanced3 · Proficient2 · Developing1 · Beginning
Interpretation / ThesisInsightful, defensible interpretation that addresses complexityClear interpretation with defensible thesisPartial or obvious interpretation; thesis needs focusUnclear, inaccurate, or missing interpretation
EvidencePrecise, sufficient evidence integrated smoothlyRelevant evidence supports major claimsSome evidence, but weak selection or integrationInsufficient, inaccurate, or missing evidence
Reasoning / CommentarySustained explanation of how evidence supports meaningReasoning usually connects evidence to claimCommentary is repetitive, summary-heavy, or incompleteLittle or no reasoning
OrganizationPurposeful structure, transitions, and paragraph unityLogical structure with clear progressionUneven organization or weak transitionsDisorganized or difficult to follow
Language / ConventionsPrecise academic voice; strong sentence control; nearly error-freeAppropriate voice and generally correct conventionsInconsistent voice or recurring errorsErrors frequently interfere with meaning
Revision / ReflectionSubstantial revision supported by specific reflectionMeaningful revision and clear reflectionLimited revision or general reflectionLittle revision evidence or reflection
