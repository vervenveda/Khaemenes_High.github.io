# English 11 · Unit 04 Evidence Alignment
## Classical Rhetoric, Structure & Persuasion

**Upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Unit 04 propagation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade-level rhythm

**Retrieve → Select Framework → Evaluate → Counter → Synthesize**

Grade 11 requires learners to select an analytical framework, evaluate persuasive effectiveness and ethics, and test claims against competing explanations and rhetorical traditions.

## Three-week architecture

### Week 10 · Classical Period Speeches and Essays: Context
1. **Retrieve:** Recover one Unit 03 lens-selection habit and apply it to a rhetorical text: which contextual factor is essential to understanding the argument, and which is merely background?
2. **Select Framework:** Choose a rhetorical framework appropriate to the text and justify why it is more useful than a competing framework.
3. **Evaluate:** Evaluate how context, speaker position, and central idea interact. Rank the evidence by explanatory importance.
4. **Counter:** Construct a reading that minimizes context and focuses on textual form alone. Where does that reading succeed, and where does it fail?
5. **Synthesize:** Defend a qualified claim about how historical or cultural context shapes rhetorical meaning without replacing close textual analysis.

### Week 11 · Evaluating Text Structures and Features
1. **Retrieve:** Revisit a Week 10 claim and ask which structural feature most strongly supports or complicates it.
2. **Select Framework:** Choose a structure-based lens—sequence, problem/solution, comparison, concession/refutation, cause/effect, or another defensible pattern.
3. **Evaluate:** Assess whether the structure genuinely strengthens reasoning or merely improves presentation. Use specific transitions and evidence placement.
4. **Counter:** Design an alternative structure and argue whether it would improve clarity, fairness, urgency, or persuasiveness.
5. **Synthesize:** Write a structural evaluation that explains both effectiveness and limitation, not simply organization.

### Week 12 · Rhetorical Appeals: Logos, Ethos, Pathos, Kairos
1. **Retrieve:** Identify where Weeks 10–11 showed that rhetorical effectiveness depends on context and structure, not appeal labels alone.
2. **Select Framework:** Choose the appeal or combination of appeals most important to the text’s purpose and justify why another appeal is secondary.
3. **Evaluate:** Assess the quality and ethics of the appeal: Is the reasoning sound? Is credibility earned? Is emotion proportional? Is timing appropriate?
4. **Counter:** Construct the strongest critique of the persuasive strategy. Does effectiveness survive the ethical or logical objection?
5. **Synthesize:** Produce a timed or polished analysis that evaluates how appeals interact with structure, context, and ethical responsibility.

## Spacing and retrieval

The Unit 04 evidence layer uses a 15-day map across Weeks 10–12. Retrieval deliberately returns to:
- prior Unit evidence, qualification, comparison, and method-selection habits;
- prior-day concepts;
- approximately one-week-delayed concepts;
- earlier Unit 04 methods;
- a cumulative Unit 04 transfer, performance, rhetorical defense, or audit task on the final instructional day.

Each prompt is tied to the actual intellectual work of the day rather than a generic recall routine.

## Reading/source custody

Required reading remains in the existing unit/week pages, reading dossier, locally embedded Academy texts, and approved authentic works. Course-created practice texts remain clearly identified as instructional examples. Exact source, edition, translator, speaker, historical-context, and provenance records remain required where applicable.

## Mastery boundary

The Unit 04 evidence layer:
- writes no learner data;
- changes no storage keys;
- changes no quiz answers or thresholds;
- changes no assessment authority;
- does not convert completion into mastery;
- adds retrieval, transfer, comparison, counterreading, composition, ethical evaluation, defense, and exit-audit prompts only.

## Release criterion

Unit 04 is ready when:
1. all 15 instructional days receive the grade-specific evidence layer;
2. the grade-level scaffold fade is preserved;
3. authentic-source custody remains visible;
4. no duplicate generic prompts appear;
5. JavaScript parses cleanly;
6. storage/assessment authority remains unchanged;
7. the unit landing page communicates the learning progression.
