Evidence-Based Grade 11 Learning Rhythm

Analyze Drama → Perform → Reflect → Launch Long Reading

Unit 11 joins advanced dramatic interpretation and performance with the launch of sustained long-form reading, requiring students to carry evidence, ambiguity, collaboration, and strategy selection across modes.

Unit 11 rule: Performance and long-form reading use different methods, but both require explicit evidence, provisional interpretation, and revision when later evidence complicates a claim.

Essential Question

How do dramatic form, performance choices, and sustained reading reveal conflict and human motive?

Learning Architecture

Study drama through stagecraft, rhetoric, juxtaposition, performance, and interpretation; launch a long-form novel or narrative nonfiction study using independent reading plans and seminar protocols.

Universal Course Expectations

  • Use complex literary and informational texts from varied periods, cultures, disciplines, and media.
  • Distinguish evidence, inference, interpretation, evaluation, and synthesis.
  • Write through planning, drafting, feedback, revision, editing, publication, and reflection.
  • Practice oral communication, active listening, intellectual fairness, and responsible source use.
  • Preserve major products and process evidence in the English III portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: Drama analysis/performance and long-form reading dossier with annotations, motif tracking, and conference.

Required close reading · Before the Audience

Original Academy instructional source, supplied offline.

The researcher revised a public talk by defining specialist terms, removing unsupported conclusions, and naming what the study could not show. The message became clearer without becoming simpler than its evidence.

Weekly assignment: Annotate competing claims and source limitations, present an interpretation supported by two textual details, and respond to a credible counterreading. Develop or revise this work through Weeks 31–33; preserve it in the course portfolio. Read full-length assigned literature in addition to this exercise.

Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1

Unit 11 Learning Progression

Analyze Contrast → Stage Interpretation → Evaluate Audience Effect → Launch Sustained Inquiry → Track Pattern

Grade 11 combines advanced drama analysis with the launch of sustained long-form reading, requiring students to move from performance evidence into independent pattern tracking and seminar-ready inquiry.

Week 31

Drama Study: Juxtaposition, Rhetoric, and Conflict

Write a qualified claim connecting juxtaposition, rhetoric, conflict, and audience interpretation.

Week 32

Drama Performance and Oral Interpretation

Complete an oral-interpretation defense covering evidence, performance choice, audience effect, counterreading, and limitation.

Week 33

Long-Form Novel Study Launch

Submit a launch dossier with reading plan, initial pattern, strongest passage, interpretive question, and one risk to sustained attention.

The 15-day sequence revisits prior evidence habits while increasing independence in authentic publication, performance interpretation, sustained reading, critical-method choice, and seminar leadership.
