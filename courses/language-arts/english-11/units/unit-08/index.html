Evidence-Based Grade 11 Learning Rhythm

Compare → Argue → Perform → Adapt → Audit

Unit 08 extends contextual poetry study into sustained written analysis, oral interpretation, and ethical multimedia adaptation while keeping provenance, translation, accessibility, and textual evidence visible.

Week 22 · Comparing Contemporaneous Poets

Shared context, distinct craft, translator/provenance notes, formal contrast, and qualified comparison.

Week 23 · Poetry Analysis Essay

Evaluative thesis, evidence/commentary, alternative reading, substantive revision, and sustained literary argument.

Week 24 · Performance & Digital Interpretation

Oral choices, digital adaptation, coherence, captioning/transcripts, contrast, descriptive text, and ethical presentation.

Multimedia rule: media elements must clarify or deepen the interpretation; decorative competition and inaccessible presentation are removed.

Essential Question

How do comparison, performance, translation, and medium change the meaning of a poem?

Universal Course Expectations

  • Use complex literary and informational texts from varied periods, cultures, disciplines, and media.

  • Practice oral communication, active listening, intellectual fairness, and responsible source use.
  • Preserve major products and process evidence in the English III portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: Comparative poetry essay, oral interpretation, and accessible multimedia adaptation.

Required close reading · A Disagreement in the Sources

Original Academy instructional source, supplied offline.

One report attributed a decline in attendance to cost; another linked it to travel. Neither examined both variables. A scholar comparing them kept the claims separate before proposing an additional study.

Weekly assignment: Annotate competing claims and source limitations, present an interpretation supported by two textual details, and respond to a credible counterreading. Develop or revise this work through Weeks 22–24; preserve it in the course portfolio. Read full-length assigned literature in addition to this exercise.

Unit 08 Learning Progression

Choose Criterion → Compare Context → Build Analysis → Perform Interpretation → Evaluate Medium

Grade 11 asks learners to compare poetry using consistent criteria, connect interpretation to historical context, and evaluate how performance or digital media changes audience experience.

Week 22

Comparing Contemporaneous Poets

Write a comparative claim that preserves both historical relationship and individual artistic difference.

Week 23

Poetry Analysis Essay

Complete a revision memo identifying the strongest analytical move, weakest assumption, and most important change.

Week 24

Poetry Performance and Digital Interpretation

Defend what the chosen medium adds, what it risks changing, and how accessibility and source attribution were preserved.

The 15-day sequence revisits prior evidence habits while increasing independence in usability testing, cultural/source custody, comparative interpretation, performance design, multimodal rhetoric, and representation audits.
