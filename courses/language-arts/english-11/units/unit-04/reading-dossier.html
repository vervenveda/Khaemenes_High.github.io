WEEK 10 · UNIT 04

Classical Speeches & Essays: Context

Original practice address

Citizens, a law written for quiet years may fail during a flood, but emergency power that never ends becomes a different danger. We must decide not only what authority can do, but when it must stop.

Context model

Classical rhetoric should be read with source/date/audience/political context, but context must not replace textual analysis. Identify the problem, speaker position and constraints, then connect rhetorical choices to the occasion.

Independent task

Use an approved classical or comparative rhetorical text. Record author/speaker, translator if relevant, title, edition/source and passage. Add one credible contextual source and explain exactly what it clarifies.

WEEK 11 · UNIT 04

Evaluating Text Structures & Features

Structure contrast

One speech begins with shared values before presenting evidence. Another begins with a narrative example, delays its claim, then closes with a direct appeal.

Advanced model

Evaluate what a structure makes possible: establishing authority, managing resistance, clarifying logic, pacing emotional pressure or reframing a dispute.

Independent task

Map the structure of an approved speech/essay in sections. For each section, identify function, evidence type, audience effect and one alternative organization the speaker could have chosen.

WEEK 12 · UNIT 04

Logos, Ethos, Pathos & Kairos

Practice problem

A speaker has strong statistics but addresses them after the audience has already lost trust. Another speaker has high credibility but offers no evidence for a new factual claim.

Appeal model

Appeals interact. Kairos concerns timing/opportunity; ethos concerns credibility and character; logos concerns reasoning/evidence; pathos concerns emotional framing. None guarantees sound argument.

Independent task

Analyze an approved rhetorical text for all four appeals, including one place where an appeal succeeds rhetorically but remains weak evidentially. Prepare a timed-analysis claim and counterassessment.
