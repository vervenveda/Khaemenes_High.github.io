WEEK 01 · AUTOBIOGRAPHY

Narrative of the Life of Frederick Douglass, Chapter VII

Author: Frederick Douglass · Published: 1845

Assigned span: Chapter VII

Reading focus: literacy; rhetoric; autobiographical evidence; historical context

Evidence task: Analyze how Douglass turns literacy into both narrative event and argument. Distinguish remembered experience, quoted dialogue, and later authorial interpretation.

Source custody: Narrative of the Life of Frederick Douglass, an American Slave, 1845. Published in 1845; public domain worldwide.

WEEK 02 · ESSAY / CULTURAL CRITICISM

The Souls of Black Folk, Chapter I: Of Our Spiritual Strivings

Author: W. E. B. Du Bois · Published: 1903

Assigned span: Chapter I

Reading focus: concept formation; metaphor; historical argument; scholarly qualification

Evidence task: Define one key concept using Du Bois's own development of it, then test whether a modern paraphrase preserves the concept's historical and rhetorical complexity.

Source custody: The Souls of Black Folk, first published 1903. Published before 1931; public domain in the United States.

WEEK 03 · ESSAY

Self-Reliance

Author: Ralph Waldo Emerson · Published: 1841

Assigned span: Opening through the discussion of consistency; teacher may assign the complete essay

Reading focus: aphorism; syntax; abstraction; thesis revision

Evidence task: Select two claims that appear to conflict. Explain the tension through syntax and context, then revise a simplistic summary into a defensible thesis.

Source custody: Essays: First Series, 1841. Published in 1841; public domain.
