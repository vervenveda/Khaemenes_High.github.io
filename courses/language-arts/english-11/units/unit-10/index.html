Unit 10

Research Writing, Narrative Craft & Publication

OverviewWeeksAssessmentStandards

Essential Question

How can writers organize complex knowledge and experience for different audiences and media?

Learning Architecture

Write a sustained researched analysis, an expository synthesis, and a complex narrative; integrate graphics and multimedia; revise for accessibility, precision, coherence, and publication ethics.

Universal Course Expectations

  • Use complex literary and informational texts from varied periods, cultures, disciplines, and media.
  • Distinguish evidence, inference, interpretation, evaluation, and synthesis.
  • Write through planning, drafting, feedback, revision, editing, publication, and reflection.
  • Practice oral communication, active listening, intellectual fairness, and responsible source use.
  • Preserve major products and process evidence in the English III portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: Multi-source research paper, complex narrative, digital presentation, publication checklist, and author reflection.

Read, evaluate, compose, revise, and demonstrate.
