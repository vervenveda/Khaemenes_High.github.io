Unit 09

Literary Research, Source Evaluation & Synthesis

Develop research questions, refine scope, evaluate authority and bias, distinguish primary and secondary evidence, cite ethically, and synthesize sources without flattening disagreement.

Begin Week 25Unit OverviewUnit Assessment
OverviewWeeksAssessmentStandards

Essential Question

How do researchers build trustworthy knowledge from primary, secondary, quantitative, and multimedia evidence?

Learning Architecture

Develop research questions, refine scope, evaluate authority and bias, distinguish primary and secondary evidence, cite ethically, and synthesize sources without flattening disagreement.

Universal Course Expectations

  • Use complex literary and informational texts from varied periods, cultures, disciplines, and media.
  • Distinguish evidence, inference, interpretation, evaluation, and synthesis.
  • Write through planning, drafting, feedback, revision, editing, publication, and reflection.
  • Practice oral communication, active listening, intellectual fairness, and responsible source use.
  • Preserve major products and process evidence in the English III portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: Research proposal, annotated bibliography, source credibility audit, synthesis matrix, and conference.

Three-Week Sequence

Read, evaluate, compose, revise, and demonstrate.
