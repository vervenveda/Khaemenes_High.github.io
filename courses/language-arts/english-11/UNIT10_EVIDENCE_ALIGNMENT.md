# English 11 · Unit 10 Evidence Alignment
## Research Writing, Narrative Craft & Publication

**Upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Unit 10 propagation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade-level rhythm

**Develop Claim → Explain Complexity → Shift Perspective → Design Publication → Audit Ethics**

Grade 11 integrates research, exposition, narrative perspective, multimodal publication, and accessibility while requiring explicit reasoning about audience and publication ethics.

## Three-week architecture

### Week 28 · Literary Analysis with Research
1. **Develop Claim:** State a literary claim that remains grounded in the primary text and identify the specific contribution research will make.
2. **Integrate Scholarship:** Use secondary evidence to clarify, complicate, or contextualize the interpretation rather than substitute for it.
3. **Test:** Identify one scholarly source that challenges your preferred interpretation and revise the argument where necessary.
4. **Synthesize:** Connect primary-text evidence, research, and original reasoning in one coherent analytical sequence.
5. **Audit Ethics:** Document source ownership, interpretive boundaries, and one place where the evidence requires qualification.

### Week 29 · Expository Analysis of Complex Texts
1. **Define Complexity:** Identify the concept, relationship, or text feature that is genuinely difficult and what background the audience needs.
2. **Organize:** Choose a structure that makes complexity visible—comparison, causal chain, classification, process, or layered explanation.
3. **Explain:** Integrate textual and researched evidence without overloading the reader or flattening disagreement.
4. **Test Audience:** Ask whether tone, vocabulary, transitions, and examples fit the intended audience while preserving intellectual precision.
5. **Audit:** Revise for clarity, coherence, accuracy, source attribution, and one explicitly acknowledged limitation.

### Week 30 · Narrative Perspective, Digital Publication & Presentation
1. **Shift Perspective:** Draft a scene or narrative moment through more than one perspective and identify what each viewpoint reveals or conceals.
2. **Integrate:** Connect narrative technique with research or contextual knowledge without turning the narrative into exposition.
3. **Design Publication:** Choose medium, sequence, visuals, audio, typography, or interface elements based on audience and meaning.
4. **Test Accessibility:** Check readability, captions, alt text, navigation, sensory dependence, and whether multimedia choices distort the narrative or evidence.
5. **Audit Ethics:** Complete an author/publisher reflection on perspective, source use, representation, accessibility, audience impact, and revision priorities.

## Spacing and retrieval

The Unit 10 evidence layer uses a 15-day map across Weeks 28–30. Retrieval deliberately returns to:
- prior-unit argument, research, source evaluation, synthesis, publication, and qualification habits;
- prior-day concepts;
- approximately one-week-delayed concepts;
- earlier Unit 10 methods;
- a cumulative debate, publication, professional-communication, accessibility, or consequence audit on the final instructional day.

Each prompt is tied to the actual intellectual work of the day rather than a generic recall routine.

## Reading/source custody

Required reading and research remain in the existing unit/week pages, reading dossier, locally embedded Academy texts, approved authentic works, civic/professional documents, and approved multimedia sources. Exact source, author/creator, publication date, edition, platform, media provenance, permissions, and citation records remain required where applicable.

## Mastery boundary

The Unit 10 evidence layer:
- writes no learner data;
- changes no storage keys;
- changes no quiz answers or thresholds;
- changes no assessment authority;
- does not convert completion into mastery;
- adds argument framing, evidence testing, research writing, counterclaim, publication design, accessibility, professional/civic communication, ethical technology review, and exit-audit prompts only.

## Release criterion

Unit 10 is ready when:
1. all 15 instructional days receive the grade-specific evidence layer;
2. the grade-level scaffold fade is preserved;
3. source, media, attribution, and publication custody remain visible;
4. no duplicate generic prompts appear;
5. JavaScript parses cleanly;
6. storage/assessment authority remains unchanged;
7. the unit landing page communicates the learning progression.
