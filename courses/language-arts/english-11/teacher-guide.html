English III Teacher Guide

A practical guide for delivering, differentiating, documenting, and evaluating the 36-week advanced course.

Recommended weekly rhythm

  1. Monday: context, vocabulary, essential question, source preview.
  2. Tuesday: advanced close reading, evidence matrix, evaluation.
  3. Wednesday: composition and craft studio.
  4. Thursday: seminar, feedback, substantive revision.
  5. Friday: transfer task, quiz, corrections, portfolio reflection.

Instructional time

Plan approximately 150–180 hours for one full high-school credit, or follow the requirement of the student’s jurisdiction or program. Reading outside direct instruction may be documented separately where allowed.

Assessment model

  • Weekly quizzes: 30%
  • Unit assessments: 25%
  • Major projects: 20%
  • Midterm: 10%
  • Final: 15%

Teacher-scored writing, speaking, research, and portfolio evidence should never be replaced by automatically scored quizzes alone.

Advanced expectations

  • Nuanced theses and qualified claims
  • Multiple sources and competing interpretations
  • Evaluation of assumptions, limitations, context, and consequences
  • Long-form reading and sustained writing
  • Ethical citation, source evaluation, and media literacy
  • Oral defense, seminar participation, and publication for authentic audiences

Required records

  • Annual calendar
  • Attendance/instructional time
  • Reading log
  • Gradebook
  • Major writing and research
  • Quiz and assessment corrections
  • Portfolio and reflection
  • Completion certificate

Access and differentiation

Use audio, chunking, alternative formats, assistive technology, extended time, visual supports, oral rehearsal, translated context, or reduced output length while preserving the same intellectual target. Offer extension through independent research, additional comparison, public presentation, or AP-style timed work.
