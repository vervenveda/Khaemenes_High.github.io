Flexible & Globally Inclusive

Reading and Source Selection Guide

Select rigorous, age-appropriate texts that satisfy the skill targets while representing varied periods, cultures, forms, perspectives, disciplines, and media.

The curriculum does not require one commercial anthology or one political, religious, cultural, or literary canon. Teachers should document selections and provide meaningful alternatives when needed.

Literary Core

  • One sustained novel or narrative nonfiction work
  • One complete drama
  • Short fiction from at least four cultural or geographic traditions
  • Poetry across ancient/classical, medieval/early modern, modern, and contemporary periods
  • At least two works in translation or with translation context

Nonfiction & Rhetoric

  • Classical speech or essay
  • Historic and contemporary civic rhetoric
  • Science, environment, technology, health, economics, or social-science writing
  • Biography, memoir, criticism, journalism, and public argument
  • Visual and quantitative sources

Balance Requirements

  • Different cultures, regions, identities, time periods, and viewpoints
  • Competing arguments rather than one-sided source sets
  • Literary and nonliterary texts
  • Print, oral, visual, digital, and multimedia forms
  • Texts with sufficient complexity and teachable context

Selection Review

  • Purpose and standards fit
  • Complexity and accessibility
  • Source reliability and copyright status
  • Cultural and historical context
  • Family/program expectations and alternatives
  • Student readiness and support needs
