About English 11

English 11 advances students toward senior-level, college, career, and civic literacy through complex reading, source evaluation, rhetoric, comparative interpretation, sustained research, advanced composition, seminar, publication, and independent long-form study. Students are expected to qualify claims, test evidence, represent competing perspectives fairly, revise substantively, and defend their reasoning.

Course Features

Advanced Reading & Global Literature Students evaluate meaning, style, context, ambiguity, translation, perspective, and evidence across literary, historical, scientific, civic, and global texts.
Rhetoric & Comparative Argument Classical and civic rhetoric, source comparison, qualified claims, counterarguments, ethical persuasion, and evidence-based debate strengthen mature reasoning.
Research, Craft & Publication Multi-source research, narrative craft, synthesis, digital composition, visual rhetoric, accessibility, citation, and publication ethics support sophisticated communication.
Portfolio Defense & Readiness Long-form study, seminar, corrections, revision memos, cumulative assessment, portfolio curation, student-led defense, and senior-readiness planning document growth.
