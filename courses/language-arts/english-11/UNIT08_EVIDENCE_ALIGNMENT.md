# English 11 · Unit 08 Evidence Alignment
## Comparative Poetry, Performance & Digital Interpretation

**Upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Unit 08 propagation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade-level rhythm

**Choose Criterion → Compare Context → Build Analysis → Perform Interpretation → Evaluate Medium**

Grade 11 asks learners to compare poetry using consistent criteria, connect interpretation to historical context, and evaluate how performance or digital media changes audience experience.

## Three-week architecture

### Week 22 · Comparing Contemporaneous Poets
1. **Choose Criterion:** Select one meaningful criterion—theme, form, speaker, image, rhetoric, sound, or context—for comparing poets from the same period.
2. **Compare Context:** Identify what the poets genuinely share in historical setting and what differs in identity, audience, tradition, or purpose.
3. **Analyze:** Explain how each poet responds differently to the shared topic or pressure using textual evidence.
4. **Counter:** Test whether your comparison overstates similarity because the poets are contemporaries. Identify evidence of divergence.
5. **Synthesize:** Write a comparative claim that preserves both historical relationship and individual artistic difference.

### Week 23 · Poetry Analysis Essay
1. **Frame Claim:** State an evaluative claim connecting time, theme, mood, and poetic choices rather than listing devices.
2. **Select Evidence:** Choose passages that represent the poem’s pattern across the whole work, including evidence that complicates the claim.
3. **Build Analysis:** Connect feature → effect → context → meaning with explicit reasoning in each paragraph.
4. **Counter:** Address a plausible alternative interpretation or contextual explanation and revise overstatement.
5. **Reflect:** Complete a revision memo identifying the strongest analytical move, weakest assumption, and most important change.

### Week 24 · Poetry Performance and Digital Interpretation
1. **Choose Interpretation:** State the reading your performance or multimedia design will communicate and identify the textual evidence supporting it.
2. **Design Medium:** Choose voice, pace, image, typography, sound, sequence, or interface elements that serve the interpretation rather than decorate it.
3. **Test Audience:** Gather evidence from a listener or viewer about what meaning the performance or media actually communicated.
4. **Revise:** Adjust performance or design where the audience response reveals distortion, distraction, or accessibility problems.
5. **Evaluate Medium:** Defend what the chosen medium adds, what it risks changing, and how accessibility and source attribution were preserved.

## Spacing and retrieval

The Unit 08 evidence layer uses a 15-day map across Weeks 22–24. Retrieval deliberately returns to:
- prior-unit evidence, synthesis, comparison, source evaluation, rhetoric, and qualification habits;
- prior-day concepts;
- approximately one-week-delayed concepts;
- earlier Unit 08 methods;
- a cumulative usability, adaptation, performance, multimedia, global-issue, or representation audit on the final instructional day.

Each prompt is tied to the actual intellectual work of the day rather than a generic recall routine.

## Reading/source custody

Required reading remains in the existing unit/week pages, reading dossier, locally embedded Academy texts, approved authentic works, and approved multimedia sources. Exact source, author/creator, publication date, edition, translator, cultural/religious context, medium, platform, and provenance remain required where applicable.

## Mastery boundary

The Unit 08 evidence layer:
- writes no learner data;
- changes no storage keys;
- changes no quiz answers or thresholds;
- changes no assessment authority;
- does not convert completion into mastery;
- adds retrieval, explanation, usability testing, adaptation comparison, cultural/contextual analysis, performance, multimedia interpretation, accessibility review, and exit-audit prompts only.

## Release criterion

Unit 08 is ready when:
1. all 15 instructional days receive the grade-specific evidence layer;
2. the grade-level scaffold fade is preserved;
3. authentic-source, cultural, translation, and multimedia custody remain visible;
4. no duplicate generic prompts appear;
5. JavaScript parses cleanly;
6. storage/assessment authority remains unchanged;
7. the unit landing page communicates the learning progression.
