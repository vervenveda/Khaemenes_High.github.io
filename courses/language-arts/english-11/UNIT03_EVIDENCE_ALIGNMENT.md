# English 11 · Unit 03 Evidence Alignment
## Perspective, Juxtaposition & Literary Argument

**Upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Unit 03 propagation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade-level rhythm

**Retrieve → Select Lens → Evaluate → Counter → Synthesize**

Grade 11 requires learners to choose and defend an analytical lens, weigh evidence quality, and respond to counterreadings rather than follow a fixed method.

## Three-week architecture

### Week 7 · Juxtaposition and Character Perspective
1. **Retrieve:** Recover a Unit 02 lens-selection principle. Decide which lens best reveals the significance of a contrast in character or perspective and justify the choice.
2. **Select Lens:** Analyze one juxtaposition through two possible lenses. Explain what each reveals and what each obscures.
3. **Evaluate:** Rank the available evidence by explanatory power. Which detail most strongly changes how the reader evaluates the character’s perspective?
4. **Counter:** Develop the strongest counterreading of your interpretation. Identify the point at which the counterreading becomes less persuasive.
5. **Synthesize:** Defend a qualified claim about perspective that incorporates both juxtaposition and the strongest counterevidence.

### Week 8 · Mood Through Figurative Language
1. **Retrieve:** Revisit one Week 7 claim about perspective. Ask whether figurative language reinforces that perspective or creates distance from it.
2. **Select Lens:** Choose whether image pattern, metaphor, syntax, sound, or another feature is the most productive route into the passage. Defend the method.
3. **Evaluate:** Distinguish a local effect from a pattern. Which repeated or structurally important feature best explains the text’s mood?
4. **Counter:** Test whether your mood interpretation survives a plausible alternative reading of the same figurative language.
5. **Synthesize:** Write an interpretive claim that explains how language produces mood and why that mood matters to the text’s larger argument or meaning.

### Week 9 · Literary Analysis, Revision & Portfolio
1. **Retrieve:** Identify the analytical method that has produced your strongest work in Unit 03 and explain why it was appropriate to the evidence.
2. **Select Lens:** Choose the lens for your final literary argument and explicitly reject at least one less useful approach.
3. **Evaluate:** Audit source passages for representativeness and strength. Replace any evidence that merely repeats the claim instead of testing it.
4. **Counter:** Write and answer the strongest reasonable objection to your argument without caricaturing it.
5. **Synthesize:** Complete the oral/written defense by stating the claim, method, strongest evidence, counterargument, limitation, and revision you would make with more time.

## Spacing and retrieval

The Unit 03 evidence layer uses a 15-day map across Weeks 7–9. Retrieval deliberately returns to:
- Unit 01–02 evidence, qualification, and comparison habits;
- prior-day concepts;
- approximately one-week-delayed concepts;
- earlier Unit 03 methods;
- a cumulative Unit 03 transfer, defense, or audit task on the final instructional day.

Each prompt is tied to the actual intellectual work of the day rather than a generic recall routine.

## Reading/source custody

Required reading remains in the existing unit/week pages, reading dossier, locally embedded Academy texts, and approved authentic works. Course-created practice texts remain clearly identified as instructional examples. Exact source, edition, translator, and provenance records remain required where applicable.

## Mastery boundary

The Unit 03 evidence layer:
- writes no learner data;
- changes no storage keys;
- changes no quiz answers or thresholds;
- changes no assessment authority;
- does not convert completion into mastery;
- adds retrieval, transfer, counterreading, qualification, revision, and exit-audit prompts only.

## Release criterion

Unit 03 is ready when:
1. all 15 instructional days receive the grade-specific retrieval/transfer layer;
2. the grade-level scaffold fade is preserved;
3. authentic-source custody remains visible;
4. no duplicate generic prompts appear;
5. JavaScript parses cleanly;
6. storage/assessment authority remains unchanged;
7. the unit landing page communicates the learning progression.
