# English 11 · Unit 06 Evidence Alignment
## Rhetorical Composition & Midyear Mastery

**Upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Unit 06 propagation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade-level rhythm

**Frame Judgment → Compose → Perform → Diagnose → Recalibrate**

Grade 11 requires evaluative rhetorical writing, oral defense, cumulative mastery evidence, and a midyear improvement plan grounded in specific performance data.

## Three-week architecture

### Week 16 · Rhetorical Analysis Essay Workshop
1. **Frame Judgment:** State the rhetorical effectiveness claim you intend to defend and define the criteria for effectiveness.
2. **Select Evidence:** Choose evidence that represents the text’s strategy across the whole argument, not just memorable devices.
3. **Compose:** Draft analysis that connects rhetorical choice, audience, context, reasoning, and effect.
4. **Counter:** Address a plausible alternative evaluation of the same rhetorical choices and explain why your judgment remains stronger or must be qualified.
5. **Reflect:** Complete a revision audit identifying your strongest analytical move, weakest assumption, and most important revision.

### Week 17 · Oral Rhetoric and Seminar
1. **Frame:** Prepare a claim for oral discussion with evidence strong enough to survive questioning.
2. **Perform:** Present the claim concisely and use delivery to clarify rather than decorate the argument.
3. **Listen:** Record one challenge or question that exposes a weakness, ambiguity, or assumption in your position.
4. **Respond:** Revise or defend the claim in response to evidence from the seminar, distinguishing concession from surrender.
5. **Reflect:** Write an oral-rhetoric reflection on evidence, audience response, delivery, and one change you would make before presenting again.

### Week 18 · Midterm Review and Midterm Test
1. **Retrieve:** Map first-semester skills across literary evaluation, rhetoric, argument, language, and evidence. Mark where your confidence is unsupported by performance.
2. **Diagnose:** Use prior work to identify recurring reasoning or writing failures rather than reviewing every topic equally.
3. **Demonstrate:** Complete the cumulative midterm under existing assessment authority and timing rules.
4. **Correct:** Classify errors by concept, reasoning, evidence use, or language control and write a correction rationale.
5. **Recalibrate:** Set a second-semester priority based on the strongest pattern in your evidence, with a concrete benchmark for improvement.

## Spacing and retrieval

The Unit 06 evidence layer uses a 15-day map across Weeks 16–18. Retrieval deliberately returns to:
- first-semester evidence, interpretation, rhetoric, argument, synthesis, and language habits;
- prior-day concepts;
- approximately one-week-delayed concepts;
- earlier Unit 06 methods;
- cumulative midyear assessment evidence, corrections, and a next-step improvement plan.

Each prompt is tied to the actual intellectual work of the day rather than a generic review routine.

## Reading/source custody

Required reading remains in the existing unit/week pages, reading dossier, locally embedded Academy texts, approved authentic works, and approved source sets. Course-created practice texts remain clearly identified as instructional examples. Exact source, author/speaker, publication date, edition, translator, data provenance, and research limitations remain required where applicable.

## Mastery boundary

The Unit 06 evidence layer:
- writes no learner data;
- changes no storage keys;
- changes no quiz or midterm answers or thresholds;
- changes no assessment authority;
- does not convert completion into mastery;
- adds retrieval, synthesis, rhetorical analysis/composition, source evaluation, corrections analysis, reflection, and evidence-based planning prompts only.

## Release criterion

Unit 06 is ready when:
1. all 15 instructional days receive the grade-specific evidence layer;
2. the grade-level scaffold fade is preserved;
3. authentic-source and research custody remain visible;
4. no duplicate generic prompts appear;
5. JavaScript parses cleanly;
6. storage/assessment authority remains unchanged;
7. the unit landing page communicates the learning progression;
8. midyear reflection is tied to demonstrated evidence rather than self-report alone.
