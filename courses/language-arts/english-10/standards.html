Standards & Assessment Alignment

English II Standards

The course emphasizes Grade 10 Florida B.E.S.T. Reading, Communication, Vocabulary, and Expectations benchmarks while remaining recognizable within national Grade 9–10 ELA/Literacy skill bands.

Coverage Principles

  • Benchmarks are revisited through increasingly independent work.
  • Reading, writing, speaking, vocabulary, language, research, and media are integrated rather than isolated.
  • Mastery requires authentic products and explanation, not selected-response scores alone.
  • Students correct errors and repeat targeted practice when mastery is below 80%.
  • Official requirements and assessment documents should be rechecked each school year.
