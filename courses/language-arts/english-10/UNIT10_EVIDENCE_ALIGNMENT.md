# English 10 · Unit 10 Evidence Alignment
## Research Writing & Digital Publication

**Upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Unit 10 propagation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade-level rhythm

**Explain → Argue → Integrate Evidence → Design for Audience → Publish**

Grade 10 moves from research foundations into audience-ready publication by requiring accurate synthesis, nuanced argument, source integration, revision, and evidence-preserving design.

## Three-week architecture

### Week 28 · Expository Research Writing
1. **Explain:** Define the complex topic and identify what the reader must understand before encountering details or debate.
2. **Organize:** Choose an explanatory structure that reveals relationships among ideas instead of following source order.
3. **Integrate Evidence:** Combine multiple sources around one explanatory point while preserving important disagreement or uncertainty.
4. **Test Clarity:** Identify one place where simplification becomes distortion or where jargon blocks comprehension, then revise it.
5. **Publish:** Produce an explanatory section with accurate attribution, clear structure, and one stated limitation.

### Week 29 · Argument Research Writing
1. **Frame Claim:** State a nuanced position with clear scope, conditions, and terms rather than an absolute claim.
2. **Integrate Evidence:** Select evidence for relevance, quality, and diversity of perspective rather than volume.
3. **Counter:** Represent the strongest opposing argument and respond with concession, distinction, or rebuttal supported by evidence.
4. **Test:** Identify where your reasoning depends on an assumption or incomplete source base and qualify the claim accordingly.
5. **Publish:** Draft a research argument section that links claim, evidence, reasoning, counterclaim, and limitation.

### Week 30 · Digital Publication, Multimedia & Presentation
1. **Design for Audience:** Define audience, purpose, platform, and accessibility needs before choosing layout or media.
2. **Integrate:** Decide which ideas belong in prose, image, chart, audio, video, or live explanation without duplicating or distorting evidence.
3. **Test:** Check captions, alt text, labels, source attribution, visual scales, contrast, and whether design implies claims the evidence does not support.
4. **Present:** Deliver findings with clear structure and evidence while adapting to audience questions.
5. **Publish:** Complete a publication audit covering accuracy, citation, accessibility, design rationale, audience effect, and one remaining limitation.

## Spacing and retrieval

The Unit 10 evidence layer uses a 15-day map across Weeks 28–30. Retrieval deliberately returns to:
- prior-unit argument, research, source evaluation, synthesis, publication, and qualification habits;
- prior-day concepts;
- approximately one-week-delayed concepts;
- earlier Unit 10 methods;
- a cumulative debate, publication, professional-communication, accessibility, or consequence audit on the final instructional day.

Each prompt is tied to the actual intellectual work of the day rather than a generic recall routine.

## Reading/source custody

Required reading and research remain in the existing unit/week pages, reading dossier, locally embedded Academy texts, approved authentic works, civic/professional documents, and approved multimedia sources. Exact source, author/creator, publication date, edition, platform, media provenance, permissions, and citation records remain required where applicable.

## Mastery boundary

The Unit 10 evidence layer:
- writes no learner data;
- changes no storage keys;
- changes no quiz answers or thresholds;
- changes no assessment authority;
- does not convert completion into mastery;
- adds argument framing, evidence testing, research writing, counterclaim, publication design, accessibility, professional/civic communication, ethical technology review, and exit-audit prompts only.

## Release criterion

Unit 10 is ready when:
1. all 15 instructional days receive the grade-specific evidence layer;
2. the grade-level scaffold fade is preserved;
3. source, media, attribution, and publication custody remain visible;
4. no duplicate generic prompts appear;
5. JavaScript parses cleanly;
6. storage/assessment authority remains unchanged;
7. the unit landing page communicates the learning progression.
