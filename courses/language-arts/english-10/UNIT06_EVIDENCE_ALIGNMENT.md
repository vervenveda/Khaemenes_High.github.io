# English 10 · Unit 06 Evidence Alignment
## Synthesis & Midyear Mastery

**Upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Unit 06 propagation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade-level rhythm

**Gather → Connect → Refine → Demonstrate → Plan**

Grade 10 shifts from single-text analysis into source synthesis, language refinement, portfolio curation, and evidence-based midyear reflection.

## Three-week architecture

### Week 16 · Expository Synthesis Across Sources
1. **Gather:** Identify what each source contributes that the others do not. Separate repetition from genuinely new information.
2. **Connect:** Group sources by relationship—agreement, extension, qualification, contradiction, or different perspective—rather than by source order.
3. **Synthesize:** Draft a paragraph that combines at least two sources around one idea without turning into source-by-source summary.
4. **Test:** Identify one place where your synthesis forces agreement or ignores an important difference. Revise it.
5. **Reflect:** Write a synthesis claim that represents the evidence accurately and identifies one unresolved tension.

### Week 17 · Grammar, Style, Vocabulary & Writing Portfolio
1. **Diagnose:** Select one repeated issue in your writing—clarity, sentence control, punctuation, diction, tone, or organization—and locate evidence across multiple pieces.
2. **Refine:** Revise a passage for clarity and audience fit. Explain exactly what changed and why.
3. **Compare:** Compare an earlier and revised version. Identify which language choices improved precision or authority.
4. **Curate:** Choose portfolio evidence that demonstrates growth rather than simply your highest score.
5. **Reflect:** Write a portfolio note identifying one strength, one persistent weakness, and one concrete language goal for the next term.

### Week 18 · Midterm Review, Assessment & Reflection
1. **Retrieve:** Use prior assessments and portfolio evidence to identify the three most important concepts or skills to retrieve before the midterm.
2. **Prioritize:** Separate skills you recognize from skills you can perform independently. Build review around evidence of actual difficulty.
3. **Demonstrate:** Complete cumulative assessment work under the existing midterm rules without changing mastery authority.
4. **Correct:** Analyze missed items or weak responses by skill, not just by question number. Identify the misconception or reasoning error.
5. **Plan:** Create a midyear improvement plan with one reading goal, one writing goal, one language goal, and the evidence that will show progress.

## Spacing and retrieval

The Unit 06 evidence layer uses a 15-day map across Weeks 16–18. Retrieval deliberately returns to:
- first-semester evidence, interpretation, rhetoric, argument, synthesis, and language habits;
- prior-day concepts;
- approximately one-week-delayed concepts;
- earlier Unit 06 methods;
- cumulative midyear assessment evidence, corrections, and a next-step improvement plan.

Each prompt is tied to the actual intellectual work of the day rather than a generic review routine.

## Reading/source custody

Required reading remains in the existing unit/week pages, reading dossier, locally embedded Academy texts, approved authentic works, and approved source sets. Course-created practice texts remain clearly identified as instructional examples. Exact source, author/speaker, publication date, edition, translator, data provenance, and research limitations remain required where applicable.

## Mastery boundary

The Unit 06 evidence layer:
- writes no learner data;
- changes no storage keys;
- changes no quiz or midterm answers or thresholds;
- changes no assessment authority;
- does not convert completion into mastery;
- adds retrieval, synthesis, rhetorical analysis/composition, source evaluation, corrections analysis, reflection, and evidence-based planning prompts only.

## Release criterion

Unit 06 is ready when:
1. all 15 instructional days receive the grade-specific evidence layer;
2. the grade-level scaffold fade is preserved;
3. authentic-source and research custody remain visible;
4. no duplicate generic prompts appear;
5. JavaScript parses cleanly;
6. storage/assessment authority remains unchanged;
7. the unit landing page communicates the learning progression;
8. midyear reflection is tied to demonstrated evidence rather than self-report alone.
