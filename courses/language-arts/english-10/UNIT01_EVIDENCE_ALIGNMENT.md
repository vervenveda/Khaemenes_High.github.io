# English 10 · Unit 01 Evidence Alignment
## Foundations of Scholarly Reading & Writing

**Audit/upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Canonical Grade 10 implementation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade 10 instructional difference

English 10 preserves the same evidence-supported rhythm as English 9 but deliberately reduces sentence-level scaffolding. Students are expected to compare interpretations, evaluate source scope and credibility, qualify claims, integrate multiple sources, and explain why one line of reasoning is more defensible than another.

The stable rhythm remains:

**Retrieve → Orient → Model → Practice → Apply → Explain → Check → Exit**

## Fifteen-step spaced retrieval map

1. **W1 Monday:** retrieve English 9 claim → evidence → explanation; establish Grade 10 baseline.
2. **W1 Tuesday:** retrieve observation / inference / limitation; add context and source conditions.
3. **W1 Wednesday:** retrieve evidence ladder; compare how perspective changes interpretation.
4. **W1 Thursday:** retrieve scope and qualifiers; revise an overclaim for accuracy and audience.
5. **W1 Friday:** retrieve the whole Week 1 process; explain growth using preserved evidence.
6. **W2 Monday:** after delay, retrieve claim scope and evidence limits; annotate a new map problem.
7. **W2 Tuesday:** retrieve context and uncertainty; compare visual/document claims against method.
8. **W2 Wednesday:** retrieve observation → evidence → inference → reasoning; compose a qualified paragraph.
9. **W2 Thursday:** retrieve charitable counterreading; revise without inventing motive.
10. **W2 Friday:** cumulative transfer; synthesize paired sources into a narrower defensible thesis.
11. **W3 Monday:** retrieve quotation / paraphrase / analysis distinctions; examine borrowed language.
12. **W3 Tuesday:** retrieve sample/scope limits; write a precise summary without overclaiming.
13. **W3 Wednesday:** retrieve source comparison; represent disagreement without fictional consensus.
14. **W3 Thursday:** retrieve attribution and scope; produce an independent paraphrase and revise meaning.
15. **W3 Friday:** cumulative Unit 01 retrieval and ethical synthesis before unit assessment/portfolio review.

## Reading custody

All required instructional materials for the core Unit 01 reasoning path are locally embedded or local-repository pages. Fictional simulations are explicitly labeled as such. Optional external media never replaces required written instruction.

A future release pass should verify:
- transcript/caption status for optional video media;
- provenance metadata for any public-domain excerpt that replaces or supplements local instructional text;
- offline caching of every required dossier asset.

## Grade 10 mastery boundary

Completion controls record workflow only. Objective quizzes support formative mastery, but writing, synthesis, source-use, revision, and portfolio outcomes require the corresponding student artifacts and evaluator evidence.

## Page organization target

The Grade 10 Unit 01 layer:
- adds a concise closed-notes retrieval prompt before each instructional day;
- identifies what earlier skill is being spaced or interleaved;
- adds an exit retrieval prompt after the day's independent work;
- preserves the weekly classroom structure for Weeks 2–3 instead of creating unnecessary standalone HTML pages;
- leaves support and extension available but secondary to the main learning path;
- writes no new learner data and changes no assessment authority.

## Promotion criterion for this blueprint

The Grade 10 pattern is ready to promote when:
1. all 15 instructional days expose retrieval → model → guided practice → independent transfer → exit retrieval;
2. the paired dossiers remain directly reachable from the week;
3. reading/source boundaries remain explicit;
4. no completion control can confer written mastery;
5. Grade 10 prompts are measurably less scaffolded than Grade 9 while preserving access supports.
