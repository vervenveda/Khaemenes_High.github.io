Flexible Text Selection

English II Reading List

The course uses text families rather than one commercial anthology. Families may select public-domain, library, school-provided, or lawfully accessed texts that meet the week’s complexity, maturity, cultural, and instructional goals.

Reading Log

Short Fiction & Coming-of-Age

  • Teacher-selected public-domain short stories
  • Family-approved contemporary short fiction
  • Memoir excerpts and personal essays

American Speeches & Essays

  • George Washington, Farewell Address (excerpt)
  • Frederick Douglass, selected speeches
  • Abraham Lincoln, selected speeches
  • Sojourner Truth, selected speech
  • Shirley Chisholm, selected speech or essay

Poetry

  • Public-domain poetry across eras and cultures
  • Teacher-selected contemporary poems with permission or lawful access
  • Paired poems sharing theme, image, or form

Classical, Mythic & Religious Sources

  • Selections from Greek and Roman mythology
  • Teacher-selected world mythology
  • Literary excerpts from religious texts studied respectfully and contextually

Drama

  • Teacher-selected public-domain drama
  • A Shakespearean scene, speech, or play
  • Short contemporary drama with lawful access

Long-Form Novel

  • One teacher- or family-approved Grade 10 novel selected for complexity, maturity, and student readiness

Selection Criteria

  • Appropriate complexity for a Grade 10 learner, with support where needed.
  • Sufficient literary, informational, rhetorical, or research value for the target benchmark.
  • Lawful access and accurate citation.
  • Family, school, or teacher approval for maturity, content, and student readiness.
  • Representation across cultures, eras, genres, viewpoints, and forms.
  • Opportunities for sustained reading as well as close study of shorter texts.

Text Record

Record every major text in the reading log, including title, author or source, dates, pages or sections, and a brief response.
