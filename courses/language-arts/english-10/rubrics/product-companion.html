Authentic Assessment

English II Product Companion Rubrics

Use these product-specific criteria beside the existing literary analysis, argument, research, speaking, and narrative rubrics. They sharpen evaluator attention without changing the 80% mastery threshold, assessment authority, or existing score records.

How to use: score the established mode rubric as usual. Then use the four companion criteria for targeted feedback and evidence review. A companion criterion does not independently award mastery and never converts completion into mastery.

Unit 01

Diagnostic portfolio

  • Baseline evidence is honest
  • Growth plan is specific
  • Revision shows response to evidence
  • Portfolio organization supports review

Unit 02

Character & perspective analysis

  • Perspective distinctions
  • Evidence map quality
  • Discussion uses textual evidence
  • Reflection revises an earlier interpretation

Unit 03

Literary analysis essay

  • Interpretive claim complexity
  • Evidence integration
  • Paragraph-level reasoning
  • Revision memo identifies substantive changes

Unit 04

Rhetorical analysis

  • Rhetorical situation
  • Choice-effect analysis
  • Historical/contextual accuracy
  • Style and evidence integration

Unit 05

Argument & oral defense

  • Defensible claim
  • Counterclaim fairness
  • Evidence and warrants
  • Oral defense responds to challenge

Unit 06

Synthesis project & conference

  • Source relationships
  • Synthesis quality
  • Portfolio evidence of growth
  • Conference explanation and next-step reasoning

Unit 07

Poetry comparison & reading

  • Comparative interpretation
  • Form/sound analysis
  • Expressive reading choices
  • Reflection connects performance to meaning

Unit 08

Adaptation/multimedia analysis

  • Medium-specific analysis
  • Comparison across versions
  • Evidence from text and adaptation
  • Accessibility/attribution of media

Unit 09

Research proposal & source matrix

  • Question scope
  • Source diversity and credibility
  • Evaluation matrix quality
  • Synthesis paragraph integrates sources

Unit 10

Research paper & digital publication

  • Research depth
  • Publication design/audience fit
  • Citation and rights integrity
  • Formal presentation communicates findings

Unit 11

Drama analysis & performance

  • Interpretation from text
  • Performance/staging choices
  • Collaboration or rehearsal evidence
  • Reflection evaluates performance decisions

Unit 12

Novel analysis & portfolio conference

  • Whole-work analysis
  • Independent reading evidence
  • Portfolio curation
  • Conference defense of growth
