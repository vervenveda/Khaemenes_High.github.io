English II Scoring Guide

Argument Writing Rubric

Score each dimension from 4 to 1. Multiply or convert the total according to the teacher’s grading plan.

Dimension4 · Advanced3 · Proficient2 · Developing1 · Beginning
Claim / PositionNuanced, precise, and fully defensible claimClear, defensible claimOverbroad, inconsistent, or partially defensible claimMissing or unclear claim
Evidence / SourcesMultiple credible sources selected and integrated strategicallyRelevant credible evidence from multiple sourcesLimited, uneven, or weakly integrated evidenceInsufficient or unreliable evidence
Reasoning / WarrantsLogical reasoning explicitly connects evidence and claimReasoning generally supports the claimReasoning contains gaps or unsupported assumptionsReasoning is missing or invalid
Counterclaim / RebuttalFairly represents complexity and offers convincing rebuttalAddresses a credible counterclaim and responds logicallyMentions counterclaim but misrepresents or weakly rebutsIgnores counterclaims
Organization / ToneStrategic organization, transitions, and formal objective toneLogical organization and appropriate toneUneven organization or inconsistent toneDisorganized or inappropriate tone
Conventions / CitationConsistent citation and strong command of conventionsMostly correct citation and conventionsRecurring citation or convention problemsErrors or missing citations undermine credibility
