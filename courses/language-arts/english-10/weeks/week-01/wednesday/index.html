Day 3 · Original short-short story

Two Voices at Dawn

At sunrise, Priya described the train station as a beginning. Her grandfather called it a place of farewells. They stood on the same platform and watched the same train depart. Each memory arranged the morning differently: she noticed light on the windows, while he counted how long the empty track stayed silent. Neither description was false, yet neither exhausted the meaning of the scene.

Close-reading question: Interpret how detail and perspective shape different tones.

Composition & Craft Studio

Objective: Transform reading and discussion evidence into diagnostic analytical response and individual grade 10 literacy growth plan.

Mini-Lesson

Analyze a mentor paragraph or product for controlling idea, organization, integrated evidence, commentary, transitions, voice, and the week’s language focus: sentence boundaries, paragraph unity, and complete thoughts.

Guided Practice

Plan the product with a claim or controlling idea, evidence sequence, reasoning notes, and audience/purpose check. Draft one section collaboratively or through teacher modeling.

Independent Work

Draft the week’s major product: Diagnostic analytical response and individual Grade 10 literacy growth plan. Highlight the sentence with the strongest reasoning and mark one place that still needs development.

Evidence to Preserve

Plan, draft, and self-identified revision target

Support and access options

Use an outline, paragraph frame, speech-to-text, model sentence patterns, or reduced writing length while maintaining the same thinking target.

Extension

Revise one paragraph twice for two different audiences or tones and explain the consequences of each revision.
