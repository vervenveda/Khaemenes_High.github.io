# English 10 · Unit 04 Evidence Alignment
## American Speeches, Essays & Rhetoric

**Upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Unit 04 propagation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade-level rhythm

**Retrieve → Trace → Analyze → Test → Synthesize**

Grade 10 shifts from identifying rhetorical devices to tracing how structure, audience, evidence, and language work together across an entire historical argument.

## Three-week architecture

### Week 10 · Historical American Speeches: Central Ideas & Structure
1. **Retrieve:** Recall one Unit 03 evidence habit and use it to distinguish a speech’s topic from its central idea or claim.
2. **Trace:** Map how the speech or essay develops its central idea across beginning, middle, and end. Identify the structural turn that matters most.
3. **Analyze:** Explain how one piece of evidence, example, or structural choice advances the argument rather than merely appearing in it.
4. **Test:** Propose a different organizational sequence. What persuasive strength would be lost or gained if the text were arranged that way?
5. **Synthesize:** Write a structural analysis claim that connects central idea, sequence, evidence, and audience effect.

### Week 11 · Purpose, Audience, Rhetorical Situation & Appeals
1. **Retrieve:** Return to Week 10 and identify one structural choice that makes more sense when audience or occasion is considered.
2. **Trace:** Identify speaker, audience, purpose, occasion, and context. Distinguish direct evidence from assumptions about the historical situation.
3. **Analyze:** Evaluate how ethos, pathos, logos, or timing is adapted to this particular audience rather than naming appeals in isolation.
4. **Test:** Imagine a different audience or occasion. Which rhetorical choices would become ineffective, inappropriate, or less persuasive?
5. **Synthesize:** Write a rhetorical-situation claim that explains why a specific choice fits the audience, purpose, and context.

### Week 12 · Rhetorical Devices, Figurative Language & Analysis Essay
1. **Retrieve:** Select one rhetorical situation insight from Week 11 that should control your interpretation of a device or figurative choice.
2. **Trace:** Track one repeated device, image, phrase, or syntax pattern across the text and explain how its function develops.
3. **Analyze:** Distinguish effect from label: explain what the rhetorical choice actually does for meaning, credibility, urgency, or audience response.
4. **Test:** Develop a competing interpretation of the same rhetorical choice. What evidence makes one reading stronger?
5. **Synthesize:** Draft and revise a rhetorical analysis paragraph that links choice → evidence → effect → purpose without relying on device-name spotting.

## Spacing and retrieval

The Unit 04 evidence layer uses a 15-day map across Weeks 10–12. Retrieval deliberately returns to:
- prior Unit evidence, qualification, comparison, and method-selection habits;
- prior-day concepts;
- approximately one-week-delayed concepts;
- earlier Unit 04 methods;
- a cumulative Unit 04 transfer, performance, rhetorical defense, or audit task on the final instructional day.

Each prompt is tied to the actual intellectual work of the day rather than a generic recall routine.

## Reading/source custody

Required reading remains in the existing unit/week pages, reading dossier, locally embedded Academy texts, and approved authentic works. Course-created practice texts remain clearly identified as instructional examples. Exact source, edition, translator, speaker, historical-context, and provenance records remain required where applicable.

## Mastery boundary

The Unit 04 evidence layer:
- writes no learner data;
- changes no storage keys;
- changes no quiz answers or thresholds;
- changes no assessment authority;
- does not convert completion into mastery;
- adds retrieval, transfer, comparison, counterreading, composition, ethical evaluation, defense, and exit-audit prompts only.

## Release criterion

Unit 04 is ready when:
1. all 15 instructional days receive the grade-specific evidence layer;
2. the grade-level scaffold fade is preserved;
3. authentic-source custody remains visible;
4. no duplicate generic prompts appear;
5. JavaScript parses cleanly;
6. storage/assessment authority remains unchanged;
7. the unit landing page communicates the learning progression.
