# English 10 · Unit 05 Evidence Alignment
## Argument, Debate & Civic Discourse

**Upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Unit 05 propagation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade-level rhythm

**Represent → Evaluate → Build → Rebut → Deliberate**

Grade 10 emphasizes fair representation of opposing views, validity, evidence quality, counterclaim, rebuttal, and ethical oral response.

## Three-week architecture

### Week 13 · Opposing Arguments & Validity
1. **Represent:** Summarize two opposing arguments so that a reasonable supporter of each position would recognize the summary as fair.
2. **Evaluate:** Separate claim, evidence, reasoning, and assumption in each argument. Identify the strongest support and the weakest link.
3. **Compare:** Apply the same validity and evidence criteria to both sides. Which differences are factual, logical, ethical, or value-based?
4. **Test:** Identify one claim that would change if a key piece of evidence were removed or disproved. Explain the dependency.
5. **Deliberate:** Write a balanced comparison that states where each argument is strongest, where each overreaches, and what remains unresolved.

### Week 14 · Claim, Evidence, Counterclaim & Rebuttal
1. **Represent:** State your own claim and the strongest reasonable counterclaim without weakening either side.
2. **Evaluate:** Rank your evidence by relevance, credibility, and sufficiency. Remove evidence that is merely related but not probative.
3. **Build:** Draft a line of reasoning in which each paragraph advances the claim rather than repeating it.
4. **Rebut:** Respond to the strongest counterclaim by conceding what is valid before explaining where your position remains stronger.
5. **Deliberate:** Revise the argument for precision: qualify overstatement, clarify assumptions, and identify one unresolved limitation.

### Week 15 · Oral Argument, Discussion & Formal Debate
1. **Represent:** Prepare an opening that states your position and the opposing position accurately enough for productive debate.
2. **Evaluate:** Listen for evidence quality and reasoning, not just delivery. Record one point from the other side that deserves serious consideration.
3. **Build:** Use questions to clarify definitions, assumptions, and evidence before challenging conclusions.
4. **Rebut:** Respond directly to the strongest opposing argument without changing the subject or attacking the speaker.
5. **Deliberate:** Complete a debate reflection identifying your strongest evidence, the best opposing point, one claim you revised, and one question still open.

## Spacing and retrieval

The Unit 05 evidence layer uses a 15-day map across Weeks 13–15. Retrieval deliberately returns to:
- prior-unit evidence, interpretation, structure, rhetoric, and qualification habits;
- prior-day concepts;
- approximately one-week-delayed concepts;
- earlier Unit 05 methods;
- a cumulative Unit 05 defense, deliberation, verification, or calibrated-judgment task on the final instructional day.

Each prompt is tied to the actual intellectual work of the day rather than a generic recall routine.

## Reading/source custody

Required reading remains in the existing unit/week pages, reading dossier, locally embedded Academy texts, and approved authentic works. Course-created practice texts remain clearly identified as instructional examples. Exact source, edition, author/speaker, publication date, historical context, data provenance, and translation records remain required where applicable.

## Mastery boundary

The Unit 05 evidence layer:
- writes no learner data;
- changes no storage keys;
- changes no quiz answers or thresholds;
- changes no assessment authority;
- does not convert completion into mastery;
- adds retrieval, transfer, fair representation, counterargument, verification, ethical evaluation, performance, deliberation, and exit-audit prompts only.

## Release criterion

Unit 05 is ready when:
1. all 15 instructional days receive the grade-specific evidence layer;
2. the grade-level scaffold fade is preserved;
3. authentic-source and data custody remain visible;
4. no duplicate generic prompts appear;
5. JavaScript parses cleanly;
6. storage/assessment authority remains unchanged;
