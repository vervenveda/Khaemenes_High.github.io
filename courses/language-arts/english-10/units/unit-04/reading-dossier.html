WEEK 10 · UNIT 04

Central Ideas & Structure

Original practice civic excerpt

Our town can widen the bridge this year, or continue repairing the same bottleneck every season. The first choice costs more now; the second has already cost us time, missed deliveries, and emergency delays.

Rhetorical model

Central ideas develop through structure: problem/solution, cause/effect, comparison, concession or chronology. Identify how sections build on one another before judging whether the argument is persuasive.

Independent task

Select an approved historical American speech or essay. Record speaker/writer, title, date, stable source and assigned passage. Map the central idea through at least three structural moves.

Return to Week 10 classroom and saved work

WEEK 11 · UNIT 04

Purpose, Audience & Rhetorical Situation

Practice situation

A mayor addresses residents after a factory closure. A union representative speaks to workers the same day. Both discuss economic recovery, but their audiences, authority and desired actions differ.

Model

Rhetorical situation includes speaker, audience, occasion, purpose, constraints and available evidence. Ethos, pathos and logos are not ingredients added mechanically; they are choices shaped by that situation.

Independent task

Analyze an approved speech/essay for audience, purpose, occasion, constraints and appeals. Include one appeal that may work differently for a second audience.

Return to Week 11 classroom and saved work

WEEK 12 · UNIT 04

Rhetorical Devices & Analysis Essay

Original practice lines

We repaired the road. We reopened the clinic. We rebuilt the school. Now we must rebuild trust.

Device model

Repetition and parallelism matter when their placement and variation shape emphasis. Figurative language, analogy and contrast should be interpreted in context rather than counted.

Independent task

Compare two approved speeches or essays using repetition, analogy, parallelism or figurative language. Build a device/effect/evidence matrix, then draft a thesis for the rhetorical analysis essay.

Return to Week 12 classroom and saved work
