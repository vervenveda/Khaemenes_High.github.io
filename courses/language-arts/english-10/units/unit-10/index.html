Unit 10

Research Writing & Digital Publication

Write expository and argumentative research products, integrate sources, revise, publish digitally, and present findings.

Begin Week 28Unit AssessmentPortfolio
OverviewThree WeeksAssessmentStandards

Essential Question

How do writers transform research into clear, ethical, audience-ready publications?

Learning Priorities

  • Read complex literary and informational texts with purposeful annotation.
  • Use precise evidence and reasoning rather than unsupported opinion.
  • Draft, revise, edit, and publish work for a clear audience and purpose.
  • Discuss ideas fairly, listen actively, and respond with intellectual honesty.
  • Preserve major artifacts and reflections in the English II portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: Research paper, publication-ready digital product, and formal presentation.

Three-Week Sequence

Learn, practice, compose, and demonstrate.

Week 28

Expository Research Writing

How can research explain a complex topic clearly without oversimplifying it?

section structure, transitions, source integration, and formal style
Open Week

Week 29

Argument Research Writing

How can research support a nuanced position while acknowledging limitations and counterclaims?

formal objective tone, concession, qualification, and citation consistency
Open Week

Week 30

Digital Publication, Multimedia & Presentation

How can design and delivery improve understanding without distorting evidence?

headings, captions, accessible text, slide economy, and speaking notes
Open Week

Performance Evidence

Research paper, publication-ready digital product, and formal presentation.

Students submit planning, text evidence, at least one meaningful draft, feedback or conference notes, the finished product, and a reflection explaining the strongest revision and the next goal.

Standards Emphasis

Measured through authentic reading, writing, speaking, and reflection.

  • ELA.10.C.1.3 — Write to argue a position using logical reasoning, credible evidence from multiple sources, counterclaims, organization, transitions, and a formal objective tone.
  • ELA.10.C.1.4 — Write expository texts to explain and analyze information from multiple sources using logical organization, purposeful transitions, and appropriate tone/voice.
  • ELA.10.C.1.5 — Improve writing by considering feedback and revising for the needs of a specific audience.
  • ELA.10.C.2.1 — Present information orally with logical organization, coherent focus, credible evidence, and a clear perspective.
  • ELA.10.C.4.1 — Conduct research, refine the scope of a question, and synthesize information from multiple reliable and valid sources.
  • ELA.10.C.5.1 — Create digital presentations to improve understanding of findings, reasoning, and evidence.
  • ELA.10.C.5.2 — Use collaborative platforms to create and export publication-ready writing tailored to a specific audience with multimedia elements.
