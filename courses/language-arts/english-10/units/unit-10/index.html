Evidence-Based Grade 10 Learning Rhythm

Explain → Argue → Design → Publish → Present

Unit 10 converts research foundations into public communication: explanatory synthesis, nuanced researched argument, accessible digital publication, and evidence-centered presentation.

Week 28 · Expository Research Writing

Scope, source roles, concept-based organization, integrated evidence, definitions, explanation, and source limitations.

Week 30 · Digital Publication, Multimedia & Presentation

Visual hierarchy, captions, accessibility, evidence-safe design, speaking notes, and formal presentation.

Unit 10 rule: Design may clarify evidence but may never exaggerate, conceal uncertainty, or substitute visual appeal for source-supported reasoning.

Essential Question

How do writers transform research into clear, ethical, audience-ready publications?

Learning Priorities

  • Read complex literary and informational texts with purposeful annotation.
  • Use precise evidence and reasoning rather than unsupported opinion.
  • Draft, revise, edit, and publish work for a clear audience and purpose.
  • Discuss ideas fairly, listen actively, and respond with intellectual honesty.
  • Preserve major artifacts and reflections in the English II portfolio.

Length: Three weeks

The student research article began with a dramatic claim about town traffic. During revision, its author replaced the opening with a narrower finding supported by counted crossings. A footnote explained that the data covered only three mornings.

Standards Emphasis

Measured through authentic reading, writing, speaking, and reflection.

  • ELA.10.C.1.3 — Write to argue a position using logical reasoning, credible evidence from multiple sources, counterclaims, organization, transitions, and a formal objective tone.
  • ELA.10.C.1.4 — Write expository texts to explain and analyze information from multiple sources using logical organization, purposeful transitions, and appropriate tone/voice.
  • ELA.10.C.1.5 — Improve writing by considering feedback and revising for the needs of a specific audience.
  • ELA.10.C.2.1 — Present information orally with logical organization, coherent focus, credible evidence, and a clear perspective.
  • ELA.10.C.4.1 — Conduct research, refine the scope of a question, and synthesize information from multiple reliable and valid sources.
  • ELA.10.C.5.1 — Create digital presentations to improve understanding of findings, reasoning, and evidence.
  • ELA.10.C.5.2 — Use collaborative platforms to create and export publication-ready writing tailored to a specific audience with multimedia elements.

Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1

Unit 10 Learning Progression

Explain → Argue → Integrate Evidence → Design for Audience → Publish

Grade 10 moves from research foundations into audience-ready publication by requiring accurate synthesis, nuanced argument, source integration, revision, and evidence-preserving design.

Week 28

Expository Research Writing

Produce an explanatory section with accurate attribution, clear structure, and one stated limitation.

Week 29

Argument Research Writing

Draft a research argument section that links claim, evidence, reasoning, counterclaim, and limitation.

Week 30

Digital Publication, Multimedia & Presentation

Complete a publication audit covering accuracy, citation, accessibility, design rationale, audience effect, and one remaining limitation.

The 15-day sequence revisits prior evidence and research habits while increasing independence in civic argument, publication design, professional communication, accessibility, and consequence-aware revision.
