VERIFIED SOURCE CUSTODY · UNIT 10

Research-writing and digital-publication custody

Weeks 28–29. The student's Unit 09 source portfolio carries forward only if each cited source has complete item-level custody. New sources added during expository or argument drafting must meet the same title/author/date/stable-locator/access/license/claim/limitation record.

Week 30 · Digital publication and multimedia. Every external image, chart, recording, video, icon, quotation, dataset, or embedded media item is a separate custody object. Record creator, title/description, source URL, date, rights/license/permission, attribution text, accessibility alternative, and whether edits/crops were made.

Accessibility baseline. Use W3C WCAG 2.2 as the reference framework for accessible digital publication. WCAG 2.2

WEEK 28 · UNIT 10

Expository Research Writing

Source set · Cooling school grounds

A fictional facilities report compares shade trees, shade sails and reflective coatings. Trees provide long-term shade but require years to mature; sails provide immediate shade but require installation and inspection; coatings can reduce surface temperature but do not create overhead shade.

Expository challenge

The goal is not to pick a winner. The writer must explain how each option works, where evidence is strong or limited, and which factors matter under different conditions.

Worked synthesis

Organize by decision factor—speed, maintenance, cost, shade coverage—rather than by one source per paragraph. This prevents a research paper from becoming a stack of summaries.

Independent task

Draft a multi-source explanatory section with a controlling idea, at least three source integrations, one limitation, and a visual/table concept that clarifies rather than decorates.

Return to Week 28 classroom and saved work

WEEK 29 · UNIT 10

Research-Based Argument

Competing evidence

A student group favors tree planting for long-term benefit. A facilities manager favors shade sails for immediate relief. A budget memo warns that both options have ongoing maintenance costs.

Argument lesson

A nuanced research argument defines the decision standard before judging evidence. Cost, speed, durability, accessibility and environmental effect may point in different directions.

Worked model

A defensible thesis might recommend a phased combination rather than forcing a false either/or choice. The argument should explain why the selected criteria justify that recommendation and acknowledge what the evidence cannot resolve.

Independent task

Write a claim, evidence chain, counterclaim, rebuttal and works-cited plan. Audit every source sentence for accurate scope and level of certainty.

WEEK 30 · UNIT 10

Digital Publication, Multimedia & Presentation

Publication problem

The research paper must become a public-facing digital product. A slide deck that simply copies paragraphs from the paper fails to adapt the material.

Design principle

Use hierarchy, direct labels, captions, descriptive links, readable contrast, alt text, transcript/captions for audio or video, and a visible source record. Design must not exaggerate a weak result through scale, animation or cropped axes.

Presentation lesson

A formal presentation should state the question, evidence, conclusion, limitation and audience action. Delivery choices should improve comprehension rather than replace evidence.

Independent task

Create a storyboard for an accessible publication and a 5–7 minute presentation. Include source credits, one visual-data integrity check, one accessibility audit and one reflection on how the medium changed your explanation.
