WEEK 01 · SHORT FICTION

The Yellow Wallpaper

Author: Charlotte Perkins Gilman · Published: 1892

Assigned span: Complete short story

Reading focus: perspective; narrator reliability; social context; interpretive qualification

Evidence task: Track three places where the narrator's description and the reader's inference diverge. Qualify a claim so it does not diagnose the narrator or exceed the text.

Source custody: First published in The New England Magazine, 1892. Public domain in the United States.

WEEK 02 · REPORTED SPEECH / PRIMARY SOURCE

Women's Rights Convention report of Sojourner Truth's speech

Author: Sojourner Truth; reported by Marius Robinson · Published: 1851

Assigned span: Complete Robinson report published June 21, 1851

Reading focus: source version; transcription; rhetoric; provenance

Evidence task: Separate Truth's reported argument from the reporter's framing. Record what can be claimed confidently from this version and what requires version comparison.

Source custody: Anti-Slavery Bugle report by Marius Robinson, the earliest full report. Published in the United States in 1851; public domain.

WEEK 03 · DECLARATION / ARGUMENT

Declaration of Sentiments

Author: Elizabeth Cady Stanton and Seneca Falls Convention participants · Published: 1848

Assigned span: Complete declaration and resolutions

Reading focus: paraphrase; structural imitation; argument; attribution

Evidence task: Paraphrase one grievance without flattening its meaning, compare its structure with the Declaration of Independence, and document exactly where the parallel ends.

Source custody: Seneca Falls Convention, 1848. Published in 1848; public domain.
