Authentic-source custody

When an authentic literary, informational, rhetorical, or historical text is assigned, record the exact title, author/speaker or tradition, editor/translator when relevant, edition or stable source, assigned passage, provenance or permission/public-domain basis, and the local/offline or approved access route. A generic label such as “literary passage” is not a complete source record.

Keep annotations, quotations with locators, drafts, corrections, teacher feedback, and reflections in the existing Week 01–03 workspaces. This hub is read-only and does not replace quizzes, diagnostics, portfolios, mastery gates, or evaluator judgment.
