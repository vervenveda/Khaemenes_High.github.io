Unit 01

Foundations of Scholarly Reading & Writing

Establish scholar habits, diagnostic baselines, annotation, inference, paraphrase, summary, academic integrity, and assessment readiness.

OverviewThree WeeksAssessmentStandards

Essential Question

How do disciplined readers and writers build trustworthy interpretations?

Learning Priorities

  • Read complex literary and informational texts with purposeful annotation.
  • Use precise evidence and reasoning rather than unsupported opinion.
  • Draft, revise, edit, and publish work for a clear audience and purpose.
  • Discuss ideas fairly, listen actively, and respond with intellectual honesty.
  • Preserve major artifacts and reflections in the English II portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: Diagnostic portfolio: annotated reading, analytical paragraph, revised writing sample, and reflection.

Three-Week Sequence

Learn, practice, compose, and demonstrate.

Performance Evidence

Diagnostic portfolio: annotated reading, analytical paragraph, revised writing sample, and reflection.

Standards Emphasis

Measured through authentic reading, writing, speaking, and reflection.

  • ELA.K12.EE.1.1 — Cite evidence to explain and justify reasoning.
  • ELA.K12.EE.2.1 — Read and comprehend grade-level complex texts proficiently.
  • ELA.K12.EE.3.1 — Make inferences to support comprehension.
  • ELA.10.R.3.2 — Paraphrase content from grade-level texts.
  • ELA.10.C.1.5 — Improve writing by considering feedback and revising for the needs of a specific audience.
  • ELA.10.C.3.1 — Follow grade-level rules of standard English grammar, punctuation, capitalization, and spelling.
  • ELA.10.V.1.1 — Integrate grade-level academic vocabulary in speaking and writing.
