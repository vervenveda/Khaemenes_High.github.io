Unit 05

Argument, Debate & Civic Discourse

Compare opposing arguments, evaluate validity, write claims and counterclaims, and participate in structured discussion and debate.

Begin Week 13Unit AssessmentPortfolio
OverviewThree WeeksAssessmentStandards

Essential Question

How can a writer or speaker argue responsibly, fairly, and persuasively?

Learning Priorities

  • Read complex literary and informational texts with purposeful annotation.
  • Use precise evidence and reasoning rather than unsupported opinion.
  • Draft, revise, edit, and publish work for a clear audience and purpose.
  • Discuss ideas fairly, listen actively, and respond with intellectual honesty.
  • Preserve major artifacts and reflections in the English II portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: Evidence-based argument essay with oral defense or formal debate.

Three-Week Sequence

Learn, practice, compose, and demonstrate.

Week 13

Opposing Arguments & Validity

How can readers compare opposing arguments without misrepresenting either side?

objective summary, qualifying language, and logical transitions
Open Week

Week 14

Claim, Evidence, Counterclaim & Rebuttal

How does a responsible argument acknowledge complexity while defending a clear position?

formal tone, concession language, transitions, and source integration
Open Week

Week 15

Oral Argument, Discussion & Formal Debate

How do speakers defend ideas while listening, questioning, and responding ethically?

spoken transitions, concise phrasing, emphasis, and audience awareness
Open Week

Performance Evidence

Evidence-based argument essay with oral defense or formal debate.

Students submit planning, text evidence, at least one meaningful draft, feedback or conference notes, the finished product, and a reflection explaining the strongest revision and the next goal.

Standards Emphasis

Measured through authentic reading, writing, speaking, and reflection.

  • ELA.10.R.2.4 — Compare two opposing arguments on the same topic, evaluating effectiveness and validity of claims.
  • ELA.10.C.1.3 — Write to argue a position using logical reasoning, credible evidence from multiple sources, counterclaims, organization, transitions, and a formal objective tone.
  • ELA.10.C.2.1 — Present information orally with logical organization, coherent focus, credible evidence, and a clear perspective.
  • ELA.K12.EE.1.1 — Cite evidence to explain and justify reasoning.
  • ELA.K12.EE.4.1 — Use appropriate collaborative techniques and active listening.
  • ELA.K12.EE.6.1 — Use appropriate voice and tone when speaking or writing.
  • ELA.10.C.3.1 — Follow grade-level rules of standard English grammar, punctuation, capitalization, and spelling.
