Unit 02

Coming-of-Age & Literary Layers

Analyze literary elements, coming-of-age experiences, conflicts, point of view, and competing perspectives.

Begin Week 4Unit AssessmentPortfolio
OverviewThree WeeksAssessmentStandards

Essential Question

How do authors use character, conflict, setting, and perspective to create layered meaning?

Learning Priorities

  • Read complex literary and informational texts with purposeful annotation.
  • Use precise evidence and reasoning rather than unsupported opinion.
  • Draft, revise, edit, and publish work for a clear audience and purpose.
  • Discuss ideas fairly, listen actively, and respond with intellectual honesty.
  • Preserve major artifacts and reflections in the English II portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: Character and perspective analysis with evidence map and discussion reflection.

Three-Week Sequence

Learn, practice, compose, and demonstrate.

Standards Emphasis

Measured through authentic reading, writing, speaking, and reflection.

  • ELA.10.R.1.1 — Analyze how key elements enhance or add layers of meaning and/or style in a literary text.
  • ELA.10.R.1.3 — Analyze coming-of-age experiences in a text and how the author represents conflicting perspectives.
  • ELA.10.R.3.1 — Analyze how figurative language creates mood in texts.
  • ELA.10.V.1.3 — Use context clues, figurative language, word relationships, reference materials, and background knowledge to determine denotative and connotative meaning.
  • ELA.K12.EE.1.1 — Cite evidence to explain and justify reasoning.
  • ELA.K12.EE.3.1 — Make inferences to support comprehension.
  • ELA.K12.EE.4.1 — Use appropriate collaborative techniques and active listening.
