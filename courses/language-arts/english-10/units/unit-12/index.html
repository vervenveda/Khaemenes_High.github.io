Novel Study, Final Portfolio & Examination

Complete a long-form novel study, synthesize themes and perspectives, curate the final portfolio, and complete the final examination.

OverviewThree WeeksAssessmentStandards

Essential Question

What evidence best demonstrates mature reading, writing, research, speaking, and reflection?

Learning Priorities

  • Read complex literary and informational texts with purposeful annotation.
  • Use precise evidence and reasoning rather than unsupported opinion.
  • Draft, revise, edit, and publish work for a clear audience and purpose.
  • Discuss ideas fairly, listen actively, and respond with intellectual honesty.
  • Preserve major artifacts and reflections in the English II portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: Novel analysis, student-led portfolio conference, cumulative final examination, and completion certificate.

Standards Emphasis

Measured through authentic reading, writing, speaking, and reflection.

  • ELA.10.R.1.1 — Analyze how key elements enhance or add layers of meaning and/or style in a literary text.
  • ELA.10.R.1.2 — Analyze and compare universal themes and their development throughout a literary text.
  • ELA.10.R.1.3 — Analyze coming-of-age experiences in a text and how the author represents conflicting perspectives.
  • ELA.10.C.1.5 — Improve writing by considering feedback and revising for the needs of a specific audience.
  • ELA.10.C.2.1 — Present information orally with logical organization, coherent focus, credible evidence, and a clear perspective.
  • ELA.10.C.5.2 — Use collaborative platforms to create and export publication-ready writing tailored to a specific audience with multimedia elements.
  • ELA.K12.EE.5.1 — Use accepted rules of a format to create quality work.
