Unit 06

Synthesis & Midyear Mastery

Synthesize information, refine grammar and style, organize portfolio evidence, and complete the cumulative midterm.

Begin Week 16Unit AssessmentPortfolio
OverviewThree WeeksAssessmentStandards

Essential Question

How do readers synthesize sources and use feedback to demonstrate midyear mastery?

Learning Priorities

  • Read complex literary and informational texts with purposeful annotation.
  • Use precise evidence and reasoning rather than unsupported opinion.
  • Draft, revise, edit, and publish work for a clear audience and purpose.
  • Discuss ideas fairly, listen actively, and respond with intellectual honesty.
  • Preserve major artifacts and reflections in the English II portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: Source synthesis project, writing portfolio conference, and cumulative midterm examination.

Standards Emphasis

Measured through authentic reading, writing, speaking, and reflection.

  • ELA.10.R.3.2 — Paraphrase content from grade-level texts.
  • ELA.10.C.1.4 — Write expository texts to explain and analyze information from multiple sources using logical organization, purposeful transitions, and appropriate tone/voice.
  • ELA.10.C.1.5 — Improve writing by considering feedback and revising for the needs of a specific audience.
  • ELA.10.C.3.1 — Follow grade-level rules of standard English grammar, punctuation, capitalization, and spelling.
  • ELA.10.C.4.1 — Conduct research, refine the scope of a question, and synthesize information from multiple reliable and valid sources.
  • ELA.10.V.1.2 — Apply knowledge of etymology and derivations to determine meanings in grade-level content.
  • ELA.K12.EE.5.1 — Use accepted rules of a format to create quality work.
