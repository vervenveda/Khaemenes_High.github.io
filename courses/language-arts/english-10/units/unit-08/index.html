Unit 08

Classical, Mythic & Religious Adaptation

Read mythical, classical, and religious source texts as literature, then compare adaptations across texts and media.

Begin Week 22Unit AssessmentPortfolio
OverviewThree WeeksAssessmentStandards

Essential Question

What changes—and what remains—when foundational stories are adapted across time, culture, genre, or medium?

Learning Priorities

  • Read complex literary and informational texts with purposeful annotation.
  • Use precise evidence and reasoning rather than unsupported opinion.
  • Draft, revise, edit, and publish work for a clear audience and purpose.
  • Discuss ideas fairly, listen actively, and respond with intellectual honesty.
  • Preserve major artifacts and reflections in the English II portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: Comparative adaptation essay or multimedia analysis.

Three-Week Sequence

Learn, practice, compose, and demonstrate.

Week 22

Mythical & Classical Source Texts

How do foundational myths and classical texts encode cultural values and recurring conflicts?

etymology, Greek and Latin roots, and allusion integration
Open Week

Week 23

Religious Texts as Literature & Adaptation

How can readers analyze sacred or religious source texts respectfully as literature and cultural influence?

objective tone, contextual framing, respectful comparison, and citation
Open Week

Week 24

Adaptation Across Media & Comparative Essay

What is gained, lost, transformed, or emphasized when a source becomes a new work?

comparative organization, multimedia captions, and precise evaluative language
Open Week

Performance Evidence

Comparative adaptation essay or multimedia analysis.

Students submit planning, text evidence, at least one meaningful draft, feedback or conference notes, the finished product, and a reflection explaining the strongest revision and the next goal.

Standards Emphasis

Measured through authentic reading, writing, speaking, and reflection.

  • ELA.10.R.3.3 — Analyze how mythical, classical, or religious texts have been adapted.
  • ELA.10.R.1.1 — Analyze how key elements enhance or add layers of meaning and/or style in a literary text.
  • ELA.10.R.1.2 — Analyze and compare universal themes and their development throughout a literary text.
  • ELA.10.C.1.4 — Write expository texts to explain and analyze information from multiple sources using logical organization, purposeful transitions, and appropriate tone/voice.
  • ELA.10.C.5.1 — Create digital presentations to improve understanding of findings, reasoning, and evidence.
  • ELA.10.V.1.2 — Apply knowledge of etymology and derivations to determine meanings in grade-level content.
  • ELA.K12.EE.5.1 — Use accepted rules of a format to create quality work.
