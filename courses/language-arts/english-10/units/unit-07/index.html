Unit 07

Poetry, Ambiguity & Sound

Analyze poetic speaker, ambiguity, connotation, figurative language, mood, structure, sound, and comparison.

Begin Week 19Unit AssessmentPortfolio
OverviewThree WeeksAssessmentStandards

Essential Question

How do poets create layered meaning through speaker, image, sound, form, and ambiguity?

Learning Priorities

  • Read complex literary and informational texts with purposeful annotation.
  • Use precise evidence and reasoning rather than unsupported opinion.
  • Draft, revise, edit, and publish work for a clear audience and purpose.
  • Discuss ideas fairly, listen actively, and respond with intellectual honesty.
  • Preserve major artifacts and reflections in the English II portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: Comparative poetry analysis essay and expressive reading.

Standards Emphasis

Measured through authentic reading, writing, speaking, and reflection.

  • ELA.10.R.1.4 — Analyze how authors create multiple layers of meaning and/or ambiguity in a poem.
  • ELA.10.R.3.1 — Analyze how figurative language creates mood in texts.
  • ELA.10.V.1.3 — Use context clues, figurative language, word relationships, reference materials, and background knowledge to determine denotative and connotative meaning.
  • ELA.10.C.1.4 — Write expository texts to explain and analyze information from multiple sources using logical organization, purposeful transitions, and appropriate tone/voice.
  • ELA.10.C.2.1 — Present information orally with logical organization, coherent focus, credible evidence, and a clear perspective.
  • ELA.K12.EE.1.1 — Cite evidence to explain and justify reasoning.
  • ELA.K12.EE.6.1 — Use appropriate voice and tone when speaking or writing.
