Unit 09

Research Foundations

Design research questions, evaluate reliability and bias, take accurate notes, paraphrase, cite, and synthesize.

Begin Week 25Unit AssessmentPortfolio
OverviewThree WeeksAssessmentStandards

Essential Question

How do researchers ask focused questions and build reliable, ethical source records?

Learning Priorities

  • Read complex literary and informational texts with purposeful annotation.
  • Use precise evidence and reasoning rather than unsupported opinion.
  • Draft, revise, edit, and publish work for a clear audience and purpose.
  • Discuss ideas fairly, listen actively, and respond with intellectual honesty.
  • Preserve major artifacts and reflections in the English II portfolio.

Unit Record

Length: Three weeks

Major assessment: Research proposal, annotated bibliography, source evaluation matrix, and synthesis paragraph.

Three-Week Sequence

Learn, practice, compose, and demonstrate.

Week 25

Research Questions, Scope & Inquiry Design

How do researchers turn broad curiosity into a focused, answerable, worthwhile question?

question syntax, precise nouns and verbs, and proposal clarity

Week 26

Source Reliability, Bias & Lateral Reading

How can researchers decide whether a source is reliable, relevant, and valid?

Open Week

Week 27

Note-Taking, Paraphrase, Citation & Synthesis

How do researchers preserve accuracy while combining source ideas ethically?

quotation integration, signal phrases, citation punctuation, and paraphrase accuracy
Open Week

Performance Evidence

Research proposal, annotated bibliography, source evaluation matrix, and synthesis paragraph.

Students submit planning, text evidence, at least one meaningful draft, feedback or conference notes, the finished product, and a reflection explaining the strongest revision and the next goal.

Standards Emphasis

Measured through authentic reading, writing, speaking, and reflection.

  • ELA.10.C.4.1 — Conduct research, refine the scope of a question, and synthesize information from multiple reliable and valid sources.
  • ELA.10.R.3.2 — Paraphrase content from grade-level texts.
  • ELA.10.C.3.1 — Follow grade-level rules of standard English grammar, punctuation, capitalization, and spelling.
  • ELA.10.V.1.1 — Integrate grade-level academic vocabulary in speaking and writing.
  • ELA.K12.EE.1.1 — Cite evidence to explain and justify reasoning.
  • ELA.K12.EE.5.1 — Use accepted rules of a format to create quality work.
