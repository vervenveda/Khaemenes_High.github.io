# English 10 · Unit 07 Evidence Alignment
## Poetry, Ambiguity & Sound

**Upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Unit 07 propagation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade-level rhythm

**Notice Ambiguity → Test Reading → Trace Pattern → Compare → Defend**

Grade 10 asks learners to preserve ambiguity while testing interpretations against speaker, diction, connotation, figurative language, sound, form, and structure.

## Three-week architecture

### Week 19 · Poetic Speaker, Layers & Ambiguity
1. **Notice Ambiguity:** Identify a word, image, speaker statement, or structural moment that supports more than one plausible interpretation.
2. **Test Reading:** Develop two readings and test both against the whole poem rather than a single line.
3. **Trace Pattern:** Track how speaker, perspective, or repeated language changes the plausibility of each interpretation.
4. **Compare:** Explain where the two readings genuinely differ and where they can coexist without contradiction.
5. **Defend:** Defend the stronger reading while preserving the textual detail that keeps the alternative reading possible.

### Week 20 · Figurative Language, Mood & Connotation
1. **Notice:** Select one figurative or connotative pattern and describe its literal components before assigning symbolic meaning.
2. **Interpret:** Explain how the pattern influences mood or attitude without reducing the poem to a single emotion label.
3. **Trace:** Follow the image or connotation across the poem. Does its effect remain stable, intensify, reverse, or complicate?
4. **Test:** Propose an alternative mood interpretation and identify the exact language that supports or resists it.
5. **Defend:** Write a claim linking figurative language, connotation, mood, and meaning with one stated limitation.

### Week 21 · Poetic Structure, Sound & Comparative Analysis
1. **Notice:** Identify one formal or sonic feature—lineation, stanza pattern, rhythm, rhyme, repetition, pause, or sound—and describe its placement.
2. **Test:** Explain how the feature changes emphasis, pacing, expectation, or interpretation when heard and when read silently.
3. **Compare:** Apply the same analytical criterion to two poems instead of comparing unrelated features.
4. **Qualify:** Identify where the comparison breaks down because form, speaker, context, or purpose differs.
5. **Defend:** Produce a comparative poetry claim that explains both a meaningful relationship and an important difference.

## Spacing and retrieval

The Unit 07 evidence layer uses a 15-day map across Weeks 19–21. Retrieval deliberately returns to:
- prior-unit evidence, synthesis, interpretation, rhetoric, research, and qualification habits;
- prior-day concepts;
- approximately one-week-delayed concepts;
- earlier Unit 07 methods;
- a cumulative research, comparative, contextual, translation, or interpretation audit on the final instructional day.

Each prompt is tied to the actual intellectual work of the day rather than a generic recall routine.

## Reading/source custody

Required reading remains in the existing unit/week pages, reading dossier, locally embedded Academy texts, approved authentic works, and approved research sources. Exact source, author, publication date, edition, translator, archive/database location, historical context, and provenance remain required where applicable.

## Mastery boundary

The Unit 07 evidence layer:
- writes no learner data;
- changes no storage keys;
- changes no quiz answers or thresholds;
- changes no assessment authority;
- does not convert completion into mastery;
- adds retrieval, research, lateral reading, ambiguity testing, contextualization, cross-cultural comparison, translation/intertextual analysis, synthesis, and exit-audit prompts only.

## Release criterion

Unit 07 is ready when:
1. all 15 instructional days receive the grade-specific evidence layer;
2. the grade-level scaffold fade is preserved;
3. authentic-source, translation, and research custody remain visible;
4. no duplicate generic prompts appear;
5. JavaScript parses cleanly;
6. storage/assessment authority remains unchanged;
7. the unit landing page communicates the learning progression.
