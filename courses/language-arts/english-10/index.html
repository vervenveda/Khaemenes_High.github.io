About English 10

English 10 builds on freshman foundations by asking students to read more complex texts, compare perspectives, analyze rhetoric and ambiguity, develop mature arguments, conduct stronger research, and communicate ideas across written, spoken, and digital forms. The course emphasizes evidence, revision, intellectual fairness, and increasingly independent literary and analytical work.

Course Features

Literary Analysis & Interpretation Students analyze characterization, structure, point of view, symbolism, ambiguity, theme, adaptation, drama, poetry, and sustained works with increasingly precise evidence.
Rhetoric & Argument Claims, counterclaims, evidence, reasoning, rhetorical choices, fallacies, audience, and ethical persuasion develop through writing, speech, debate, and revision.
Research & Publication Inquiry, source credibility, synthesis, citation, informational writing, digital composition, visual rhetoric, and publication strengthen independent academic communication.
Mastery & Portfolio Weekly evidence, corrections, revision memos, major writing products, novel study, cumulative assessments, and portfolio reflection document sophomore-level growth.
