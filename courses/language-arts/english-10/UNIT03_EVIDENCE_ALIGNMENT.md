# English 10 · Unit 03 Evidence Alignment
## Theme, Voice & Literary Analysis

**Upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Unit 03 propagation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade-level rhythm

**Retrieve → Compare → Interpret → Test → Synthesize**

Grade 10 reduces sentence-level scaffolds and requires comparison, competing interpretations, and revision decisions supported by textual evidence.

## Three-week architecture

### Week 7 · Universal Themes Across Texts
1. **Retrieve:** Recover one Unit 02 comparison habit and apply it to distinguish a broad topic from a defensible thematic claim.
2. **Compare:** Trace how two texts develop a similar theme through different character, setting, or structural choices. Preserve the important differences.
3. **Interpret:** Select the most consequential similarity and explain why it matters more than a superficial resemblance.
4. **Test:** Construct a competing thematic interpretation. Identify which pieces of evidence support each reading and where one interpretation overreaches.
5. **Synthesize:** Write a comparative claim that explains both connection and difference, then qualify the claim with one contextual or textual limitation.

### Week 8 · Diction, Syntax, Voice & Tone
1. **Retrieve:** Recall a thematic claim from Week 7. Identify one diction or syntax choice that could strengthen, complicate, or contradict that reading.
2. **Compare:** Compare two short passages for diction and sentence design. Explain how the patterns create different voices or tones.
3. **Interpret:** Move beyond labeling tone: identify the language pattern that produces it and explain the effect on meaning or audience.
4. **Test:** Propose two plausible interpretations of an ambiguous stylistic choice. Which is more defensible, and what evidence would change your judgment?
5. **Synthesize:** Write a style claim that connects diction, syntax, voice, and meaning without treating any single word as proof by itself.

### Week 9 · Literary Analysis Essay & Revision
1. **Retrieve:** Select the strongest analytical habit from Weeks 7–8 and explain how it should shape your thesis rather than merely appear in a body paragraph.
2. **Plan:** Build a claim-evidence-reasoning map that includes one competing interpretation or complication your essay must address fairly.
3. **Draft:** Draft a paragraph in which every quotation is followed by analysis of how and why the language supports the claim.
4. **Test:** Audit your draft for summary, unsupported inference, and overstatement. Revise one passage where the evidence does not fully justify the wording.
5. **Synthesize:** Submit a revision memo identifying your most important change, the evidence that motivated it, and how the final argument became more precise.

## Spacing and retrieval

The Unit 03 evidence layer uses a 15-day map across Weeks 7–9. Retrieval deliberately returns to:
- Unit 01–02 evidence, qualification, and comparison habits;
- prior-day concepts;
- approximately one-week-delayed concepts;
- earlier Unit 03 methods;
- a cumulative Unit 03 transfer, defense, or audit task on the final instructional day.

Each prompt is tied to the actual intellectual work of the day rather than a generic recall routine.

## Reading/source custody

Required reading remains in the existing unit/week pages, reading dossier, locally embedded Academy texts, and approved authentic works. Course-created practice texts remain clearly identified as instructional examples. Exact source, edition, translator, and provenance records remain required where applicable.

## Mastery boundary

The Unit 03 evidence layer:
- writes no learner data;
- changes no storage keys;
- changes no quiz answers or thresholds;
- changes no assessment authority;
- does not convert completion into mastery;
- adds retrieval, transfer, counterreading, qualification, revision, and exit-audit prompts only.

## Release criterion

Unit 03 is ready when:
1. all 15 instructional days receive the grade-specific retrieval/transfer layer;
2. the grade-level scaffold fade is preserved;
3. authentic-source custody remains visible;
4. no duplicate generic prompts appear;
5. JavaScript parses cleanly;
6. storage/assessment authority remains unchanged;
