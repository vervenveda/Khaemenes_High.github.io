Constructed Response / Performance Task

Research paper, publication-ready digital product, and formal presentation.

Using the unit's texts and source materials, create the major performance product for Unit 10. Address the unit essential question: How do writers transform research into clear, ethical, audience-ready publications? Preserve planning, evidence, a draft, feedback, the final product, and a reflection.

Scoring

  • Clear controlling idea, claim, or interpretation
  • Accurate and sufficient evidence
  • Reasoning that explains significance
  • Organization and transitions
  • Audience-appropriate voice and conventions
  • Revision evidence and reflection
