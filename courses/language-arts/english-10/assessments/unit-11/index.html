Constructed Response / Performance Task

Dramatic analysis and staged or recorded performance with reflection.

Using the unit's texts and source materials, create the major performance product for Unit 11. Address the unit essential question: How does performance reveal conflict, irony, rhetoric, and character more fully than silent reading alone? Preserve planning, evidence, a draft, feedback, the final product, and a reflection.

Scoring

  • Clear controlling idea, claim, or interpretation
  • Accurate and sufficient evidence
  • Reasoning that explains significance
  • Organization and transitions
  • Audience-appropriate voice and conventions
  • Revision evidence and reflection
