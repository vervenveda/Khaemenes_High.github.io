Constructed Response / Performance Task

Evidence-based argument essay with oral defense or formal debate.

Using the unit's texts and source materials, create the major performance product for Unit 05. Address the unit essential question: How can a writer or speaker argue responsibly, fairly, and persuasively? Preserve planning, evidence, a draft, feedback, the final product, and a reflection.

Scoring

  • Clear controlling idea, claim, or interpretation
  • Accurate and sufficient evidence
  • Reasoning that explains significance
  • Organization and transitions
  • Audience-appropriate voice and conventions
  • Revision evidence and reflection
