Constructed Response / Performance Task

Final Source-Based Synthesis

Using unfamiliar Grade 10 literary and informational sources selected by the teacher, write a formal response that synthesizes ideas, analyzes author choices, and demonstrates transfer of course skills. Complete an error analysis and final reflection after scoring.

Scoring
