Constructed Response / Performance Task

Comparative poetry analysis essay and expressive reading.

Using the unit's texts and source materials, create the major performance product for Unit 07. Address the unit essential question: How do poets create layered meaning through speaker, image, sound, form, and ambiguity? Preserve planning, evidence, a draft, feedback, the final product, and a reflection.

  • Reasoning that explains significance
  • Organization and transitions
  • Audience-appropriate voice and conventions
