# English 10 · Unit 08 Evidence Alignment
## Classical, Mythic & Religious Adaptation

**Upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Unit 08 propagation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade-level rhythm

**Establish Source → Preserve Context → Compare Transformation → Evaluate Adaptation → Qualify**

Grade 10 emphasizes respectful source custody, cultural context, adaptation evidence, and careful distinction between influence, transformation, and superficial resemblance.

## Three-week architecture

### Week 22 · Mythical & Classical Source Texts
1. **Establish Source:** Identify the source text, version or translation, cultural origin, and the values or conflicts that are actually evidenced in the text.
2. **Interpret:** Analyze one archetype, conflict, symbol, or structural pattern without treating it as culturally universal by default.
3. **Compare:** Compare two foundational texts using one meaningful criterion while preserving differences in purpose, context, and worldview.
4. **Test:** Identify one tempting cross-cultural similarity that becomes weaker after closer contextual analysis.
5. **Qualify:** Write a claim about recurring conflict or cultural value that distinguishes textual evidence from broader inference.

### Week 23 · Religious Texts as Literature & Adaptation
1. **Establish Source:** Record the exact passage, edition or translation, literary form, and cultural/religious context before analysis.
2. **Analyze:** Focus on narrative, imagery, structure, characterization, symbolism, or rhetoric while maintaining respectful language about living traditions.
3. **Compare:** Compare a source passage with an adaptation or later literary work, identifying what is preserved, altered, omitted, or recontextualized.
4. **Test:** Ask whether the later work demonstrates direct influence, common motif, or only resemblance. Identify the evidence for the relationship.
5. **Qualify:** Produce a respectful literary comparison that states both the interpretive claim and the limits of the evidence.

### Week 24 · Adaptation Across Media & Comparative Essay
1. **Establish Relationship:** Define the source/adaptation relationship and identify the adaptation’s medium, audience, and context.
2. **Compare Transformation:** Track one element—character, conflict, structure, symbol, theme, or point of view—across source and adaptation.
3. **Evaluate:** Judge what the adaptation gains, loses, emphasizes, or resists using explicit criteria rather than preference.
4. **Counter:** Develop an alternative evaluation that values a different adaptation choice. What evidence makes each judgment defensible?
5. **Qualify:** Write a comparative thesis that explains transformation while acknowledging contextual or medium-based limitations.

## Spacing and retrieval

The Unit 08 evidence layer uses a 15-day map across Weeks 22–24. Retrieval deliberately returns to:
- prior-unit evidence, synthesis, comparison, source evaluation, rhetoric, and qualification habits;
- prior-day concepts;
- approximately one-week-delayed concepts;
- earlier Unit 08 methods;
- a cumulative usability, adaptation, performance, multimedia, global-issue, or representation audit on the final instructional day.

Each prompt is tied to the actual intellectual work of the day rather than a generic recall routine.

## Reading/source custody

Required reading remains in the existing unit/week pages, reading dossier, locally embedded Academy texts, approved authentic works, and approved multimedia sources. Exact source, author/creator, publication date, edition, translator, cultural/religious context, medium, platform, and provenance remain required where applicable.

## Mastery boundary

The Unit 08 evidence layer:
- writes no learner data;
- changes no storage keys;
- changes no quiz answers or thresholds;
- changes no assessment authority;
- does not convert completion into mastery;
- adds retrieval, explanation, usability testing, adaptation comparison, cultural/contextual analysis, performance, multimedia interpretation, accessibility review, and exit-audit prompts only.
