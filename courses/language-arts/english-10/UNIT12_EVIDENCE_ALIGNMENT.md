# English 10 · Unit 12 Evidence Alignment
## Novel Study, Final Portfolio & Examination

**Upgrade:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** Unit 12 propagation of the Khaemenes Academy Evidence-Based English Architecture v1.

## Grade-level rhythm

**Track Whole-Text Pattern → Synthesize Theme → Curate Growth → Demonstrate Transfer → Plan Forward**

Grade 10 closes by combining sustained long-form reading with portfolio evidence, cumulative assessment, corrections, and an explicit transition into English 11.

## Three-week architecture

### Week 34 · Long-Form Novel Study: Character, Motif & Structure
1. **Track Whole-Text Pattern:** Trace a character arc, motif, structural pattern, or recurring image across the complete work rather than isolated chapters.
2. **Connect:** Explain how a later development changes the meaning of an earlier event, symbol, or relationship.
3. **Interpret:** Build a whole-text claim using evidence from beginning, middle, and end.
4. **Test:** Identify counterevidence or a pattern that resists the interpretation and revise or qualify the claim.
5. **Synthesize:** Write a sustained-reading statement connecting character, motif, structure, and meaning across the novel.

### Week 35 · Novel Themes, Perspectives & Final Portfolio Conference
1. **Synthesize Theme:** Compare how theme develops through more than one character, perspective, or structural pattern.
2. **Curate Growth:** Select portfolio evidence that demonstrates development across the year, including at least one artifact that required meaningful revision.
3. **Connect:** Link novel-study evidence to broader Grade 10 skills such as analysis, research, rhetoric, poetry, or publication.
4. **Conference:** Prepare evidence-based responses to questions about strengths, weaknesses, revision choices, and readiness.
5. **Reflect:** Complete the portfolio conference with one proven strength, one persistent challenge, and one measurable English 11 goal.

### Week 36 · Final Review, Final Examination & Completion
1. **Retrieve:** Build a review plan from actual performance evidence rather than reviewing every topic equally.
2. **Demonstrate Transfer:** Complete cumulative work that requires skills to transfer across unfamiliar passages, questions, or contexts.
3. **Correct:** Classify mistakes by reading, reasoning, evidence, writing, language, or execution and complete targeted corrections.
4. **Evaluate Readiness:** Use final, portfolio, and novel-study evidence to judge readiness for English 11 without relying on course completion alone.
5. **Plan Forward:** Create an English 11 readiness plan with one reading, writing, research, and speaking priority tied to evidence.

## Full-year retrieval and transfer

The Unit 12 evidence layer uses a 15-day map across Weeks 34–36. Retrieval deliberately returns to:
- full-year reading, writing, research, rhetoric, media, speaking, publication, and revision habits;
- earlier portfolio artifacts;
- prior assessments and corrections;
- long-form or capstone evidence;
- a cumulative final, oral defense, and next-stage literacy plan.

Each prompt requires students to demonstrate growth with evidence rather than rely on completion, self-description, or score alone.

## Reading/source custody

Required texts and evidence remain in the existing unit/week pages, reading dossier, student portfolio, approved authentic works, research sources, capstone materials, and assessment records. Exact source, author/creator, edition, translator, publication date, provenance, and citation records remain required where applicable.

## Mastery boundary

The Unit 12 evidence layer:
- writes no learner data;
- changes no storage keys;
- changes no final-exam answers or thresholds;
- changes no assessment authority;
- does not convert completion, portfolio presence, or oral participation into mastery;
- adds curation, revision, synthesis, cumulative retrieval, corrections, oral defense, readiness evaluation, transfer planning, and exit-audit prompts only.

## Release criterion

Unit 12 is ready when:
1. all 15 instructional days receive the grade-specific evidence layer;
2. the grade-level scaffold fade is preserved through final defense;
3. portfolio and capstone claims are tied to concrete evidence;
4. corrections remain separate from simple completion;
5. no duplicate generic prompts appear;
6. JavaScript parses cleanly;
7. storage/assessment authority remains unchanged;
8. the unit landing page communicates the final learning progression;
9. the learner exits with a documented next-stage literacy plan.
