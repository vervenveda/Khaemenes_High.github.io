Four-Year Language Arts Continuum

Choose the course that meets you here.

All four grade-level course portals are present. Each year should deepen textual complexity, independence, research, rhetorical control, writing craft, and public communication.

Grade 9 · English I

A+++ Progression

Four years should feel like four years of growth.

The department is being forensically hardened so progression is demonstrated in the work students actually perform—not merely in course titles.

Read

Move from supported close reading toward independent interpretation, comparison, synthesis, and sustained engagement with complex literary and informational texts.

Write

Move from controlled paragraphs and essays toward purposeful style, sophisticated argument, research, revision, publication, and senior capstone communication.

Research & Speak

Move from evidence selection toward source evaluation, transparent citation, media literacy, seminar leadership, presentations, civic discourse, and independent inquiry.

Language Arts Mentor

Eiren

Eiren is the designated mentor for Khaemenes Sr. High Language Arts. Mentor support belongs beside the learning: helping students question, interpret, plan, revise, reflect, and grow without replacing their own reading, thinking, or writing.

Work with Eiren

Use Eiren as a learning companion for difficult passages, discussion preparation, writing conferences, revision thinking, vocabulary and language questions, research reflection, and next-step planning.

Open Eiren · Language Arts Mentor

Mentor, not substitute

Bring your evidence, draft, question, or interpretation. Eiren should help you examine your reasoning and make stronger choices; the student's voice, judgment, and work remain the student's own.

Portfolio reflection

Use mentoring conversations to identify growth, recurring challenges, revision priorities, reading habits, and goals worth carrying into the next assignment or course.

Beta Learning Program

Help us improve the classroom.

Language Arts is a living educational program. Students, families, educators, and testers are invited to notice what works and identify what needs repair as the courses undergo forensic review.

What to notice

Report confusing instructions, broken or mismatched links, inaccessible material, unclear assessment logic, duplicated lessons, factual concerns, missing supports, or tools that do not strengthen the learning goal.

What Beta is not

Beta feedback does not replace coursework, mentoring, grading, or mastery evidence. It is a human feedback pathway for improving the educational experience.

Beta access

The department is standardizing the Beta pathway across every course and lesson during this audit. Until a canonical public Beta destination is verified, no guessed or internal route will be published here.

Academic & Public-Surface Contract

Rigorous learning. Clean public boundaries.

Language Arts is designed as a broad national and international pathway with standards as a floor rather than a ceiling. Student-facing pages may identify approved public educational destinations and their learning purpose, but they must not expose credentials, secrets, internal service maps, protected routes, infrastructure topology, or administrative implementation details.
