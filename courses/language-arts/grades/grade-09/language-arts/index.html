Department Framework

More than a literature survey.

The sequence integrates reading, writing, speaking, listening, language, research, digital literacy, creativity, and ethical publication.

Foundation

Explicit models, guided annotation, structured writing frames, vocabulary support, read-aloud options, and additional practice without lowering the intellectual goal.

Core

The complete grade-level program with sustained reading, varied composition, research, seminars, presentations, examinations, and portfolio evidence.

Extended

Honors-level text complexity, independent reading, longer research, rhetorical imitation, comparative criticism, publication leadership, and early-college skill exposure.
