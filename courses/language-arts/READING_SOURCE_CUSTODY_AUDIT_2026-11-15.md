# High School English 9–12 · Reading & Source Custody Audit
## Unit-Level Sweep · Phase 1

**Audit date:** 2026-11-15  
**Scope:** English 9, 10, 11, 12 · Units 01–12 · unit overviews and unit reading dossiers  
**Mode:** Evidence-preserving / fail-closed  
**Status:** PHASE 1 COMPLETE · AUTHENTIC-SOURCE HARDENING CONTINUES

## Executive finding

The English continuum has a usable first-party reading path in every unit, but it does not yet have complete custody records for every authentic, published, historical, long-form, scholarly, dramatic, poetic, or critical source described by the curriculum.

Two different layers must remain separate:

1. **Embedded Academy reading** — the actual first-party microtext displayed inside the unit overview.
2. **Authentic / extended reading** — any published essay, speech, poem, novel, drama, criticism, research source, historical document, translation, archive item, or teacher-selected work required beyond the embedded microtext.

A unit may PASS Layer 1 while Layer 2 remains HOLD.

## Layer 1 · Embedded Academy reading

**Status: PASS across all 48 units.**

The required embedded microtexts are:
- directly readable on the unit page;
- first-party/original Academy instructional texts as identified by the course;
- not dependent on an external website;
- tied to a reading, annotation, evidence, revision, or reflection task;
- usable as a stable local reading path.

These texts can support instructional modeling, retrieval, close reading, and passage-specific student evidence.

### Important boundary

A short Academy microtext does **not** automatically satisfy a curricular requirement for:
- a full novel;
- a complete play or substantial dramatic work;
- an authentic speech or essay;
- public-domain poetry;
- historical or civic documents;
- scholarly criticism;
- research studies;
- translated literature;
- archival or primary sources;
- a published mentor text.

Where the curriculum requires one of those source types, Layer 2 must pass independently.

## Layer 2 · Authentic / extended sources

The existing dossiers are generally strong instructional companions. They teach methods, provide original practice texts, and preserve return paths. However, many dossiers describe the authentic reading only as a category or selection instruction.

- English 9 Unit 06: public-domain speeches and essays by several civic authors, without fixed source records
- English 9 Unit 09: a teacher-approved full novel, with several public-domain choices, but no single learner-specific title/edition/access record
- English 10 Week 31: “a public-domain or licensed drama excerpt”
- English 10 Week 32: “a teacher-selected Shakespearean scene, speech, or soliloquy”
- English 10 Week 33: “a complete short play, act, or extended scene plus production notes”

These are legitimate curriculum specifications, but they are not complete custody records.

## External locator finding

Across the 44 pre-existing Unit 02–12 reading dossiers, the audit found no authenticated external source locator embedded for the actual assigned authentic work.

That is not automatically a defect for first-party practice material, but it means a dossier cannot be marked PASS for an external/authentic work solely because it says:
- “public-domain”;
- “licensed”;
- “teacher-selected”;
- “approved”;
- “selected criticism”;
- “complete novel”;
- “historical source”;
- or similar.

## Required custody record for every authentic work

Before release, record all applicable fields:

1. exact title;
2. author, speaker, tradition, institution, or creator;
3. editor and/or translator where relevant;
4. exact edition or stable source;
5. assigned chapter, act, scene, poem, speech, pages, section, or passage;
6. provenance basis;
7. public-domain, permission, license, or first-party basis;
8. jurisdiction restriction where relevant;
9. approved local/offline route or stable access route;
10. accessibility route or equivalent format;
11. student-facing assignment tied to that specific source;
12. citation/attribution expectation;
13. teacher/evaluator boundary where human judgment is required.

A category is not a custody record.

## Release model

Use these states:

- **PASS · FIRST-PARTY** — exact Academy-created source is embedded/local and tied to a specific task.
- **PASS · AUTHENTIC** — exact authentic work, edition/source, provenance and access route are documented.
- **CONDITIONAL** — a learner/teacher may choose from a pre-cleared set, but the chosen item must be written into the learner/course custody record before use.
- **HOLD** — source is described only by category, author list, work family, “approved,” “selected,” or similar.
- **BLOCK** — source provenance/access is unsafe, unavailable, contradictory, or cannot be verified.
