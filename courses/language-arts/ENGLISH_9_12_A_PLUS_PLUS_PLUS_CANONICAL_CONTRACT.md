# Khaemenes Academy · English 9–12 A+++ Canonical Governance Contract

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Canonical scope:** English 9, English 10, English 11, English 12  
**Authority:** This file is the shared governance source of truth for the four-course high-school English continuum. Grade-local governance files are overlays only and may not weaken this contract.

## 1. Mastery

- Default mastery threshold is **80%**.
- Mastery is distinct from course grade, completion, participation, practice, and game activity.
- Canonical assessment evidence or approved human-reviewed evidence may grant mastery.
- Practice, support activities, games, viewing, attendance, or page completion may prepare a learner but may not grant mastery by themselves.
- Resources remain available before mastery.
- Retakes are unlimited unless an authorized teacher sets a local limit.
- A lower voluntary retake may not erase previously demonstrated mastery.
- Corrections target the failed skill; students should not repeat an entire unit when evidence identifies a narrower need.

## 2. Required attempt history

Canonical assessments preserve:
- firstScore
- latestScore
- bestScore
- attemptCount
- masteredAt
- missedQuestionIds
- skillSignals
- attemptedAt

## 3. Weekly learning loop

Every week should make this learning logic visible:
1. Learn
2. Explore
3. Practice
4. Check
5. Correct
6. Reassess
7. Extend
8. Record evidence

The evidence-based architecture may add retrieval, spacing, comparison, counterreading, transfer, reflection, or defense, but may not silently change assessment authority.

## 4. Human-review boundary

Human review remains authoritative for:
- essays
- research reports
- speeches
- debates
- portfolios
- oral defenses
- projects
- complex reasoning
- multimodal products
- capstones

Automated systems may support drafting, practice, organization, retrieval, feedback, or objective scoring where valid, but may not falsely certify complex human performance as mastered.

## 5. Four-grade scaffold fade

- **English 9 · Foundation:** explicit evidence prompts, visible reasoning steps, source custody, guided transfer.
- **English 10 · Intermediate:** comparison, competing interpretations, synthesis, audience adaptation, reduced sentence-level scaffolds.
- **English 11 · Advanced:** learner selects analytical framework, evaluates evidence quality, counters alternatives, and defends method.
- **English 12 · Senior Scholar:** learner frames inquiry, chooses method, tests scope and falsification conditions, audits limitations, and defends independent judgment.

Advanced learning increases complexity, independence, transfer, synthesis, source difficulty, and audience authenticity. It does not merely increase workload.

## 6. Accessibility contract

Learner-facing work should preserve:
- semantic headings and landmarks
- keyboard access and visible focus
- screen-reader-compatible structure
- sufficient contrast and no color-only meaning
- reduced-motion behavior
- readable print layout
- minimum body/button text of 16px
- printable/paper response where valid
- typed response where valid
- oral/audio response where academically valid
- captions/transcripts where applicable
- descriptive labels and concise instructions
- language support where academically valid

Alternate response modes must preserve the competency being measured.

## 7. Source and media custody

Where applicable, evidence records should retain:
- source title
- author/creator/speaker
- publication date
- edition
- translator
- archive/database/platform
- media provenance
- research method or data origin
- citation/attribution
- limitations relevant to interpretation

Academy-created instructional texts must remain distinguishable from authentic external sources.

## 8. Release gates

A release candidate should verify:
- 12 units
- 36 weeks
- 180 daily lesson blocks
- Unit 01–12 evidence-alignment records
- explicit 80% mastery contract
- completion is not mastery
- correction and reassessment remain available
- attempt history is preserved
- human-review work is marked
- source custody is visible
- no broken internal links
- no duplicate IDs on audited pages
- valid HTML/JSON/JavaScript where applicable
- no accidental answer-key or threshold changes
- accessibility and print behavior remain usable
- learner records remain isolated
- grade-level scaffold fade remains intact

## 9. Versioning rule

There is one canonical English 9–12 governance contract. Grade-local files may add course IDs, readiness keys, grade labels, standards, or course-specific release notes, but they must reference this contract rather than fork its rules.

When this contract changes, update the version here first, then validate grade overlays against it.
