The continuum now has a stronger structural reading path, but reading custody remains a continuing quality gate. A unit or week should not be considered fully released merely because a dossier exists; the assigned text/source must remain reachable, appropriately licensed or original, attributable, accessible, and connected to passage-specific student evidence.
