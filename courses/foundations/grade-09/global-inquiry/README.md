# Grade 09 Global Inquiry Foundations

**Status:** forensic candidate on `hardening/archaemenes-highschool`

This self-paced support pathway strengthens history, geography, civics, economics, source evaluation, and inquiry habits for Grade 09 learners without changing grade identity or lowering academic expectations.

## Model

- 36 weeks × 5 learning days
- 180 daily practice checks
- 80% daily and weekly mastery gates
- checkpoints at Weeks 9, 18, 27, 36
- learner-scoped formal records for eligible Grade 09 learners
- separate preview/practice state otherwise
- no automatic Family Registry or formal placement writes

## Scope

The sequence begins with chronology, maps, source types, sourcing, contextualization, corroboration, and causation; expands into human geography, civics, economics, trade, belief systems, power, conflict, technology, demography, revolution, imperialism, and industrialization; then builds toward nationalism, war evidence, genocide studies, Cold War systems, decolonization, comparative government, media verification, and a Global Studies 9 bridge.

## Instructional principle

The course is designed to teach learners to ask:

- What does this source actually show?
- Who created it, when, for whom, and why?
- What context changes its meaning?
- What other evidence corroborates or challenges it?
- What can be inferred, and what remains uncertain?

## Authority boundary

This course owns its learning evidence only. Family Registry owns learner identity/formal grade. NAIB may interpret and route next-step context. Family/Academy review remains the formal placement boundary. Archaemenes mentors but does not award mastery.
