# Grade 09 Mathematics Foundations

**Status:** forensic candidate on `hardening/archaemenes-highschool`  
**Role:** Grade 09 Foundation pathway below the Pre-Algebra Bridge  
**Mastery target:** 80%

## Purpose

Mathematics Foundations is for Grade 09 learners whose readiness evidence shows that immediate entry into Pre-Algebra would create unnecessary frustration or gaps. It is not a lower grade identity and is not labeled remedial.

The learner remains a Grade 09 student while strengthening the prerequisites needed to succeed in Pre-Algebra and later Algebra I.

## Pathway

`Grade 09 Readiness Gateway → Mathematics Foundations → Pre-Algebra Bridge → Algebra I / Integrated Mathematics I → Geometry / higher mathematics`

Placement recommendations remain separate from formal Academy placement. The course never rewrites the Family Registry.

## 36-week scope

The course contains 36 weeks × 5 instructional days = **180 daily lesson checks** covering:

- place value, magnitude, estimation, and whole-number operations;
- multiplication, division, factors, multiples, and primes;
- fraction meaning, equivalence, and all four operations;
- decimals and fraction/decimal/percent conversion;
- ratios, unit rates, proportions, and percent applications;
- financial percent, percent change, and introductory simple interest;
- signed numbers and integer operations;
- variables, expressions, properties, equations, and formulas;
- coordinate planes, patterns, functions, and slope intuition;
- measurement, perimeter, area, composite figures;
- tables, graphs, center, probability, sampling, and misleading displays;
- a Pre-Algebra readiness integration/capstone week.

## Interactive progression

Each week contains five sequential interactive lesson checks.

- A daily lesson is complete at **80% or higher** (4 of 5).
- The next day opens only after the prior day is mastered.
- The weekly mastery check opens after all five daily lessons are complete.
- Weekly mastery requires **80% or higher** (8 of 10).
- The next week remains locked until weekly mastery is reached.
- Friendly reminders explain locked states.
- Reassessment remains available.

## Flexible exit

Readiness checkpoints are designated after Weeks **9, 18, 27, and 36**.

A learner who demonstrates sufficient prerequisite mastery may be recommended for a Pre-Algebra verification before completing all 36 weeks. The support pathway therefore does not become an arbitrary year-long trap.

## Learner records

When a formal Grade 09 learner is available through `KhaemenesHighContinuity`, progress is stored under the Academy learner ID in:

`khaemenes_math_foundations_grade09_records_v1`

Preview/practice uses a separate non-formal local store and does not create Academy mastery or placement.

Formal records cannot be reset from the student-facing course page.

## Assessment generation

Daily and weekly checks are generated deterministically from week/day/item seeds so the formal weekly set is distinct from the daily practice set. Correct-answer choice positions are shuffled rather than fixed to one index.

Before final A+++ certification, the generated bank still requires automated/static duplicate review and independent mathematical answer verification across all generated parameter combinations.

## Tool philosophy

Tools support reasoning; they do not replace it. Planned or existing ecosystem matches include:

- Scientific Calculator;
- Fraction Studio;
- Ratio Builder;
- Integer Explorer;
- Equation Forge;
- Coordinate Cartographer;
- Pattern Machine;
- Function Machine Lab;
- Geometry/CAD resources;
- Data Detective;
- Math Market / Finance Hall.

Only tools with a clear instructional purpose should be assigned to a week.
