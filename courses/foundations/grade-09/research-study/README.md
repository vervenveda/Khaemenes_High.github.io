# Grade 09 Academic Research & Study Studio

**Status:** forensic candidate on `hardening/archaemenes-highschool`

This cross-subject studio strengthens planning, organization, source integrity, data literacy, revision, and independent learning. It may run beside any Grade 09 course and must not be used by itself to lower subject placement.

## Model

- 36 weeks × 5 learning days
- 180 daily practice checks
- 80% studio mastery progression
- checkpoints at Weeks 9, 18, 27, 36
- learner-scoped formal records for eligible Grade 09 learners
- separate preview/practice state otherwise
- no automatic grade or placement mutation

## Scope

The sequence covers assignment reading, project planning, time calibration, focus, file organization, notes, retrieval practice, feedback, search, provenance, lateral reading, claim tracing, fact/inference/opinion, perspective, corroboration, citation, tables, graphs, rates, statistics, causation, sampling, uncertainty, data-to-claim reasoning, research questions, outlining, synthesis, quotation, revision, portfolio practice, help-seeking, and an independent project bridge.

## Design principle

This track is support infrastructure, not a remedial label. A learner may be advanced in a subject and still use the Studio for planning, research, or organization. Support remains available when useful even after formal readiness is established.

## Authority boundary

The Studio owns only its learning evidence. Family Registry owns identity/formal grade. Course portals own subject mastery. NAIB may interpret needs and route resources. Family/Academy retains formal placement authority. Archaemenes mentors but does not award mastery.

## Forensic status

Before production use, the generated item space, duplicate patterns, correct-answer positions, data-literacy calculations, source-integrity wording, browser/accessibility behavior, print output, and learner-state isolation require final validation. Workspace evidence needs a clear adult/Academy rubric before any high-stakes use.
