# Grade 09 Science Foundations & Lab Reasoning

**Status:** forensic candidate on `hardening/archaemenes-highschool`  
**Purpose:** a non-stigmatizing support pathway for Grade 09 learners who need stronger scientific reasoning prerequisites before or alongside Integrated Science 9.

## Course model

- 36 self-paced weeks
- 5 learning days per week
- 180 daily practice checks
- daily mastery threshold: 80%
- weekly mastery threshold: 80%
- readiness checkpoints at Weeks 9, 18, 27, and 36
- learner-scoped formal records when a valid Grade 09 Academy learner is active
- separate preview/practice storage otherwise
- no automatic grade or course placement writes

## Scope

The course deliberately begins with scientific practice rather than vocabulary memorization:

1. testable questions;
2. variables and fair tests;
3. measurement and units;
4. tables and graphs;
5. rates and proportional reasoning;
6. controls and comparisons;
7. repeated trials;
8. evidence quality;
9. investigation checkpoint;
10. lab safety;
11–18. physical-science reasoning and checkpoint;
19–27. life/Earth systems reasoning and checkpoint;
28–35. causation, uncertainty, CER, source evaluation, investigation design, data, argument, and bridge work;
36. Integrated Science readiness checkpoint.

## Progression rule

Each learning day opens only after the prior day reaches 80% or higher. The weekly mastery check opens after all five days reach the threshold. The next week opens only after weekly mastery reaches 80% or higher.

Locked attempts receive a friendly prerequisite reminder. Reassessment remains available.

## Authority boundary

This course owns only its learner-scoped learning evidence. Family Registry owns learner identity and formal grade placement. NAIB may interpret or route next-step context. Khaemenes Academy/Family review remains the formal pathway decision boundary. Archaemenes may mentor but does not award mastery.

## Exit philosophy

A learner should not be held in Foundations for an arbitrary calendar year. Checkpoints provide evidence for Academy review of whether the learner is ready to transition into or continue more independently in Integrated Science 9.

## Current forensic limitations

This is not yet production-certified. Before release, the generated assessment space must be independently enumerated and verified for answer integrity, near-duplicate patterns, answer-position distribution, distractor collisions, accessibility, browser behavior, printing, and multi-learner isolation. Constructed CER/notebook work also needs a formal adult/Academy rubric for any high-stakes use.
