# Grade 09 Literacy Foundations Validation Gate

**Status:** FORENSIC CANDIDATE — not yet production approved.

## Required checks before release

### Content integrity

- Independently verify every question stem, key, distractor, and explanation.
- Confirm all grammar and punctuation items have one defensible answer.
- Confirm source-evaluation items do not reward political or ideological agreement.
- Confirm inference and tone items are sufficiently supported by the passage text.
- Confirm paraphrase/citation guidance is accurate and consistent.

### Assessment diversity

- Enumerate all generated daily, weekly, and checkpoint items across the full seed space.
- Detect exact and near-duplicate stems and answer-choice sets.
- Verify checkpoint items are not mere copies of the same week's mastery check.
- Audit answer-position distribution for exploitable patterns.
- Confirm distractor collisions cannot create two correct answers.

### Progression logic

- Day 1 opens for an eligible learner.
- Day 2–5 remain locked until the prior day reaches 80%.
- Weekly mastery remains locked until all five days are complete.
- Score below 80% preserves access to reassessment and keeps the next week locked.
- Score at/above 80% unlocks the next week.
- Weeks 9, 18, 27, and 36 checkpoints appear only after weekly mastery.
- Checkpoint success does not automatically rewrite formal placement.

### Record integrity

- Formal Grade 09 records are learner-scoped.
- Switching learners does not transfer mastery, workspace writing, or checkpoint scores.
- Preview/practice records remain separate from formal records.
- No learner or family identifier is passed through URLs or NAIB routing.

### Writing evidence

Current objective checks are not sufficient alone for high-stakes English placement. Before production use:

- add scored constructed-response rubrics;
- verify paragraph and analytical-writing evidence;
- add adult/Academy review of writing samples;
- calibrate exit decisions against English 9 entry expectations.

### Accessibility and UX

- keyboard-only navigation;
- visible focus states;
- screen-reader labels and logical reading order;
- no overflow on common mobile widths;
- print layout review;
- readable contrast and font sizing;
- friendly lock messaging;
- browser tests on current Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge.

## Completion criterion

Do not mark Literacy Foundations A+++ forensic validated until all content, assessment, progression, record, writing-evidence, accessibility, browser, and print checks pass.
