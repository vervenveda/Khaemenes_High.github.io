# Grade 09 Literacy Foundations

Khaemenes Academy · High School · Foundations Studio

## Purpose

Literacy Foundations is a self-paced Grade 09 support pathway for learners who need stronger reading, writing, evidence, grammar, source-use, or academic communication skills before or alongside English 9.

It is not a remedial identity and does not change the learner's Grade 09 placement. A learner may use Literacy Foundations while remaining core or advanced in other subjects.

## Structure

- 36 weeks
- 5 instructional days per week
- 180 daily learning checks
- 5-question daily mastery checks
- 10-question weekly mastery checks
- 80% mastery gate
- readiness checkpoints at Weeks 9, 18, 27, and 36
- learner-scoped formal records when an eligible Grade 09 Family Registry learner is active
- separate preview/practice storage otherwise
- printable current lesson
- learner writing workspace saved locally

## Curriculum sequence

The course progresses through:

1. reading for meaning and directions;
2. vocabulary and sentence foundations;
3. paragraphing, inference, evidence, and summary;
4. purpose, tone, structure, and point of view;
5. argument, thesis, coherence, and revision;
6. grammar and punctuation in context;
7. paraphrase, quotation, source evaluation, bias, and synthesis;
8. analytical, narrative, informative, and argument writing;
9. research questions, citation, editing, genre reading, and English 9 bridge work.

## Mastery rule

A daily lesson is complete at 80% or above. Days unlock sequentially. The weekly mastery check opens only after all five daily lessons are complete. The next week opens only after the current weekly mastery check reaches 80% or above.

Reassessment remains available.

## Flexible exit

Weeks 9, 18, 27, and 36 include readiness verification. A successful checkpoint is evidence for Family/Academy review; it does not automatically rewrite course placement. Learners who demonstrate readiness may move into English 9 or a targeted bridge without waiting for the end of the year.

## Authority boundary

Family Registry owns learner identity and formal placement. NAIB receives, interprets, delegates, and connects. Khaemenes Academy provides Archaemenes. The course owns learning evidence; it does not create learner identity or silently change formal placement.
