Grade 09 Foundations Studio

Strengthen what is needed. Keep moving forward.

A Grade 09 learner may need support in one subject and advanced work in another. These tracks strengthen prerequisites without changing grade membership, lowering expectations, or trapping a learner in a year-long remedial label.

One Grade 09 learner. Different starting points.

Readiness evidence determines which support track is useful. Tracks are mastery-based and may be exited as soon as the learner demonstrates readiness for the next formal course.

Five support tracks

Mathematics FoundationsNumber → Pre-Algebra readiness

Number sense, operations, fractions, decimals, ratios, percent, signed numbers, expressions, one-step equations, coordinate reasoning, geometry, data, and mathematical habits.

Exit evidence: prerequisite strand mastery at 80%+ plus a Pre-Algebra readiness verification.

Literacy FoundationsReading + Writing

Fluency with complex directions, vocabulary in context, comprehension, inference, paragraph structure, sentence control, evidence use, revision, and short analytical writing.

Exit evidence: reading/evidence/writing readiness at 80%+ plus an English 9 bridge verification.

Science Foundations & Lab ReasoningEvidence + Measurement

Variables, measurement, units, graph reading, proportional reasoning in science, models, CER, laboratory safety, uncertainty, and evidence quality.

Exit evidence: scientific-reasoning readiness at 80%+ plus safe-lab and data interpretation verification.

Global Inquiry FoundationsHistory + Civics + Geography

Chronology, maps, cause/effect, primary and secondary sources, corroboration, basic civics/economics, historical comparison, and evidence-based interpretation.

Exit evidence: source/geography/civics reasoning at 80%+ plus a Grade 09 Global Studies readiness verification.

Academic Research & Study StudioAcross all subjects

Following multistep directions, project planning, note-taking, citation, source provenance, lateral reading, data tables/graphs, file organization, portfolio habits, and independent review.

This track may run alongside any Grade 09 course and should not be used to lower academic placement by itself.

Flexible entry and exit

Recommended checkpoints occur after focused blocks rather than only at semester end. A learner who demonstrates mastery may move from Foundations to Bridge or standard course work immediately after Academy review. A learner who needs longer may continue without being treated as unsuccessful.

Mastery rule: support is not a bypass around the Academy standard. Formal course progression still uses the 80% mastery gate. Foundations provide the instruction necessary to reach that standard.

Current build status

This portal defines the Grade 09 support architecture. The individual track lesson banks, interactive assignments, assessments, tool mappings, and print packages are the next curriculum-build layer and must pass the same Grade 09 forensic uniqueness and answer-integrity tests as the core courses.
