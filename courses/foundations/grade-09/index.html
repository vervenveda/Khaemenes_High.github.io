A Grade 09 learner may need support in one subject and advanced work in another. These tracks strengthen prerequisites without changing grade membership, lowering expectations, or trapping a learner in a year-long remedial label.

Flexible entry and exit

Recommended checkpoints occur after focused blocks rather than only at semester end. A learner who demonstrates mastery may move from Foundations to Bridge or standard course work immediately after Academy review. A learner who needs longer may continue without being treated as unsuccessful.

Mastery rule: support is not a bypass around the Academy standard. Formal course progression still uses the 80% mastery gate. Foundations provide the instruction necessary to reach that standard.

Current build status

All five Grade 09 support tracks now have 36-week interactive forensic-candidate portals with 180 daily learning checks, 80% weekly progression, learner-scoped formal records, separate preview records, and four checkpoints. They are built for forensic validation, not yet production-certified: answer integrity, duplicate analysis, independent cumulative checkpoint pools, accessibility, browser/mobile/print behavior, and learner-isolation tests remain required.
