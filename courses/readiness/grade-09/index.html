How this works

The core diagnostic samples Mathematics, Language Arts, Science, Social Studies, and Research/Academic Readiness. Borderline or advanced-looking results may trigger a short verification set so one lucky or unlucky cluster of questions does not decide placement.

Authority boundary: this gateway produces recommendations, not automatic promotion or demotion. NAIB coordinates interpretation/delegation. Family/Academy review confirms formal placement. Once a course begins, the Academy's normal 80% mastery progression applies.

Placement language

Foundations Recommended means prerequisite skills should be strengthened before the next formal course. Bridge Support means the learner is close and can work through targeted support while remaining in Grade 09. Grade 09 Ready means the standard course pathway is appropriate. Advanced Verification means the evidence is strong enough to justify a second-stage acceleration check, not automatic placement.

This version is a forensic-candidate educational placement tool. Its question bank and thresholds still require final cross-item validation, browser testing, accessibility review, and Academy approval before production use.
