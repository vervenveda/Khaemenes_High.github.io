Core Academics

Grade 9 Courses

Each subject begins with a common Grade 9 foundation, then uses readiness evidence and parent/guardian review to choose the most appropriate pathway. Individual courses remain authoritative for grades, assessments, and 80% mastery gates.

Adaptive Core Pathways

Right Level. Right Challenge. Parent Reviewed.

Unit 01 is the common foundation and readiness experience in every core subject. NAIB can organize the evidence and recommend a next step; the parent or guardian makes the placement decision.

The Grade 9 Bridge

Readiness is not a label.

Students should not be held in material they have already mastered, and students who need stronger foundations should receive targeted preparation without being treated as behind.

01Common FoundationUnit 01 introduces the subject, course environment, and essential starting skills.
02Readiness EvidenceCourse work, diagnostic challenges, corrections, projects, and other demonstrated learning form the evidence.
03NAIB AdvisoryNAIB identifies strengths, developing foundations, and possible acceleration without making the placement decision.
04Parent / Guardian ReviewThe family selects the pathway after reviewing the evidence and recommendation.
05Integrated LearningUnit 02 and beyond combine lessons, projects, labs, tools, games, research, reflection, and mastery evidence.
Placement principle: NAIB advises. Parent or guardian decides. Once a pathway is selected, the course's 80% mastery standard governs advancement within that pathway.
