# Grade 09 Placement and Course Standard

Pre-Algebra is the preserved reference implementation for Grade 09 course entrance, mastery, correction, pathway, record, mentor, and family behavior. It is not to be flattened, replaced, or reduced while other courses are brought into alignment.

## Required entrance sequence

1. Identify the learner through the future STOS account bridge.
2. Complete a pre-course readiness assessment for each core subject.
3. Record readiness separately from Course Mastery, Retention, and Refresh.
4. Assign either `core_36_week` or `supported_42_week`.
5. For a supported route, complete six foundation weeks before Official Week 01.
6. Require 80% mastery and meaningful corrections before advancement.
7. Preserve valid returning-learner evidence.

## Mathematics rule

Mathematics requires two separate decisions:

- `course_placement`: the appropriate mathematics course, such as Pre-Algebra or Algebra I.
- `duration_pathway`: the 36-week core route or the 42-week supported route within the selected course.

Pre-Algebra already implements a pre-course diagnostic, conditional six-week Unit 0, and the same 36 official course weeks for both pathways. Algebra I must not treat mere diagnostic completion as proof of readiness.

## Assessment authority

Browser storage is temporary continuity evidence only. It may support offline learning, drafts, and exports, but it is not an authoritative identity, grade, placement, role, or certificate source. STOS will verify and retain authoritative records when its sovereign connection is ready.

## Shared mastery rules

- Mastery threshold: 80%.
- Readiness and foundation work do not count in the official course grade.
- A review flag is not mastery.
- Preserve best demonstrated mastery and attempt history.
- A retry following a below-mastery result requires a meaningful reasoning correction.
- Direct entry to a gated lesson, week, unit, or assessment fails closed.
- Human-reviewed work remains `awaiting_human_review` until an authorized reviewer acts.

## Required course surfaces

Every Grade 09 core course must provide:

- pre-course readiness gateway;
- six-week foundation route;
- 36-week official route;
- weekly lesson and mastery evidence;
- midterm and final assessment;
- student progress and export view;
- mentor review guidance;
- family guide and family-safe progress summary;
- printable records and STOS-ready JSON export;
- offline fallback without required third-party runtime code.

## Canonical terminology

Use `Readiness Gateway`, `Foundation Weeks`, `Core 36-week Pathway`, `Supported 42-week Pathway`, `Official Week 01`, `Course Mastery`, `Refresh`, `Retention`, and `Readiness`. Avoid introducing equivalent route names without a migration map.
