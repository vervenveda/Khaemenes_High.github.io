## The Nervous System & Endocrine System

**Essential Question:** How does the body coordinate information, action, and internal regulation?

### Vocabulary in Context
central nervous system · peripheral nervous system · somatic · autonomic · sympathetic · parasympathetic · endocrine system · hormone

### Learning Objectives
- Explain the central concept accurately in psychological language.
- Apply it to a fictional, public, or academic example.
- Evaluate evidence using an alternative explanation and a meaningful limitation.

### Week Anchor
> Biology influences behavior without reducing a person to one brain region, one chemical, or one gene.
