# Week 02 · Day 1
## Neurons & Neural Communication

**Essential Question:** How can billions of small cells contribute to complex thought and behavior?

### Vocabulary in Context
neuron · dendrite · cell body · axon · myelin · action potential · synapse · neurotransmitter · receptor

### Learning Objectives
- Explain the central concept accurately in psychological language.
- Apply it to a fictional, public, or academic example.
- Evaluate evidence using an alternative explanation and a meaningful limitation.

### Week Anchor
> Biology influences behavior without reducing a person to one brain region, one chemical, or one gene.

### Psychological Scientist's Desk
**Claim → Measure → Evidence → Alternative Explanation → Limitation → Conclusion**

### Data Lens
The implemented lesson must include a small table, graph, dataset, or research-design comparison appropriate to the day's concept.

### Claims Laboratory
Classify a relevant popular claim as **Supported / Partly Supported / Unsupported / Cannot Tell**, then explain why.

### Guided Investigation
Build a neural communication model

### Independent Assignment
Correct a one-chemical-one-emotion claim

### Research Notebook Checkpoint
Identify one possible biological contributor without calling it the cause.

### Academic Journal
What changed or sharpened in your thinking today? What evidence would make your explanation stronger? What question remains?

### Daily Review
5 questions: 2 concept · 1 application · 1 research/data · 1 limitation/ethics.
