## Consciousness, Sleep & Biological Rhythms

**Essential Question:** How do changing biological states influence attention, awareness, and behavior?

### Vocabulary in Context
consciousness · circadian rhythm · biological clock · sleep-wake cycle · non-REM · REM · attention · memory consolidation

### Learning Objectives
- Explain the central concept accurately in psychological language.
- Apply it to a fictional, public, or academic example.
- Evaluate evidence using an alternative explanation and a meaningful limitation.

### Week Anchor
> Biology influences behavior without reducing a person to one brain region, one chemical, or one gene.
