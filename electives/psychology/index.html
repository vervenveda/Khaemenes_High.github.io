About the Psychology Pathway

The Psychology pathway is designed to teach students how to study behavior and mental processes responsibly. Course work emphasizes scientific reasoning, evidence quality, data interpretation, alternative explanations, ethical boundaries, individual dignity, and the difference between psychological education and personal diagnosis.

Permanent reasoning routine: Claim → Measure → Evidence → Alternative Explanation → Limitation → Conclusion. Psychology 101 is complete and student ready. Psychology 102, 201, and 202 will be released only after their curricula, assessments, accessibility, and student-facing experiences pass the Academy review process.
