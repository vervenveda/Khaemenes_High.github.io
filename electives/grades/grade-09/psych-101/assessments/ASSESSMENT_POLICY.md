# ASSESSMENT POLICY

## Course weights
- Weekly Lessons & Assignments — 25%
- Knowledge Checks — 15%
- Critical Thinking Activities — 15%
- Psychology Journal — 10%
- Unit Assessments — 15%
- Final Psychology Research Brief — 20%

## Weekly Knowledge Check blueprint
Each 10-question weekly assessment should include:
- 4 core knowledge questions
- 2 application/scenario questions
- 2 research/evidence questions
- 1 graph/data interpretation question
- 1 ethics, limitation, or context question

## Mastery
- 80% or higher = mastery
- Below 80% = targeted review, correction, and parallel-form retake
- Retakes should not simply repeat the identical question set

## Cumulative checkpoints
Planned cumulative assessments:
- after Week 4
- after Week 8
- after Week 12

Week 13 culminates in the Psychology Research Brief rather than a giant conventional final exam.
