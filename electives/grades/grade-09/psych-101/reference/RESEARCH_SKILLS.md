# RESEARCH SKILLS CENTER

## Scientific Method
**Question → Hypothesis → Evidence → Analysis → Conclusion**

## Core concepts
hypothesis · theory · operational definition · independent variable · dependent variable · control/comparison condition · population · sample · representative sample · quantitative research · qualitative research · correlation · causation · third variable · reliability · validity · replication · bias · ethics

## Data literacy strand
Students should progressively learn to read tables, bar graphs, line graphs, scatterplots,
percentages, means, medians, ranges, simple distributions, correlations, and comparison-condition data.

They should also recognize misleading axes, tiny samples, cherry-picked evidence,
correlation/causation errors, and overgeneralization.
