# PSYCHOLOGY & ETHICS

Psychology studies real people, so scientific curiosity must be balanced with ethical responsibility.

## Core responsibilities
- informed consent
- privacy
- confidentiality
- minimizing harm
- voluntary participation
- ethical review
- deception only under justified and approved conditions
- debriefing
- responsible communication of psychological information
- appropriate protection in non-human animal research

## Course boundary
This course does not train students to diagnose themselves or other people.
Students should not infer invisible biological states, disorders, personality diagnoses,
or mental-health conditions from short observations.
