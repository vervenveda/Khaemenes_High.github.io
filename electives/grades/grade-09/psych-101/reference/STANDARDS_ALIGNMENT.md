# Psychology 101 — Standards Alignment

## Primary Framework

This 13-week Grade 9 course is aligned to the **American Psychological Association (APA), National Standards for High School Psychology Curricula (2022 revision)**.

Official framework:
`https://www.apa.org/education-career/k12/national-standards`

Teacher resources:
`https://www.apa.org/education-career/k12/national-standards-resources`

The APA framework treats **Scientific Inquiry and Research Methods as the foundational base** for the course and organizes content into five pillars:
- Biological
- Cognition
- Development and Learning
- Social and Personality
- Mental and Physical Health

Psychology 101 preserves that structure while organizing the material into 13 integrated weeks.

## Scientific Inquiry Across All 13 Weeks

Scientific inquiry is not isolated in Week 1. Every locked week includes:
- operationalization/measurement;
- Psychological Scientist's Desk;
- Data Lens;
- Claims Laboratory;
- alternative explanations;
- explicit limitations;
- ethics/context;
- Research Notebook checkpoint.

Permanent reasoning routine:

**Claim → Measure → Evidence → Alternative Explanation → Limitation → Conclusion**

## APA Integrative Themes Preserved

The course repeatedly reinforces the APA themes that:
- psychological science relies on empirical evidence and adapts as evidence develops;
- general principles coexist with individual differences;
- psychological, biological, social, and cultural factors interact;
- diversity/context matter to interpretation;
- perceptions and biases can filter experience;
- psychological principles can improve lives, organizations, and communities;
- ethical principles guide research and practice.

## Mental & Physical Health Boundary

Weeks 11–12 deliberately separate:
- ordinary stress and health behavior;
- psychological disorders;
- treatment and professional practice.

This prevents normal experiences from being pathologized and keeps classroom literacy distinct from diagnosis/treatment.

Authoritative factual support for mental-health education and treatment literacy may use the U.S. National Institute of Mental Health (NIMH), including:
- Mental Health Topics: `https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics`
- Psychotherapies: `https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/psychotherapies`
- Child and Adolescent Mental Health: `https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/child-and-adolescent-mental-health`

The course does not substitute these educational materials for professional medical advice.

## Above-Minimum Course Features

Beyond content coverage, Psychology 101 adds:
- weekly research/data labs;
- cumulative evidence reasoning at Weeks 4, 8, and 12;
- a 13-week longitudinal Research Notebook;
- a final evidence-synthesis Research Brief;
- repeated graph/data interpretation;
- explicit misinformation/claim auditing;
- source hierarchy and evidence synthesis;
- person-level inference safeguards;
- anti-stigma and anti-diagnosis boundaries;
- offline/local-first technology design;
- 65 lesson-aligned psychology anchor terms;
- optional completion rewards that never control mastery.

## Alignment Verdict

**GREEN — COMPLETE ARCHITECTURE ALIGNMENT.**

The curriculum contains direct coverage for the APA 2022 high-school psychology foundation and standard areas, with scientific inquiry, evidence, ethics, diversity/context, and application woven across the full 13-week sequence rather than isolated into single chapters.
