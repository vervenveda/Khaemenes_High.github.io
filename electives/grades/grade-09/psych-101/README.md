## Permanent Interpretation Rules

- description is not explanation
- association is not automatically causation
- a measure is not the construct itself
- group findings do not automatically describe an individual
- psychological explanation is not diagnosis
- developmental averages are not personal deadlines or destinies
- trait descriptions summarize tendencies rather than identity
- a reliable measure is not automatically valid
- social influence is not mind control
- culture is context, not a fixed personality assigned to a group
- understanding behavior does not excuse harm
- stress is not automatically disorder
- symptoms are not diagnoses
- diagnosis is not identity
- treatment evidence is not personal treatment advice
- evidence-based does not mean guaranteed
- statistical detectability is not automatically practical importance
- internal validity is not automatic generalizability
- scientific revision is a strength, not a failure

## Assessment Model

Course weights:
- Weekly Lessons & Assignments — 25%
- Knowledge Checks — 15%
- Critical Thinking Activities — 15%
- Psychology Journal — 10%
- Unit / Cumulative Assessments — 15%
- Final Psychology Research Brief — 20%

Weekly mastery uses a 10-question mixed-format blueprint and an **80% threshold**. Below mastery, students receive targeted review/correction followed by a parallel-form retake.

The cumulative checkpoints after Weeks 4, 8, and 12 test transfer across units rather than pasting weekly quizzes together.

Week 13 uses the Research Brief and synthesis check rather than a giant conventional final exam.

## Student Privacy Boundary

The journal and assessed activities are academic, not therapeutic.

No student is required to disclose:
- trauma;
- diagnosis;
- medication;
- treatment history;
- family mental-health information;
- substance use;
- sexuality;
- other sensitive private material.

Fictional cases, public/non-sensitive examples, supplied datasets, and hypothetical research scenarios remain acceptable substitutes.

## Sovereign Design

The course is designed to operate without:
- login or account creation;
- advertising;
- external APIs;
- remote analytics;
- required internet access.

Student work and progress remain local with explicit export/import backup controls.

## Vocabulary & Enrichment

Each daily lesson includes one lesson-synchronized Psychology Word of the Day while the full lesson vocabulary remains in context.

The local Word Lab will adapt the useful learning pattern from the Arcade's Learn a New Word application without creating a runtime dependency on the Arcade repository.

Optional Completion Challenges may offer brief local rewards after genuine academic completion. Arcade contributes quick-play mechanics; Aurora contributes calm creative options. These activities are never part of mastery and are never used to infer a student's psychological state or ability.
