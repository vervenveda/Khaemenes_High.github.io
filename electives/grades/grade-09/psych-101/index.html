Course Commitments

Scientific depth with human dignity

Scientific Inquiry

Every week carries research design, evidence interpretation, data literacy, Claims Laboratory work, and explicit limitations.

Mastery

Weekly assessment uses an 80% threshold with targeted correction and parallel-form retakes. Reviewed is never mistaken for mastered.

Research Notebook

A 13-week question develops into the final Psychology Research Brief through repeated measurement, alternatives, ethics, and synthesis.

Clinical Boundary

The course teaches mental-health literacy without turning ordinary stress into disorder or classroom knowledge into amateur diagnosis/treatment.

Privacy

Academic journaling never requires disclosure of trauma, diagnosis, medication, treatment history, or other sensitive private information.

Word of the Day

Each lesson receives one anchor psychology term while the full vocabulary remains embedded in context.

Optional Completion Rewards

Short Arcade/Aurora-derived local challenges may appear after genuine academic completion. They are never graded or used to profile students.

Sovereign by Design

No required login, ads, remote analytics, or external API dependency. Progress remains local with explicit export/import.
