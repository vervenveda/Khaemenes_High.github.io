Course Commitments

Scientific depth with human dignity

Mastery

Weekly assessments use an 80% threshold with targeted correction and parallel-form retakes. Reviewed is never mistaken for mastered.

Clinical Boundary

Psychological literacy is not permission to diagnose oneself or real people; treatment evidence is not personal treatment advice.

Privacy

Journal and research work are academic, not therapeutic. Fictional, public, non-sensitive, and course-supplied examples are always acceptable.

Sovereign Design

No login, ads, remote analytics, external APIs, or external runtime dependency is required. Student evidence stays local with explicit export/import recovery.
