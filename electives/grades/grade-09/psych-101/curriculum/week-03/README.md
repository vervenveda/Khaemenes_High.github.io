# WEEK 03 — Sensation, Perception & Attention
## STATUS: LOCKED

**Central Question:** How does the nervous system transform physical information into our experience of the world?

> The environment provides information; sensory systems detect some of it; attention selects some of it; perception organizes and interprets it.

## Daily Sequence
1. **Sensation & Transduction** — How does information from the physical world become information the nervous system can use?
2. **Vision, Hearing & the Other Senses** — How do different sensory systems extract different kinds of information from the same world?
3. **Perception: Building Meaning** — How does the brain organize sensory information into meaningful objects and events?
4. **Attention & Awareness** — Why can something reach the senses without reaching conscious awareness?
5. **Context, Experience & Perceptual Reality** — How can people perceive the same situation differently while still sharing a physical world?
