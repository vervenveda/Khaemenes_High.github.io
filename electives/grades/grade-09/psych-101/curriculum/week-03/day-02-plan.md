# Week 03 · Day 2
## Vision, Hearing & the Other Senses

**Essential Question:** How do different sensory systems extract different kinds of information from the same world?

### Vocabulary in Context
vision · rod · cone · frequency · amplitude · pitch · touch · taste · smell · vestibular sense · proprioception · cross-modal perception

### Learning Objectives
- Explain the central concept accurately in psychological language.
- Apply it to a fictional, public, or academic example.
- Evaluate evidence using an alternative explanation and a meaningful limitation.

### Week Anchor
> The environment provides information; sensory systems detect some of it; attention selects some of it; perception organizes and interprets it.

### Psychological Scientist's Desk
