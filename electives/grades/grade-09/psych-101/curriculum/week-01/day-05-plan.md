# Week 01 · Day 5
## Ethics, Replication & Scientific Judgment

**Essential Question:** What responsibilities come with studying behavior scientifically?

### Vocabulary in Context
informed consent · privacy · confidentiality · minimizing harm · deception · debriefing · ethical review · replication · converging evidence

### Learning Objectives
- Explain the central concept accurately in psychological language.
- Apply it to a fictional, public, or academic example.
- Evaluate evidence using an alternative explanation and a meaningful limitation.

### Week Anchor
> Do not ask only, “What explains this?” Ask, “What evidence permits me to say that?”

### Psychological Scientist's Desk
**Claim → Measure → Evidence → Alternative Explanation → Limitation → Conclusion**

### Data Lens
The implemented lesson must include a small table, graph, dataset, or research-design comparison appropriate to the day's concept.

### Claims Laboratory
Classify a relevant popular claim as **Supported / Partly Supported / Unsupported / Cannot Tell**, then explain why.
