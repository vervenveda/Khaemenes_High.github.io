**Central Question:** How do psychologists know what they claim to know?

> Do not ask only, “What explains this?” Ask, “What evidence permits me to say that?”

## Daily Sequence
1. **What Is Psychology?** — What makes psychology a science rather than simply the study of interesting human behavior?
2. **Psychological Perspectives & Scientific Explanation** — Why can several psychological explanations of the same behavior be useful at the same time?
3. **Research Methods & Measurement** — How can psychologists turn abstract ideas into things they can investigate?
4. **Data, Correlation & Causal Reasoning** — How do data change what we are justified in claiming?
5. **Ethics, Replication & Scientific Judgment** — What responsibilities come with studying behavior scientifically?
