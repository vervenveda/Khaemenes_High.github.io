# Week 02 · Day 5
## Studying the Brain, Neuroplasticity & Evidence

**Essential Question:** How can scientists study a living brain without mistaking measurement for explanation?

### Vocabulary in Context
EEG · fMRI · lesion evidence · behavioral measure · comparison condition · neuroplasticity · converging evidence · limitation

### Learning Objectives
- Explain the central concept accurately in psychological language.
- Apply it to a fictional, public, or academic example.
- Evaluate evidence using an alternative explanation and a meaningful limitation.

### Week Anchor
> Biology influences behavior without reducing a person to one brain region, one chemical, or one gene.

### Psychological Scientist's Desk
**Claim → Measure → Evidence → Alternative Explanation → Limitation → Conclusion**
