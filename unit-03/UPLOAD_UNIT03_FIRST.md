# Upload Unit 3

1. Unzip this package on your computer.
2. Upload the complete `courses/mathematics/pre-algebra/units/unit-03/` folder beside `unit-01/` and `unit-02/`.
3. Replace `courses/mathematics/pre-algebra/index.html` and `course-map.json`.
4. Replace root `README.md`, `manifest.webmanifest`, and `service-worker.js`.
5. Add `docs/releases/UNIT03_RELEASE.md` and `docs/audits/UNIT03_VALIDATION.md`.
6. Do not move or replace Unit 1, Unit 2, Grade 9, or the diagnostic.
