Community Scale & Rate Investigation

Investigate a real comparison involving rates, scale, resource use, travel, production, pricing, or design. Build a proportional model, test its assumptions, and communicate what the model can and cannot predict.

Open Project Workspace

Project sequence

Build and evaluate a multiplicative model.

Select a context and question

Examples: package pricing, travel speed, public-space scale, resource use, recycling rates, recipe production, art enlargement, garden planning, or community service data.

Collect paired quantities

Record at least ten input-output pairs with units and sources or measurement methods. Identify the independent and dependent variables.

Represent the relationship

Create a ratio table, double number line, equation, and graph. Calculate a unit rate and test whether the quotient remains constant.

Use the model

Solve at least four questions, including one proportion, one unit conversion, one scale or prediction problem, and one comparison between options.

Evaluate and communicate

Explain assumptions, identify any fixed fee, threshold, changing rate, or capacity limit, and state where a proportional model is useful or misleading.
