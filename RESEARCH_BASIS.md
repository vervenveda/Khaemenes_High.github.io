# Repair 07 — Research Basis

Checked: 2026-08-09

## National Academies Framework

The National Academies Framework calls for three-dimensional science learning integrating scientific/engineering practices, crosscutting concepts, and disciplinary core ideas in physical science, life science, Earth/space science, and engineering/technology.

Primary source:
https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/13165/

## Next Generation Science Standards

NGSS high-school performance expectations are written for the 9–12 grade band rather than prescribing one national grade-by-grade course order.

Repair 07 distributes and deepens those expectations across Grade 9 Integrated Science, Grade 10 Biology, Grade 11 Chemistry, Grade 12 Physics & Engineering, and federated Solanar Earth/atmospheric extensions.

Primary source:
https://www.nextgenscience.org/

## Laboratory benchmark

College Board science Course Audit pages specify at least 25% instructional time in inquiry/hands-on laboratory work for AP Biology, AP Chemistry, and AP Physics 1. Repair 07 adopts a comparable laboratory/inquiry target.

## Legal/accreditation boundary

There is no single U.S. national high-school science graduation requirement that certifies a course for every jurisdiction. Formal credit, graduation, scholarship, NCAA, AP, IB, Cambridge, state virtual-school, or accreditation claims require the applicable jurisdiction/program review.
